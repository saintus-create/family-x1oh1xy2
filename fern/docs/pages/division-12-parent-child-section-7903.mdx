# § 7903

*The phrase “appropriate public authorities”*

The phrase “appropriate public authorities”
as. used in
Article 3 of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of
Children means, with reference
to this state, the State
Department of Social Services, and that department shall
receive and act with reference to notices required by Article 3
of the compact.
