# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1515

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

No guardian of person for married minor Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, no guardian of the person may be appointed for a minor who is married or whose marriage has been dissolved. 
This section does not apply in the case of a minor whose marriage has been adjudged a nullity.
