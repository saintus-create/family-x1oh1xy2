No guardian of person for married minor

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, no
guardian of the person may be appointed for a minor who is
married or whose marriage has been dissolved. This section
does not apply in the case of a minor whose marriage has
been adjudged a nullity. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), 14,
operative July 1, 1991.)
