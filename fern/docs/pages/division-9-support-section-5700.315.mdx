# § 5700.315 — Nonparentage as defense

Nonparentage as defense
A party whose parentage of a child has been previously
determined by or pursuant to law may not plead nonparen-
tage as a defense to a proceeding under this part. (Added by
c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
