# Division of Property

## Section 2621

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Premarital debts; confirmation Debts incurred by either spouse before the date of marriage shall be confirmed without offset to the spouse who incurred the debt.
