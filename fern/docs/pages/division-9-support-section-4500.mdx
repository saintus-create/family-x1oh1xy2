# § 4500 — See also Sections 3557 (attorney’s fees

An order for child, family, or spousal support that is made, entered, or enforceable in this state is enforceable under this code, whether or not the order was made or entered pursuant to this code. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
