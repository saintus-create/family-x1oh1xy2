# Marriage

## Section 641.2

Environmental actions; objection to referee on grounds of technical qualifications In any action brought under Article 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=12600.">§ 12600</a>) of Chapter 6, Part 2, <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=3.">Division 3</a>, Title 3! of the Government Code, a party may object to the appointment of any person as referee on the ground that the person is not technically qualified with respect to the particular subject matter of the proceeding.
