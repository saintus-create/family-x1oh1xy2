Environmental actions;
objection to referee on
grounds of technical qualifications
In any action brought under Article 8 (commencing with
Section 12600) of Chapter 6, , , Title 3! of
the Government Code, a party may object to the appointment
of any person as referee on the ground that the person is not
technically qualified with respect to the particular subject
matter of the proceeding.
