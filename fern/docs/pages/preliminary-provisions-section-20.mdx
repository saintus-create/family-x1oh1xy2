# 20

Counsel of record, has affirmed and assented to the agree-

counsel of record, has affirmed and assented to the agree-
ment in open court or by written stipulation.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An agreement may be confirmed or otherwise incorporated in an order if a party fails to appear at a noticed hearing
on the issue involved in the agreement. (Added by Stats.1993,
c. 219 (A.B,1500), 116.87.)
