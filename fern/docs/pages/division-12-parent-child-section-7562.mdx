# 7562

Genetic testing of deceased person

If a person seeking genetic testing demonstrates good cause, the court may order genetic testing of a deceased person. _(Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 24. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
