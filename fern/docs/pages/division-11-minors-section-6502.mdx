# Minors

## Section 6502

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Transitional provisions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The use of or reference to the words “age of majority,” 
“age of minority,” “adult,” “minor,” or words of similar intent in any instrument, order, transfer, or governmental communication made in this state: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Before March 4, 1972, makes reference to individuals 21 years of age and older, or younger than 21 years of age. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  On or after March 4, 1972, makes reference to individuals 18 years of age and older, or younger than 18 years of age. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in subdivision (a) or in Chapter 1748 of the Statutes of 1971 prevents amendment of any court order, will, trust, contract, transfer, or instrument to refer to the 18-yearold age of majority if the court order, will, trust, contract, transfer, or instrument satisfies all of the following conditions: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  It was in existence on March 4, 1972. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  It is subject to amendment by law, and amendment is allowable or not prohibited by its terms. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  It is otherwisesubject to the laws of this state.
