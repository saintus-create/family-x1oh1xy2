# § 6502 — Transitional provisions

Transitional provisions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The use of or reference to the words “age of majority,”
“age of minority,” “adult,” “minor,” or words of similar
intent in any instrument, order, transfer, or governmental
communication made in this state:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Before March 4, 1972, makes reference to individuals
21 years of age and older, or younger than 21 years of age.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  On or
after March
4, 1972, makes
reference
to
individuals 18 years of age and older, or younger than 18
years of age.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  or in Chapter 1748 of the
Statutes of 1971 prevents amendment of any court order, will,
trust, contract, transfer, or instrument to refer to the 18-year-
old age of majority if the court order, will, trust, contract,
transfer, or instrument satisfies all of the following condi-
tions:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  It was in existence on March 4, 1972.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  It is subject to amendment by law, and amendment is
allowable or not prohibited by its terms.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  It is otherwise-
subject to the laws of this state.
