# 5700.712
*Part 6  Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 7  Support Proceeding Under Convention*

Personal information gathered or transmitted under this chapter may be used only for the purposes for which it was gathered or transmitted. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
