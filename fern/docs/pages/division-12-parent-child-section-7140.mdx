# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7140

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Entry of identifying information into department of motor vehicles records systems; statement of emancipation upon identification card. 
Notification to Department of Motor Vehicles upon voiding or rescission of declaration of emancipation; invalidation of identification cards.
