# 2201 — Bigamous and polygamous marriages; exceptions;

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

Bigamous and polygamous marriages; exceptions;
absentees
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A subsequent marriage contracted by a person during
the life of his or her former spouse, with a person other than
the former spouse, is illegal and void, unless:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The former marriage has been dissolved or adjudged a
nullity before the date of the subsequent marriage.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The former spouse <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  is absent, and not known to the
person to be living for the period of five successive years
immediately preceding the subsequent marriage, or <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  is
generally reputed or believed by the person to be dead at the
time the subsequent marriage was contracted.
