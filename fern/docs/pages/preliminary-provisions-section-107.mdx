# § 107 — Heard, notice the court determines is reasonable shall be

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Before the petition for a declaration of emancipation is
heard, notice the court determines is reasonable shall be
given to the minor’s parents, guardian, or other person
entitled to the custody of the minor, or proof shall be made to
the court that their addresses are unknown or that for other
reasons the notice cannot be given.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The clerk of the court shall also notify the local child
support agency of the county in which the matter is to be
heard of the proceeding. If the minor is a ward of the court,
notice shall be given to the probation department. If the
child is a dependent child of the court, notice shall be given to
the county welfare department.
