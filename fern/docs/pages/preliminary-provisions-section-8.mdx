# § 8 — Unless otherwise expressly stated:

Unless otherwise expressly stated:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Division” means a division of this code.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Part” means a part of the division in which that term
occurs.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Chapter” means a chapter of the division or part, as
the case may be, in which that term occurs.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Article” means an article of the chapter in which that
term occurs.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Section” means a section of this code.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Subdivision” means a subdivision of the section in
which that term occurs.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  “Paragraph” means a paragraph of the subdivision in
which that term occurs.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  “Subparagraph” means a subparagraph of the para-
graph in which that term occurs.
