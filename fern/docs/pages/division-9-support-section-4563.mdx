# 4563

Dissolution of account; return of funds

An account established pursuant to this chapter shall be dissolved and any remaining funds in the account shall be returned to the support obligor, with any interest earned thereon, upon the full payment and cessation of the child support obligation as provided by court order or operation of law. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
