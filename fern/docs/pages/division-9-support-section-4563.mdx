# 4563. Dissolution of account; return of funds

*Support*

Dissolution of account; return of funds
An account established pursuant to this chapter shall be
dissolved and any remaining funds in the account shall be
returned to the support obligor, with any interest earned
thereon, upon the full payment and cessation of the child
support obligation as provided by court order or operation of
law.
