# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 11482.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Applications claiming multiple entitlements, false identity, or for fictitious or nonexistent persons; punishment Any person who knowingly makes more than one application for aid with the intent of establishing multiple entitlements for any person for the same period, or who makes an application for aid by claiming a false identity for any person or by making an application for a fictitious or nonexistent person, is guilty of a felony and shall be subject to prosecution under the provisions of Chapter 9 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=10980.">§ 10980</a>) of Part 2.
