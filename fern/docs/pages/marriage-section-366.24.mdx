# General Provisions

## Section 366.24

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Tribal customary adoptions 
(a)(1) For purposes of this section, “tribal customary adoption” means adoption by and through the tribal custom, traditions, or law of an Indian child’s tribe. 
Termination of parental rights is not required to effect the tribal customary adoption.
