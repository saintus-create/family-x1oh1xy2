# § 3114 — Appointment of counsel for minor children;

Nothing in this chapter prohibits a court-appointed investigator from recommending to the court that counsel be appointed pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 3150) to represent the minor child. In making that recommendation, the court-appointed investigator shall inform the court of the reasons why it would be in the best interest of the child to have counsel appointed. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.81. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
