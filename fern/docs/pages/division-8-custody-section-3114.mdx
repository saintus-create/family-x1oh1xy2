# 3114 — Appointment of counsel for minor children;

Custody of Children

Appointment of counsel for minor children;
_rec-
ommendations
Nothing in this chapter prohibits a court-appointed investi-
gator from recommending to the court that counsel be
appointed pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with Section
3150) to represent the minor child.
In making that recom-
mendation, the court-appointed investigator shall inform the
court of the reasons why it would be in the best interest of the
child to have counsel appointed.
