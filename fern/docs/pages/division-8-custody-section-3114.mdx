# Custody of Children

## Section 3114

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Appointment of counsel for minor children; 
_recommendations Nothing in this chapter prohibits a court-appointed investigator from recommending to the court that counsel be appointed pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3150.">§ 3150</a>) to represent the minor child. 
In making that recommendation, the court-appointed investigator shall inform the court of the reasons why it would be in the best interest of the child to have counsel appointed.
