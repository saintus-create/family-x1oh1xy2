# 3105. Former legal guardians; visitation rights

*Custody of Children*

Former legal guardians; visitation rights
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Legislature finds and declares that a parent’s
fundamental right to provide for the care, custody, companionship, and management of * * * the parent’s children,

while compelling, is not absolute. Children have a fundamental right to maintain healthy, stable relationships with a
person who has served in a significant, judicially approved
parental role.
