# 6327

Ex parte orders; application of Part 4 of

Part 4 (commencing with Section 240) of Division 2 applies to the issuance of any ex parte order under this article, other than an order under Section 6322.5. Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 511, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.<div class="source-note">
