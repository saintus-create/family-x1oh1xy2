---
title: "6327. Ex parte orders; application of Part 4 of"
description: "Ex parte orders; application of Part 4 of"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-6327
---

Part 4 (commencing with Section 240) of Division 2 applies to the issuance of any ex parte order under this article, other than an order under Section 6322.5.

> *Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 511, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.*
