# § 3022

*Order for custody*

Order for custody
The court may, during the pendency of a proceeding or at
any time thereafter, make an order for the custody of a child
during minority that seems necessary or proper.
(Formerly
§ 3021, enacted by Stats.1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Renumbered § 3022 and amended by
Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
