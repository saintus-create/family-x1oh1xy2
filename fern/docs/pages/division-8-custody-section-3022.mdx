# Custody of Children

## Section 3022

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Order for custody The court may, during the pendency of a proceeding or at any time thereafter, make an order for the custody of a child during minority that seems necessary or proper. 
(Formerly 
§ 3021, enacted by Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), 
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
