# 7807 — Applicability of proceedings;

Parent and Child Relationship

Applicability of proceedings;
stay of proceedings
under specified statutory provisions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Sections 3020, 3022, 3040 to 3043, inclusive, and 3409
do not apply in a proceeding under this part.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in this subdivision and subdivision
(c), all proceedings affecting a child, including proceedings
under Divisions 8 (commencing with Section 3000) to 11
(commencing with Section 6500), inclusive, (commenc-
ing with Section 7500) to (commencing with Section
7600), inclusive, of this division, and (commencing with
Section 1400), (commencing with Section 1500), and
(commencing with Section 2100) of of the
Probate Code, and any motion or petition for custody or
visitation filed in a proceeding under this part, shall be stayed.
The petition to free the minor from parental custody and
control under this section is the only matter that may be
heard during the stay until the court issues a final ruling on
the petition.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not limit the jurisdiction of the court
pursuant to (commencing with Section 6240) and (commencing with Section 6300) of with respect
to domestic violence orders, or pursuant to Article 6 (com-
mencing with Section 300) of Chapter 2 of Part 1 of of the Welfare and Institutions Code with respect to
dependency proceedings.
