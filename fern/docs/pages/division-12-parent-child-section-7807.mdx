# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7807

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Applicability of proceedings; stay of proceedings under specified statutory provisions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Sections 3020, 3022, 3040 to 3043, inclusive, and 3409 do not apply in a proceeding under this part. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in this subdivision and subdivision 
(c), all proceedings affecting a child, including proceedings under Divisions 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3000.">§ 3000</a>) to 11 
(commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6500.">§ 6500</a>), inclusive, Part 1 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7500.">§ 7500</a>) to Part 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7600.">§ 7600</a>), inclusive, of this division, and Part 1 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1400.">§ 1400</a>), Part 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1500.">§ 1500</a>), and Part 4 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2100.">§ 2100</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of the Probate Code, and any motion or petition for custody or visitation filed in a proceeding under this part, shall be stayed. 
The petition to free the minor from parental custody and control under this section is the only matter that may be heard during the stay until the court issues a final ruling on the petition. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not limit the jurisdiction of the court pursuant to Part 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6240.">§ 6240</a>) and Part 4 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6300.">§ 6300</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.">Division 10</a> with respect to domestic violence orders, or pursuant to Article 6 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">§ 300</a>) of Chapter 2 of Part 1 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a> of the Welfare and Institutions Code with respect to dependency proceedings.
