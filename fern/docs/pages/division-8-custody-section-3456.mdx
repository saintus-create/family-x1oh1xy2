# § 3456

*At the request of a district attorney acting under Section*

At the request of a district attorney acting under Section
3455, a law enforcement officer may take any lawful action
reasonably necessary to locate a child or a party and assist the
district attorney with responsibilities under Section 3455.
Stats.1999, c. 867 (S.B.668), § 3.)
(Added by Stats.1999, c. 867 (S.B.668), § 3.)
