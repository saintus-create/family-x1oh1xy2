# § 3456 — Law enforcement officers; locating child or party

At the request of a district attorney acting under Section 3455, a law enforcement officer may take any lawful action reasonably necessary to locate a child or a party and assist the district attorney with responsibilities under Section 3455. _(Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)_
