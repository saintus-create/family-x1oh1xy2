# § 7803.

*Part 4 — Freedom From Parental Custody And Control · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

A declaration of freedom from parental custody and control pursuant to this part terminates all parental rights and responsibilities with regard to the child. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
