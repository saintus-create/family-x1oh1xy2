# Custody of Children

## Section 3454

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Appeals An appeal may be taken from a final order in a proceeding under this chapter in accordance with expedited appellate procedures in other civil cases. 
Unless the court enters a temporary emergency order under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3424.">§ 3424</a>, the enforeing court may not stay an order enforcing a child custody determination pending appeal.
