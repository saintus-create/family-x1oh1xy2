# § 3454 — Appeals

Appeals
An appeal may be taken from a final order in a proceeding
under this chapter in accordance with expedited appellate
procedures in other civil cases. Unless the court enters a
temporary emergency order under Section 3424, the enforeing court may not stay an order enforcing a child custody
of the case, the court may authorize law enforcement officers
to make a forcible entry at any hour.

UNIFORM
determination pending appeal.
(S.B.668), § 3.)
