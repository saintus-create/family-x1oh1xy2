# § 6211 — "Domestic violence" defined

“Domestic violence” is abuse perpetrated against any of the following persons:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A spouse or former spouse.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A cohabitant or former cohabitant, as defined in Section 6209.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A person with whom the respondent is having or has had a dating or engagement relationship.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A person with whom the respondent has had a child, where the presumption applies that the male parent is the father of the child of the female parent under the Uniform Parentage Act ( (commencing with Section 7600) of ).

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A child of a party or a child who is the subject of an action under the Uniform Parentage Act, where the presumption applies that the male parent is the father of the child to be protected.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Any other person related by consanguinity or affinity within the second degree.
