## Family Law Facilitator Act

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Short title">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 10000</Badge></div>
        <p>This division shall be known and may be cited as the Family Law Facilitator Act.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Office of the family law facilitator">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 10002</Badge></div>
        <p>Each superior court shall maintain an office of the family law facilitator staffed by an attorney licensed to practice law in this state who has mediation or litigation experience in family law.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Services provided by facilitator">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 10004</Badge></div>
        <p>Services include: providing educational materials; distributing forms; preparing support schedules based upon statutory guidelines; and providing referrals to local child support agencies and other community resources.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No cost to parties">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 10007</Badge></div>
        <p>The court shall provide the family law facilitator at no cost to the parties.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No attorney-client relationship">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 10013</Badge></div>
        <p>The family law facilitator shall not represent any party. No attorney-client relationship is created. Communications are not privileged, and the facilitator may provide services to the other party.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
