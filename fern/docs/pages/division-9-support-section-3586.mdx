# Support

## Section 3586

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Child support and spousal support agreements; failure to designate amount of payments If an agreement between the parents combines child support and spousal support without designating the amount to be paid for child support and the amount to be paid for spousal support, the court is not required to make a separate order for child support.
