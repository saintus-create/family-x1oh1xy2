# § 3586 — Child support and spousal support agreements;

If an agreement between the parents combines child support and spousal support without designating the amount to be paid for child support and the amount to be paid for spousal support, the court is not required to make a separate order for child support. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
