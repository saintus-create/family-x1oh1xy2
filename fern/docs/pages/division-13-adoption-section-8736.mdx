# Adoption

## Section 8736

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Chapter provisions as grounds for removal of child from placement The requirements of this chapter shall not be used as basis for removing a child who has been placed with a relative caregiver or foster parent prior to January 1, 1999, unless the noncompliance with the standards described therein present a danger to the health, safety, or emotional well-being of the child. (Added by Stats.1998, c. 983 (A.B.2286),
