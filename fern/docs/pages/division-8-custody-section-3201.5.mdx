Administration of programs;
education about
protecting children during family disruption
(a)  The programs
described
in this chapter shall be
administered by the family law division of the superior court
in the county.
(b)  For purposes of this chapter, “education about protect-
ing children during family disruption” includes education on
parenting
skills and the impact of parental conflict on
children, how to put a parenting agreement into effect, and
the responsibility of both parents to comply with custody and
visitation orders.
(Formerly 3201, added by Stats.1999, c.
1004 (A.B.673),
