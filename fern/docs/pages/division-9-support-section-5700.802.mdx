A record filed with a tribunal of this state under this
chapter must be in the original language and, if not in
English, must be accompanied. by an English translation.
(Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)

CHAPTER 9.
INTERSTATE RENDITION
Section
5700.801.
Husband
criminally in that state with having failed to provide for the
support of a child or other individual to whom a duty of
support is owed, the Governor may require a prosecutor to
investigate the demand and report whether a proceeding for
support has been initiated or would be effective.
If it
appears that a proceeding would be effective but has not been
initiated, the Governor may delay honoring the demand for a
reasonable time to permit the initiation of a proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If a proceeding for support has been initiated and the
individual whose rendition is demanded prevails, the Governor may decline to honor the demand.
If the petitioner
prevails and the individual whose rendition is demanded is
subject to a support order, the Governor may decline to
honor the demand if the individual is complying with the
support order. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5,
eff. Jan. 1, 2016.)
Personal information gathered or transmitted under this
chapter may be used only for the purposes for which it was
gathered or transmitted. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.
