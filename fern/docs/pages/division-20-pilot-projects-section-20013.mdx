---
title: "20013. Family Law Evaluator; cost to parties"
description: "Family Law Evaluator; cost to parties"
slug: division-20-pilot-projects-section-20013
---

The court shall provide the Family Law Evaluator at no cost to the parties.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.*
