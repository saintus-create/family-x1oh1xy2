# 981

There is no privilege under this article if the communica-

There is no privilege under this article if the communica-
tion was made, in whole or in part, to enable or aid anyone to
commit or plan to commit a crime or a fraud.
(Stats. 1965, c.
299, 2, operative Jan..1, 1967.)
Exception: Crime or fraud
