# 4205
Any notice from the local child support agency requesting a meeting with the support obligor for any purpose authorized under this part shall contain a statement advising the support obligor of the obligor’s right to have an attorney present at the meeting. _(Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 48. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
