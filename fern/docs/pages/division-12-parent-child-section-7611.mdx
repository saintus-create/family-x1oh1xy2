# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7611

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The petition or motion to challenge a presumption under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7540.">§ 7540</a> pursuant to this section shall be supported by a declaration under oath submitted by the moving party stating the factual basis for placing the issue of parentage before the court. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Genetic testing may not be used to challenge parentage, in either of the following cases: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A case that reached final judgment of parentage on or before September 30, 1980. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A case challenging the parentage of a spouse who is a parent pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7962.">§ 7962</a> or subdivision (a) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7613.">§ 7613</a>, except to resolve a dispute regarding whether the child was conceived through assisted reproduction.
