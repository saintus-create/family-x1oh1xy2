# Adoption

## Section 8606.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Consent to adoption of Indian children 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other section in this part, and in accordance with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1913.">§ 1913</a> of the Indian Child Welfare Act (25 U.S.C. Sec. 1901 et seq.), consent to adoption given by an Indian child’s parent is not valid unless both of the following occur: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The consent is executed in writing at least 10 days after the child’s birth and recorded before a judge. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The judge certifies that the terms and consequences of the consent were fully explained in detail in English and were fully understood by the parent or that they were interpreted into a language that the parent understood. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The parent of an Indian child may withdraw consent to adoption for any reason at any time prior to the entry of a final decree of adoption and the child shall be returned to the parent. 

 OF 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  After the entry of a final decree of adoption of an Indian child, the Indian child’s parent may withdraw consent to the adoption upon the grounds that consent was obtained through fraud or duress and may petition the court to vacate the decree. Upon a finding that consent was obtained through fraud or duress, the court shall vacate the decree and return the child to the parent, provided that no adoption that has been effective for at least two years may be invalidated unless otherwise permitted under state law.
