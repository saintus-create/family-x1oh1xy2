# § 4063

*Uninsured health care costs; payment procedures*

Uninsured health care costs; payment procedures
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When making an order pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of
subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 4062, the court shall:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Advise each parent, in writing or on the record, of
* * * the parent’s rights and liabilities, including financial
responsibilities.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Include in its order the time period for a parent to
reimburse the other parent for the reimbursing parent’s share
of the reasonable additional child support costs subject to the
requirements of this section.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Unless there has been an assignment of rights pursuant
to Section 11477 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, when
either parent accrues or pays costs pursuant to an order
under this section, that parent shall provide the other parent
with an itemized statement of the costs within a reasonable
time, but not more than 30 days after accruing the costs.
These costs shall then be paid as follows:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If a parent has already paid all of these costs, that
parent shall provide proof of payment and a request for
reimbursement of * * * that parent’s court-ordered share to
the other parent.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If a parent has paid * * * the parent’s court-ordered
share of the costs only, that parent shall provide proof of
payment to the other parent, request the other parent to pay
the remainder of the costs directly to the provider, and
provide the reimbursing parent with any necessary information about how to make the payment to the provider.
