# 2024.7. Health coverage

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

health coverage
or no-cost
On and after January 1, 2014, upon the filing of a petition
for dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage, or legal
separation, the court shall provide to the petitioner and the
respondent a notice informing * * * them that * * * they
may be eligible for reduced-cost coverage through the
California Health Benefit Exchange established under Title
22 (commencing with Section 100500) of the Government
Code or no-cost coverage through Medi-Cal.
The notice
shall include information on obtaining coverage pursuant to
those programs, and shall be developed by the California
Health Benefit Exchange.
2012, c. 851
(A.B.792), § 1. Amended by Stats.2019, c. 115 (A.B.1817),
§ 14, eff. Jan. 1, 2020.)
