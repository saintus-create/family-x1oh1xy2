# California Family Code § 7552

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Genetic testing shall be of a type reasonably relied on by experts in the field of genetic testing and performed in a testing laboratory accredited by either of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The AABB, formerly known as the American Association of Blood Banks, or a successor organization.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An accrediting body designated by the Secretary of the United States Department of Health and Human Services.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A specimen used in genetic testing may consist of a sample or a combination of samples of blood, buccal cells, bone, hair, or other body tissue or fluid. The specimen used in the testing need not be of the same kind for each person undergoing genetic testing.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Based on the ethnic or racial group of a person undergoing genetic testing, a testing laboratory shall determine the databases from which to select frequencies for use in calculating a relationship index. If a person or a local child support agency objects to the laboratory’s choice, the following rules shall apply:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Not later than 30 days after receipt of the report of the test, the objecting person or local child support agency may request the court to require the laboratory to recalculate the relationship index using an ethnic or racial group different from that used by the laboratory.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The person or local child support agency objecting to the choice of laboratories under this subdivision shall do either of the following:

<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  If the requested frequencies are not available to the laboratory for the ethnic or racial group requested, provide the requested frequencies compiled in a manner recognized by accrediting bodies.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Engage another laboratory to perform the calculations.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The laboratory may use its own statistical estimate if there is a question of which ethnic or racial group is appropriate. The laboratory shall calculate the frequencies using statistics, if available, for any other ethnic or racial group requested.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If, after recalculation of the relationship index pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  using a different ethnic or racial group, genetic testing does not identify a person as a genetic parent of the child, the court may require a person who has been tested to submit to additional genetic testing to identify a genetic parent.
