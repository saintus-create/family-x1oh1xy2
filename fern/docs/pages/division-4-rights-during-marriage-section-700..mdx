# § 700.

*Part 1 — General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

For the purposes of this division, a leasehold interest in real property is real property, not personal property. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
