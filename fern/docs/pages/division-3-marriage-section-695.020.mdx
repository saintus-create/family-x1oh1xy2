# § 695.020 — Transactions

Transactions
Community property
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Community property is subject to enforcement of a
money judgment as provided in the Family Code.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, if
community property that is subject to enforcement of a
money judgment is sought to be applied to the satisfaction of
a money judgment:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Any provision of this division that applies to the
property of the judgment debtor or to obligations owed to the
judgment debtor also applies to the community property
interest of the spouse of the judgment debtor and to
obligations owed to the other spouse that are community
property.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any provision of this division that applies to property
in the possession or under the control of the judgment debtor
also applies to community property in the possession or
under the control of the spouse of the judgment debtor.
1982, c. 1364, p. 5103, § 2, operative July 1,
1983. Amended by Stats.1983, c. 155, § 12, eff. June 30, 1983,
operative July 1, 1983; Stats.1992, c. 163 (A.B.2641), § 33,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
