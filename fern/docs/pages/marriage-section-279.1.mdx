# General Provisions

## Section 279.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Continuation of offenses The offenses enumerated in Sections 278 and 278.5 are continuous in nature, and continue for as long as the minor child is concealed or detained.
