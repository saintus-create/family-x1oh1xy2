# § 279.1

*Continuation of offenses*

Continuation of offenses
The offenses enumerated in Sections 278 and 278.5 are
continuous in nature, and continue for as long as the minor
child is concealed or detained. (Added by Stats.1996, c. 988
(A.B.2936), § 9.)
