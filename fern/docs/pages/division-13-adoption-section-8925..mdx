# § 8925.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 4 — Intercountry Adoptions*

A Hague adoption certificate or, in outgoing cases, a Hague custody declaration, obtained pursuant to Part 97 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations shall be recognized as a final valid adoption for purposes of all state and local laws. (Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 583, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2008.) FAM Family Code - FAM
