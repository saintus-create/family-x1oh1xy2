# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2253

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Children of annulled marriage;
custody
determination of
In a proceeding under this part, custody of the children
shall be determined according to Division 8 (commencing
with Section 3000). (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
