# § 2253 — Children of annulled marriage; determination of custo-

In a proceeding under this part, custody of the children shall be determined according to Division 8 (commencing with Section 3000). _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
