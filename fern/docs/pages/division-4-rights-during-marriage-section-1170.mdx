# § 1170 — Probation is granted, the court shall require the following

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If a person is convicted of violating this section and
probation is granted, the court shall require the following
minimum conditions of probation:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A mandatory minimum period of probation of 36
months.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A criminal court protective order protecting the victim
from further acts of violence or threats, and, if appropriate,
residence exclusion or stay-away conditions.
(3)<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Successful completion of no less than one year of a
child abuser’s treatment counseling program.

to begin participation in the program
immediately upon the grant of probation.
