# § 3930.

*Part 2 — Child Support · Chapter 1 — Duty Of Parent To Support Child*

A parent does not have the duty to support a child of the parent’s child. (Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 132. Effective January 1, 1994.) ARTICLE 4. Liability to Others Who Provide Support for Child [3950 - 3952] ( Article 4 enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. )
