# Support

## Section 3806

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Payment of maintenance and capital improvement costs; order The court may make an order specifying the parties’ 
respective responsibilities for the payment of the costs of routine maintenance and capital improvements.
