# § 3806 — Payment of maintenance and capital improvement

The court may make an order specifying the parties’ respective responsibilities for the payment of the costs of routine maintenance and capital improvements. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
