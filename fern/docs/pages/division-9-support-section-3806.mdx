# § 3806

*costs; order*

costs; order
Comments
[Revised Comment]
The court may make an order specifying the parties’
respective responsibilities for the payment of the costs of
routine maintenance and capital improvements. (Stats.1992,
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 3802 continues without substantive
change the introductory clause of the fourth sentence of subdivision
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  and the first sentence of subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of former Civil Code
Section 4700.10. Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  continues the remainder of the
c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
