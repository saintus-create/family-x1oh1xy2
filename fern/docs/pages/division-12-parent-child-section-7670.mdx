# 7670

There shall be no filing fee charged for a petition filed pursuant to Section 7662

There shall be no filing fee charged for a petition filed pursuant to Section 7662.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
