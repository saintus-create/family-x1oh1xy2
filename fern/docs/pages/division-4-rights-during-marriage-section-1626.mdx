# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1626

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Written contracts; effective upon delivery A contract in writing takes effect upon its delivery to the party in whose favor it is made, or to his agent. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
