# 1626. Written contracts;

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Written contracts;
effective upon delivery
California Practice Guide Civil Procedure before Trial - Claims and
Defenses Ch. 9(1)-B, Elements of Claim.

(2018), Agreement Violating Public Policy.
A contract in writing takes effect upon its delivery to the
party in whose favor it is made, or to his agent. (Enacted in
1872.)
