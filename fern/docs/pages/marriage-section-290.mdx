# § 290

A judgment or order made or entered pursuant to this code may be enforced by the court by execution, the appointment of a receiver, or contempt, or by any other order as the court in its discretion determines from time to time to be necessary. _(Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 86, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)_
