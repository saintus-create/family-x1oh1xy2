# 306.5. Parties to marriage; names; procedure for change

*Premarital Agreements*

Parties to marriage; names; procedure for change
of name
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Parties to a marriage shall not be required to have the
same name.
Neither party shall be required to change their name.
A person’s name
shall not change upon
marriage unless that person elects to change their
name pursuant to subdivision (b).
(b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  One party or both parties to a marriage may elect to
change the middle or last names, or both, by which that party
wishes to be known after solemnization of the marriage by
entering the new name
in the spaces provided on the
marriage license application without intent to defraud.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person may adopt any of the following last names
pursuant to paragraph (1):
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The current last name of the other spouse.
(B). The last name of either spouse given at birth.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A name combining into a single last name all or a
segment of the current last name or the last name of either
spouse given at birth.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A combination of last names.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A person may adopt any of the following middle names
pursuant to paragraph (1):
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The current last name of either spouse.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The last name of either spouse given at birth.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A combination of the current middle name and the
current last name of the person or spouse.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A combination of the current middle name and the
last name given at birth of the person or spouse.
(4)<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  An election by a person to change their
name pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  shall serve as a record of the
name change. A certified copy of a marriage certificate
containing the new name, or retaining the former name, shall
constitute proof that the use of the new name or retention of
the former name is lawful.
