# § 17316 — Ment

No person, while holding the office of director, shall be a trustee, manager, director, or other officer or employee of any agency performing any function supervised by the department or any institution that is subject to examination, inspection, or supervision by the department. _(Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 478, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)_
