# Support Services

## Section 17316

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

ment No person, while holding the office of director, shall be a trustee, manager, director, or other officer or employee of any agency performing any function supervised by the department or any institution that is subject to examination, inspection, or supervision by the department.
