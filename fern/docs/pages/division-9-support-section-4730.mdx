# Support

## Section 4730

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Hearings to set or modify amount of child support; consideration of penalties At any hearing to set or modify the amount payable for the support of a child, the court shall not consider any penalties imposed under this chapter in determining the amount of current support to be paid.
