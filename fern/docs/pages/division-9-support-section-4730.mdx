# § 4730

*Hearings to set or modify amount of child support;*

Hearings to set or modify amount of child support;
consideration of penalties
At any hearing to set or modify the amount payable for the
support of a child, the court shall not consider any penalties
imposed under this chapter in determining the amount of
current support to be paid.
