# 17407.5
A declaration of state reciprocity issued by the Attorney General on or before December 31, 2015, and a declaration issued pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 5700.308, shall remain in full force and effect unless one of the following occurs:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The declaration is revoked or declared invalid by the Attorney General, in consultation with the department, or by the other party to the reciprocity agreement.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The declaration is superseded by a subsequent federal bilateral agreement with the other party.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The declaration is superseded by the other party’s ratification of or accession to the Hague Convention on the International Recovery of Child Support and Other Forms of Family Maintenance.
