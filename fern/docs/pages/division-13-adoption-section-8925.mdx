# Adoption

## Section 8925

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Hague adoption certificate or custody declaration A Hague adoption certificate or, in outgoing cases, a Hague custody declaration, obtained pursuant to Part 97 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations shall be recognized as a final valid adoption for purposes of all state and local laws.
