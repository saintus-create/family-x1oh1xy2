# § 1456

*Court-appointed attorneys, examiners and investi-*

Court-appointed attorneys, examiners and investi-
gators; educational requirements
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In addition to any other requirements that are part of
the judicial branch education program, on or before January
1, 2008, the Judicial Council shall adopt a rule of court that
shall do all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Specifies the qualifications of a court-employed staff
attorney, examiner, and investigator, and any attorney ap-
pointed pursuant to Sections 1470 and 1471.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Specifies the number of hours of education in classes
related to conservatorships or guardianships that a judge who
is regularly assigned to hear probate matters shall complete,
1063

upon assuming the probate assignment, and then over a
three-year period on an ongoing basis.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Specifies the number of hours of education in classes
related to conservatorships or guardianships that a court-
employed staff attorney, examiner, and investigator, and any
attorney appointed pursuant to Sections 1470 and 1471 shall
complete each year.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Specifies the particular subject matter that shall be
included in the education required each year.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Specifies reporting requirements to ensure compliance
with this section.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In formulating the rule required by this section, the
Judicial Council shall consult with interested parties, includ-
ing, but not limited to, the California Judges Association, the
California Association of Superior Court Investigators, the
California Public Defenders Association, the County Coun-
sels’ Association of California, the State Bar of California,
the National
Guardianship
Association,
the Professional
Fiduciary Association of California, the California Associa-
tion of Public Administrators, Public Guardians and Public
Conservators, a disability rights organization, and the Associ-
ation of Professional Geriatric Care Managers.
