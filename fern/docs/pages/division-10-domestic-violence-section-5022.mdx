# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5022

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Written consent not a transmutation; exception 
(a) Except as provided in subdivision 
(b), a spouse’s written consent to a provision for a nonprobate transfer of community property on death is not a transmutation of the consenting spouse’s interest in the property. 
(b) This chapter does not apply to a spouse’s written consent to a provision for a nonprobate transfer of community property on death that satisfies <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=852.">Section 852</a> of the Family Code. 
Such a consent is a transmutation and is governed by the law applicable to transmutations.
