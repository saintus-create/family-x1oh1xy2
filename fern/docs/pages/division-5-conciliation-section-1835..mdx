# § 1835.

*Part 1 — Family Conciliation Court Law · Chapter 3 — Proceedings For Conciliation*

No fee shall be charged by any officer for filing the petition. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
