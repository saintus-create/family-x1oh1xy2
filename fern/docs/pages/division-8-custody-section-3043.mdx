Custody of Children

1500) of Chapter, 1 of Part 2 of of the Probate

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this section, a person has “perpetrated
domestic violence” when * * * the person is found by the
court to have intentionally or recklessly caused or attempted
to cause bodily injury, or sexual assault, or to have placed a
person in reasonable apprehension of imminent serious
bodily injury to that person or to another, or to have engaged
domestic violence, the court shall make a determination as to
