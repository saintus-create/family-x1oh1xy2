# § 3043 — Nomination of guardian by parent

Nomination of guardian by parent
In determining the person or persons to whom custody
should be granted under paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 3040, the court shall consider and give due
weight to the nomination of a guardian of the person of the
child by a parent under Article 1 (commencing with Section
1500) of Chapter, 1 of Part 2 of of the Probate
Code. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
