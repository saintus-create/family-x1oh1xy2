# Custody of Children

## Section 3043

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Nomination of guardian by parent In determining the person or persons to whom custody should be granted under paragraph (2) or (3) of subdivision 
(a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3040.">Section 3040</a>, the court shall consider and give due weight to the nomination of a guardian of the person of the child by a parent under Article 1 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1500.">Section 1500</a>) of Chapter, 1 of Part 2 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of the Probate Code.
