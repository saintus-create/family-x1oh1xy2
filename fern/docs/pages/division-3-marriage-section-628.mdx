# § 628 — Home supervision is not detained for the purposes of federal

1, or 636. A child released or placed on
home supervision is not detained for the purposes of federal
foster care funding.

have chosen to share one. another’s lives in an intimate and
committed relationship of mutual caring as described in
Family Code
