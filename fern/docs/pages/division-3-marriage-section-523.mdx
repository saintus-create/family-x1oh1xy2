Exceptions to discharge

Exceptions to discharge
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A discharge under section 727, 1141, 1228(a), 1228(b),
or 1328(b) of this title does not discharge an individual
debtor from any debt—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  for a tax or a customs duty—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  of the kind and for the periods specified in
section 507(a)<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  or 507(a)<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  of this title, whether
or not a claim for such tax was filed or allowed;
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  with respect to which a return, or equivalent
report or notice, if required—
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  was not filed or given; or
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  was filed or given after the date on
which such return, report, or notice was last
due, under applicable law or under any exten-
sion, and after two years before the date of the
filing of the petition; or
