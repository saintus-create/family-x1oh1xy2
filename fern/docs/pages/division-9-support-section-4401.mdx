# Support

## Section 4401

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Promise of adult child to pay for necessaries The promise of an adult child to pay for necessaries previously furnished to a parent described in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4400.">§ 4400</a> is binding.
