# 4401. Promise of adult child to pay for necessaries

*Support*

Promise of adult child to pay for necessaries
The promise of an adult child to pay for necessaries
previously furnished to a parent described in Section 4400 is
binding. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
1994.)
