# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 10980

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

The action for restitution may be satisfied by sending a registered letter requesting restitution to the last address at which the person was receiving public assistance.
