---
title: "10980. The action for restitution may be satisfied by"
description: "The action for restitution may be satisfied by"
slug: division-14-family-law-facilitator-section-10980
---

The action for restitution may be satisfied by
sending a registered letter requesting restitution to the last
address at which the person was receiving public assistance.
