# 16000.6
.
Negotiation
of agreements
in good faith on
behalf of Indian children
The State Department of Social Services shall negotiate in
good faith with the Indian tribe, organization, or consortium
in the state that requests development of an agreement with
the state to administer all or part of the programs under Title
IV-E of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C.
Sec. 671 et seq.)
on behalf of the Indian children who are under the authority
of the tribe, organization, or consortium.
