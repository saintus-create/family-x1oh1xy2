Petition; right to file; purpose
Before the filing of a proceeding for determination of
custody or visitation rights, for dissolution of marriage, for
nullity of a voidable marriage, or for legal separation of the
parties, either spouse or parent, or both, may file in the
family conciliation court a petition invoking the jurisdiction
of the court for the purpose of preserving the marriage by
effecting a reconciliation between the parties, or for amicable
settlement of the controversy between the spouses or parents,

so as to avoid further litigation over the issue involved.
