# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5624

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Signature, date and acknowledgment A revocable transfer on death deed is not effective unless the transferor signs and dates the deed and acknowledges the deed before a notary public.
