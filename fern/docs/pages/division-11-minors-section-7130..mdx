# § 7130.

*Part 6 — Emancipation Of Minors Law · Chapter 3 — Court Declaration Of Emancipation*

(a) A declaration of emancipation obtained by fraud or by the withholding of material information is voidable.

(b) A declaration of emancipation of a minor who is indigent and has no means of support is subject to rescission. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
