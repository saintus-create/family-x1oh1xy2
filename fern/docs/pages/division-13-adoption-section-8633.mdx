# Adoption

## Section 8633

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Civil penalty Any person or entity that violates this chapter is subject to a civil penalty of one thousand dollars ($1,000) or the amount of the contract fees, whichever is greater.
