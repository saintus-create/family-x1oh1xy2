# § 2031 — Applications for temporary orders

Applications for temporary orders
(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (b), during the
pendency of a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, for
nullity of marriage, for legal separation of the parties, or any
proceeding subsequent to entry of a related judgment, an
application for a temporary order making, augmenting, or
modifying an award of attorney’s fees, including a reasonable
retainer to hire.an attorney, or costs or both shall be made by
motion on notice or by an order to show cause.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The court shall rule on an application within 15 days of
the hearing on the motion or order to show cause.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An order described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  may be made
without notice by an oral motion in open court at either of
the following times:
.
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  At the time of the hearing of the cause on the merits.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  At any time before entry of judgment against a party
whose default has been entered pursuant to Section 585 or
586 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
The court shall rule on
any motion made pursuant to this subdivision within 15 days
and prior to the entry of any judgment.
