# § 6225 — Explicit statement of address not required

Explicit statement of address not required
A petition for an order described in this division is valid
and the order is enforceable without explicitly stating the
address of the petitioner or the petitioner’s place of resi-
dence, school, employment, the place where the petitioner’s
child is provided child care services, or the child’s school.
