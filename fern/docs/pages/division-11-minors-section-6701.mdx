# 6701

Restrictions on authority to contract

A minor cannot do any of the following:

(a) Give a delegation of power.

(b) Make a contract relating to real property or any interest therein.

(c) Make a contract relating to any personal property not in the immediate possession or control of the minor. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="source-note">
