# Minors

## Section 6701

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Restrictions on authority to contract A minor cannot do any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Give a delegation of power. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Make a contract relating to real property or any interest therein. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Make a contract relating to any personal property not in the immediate possession or control of the minor. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), 
§ 10, operative Jan. 
1, 1994.)
