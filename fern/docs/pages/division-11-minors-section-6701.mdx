# § 6701 — Restrictions on authority to contract

Restrictions on authority to contract
A minor cannot do any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Give a delegation of power.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Make a contract relating to real property or any
interest therein.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Make a contract relating to any personal property not
in the immediate possession or control of the minor.
(Stats.
1992,
c.
162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan.
1, 1994.)
