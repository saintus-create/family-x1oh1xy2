# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5142

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

P.O.D. payee 
“P.O.D. payee” means a person designated on a P.O.D. 
account as one to whom the account is payable on request after the death of one or more persons.
