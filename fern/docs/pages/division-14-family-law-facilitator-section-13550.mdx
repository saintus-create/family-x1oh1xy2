# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 13550

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Identification by insurers of claimants owing past-due child support; reporting; exceptions; withholding 909 Section from disability insurance payment; notice by department and compliance. 
13551. 
Immunity from liability for parties releasing information, withholding payments, or making disbursements; claimants owing past-due child support.
