# § 7135 — Effect upon

Effect upon
contractual
and property obligations
Reliance on representation of emancipation;
effect
A person who, in good faith, has examined a minor’s
identification card and relies on a minor’s representation that
the minor is emancipated, has the same rights and obligations
as if the minor were in fact emancipated at the time of the
Voiding or rescission of the declaration of emancipation
does not alter any contractual obligation or right or any
property right or interest that arose during the period that the

MINORS
representation.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
