# 695.040

Property that is not subject to enforcement of a money

Property that is not subject to enforcement of a money
judgment may not be levied upon or in any other manner
applied to the satisfaction of a money judgment. If property
that is not subject to enforcement of a money judgment has
been levied upon, the property may be released pursuant to
the claim of exemption procedure provided in Article 2
