(d)(1) A person listed in subdivision (c) shall not accept compensation for solemnizing a marriage while holding office. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person listed in subdivision (c) shall not solemnize a marriage pursuant to this section if they have been removed from office due to committing an offense or have been convicted of an offense that involves moral turpitude, dishonesty, or fraud.
