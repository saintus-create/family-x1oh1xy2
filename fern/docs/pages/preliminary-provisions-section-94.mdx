<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A justice or retired justice of the United States

<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A judge or retired judge of a bankruptcy court or a tax

also be solemnized by any of the following persons who are 18
years of age or older:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A Member of the Legislature or constitutional officer
of this state or
a Member of Congress of the United States
who represents a district within this state, or a former
Member of the Legislature or constitutional officer of this
state or a former Member of Congress of the United States
who represented a district within this state.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person that holds or formerly held an elected office
of a city, county, or city and county.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A city clerk of a charter city or serving in accordance
with subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 36501 of the Government
Code, while that person holds office.
(d)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A person listed in subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  shall not accept
compensation
for solemnizing
a marriage while holding
office.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person listed in subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  shall not solemnize a
marriage pursuant to this section if they have been removed
from office due to committing an offense or have been
convicted of an offense that involves moral turpitude, dishon-
esty, or fraud.
