# 5692

Tions period

tions period
action;
accrual of limita-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A contest proceeding pursuant to Section 5690 shall

not be commenced before the transferor’s death.
,
Contest of Validity of Transfer.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For the purposes of the applicable limitations period, a
contest proceeding accrues on the date of the transferor’s
death. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 293 (A.B.139), 17, eff: Jan.
1, 2016.)
