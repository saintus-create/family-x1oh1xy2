# 7142. Dated

*Minors*

dated.
No public entity or employee is liable for any loss or injury
resulting directly or indirectly from false or inaccurate
information contained in the Department of Motor Vehicles
records system or identification cards as provided in this part.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. I, 1994.)
