# § 3810 — Application of chapter

This chapter is applicable regardless of whether the deferred sale of home order is made before or after January 1, 1989. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
