# § 337.15

*of real property; latent deficiency in design, supervision,*

of real property; latent deficiency in design, supervision,
etc.; injury to property
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  No action may be brought to recover damages from
any person, or the surety of a person, who develops real
property or performs or furnishes the design, specifications,
surveying, planning, supervision, testing, or observation of
construction or construction of an improvement to real
property more than 10 years after the substantial completion
of the development or improvement for any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Any latent deficiency in the design, specification,
surveying, planning, supervision, or observation of construction or construction of an improvement to, or survey of, real
property.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Injury to property, real or personal, arising out of any
such latent deficiency.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  As used in this section, “latent deficiency” means a
deficiency which is not apparent by reasonable inspection.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  As used in this section, “action” includes an action for
indemnity brought against a person arising out of that
property;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Injury to property, real or personal, arising out of any
such patent deficiency; or
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Injury to the person or for wrongful death arising out
of any such patent deficiency.
