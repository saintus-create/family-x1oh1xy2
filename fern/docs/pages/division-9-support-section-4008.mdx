# § 4008

*children*

children
may
be subjected
to support
of
The community property, the quasi-community property,
and the separate property may be subjected to the support of
the children in the proportions the court determines are just.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
