# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 10501

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

The Judicial Council may prescribe the form of letters. 
See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1001.">§ 1001</a> (Judicial Council and local court rules). 
See also <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=10454.">§ 10454</a> (new letters to be issued when court orders limits or revokes independent administration authority).
