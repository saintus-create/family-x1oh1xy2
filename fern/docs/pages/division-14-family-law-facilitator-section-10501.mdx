The Judicial Council may prescribe
the form of letters.
See Section 1001 (Judicial Council and local
court rules).
See also Section 10454 (new letters to be issued when
court orders limits or revokes independent administration authority).
Background on Section 8405 of Repealed Code
Section 8405 was added by 1988 Cal.Stat. ch. 1199
