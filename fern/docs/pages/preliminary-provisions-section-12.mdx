# § 12 — Meaning of “shall” and “may”

“Shall” is mandatory and “may” is permissive. “Shall not” and “may not” are prohibitory. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
