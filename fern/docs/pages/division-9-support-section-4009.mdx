# § 4009 — Initial pleading. If the parent ordered to pay support was not

An original order for child support may be made retroactive to the date of filing the petition, complaint, or other
initial pleading. If the parent ordered to pay support was not
served with the petition, complaint, or other initial pleading
within 90 days after filing and the court finds that the parent
was not intentionally evading service, the child support order
shall be effective no earlier than the date of service.
