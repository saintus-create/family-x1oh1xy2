# § 6275 — Conditions under which an officer is to inform a

Conditions under which an officer is to inform a
person for whom emergency protective order may be
sought
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A law enforcement officer who responds to a situation
in which the officer believes that there may be grounds for
the issuance of an emergency protective order pursuant to
Section 6250 of this code or
