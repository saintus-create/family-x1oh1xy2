# § 1578

*Mistake of law*

Mistake of law
MISTAKE
OF LAW.
1 California Transactions Forms--Business Transactions § 6:21, Acceptance.
1 California Transactions Forms--Business Transactions § 6:23, Essentials of Assent.
Mistake of law constitutes a mistake,
within the meaning of this Article, only when it arises from:
1. A misapprehension of the law by all parties, all
supposing that they knew and understood it, and all making
Treatises and Practice Aids
substantially the same mistake as to the law; or,
1 , When
2. A misapprehension of the law by one party, of which
the others are aware at the time of contracting, but which
they do not rectify. (Enacted in 1872.)
Revocation Becomes Effective.
1 , Necessity
of Communication.
