# § 4003

*custody*

custody
10th
In a proceeding where there is at issue the support of a
child, the court shall require the parties to. reveal whether a
party is currently receiving, or intends to apply for, public
assistance under the Family Economic Security Act of 1982
(Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11200) of Part 3 of
of the Welfare and Institutions Code) for the
maintenance of the child.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219
(A.B.1500), § 134.)
