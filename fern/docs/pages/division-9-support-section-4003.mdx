# § 4003 — Separate trial; calendar preference; joinder with

Separate trial; calendar preference; joinder with
custody
In any case in which the support of a child is at issue, the
court may, upon a showing of good cause, order a separate
trial on that issue.
The separate
trial shall be given
preference over other civil cases, except matters to which
special precedence may be given by law, for assigning a trial
date.
If the court has also ordered a separate trial on the
issue of custody pursuant to Section 3023, the two issues shall
be tried together.
