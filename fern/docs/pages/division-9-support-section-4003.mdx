# Support

## Section 4003

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Separate trial; calendar preference; joinder with custody In any case in which the support of a child is at issue, the court may, upon a showing of good cause, order a separate trial on that issue. 
The separate trial shall be given preference over other civil cases, except matters to which special precedence may be given by law, for assigning a trial date. 
If the court has also ordered a separate trial on the issue of custody pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3023.">Section 3023</a>, the two issues shall be tried together.
