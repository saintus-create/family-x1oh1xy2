The section continued the
substance of a portion of former Probate Code Section 681 (repealed
by 1983 Cal.Stat. ch. 842 39). The 1987 amendments revised the
provisions relating to notice.
As to the 1987 amendment, see
Communication from California Law Revision Commission Concern-
ing Assembly Bill 708, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 545, 561
(1988);

to Conforming Revisions
and Repeals,

Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 391, 450 (1988).
For background
on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part under the
part heading. [20 Cal.L.Rev-Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
