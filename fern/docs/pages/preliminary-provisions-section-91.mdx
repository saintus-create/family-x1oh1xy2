# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 91

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

The section continued the substance of a portion of former Probate Code <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=681.">§ 681</a> (repealed by 1983 Cal.Stat. ch. 842 § 39). The 1987 amendments revised the provisions relating to notice. 
As to the 1987 amendment, see Communication from California Law Revision Commission Concerning Assembly Bill 708, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 545, 561 
(1988); Comments to Conforming Revisions and Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 391, 450 (1988). 
For background on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part under the part heading. [20 Cal.L.Rev-Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
