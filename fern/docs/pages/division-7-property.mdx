## Division of Property

  <h3>General rule of community property division</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 2550</Badge></div>
        <p>Upon dissolution of marriage, the community estate shall be divided equally between the parties unless they agree otherwise.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Reimbursement — separate property contributions</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 2640</Badge></div>
        <p>A party who contributes separate property to the acquisition of community property is entitled to reimbursement of that contribution, unless waived.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
