# 3176 — Notice of mediation and hearing

Custody of Children

Notice of mediation and hearing
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notice of mediation and of any hearing to be held
pursuant to this chapter shall be given to the following
persons:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Where mediation is required to settle a contested issue
of custody or visitation, to each party and to each party’s
counsel of record.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Where a stepparent or grandparent seeks visitation
rights, to the stepparent or grandparent seeking visitation

rights, to each parent of the child, and to each parent’s
counsel of record.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notice shall be given by certified mail, return receipt
requested, postage prepaid,
to the last known
address.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notice of mediation pursuant to Section 3188 shall
state that all communications involving the mediator shall be
kept confidential between the mediator and the disputing
parties.
