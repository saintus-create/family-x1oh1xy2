# Custody of Children

## Section 3176

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Notice of mediation and hearing 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notice of mediation and of any hearing to be held pursuant to this chapter shall be given to the following persons: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Where mediation is required to settle a contested issue of custody or visitation, to each party and to each party’s counsel of record. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Where a stepparent or grandparent seeks visitation rights, to the stepparent or grandparent seeking visitation 243 
§ 3178 rights, to each parent of the child, and to each parent’s counsel of record. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notice shall be given by certified mail, return receipt requested, postage prepaid, to the last known address. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notice of mediation pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3188.">§ 3188</a> shall state that all communications involving the mediator shall be kept confidential between the mediator and the disputing parties.
