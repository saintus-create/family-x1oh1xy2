# 16121.05. Adoption assistance and receipt of benefits;

*Support Services*

Adoption assistance and receipt of benefits;
recovery of overpayments; regulations
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department or county adoption agency may recov-
er any overpayments of financial assistance under the Adop-
tion Assistance Program, and shall develop regulations that
establish the means to recoup them, including an appropriate
notice of action and appeal rights, when the department
determines either of the following applies:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The adoptive parents are no longer legally responsible
for the support of the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child is no longer receiving support from the
adoptive family.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The adoptive family has committed fraud in its applica-
tion for, or reassessment of, the adoption assistance.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Children
on whose behalf an adoption assistance
agreement had been executed prior to October 1, 1992, shall
continue to receive adoption assistance in accordance with
the terms of that agreement.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Payment shall begin on or after the effective date of an
adoption assistance agreement, or a deferred adoption assis-
tance agreement, or a final decree of adoption, provided that
the adoption assistance agreement has been signed by all
required parties prior to or at the time the adoption decree is
issued by the court.
The amount and duration of assistance
shall not be changed without the concurrence of the adoptive
parents, unless any of the following has occurred:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child has attained 18 years of age, or 21 years of
age where the child has a mental or physical disability that
warrants the continuation of assistance.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The adoptive parents are no longer legally responsible
for the support of the child.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The child is no longer receiving any support from
adoptive parents.
