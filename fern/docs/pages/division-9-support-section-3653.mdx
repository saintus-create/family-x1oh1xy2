# § 3653 — Activation of military service and request to modify a support

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A servicemember who does not file a notice of
activation of military service and request to modify a support
order or order to show cause or notice of motion prior to
deployment out of state nonetheless shall not be subject to
penalties otherwise authorized by Chapter 5 (commencing
with Section 4720) of on the amount of child support
that would not have accrued if the order had been modified
pursuant to paragraph (2), absent a finding by the court of
good cause.
Any such finding shall be stated on the record.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, interest
shall not accrue on that amount of a child support obligation
that would not have become due and owing if the activated
servicemember modified the support order upon
activation
to reflect the change in income due to the
activation.
Upon a finding by the court that good cause did
not exist for the servicemember’s failure to seek, or
delay in seeking, the modification, interest shall accrue as
otherwise allowed by law.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  An order for spousal support may not be modified or
terminated to the extent that a written agreement or, if
there is no written agreement, an oral agreement entered into
in open court between the parties, specifically provides that
the spousal support is not subject to modification or termi-
nation.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  This section applies whether or not the support order is
based upon an agreement between the parties.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  This section is effective only with respect to a property
settlement agreement entered into on or after January 1,
1970, and does not affect an agreement entered into before
January 1, 1970, as to which Chapter 1308 of the Statutes of
1967 shall apply.
(g)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The Judicial Council, no later than 90 days after the
effective date of the act adding this section, shall develop
forms and procedures necessary to implement para-
graph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision (c).
The Judicial Council shall
ensure that all forms adopted pursuant to this section are in
plain language.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The form developed by the Judicial Council, in addi-
tion to other items the Judicial Council determines to be
necessary or appropriate, shall include the following:

DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The date of deployment and all information relevant
to the determination of the amount of child support, includ-
ing whether the servicemember’s employer will supple-
ment the servicemember’s income during the deploy-
ment.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A notice informing the opposing party that, absent a
finding of good cause, the order will be made retroactive to
the date of service of the form or the date of deployment,
whichever is later.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Notice that the requesting party must notify the court
and the other party upon return from military duty and seek
to bring any unresolved request for modification to hearing
within 90 days of return, or else lose the right to modify the
order pursuant to this section.
