<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If an order decreasing or terminating a support order
is entered retroactively pursuant to this section, the support
obligor may be entitled to, and the support obligee may be
ordered to repay, according to the terms specified in the
order, any amounts previously paid by the support obligor
pursuant to the prior order that are in excess of the amounts
due pursuant to the retroactive order. The court may order
that the repayment by the support obligee shall be made over
any period of time and in any manner, including, but not
limited to, by an offset against future support payments or
wage assignment, as the court deems just and reasonable. In
determining whether to order a repayment, and in establishing the terms of repayment, the court shall consider all of the
following factors:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The amount to be repaid.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The duration of the support order prior to modification or termination.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The financial impact on the support obligee of any
particular method of repayment such as an offset against
future support payments or wage assignment.
