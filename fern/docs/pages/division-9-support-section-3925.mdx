(d)  To the extent that this part, by virtue of subdivision (c),
does not apply to transfers made in a manner prescribed in
the California Uniform Gifts to Minors Act or to the powers,
duties, and immunities conferred by transfers in that manner
upon custodians and persons dealing with custodians, the
repeal of the California Uniform Gifts to Minors Act does
not affect those transfers or those powers, duties, and
immunities.
(Stats.1990,
c. 79 (A.B.759),
14, operative July I, 1991.)
