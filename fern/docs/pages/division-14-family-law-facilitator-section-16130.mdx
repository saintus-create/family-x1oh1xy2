# § 16130 — Of

of
Establishment of services
In any county which does not have a county adoption
agency established pursuant to Section 16100, the department
may establish services incident to the relinquishment of
children for adoption. The services shall be provided in such
manner as may be deemed advisable by the department.
1968, c. 879, p. 1669, § 6.)
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The host county mental health plan shall be responsible for submitting the treatment authorization request (TAR)
to the mental health plan in the county of origin.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The requesting public or private service provider shall
prepare the TAR.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The county of origin shall retain responsibility for
authorization and reauthorization of services utilizing an
expedited TAR process.
,
County Adoption Agencies.
