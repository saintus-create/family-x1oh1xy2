# Support

## Section 3704

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Notice of hearing 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notice of the nature of the proceedings and the time and place of the hearing shall be given by the petitioner at least 15 days before the hearing date by all of the following means: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  By delivery pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1215.">§ 1215</a>, together with a copy of the petition, to all persons comprising the family of the absentee. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  By delivery by a method that would be sufficient for service of summons in a civil action, together with a copy of the petition, to the secretary concerned or to the head of the United States department or agency concerned. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  By publication pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6061.">§ 6061</a> of the Government Code in a newspaper of general circulation in the county in which the proceedings will be held. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If notice to an officer or agency of this state or of the United States is required under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1461.">§ 1461</a> or 1822 upon petition for appointment of a conservator, notice shall be given of the petition under this chapter.
