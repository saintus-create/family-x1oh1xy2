# 8513 — “County adoption agency” means an adoption

Parent and Child Relationship

“County adoption agency” means an adoption
agency
operated by a county or consortium of counties. (Added by
Stats.2012, c. 35 (S.B.1013), § 4, eff. June 27, 2012.)
Amended by Stats.
2012, c. 35 (S.B.1013), § 2, eff: June 27, 2012.)

~

DEFINITIONS
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Assumes care, custody, and control of a child through
relinquishment of the child to the agency or involuntary
termination of parental rights to the child.
Treatises and Practice Aids
10 ,
Types of Agencies.
