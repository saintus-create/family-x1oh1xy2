# 6925

Prevention or treatment of pregnancy

(a) A minor may consent to medical care related to the prevention or treatment of pregnancy.

(b) This section does not authorize a minor to be sterilized without the consent of the minor’s parent or guardian. _(Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 136, Sec. 12. (AB 260) Effective September 26, 2025.)_
