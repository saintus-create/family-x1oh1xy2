# § 6925 — Prevention or treatment of pregnancy

Prevention or treatment of pregnancy
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A minor may consent to medical care related to the
prevention or treatment of pregnancy.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section does not authorize a minor:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  To be sterilized without the consent of the minor’s
parent or guardian.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  To receive an abortion without the consent of a parent
or guardian other than as provided in Section 123450 of the
Health and Safety Code.
