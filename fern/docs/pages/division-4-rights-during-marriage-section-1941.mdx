# § 1941 — Tenant’s dwelling unit upon written request of the protected

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A landlord shall change the locks of a protected
tenant’s dwelling unit upon written request of the protected
tenant not later than 24 hours after the protected tenant gives
the landlord a copy of a court order or police report, and
shall give the protected tenant a key to the new locks.
(c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If a landlord fails to change the locks within 24
hours, the protected tenant may change the locks without the
landlord’s permission, notwithstanding any provision in the
lease to the contrary.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the protected tenant changes the locks pursuant to
this subdivision, the protected tenant shall do all of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Change the locks in a workmanlike manner with locks
of similar or better quality than the original lock.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Notify the landlord within 24 hours that the locks have
been changed.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Provide the landlord with a key by any reasonable
method agreed upon by the landlord and protected tenant.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  This subdivision shall apply to leases executed on or
after the date the act that added this section takes effect.
