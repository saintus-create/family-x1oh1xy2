# 17454 — (e) Unless otherwise required by applicable law, a finan-

Support Services

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Unless otherwise required by applicable law, a finan-
cial institution furnishing a report or providing information to
the department pursuant to this section shall not disclose to a
depositor, accountholder, codepositor, or coaccountholder,
that the name, address, social security number, or other
taxpayer identification number or other identifying informa-
tion of that person has been received from, or furnished to,
the department.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A financial institution shall incur no obligation or
liability to any person arising from any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Furnishing information to the department as required
by this section.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Failing to disclose
to a depositor, accountholder,
codepositor, or coaccountholder,
that the name, address,
social security number, or other taxpayer identification num-
ber or other identifying information of that person was
included in the data exchange with the department required
by this section.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Withholding or transmitting any assets in response to a
notice or order to withhold issued by the department as a
result of the data exchange.
