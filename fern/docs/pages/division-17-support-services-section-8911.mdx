# § 8911 — Conditions of placement; filing of petition

As a condition of placement, the prospective adoptive parents shall file a petition to adopt the child under Section 8912 within 30 days of placement. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
