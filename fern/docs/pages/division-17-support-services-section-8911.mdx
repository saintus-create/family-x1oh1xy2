---
title: "8911. Conditions of placement; filing of petition"
description: "Conditions of placement; filing of petition"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-8911
---

As a condition of placement, the prospective adoptive parents shall file a petition to adopt the child under Section 8912 within 30 days of placement.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
