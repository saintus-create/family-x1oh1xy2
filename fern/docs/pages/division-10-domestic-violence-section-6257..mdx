# § 6257.

*Part 3 — Emergency Protective Orders · Chapter 2 — Issuance And Effect Of Emergency Protective Order*

If an emergency protective order concerns an endangered child, the child’s parent or guardian who is not a restrained person, or a person having temporary custody of the endangered child, may apply to the court for a restraining order under Section 213.5 of the Welfare and Institutions Code. (Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
