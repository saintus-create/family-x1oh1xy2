# Division of Property

## Section 2605

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Care and ownership of pet animal 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court, at the request of a party to proceedings for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties, 178 OF may enter an order, prior to the final determination of ownership of a pet animal, to require a party to care for the pet animal. 
The existence of an order providing for the care of a pet animal during the course of proceedings for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties shall not have any impact on the court’s final determination of ownership of the pet animal. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, including, but not limited to, <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2550.">§ 2550</a>, the court, at the request of a party to proceedings for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties, may assign sole or joint ownership of a pet animal taking into consideration the care of the pet animal. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this section, the following definitions shall apply: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Care” includes, but is not limited to, the prevention of acts of harm or cruelty, as described in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=597.">§ 597</a> of the Penal Code, and the provision of food, water, veterinary care, and safe and protected shelter. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Pet animal” means any animal that is community property and kept as a household pet. (Added by Stats.2018, c. 820 (A.B.2274), § 1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019.)
