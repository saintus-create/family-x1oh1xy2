# 2605. “

*Division of Property*

“
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Order the disposition of * * * retirement benefits
payable upon or after the death of either party in a manner
consistent with Section 2550.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Order a party to elect a survivor benefit annuity or
other similar election for the benefit of the other party, as
specified by the court, * * * when a retirement plan provides
for * * * that election, provided that no court shall order a
retirement plan to provide increased benefits determined on
the basis of actuarial value.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Upon the agreement of the nonemployee spouse,
order the division of accumulated community property contributions and service credit as provided in the following or
similar enactments:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Article 2 (commencing with Section 21290) of Chapter
9 of Part 3 of of Title 2 of the Government Code.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 22650) of Part
13 of of Title 1 of the Education Code.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Article 8.4 (commencing with Section 31685) of Chap-
Section 2604 continues former Civil Code Section 4800(b)<span class="subsec">(3)</span> 
without substantive change. A reference to the “community estate”
has been added to conform with language in the remainder of this
division.
See, e.g., Section 2550 (equal division of community
estate). See also Section 63 (“community estate” defined). [23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
