# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7710

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Issuance of orders During the pendency of a proceeding under this part, on application of a party in the manner provided by Part 4 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=240.">Section 240</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a>, the court may issue ex parte a protective order as defined in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6218.">Section 6218</a> and any other order as provided in Article 1 (commencing with Section.6320) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.">Division 10</a>.
