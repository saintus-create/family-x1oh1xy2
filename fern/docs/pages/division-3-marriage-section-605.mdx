# Marriage

## Section 605

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Statute of limitations; suspension Whenever a petition is filed in a juvenile court alleging that a minor is a person within the description of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=602.">§ 602</a>, and while the case is before the juvenile court, the statute of limitations applicable under the general law to the offense alleged to bring the minor within such description is suspended. (Added by Stats.1961, c. 1616, p. 3473,
