# Support

## Section 4411

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

The court shall make the order requested pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4410.">§ 4410</a> only if the petition alleges and the court finds ail of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The child was abandoned by the parent when the child was a minor. 
Grounds for order 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The abandonment continued for a period of two or more years before the time the child attained the age of 18 years. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  During the period of abandonment the parent was physically and mentally able to provide support for the child.
