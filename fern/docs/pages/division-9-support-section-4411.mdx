# § 4411

*The court shall make the order requested pursuant to*

The court shall make the order requested pursuant to
Section 4410 only if the petition alleges and the court finds ail
of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The child was abandoned by the parent when the child
was a minor.
Grounds for order
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The abandonment continued for a period of two or
more years before the time the child attained the age of 18
years.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  During the period of abandonment the parent was
physically and mentally able to provide support for the child.
