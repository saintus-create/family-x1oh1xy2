# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1834

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Blank forms; assistance in preparing and presenting petition; references; coextensive jurisdiction 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The clerk of the court shall provide, at the expense of the court, blank forms for petitions for filing pursuant to this part. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The probation officers of the county and the attachés and employees of the family conciliation court shall assist a person in the preparation and presentation of a petition under this part if the person requests assistance. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  All public officers in each county shall refer to the family conciliation court all petitions and complaints made to them in respect to controversies within the jurisdiction of the family conciliation court. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The jurisdiction of the family conciliation court in respect to controversies arising out of an instance of domestic violence is not exclusive but is coextensive with any other remedies either civil or criminal in nature that may be available.
