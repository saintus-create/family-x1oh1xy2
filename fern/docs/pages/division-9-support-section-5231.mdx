# 5231 — Unless stayed pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with

Support

Unless stayed pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with
Section 5260), an assignment order is effective and binding
upon any existing or future employer of the obligor upon
whom a copy of the order is served in compliance with
Sections 5232 and 5233, notwithstanding the absence of the
name, address, or other identifying information regarding the
obligor’s employer, or the inclusion of incorrect information
regarding the support obligor’s employer.
