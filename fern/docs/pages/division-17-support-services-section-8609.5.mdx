# 8609.5

Location of filing an adoption request for a

Location of filing an adoption request for a
nondependent minor
An adoption request for the adoption of a nondependent
minor may be filed with the court in the county in which one
of the following applies:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The petitioner resides.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The child was born or resides at the time of filing.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An office of the agency that placed the child for
adoption is located.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  An office of the department or a public adoption
agency that is investigating the petition is located.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The county in which a placing birth parent or parents
resided when the adoptive placement agreement, consent, or
relinquishment was signed.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  The county in which a placing birth parent or parents
resided when the petition was filed.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  The county in which the child was freed for adoption.
