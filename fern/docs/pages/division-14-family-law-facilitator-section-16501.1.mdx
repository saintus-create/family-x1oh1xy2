# § 16501.1 — A child is removed from a parent’s or

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Whenever
a child is removed from a parent’s or
guardian’s custody, the court shall make a finding as to
whether the social worker has exercised due diligence in
conducting the investigation, as required pursuant to para-
graph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of Section 309, to identify, locate,
and notify the child’s relatives, including both maternal and
paternal relatives.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  When making the determination required pursuant to
paragraph (2), the court may consider, among other examples
of due diligence, the extent to which the social worker has
complied with paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of Section 309,
and has done any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Asked the child, in an age-appropriate manner and
consistent with the child’s best interest, about their
relatives.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Obtained information regarding the location of the
child’s relatives.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Reviewed the child’s case file for any information
regarding the child’s relatives.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  Telephoned, emailed, or visited all identified relatives.
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  Asked located relatives for the names and locations of
other relatives.
<span class="subsec">(F)</span>  Used internet search tools to locate relatives identified
as supports.
