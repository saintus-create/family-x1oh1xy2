# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16501.1

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Whenever a child is removed from a parent’s or guardian’s custody, the court shall make a finding as to whether the social worker has exercised due diligence in conducting the investigation, as required pursuant to paragraph (1) of subdivision (e) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=309.">§ 309</a>, to identify, locate, and notify the child’s relatives, including both maternal and paternal relatives. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  When making the determination required pursuant to paragraph (2), the court may consider, among other examples of due diligence, the extent to which the social worker has complied with paragraph (1) of subdivision (e) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=309.">§ 309</a>, and has done any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Asked the child, in an age-appropriate manner and consistent with the child’s best interest, about their relatives. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Obtained information regarding the location of the child’s relatives. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Reviewed the child’s case file for any information regarding the child’s relatives. 
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  Telephoned, emailed, or visited all identified relatives. 
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  Asked located relatives for the names and locations of other relatives.
