# 5666. Provisions

*Support*

Provisions
applicable
to revocable
transfer
on
death deed of community property
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 5010) of
applies to a revocable transfer on death deed of community
_ property.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For the purposeof application of Chapter 2 (com-
mencing with Section 5010) of to a revocable transfer
on death deed of community property, written consent to the
deed, revocation of written consent to the deed; or modifica-
tion of the deed, is ineffective unless recorded within the time
required by that chapter for execution or service of the
written consent, revocation,
or modification.
