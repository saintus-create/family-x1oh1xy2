# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2252

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Liability of quasi-marital property for debts of parties The property divided pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2251.">§ 2251</a> is liable for debts of the parties to the same extent as if the property had been community property or quasi-community property.
