# 2252 — parties

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

parties
property
for debts
of
The property divided pursuant to Section 2251 is liable for
debts of the parties to the same extent as if the property had
been community property or quasi-community property.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
