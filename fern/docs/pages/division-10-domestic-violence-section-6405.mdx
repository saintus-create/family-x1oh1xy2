# § 6405

*Immunity from*

Immunity from
civil liability;
multiple orders;
precedence in enforcement; unreasonable use of force
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  There shall be no civil liability on the part of, and no
cause of action for false arrest or false imprisonment against,
a peace officer who makes an arrest pursuant to a foreign
protection order that is regular upon its face, if the peace
officer, in making the arrest, acts in good faith and has
reasonable cause to believe that the person against whom the
order is issued has notice of the order and has committed an
act in violation of the order.
