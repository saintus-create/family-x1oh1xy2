# Marriage

## Section 469

<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  Inflation adjustments.— 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general.—tIn the case of any taxable year beginning after 2015, each of the dollar amounts in subsections (b)(2) and (i)(1) shall be increased by an amount equal to— 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  such dollar amount, multiplied by 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for the calendar year in which the taxable year begins, determined by substituting in subparagraph (A)(ii) thereof— 
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  in the case of amounts in subsections 
(b)(2)(A) and (i)(1), “calendar year 1995” for 
“calendar year 2016”, and 
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  in the case of the $5,000 amount in subsection (b)(2)(B), “calendar year 2008” for 
“calendar year 2016”. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Rounding.— 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  In general.—If any dollar amount in subsection (b)(2)(A) (after being increased under subparagraph (B) thereof), after being increased under paragraph (1), is not a multiple of $10, such dollar amount shall be rounded to the nearest multiple of 
$10. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Disqualified income threshold amount.—If the dollar amount in subsection (i)(1), after being increased under paragraph (1), is not a multiple of 
$50, such amount shall be rounded to the next lowest multiple of $50. 
<span class="subsec">(k)</span>  Restrictions on taxpayers who improperly claimed credit in prior year 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Taxpayers making prior fraudulent or reckless claims 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  In general.—No credit shall be allowed under this section for any taxable year in the disallowance period. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Disallowance period.—For purposes of paragraph (1), the disallowance period is— 
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  the period of 10 taxable years after the most recent taxable year for which there was a final determination that the taxpayer’s claim of credit under this section was due to fraud, and 
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  the period of 2 taxable years after the most recent taxable year for which there was a final determination that the taxpayer’s claim of credit under this section was due to reckless or intentional disregard of rules and regulations 
(but not due to fraud). 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Taxpayers making improper prior claims.—In the case of a taxpayer who is denied credit under this section for any taxable year as a result of the deficiency procedures under subchapter B of chapter 63, no credit shall be allowed under this section for any subsequent taxable year unless the taxpayer provides such information as the Secretary may require to demonstrate eligibility for such credit. 
(L) Coordination with certain means-tested programs.— 
For purposes of— 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the United States Housing Act of 1937, 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  title V of the Housing Act of 1949, 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  section 101 of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1965, 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  sections 221(d)(3), 235, and 236 of the National Housing Act, and 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008, any refund made to an individual (or the spouse of an individual) by reason of this section shall not be treated as income (and shall not be taken into account in determining resources for the month of its receipt and the following month). 
<span class="subsec">(m)</span>  Identification numbers.—Solely for purposes of subsections (c)(1)(E) and (c)(3)(D), a taxpayer identification number means a social security number issued to an individual by the Social Security Administration (other than a social security number issued pursuant to clause (II) (or that portion of clause () that relates to clause (II)) of section 205(c)(2)(B)(i) of the Social Security Act) on or before the due date for filing the return for the taxable year.
