# § 9007.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 5 — Stepparent Adoptions*

Except as provided in Section 9000.5, the prospective adoptive parent and the child proposed to be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections 8612, 8613, and 8613.5. (Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 636, Sec. 7. (AB 2344) Effective January 1, 2015.) FAM Family Code - FAM
