# § 20018 — Temporary child support orders; compliance with

Orders for temporary support issued pursuant to this chapter shall comply with the statewide uniform guideline set forth in Article 2 (commencing with Section 4050) of Chapter 2 of Part 2 of Division 9 and shall be based on the economic evidence supplied by the parties or otherwise available to the court. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
