# § 2626.

*Part 6 — Debts And Liabilities*

The court has jurisdiction to order reimbursement in cases it deems appropriate for debts paid after separation but before trial. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
