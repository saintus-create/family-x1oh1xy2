<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a
person is not competent to act as personal representative in
any of the following circumstances:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The person is under the age of majority.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The person is subject to a conservatorship of the estate
or is otherwise incapable of executing, or is otherwise unfit to
execute, the duties of the office.
