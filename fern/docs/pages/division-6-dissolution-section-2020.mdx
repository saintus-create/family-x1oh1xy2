# 2020

Responsive pleadings

A responsive pleading, if any, shall be filed and a copy served on the petitioner within 30 days of the date of the service on the respondent of a copy of the petition and summons. Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 581, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.
