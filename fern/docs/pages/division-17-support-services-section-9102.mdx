# § 9102

*Limitation of actions;*

Limitation of actions;
findings of fact; duty of
department
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in Section 9100, an action or
proceeding of any kind to vacate, set aside, or otherwise
nullify an order of adoption on any ground, except fraud,
shall be commenced within one year after entry of the order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in Section 9100, an action or
proceeding of any kind to vacate, set aside, or nullify an order
of adoption, based on fraud, shall be commenced within three
years after entry of the order, or within 90 days of discovery
of the fraud, whichever is earlier.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In any action to set aside an order of adoption
pursuant to this section or Section 9100, the court shall first
determine whether the facts presented are legally sufficient to
set aside the order of adoption:
If the facts are not legally
sufficient, the petition shall be denied.
If the facts are legally
sufficient, the court’s final ruling on the matter shall take into
consideration the best interests of the child, in conjunction
with all other factors required by law.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The department shall not be required under any
circumstances to investigate a petition filed pursuant to this
section or to represent a child who is the subject of a
proceeding under this section.
