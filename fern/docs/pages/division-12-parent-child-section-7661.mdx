# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7661

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Relinquishment or consent by other parent; notice to and rights of mother If the other parent relinquishes for or consents to, or proposes to relinquish for or consent to, the adoption of a child, the mother shall be given notice of the adoption proceeding and have the rights provided under Part 2 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8600.">Section 8600</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=13.">Division 13</a>, unless the mother’s relationship to the child has been previously terminated by a court or the mother has voluntarily relinquished for or consented to the adoption of the child. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. 
Amended by Stats.2013, c. 510 (A.B.1403),
