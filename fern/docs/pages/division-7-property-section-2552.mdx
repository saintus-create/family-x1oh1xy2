# § 2552

*1 California Transactions Forms--Family Law § 2:12, Overview.*

1 California Transactions Forms--Family Law § 2:12, Overview.
1 California Transactions Forms--Family Law § 2:13, Equalizing
Valuation of assets and liabilities
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For the purpose of division of the community estate
upon dissolution of marriage or legal separation of the
parties, except as provided in subdivision (b), the court shall
value the assets and liabilities as near as practicable to the
time of trial.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon 30 days’ notice by the moving party to the other
party, the court for good cause shown may value all or any
portion of the assets and liabilities at a date after separation
and before trial to accomplish an equal division of the
community estate of the parties in an equitable manner.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
