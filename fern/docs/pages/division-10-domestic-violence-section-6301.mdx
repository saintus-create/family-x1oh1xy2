# 6301. Persons who may seek protective order

*Prevention of Domestic Violence*

<div class="case-callout">
<strong>McCord v. Smith (2020)</strong>

*51 Cal.App.5th*

In this case, McCord repeatedly showed up at Smith’s home and work uninvited, texted and called nonstop, and made threats in order to force Smith to speak with him. The court found that McCord’s statements and actions were a means of exercising control and dominion over Smith and that those actions were sufficient to disturb her peace, as well as stalking, threatening and harassing and so were abuse.
