# § 7573 — Of paternity; force and effect

of paternity; force and effect
Section operative until Jan. 1, 2020. See,
also, § 7573 operative Jan. 1, 2020.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in Sections 7575, 7576, 7577, and
7612, a completed voluntary declaration of paternity, as
described
in Section
7574,
that has been
filed with
the
Department of Child Support Services shall establish the
paternity of a child and shall have the same force and effect
as a judgment for paternity issued by a court of competent
jurisdiction. The voluntary declaration of paternity shall be
recognized as a basis for the establishment of an order for
child custody, visitation, or child support.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall remain in effect only until January 1,
2020, and as of that date is repealed.
