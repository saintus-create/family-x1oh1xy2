# Adoption

## Section 9202.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Blood sample; access 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, the laboratory that is storing a blood sample pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8706.">§ 8706</a>, 8817, or 8909 shall provide access to the blood sample to only the following persons upon the person’s request: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A person who has been adopted pursuant to this part. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The adoptive parent of a person under the age of 18 years who has been adopted pursuant to this part. 
The adoptive parent may receive access to the blood sample only after entry of the order of adoption. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The birth parent or parents shall be given access to any test results related to the blood sample on request. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (b), no person other than the adoptive parent and the adopted child shall have access to the blood sample or any test results related to the blood sample, unless the adoptive parent or the child authorizes another person or entity to have that access.
