# § 245 — Continuance

Continuance
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The respondent shall be entitled, as a matter of course,
to one continuance for a reasonable period, to respond to the
petition.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Either party may request'a continuance of the hearing,
which the court shall grant on a showing of good cause.
The
request may be made in writing before or at the hearing or
orally at the hearing.
The court may also grant a continu-
ance on its own motion.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the court grants a continuance,
any temporary
restraining order that has been issued shall remain in effect
until the end of the continued hearing, unless otherwise
ordered by the court.
In granting a continuance, the court
may modify or terminate
a temporary restraining order.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If the court grants a continuance, the extended tempo-
rary restraining order shall state on its face the new date of
expiration of the order.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A fee shall not be charged for the extension of the
temporary restraining order.
