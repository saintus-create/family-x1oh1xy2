# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 865.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Prohibited actions Under no circumstances shall a mental health provider engage in sexual orientation change efforts with a patient under 18 years of age.
