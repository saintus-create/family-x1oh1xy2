---
title: "3554. Appeals"
description: "Appeals"
slug: division-9-support-section-3554
---

An appeal may be taken from an order or judgment under this division as in other civil actions.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
