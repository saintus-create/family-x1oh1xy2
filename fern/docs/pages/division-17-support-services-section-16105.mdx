# 16105 — of administration or care

Support Services

of administration or care
If any grants-in-aid are made by the federal government for
the cost of administering an adoption program, or for the cost
of care of children relinquished for adoption, the amount of
the federal grant shall be applied to defer the cost of
administration or of care.

SERVICES
16125.
