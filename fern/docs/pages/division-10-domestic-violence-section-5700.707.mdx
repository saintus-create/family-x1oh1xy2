# 5700.707
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, Sections 5700.605 through 5700.608 apply to a contest of a registered Convention support order.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A party contesting a registered Convention support order shall file a contest not later than 30 days after notice of the registration, but if the contesting party does not reside in the United States, the contest must be filed not later than 60 days after notice of the registration.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the nonregistering party fails to contest the registered Convention support order by the time specified in subsection (b), the order is enforceable.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A contest of a registered Convention support order may be based only on grounds set forth in Section 5700.708. The contesting party bears the burden of proof.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  In a contest of a registered Convention support order, a tribunal of this state:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  is bound by the findings of fact on which the foreign tribunal based its jurisdiction; and

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  may not review the merits of the order.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A tribunal of this state deciding a contest of a registered Convention support order shall promptly notify the parties of its decision.

<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  A challenge or appeal, if any, does not stay the enforcement of a Convention support order unless there are exceptional circumstances.
