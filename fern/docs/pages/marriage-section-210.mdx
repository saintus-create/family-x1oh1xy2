# § 210 — Rules for practice and procedure

Except to the extent that any other statute or rules adopted by the Judicial Council provide applicable rules, the rules of practice and procedure applicable to civil actions generally, including the provisions of Title 3a (commencing with Section 391) of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, apply to, and constitute the rules of practice and procedure in, proceedings under this code. _(Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1118, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)_
