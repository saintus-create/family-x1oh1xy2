---
title: "7821. a finding pursuant to this chapter shall be supported by"
description: "A finding pursuant to this chapter shall be supported by"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-7821
---

A finding pursuant to this chapter shall be supported by clear and convincing evidence, except as otherwise provided.

> *Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 838, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.*
