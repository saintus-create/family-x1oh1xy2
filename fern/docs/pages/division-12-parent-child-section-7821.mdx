# § 7821

*Findings; sufficiency of evidence*

Findings; sufficiency of evidence
A finding pursuant to this chapter shall be supported by
clear and convincing evidence, except as otherwise provided.
(Stats.1992, c..162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.2006, c. 838 (S.B.678), § 4.)
