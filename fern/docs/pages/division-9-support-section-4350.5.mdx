# Support

## Section 4350.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

The terms “spouses” and 
“married persons,” and the reference to a “comparable term,” have been added. 
The former provision applied only to the former Family Law Act (former Part 5 (commencing with former <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4000.">§ 4000</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of the Civil Code), whereas <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11.">§ 11</a> applies to the entire Family Code. The rule stated in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11.">§ 11</a> applies unless the provision or context otherwise requires.
