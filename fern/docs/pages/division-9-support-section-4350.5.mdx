# § 4350.5 — The terms “spouses” and

The terms “spouses” and
“married persons,” and the reference to a “comparable term,” have
been added.
The former provision applied only to the former
Family Law Act (former (commencing with former Section
4000) of of the Civil Code), whereas Section 11 applies to
the entire Family Code. The rule stated in Section 11 applies unless
the provision or context otherwise requires.
