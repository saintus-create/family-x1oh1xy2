# 1516.5

Proceeding

Proceeding
to have child declared
free from
custody and control of one or both parents
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding to have a child declared free from the
custody and control of one or both parents may be brought in
accordance with the procedures specified in (com-
mencing with Section 7800) of of the Family
Code within an existing guardianship proceeding, in an
adoption action, or in a separate action filed for that purpose,
if all of the following requirements are satisfied:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  One or both parents do not have the legal custody of
the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child has been in the physical custody of the
guardian for a period of not less than two years.
