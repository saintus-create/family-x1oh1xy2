# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1516.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Proceeding to have child declared free from custody and control of one or both parents 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding to have a child declared free from the custody and control of one or both parents may be brought in accordance with the procedures specified in Part 4 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7800.">§ 7800</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=12.">Division 12</a> of the Family Code within an existing guardianship proceeding, in an adoption action, or in a separate action filed for that purpose, if all of the following requirements are satisfied: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  One or both parents do not have the legal custody of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child has been in the physical custody of the guardian for a period of not less than two years.
