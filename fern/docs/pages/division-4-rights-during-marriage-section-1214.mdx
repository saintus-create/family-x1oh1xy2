Warrant of attachment; execution
Upon executing the warrant of attachment, the officer
executing the warrant must keep the person in custody, bring
him before the court or judge, and detain him until an order
be made in the premises, unless the person arrested entitle
himself to be discharged, as provided in the next section.
(Enacted in 1872.
Amended by Stats.1933, c. 745, p. 1906,
14; Stats.1951,
c. 1737, p. 4142, 165, operative Jan. 1,
1952 )
