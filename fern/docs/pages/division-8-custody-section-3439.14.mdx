# Custody of Children

## Section 3439.14

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Application;. prior provisions; construction 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The changes to this chapter made by the act adding this subdivision apply only to a right of action that accrued, transfer made, or obligation incurred, on or after the effective date of that act. 
; 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This chapter, and the other changes in the law made by Chapter 383 of the Statutes of 1986, apply only to transfers made or obligations incurred before the effective date of the act that added subdivision (a) and on or after January 1, 1987. 
As to transfers made or obligations incurred prior to January 1, 1987, the law in effect at the time the transfer was made or the obligation was incurred shall apply. 
(c).
