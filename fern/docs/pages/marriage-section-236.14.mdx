# 236.14. Court to modify delinquency jurisdiction to transition jurisdic-

*Marriage*

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Setting and conduct of hearing
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The probation officer must request a hearing for the
court to modify delinquency jurisdiction to transition jurisdic-
tion and vacate the underlying adjudication.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The hearing must be held before a judicial officer and
recorded by a court reporter.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The hearing must be continued for no more than five
court days for the submission of additional evidence if the
court finds that the report and, if required, the Transitional
Independent Living Case Plan submitted by the probation
officer do not provide the information required by (d), and
the court is unable to make all the findings required by (e).
