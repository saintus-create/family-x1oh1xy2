# 327 — Right of possession; death of person in possession;

Premarital Agreements

Right of possession; death of person in possession;
effect of descent
The right of a person to the possession of real property is
not impaired or affected by a descent cast in consequence of
the death of a person in possession of such property.
(Enacted in 1872.)
