# § 695.030 — Non-assignable and non-transferable property;

Non-assignable and non-transferable property;
interest in trust; cause of action for money or property
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided by statute, property of
the judgment debtor that is not assignable or transferable is
not subject to enforcement of a money judgment.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The following property is subject to enforcement of a
money judgment:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  An interest in a trust, to the extent provided by law.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A cause of action for money or property that is the
subject of a pending action or special proceeding. (Added by
c. 1364, p. 5103, § 2, operative July 1, 1983.
operative July 1, 1983.)
