# § 7801.

*Part 4 — Freedom From Parental Custody And Control · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

This part shall be liberally construed to serve and protect the interests and welfare of the child. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
