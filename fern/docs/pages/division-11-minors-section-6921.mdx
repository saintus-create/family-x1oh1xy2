---
title: "6921. Effect of minority upon consent"
description: "Effect of minority upon consent"
slug: division-11-minors-section-6921
---

A consent given by a minor under this chapter is not subject to disaffirmance because of minority.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
