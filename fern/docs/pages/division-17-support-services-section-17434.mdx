# Support Services

## Section 17434

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Booklet about support; toll-free information hotline 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department shall publish a booklet describing the proper procedures and processes for the collection and payment of child and spousal support. 
The booklet shall be written in language understandable to the lay person and shall direct the reader to obtain the assistance of the local child support agency, the family law facilitator, or legal counsel where appropriate. 
The department may contract on a competitive basis with an organization or individual to write the booklet. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The department shall have primary responsibility for the design and development of the contents of the booklet. 
The department shall solicit comment regarding the content of the booklet from the Director of the Administrative Office of the Courts. 
The department shall verify the appropriateness and accuracy of the contents of the booklet with at least one representative of each of the following organizations: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A local child support agency. 
(2). The State Attorney General’s office. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A community organization that advocates for the rights of custodial parents. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A community organization that advocates for the rights of supporting parents. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Upon receipt of booklets on support collection, each county welfare department shall provide a copy to each head of household whose application for public assistance under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=9.">Division 9</a> (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=10000.">§ 10000</a>) of the Welfare and Institutions Code has been approved and for whom support rights have been assigned pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11477.">§ 11477</a> of the Welfare and Institutions Code. 
The department shall provide copies of the booklet to local child support agencies for distribution, and to any person upon request. 
The department shall also distribute the booklets to all superior 697 
§ 17440 courts. 
Upon receipt of those booklets, each clerk of the court shall provide two copies of the booklet to the petitioner or plaintiff in any action involving the support of a minor child. The moving party shall serve a copy of the booklet on the responding party. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The department shall expand the information provided under its toll-free information hotline in response to inquiries regarding the process and procedures for collection and payment of child and spousal support. 
This toll-free number shall be advertised as providing information on child and spousal support. 
The hotline personnel shall not provide legal consultation or advice, but shall provide only referral services. 
(ec) The department shall maintain a file of referral sources to provide callers to the telephone hotline with the following information specific to the county in which the caller resides: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The location and telephone number of the local child support agency, the county welfare office, the family law facilitator, and any other government agency that handles child and spousal support matters. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The telephone number of the local bar association for referral to attorneys in family law practice. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The name and telephone number of at least one organization that advocates the payment of child and spousal support or the name and telephone number of at least one organization that advocates the rights of supporting parents, if these organizations exist in the county.
