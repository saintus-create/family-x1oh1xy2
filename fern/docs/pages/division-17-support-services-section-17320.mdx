# § 17320 — Temporary

Temporary
federal penalties
Staff; regional state administrators
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to the State Civil Service Act ( (commencing with Section 18500) of of Title 2 of the
Government Code), the director shall appoint any assistants
and other employees that are necessary for the administration
of the affairs of the department and shall prescribe their
duties and, subject to the approval of the Department of
Assistance to Needy Families grant;
