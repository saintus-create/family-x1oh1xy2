# California Family Code § 7282

1,
14124.71-14124.76,
17109,
17403.
.
1993. Amendment
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 3611 is amended to require a hearing
before the court may order money to be paid to a special needs trust
under this section, and to require notice to affected state agencies.
This amendment conforms Section 3611(c) to Section 3602(f).
[23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 901 (1993) (Annual Report, App. 4)].
