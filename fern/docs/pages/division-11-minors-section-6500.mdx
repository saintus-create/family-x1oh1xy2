“Minor” defined
A minor is an individual who is under 18 years of age. The
period of minority is calculated from the first minute of the
day on which the individual is born to the same minute of the
corresponding day completing the period of minority. (Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
