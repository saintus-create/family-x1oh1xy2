# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5696

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Fraud, undue influence, duress, mistake, or other invalidating causes; petition by conservator or guardian of transferor 
(a) Nothing in this chapter limits the application of principles of fraud, undue influence, duress, mistake, or other invalidating cause to a transfer of property by a revocable transfer on death deed. 
(b) Notwithstanding subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5692.">Section 5692</a>, the conservator or guardian of a transferor may, before the transferor’s death, petition the court for invalidation of a revocable transfer on death deed executed by the transferor.
