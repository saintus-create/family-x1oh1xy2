# § 5150 — Sums on deposit

Sums on deposit
“Sums on deposit” means both of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The balance payable on an account, including interest
and dividends earned, whether or not included in the current
balance.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any life insurance proceeds added to the account by
reason of the death of a party.
