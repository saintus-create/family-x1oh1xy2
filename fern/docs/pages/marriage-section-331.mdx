# General Provisions

## Section 331

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Failure of social worker to file petition; review of decision When any person has applied to the social worker, pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=329.">Section 329</a>, to commence juvenile court proceedings and the social worker fails to file a petition within three weeks after the application, the person may, within one month after making the application, apply to the juvenile court to review the decision of the social worker, and the court may either affirm the decision of the social worker or order him or her to commence juvenile court proceedings.
