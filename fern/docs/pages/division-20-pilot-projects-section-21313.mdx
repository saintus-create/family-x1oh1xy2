# Pilot Projects

## Section 21313

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Legislative intent; codification; common law This part is not intended as a complete codification of the law governing enforcement of a no contest clause. 
The common law governs enforcement of a no contest clause to the extent this part does not apply.
