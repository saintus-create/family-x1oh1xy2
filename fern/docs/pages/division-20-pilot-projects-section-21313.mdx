# 21313. Legislative intent; codification; common law

*Pilot Projects*

Legislative intent; codification; common law
This part is not intended as a complete codification of the
law governing enforcement of a no contest clause.
The
common law governs enforcement of a no contest clause to
the extent this part does not apply.
