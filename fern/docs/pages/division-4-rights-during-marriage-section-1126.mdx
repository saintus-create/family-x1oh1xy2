# § 1126 — Inadmissible, protected from disclosure, and confidential

inadmissible, protected from disclosure, and confidential
under this chapter before a mediation ends, shall remain
inadmissible, protected from disclosure, and confidential to
the same extent after the mediation
c. 772 (A.B.939), $ 3.)
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The parties execute a written settlement agreement
that partially resolves the dispute.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An oral agreement that partially resolves the dispute is
reached in accordance with Section 1118.
only during a mediation, but also after the mediation ends pursuant
to Section 1125 (when mediation ends).
See Section 1115(a) (“mediation” defined). [1997-98 Annual
Report, 27 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports App. 5 (1997)].
