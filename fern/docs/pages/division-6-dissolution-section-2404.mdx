# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2404

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Final judgment as final adjudication of rights and obligations Entry of the judgment pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2403.">§ 2403</a> constitutes: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A final adjudication of the rights and obligations of the parties with respect to the status of the marriage and property rights. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A waiver of their respective rights to spousal support, rights to appeal, and rights to move for a new trial.
