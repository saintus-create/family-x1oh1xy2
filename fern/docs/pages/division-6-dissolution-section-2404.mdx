# § 2404 — Final judgment as final adjudication of rights and

Final judgment as final adjudication of rights and
obligations
Entry of the judgment pursuant to Section 2403 consti-
tutes:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A final adjudication of the rights and obligations of the
parties with respect to the status of the marriage and property
rights.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A waiver of their respective rights to spousal support,
rights to appeal, and rights to move
for a new
trial.
