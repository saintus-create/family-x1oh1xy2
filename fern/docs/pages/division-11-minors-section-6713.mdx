# § 6713 — Recovery from good faith purchaser

If, before the contract of a minor is disaffirmed, goods the minor has sold are transferred to another purchaser who bought them in good faith for value and without notice of the transferor’s defect of title, the minor cannot recover the goods from an innocent purchaser. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10.
