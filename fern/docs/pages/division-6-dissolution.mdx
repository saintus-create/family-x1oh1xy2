## Dissolution

  <h3>Grounds for dissolution</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 2300</Badge></div>
        <p>A marriage may be dissolved on the ground of irreconcilable differences. Incurable insanity is also a ground, but requires a corroborated finding by a medical physician.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Legal separation</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 2310</Badge></div>
        <p>Either party may bring an action for legal separation without alleging the grounds required for dissolution. A judgment of legal separation has the same effect as dissolution, except the parties remain married.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Judgment</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 2400</Badge></div>
        <p>Entry of judgment in a dissolution proceeding. The superior court has jurisdiction to enter judgment and make orders regarding status, property, support, and custody.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
