## Dissolution

        <h4 style={{marginTop: '0.5rem'}}>Grounds for dissolution</h4>

        <h4 style={{marginTop: '0.5rem'}}>Legal separation</h4>
        <p>Either party may bring an action for legal separation without alleging the grounds required for dissolution. A judgment of legal separation has the same effect as dissolution, except the parties remain married.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
