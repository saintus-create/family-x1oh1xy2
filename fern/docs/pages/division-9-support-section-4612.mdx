# Support

## Section 4612

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Grounds for defense of obligor-parent alleged to be in arrears An obligor-parent alleged to be in arrears may use any of the following grounds as a defense to the motion filed pursuant to this article or as a basis for filing a motion to stop a sale or use of assets under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4631.">§ 4631</a>: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Child support payments are not in arrears. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Laches. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  There has been a change in the custody of the children. 

§ 4612 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  There is a pending motion for reduction in support due to a reduction in income. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Illness or disability. 
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Unemployment. 
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Serious adverse impact on the immediate family of the obligor-parent residing with the obligor-parent that outweighs the impact of denial of the motion or stopping the sale on obligee. 
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Serious impairment of the ability of the obligor-parent to generate income. 
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  Other emergency conditions.
