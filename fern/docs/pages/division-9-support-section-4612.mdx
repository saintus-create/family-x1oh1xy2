# 4612 — Grounds for defense of obligor-parent alleged to be

Support

Grounds for defense of obligor-parent alleged to be
in arrears
An obligor-parent alleged to be in arrears may use any of
the following grounds as a defense to the motion filed
pursuant to this article or as a basis for filing a motion to stop
a sale or use of assets under Section 4631:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Child support payments are not in arrears.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Laches.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  There has been a change in the custody of the children.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  There is a pending motion for reduction in support due
to a reduction in income.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Illness or disability.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Unemployment.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Serious adverse impact on the immediate family of the
obligor-parent residing with the obligor-parent that out-
weighs the impact of denial of the motion or stopping the sale
on obligee.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Serious impairment of the ability of the obligor-parent
to generate income.
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  Other emergency conditions.
