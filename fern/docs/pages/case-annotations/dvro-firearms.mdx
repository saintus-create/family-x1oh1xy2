If the court issues a DVRO, it has certain obligations to ensure the restrained party has no prohibited items or, if they have prohibited items, they turn them over to proper authority within a certain period of time. (Fam. Code, §§ 6322.5, 6389.) The cases below explain that this automatic firearms prohibition generally cannot be removed from a DVRO, except that under California law, the court may grant an exemption for the restrained party to have a gun for and during employment, if certain strict requirements are met.
