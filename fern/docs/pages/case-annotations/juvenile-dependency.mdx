## J.H. v. Superior Court (2018)

**Citation:** 20 Cal.App.5

th 530 Father sought extraordinary writ review of the juvenile court’s order terminating his reunification services and setting the matter for a permanency plan hearing. The trial court properly found there was not a substantial probability Father’s daughters would be returned to his custody in the next six months because he minimally complied with his case plan, was hostile to the social workers and court, and denied he needed to address his history of DV and anger issues. The appellate court also held Father’s due process rights were not violated when the trial court did not allow him to cross-examine the social worker who had authored a report, when that worker had left the agency before the hearing and her supervisor, an expert witness at the trial, instead testified on the report, ...
