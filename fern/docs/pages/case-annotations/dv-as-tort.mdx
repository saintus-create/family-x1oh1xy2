## Boblitt v. Boblitt (2010)

**Citation:** *190 Cal.App.4th*

The first trial court presided over the parties’ divorce proceeding and, since it had to consider allegations of DV when awarding spousal support, found Husband had not committed DV as Wife described. While Wife was appealing that order, she filed a (second) tort lawsuit, suing Husband for the DV and intentional infliction of emotional distress he committed against her. The second trial court dismissed the case on Husband’s motion, concluding Wife was barred from “relitigating” the DV allegations after the first court had already addressed them when awarding spousal support.
