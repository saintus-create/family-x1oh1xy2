# Adoption

## Section 9002

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Definitions For the purposes of this chapter— 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the term “applicant” means any person who, pursuant to the Convention, files an application with the United States Central Authority or a Central Authority of any other party to the Convention for the 1572 return of a child alleged to have been wrongfully removed or retained or for arrangements for organizing or securing the effective exercise of rights of access pursuant to the Convention; 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the term “Convention” means the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, done at The Hague on October 25, 1980; 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  the term “Parent Locator Service” means the service established by the Secretary of Health and Human Services under section 653 of Title 42; 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  the term “petitioner” means any person who, in accordance with this chapter, files a petition in court seeking relief under the Convention; 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  the term “person” includes any individual, institution, or other legal entity or body; 
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  the term “respondent” means any person against whose interests a petition is filed in court, in accordance with this chapter, which seeks relief under the Convention; 
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  the term 
“rights of access” 
means visitation rights; 
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  the term “State” means any of the several States, the District of Columbia, and any commonwealth, territory, or possession of the United States; and 
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  the term 
“United States Central Authority” 
means the agency of the Federal Government designated by the President under section 9006(a) of this title. 
(Pub.L. 100-300, § 3, Apr. 29, 1988, 102 Stat. 437.)
