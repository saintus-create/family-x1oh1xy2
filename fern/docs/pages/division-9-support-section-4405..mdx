# § 4405.

*Part 4 — Support Of Parents · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

The court retains jurisdiction to modify or terminate an order for support where justice requires. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
