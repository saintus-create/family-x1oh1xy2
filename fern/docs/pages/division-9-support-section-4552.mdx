# 4552 — forms by Judicial Council

Support

forms by Judicial Council
“Child support obligee” as used in this chapter means
either the parent, guardian, or other person to whom child
support has been ordered to be paid or the local child support
agency designated by the court to receive the payment. The
local child support agency is the “child support obligee” for
the purposes of this chapter for all cases in which an
application for services has been filed under Part D of Title
IV of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 651 et seq.).
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994;
Stats.2001, c. 755 (S.B.943), § 5, eff: Oct. 12, 2001.)
The Judicial Council shall promulgate such rules of court
and publish such related judicial forms as the Judicial Council
determines are necessary and appropriate to implement this
chapter. In taking these steps, the Judicial Council shall
ensure the uniform statewide application of this chapter and
compliance with Part D of Title IV of the Social Security Act
(42 U.S.C. Sec. 651 et seq.) and any regulations promulgated
thereunder.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
