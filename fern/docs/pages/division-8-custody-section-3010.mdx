# Custody of Children

## Section 3010

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Custody of unemancipated minor children. 
(a) The mother of an unemancipated minor child and the father, if presumed to be the father under Section Is are equally entitled to the custody of the child. 
(b) If one parent is dead, is unable or refuses to take custody, or has abandoned the child, the other parent is entitled to custody of the child.
