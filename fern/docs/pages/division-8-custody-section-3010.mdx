# § 3010

*Custody of unemancipated minor children.*

Custody of unemancipated minor children.
3011.
Best interests of child; considerations.
3012.
Use of telepresence in child custody proceedings.
3010.
Custody of unemancipated minor children
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The mother of an unemancipated minor child and the
father, if presumed to be the father under Section Is are
equally entitled to the custody of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If one parent is dead, is unable or refuses to take
custody, or has abandoned the child, the other parent is
entitled to custody of the child.
