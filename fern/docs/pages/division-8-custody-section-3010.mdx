# 3010

Custody of unemancipated minor children

(a) The mother of an unemancipated minor child and the father, if presumed to be the father under Section 7611, are equally entitled to the custody of the child.

(b) If one parent is dead, is unable or refuses to take custody, or has abandoned the child, the other parent is entitled to custody of the child. Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 115.5. Effective January 1, 1994.
