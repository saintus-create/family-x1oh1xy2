# § 351 — Contents of license

Contents of license
The marriage license shall show all of the following:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The identity of the parties to the marriage.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The parties’ full given names at birth or by court order,
and mailing addresses.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The parties’ dates of birth.
