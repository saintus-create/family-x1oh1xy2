# § 7863 — Compensation and expenses

Compensation and expenses
Private counsel appointed under this article shall receive a
reasonable sum for compensation and expenses, the amount
of which shall be determined by the court.
The amount so
determined shall be paid by the real parties in interest, other
than the child, in proportions the court deems just. Howev-
er, if the court finds that any of the real parties in interest are
unable to afford counsel, the amount shall be paid out of the
general fund of the county.
