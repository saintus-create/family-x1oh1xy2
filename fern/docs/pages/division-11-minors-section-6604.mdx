# § 6604 — Contents of petition

Contents of petition
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The petition shall allege that this chapter applies and
request that an order be made setting aside the estate of the
decedent as provided in this chapter.

Background on Section 6604 of Repealed Code
Section 6604 was added by 1986 Cal.Stat. ch. 783 § 23. Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  continued the first portion of the first sentence of former
Probate Code Section 641 (repealed by 1986 Cal.Stat. ch. 783 § 9)
without substantive change. Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  superseded the last
sentence of former Section 641 which specified the contents of the
petition.
Paragraphs <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  were new.
Paragraphs (3), (4), and <span class="subsec">(5)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  superseded the
provision of former Probate Code Section 641 that required that the
petition include “a specific description and an estimate of the value
of all of the decedent’s property” and “a list of all liens and
encumbrances at the date of death.” Paragraph <span class="subsec">(6)</span>  of subdivision
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  continued a requirement of former Probate Code Section 641
without substantive change. Paragraphs <span class="subsec">(7)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(8)</span>  of subdivision
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  were new. For background on the provisions of this chapter, see
