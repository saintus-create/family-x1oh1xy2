# Minors

## Section 6604

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Contents of petition 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The petition shall allege that this chapter applies and request that an order be made setting aside the estate of the decedent as provided in this chapter. 
1202 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition shall include the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If proceedings for administration of the estate are not pending, the facts necessary to determine the county in which the estate of the decedent may be administered. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The name, age, address, and relation to the decedent of each heir and devisee of the decedent, so far as known to the petitioner. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A specific description and estimate of the value of the decedent’s estate and a list of all liens and encumbrances at the date of death. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A specific description and estimate of the value of any of the decedent’s real property located outside this state that passed to the surviving spouse and minor children of the decedent, or any one or more of them, under the will of the decedent or by intestate succession. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  A specific description and estimate of the value of any of the decedent’s property described in subdivision (b) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6600.">§ 6600</a> that passed to the surviving spouse and minor children of the decedent, or any one or more of them, upon the death of the decedent.
