# § 305

*Proof of consent and solemnization*

Proof of consent and solemnization
Consent to and solemnization of marriage may be proved
under the same general rules of evidence as facts are proved
in other cases. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
