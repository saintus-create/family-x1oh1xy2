# California Family Code § 7611.5

Where Section 7611 does not apply, a man shall not be presumed to be the natural father of a child if either of the following is true:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The child was conceived as a result of an act in violation of Section 261 of the Penal Code and the father was convicted of that violation.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The child was conceived as a result of an act in violation of Section 261.5 of the Penal Code, the father was convicted of that violation, and the mother was under the age of 15 years and the father was 21 years of age or older at the time of conception.
