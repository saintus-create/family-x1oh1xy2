# § 366.25 — Subdivision (b) of Section 366.22, the subsequent permanency

(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  When a case has been continued pursuant to
subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 366.22, the subsequent permanency
review hearing shall occur within 24 months after the date the
child was originally removed from the physical custody of his
or her parent or legal guardian.
