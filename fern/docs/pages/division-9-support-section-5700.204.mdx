# § 5700.204

*Simultaneous proceedings*

Simultaneous proceedings
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state may exercise jurisdiction to
establish
a support order if the petition or comparable
pleading is filed after a pleading is filed in another state or a
foreign country only if:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the petition or comparable pleading in this state is filed
before the expiration of the time allowed in the other state or
the foreign country for filing a responsive pleading challeng-
ing the exercise of jurisdiction by the other state or the
foreign country;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the contesting party timely challenges the exercise of
jurisdiction in the other state or the foreign country; and
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  if relevant, this state is the home state of the child.
