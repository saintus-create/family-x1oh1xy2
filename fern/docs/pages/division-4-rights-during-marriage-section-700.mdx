# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 700

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Leasehold interest in real property For the purposes of this division, a leasehold interest in real property is real property, not personal property. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 
1, 1994.)
