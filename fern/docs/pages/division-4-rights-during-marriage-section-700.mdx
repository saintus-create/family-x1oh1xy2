---
title: "700. Leasehold interest in real property"
description: "Leasehold interest in real property"
slug: division-4-rights-during-marriage-section-700
---

For the purposes of this division, a leasehold interest in real property is real property, not personal property.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
