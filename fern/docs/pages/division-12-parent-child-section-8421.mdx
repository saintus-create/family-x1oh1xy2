right to appointment as personal representative
,
Power to Designate Executor.
If a person is not named as executor in a will but it appears
by the terms of the will that the testator intended to commit
the execution of the will and the administration of the estate
to the person, the person is entitled to appointment as
personal representative in the same manner as if named as
executor.
