---
title: "6901. “Dental care” means X-ray examination, anesthetic, dental"
description: "“Dental care” means X-ray examination, anesthetic, dental"
slug: division-11-minors-section-6901
---

“Dental care” means X-ray examination, anesthetic, dental or surgical diagnosis or treatment, and hospital care by a dentist licensed under the Dental Practice Act.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
