# § 278.6 — Sentencing;

Sentencing;
relevant factors and circumstances;
aggravation; mitigation; expenses and costs in recover-
ing child
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  At the sentencing hearing following a conviction for a
violation of Section 278 or 278.5, or both, the court shall
consider any relevant factors and circumstances in aggrava-
tion, including, but not limited to, all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child was exposed to a substantial risk of physical
injury or illness.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The defendant inflicted or threatened to inflict physical
harm on a parent or lawful custodian of the child or on the
child at the time of or during the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The defendant harmed or abandoned the child during
the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The child was taken, enticed away, kept, withheld, or
concealed outside the United States.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The child has not been returned to the lawful custodi-
an.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The defendant previously abducted or threatened to
abduct the child.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The defendant substantially altered the appearance or
the name of the child.
(8). The defendant denied the child appropriate education
during the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  The length of the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  The age of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  At the sentencing hearing following a conviction for a
violation of Section 278 or 278.5, or both, the court shall
consider any relevant factors and circumstances in mitigation,
including, but not limited to, both of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The defendant returned the child unharmed and prior
to arrest or issuance of a warrant for arrest, whichever is first.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The defendant provided information and assistance
leading to the child’s safe return.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In addition to any other penalties provided for a
violation of Section 278 or 278.5, a court shall order the
defendant to pay restitution to the district attorney for any
costs incurred in locating and returning the child as provided
in Section 3134 of the Family Code, and to the victim for
those expenses and costs reasonably incurred by, or on behalf

made pursuant to this section shall constitute a final judg-
ment and shall be enforceable as such.
