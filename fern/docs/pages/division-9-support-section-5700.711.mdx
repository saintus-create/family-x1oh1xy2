# California Family Code § 5700.711

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state may not modify a Convention child-support order if the obligee remains a resident of the foreign country where the support order was issued unless:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the obligee submits to the jurisdiction of a tribunal of this state, either expressly or by defending on the merits of the case without objecting to the jurisdiction at the first available opportunity; or

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the foreign tribunal lacks or refuses to exercise jurisdiction to modify its support order or issue a new support order.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If a tribunal of this state does not modify a Convention child-support order because the order is not recognized in this state, Section 5700.708(c) applies.
