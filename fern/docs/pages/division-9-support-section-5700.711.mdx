# § 5700.711 — Of this state does not recognize

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If a tribunal
of this state does not recognize
a
Convention support order under subsection (b)(2), (4), or
(9):
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the tribunal may not dismiss the proceeding without
allowing a reasonable
time for a party to request the
establishment
of a new Convention support order; and
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the Department of Child Support Services shall take all
appropriate measures to request a child-support order for the
obligee if the application for recognition and enforcement
was received under
