# 1209

Pending appeal

pending appeal
OF A CIVIL NATURE
stay of sentence
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The following acts or omissions in respect to a court of
justice, or proceedings therein, are contempts of the authority
of the court:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Disorderly, contemptuous, or insolent behavior toward
the judge while holding the court, tending to interrupt the
due course of a trial or other judicial proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A breach of the peace, boisterous conduct, or violent
disturbance, tending to interrupt the due course of a trial or
other judicial proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Misbehavior in office, or other willful neglect or
violation of duty by an attorney, counsel, clerk, sheriff,
coroner, or other person, appointed or elected to perform a
judicial or ministerial service.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Abuse of the process or proceedings of the court, or
falsely pretending to act. under authority of an order or
process of the court.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Disobedience of any lawful judgment, order, or process
of the court.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Willful disobedience by a juror of a court admonishment related to the prohibition on any form of communication or research about the case, including all forms of
electronic or wireless communication or research.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Rescuing any person or property in the custody of an
officer by virtue of an order or process of that court.
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  Unlawfully detaining a witness or party to an action
while going to, remaining at, or returning from the court
where the action is on the calendar for trial.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  Any other unlawful interference with the process or
proceedings of a court.
