# 17210

Local agency accessibility

The department shall ensure that the local child support agency offices and services are reasonably accessible throughout the counties, and shall establish systems for informing the public, including custodial and noncustodial parents of dependent children, of its services and operations. Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 478, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.
