# § 679.11 — Of the following:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For purposes of this section, a “certifying entity” is any
of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A state or local law enforcement agency.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A prosecutor.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A judge.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The Department of Industrial Relations.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Any other state or local government agencies that have
criminal, civil, or administrative investigative or prosecutorial
authority relating to human trafficking.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of this section, a “certifying official” is
any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The head of the certifying entity.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person in a supervisory role who has been specifically designated by the head of the certifying entity to issue
Form I-914 Supplement B declarations on behalf of that
agency.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A judge.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Any other certifying official defined under Section
214.14(a)<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of Title 8 of the Code of Federal Regulations.
