# 362.4

Order; restraining visitation or custody orders; opening

order; restraining visitation or custody orders; opening
of file relating to custody
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the juvenile court terminates its jurisdiction over a
minor who has been adjudged a dependent child of the
juvenile court prior to the minor’s attainment of the age of 18
years, and proceedings for dissolution of marriage, for nullity
of marriage, or for legal separation, of the minor’s parents, or
proceedings to establish the paternity of the minor child
brought under the Uniform Parentage Act, (commencing with Section 7600) of of the Family Code, are
pending in the superior court of any county, or an order has
been entered with regard to the custody of that minor, the
juvenile court on its own motion, may issue a protective order
as provided for in Section 213.5 or as defined in Section 6218
of the Family Code, and an order determining the custody of,
or visitation with, the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any order issued pursuant to this section shall continue until modified or terminated by a subsequent order of the
superior court. The order of the juvenile court shall be filed

Law 370
(2018), Visitation With Siblings.

(2018), Determination Whether to Order Services.
(2018), Visitation.
