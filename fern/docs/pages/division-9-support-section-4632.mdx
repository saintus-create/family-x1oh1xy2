# § 4632 — Grounds

Grounds
for filing motion to stop sale or use of
assets
An obligor-parent alleged to be in arrears under this article
may use any ground set forth in Section 4612 as a basis for
filing a motion under Section 4631 to stop a sale or use of
assets under this article.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
