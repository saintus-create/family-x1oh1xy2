# Support

## Section 4632

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Grounds for filing motion to stop sale or use of assets An obligor-parent alleged to be in arrears under this article may use any ground set forth in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4612.">§ 4612</a> as a basis for filing a motion under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4631.">§ 4631</a> to stop a sale or use of assets under this article.
