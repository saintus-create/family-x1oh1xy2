# § 4512 — ”

”
This is not a substantive change,
since former Civil Code Section 4512 provided for a judgment of
dissolution.
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  includes new language concerning the
effect of the expiration of the waiting period.
This is not a
substantive
change.
See Sections 2300 (effect of judgment of
dissolution when it becomes final), 2340 (date judgment becomes
final for purpose of terminating marriage relationship).
[23 Cal.
L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
Commentary
In re Marriage of Sanabia, 95 Cal.App.3d 483, 157 Cal.Rptr. 56
(1979), holds that the court may not condition entry of final judgment
on reimbursement to the county for AFDC benefits and filing fees
previously waived because of the party’s indigence.
