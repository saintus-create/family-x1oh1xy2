# 11485. Divorce or separation; children receiving or likely

*Support Services*

Divorce or separation; children receiving or likely
to receive aid; notices; non-exclusive remedies
If, to the knowledge of the court, aid has been applied for
or granted to a child of parents who are engaged in a divorce
or separate maintenance action which is pending, or if the
court at any stage of the litigation believes that within the
near future there is a likelihood that aid will be applied for on
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Making a fraudulent statement or representation with
respect to the place of residence of the individual in order to
receive assistance simultaneously from two or more states or
counties.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Submitting documents for nonexistent children, or
submitting false documents for the purpose of showing
ineligible children to be eligible for aid.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  When there has been a receipt of cash benefits that
exceeds ten thousand dollars ($10,000) as a result of intentionally and willfully doing any of the following acts for the
purpose of establishing or maintaining the family’s eligibility
for aid or increasing or preventing a reduction in the amount
of aid:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Making a false or misleading statement or misrepresenting, concealing, or withholding facts.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Committing any act intended to mislead, misrepresent,
conceal, or withhold facts or propound a falsity.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The needs of any individual who is a member of a
family applying for, or receiving, aid under this chapter shall
not be taken into account in making the determination under
Section 11450 with respect to his or her family for the
following periods beginning on the date or any time thereafter the individual is convicted of a felony in state or federal
court, including any determination made on the basis of a
plea of guilty or nolo contendere, for committing fraud in the
receipt or attempted receipt of aid:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  For two years, if the amount of aid is less than two
thousand dollars ($2,000).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  For five years, if the amount of aid is two thousand
dollars ($2,000) or more but is less than five thousand dollars
($5,000).
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Permanently, if the amount of aid is five thousand
dollars ($5,000) or more.
