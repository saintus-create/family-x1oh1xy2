See the definition of “person” in
Section 3901(/).
Section 3916 does not alter the requirements for bona fide
purchaser or holder in due course status under other law for persons
who acquire from a custodial property subject to recordation or
registration.
Background on Section 3916 of Repealed Code
Section 3916 was added by 1984 Cal.Stat. ch. 243
