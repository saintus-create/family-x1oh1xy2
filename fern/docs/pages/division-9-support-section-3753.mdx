---
title: "3753. Health insurance cost; separation from child sup-"
description: "Health insurance cost; separation from child sup-"
slug: division-9-support-section-3753
---

The cost of the health insurance shall be in addition to the child support amount ordered under Article 2 (commencing with Section 4050), with allowance for the costs of health insurance actually obtained given due consideration under subdivision (d) of Section 4059. Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1269, Sec. 36. Effective January 1, 1995.
