,
Testator’s Direction or Intention.
Minor named as executor
(a)  If a person named as executor is under the age of
majority and there is another person named as executor, the
other person may be appointed and may administer the estate
until the majority of the minor, who may then be appointed
ARTICLE 3. ADMINISTRATORS
WITH THE WILL ANNEXED
as coexecutor.
(b)  If a person named as executor is under the age of
majority and there is no other person named as executor,
another person may be appointed as personal representative,
but the court may revoke the appointment on the majority of
the minor, who may then be appointed as executor. (Stats.
