(f)  The nonmember spouse who receives a retirement
allowance is not a retired member under this part. However,
the allowance of the nonmember spouse shall be increased by
application of the improvement factor and shall be eligible
for the application of supplemental increases and other
benefit maintenance provisions under this part, including, but
not limited to, Sections 24412 and 24415 based on the same
criteria used for the application of these benefit maintenance
increases to the service retirement allowances of members.

not apply to a nonmember spouse of a member subject to the
