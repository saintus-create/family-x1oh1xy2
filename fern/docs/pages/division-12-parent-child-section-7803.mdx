# 7803. Effect of declaration of freedom

*Parent and Child Relationship*

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Court personnel.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child who is the subject of the proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The parents or guardian of the child.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The attorneys for the parties.
Effect of declaration of freedom
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Any other person designated by the judge.
A declaration of freedom from parental custody and
control pursuant to this part terminates all parental rights and
responsibilities with regard to the child. (Stats.1992, c. 162
(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
