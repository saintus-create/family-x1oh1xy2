# 5021. Community property on death made pursuant to a provision

*Support*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a proceeding to set aside a nonprobate transfer of
community property on death made pursuant to a provision
for transfer of the property executed by a married person
without the written consent of the person’s spouse, the court
shall set aside the transfer as to the nonconsenting spouse’s
interest in the property, subject to terms and conditions or
other remedies that appear equitable under the circumstances of the case, taking into account the rights of all
interested persons.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  affects any additional remedy the nonconsenting spouse may have against the person’s
estate for a nonprobate transfer of community property on
death without the spouse’s written consent.
1992, c. 51 (A.B.1719), $ 6.)
