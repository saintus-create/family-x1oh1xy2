# Support Services

## Section 17556

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Annual report f On or before March 1, 2019, and annually thereafter, the department shall submit a report to the Legislature providing information on the status of all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Case-to-staff ratios for each local child support agency. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Collections to families and recoupment collections to county, state, and federal governmental agencies. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Cost avoidance benefits. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The number of families served by the child support program.
