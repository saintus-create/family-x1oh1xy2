# § 17556 — Annual report

Annual report
f
On or before March 1, 2019, and annually thereafter, the
department shall submit a report to the Legislature providing
information on the status of all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Case-to-staff ratios for each local child support agen-
cy.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Collections to families and recoupment collections to
county, state, and federal governmental agencies.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Cost avoidance benefits.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The number of families served by the child support
program.
