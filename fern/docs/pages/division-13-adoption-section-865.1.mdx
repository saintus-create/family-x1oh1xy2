# § 865.1

*(Added by Stats.2012, c. 835*

(Added by Stats.2012, c. 835
(S.B.1172), $ 2.)
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Aclinical examination.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon registration with the board, an associate marriage and family therapist’ shall, within the first year of
registration, take an examination on California law and
ethics.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A registrant or an applicant for licensure may take the
clinical examination only upon meeting all of the following
requirements:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Completion of all required supervised work experience.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Completion of all education requirements,
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Passage of the California law and ethics examination.
(Added by Stats.2011, c. 387 (S.B.704), § 2, operative Jan. 1,
Prohibited actions
Under no circumstances shall a mental health provider
engage in sexual orientation change efforts with a patient
under 18 years of age. (Added by Stats.2012, c. 835 (S.B.
1172), § 2.)
