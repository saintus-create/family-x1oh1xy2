# Support

## Section 4414

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Order for relief; effect of order 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If, upon hearing, the court determines that the requirements of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4411.">§ 4411</a> are satisfied, the court shall make an order that the petitioner is relieved from the obligation otherwise imposed by law to support the parent. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An order under this section also releases the petitioner with respect to any state law under which a child is required to do any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Pay for the support, care, maintenance, and the like of a parent. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Reimburse the state or a local public agency for furnishing the support, care, maintenance, or the like of a parent.
