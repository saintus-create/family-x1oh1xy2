# § 14944

*Enforcement*

Enforcement
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Civil penalties
Any person who—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  violates section 14921 of this title;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  makes a false or fraudulent statement, or misrep-
resentation, with respect to a material fact, or offers,
gives, solicits, or accepts inducement by way of compen-
sation, intended to influence or affect in the United
States or a foreign country—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  a decision by an accrediting entity with re-
spect to the accreditation of an agency or approval
of a person under subchapter II;
42 § 14952
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  the relinquishment of parental rights or the
giving of parental consent relating to the adoption
of a child in a case subject to the Convention;
or
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  a decision or action of any entity performing
a central authority function; or
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  engages another person as an agent, whether in
the United States or in a foreign country, who in the
course of that agency takes any of the actions described
in paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  or (2),
shall be subject, in addition to any other penalty that may be
prescribed by law, to a civil money penalty of not more than
$50,000 for a first violation, and not more than $100,000 for
each succeeding violation.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Civil enforcement
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Authority of Attorney General
The Attorney General may bring a civil action to
enforce subsection <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  against any person in any United
States district court.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Factors to be considered in imposing penalties
In imposing penalties the court shall consider the
gravity of the violation, the degree of culpability of the
defendant, and any history of prior violations by the
defendant.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Criminal penalties
Whoever knowingly and willfully violates paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subsection <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall be subject to a fine of not more
than $250,000, imprisonment for not more than 5 years, or
both.
(Pub.L. 106-279, Title IV, § 404, Oct. 6, 2000, 114
Stat. 842.)
