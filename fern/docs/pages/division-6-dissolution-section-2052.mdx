# § 2052 — Method of notice

Method of notice
Notice pursuant to this chapter may be sent by first-class
mail, postage prepaid, to the last known address of the
covered dependents, named beneficiaries, or other specified

NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION
persons who have requested receipt of notification.
(Stats.
1992,
c.
162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan.
1, 1994.)
