# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.102

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Definitions In this part: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Child” means. an individual, whether over or under the age of majority, who is or is alleged to be owed a duty of support by the individual’s parent or who is or is alleged to be the beneficiary of a support order directed to the parent. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Child-support order” means a support order for a child, including a child who has attained the age of majority under the law of the issuing state or foreign country. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  “Convention” means the Convention on the International Recovery of Child Support and Other Forms of Family Maintenance, concluded at The Hague on November 23, 2007. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  “Duty of support” means an obligation imposed or imposable by law to provide support for a child, spouse, or former spouse, including an unsatisfied obligation to provide support. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  “Foreign country” means a country, including a political subdivision thereof, other than the United States, that authorizes the issuance of support orders and:
