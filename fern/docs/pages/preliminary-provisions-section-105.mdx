# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 105

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Person 
“Person” 
includes a natural person, firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, corporation, limited liability company, or public entity.
