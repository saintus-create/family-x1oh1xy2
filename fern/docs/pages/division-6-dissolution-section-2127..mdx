# § 2127.

*Part 1 — General Provisions · Chapter 10 — Relief From Judgment*

As to actions or motions filed under this chapter, if a timely request is made, the court shall render a statement of decision where the court has resolved controverted factual evidence. (Amended (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219) by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 17. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)
