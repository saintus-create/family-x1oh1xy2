# § 2093 — Application of title; full faith and credit

The application of this chapter is limited by the requirement of the Constitution of the United States that full faith and credit shall be given in each state to the public acts, records, and judicial proceedings of every other state. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
