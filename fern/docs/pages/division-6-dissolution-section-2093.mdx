# 2093 — Application of title; full faith and credit

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

Application of title; full faith and credit
The application of this chapter is limited by the require-
ment of the Constitution of the United States that full faith
and credit shall be given in each state to the public acts,
records, and judicial proceedings of every other state.
(Stats.
1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan.
1, 1994.)
