# 3752 — as assigned payee for child support; policy information

Support

as assigned payee for child support; policy information
to be provided to custodial parent
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the local child support agency has been designated as
the assigned payee for child support, the court shall order the
parent to notify the local child support agency upon applying
for and obtaining health insurance coverage for the child
within a reasonable period of time.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The local child support agency shall obtain a completed medical form from the parent in accordance with Section
17422 and shall forward the completed form to the State
Department of Health Services.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In those cases where the local child support agency is
providing medical support enforcement services, the local
child support agency shall provide the parent or person
having custody of the child with information pertaining to the
health insurance policy that has been secured for the child.
