# Support

## Section 3752

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Notice to the local child support agency designated as assigned payee for child support; policy information to be provided to custodial parent 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the local child support agency has been designated as the assigned payee for child support, the court shall order the parent to notify the local child support agency upon applying for and obtaining health insurance coverage for the child within a reasonable period of time. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The local child support agency shall obtain a completed medical form from the parent in accordance with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17422.">§ 17422</a> and shall forward the completed form to the State Department of Health Services. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In those cases where the local child support agency is providing medical support enforcement services, the local child support agency shall provide the parent or person having custody of the child with information pertaining to the health insurance policy that has been secured for the child.
