Section 916 prescribes rules of liability of former community,
quasi-community, or community estate property and former separate
property following a division of the property pursuant to a court
judgment of separation, dissolution, or later division.
Subdivision (a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  states the rule that the rights of a creditor
against the property of a debtor are not affected by assignment of the
debt to the other spouse for payment pursuant to a property division.
A creditor who is not paid may seek to satisfy the debt out of
property of the debtor. The debtor in such a case will have a right of
reimbursement against the former spouse pursuant to subdivision
(b).
