An official written report or, record of the
military department or civilian department or agency that the
absentee has returned to its controllable jurisdiction or is
deceased shall be received as evidence of that fact.
(b)  This section does not in any manner derogate the
finality and conclusiveness of any order, judgment, or decree
previously entered in the proceeding.
