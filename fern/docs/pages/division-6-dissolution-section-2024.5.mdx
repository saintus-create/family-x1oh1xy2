# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2024.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Redacting social security numbers from pleadings, attachments, documents or other written material filed pursuant to petitions for dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage or legal separation; exceptions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (b), the petitioner or respondent may redact any social security number from any pleading, attachment, document, or other written material filed with the court pursuant to a petition for dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage, or legal separation.
