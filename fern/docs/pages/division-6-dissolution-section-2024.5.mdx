# § 2024.5 — Redacting social security numbers from plead-

Redacting social security numbers from plead-
ings, attachments, documents or other written material
filed pursuant to petitions for dissolution of marriage,
nullity of marriage or legal separation;
exceptions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (b), the petitioner or
respondent may redact any social security number from any
pleading, attachment, document, or other written material
filed with the court pursuant to a petition for dissolution of
marriage, nullity of marriage, or legal separation.
