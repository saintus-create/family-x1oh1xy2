# § 222 — Without

without
substantive change.
Throughout this section, “birth parent” has
been substituted for “birth father or mother.”
See Section 8512
(“birth parent” defined).
The word “duly,” formerly preceding
“acknowledged,” has been omitted as surplus.
See also Sections
8518 (“department” defined), 8530 (“licensed adoption agency”
defined).
For related provisions, see Sections 8814 (consent to independent
adoption), 9003 (consent to stepparent adoption).
[23 Cal.L.Rev.
Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
