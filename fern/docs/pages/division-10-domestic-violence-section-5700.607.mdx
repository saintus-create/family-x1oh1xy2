# 5700.607
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A party contesting the validity or enforcement of a registered support order or seeking to vacate the registration has the burden of proving one or more of the following defenses:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the issuing tribunal lacked personal jurisdiction over the contesting party;

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the order was obtained by fraud;

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  the order has been vacated, suspended, or modified by a later order;

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  the issuing tribunal has stayed the order pending appeal;

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  there is a defense under the law of this state to the remedy sought;

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  full or partial payment has been made;

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  the statute of limitation under Section 5700.604 precludes enforcement of some or all of the alleged arrearages; or

<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  the alleged controlling order is not the controlling order.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If a party presents evidence establishing a full or partial defense under subsection (a), a tribunal may stay enforcement of a registered support order, continue the proceeding to permit production of additional relevant evidence, and issue other appropriate orders. An uncontested portion of the registered support order may be enforced by all remedies available under the law of this state.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the contesting party does not establish a defense under subsection <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  to the validity or enforcement of a registered support order, the registering tribunal shall issue an order confirming the order.
