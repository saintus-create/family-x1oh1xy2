# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2110

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Default judgments; declarations of disclosure In the case of a default judgment, the petitioner may waive the final declaration of disclosure requirements provided in this chapter, and shall not be required to serve a final declaration of disclosure on the respondent nor receive a final declaration of disclosure from the respondent. 
However, a preliminary declaration of disclosure by the petitioner is required unless the petitioner served the summons and petition by publication or posting pursuant to court order and the respondent has defaulted.
