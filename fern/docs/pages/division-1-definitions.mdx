  <Accordion title="Date of separation">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 70</Badge></div>
        <p><strong>(a)</strong> Date of separation means the date that a complete and final break in the marital relationship has occurred, as evidenced by both of the following: (1) The spouse has expressed to the other spouse the intent to end the marriage. (2) The conduct of the spouse is consistent with the intent to end the marriage. <strong>(b)</strong> In determining the date of separation, the court shall take into consideration all relevant evidence.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Amended Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 1</Badge>
          <Badge intent="success" minimal>AB 1817</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2020</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Person">
    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 105</Badge></div>
        <p>Person includes a natural person, firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, corporation, limited liability company, or public entity.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Amended Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 107</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1995</Badge>

          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>

It also includes past due support or arrearage when it exists.</p>

        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Stats. 2006, Ch. 838, Sec. 1</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2007</Badge>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 180</Badge></div>
        <p>Notice must be sent by registered or certified mail to the minor's parent or legal guardian, Indian custodian, and the Indian child's tribe. Must include the child's name, birthdate, birthplace, and biological parentage.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Stats. 2006, Ch. 838, Sec. 1</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2007</Badge>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 185</Badge></div>
        <p>Allows a tribe from which an Indian child is descended to participate upon request. The tribe may address the court, examine documents, and submit written reports and recommendations.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Stats. 2006, Ch. 838, Sec. 1</Badge>
