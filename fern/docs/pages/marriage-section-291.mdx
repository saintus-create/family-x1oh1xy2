<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A money judgment or judgment for possession or sale
of property that is made or entered under this code, including
a judgment for child, family, or spousal support, is enforceable until paid in full or otherwise satisfied.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A judgment described in this section is exempt from
any requirement that a judgment be renewed. Failure to
renew a judgment described in this section has no effect on
the enforceability of the judgment.
