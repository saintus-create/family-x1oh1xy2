# § 13554 — “Central reporting organization” defined

“Central reporting organization” defined
Section operative Jan. 1, 2020.
For the purposes of this article, “central reporting organization” means a third-party service that automates the claims
identifying process or provides interactive lookups. (Added
by Stats.2018, c. 439 (A.B.2802), § 1, eff: Jan. 1, 2019,
operative Jan. 1, 2020.)
