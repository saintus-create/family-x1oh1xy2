# 16131. Final, who is

*Support Services*

SERVICES
final, who is
receiving or is eligible to receive Adoption Assistance Program assistance, including Medi-Cal, and whose foster care
court supervision has been terminated, shall be provided
medically necessary specialty mental health services by the
local mental health plan in the county of residence of his or
her adoptive parents, pursuant to all of the following:
16131.5.
Older children;
16132.
Federal Fostering Connections to Success and Increasing Adoptions Act; conformity of state stat-
funds;
federal incentive payments.
16133.
Former employees of the State Department
Social Services; employee benefits.
utes; use of funds.
