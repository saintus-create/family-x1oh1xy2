---
title: "7801. Liberal construction"
description: "Liberal construction"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-7801
---

This part shall be liberally construed to serve and protect the interests and welfare of the child.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
