# § 7907.

*Part 5 — Interstate Compact On Placement Of Children*

No provision of law restricting out-of-state placement of children for adoption shall apply to placements made pursuant to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
