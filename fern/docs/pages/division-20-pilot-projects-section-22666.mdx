# 22666. Terminable interest doctrine; legislative intent

*Pilot Projects*

Terminable interest doctrine; legislative intent
It is the intent of the Legislature to abolish any remaining
application of the terminable interest doctrine in California
relating to the division of public retirement benefits of a
member in the event of dissolution of marriage or death if the
division is made under this chapter.
