# Pilot Projects

## Section 22666

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Terminable interest doctrine; legislative intent It is the intent of the Legislature to abolish any remaining application of the terminable interest doctrine in California relating to the division of public retirement benefits of a member in the event of dissolution of marriage or death if the division is made under this chapter.
