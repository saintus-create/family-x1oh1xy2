# 7894 — Effect of order

Parent and Child Relationship

Effect of order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An order and judgment of the court declaring a child
free from the custody and control of a parent or parents
under this part is conclusive and binding upon the child, upon
the parent or parents, and upon all other persons who have
been served with citations by publication or otherwise as
provided in this part.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  After making the order and judgment, the court has no
power to set aside, change, or modify it.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section limits the right to appeal from
the order and judgment.
