# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 140

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Waiver As used in this chapter, “waiver” means a waiver by the surviving spouse of any of the rights listed in subdivision (a) 
of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=141.">Section 141</a>, whether signed before or during marriage.

**Source:** California Family Code Annotated (Grace Ganz Blumberg, 2020 Desktop Edition). [View on California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=140.).
