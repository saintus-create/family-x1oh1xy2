# 782 — Injuries to married person by spouse;

Premarital Agreements

Injuries to married person by spouse;
primary
resort to separate property; consent of injured spouse
to
use of community property; indemnity
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Where an injury to a married person is caused in whole
or in part by the negligent or wrongful act or omission of the
person’s spouse, the community property may not be used to
discharge the liability of the tortfeasor spouse to the injured
spouse or the liability to make contribution
to a joint
tortfeasor
until the separate property of the tortfeasor
spouse, not exempt from enforcement of a money judgment,
is exhausted.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section does not prevent the use of community
property to discharge a liability referred to in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span> 
if the injured spouse gives written consent thereto after the
occurrence of the injury.
_(c) This section does not affect the right to indemnity
provided by an insurance or other contract to discharge the
tortfeasor spouse’s liability, whether or not the consideration
"Bh

given for the contract consisted: of community property.
