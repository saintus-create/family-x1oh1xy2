# § 20014 — Unrepresented party; evaluator requirement no-

The clerk shall stamp all moving papers in which a party is not represented by counsel with a notice of a requirement to see the Family Law Evaluator. The unrepresented party shall serve the stamped pleadings on the other party. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
