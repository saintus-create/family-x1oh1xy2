# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 953

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Holder of the privilege As used in this article, “holder of the privilege” means: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The client, if the client has no guardian or conservator. 
(b)(1) A guardian or conservator of the client, if the client has a guardian or conservator, except as provided in paragraph (2). 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the guardian or conservator has an actual or apparent conflict of interest with the client, then the guardian or conservator does not hold the privilege. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The personal representative of the client if the client is dead, including a personal representative appointed pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=12252.">§ 12252</a> of the Probate Code. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A successor, assign, trustee in dissolution, or any similar representative of a firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, corporation, or public entity that is no longer in existence. . (Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967. 
Amended by Stats.2009, c. 8 (A.B.1163), § 1; Stats.2018, c. 475 (A.B.1290), § 1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019.)
