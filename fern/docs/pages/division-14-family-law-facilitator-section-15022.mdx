# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 15022

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Partner’s right to formal accounting Any partner shall have the right to a formal account as to partnership affairs: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If he is wrongfully excluded from the partnership business or possession of its property by his copartners, 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Ifthe right exists under the terms of any agreement, 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  As provided by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=15021.">§ 15021</a>, 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Whenever other circumstances render it just and reasonable.
