# § 3082 — Statement of reasons for grant or denial

Statement of reasons for grant or denial
When a request for joint custody is granted or denied, the
court, upon the request of any party, shall state in its decision
the reasons for granting or denying the request. A statement
that joint physical custody is, or is not, in the best interest of
the child is not sufficient to satisfy the requirements of this
section.
