# Custody of Children

## Section 3082

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Statement of reasons for grant or denial When a request for joint custody is granted or denied, the court, upon the request of any party, shall state in its decision the reasons for granting or denying the request. A statement that joint physical custody is, or is not, in the best interest of the child is not sufficient to satisfy the requirements of this section.
