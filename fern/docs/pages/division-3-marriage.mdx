## Marriage

  <h3>Marriage is a civil contract</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 300</Badge></div>
        <p>Marriage is a personal relation arising out of a civil contract between two persons, to which the consent of the parties is required.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Amended Stats. 2014, Ch. 69, Sec. 1</Badge>

  <h3>Consent required</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 301</Badge></div>
        <p>Marriage requires the consent of both parties, capable of being given freely, openly, and understandingly. Consent obtained by fraud is voidable.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1994</Badge>

  <h3>Certificate of registered domestic partnership as identification</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 306.5</Badge></div>
        <p>A certified copy of a marriage certificate shall be accepted as identification establishing a true, full name for purposes of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=12800.7.">Section 12800.7 of the Vehicle Code</a>.
