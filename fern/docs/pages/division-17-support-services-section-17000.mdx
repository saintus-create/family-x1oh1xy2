# Support Services

## Section 17000

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Definitions The definitions contained in this section, and definitions applicable to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=9.">Division 9</a> (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3500.">§ 3500</a>), shall govern the construction of this division, unless the context requires otherwise. 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Child support debt” means the amount of money owed as child support pursuant to a court order. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Child support order” means a court order for the payment of a set or determinable amount of support by a parent or a court order requiring a parent to provide for health insurance coverage. 
“Child support order” includes any court order for spousal support or for medical support to the extent these obligations are to be enforced by a single state agency for child support under Title IV-D. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Court” means any superior court of this state and any court or tribunal of another state that has jurisdiction to determine the liability of persons for the support of another person. 
_ 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Court order” means a judgment, decree, or order of any court of this state that orders the payment of a set or determinable amount of support by a parent. 
It does not include any order or decree of any proceeding in which a court did not order support. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Department” means the Department of Child Support Services. 
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Dependent child” means any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Any person under 18 years of age who is not emancipated, self-supporting, married, or a member of the Armed Forces of the United States. 
_ 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any unmarried person who is at least 18 years of age but who has not reached 19 years of age, is not emancipated, and is a student regularly attending high school or a program of vocational or technical training designed to train that person for gainful employment. 
667 
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  “Director” means the Director of Child Support Services or an authorized representative. 
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  “Local child support agency” means the county department of child support services created pursuant to this chapter and with which the department has entered into a cooperative agreement, to secure child and spousal support, medical support, and determine paternity.
