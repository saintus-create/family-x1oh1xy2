# Division of Property

## Section 2944

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

. Liability for failure to take possession The public guardian is not liable for failing to take possession or control of property that is beyond the ability of the public guardian to possess or control.
