# 3914 — Use of custodial property

Support

Use of custodial property
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A custodian may deliver or pay to the minor or expend
for the minor’s benefit as much of the custodial property as
the custodian considers advisable for the use and benefit of
the minor, without court order and without regard to <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the
duty or ability of the custodian personally, or of any other
person, to support the minor or <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  any other income or
property of the minor which may be applicable or available
for that purpose.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  On petition of an interested person or the minor if the
minor has attained the age of 14 years, the court may order
the custodian to deliver or pay to the minor or expend for the
minor’s benefit so much of the custodial property as the court
considers advisable for the use and benefit of the minor.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A delivery, payment, or expenditure under this section
is in addition to, not in substitution for, and does not affect,
any obligation of a person to support the minor.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  In lieu of the powers and duties described in subdivi-
sion (a), a transferor who is also the custodian may elect to
