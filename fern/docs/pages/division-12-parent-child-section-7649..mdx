# § 7649.

*Part 3 — Uniform Parentage Act · Chapter 4 — Determination Of Parent And Child Relationship*

This article does not limit the rights and remedies available under any other law with regard to setting aside or vacating a judgment of parentage. (Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 62. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)
