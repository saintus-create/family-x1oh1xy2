# 221.20 — Throughout this section, “birth parent” has

Marriage

Throughout this section, “birth parent” has
been substituted for “birth father or mother.”
See Section 8512
(“birth parent” defined).
See also Section 8530 (“licensed adoption
agency” defined).
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
Commentary
See Section 8604 Commentary.
