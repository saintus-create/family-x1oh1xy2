# § 79 — The section continued

The section continued
former Probate Code Section 234 (repealed by 1983 Cal.Stat. ch. 842
§ 19). The 1988 amendment corrected a section reference.
As to
the 1988 amendment, see Comments to Conforming Revisions and
Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 1031, 1091 (1988). For
background on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this
part under the part heading.
.
