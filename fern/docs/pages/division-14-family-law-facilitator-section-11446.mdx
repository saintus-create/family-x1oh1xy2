Notwithstanding any other statute, funeral expenses and
expenses of last illness shall be charged against the estate of
the decedent and shall not be allocated to, or charged against
the community share of, the surviving spouse, whether or not
the surviving spouse is financially able to pay the expenses
and whether or not the surviving spouse or any other person
is also liable for the expenses. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759),
14, operative July 1, 1991.)
