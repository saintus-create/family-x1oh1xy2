# § 6525

*Petition;*

Petition;
against
estate;
homestead
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Property of the decedent set apart as a probate
homestead is liable for claims against the estate of the
decedent, subject to the probate homestead right. The
probate homestead right in property of the decedent is liable
for claims that are secured by liens and encumbrances on the
property at the time of the decedent’s death but is exempt to
the extent of the homestead exemption as to any claim that
would have been subject to a homestead exemption at the
time of the decedent’s death under Article 4 (commencing
with Section 704.710) of Chapter 4 of of Title 9 of
Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The probate homestead right in the property of the
decedent is not liable for claims against the person for whose
use the probate homestead is set apart.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Property of the decedent set apart as a probate
homestead is liable for claims against the testate or intestate
successors of the decedent or other successors to the property
after administration, subject to the probate homestead right.
notice
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A petition to select and set apart a probate homestead
may be filed by any interested person.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notice of the hearing on the petition shall be given as
provided in Section 1220 to all of the following persons:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Each person listed in Section 1220.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Each known heir whose interest in the estate would be
affected by the petition.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Each known devisee whose interest in the estate would
be affected by the petition. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14,
operative July 1, 1991.)
