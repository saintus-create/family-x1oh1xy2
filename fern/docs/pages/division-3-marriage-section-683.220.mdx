---
title: "683.220. If a judgment is renewed pursuant to this article, the date"
description: "If a judgment is renewed pursuant to this article, the date"
slug: division-3-marriage-section-683.220
---

If a judgment is renewed pursuant to this article, the date
of the filing of the application for renewal shall be deemed to
be the date that the period for commencing an action on the
renewed judgment commences to run under Section 337.5.
1982, c. 1364, p. 5076, 2, operative July 1,
1983.)
