# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 28

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Community property 
“Community property” means: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Community property heretofore or hereafter acquired during marriage by a married person while domiciled in this state. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  All personal property wherever situated, and all real property situated in this state, heretofore or hereafter acquired during the marriage by a married person while domiciled elsewhere, that is community property, or a substantially equivalent type of marital property, under the laws of the place where the acquiring spouse was domiciled at the time of its acquisition. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  All personal property wherever situated, and all real property situated in this state, heretofore or hereafter acquired during the marriage by a married person in exchange for real or personal property, wherever situated, that is community property, or a substantially equivalent type of marital property, under the laws of the place where the acquiring spouse was domiciled at the time the property so exchanged was acquired.
