# Division of Property

## Section 2555

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Disposition of community estate; revision on appeal The disposition of the community estate, as provided in this division, is subject to revision on appeal in all particulars, including those which are stated to be in the discretion of the court.
