# § 3442 — Orders made under the Hague Convention on the

Orders made under the Hague Convention on the
Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction

,
Under this chapter, a court of this state may enforce an
order for the return of a child made under the Hague
Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child
Abduction as if it were a child custody determination.
