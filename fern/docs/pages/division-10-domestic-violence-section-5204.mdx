---
title: "5204. “Due date of support payments” is the date specifically"
description: "“Due date of support payments” is the date specifically"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-5204
---

“Due date of support payments” is the date specifically
stated in the order of support or, if no date is stated in the
support order, the last day of the month in which the support
payment is to be paid.


[Source](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5204.)
