# § 11484

*Public assistance fraud; cooperation of agencies*

Public assistance fraud; cooperation of agencies
to assist in investigation and prevention
On request,
all state, county, and local agencies shall
cooperate with an investigator of an agency whose primary
function is to detect, prevent, or prosecute public assistance
fraud, by providing all information on hand relative to the
location and prosecution of any person who has, by means of
false statement or representation or by impersonation or
other fraudulent device, obtained aid, or attempted to obtain
aid for an individual under this chapter.
That information is
subject to confidentiality requirements under Chapter 5
(commencing with Section 10850) of . For purposes of
this section, “information” shall not include taxpayer return
information as defined in Section 19549 of the Revenue and
Taxation
Code, unless
disclosure
of this information
is
expressly authorized pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with
Section 19501) of Chapter 7 of .2 of the Revenue and
Taxation Code.
