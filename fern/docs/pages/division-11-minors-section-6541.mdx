# § 6541 — Grant or modification; petition; hearing; notice

10th Wills and Probate § 824 (2018),
10th Wills and Probate § 825 (2018),
10th Wills and Probate § 827 (2018),
Grant or modification; petition; hearing; notice
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may grant or modify a family allowance on
petition of any interested person.
family allowance shall be granted only to those who do not
have a reasonable maintenance from other sources. (Stats.
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  With respect to an order for the family allowance
provided for in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 6540:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Before the inventory is filed, the order may be made or
modified either <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  ex parte or <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  after notice of the hearing
on the petition has been given as provided in Section 1220.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  After the inventory is filed, the order may be made or
modified only after notice of the hearing on the petition has
been given as provided in Section 1220.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An order for the family allowance provided in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 6540 may be made only after notice of the
hearing on the petition has been given as provided in Section
1220 to all of the following persons:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Each person listed in Section 1220.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Each known heir whose interest in the estate would be
affected by the petition.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Each known devisee whose interest in the estate would
be affected by the petition.
order, whether before or after the date of the order, as the
court in its discretion determines, but the allowance may not
be made retroactive to a date earlier than the date of the
decedent’s death. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative
July 1, 1991.)
