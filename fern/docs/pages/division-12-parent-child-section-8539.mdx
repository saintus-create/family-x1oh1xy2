# 8539 — “Licensed adoption agency” defined

Parent and Child Relationship

“Licensed adoption agency” defined
“Place for adoption” means, in the case of an independent
adoption, the selection of a prospective adoptive parent or
parents for a child by the birth parent or parents and the
completion of an adoptive placement agreement on a form
prescribed by the department by the birth parent or parents
placing the child with prospective adoptive parents.
“Licensed adoption agency” means an agency licensed by
the department to provide adoption services. (Stats.1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended
by
Stats.2012, c. 35 (S.B.1013), § 8, eff: June 27, 2012.)
