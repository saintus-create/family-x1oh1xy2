# 1542

And local agency

and local agency
In each case involving a petition for guardianship of the
person, the petitioner shall deliver pursuant to Section 1215 a
notice of the hearing and a copy of the petition, at least 15
days before the hearing, to the Director of Social Services at
the director’s office in Sacramento and to the local agency
designated by the board of supervisors to investigate guardianships for the court.
operative July 1, 1991.
(Stats.1990,
Amended
c. 79 (A.B.759), 14,
by Stats.2017, c. 319
(A.B.976), 33, eff. Jan. 1, 2018.)
1990 Enactment
