# Support

## Section 4615

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Performance bonds In lieu of depositing cash or other assets as provided in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4614.">§ 4614</a>, the obligor-parent may, if approved by: the court, provide a performance bond secured by real property or other assets of the obligor-parent and equal in value to one year of payments.
