# § 89 — The section continued the

The section continued the
substance of former Probate Code Section 662 (repealed by 1983
Cal.Stat. ch. 842 § 39). The 1987 amendment <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  revised the cross-
reference to the procedure for mailing notice of the hearing and <span class="subsec">(2)</span> 
deleted the provision requiring the clerk to set the petition for
hearing, this provision being unnecessary in view of Section 1285
which is a general provision that imposes this duty on the clerk. As
to the 1987 amendment, see Communication from California Law
Revision Commission Concerning Assembly Bill 708, 19 Cal.L.Revi-
sion Comm’n Reports 545, 560 (1988); Comments to Conforming
Revisions and Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 391, 449
(1988).
