# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5122

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Account 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Account” means a contract of deposit of funds between a depositor and a financial institution, and includes a 1148 checking account, savings account, certificate of deposit, share account, and other like arrangement. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Account” does not include: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  An account established for deposit of funds of a partnership, joint venture, or other association for business purposes. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An account controlled by one or more persons as the duly authorized agent or trustee for a corporation, unincorporated association, or charitable or civic organization. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A regular fiduciary or trust account where the relationship is established other than by deposit agreement. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  An account established for the deposit of funds of the estate of a ward, conservatee, or decedent.
