# 8915 — Report or findings of agency; copy to attorney or

Support Services

Report or findings of agency; copy to attorney or
petitioner
When any report or findings are submitted to the court by
a licensed adoption agency, a copy of the report or findings,
whether favorable or unfavorable, shall be given to the
petitioner’s attorney in the proceeding, if the petitioner has
an attorney of record, or to the petitioner.
(Stats. 1992, c.

(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
