# Support Services

## Section 17450

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Definitions; manner of collecting child support delinquencies; return or retention; delegation of functions to Franchise Tax Board; letter of agreement and interagency agreement between department and Franchise Tax Board. 
Tax return information available to department; no obligation or liability incurred; privacy and confidentiality. 
Financial Institution Data Match System; guidelines; governmental access and misuse of information; compilation of records; identifying information; no obligation or liability incurred; notification of certain circumstances of obligors of past-due support; cost reimbursement. 
Request for depository institution to provide designated address for receiving notices to withhold; liability for failure to withhold; withholding delinquency and interest by depository institution from credits or things of value. 
Compliance without legal or equitable action; deposit accounts or accounts to be withheld; notice to each person named on account; contents and service charge. 
Reciprocal agreements with other states; exchange of information with Internal Revenue Service; interagency agreements; cost reimbursement and funding.
