# Custody of Children

## Section 3179

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Modification of agreements A custody or visitation agreement reached as a result of mediation may be modified at any time at the discretion of the court, subject to Chapter 1 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3020.">Section 3020</a>), Chapter 2 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3040.">Section 3040</a>), Chapter 4 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3080.">Section 3080</a>), and Chapter 5 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3100.">Section 3100</a>).
