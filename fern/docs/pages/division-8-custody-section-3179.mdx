<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any intake form that an agency charged with providing
family court services requires the parties to complete before
the commencement of mediation shall state that, if a party
alleging domestic violence in a written declaration under
penalty of perjury or a party protected by a protective order
so requests, the mediator will meet with the parties separately
and at separate times. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.
1500), § 116.87.)
[Revised Comment]
Section 3179 restates the last sentences of former Civil Code
Sections 4351.5(f) and 4607(e) without substantive change. Broader
references to Family Code sections have been substituted for the
references to former Civil Code
Sections 4600, 4600.5, and 4601.
