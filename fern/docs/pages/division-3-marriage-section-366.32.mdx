# 366.32

Long-term foster care; continuing jurisdiction

long-term foster care; continuing jurisdiction
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The first phase of expansion shall apply to a child who
is 10 years of age or older and placed with a nonrelative for
six months or longer.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The second phase of expansion shall apply to a child
who is 10 years of age or older and placed with a nonrelative
or in permanent placement relative care for six months or
longer.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The final phase of expansion shall apply to a child who
is 10 years of age or older and who has been in out-of-home
placement for six months or longer.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  With respect to a nonminor dependent, as defined in
subdivision <span class="subsec">(v)</span>  of Section 11400, who has a permanent plan of
long-term foster care that was ordered pursuant to Section
366.21, 366.22, 366.25, or 366.26, the court
may
continue
jurisdiction of the nonminor as a nonminor dependent of the
juvenile court or may dismiss dependency jurisdiction pursuant to Section 391.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the court continues dependency jurisdiction of the
nonminor as a nonminor dependent of the juvenile court, the
court shall order the development of a planned permanent
living arrangement under a mutual agreement, as described
in subdivision <span class="subsec">(u)</span>  of Section 11400, which may include
continued placement with the current caregiver or another
licensed or approved caregiver or in a supervised independent living placement, as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(w)</span>  of Section
11400, consistent with the youth’s Transitional Independent
Living Case Plan. At the request of the nonminor dependent
who has an established relationship with an adult determined
to be the nonminor dependent’s permanent connection, the
court may order nonminor dependent adoption pursuant to
subdivision <span class="subsec">(f)</span>  of Section 366.31 as the nonminor dependent’s
permanent plan.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  All phases of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall be subject to
appropriation through the budget process, Those appropriations shall apply only to the state’s share of costs. Counties
shall remain responsible for their nonfederal share of costs.
