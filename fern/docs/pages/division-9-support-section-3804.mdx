# § 3804

*Recordation of order*

Recordation of order
A deferred sale of home order may be recorded in the
office of the county recorder of the county in which the real
property is located.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
