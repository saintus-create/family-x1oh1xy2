# California Family Code § 8521

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Full-service adoption agency” means a licensed or authorized entity engaged in the business of providing adoption services, that does all of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Assumes care, custody, and control of a child through relinquishment of the child to the agency or involuntary termination of parental rights to the child.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Assesses the birth parents, prospective adoptive parents, or child.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Places children for adoption.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Supervises adoptive placements.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Recruits prospective adoptive parents, locates children for adoption, or acts as an intermediary between the parties to an adoption.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Private full-service adoption agencies shall be organized and operated on a nonprofit basis. As a condition of licensure to provide intercountry adoption services, a private full-service adoption agency shall be accredited by the Council on Accreditation, or supervised by an accredited primary provider, or acting as an exempted provider, in compliance with Subpart F (commencing with Section 96.29) of Part 96 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations.
