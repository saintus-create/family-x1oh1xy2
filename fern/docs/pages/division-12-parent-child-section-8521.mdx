# § 8521 — “Full-service adoption agency” defined

“Full-service adoption agency” defined
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Full-service adoption agency” means a licensed or
authorized
entity engaged in the business of providing
adoption services, that does all of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Assumes care, custody, and control of a child through
relinquishment of the child to the agency or involuntary
termination of parental rights to the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Assesses the birth parents, prospective adoptive par-
ents, or child.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Places children for adoption.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Supervises adoptive placements.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Private full-service adoption agencies shall be orga-
nized and operated on a nonprofit basis.

full-service
