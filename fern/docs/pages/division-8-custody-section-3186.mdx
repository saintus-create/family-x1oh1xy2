# § 3186 — Report of agreement;

Report of agreement;
ration of agreement in order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An agreement reached by the parties as a result of
mediation shall be reported to counsel for the parties by the
mediator on the day set for mediation or as soon thereafter as
practical, but before the agreement is reported to the court.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An agreement may not be confirmed or otherwise
incorporated in an order unless each party, in person or by
counsel of record, has affirmed and assented to the agree-
ment in open court or by written stipulation.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An agreement may be confirmed or otherwise incorpo-
rated in an order if a party fails to appear at a noticed hearing
on the issue involved in the agreement.
