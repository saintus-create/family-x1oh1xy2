# § 7645

ACT
Definitions
For purposes of this article, the following definitions shall
apply:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Child” means the child of a previously established
father or mother, as determined by the superior court in a
judgment that is the subject of a motion brought pursuant to
ber 24, 2006, and December 31, 2006, inclusive.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The motion was denied solely on the basis that it was
untimely.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The request for reconsideration of the motion is filed
on or before December 31, 2009. (Added by Stats.2004, c.
this article, or as a matter of law:
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Judgment” means a judgment, order, or decree
entered in a court of this state that establishes parentage,
including a determination of parentage made pursuant to a
849 (A.B.252), § 4. Amended by Stats.2008, c. 58 (S.B.1333),
§ 1; Stats.2018, c. 876 (A.B.2684), § 58, eff. Jan. 1, 2019;
Stats.2019, c. 115 (A.B.1817), § 93, eff. Jan. 1,:2020:)
petition filed under Section 300, 601, or 602 of the Welfare
and Institutions Code. For purposes of this article, “judgment” does not include a judgment in any action for marital
dissolution, legal separation, or nullity.
