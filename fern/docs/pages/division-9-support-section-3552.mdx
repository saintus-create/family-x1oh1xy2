# Support

## Section 3552

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

State and federal income tax returns; submission to court; examination and discovery 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a proceeding involving child, family, or spousal support, no party to the proceeding may refuse to submit copies of the party’s state and federal income tax returns to the court, whether individual or joint. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The tax returns may be examined by the other party and are discoverable by the other party. A party also may be examined by the other party as to the contents of a tax return submitted pursuant to this section. 
269 
§ 3552 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the court finds that it is relevant to the case to retain the tax return, the tax return shall be sealed and maintained as a confidential record of the court. 
If the court finds that the tax return is not relevant to disposition of the case, all copies of the tax return shall be returned to the party who submitted it.
