# § 3002.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

“Joint custody” means joint physical custody and joint legal custody. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
