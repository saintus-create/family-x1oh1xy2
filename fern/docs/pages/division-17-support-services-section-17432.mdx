# Support Services

## Section 17432

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Setting aside part of judgment or order concerning amount of child support to be paid 
(a) In any action filed by the local child support agency pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17400.">Section 17400</a>, 17402, or 17404, the court may, on any terms that may be just, set aside that part of the judgment or order concerning the amount of child support to be paid. 
This relief may be granted after the sixmonth time limit of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=473.">Section 473</a> of the Code of Civil Procedure has elapsed, based on the grounds, and within the time limits, specified in this section. 
(b) This section shall apply only to judgments or orders for support that were based upon presumed income as specified in subdivision (d) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17400.">Section 17400</a> and that were entered after the entry of the default of the defendant under
