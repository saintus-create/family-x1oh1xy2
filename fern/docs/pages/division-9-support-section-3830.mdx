# Support

## Section 3830

(a) On and after January 1, 1994, no court shall use any
computer software to assist in determining the appropriate
amount of child support or spousal support obligations,
unless the software conforms to rules of court adopted by the
Judicial Council prescribing standards for the software, which
shall ensure that it performs in a manner consistent with the
applicable statutes and rules of court for determination of
child support or spousal support.
