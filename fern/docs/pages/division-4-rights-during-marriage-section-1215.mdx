# § 1215 — Discharge on undertaking

Discharge on undertaking
The person arrested must be discharged from the arrest
upon executing and delivering to the officer, at any time
before the return day of the warrant, an undertaking to the
effect that the person arrested will appear on the return of
the warrant and abide the order of the court or judge
