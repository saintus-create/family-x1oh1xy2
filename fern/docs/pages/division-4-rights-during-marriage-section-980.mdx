# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 980

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

(commencing with Section 980) of this chapter (the privilege for
confidential marital communications).
A discussion of comparable exceptions may be found in the
Comments to the sections in Article 5 of this chapter. [7 Cal.L.Rev.
Comm. Reports 1 (1965)].
