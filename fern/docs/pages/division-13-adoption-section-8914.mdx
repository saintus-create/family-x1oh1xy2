# Adoption

## Section 8914

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Agency as party to petition; report of facts; submission to court If the licensed adoption agency is a party to or joins in the adoption petition, it shall submit a full report of the facts of the case to the court. 
The department may also submit a report.
