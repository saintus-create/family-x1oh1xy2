# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 13751

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Mandatory training programs Each family justice center established pursuant to subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13750.">Section 13750</a> shall maintain a formal training program with mandatory training for all staff members, volunteers, and agency professionals of not less than eight hours per year on subjects, including, but not limited to, privileges and confidentiality, information sharing, risk assessment, safety planning, victim advocacy, and high-risk case response.
