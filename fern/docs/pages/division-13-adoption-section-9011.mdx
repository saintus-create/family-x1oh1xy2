# Adoption

## Section 9011

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Authorization of appropriations There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of the Convention and this chapter.

100-300, 12, Apr. 29, 1988, 102 Stat. 442.)
