# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2320

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Entry of judgment of dissolution; entry of judgment for dissolution, nullity, or legal separation of a marriage between persons of the same sex. 
Conversion of separation proceeding to dissolution proceeding; notice. 
HN.
