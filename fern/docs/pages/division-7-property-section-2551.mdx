# § 2551 — By contrast, In re Marriage of Cantarella,

By contrast, In re Marriage of Cantarella,
191 Cal.App.4th 916, 119
Cal.Rptr.3d 829 (2011), interpreting the Family Law Act (FLA —
former Civil Code § 4000 et seq.), held that a couple’s 1991 duly
licensed and ceremonialized marriage was valid even though the
couple never registered the certificate of marriage. To the same
effect, see Chaney v. Netterstrom, 21 Cal.App.5th 61, 229 Cal.Rptr.3d
860 (2018), review denied (parties’ decision not to file solemnized
marriage license did not invalidate marriage).
