# § 3625 — Response to application; service; effect of filing

Response to application; service; effect of filing
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A response to the application for the proposed expedited support order and the obligated parent’s income and

expense declaration may be filed with the court at any time
before the effective date of the expedited support order and,
on filing, shall be served upon the applicant by any method by
which a response to a notice of motion may be served.
