# 2026. Reconciliation

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

Reconciliation
of con-
The reconciliation of the parties, whether conditional or
unconditional, is an ameliorating factor to be considered by
the court in considering a contempt of an existing court
order. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
1994.)
