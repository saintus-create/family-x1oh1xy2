# Support

## Section 4363

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

1(a) without substantive change. 
The reference to a proceeding under “this part,” meaning the former Family Law Act (former Part 5 (commencing with former <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4000.">§ 4000</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of the Civil Code), has been omitted as surplus. 
See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2000.">§ 2000</a> (application to dissolution, nullity, or legal separation proceedings). 
Subdivision (b) continues the last part of former Civil Code <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4351.">§ 4351</a> without substantive change. 
For an exception to this general rule, see <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5103.">§ 5103</a> (support order enforceable against employee pension benefit plan regardless of whether joined as party). 
See also Sections 80 (“employee pension benefit plan” defined), 755 
(discharge of employee benefit plan from adverse claims). 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
