# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1469

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

References to § 1220 deemed references to this chapter Where a provision of this division applies the provisions of this code applicable to personal representatives to proceedings under this division, a reference to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1220.">§ 1220</a> in the provisions applicable to personal representatives shall be deemed to be a reference to this chapter.
