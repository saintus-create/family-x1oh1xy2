# § 7503

*Payment of earnings to minor*

Payment of earnings to minor
The employer of a minor shall pay the earnings of the
minor to the minor until the parent or guardian entitled to
the earnings gives the employer notice that the parent or
guardian claims the earnings. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219
(A.B.1500), § 166.)
