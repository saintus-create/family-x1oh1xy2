# 290.2

Initial petition hearing; petition filed

Initial petition hearing; petition filed
Upon the filing of a petition by a probation officer or social
worker, the clerk of the juvenile court shall issue notice, to
which shall be attached a copy of the petition, and he or she
shall cause the same to be served as prescribed in this section.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notice shall be given to the following persons whose
address is known or becomes known prior to the initial
petition hearing:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The mother.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The father or fathers, presumed and alleged.
Amended by Stats.2003, c. 558 (A.B.579), 1; Stats.2006, c.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The legal guardian or guardians,
838 (S.B.678), 36; Stats.2015, c. 219 (A.B.879), 1, eff. Jan.
1, 2016; Stats.2017, c. 319 (A.B.976), 111, eff. Jan. 1, 2018;
c. 833 (A.B.3176), $ 9, eff: Jan. 1, 2019.)
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The Indian custodian, if it is known that the child is an
Indian child, as defined by Section 224.1.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The child, if the child is 10 years of age or older.
