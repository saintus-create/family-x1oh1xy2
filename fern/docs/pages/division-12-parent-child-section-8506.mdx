# § 8506 — “Agency adoption” defined

2 California Transactions Forms--Family Law § 6:3, Definitions.
“Agency adoption” defined
,
“Agency adoption” means the adoption of a minor, other
than an intercountry adoption, in which the department,
county adoption agency, or licensed adoption agency is a
party to, or joins in, the adoption petition. (Stats. 1992, c. 162
(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.

Section 8509 continues former Civil Code Section 220.20(c)
without change. See also Sections 8518 (“department” defined),
8530 (“licensed adoption agency” defined). [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.
Reports 1 (1993)].
In General.
