# § 8506 — “Agency adoption” means the adoption of a minor, other

“Agency adoption” means the adoption of a minor, other
than an intercountry adoption, in which the department,
county adoption agency, or licensed adoption agency is a
party to, or joins in, the adoption petition.
(Stats. 1992, c. 162
(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.
2012, c. 35 (S.B.1013), § 2, eff: June 27, 2012.)
