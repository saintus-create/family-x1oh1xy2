---
title: "4732. Adoption of forms or notices by Judicial Counsel"
description: "Adoption of forms or notices by Judicial Counsel"
slug: division-9-support-section-4732
---

The Judicial Council shall adopt forms or notices for the use of the procedures provided by this chapter.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
