# Support

## Section 4732

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Adoption of forms or notices by Judicial Counsel The Judicial Council shall adopt forms or notices for the use of the procedures provided by this chapter. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
