# 4732. Adoption of forms or notices by Judicial Counsel

*Support*

Adoption of forms or notices by Judicial Counsel
The Judicial Council shall adopt forms or notices for the
use of the procedures provided by this chapter.
(Stats. 1992,
c.
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
