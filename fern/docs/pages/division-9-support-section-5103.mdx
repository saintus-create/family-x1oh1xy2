# 5103 — Enforcement of support against employee pension

Support

Enforcement of support against employee pension
benefit plan
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding Section 2060, an order for the pay-
ment of child, family, or spousal support may be enforced
against an employee benefit plan regardless of whether the
plan has been joined as a party to the proceeding in which the
support order was obtained.
