# § 5700.504

*An employer that complies with an income-withholding*

An employer that complies with an income-withholding
order issued in another state in accordance with this chapter
is not subject to civil liability to an individual or agency with
regard to the employer’s withholding of child support from
the obligor’s income. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646),
