# § 9322.

*Part 3 — Adoption Of Adults And Married Minors · Chapter 2 — Procedure For Adult Adoption*

When the petition for approval of the adoption agreement is filed, the court clerk shall set the matter for hearing. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
