# § 4572 — Service upon depository institution and local child

Service upon depository institution and local child
support agency
The court shall cause a copy of its order to disburse and
replenish funds to be served upon the depository institution
where the child support security deposit is maintained, and
upon the child support agency with jurisdiction over the case.
