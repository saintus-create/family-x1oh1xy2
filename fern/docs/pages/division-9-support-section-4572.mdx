# § 4572 — Service upon depository institution and local child

The court shall cause a copy of its order to disburse and replenish funds to be served upon the depository institution where the child support security deposit is maintained, and upon the child support agency with jurisdiction over the case. _(Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 755, Sec. 6. Effective October 12, 2001.)_
