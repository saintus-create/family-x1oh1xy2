# § 4338 — Enforcement of order for support; property to be

Enforcement of order for support; property to be
utilized by court
In the enforcement of an order for spousal support, the
court shall resort to the property described below in the order
indicated:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The earnings, income, or accumulations
of either
spouse after the date of separation, as defined in Section 70,
which would have been community property if the spouse had
not been separated from the other spouse.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The community property.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The quasi-community property.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The other separate property of the party required to
make the support payments.
