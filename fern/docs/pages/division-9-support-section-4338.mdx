# Support

## Section 4338

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Enforcement of order for support; property to be utilized by court In the enforcement of an order for spousal support, the court shall resort to the property described below in the order indicated: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The earnings, income, or accumulations of either spouse after the date of separation, as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=70.">§ 70</a>, which would have been community property if the spouse had not been separated from the other spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The community property. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The quasi-community property. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The other separate property of the party required to make the support payments.
