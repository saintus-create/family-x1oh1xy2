# 7907 — Section 271 without

Parent and Child Relationship

Section 271 without
state
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Nothing in this section shall be construed to require
the county to have completed, within the applicable period,
those portions of the home study concerning the education
and training of the prospective foster parent or adoptive
subdivision (a).
Civil Code
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
