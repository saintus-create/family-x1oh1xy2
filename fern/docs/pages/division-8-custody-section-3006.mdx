# § 3006

*Sole legal custody*

Sole legal custody
“Sole legal custody” means that one parent shall have the
right and the responsibility to make the decisions relating to
the health, education, and welfare of a child.
(Stats. 1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
