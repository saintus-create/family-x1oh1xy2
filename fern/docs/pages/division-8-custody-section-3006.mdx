# Custody of Children

## Section 3006

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Sole legal custody 
“Sole legal custody” means that one parent shall have the right and the responsibility to make the decisions relating to the health, education, and welfare of a child. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
