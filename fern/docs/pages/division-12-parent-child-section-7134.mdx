# § 7134 — If the petition is sustained, the court shall forthwith issue

If the petition is sustained, the court shall forthwith issue
an order voiding or rescinding the declaration of emancipa-
tion, which shall be filed by the clerk of the court.
(Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amend-
ed by Stats.2002, c. 784 (S.B.1316),
