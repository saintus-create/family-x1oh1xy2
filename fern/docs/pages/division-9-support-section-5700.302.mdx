# 5700.302 — 10th Husband

Support

10th Husband
and Wife
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  determine the amount of any arrearages, and specify a
method of payment;

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  enforce orders by civil or criminal contempt, or both;
Proceeding by minor parent
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  set aside property for satisfaction of the support order;
A minor parent, or a guardian or other legal representative
of a minor parent, may maintain a proceeding on behalf of or
for the benefit of the minor’s child. (Added by Stats.2015, c.
493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff. Jan. 1, 2016.)
