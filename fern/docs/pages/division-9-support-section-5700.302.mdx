# § 5700.302

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 3 — Civil Provisions Of General Application*

A minor parent, or a guardian or other legal representative of a minor parent, may maintain a proceeding on behalf of or for the benefit of the minor’s child. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
