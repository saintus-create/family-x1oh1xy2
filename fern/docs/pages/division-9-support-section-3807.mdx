# 3807

Time for modification or termination of orders;

Except as otherwise agreed to by the parties in writing, a deferred sale of home order may be modified or terminated at any time at the discretion of the court. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
