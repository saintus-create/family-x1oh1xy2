# Support

## Section 3807

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Time for modification or termination of orders; exceptions Except as otherwise agreed to by the parties in writing, a deferred sale of home order may be modified or terminated at any time at the discretion of the court.
