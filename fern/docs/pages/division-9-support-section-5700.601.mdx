# § 5700.601

*A support order or income-withholding order issued in*

A support order or income-withholding order issued in
another state or a foreign support order may be registered in
this state for enforcement.
(Added by Stats.2015, c. 493
(S.B.646), § 5, eff. Jan. 1, 2016.)

any; and

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A request for a determination of which is the controlling order may be filed separately or with a request for
registration and enforcement or for registration and modification. The person requesting registration shall give notice
of the request to each party whose rights may be affected by
the determination.
