# § 5700.601

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 6 — Registration, Enforcement, And Modification Of Support Order*

A support order or income-withholding order issued in another state or a foreign support order may be registered in this state for enforcement. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
