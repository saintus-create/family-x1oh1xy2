# § 8914 — Agency as party to petition; report of facts; sub-

If the licensed adoption agency is a party to or joins in the adoption petition, it shall submit a full report of the facts of the case to the court. The department may also submit a report. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
