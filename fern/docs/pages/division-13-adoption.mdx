## Adoption

  <h3>Adoption of unmarried minor: petition</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 8600</Badge></div>
        <p>Any person who is at least 18 years of age and at least 10 years older than the adoptee may petition the court to adopt an unmarried minor.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Stepparent adoption</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 9000</Badge></div>
        <p>A stepparent may adopt the child of their spouse if the spouse is the custodial parent, or if both parents' consent is obtained or their rights are terminated.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Vacation of adoption</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 9100</Badge></div>
        <p>An adoption may be vacated on grounds of fraud, duress, or undue influence within the earlier of five years from the adoption decree or the child's attainment of majority.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
