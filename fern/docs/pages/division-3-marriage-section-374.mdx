# 374

Minor under age 12; appearance without counsel

Minor under age 12; appearance without counsel
when accompanied by guardian ad litem;
conditions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A minor under 12 years of age, accompanied by a duly
appointed and acting guardian ad litem, shall be permitted to
appear in court without counsel for the limited purpose of
requesting or opposing a request for <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  an injunction or
temporary restraining order or both to prohibit harassment
