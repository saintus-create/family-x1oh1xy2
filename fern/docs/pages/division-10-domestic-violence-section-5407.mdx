# § 5407 — Payment to minor

Payment to minor
If a financial institution is required or permitted to make
payment pursuant to this chapter.to a person who is a minor:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the minor is a party to a multiple-party account,
payment may be made to the minor or to the minor’s order,
and payment so made is a valid release and discharge of the
financial institution, but this subdivision does not apply if the
account is to be paid to the minor because the minor was
designated as a P.O.D. payee or as a beneficiary of a Totten
trust account.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In cases where subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  does not apply, payment
shall be made pursuant to the California Uniform Transfers
to Minors Act ( (commencing with Section 3900) of
), or as provided in Chapter 2 (commencing with
Section 3400) of Part 8 of .
