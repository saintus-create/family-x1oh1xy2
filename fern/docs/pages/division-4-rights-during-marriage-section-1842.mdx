<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The establishment of criteria to ensure that a child

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If an application is made to the family conciliation
court for conciliation proceedings in respect to a controversy
between spouses, or a contested proceeding for dissolution of
marriage, for nullity of a voidable marriage, or for legal
separation of the parties, but there is no minor child whose
welfare may be affected by the results of the controversy, and
it appears to the court that reconciliation of the spouses or
amicable adjustment of the controversy can probably be
achieved, and that the work of the court in cases involving
children will not be seriously impeded by acceptance of the
case, the court may accept and dispose of the case in the same
manner as similar cases involving the welfare of children are
disposed of.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the court accepts the case under subdivision (a), the
court has the same jurisdiction over the controversy and the
parties to the controversy and those having a relation to the
controversy that it has under this part in similar cases
involving the welfare of children. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.
