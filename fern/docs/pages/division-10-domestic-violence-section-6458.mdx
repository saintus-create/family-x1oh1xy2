# § 6458 — Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and

Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and
National Commerce Act
This part modifies, limits, or supersedes the federal Elec-
tronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (15
U.S.C. Sec. 7001 et seq.), but does not modify, limit, or
supersede Section 101(c) of that act (15 U.S.C. Sec. 7001(c)),
or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices de-
scribed in Section 103(b) of that act (15 U.S.C. Sec. 7003(b)).
