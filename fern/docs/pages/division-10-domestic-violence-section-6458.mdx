# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6458

Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act This part modifies, limits, or supersedes the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (15 U.S.C. Sec. 7001 et seq.), but does not modify, limit, or supersede <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=101.">§ 101</a>(c) of that act (15 U.S.C. Sec. 7001(c)), or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=103.">§ 103</a>(b) of that act (15 U.S.C. Sec. 7003(b)).
