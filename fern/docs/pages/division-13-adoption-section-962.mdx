# 962

Exception: Joint clients

Exception: Joint clients
Where two or more clients have retained or consulted a
lawyer upon a matter of common interest, none of them, nor
the successor in interest of any of them, may claim a privilege
under this article as to a communication made in the course
of that relationship when such communication is offered in a
civil proceeding between one of such clients (or his successor
in interest) and another of such clients (or his successor in
interest).
