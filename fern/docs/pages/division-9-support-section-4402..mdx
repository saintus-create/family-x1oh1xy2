# § 4402.

*Part 4 — Support Of Parents · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

The duty of support under this part is cumulative and not in substitution for any other duty. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
