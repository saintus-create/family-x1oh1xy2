# 6147 — compensation benefits

Prevention of Domestic Violence

compensation benefits
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An attorney who contracts to represent a client on a
contingency fee basis shall, at the time the contract is entered
into, provide a duplicate copy of the contract, signed by both
the attorney and the client, or the client’s guardian or
representative, to the plaintiff, or to the client’s guardian or
representative.
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A statement of the contingency fee rate that the client
and attorney have agreed upon.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A statement as to how disbursements and costs incurred in connection with the prosecution or settlement of
the claim will affect the contingency fee and the client’s
recovery.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A statement as to what extent, if any, the client could
be required to pay any compensation to the attorney for
related matters that arise out of their relationship not
covered by their contingency fee contract. This may include
any amounts collected for the plaintiff by the attorney.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Unless the claim is subject to the provisions of Section
6146, a statement that the fee is not set by law but is
negotiable between attorney and client.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  If the claim is subject to the provisions of Section 6146,
a statement that the rates set forth in that section are the
maximum limits for the contingency fee agreement, and that
the attorney and client may negotiate a lower rate.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Failure to comply with any provision of this section
renders the agreement voidable at the option of the plaintiff,
and the attorney shall thereupon be entitled to collect a
reasonable fee.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section shall not apply to contingency fee contracts for the recovery of workers’ compensation benefits.
