. 
Liability for tort of domestic violence 
(a) A person is liable for the tort of domestic violence if the plaintiff proves both of the following elements: 
(1) The infliction of injury upon the plaintiff resulting from abuse, as defined in subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13700.">Section 13700</a> of the Penal Code. 
(2) The abuse was committed by the defendant, a person having a relationship with the plaintiff as defined in subdivision (b) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13700.">Section 13700</a> of the Penal Code.
