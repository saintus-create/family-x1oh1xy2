. 
Liability for tort of domestic violence 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A person is liable for the tort of domestic violence if the plaintiff proves both of the following elements: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The infliction of injury upon the plaintiff resulting from abuse, as defined in subdivision (a) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13700.">§ 13700</a> of the Penal Code.
