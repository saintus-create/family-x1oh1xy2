# 5302

Sums remaining upon death of party

Sums remaining upon death of party
Subject to Section 5040: -

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Sums remaining on deposit at the death of a party to a
joint account belong to the surviving party or parties as
against the estate of the decedent unless there is clear and
convincing evidence of a different intent.
If there are two or
more surviving parties, their respective ownerships during
lifetime are in proportion to their previous ownership inter-
ests under Section 5301 augmented by an equal share for
each survivor of any interest the decedent may have owned in
the account immediately before the decedent’s death; and the
right of survivorship continues between the surviving parties.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the account is a P.O.D. account:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  On death of one of two or more parties, the rights to
any sums remaining on deposit are governed by subdivision
(a).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  On death of the sole party or of the survivor of two or
more parties, <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  any sums remaining on deposit belong to
the P.O.D. payee or payees if surviving, or to the survivor of
them if one or more die before the party, <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  if two or more
P.O.D. payees survive, any sums remaining on deposit belong
to them in equal and undivided shares unless the terms of the
account or deposit agreement expressly provide for different
shares, and <span class="subsec">(C)</span>  if two or more P.O.D. payees survive, there is
no right of survivorship in the event of death of a P.O.D.
payee thereafter unless the terms of the account or deposit
agreement expressly provide for survivorship between them.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Ifthe account is a Totten trust account:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  On death of one of two or more trustees, the rights to
any sums remaining on deposit are governed by subdivision
(a).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  On death of the sole trustee or the survivor of two or
more trustees, <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  any sums remaining on deposit belong to
the person or persons named as beneficiaries, if surviving, or
to the survivor of them if one or more die before the trustee,
unless there is clear and convincing evidence of a different
intent, <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  if two or more beneficiaries survive, any sums
remaining on deposit belong to them in equal and undivided
shares unless the terms of the account or deposit agreement
expressly provide for different shares, and <span class="subsec">(C)</span>  if two or more
beneficiaries survive, there is no right of survivorship in event
of death of any beneficiary thereafter unless the terms of the
account or deposit agreement expressly provide for survivor-
ship between them.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  In other cases, the death of any party to a multiparty
account has no effect on beneficial ownership of the account
other than to transfer the rights of the decedent as part of the
decedent’s estate.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A right of survivorship arising from the express terms
of the account or under this section, .a beneficiary designation
in a Totten trust account, or a P.O.D. payee designation,
cannot be changed by will.
