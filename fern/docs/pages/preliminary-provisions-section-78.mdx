# § 78 — Commentary

3, eff: Sept. 28, 2000;. Stats.2003, c. 308 (A.B.738),
§ 3; Stats.2019, c. 115 (A.B.1817), § 140, eff: Jan. 1, 2020.)
Commentary
Relying on the final sentence of subsection <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  and subsection (/),
In re Marriage of Lamoure, 198 Cal.App.4th 807, 132 Cal.Rptr.3d 1
(2011),
review denied, holds that a parent who has requested
enforcement by the Department of Child Support Services (DCSS)
may collect spousal support as well as child support arrearages under
the Financial Institution Data Match provisions (§ 17453 et seq.).
Enforcement need not be originally initiated by the DCSS; nor need
the parent be receiving public assistance. All that is required is that
the support obligee have requested DCSS enforcement and the
obligor have been ordered to send all support payments to DCSS
(§ 17400(a)).
