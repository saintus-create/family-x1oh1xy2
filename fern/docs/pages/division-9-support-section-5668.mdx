# § 5668 — Provisions

Provisions
applicable
to revocable
transfer
on
death deed of community property with right of surviv-
orship
A revocable transfer on death deed of community property
with right of survivorship is subject. to Section 5666, relating
to a revocable transfer on death deed of community property.
