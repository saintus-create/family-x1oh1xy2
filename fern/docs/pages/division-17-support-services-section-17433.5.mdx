# § 17433.5 — Child, spousal, family, or medical support due in a given

child, spousal, family, or medical support due in a given
month
In any action enforced pursuant to this article, no interest
shall accrue onan obligation for current child, spousal,
family, or medical support due in a given month until the first
day of the following month.
2006, c. 75
(A.B.1808), § 7, eff. July 12, 2006.)
