# Support Services

## Section 17433.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Interest accruing on obligation for current child, spousal, family, or medical support due in a given month In any action enforced pursuant to this article, no interest shall accrue onan obligation for current child, spousal, family, or medical support due in a given month until the first day of the following month.
