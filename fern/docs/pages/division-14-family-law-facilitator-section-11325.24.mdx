# 11325.24

From the assistance unit or exits the CalWORKs program,

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  If the parent or assisted caretaker has been removed
from the assistance unit or exits the CalWORKs program,
voluntary home visiting services may continue until comple-
tion of the evidence-based home visiting program or until
™ the parent or assisted caretaker relative terminates
their own participation.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  A county and the home visiting program may incorpo-
rate participation of the noncustodial parent of a child who is
a member of a CalWORKs assistance unit into home visiting
services, subject to the mutual agreement of the custodial and
noncustodial parents.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The following definitions shall apply for purposes of
this article:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Cultural competence’? means the ability to interact
effectively with people of different cultures.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Evidence-based home visiting” means a home visiting
model approved by the department, which shall be evaluated
considering criteria developed by the United States Depart-
ment of Health and Human Services for evidence-based
home visiting.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  “Home” means a temporary or permanent residence
or living space, or another location identified by the assis-
tance unit.
