# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8401

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

A person named executor in the will is under no duty to defend a contest of the will until appointment as executor. 
See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8250.">§ 8250</a> (summons). 
See also Sections 300-301 (trust company as personal representative), 9605 (appointment does not discharge any claim the decedent has against personal representative).
