# § 3429 — If the court

If the court
determines that a child custody proceeding has been com-
menced
in a court
in another
state having jurisdiction
substantially in accordance with this part, the court of this
state shall stay its proceeding and communicate with the
court of the other state.
If the court of the state having
jurisdiction substantially in accordance with this part does not
determine that the court of this state is a more appropriate
forum, the court of this state shall dismiss the proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In a proceeding to modify a child custody determina-
tion, a court of this state shall determine whether a proceed-
ing to enforce the determination has been commenced in
another state.
If a proceeding to enforce a child custody
determination has been commenced in another state, the
court may do any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Stay the proceeding for modification pending the entry
of an order of a court of the other state enforcing, staying,
denying,
or dismissing the proceeding for enforcement.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Enjoin the parties from continuing with the proceeding
for enforcement.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Proceed with the modification under conditions
it
considers appropriate.
