# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 115

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Property declaration 
“Property declaration” means the form for a property declaration in family law matters adopted by the Judicial Council.
