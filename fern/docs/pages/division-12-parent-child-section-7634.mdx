# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7634

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Action brought by local child support agency; review of current practices 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The local child support agency may, in the local child Support agency’s discretion, bring an action under this chapter in any case in which the local child support agency believes it to be appropriate. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The Department of Child Support Services may review the current practices of service of process used by the local child support agencies pursuant to subdivision (a), and may develop methods to increase the number of persons served using personal delivery.
