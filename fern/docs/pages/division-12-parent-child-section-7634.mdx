# § 7634 — Action brought by local child support agency;

Action brought by local child support agency;
review of current practices
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The local child support agency may, in the local child

agency’s
discretion, bring an
action under this
chapter in any case in which the local child support agency
believes it to be appropriate.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The Department of Child Support Services may review
the current practices of service of process used by the local
child support agencies pursuant to subdivision (a), and may
develop methods to increase the number of persons served
using personal delivery.
