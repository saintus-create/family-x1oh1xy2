<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A child support order issued or modified pursuant to
this division shall include a provision requiring the child
support obligor to keep the obligee informed of whether the
obligor has health insurance made available through the
obligor’s employer or has other group health insurance and, if
so, the health insurance policy information. The support
obligee under a child support order shall inform the support
obligor of whether the obligee has health insurance made
available through the employer or other group health insurance and, if so, the health insurance policy information.

this division shall include a provision requiring the child
support obligor and obligee to provide the information
described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  for a child or an adult who meets
the criteria for continuation of health insurance coverage
