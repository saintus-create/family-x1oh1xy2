# Support

## Section 3752.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Notice of availability of coverage; continuation of health insurance coverage upon child or adult attaining limiting age; modification of form of order by Judicial Council 
(a) A child support order issued or modified pursuant to this division shall include a provision requiring the child support obligor to keep the obligee informed of whether the obligor has health insurance made available through the obligor’s employer or has other group health insurance and, if so, the health insurance policy information. 
The support obligee under a child support order shall inform the support obligor of whether the obligee has health insurance made available through the employer or other group health insurance and, if so, the health insurance policy information. 
(b) A child support order issued or modified pursuant to this division shall include a provision requiring the child support obligor and obligee to provide the information described in subdivision (a) for a child or an adult who meets the criteria for continuation of health insurance coverage upon attaining the limiting age pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1373.">Section 1373</a> of the Health and Safety Code or <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=10277.">Section 10277</a> or 10278 of the Insurance Code.
