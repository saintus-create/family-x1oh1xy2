# § 7132 — Filing of petitions to rescind declarations

Filing of petitions to rescind declarations
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A petition to rescind a declaration of emancipation on
the ground that the minor is indigent and has no means of
support may be filed by the minor declared emancipated, by
the minor’s conservator, or by the district attorney of the
county in which the minor resides.
The petition shall be filed
in the county in which the minor or the conservator resides.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The minor may be considered indigent if the minor’s
only source of income is from public assistance benefits. The
court shall consider the impact of the rescission of the
declaration of emancipation on the minor and shall find the
rescission of the declaration of emancipation will not be
contrary to the best interest of the minor before granting the
order
to rescind.
