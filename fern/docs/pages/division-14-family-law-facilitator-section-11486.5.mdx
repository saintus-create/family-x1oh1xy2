# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 11486.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Eligibility for aid; fleeing prosecution or custody or confinement; violation of condition of probation or parole 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An individual shall not be eligible for aid under this chapter if he or she is either: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Fleeing to avoid prosecution, or custody and confinement after conviction, under the laws of the place from which the individual is fleeing, for a crime or an attempt to commit a crime that is a felony under the laws of the place from which the individual is fleeing, or which, in the case of the State of New Jersey, is a high misdemeanor under the laws of that state. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Violating a condition of probation or parole imposed under federal law or the law of any state. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Subdivision (a) shall not apply with respect to conduct of an individual for any month beginning after the President of the United States grants a pardon with respect to the conduct.
