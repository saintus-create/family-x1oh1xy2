# Support

## Section 3602

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Reconciliation Unless the order specifies otherwise, an order made pursuant to this chapter is not enforceable during any period in which the parties have reconciled and are living together.
