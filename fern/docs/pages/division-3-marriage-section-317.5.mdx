# 317.5

Proceeding is a party to that proceeding. (Added by Stats

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  All parties who are represented by counsel at dependency proceedings shall be entitled to competent counsel.
(S.B.783),
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Each minor who is the subject of a dependency
proceeding is a party to that proceeding. (Added by Stats.
1994, c. 1073 (S.B.783), 1.)
