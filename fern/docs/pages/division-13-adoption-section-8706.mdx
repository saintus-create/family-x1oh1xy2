# Adoption

## Section 8706

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Medical report; background of child and biological parents; contents; blood sample 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An agency may not place a child for adoption unless a written report on the child’s medical background and, if available, the medical background of the child’s biological parents so far as ascertainable, has been submitted to the prospective adoptive parents and they have acknowledged in writing the receipt of the report. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The report on the child’s background shall contain all known diagnostic information, including current medical reports on the child, psychological evaluations, and scholastic information, as well as all known information regarding the child’s developmental history and family life. 
(c)(1) The biological parents may provide a blood sample at a clinic or hospital approved by the State Department of Health Services. 
The biological parents’ failure to provide a blood sample shall not affect the adoption of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The blood sample shall be stored at a laboratory under contract with the State Department of Health Services for a period of 30 years following the adoption of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The purpose of the stored sample of blood is to provide a blood sample from which testing can be done at a later date after entry of the order of adoption at the request of the adoptive parents or the adopted child.
