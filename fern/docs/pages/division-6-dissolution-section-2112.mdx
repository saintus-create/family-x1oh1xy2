# 2112
The Judicial Council shall adopt appropriate forms and modify existing forms to effectuate the purposes of this chapter. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 14. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.
