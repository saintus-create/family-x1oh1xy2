# Minors

## Section 7001

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Purpose of part It is the purpose of this part to provide a clear statement defining emancipation and its consequences and to permit an emancipated minor to obtain a court declaration of the minor’s status. 
This part is not intended to affect the status of minors who may become emancipated under the decisional case law that was in effect before the enactment of Chapter 1059 of the Statutes of 1978.
