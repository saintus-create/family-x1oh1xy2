# § 7001 — Purpose of part

Purpose of part
It is the purpose of this part to provide a clear statement
defining emancipation and its consequences and to permit an
emancipated minor to obtain a court declaration of the
minor’s status.
This part is not intended to affect the status
of minors who may become emancipated under the decisional
case law that was in effect before the enactment of Chapter
1059 of the Statutes of 1978.
