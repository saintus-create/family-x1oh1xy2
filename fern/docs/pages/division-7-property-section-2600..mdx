# § 2600.

*Part 4 — Special Rules For Division Of Community Estate*

Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, the court may divide the community estate as provided in this part. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
