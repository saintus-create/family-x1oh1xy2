# § 224.4

*Intervention in proceedings by tribe*

Intervention in proceedings by tribe
The Indian child’s tribe and Indian custodian have the right
to intervene at any point in an Indian child custody proceed-
ing. (Added by Stats.2006, c. 838 (S.B.678), § 33.)
