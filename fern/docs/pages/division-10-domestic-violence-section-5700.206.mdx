# § 5700.206 — Initiating tribunal to request tribunal of anoth-

Initiating tribunal to request tribunal of anoth-
er state to enforce; continuing jurisdiction to enforce
child-support order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state that has issued a child-support
order consistent with the law of this state may serve as an
initiating tribunal to request a tribunal of another state to
enforce:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the order if the order is the controlling order and has
not been modified by a tribunal of another state that assumed
jurisdiction pursuant to the Uniform Interstate Family Sup-
port Act; or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  a money judgment for arrears of support and interest
on the order accrued before a determination that an order of
a tribunal of another state is the controlling order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A tribunal of this state having continuing jurisdiction
over a support order may act as a responding tribunal to
enforce the order.
