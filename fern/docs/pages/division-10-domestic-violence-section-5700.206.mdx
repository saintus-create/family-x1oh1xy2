# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.206

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Initiating tribunal to request tribunal of another state to enforce; continuing jurisdiction to enforce child-support order 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state that has issued a child-support order consistent with the law of this state may serve as an initiating tribunal to request a tribunal of another state to enforce: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the order if the order is the controlling order and has not been modified by a tribunal of another state that assumed jurisdiction pursuant to the Uniform Interstate Family Support Act; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  a money judgment for arrears of support and interest on the order accrued before a determination that an order of a tribunal of another state is the controlling order. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A tribunal of this state having continuing jurisdiction over a support order may act as a responding tribunal to enforce the order.
