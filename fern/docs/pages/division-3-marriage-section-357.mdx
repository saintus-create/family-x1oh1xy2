No acknowledgment or promise is sufficient evidence of a
new or continuing contract, by which to take the case out of
the operation of this title, unless the same is contained in
some writing, signed by the party to be charged thereby,
provided that any payment on account of principal or interest
due on a promissory note made by the party to be charged
shall be deemed a sufficient acknowledgment or promise of a
continuing contract to stop, from time to time as any such
payment is made, the running of the time within which an
action may be commenced upon the principal sum or upon
any installment of principal or interest due on such note, and
to start the running of a new period of time, but no such
payment of itself shall revive a cause of action once barred.
(Enacted in 1872.)
