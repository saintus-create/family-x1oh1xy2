# Support

## Section 4010

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Information on procedures to modify support order In a proceeding in which the court orders a payment for the support of a child, the court shall, at the time of providing written notice of the order, provide the parties with a document describing the procedures by which the order may be modified.
