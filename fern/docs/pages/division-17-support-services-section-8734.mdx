---
title: "8734. Adoption training programs"
description: "Adoption training programs"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-8734
---

The department shall encourage adoption agencies to make adoption training programs available to prospective adoptive families.

> *Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 983, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.*
