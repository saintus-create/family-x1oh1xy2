# § 17452 — Tax return information available to department;

Tax return information available to department;
no obligation or liability incurred; privacy and confi-
dentiality
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to state and federal privacy and information
security laws, the Franchise Tax Board shall make tax return
information available to the department, upon request, for
the purpose of collecting child support delinquencies referred
to the department.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of this article, the Franchise Tax Board
shall incur no obligation or liability to any person arising from
any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Furnishing information to the department as required
by this section.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Failing to disclose to a taxpayer or accountholder that
the name, address, social security number, or other taxpayer
identification number or other identifying information of that
person was included in the data exchange with the depart-
ment required by this section.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Any other action taken in good faith to comply with
the requirements of this section.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  It is the intent of the Legislature that any provision of
income tax return information by the Franchise Tax Board to
the department pursuant to this article shall be done in
accordance with the privacy and confidential information
laws of this state and of the United States, and to the
satisfaction of the Franchise Tax Board.
