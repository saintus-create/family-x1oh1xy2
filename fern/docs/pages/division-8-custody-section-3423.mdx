# 3423. Court of another state

*Custody of Children*

court of another state
,
made
by
Except as otherwise provided in Section 3424, a court of
this state may not modify a child custody determination made
by a court of another state unless a court of this state has
jurisdiction to make an initial determination under paragraph
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 3421 and either of the
following determinations is made:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court of the other state determines it no longer
has exclusive, continuing jurisdiction under Section 3422 or
that a court of this state would be a more convenient forum
under Section 3427.
Petition and Order to Appear.
,
Order.
