# California Family Code § 3423

Except as otherwise provided in Section 3424, a court of this state may not modify a child custody determination made by a court of another state unless a court of this state has jurisdiction to make an initial determination under paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 3421 and either of the following determinations is made:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court of the other state determines it no longer has exclusive, continuing jurisdiction under Section 3422 or that a court of this state would be a more convenient forum under Section 3427.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A court of this state or a court of the other state determines that the child, the child’s parents, and any person acting as a parent do not presently reside in the other state.
