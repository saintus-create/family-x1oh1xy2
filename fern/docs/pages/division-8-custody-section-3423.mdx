# Custody of Children

## Section 3423

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Modification of custody determination made by court of another state Except as otherwise provided in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3424.">Section 3424</a>, a court of this state may not modify a child custody determination made by a court of another state unless a court of this state has jurisdiction to make an initial determination under paragraph 
(1) or (2) of subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3421.">Section 3421</a> and either of the following determinations is made: 
(a) The court of the other state determines it no longer has exclusive, continuing jurisdiction under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3422.">Section 3422</a> or that a court of this state would be a more convenient forum under
