# Family Law Facilitator Act

Income in excess of amount for education and support; application to creditors’ claim Notwithstanding a restraint on transfer of a beneficiary’s interest in the trust under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=15300.">§ 15300</a> or 15301, any amount to which the beneficiary is entitled under the trust instrument or that the trustee, in the exercise of the trustee’s discretion, has determined to pay to the beneficiary in excess of the amount that is or will be necessary for the education and support of the beneficiary may be applied to the satisfaction of a money judgment against the beneficiary.
