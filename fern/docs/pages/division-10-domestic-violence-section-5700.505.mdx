# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.505

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Penalties for noncompliance An employer that willfully fails to comply with an incomewithholding order issued in another state and received for enforcement is subject to the same penalties that may be imposed for noncompliance with an order issued by a tribunal of this state.
