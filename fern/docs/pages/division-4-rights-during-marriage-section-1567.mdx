# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1567

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Reality or freedom of consent; causes for defeating , . 
An apparent consent is not real or free when obtained through: 1. 
Duress; 2. 
Menace; 3. 
Fraud; 4. Undue influence; or, 5. 
Mistake. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
