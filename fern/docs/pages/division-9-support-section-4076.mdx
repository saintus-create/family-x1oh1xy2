Modification of child support orders; two-step phase-in of formula amount of support; conditions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When the court is requested to modify a child support order issued prior to July 1, 1992, for the purpose of conforming to the statewide child support guideline, and it is not using its discretionary authority to depart from the 

§ 4076 guideline pursuant to paragraph (3), (4), or (5) of subdivision 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4057.">§ 4057</a>, and the amount of child support to be ordered is the amount provided under the guideline formula in subdivision (a) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4055.">§ 4055</a>, the court may, in its discretion, order a two-step phase-in of the formula amount of support to provide the obligor with time for transition to the full formula amount if all of the following are true: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The period of the phase-in is carefully limited to the time necessary for the obligor to rearrange the obligor’s financial obligations in order to meet the full formula amount of support. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The obligor is immediately being ordered to pay not less than 30 percent of the amount of the child support increase, in addition to the amount of child support required under the prior order.
