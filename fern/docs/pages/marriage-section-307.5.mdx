# General Provisions

## Section 307.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Taking minor to community service program for abused or neglected children; employees of program; notice to parent or guardian Notwithstanding the provisions of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=307.">Section 307</a>, an officer who takes a minor suspected of being a person described in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">Section 300</a> into temporary custody pursuant to subdivision 
(a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=305.">Section 305</a> may, in a case where he or she deems that it is in the best interest of the minor and the public, take the minor to a community service program for abused or neglected children.
