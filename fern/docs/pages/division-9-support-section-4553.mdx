# Support

## Section 4553

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Construction of chapter; compliance with federal law Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to permit any action or omission by the state or any of its political subdivisions that would place the state in noncompliance with any requirement of federal law, including, but not limited to, the state reimbursement requirements of Part D of Title IV OF of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 651 et seq.) and any regulations promulgated thereunder.
