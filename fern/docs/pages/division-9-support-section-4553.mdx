# § 4553 — Construction of chapter; compliance with federal

Construction of chapter; compliance with federal
law
Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to permit any
action or omission by the state or any of its political
subdivisions that would place the state in noncompliance with
any requirement of federal law, including, but not limited to,
the state reimbursement requirements of Part D of Title IV

ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS
of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 651 et seq.) and any
regulations promulgated thereunder.
