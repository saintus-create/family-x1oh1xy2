# § 17305 — The new system with minimal disruption of services, the

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In order to achieve an orderly and timely transition to
the new system with minimal disruption of services, the
director shall begin the transition from the office of the
district attorney to the local child support agencies pursuant
to Section 17304, commencing January 1, 2001. The director
shall transfer the appropriate number of counties, equaling at
least 50 percent of the statewide caseload into the new system
by January 1, 2002. The transition shall be completed by
January 1, 2003. A county that has appointed an administrator for the local child support agency and has complied with
the requirements of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  may transition prior to
January 1, 2001, subject to the approval of the director. In
determining the order in which counties will be transferred
from the office of the district attorney to the local child
support agencies, the director shall do all of the following:
