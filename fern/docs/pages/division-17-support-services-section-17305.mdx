# Support Services

## Section 17305

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Transition from district attorney to local agency 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In order to achieve an orderly and timely transition to the new system with minimal disruption of services, the director shall begin the transition from the office of the district attorney to the local child support agencies pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17304.">§ 17304</a>, commencing January 1, 2001. The director shall transfer the appropriate number of counties, equaling at least 50 percent of the statewide caseload into the new system by January 1, 2002. 
The transition shall be completed by January 1, 2003. A county that has appointed an administrator for the local child support agency and has complied with the requirements of subdivision (b) may transition prior to January 1, 2001, subject to the approval of the director. 
In determining the order in which counties will be transferred from the office of the district attorney to the local child support agencies, the director shall do all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Consider the performance of the counties in establishing and collecting child support. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Minimize the disruption of the services provided by the counties. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Optimize the chances of a successful transition.
