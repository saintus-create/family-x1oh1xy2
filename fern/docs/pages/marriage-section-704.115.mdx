# 704.115. See Section 82 (“trusts”

*Marriage*

See Section 82 (“trusts”
defined to exclude trusts for the primary purpose of paying pensions).
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  is drawn from Section 156(2) of the Restatement
(Second) of Trusts (1957).
The limitation on the amount that may
be reached by transferees and creditors to the proportionate amount
of the settlor’s contribution is drawn from Wisconsin law.
See
Wis.Stat.Ann.
