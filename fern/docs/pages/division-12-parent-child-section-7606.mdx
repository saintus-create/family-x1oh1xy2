# § 7606 — “Assisted reproduction” and “assisted reproduc-

“Assisted reproduction” and “assisted reproduc-
tion agreement” defined
As used in this part, the following definitions shall apply:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Assisted reproduction” means conception by any
means other than sexual intercourse.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Assisted reproduction agreement” means a written
contract that includes a person who intends to be the legal
parent of a child or children born through assisted reproduc-
tion and that defines the terms of the relationship between
the parties to the contract.
