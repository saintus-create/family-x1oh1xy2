# 7606

“Assisted reproduction” and “assisted reproduc-

As used in this part, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “Assisted reproduction” means conception by any means other than sexual intercourse.

(b) “Assisted reproduction agreement” means a written contract that includes a person who intends to be the legal parent of a child or children born through assisted reproduction and that defines the terms of the relationship between the parties to the contract. Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 806, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.<div class="fl-section-nav">
