# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8461

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Subdivision (b) gives priority to devisees, who need not be entitled to succeed to all or part of the estate under the law of succession in order to have priority. However, subdivision (b) 
also gives the court discretion to disregard the priority of a devisee in an appropriate case where another person would take a substantial statutory intestate or omitted heir share. 
See also Sections 140-147 
(surviving spouse’s waiver of rights at death), 9605 (appointment does not discharge claim decedent has against personal representative).
