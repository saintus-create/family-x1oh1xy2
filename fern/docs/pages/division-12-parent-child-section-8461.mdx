# § 8461

*Section 8461 was added by 1988 Cal.Stat. ch. 1199 § 81.5. The*

Section 8461 was added by 1988 Cal.Stat. ch. 1199 § 81.5. The
section restated a portion of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of former Probate Code
Section 422 (repealed by 1988 Cal.Stat» ch. 1199 § 45), with the
addition of subdivisions (d), (g), (h), and <span class="subsec">(i)</span>  to reflect changes in the
law governing intestate succession. See Section 6402 (intestate share
of heirs other than surviving spouse). Section 8461 was amended by
1989 Cal.Stat. ch. 544 § 8 to conform the priorities for appointment
as administrator more closely to the priorities to take from the
decedent by intestate succession.
See Section 6402. For background on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part
under the part heading. [20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].

Subject to the provisions of this article, a person in the
following relation to the decedent is entitled to appointment
as administrator in the following order of priority:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Surviving spouse or domestic partner as defined in
Section 37.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Children.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Grandchildren.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Other issue.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Parents.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Brothers and sisters.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Issue of brothers and sisters.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Grandparents.
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  Issue of grandparents.
<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  Children of a predeceased spouse or domestic partner.
<span class="subsec">(k)</span>  Other issue of a predeceased spouse or domestic
partner.
