# § 3660 — Purpose of article

The purpose of this article is to permit inexpensive discovery of facts before the commencement of a proceeding for modification or termination of an order for child, family, or spousal support. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
