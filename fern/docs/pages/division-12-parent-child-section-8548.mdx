---
title: "8548. “Stepparent adoption” means an adoption of a child by a"
description: "“Stepparent adoption” means an adoption of a child by a"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-8548
---

“Stepparent adoption” means an adoption of a child by a stepparent where one birth parent retains custody and control of the child.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
