# § 8548

*Section 8543 continues without substantive change part of former*

Section 8543 continues without substantive change part of former
Civil Code Section 220.20 added by Section 1 of Chapter 472 of the
Statutes of 1992, but inadvertently chaptered out by amendments to
the same section made by Section 2 of Chapter 1353 of the Statutes
of 1992. [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
“Stepparent adoption” defined
“Stepparent adoption” means an adoption of a child by a
stepparent where one birth parent retains custody and control
of the child.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
$ 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
