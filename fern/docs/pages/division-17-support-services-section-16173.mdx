# § 16173

*(Added by Stats.1999, c. 887 (S.B.1270), § 9.)*

(Added by Stats.1999, c. 887 (S.B.1270), § 9.)
Authorization
The State Department of Social Services and the State
Department of Health Services are authorized to develop,
participate in the development of, negotiate, or enter into
one or more interstate compacts on behalf of this state with
other states to implement one or more of the purposes set
forth in this chapter. When entered into, and for so long as
it shall remain in force, a compact shall have the force and
effect of law. (Added by Stats.1999, c. 887 (S.B.1270), § 9.)
