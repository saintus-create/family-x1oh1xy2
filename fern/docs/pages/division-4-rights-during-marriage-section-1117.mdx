# 1117 — Application of chapter

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

Application of chapter
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (b), this chapter
applies to a mediation as defined in Section 1115.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This chapter does not apply to either of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A proceeding under (commencing with Section
1800) of of the Family Code or Chapter 11
§ 14:30,
Confidentiality of Negotiations.
