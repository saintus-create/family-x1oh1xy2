# § 4404.

*Part 4 — Support Of Parents · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

In determining the amount to be ordered for support, the court shall consider the following circumstances of each party:

(a) Earning capacity and needs.

(b) Obligations and assets.

(c) Age and health.

(d) Standard of living.

(e) Other factors the court deems just and equitable. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
