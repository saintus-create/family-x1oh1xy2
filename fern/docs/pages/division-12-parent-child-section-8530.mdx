# § 8530

“Licensed adoption agency” means any agency licensed by the department to provide the adoption services specified in paragraphs <span class="subsec">(9)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(10)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 1502 of the Health and Safety Code.
