# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2345

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Consent to legal separation The court may not render a judgment of the legal separation of the parties without the consent of both parties 161 
§ 2346 unless one party has not made a general appearance and the petition is one for legal separation.
