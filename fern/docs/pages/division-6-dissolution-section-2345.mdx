# 2345

Consent to legal separation

The court may not render a judgment of the legal separation of the parties without the consent of both parties unless one party has not made a general appearance and the petition is one for legal separation. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
