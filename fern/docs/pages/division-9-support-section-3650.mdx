# § 3650

*Support order*

Support order
Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, as used
in this chapter, “support order” means a child, family, or
spousal support order. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219
(A.B.1500), § 124.5.)
