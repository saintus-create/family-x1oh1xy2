# § 16125 — Foster child whose adoption becomes final who is

Foster child whose adoption becomes final who is
receiving Adoption Assistance Program assistance; pro-
viding mental health services; identification of barriers
to receiving care
A foster child whose adoption has become final, who is
receiving or is eligible to receive Adoption Assistance Pro-
gram assistance, including Medi-Cal, and whose foster care
court supervision has been terminated, shall be provided
medically necessary specialty mental health services by the
local mental health plan in the county of residence of his or
her adoptive parents, pursuant to all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The host county mental health plan shall be responsi-
ble for submitting the treatment authorization request (TAR)
to the mental health plan in the county of origin.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The requesting public or private service provider shall
prepare the TAR.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The county of origin shall retain responsibility for
authorization and reauthorization of services utilizing an
expedited TAR process.
(d)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The State Department of Social Services
shall
convene
a stakeholder group to identify barriers to the
provision of mental health services by mental health profes-
sionals with specialized
clinical training in adoption
or
permanency issues to children who are receiving services
pursuant
to this section.
The stakeholder group
shall
include, but is not limited to, all of the following persons:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Adoptive parents.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Former foster youth.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Representatives from the mental health and child
welfare fields, including associations representing county
mental health departments and private organizations provid-
ing specialty mental health services.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  Representatives from mental health and social work
graduate degree-granting postsecondary education institu-
tions.
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  Representatives from relevant state and local agencies.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The stakeholder group shall, on or before January 31,
2016, make specific recommendations for voluntary measures
available to state and local government agencies and private
entities, as appropriate, to address those barriers.
The
department shall collect existing research and professional
literature
pertinent to the need for specialized
clinical
training in adoption and permanency issues, and shall distrib-
ute the information to the stakeholder group for consider-
ation and use in making its recommendations.
The stake-
holder group shall coordinate with, and endeavor not to
duplicate, existing local, state, or national initiatives.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A recommendation made pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span> 
shall not be construed to be binding on any state or local
government agency or private entity. (Added by Stats.2007, c.
