# § 2081

*Grounds for denial*

Grounds for denial
The restoration of a former name or birth name requested
under Section 2080 shall not be denied <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  on the basis that
the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different
name or <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  for any other reason other than fraud. (Stats.
