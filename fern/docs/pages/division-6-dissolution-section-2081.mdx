# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2081

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

The restoration of a former name or birth name requested under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2080.">§ 2080</a> shall not be denied (a) on the basis that the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different name or (b) for any other reason other than fraud. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats. 1996, c. 1061 (S.B.1033),
