# § 2081 — The restoration of a former name or birth name requested

The restoration of a former name or birth name requested
under Section 2080 shall not be denied <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  on the basis that
the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different
name or <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  for any other reason other than fraud.
(Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amend-
ed by Stats. 1996, c. 1061 (S.B.1033),
