---
title: "2081. The restoration of a former name or birth name requested"
description: "The restoration of a former name or birth name requested"
slug: division-6-dissolution-section-2081
---

The restoration of a former name or birth name requested under Section 2080 shall not be denied on the basis that the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different name, or for any other reason except fraud. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 780, Sec. 3. (SB 1150) Effective January 1, 2025.*
