# § 2081 — The restoration of a former name or birth name requested

The restoration of a former name or birth name requested under Section 2080 shall not be denied on the basis that the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different name, or for any other reason except fraud. _(Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 780, Sec. 3. (SB 1150) Effective January 1, 2025.)_
