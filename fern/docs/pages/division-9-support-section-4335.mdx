# § 4335 — Termination of spousal support order; extension

An order for spousal support terminates at the end of the period provided in the order and shall not be extended unless the court retains jurisdiction in the order or under Section 4336. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
