# § 17318 — Except as otherwise expressly provided, (commenc-

Application of Government Code § 11000 et seq.
Except as otherwise expressly provided, (commenc-
ing with Section 11000) of of Title 2 of the
Government Code, as it may be added to or amended from
time to time, shall apply to the conduct of the department.
