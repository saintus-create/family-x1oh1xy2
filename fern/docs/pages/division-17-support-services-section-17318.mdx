# § 17318 — Be’ made available pursuant to the California Code of

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The department’s regulations and other materials shall
be’ made available pursuant to the California Code of
Regulations and in the same manner as are materials of the
State Department of Social Services under the provisions of
Section 205.70 of Title 45 of the Code of Federal Regulations. (Added by Stats.1999, c. 478 (A.B.196), § 1. Amended
by Stats.1999, c. 480 (S.B.542), § 12.)
Except as otherwise expressly provided, (commencing with Section 11000) of of Title 2 of the
Government Code, as it may be added to or amended from
time to time, shall apply to the conduct of the department.
1999, c. 478 (A.B.196), § 1.)
