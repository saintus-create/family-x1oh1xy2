# Support Services

## Section 17318

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Application of Government Code § 11000 et seq. 
Except as otherwise expressly provided, Part 1 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11000.">Section 11000</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=3.">Division 3</a> of Title 2 of the Government Code, as it may be added to or amended from time to time, shall apply to the conduct of the department.
