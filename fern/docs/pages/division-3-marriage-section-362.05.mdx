# § 362.05 — Extracurricular, enrichment, and social activities

Extracurricular, enrichment, and social activities
for dependent children
(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Every child adjudged a dependent child of the
juvenile court shall be entitled to participate in age-appropri-
ate extracurricular, enrichment, and social activities, includ-
ing, but not limited to, access to computer technology and the
Internet.
A state or local regulation or policy shall not
prevent, or create barriers to, participation in those activities.
Each state and local entity shall ensure that private agencies
that provide foster care services to dependent children have
policies consistent with this section and that those agencies
