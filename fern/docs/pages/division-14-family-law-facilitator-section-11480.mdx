# § 11480 — Receipt or use of aid for purpose other than

Receipt or use of aid for purpose other than

Any person other than a needy child, who willfully and
knowingly receives or uses any part of an aid grant paid
pursuant to this chapter for a purpose other than support of
the needy children and the caretaker involved, is guilty of a
misdemeanor.
