# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1921

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Higher State or Federal standard applicable to protect rights of parent or Indian custodian of Indian child In any case where State or Federal law applicable to a child custody proceeding under State or Federal law provides a higher standard of protection to the rights of the parent or Indian custodian of an Indian child than the rights provided under this subchapter, the State or Federal court shall apply the State or Federal standard. 
(Pub.L. 95-608, Title I, 111, Nov. 8, 1978, 92 Stat. 3075.)
