# § 3178 — Restrictions on mediation agreements

Restrictions on mediation agreements
An agreement reached by the parties as a result of
mediation shall be limited as follows:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Where mediation is required to settle a contested issue
of custody or visitation, the agreement shall be limited to the
resolution of issues relating to parenting plans, custody,
visitation, or a combination of these issues.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Where a stepparent or grandparent seeks visitation
rights, the agreement shall be limited to the resolution of
issues relating to visitation.
