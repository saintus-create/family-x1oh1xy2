In re A.A., 114 Cal.App.4th 771, 7 Cal.Rptr.3d 755 (2003), review

child’s biological father was a presumed father and that the man who
held the child out as his own was merely a de facto parent. In other
words, the court’s determination of biological fatherhood in favor of
one man does not necessarily defeat another man’s presumed father
status when the two issues arise in the same action. The court
reasoned that § 7612(c) provides that a judgment establishing
