# § 7852 — “Qualified court investigator” defined

“Qualified court investigator,” as used in this article, has the meaning provided by Section 8543. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 182. Effective January 1, 1994.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
