---
title: "7852. “Qualified court investigator” defined"
description: "“Qualified court investigator” defined"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-7852

“Qualified court investigator,” as used in this article, has the meaning provided by Section 8543.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 182. Effective January 1, 1994.*
