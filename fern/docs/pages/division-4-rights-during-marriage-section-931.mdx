---
title: "931. Application of provisions governing reimbursement"
description: "Application of provisions governing reimbursement"
slug: division-4-rights-during-marriage-section-931
---

The provisions of this part that govern reimbursement apply to all debts, regardless of whether satisfied before, on, or after January 1, 1985.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
