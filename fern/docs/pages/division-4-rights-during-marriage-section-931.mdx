# § 931 — Application of provisions governing reimbursement

The provisions of this part that govern reimbursement apply to all debts, regardless of whether satisfied before, on, or after January 1, 1985. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
