# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5146

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Receives (order or notice received by institution) 
Except to the extent the terms of the account or deposit agreement expressly provide otherwise, a financial institution 
“receives” an order or notice under this part when it is received by the particular office or branch office of the financial institution where the account is carried. 
(Stats. 
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July I, 1991.)
