# 6527

Modification, terms and conditions; termination;

Modification, terms and conditions; termination;
petition for order; notice of hearing
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may by order modify the term or conditions
of the probate homestead right or terminate the probate
homestead right at any time prior to entry of an order for
final distribution of the decedent’s estate if in the court’s
discretion to do so appears appropriate under the circum-
stances of the case.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A petition for an order under this section may be filed
by any of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The person for whose use the probate homestead is set
apart.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The testate or intestate successors of the decedent or
other successors
to the property set apart as a probate
homestead.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Persons having claims secured by liens or encum-
brances on the property set apart. as a probate homestead.
