# 8509 — “Applicant” means a person who has submitted a written

Parent and Child Relationship

“Applicant” means a person who has submitted a written
application to adopt a child from the department, county
adoption agency, or licensed adoption agency and who is
being considered by the adoption agency for the adoptive
placement of a child. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.2012, c. 35 (S.B.
10 ,
Adoption Service Providers.
