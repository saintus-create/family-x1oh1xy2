# 7505. Parental authority; termination

*Parent and Child Relationship*

Parental authority; termination

The authority of a parent ceases on any of the following:
,
In General.
,
Agreement to Support Adult Child.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The appointment, by a court, of a guardian of the
person of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The marriage of the child.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The child attaining the age of majority.
c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 168.)
