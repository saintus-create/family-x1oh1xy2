# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7505

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Parental authority; termination The authority of a parent ceases on any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The appointment, by a court, of a guardian of the person of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The marriage of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The child attaining the age of majority. 
Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
