# § 2626

*Comments*

Comments
The court has jurisdiction to order reimbursement in cases
it deems appropriate for debts paid after separation but
before trial. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
[Revised Comment]
Section 2624 continues former Civil Code Section 4800(c)<span class="subsec">(4)</span> 
without substantive change.
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1
(1993)].
