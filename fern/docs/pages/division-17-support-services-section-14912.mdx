# § 14912 — Responsibilities of the Secretary of State

Responsibilities of the Secretary of State
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Liaison responsibilities
The Secretary shall have responsibility for—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  liaison with the central authorities of other Con-

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the coordination of activities under the Conven-

States.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Accreditation and approval responsibilities
The Secretary shall carry out the functions prescribed by
the Convention with respect to the accreditation of agencies
and the approval of persons to provide adoption services in
the United States in cases subject to the Convention as
provided in subchapter II. Such functions may not be dele-
gated to any other Federal agency.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Establishment of registry
The Secretary and the Attorney General shall jointly
establish a case registry of all adoptions involving immigra-
tion of children into the United States and emigration of
children from the United States, regardless of whether the
adoption occurs under the Convention.

permit tracking of pending cases and retrieval of information
on both pending and closed cases.
