# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8422

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Power in will to designate executor or coexecutor; bond; designation; executor authority to name coexecutor or successor. 
Court appointment of fewer than all persons named in will as executors; authority of appointees.
