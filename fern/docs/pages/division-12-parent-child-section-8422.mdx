# 8422 — Power in will to designate executor or coexecutor;

Parent and Child Relationship

Power in will to designate executor or coexecutor;
bond; designation; executor authority to name coex-
ecutor or successor.
8423.
Trust company named as executor;
sale to, merger
with, etc., another trust company.
8424.
Minor named as executor.
8425.
Court appointment of fewer than all persons named in
will as executors; authority of appointees.
