# § 20002.

*Part 1 — Family Law Pilot Projects · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

The duration of the pilot projects shall be two years. (Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
