# § 2941

*The public guardian may, if necessary and in the public*

The public guardian may, if necessary and in the public
guardian’s discretion, employ private attorneys where the cost
of employment can be defrayed out of estate funds or where
satisfactory pro bono or contingency fee arrangements can be
made.
