---
title: "235. Nothing in this part precludes either party from applying to"
description: "Nothing in this part precludes either party from applying to"
slug: marriage-section-235
---

Nothing in this part precludes either party from applying to the court for modification or revocation of the temporary restraining order provided for in this part or for further temporary orders or an expanded temporary ex parte order.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994. FAMFamily Code - FAM*
