# § 2099 — Business and Professions Code, or an individual duly licensed

5) of Chapter 5 of of the
Business and Professions Code, or an individual duly licensed
as a physician, surgeon, or osteopathic physician or surgeon
in another
state
or jurisdiction, who directly treats
or
supervises the treatment of the serious health condition.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Any other person determined by the United States
Secretary of Labor to be capable of providing health care
services under the FMLA.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  “Parent” means a biological, foster, or adoptive parent,
a stepparent, a legal guardian, or other person who stood in
loco parentis to the employee when the employee was a child.
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  “Serious health condition” means an illness, injury,
impairment, or physical or mental condition that involves
either of the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Inpatient care in a hospital, hospice, or residential
health care facility.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Continuing treatment or continuing supervision by a
health care provider.

for any leave taken pursuant to subdivision (a), except as
required by subdivision (e).
