# 8925

Hague adoption certificate or custody declaration

A Hague adoption certificate or, in outgoing cases, a Hague custody declaration, obtained pursuant to Part 97 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations shall be recognized as a final valid adoption for purposes of all state and local laws. Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 583, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2008.<div class="fl-section-nav">
