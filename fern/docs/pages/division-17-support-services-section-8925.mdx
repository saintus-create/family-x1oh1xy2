# § 8925

*Hague adoption certificate or custody declaration*

Hague adoption certificate or custody declaration
A Hague adoption certificate or, in outgoing cases, a
Hague custody declaration, obtained pursuant to Part 97 of
Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations shall be
recognized as a final valid adoption for purposes of all state
and local laws. (Added by Stats.2007, c. 583 (S.B.703), § 10.)
