(a)  Except in the cases described in subdivision (b), a
contract for the sale of personal property is not enforceable
by way of action or defense beyond five thousand dollars
($5,000) in amount or value of remedy unless there is some
record, as defined in subdivision (m)  of Section 1633.2, but
