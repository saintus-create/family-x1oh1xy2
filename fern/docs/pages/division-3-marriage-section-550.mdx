# 550
Notwithstanding any other law, including, but not limited to, Section 420, a county clerk may issue a marriage license, including a confidential marriage license, or solemnize or witness a marriage ceremony under state law using remote technology pursuant to this part, except for a marriage of a minor, as set forth in Sections 302 to 304, inclusive, or any successor provisions. _(Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 620, Sec. 5. (AB 583) Effective October 7, 2021.)_
