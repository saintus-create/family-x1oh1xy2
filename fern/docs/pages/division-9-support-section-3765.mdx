# 3765. Motion to quash assignment; grounds

*Support*

Motion to quash assignment; grounds
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The obligor may move to quash a health insurance
coverage assignment order as provided in this section if the
obligor declares under penalty of perjury that there is error
on any of the following grounds:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  No order to maintain health insurance has been issued
under Article 1 (commencing with Section 3750).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The amount to be withheld for premiums is greater
than that permissible under Article 1 (commencing with
Section 3750) or greater than the amount otherwise ordered
by the court.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The amount of the increased premium is unreasonable.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The alleged obligor is not the obligor from whom
health insurance coverage is due.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The child is or will be otherwise provided health care
coverage.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The employer’s choice of coverage is inappropriate.
