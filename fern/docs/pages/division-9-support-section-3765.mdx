# California Family Code § 3765

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The obligor may move to quash a health insurance coverage assignment order as provided in this section if the obligor declares under penalty of perjury that there is error on any of the following grounds:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  No order to maintain health insurance has been issued under Article 1 (commencing with Section 3750).

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The amount to be withheld for premiums is greater than that permissible under Article 1 (commencing with Section 3750) or greater than the amount otherwise ordered by the court.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The amount of the increased premium is unreasonable.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The alleged obligor is not the obligor from whom health insurance coverage is due.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The child is or will be otherwise provided health care coverage.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The employer’s choice of coverage is inappropriate.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The motion and notice of motion to quash the assignment order, including the declaration required by subdivision (a), shall be filed with the court issuing the assignment order within 15 days after delivery of a copy of the order to the obligor pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 3764. The court clerk shall set the motion for hearing not less than 15 days, nor more than 30 days, after receipt of the notice of motion. The clerk shall, within five days after receipt of the notice of motion, deliver a copy of the notice of motion to <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the district attorney personally or by first-class mail, and <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the applicant and the employer or other person providing health insurance, at the appropriate addresses contained in the application, by first-class mail.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Upon a finding of error described in subdivision (a), the court shall quash the assignment.
