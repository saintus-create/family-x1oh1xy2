---
title: "2111. a disclosure required by this chapter does not abrogate the"
description: "A disclosure required by this chapter does not abrogate the"
slug: division-6-dissolution-section-2111
---

A disclosure required by this chapter does not abrogate the attorney work product privilege or impede the power of the court to issue protective orders.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 13. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.*
