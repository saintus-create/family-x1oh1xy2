# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2111

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

A disclosure required by this chapter does not abrogate the attorney work product privilege or impede the power of the court to issue protective orders.
