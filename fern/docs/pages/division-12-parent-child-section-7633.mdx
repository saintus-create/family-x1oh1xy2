# 7633. Time for action, order or judgment

*Parent and Child Relationship*

Time for action, order or judgment
An action under this chapter may be brought, an order or
judgment may be entered before the birth of the child, and
enforcement of that order or judgment shall be stayed until
the birth of the child.
