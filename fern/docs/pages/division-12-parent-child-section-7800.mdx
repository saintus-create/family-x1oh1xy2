# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7800

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Appointment of party to act in minor’s behalf.
Inspection of petitions, reports, and court records and
briefs.
Filing fees.
Applicability of proceedings; stay of proceedings under
specified statutory provisions.
Application of part.
Purpose of part
10 Witkin, California Summary 10th Parent and Child § 365 (2018),
Nature of Action.
10 Witkin, California Summary 10th Parent and Child § 366 (2018),
Dependency Proceeding Distinguished.
10 Witkin, California Summary 10th Parent and Child § 368 (2018),
Other Proceedings Stayed.
10 Witkin, California Summary 10th Parent and Child § 378 (2018),
Statutory Ground.
10 Witkin, California Summary 10th Parent and Child § 380 (2018),
Insufficient Showing.
10 Witkin, California Summary 10th Parent and Child § 384 (2018),
The purpose of this part is to serve the welfare and best
interest of a child by providing the stability and security of an
adoptive home when those conditions are otherwise missing
from the child’s life. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
