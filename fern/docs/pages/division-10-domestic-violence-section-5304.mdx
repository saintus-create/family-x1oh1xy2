# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5304

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Transfers not testamentary; necessity of writing Any transfers resulting from the application of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5302.">Section 5302</a> are effective by reason of the account contracts involved and this part and are not to be considered as testamentary. 
The right under this part of a surviving party to a joint account, or of a beneficiary, or of a P.O.D. payee, to the sums on deposit on the death of a party to a multiple-party account shall not be denied, abridged, or affected because such right has not been created by a writing executed in accordance with the laws of this state prescribing the requirements to effect a 1159 

§ 5304 valid testamentary disposition of property.
