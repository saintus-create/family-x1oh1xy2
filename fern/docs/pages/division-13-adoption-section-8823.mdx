# Adoption

## Section 8823

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Prospective adoptive parents; appearance before court The prospective adoptive parents and the child proposed to be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections 8612 and 8613.
