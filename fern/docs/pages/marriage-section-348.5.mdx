# General Provisions

## Section 348.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

No limitation; action upon bonds or coupons issued by State of California An action upon any bonds or coupons issued by the State of California shall have no limitation.

**Source:** California Family Code Annotated (Grace Ganz Blumberg, 2020 Desktop Edition). [View on California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=348.5.).
