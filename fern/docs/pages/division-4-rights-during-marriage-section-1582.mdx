# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1582

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Communication; acceptance of proposal OF OF . 
If a proposal prescribes. any conditions concerning the communication of its acceptance, the proposer is not bound unless they are conformed to; but in other cases any reasonable and usual mode may be adopted. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
