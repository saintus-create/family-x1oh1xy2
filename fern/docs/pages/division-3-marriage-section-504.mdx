# § 504

*Duration of license*

Duration of license
A confidential marriage license is valid only for a period of
90 days after its issuance by the county clerk. (Stats.1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by
Stats.2014, c. 913 (A.B.2747), § 16, eff: Jan. 1, 2015.)
