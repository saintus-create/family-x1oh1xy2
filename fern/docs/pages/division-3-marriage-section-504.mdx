---
title: "504. Duration of license"
description: "Duration of license"
slug: division-3-marriage-section-504
---

A confidential marriage license is valid only for a period of 90 days after its issuance by the county clerk. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 913, Sec. 16. (AB 2747) Effective January 1, 2015.*
