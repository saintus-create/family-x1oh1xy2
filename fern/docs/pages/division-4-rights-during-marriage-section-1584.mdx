# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1584

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Acceptance of proposal; performing conditions or acceptance of consideration BY OF . Performance of the conditions of a proposal, or the acceptance of the consideration offered with a proposal, is an acceptance of the proposal. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
