Ex parte order regarding real or personal property
The court may issue an ex parte order determining the
temporary use, possession, and control of real or personal
property of the parties and the payment of any liens or
encumbrances coming due during the period the order is in
effect. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 154.)
