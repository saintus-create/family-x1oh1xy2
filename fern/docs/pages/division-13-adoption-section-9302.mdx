# Adoption

## Section 9302

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Consent of spouse of proposed adoptee; consent of others not required 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A married person who is not lawfully separated from the person’s spouse may not be adopted without the consent of the spouse, provided that the spouse is capable of giving that consent. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The consent of the parents of the proposed adoptee, of the department, or of any other person is not required.
