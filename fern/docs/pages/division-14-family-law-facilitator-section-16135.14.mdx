# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16135.14

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Specialized prospective adoptive homes 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The county shall determine whether a child is eligible for services pursuant to this section. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A participating county shall select a specialized prospective adoptive home for the child. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If an eligible child’s adoptive placement changes from one participating county to another participating county, the child shall remain eligible for services.
