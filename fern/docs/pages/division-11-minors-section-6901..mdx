# § 6901.

*Part 4 — Medical Treatment · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

“Dental care” means X-ray examination, anesthetic, dental or surgical diagnosis or treatment, and hospital care by a dentist licensed under the Dental Practice Act. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
