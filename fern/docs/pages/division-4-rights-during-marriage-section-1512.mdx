# 1512. Amendment of petition to disclose newly discovered

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Amendment of petition to disclose newly discovered
proceeding affecting custody
Within 10 days after the petitioner in the guardianship
proceeding becomes aware of any proceeding not disclosed in
the guardianship petition affecting the custody of the pro-
posed ward (including any adoption, juvenile court, marriage
dissolution, domestic relations, or other similar proceeding
affecting the proposed ward), the petitioner shall amend the
guardianship
petition
to disclose
the other proceeding.
