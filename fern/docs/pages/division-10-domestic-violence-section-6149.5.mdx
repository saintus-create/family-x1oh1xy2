# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6149.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Third-party liability claim; settlement by insurer; notice to claimant; effect on action or defense 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon the payment of one hundred dollars ($100) or more in settlement of any third-party liability claim the insurer shall provide written notice to the claimant if both of the following apply: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The claimant is a natural person. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The payment is delivered to the claimant’s lawyer or other representative by draft, check, or otherwise. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of this section, “written notice” includes providing to the claimant a copy of the cover letter sent to the claimant’s attorney or other representative that accompanied the settlement payment. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section shall not create any cause of action for any person against the insurer based upon the insurer’s failure to provide the notice to a claimant required by this section. 
This section shall not create a defense for any party to any cause of action based upon the insurer’s failure to provide this notice.
