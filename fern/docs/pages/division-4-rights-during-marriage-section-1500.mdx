# § 1500 — Effect of premarital agreements and other marital

The property rights of spouses prescribed by statute may be altered by a premarital agreement or other marital property agreement. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 19. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)_
