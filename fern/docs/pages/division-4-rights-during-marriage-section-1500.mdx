---
title: "1500. Effect of premarital agreements and other marital"
description: "Effect of premarital agreements and other marital"
slug: division-4-rights-during-marriage-section-1500
---

The property rights of spouses prescribed by statute may be altered by a premarital agreement or other marital property agreement. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 19. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.*
