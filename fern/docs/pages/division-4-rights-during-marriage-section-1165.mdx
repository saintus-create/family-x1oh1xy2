term
“election
period”

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Effect of election on other beneficiaries
Except as otherwise specified in an election, any
election of continuation coverage by a qualified benefi-
ciary described in subparagraph (A)<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  of section
1167(3) of this title shall be deemed to include an
election of continuation coverage on behalf of any other
qualified beneficiary who would lose coverage under the
plan by reason of the qualifying event.
If there is a
choice among types of coverage under the plan, each
qualified beneficiary is entitled to make a separate
selection among such types of coverage.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Temporary extension of COBRA election period for
certain individuals
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general
In the case of a nonelecting TAA-eligible individual
and notwithstanding subsection (a), such individual may
elect continuation coverage under this part during the
60-day period that begins on the first day of the month
in which the individual becomes a TAA-eligible individ-
ual, but only if such election is made not later than 6
months after the date of the TAA-related loss of cover-
age.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Commencement of coverage; no reach-back
Any continuation coverage elected by a TAA-eligible
individual under paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  shall commence at the
beginning of the 60-day election period described in
such paragraph and shall not include any period prior
to such 60-day election period.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Preexisting conditions
With respect to an individual who elects continuation
coverage pursuant to paragraph (1), the period—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  beginning on the date of the TAA-related
loss of coverage, and
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  ending on the first day of the 60-day elec-
tion period described in paragraph (1), shall be
disregarded for purposes of determining the 63—day
periods referred to in section 1181(c)<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of this
title, section 2701(c)<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of the Public Health Ser-
vice Act, and section 9801(c)<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of Title 26.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Definitions
For purposes of this subsection:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Nonelecting TAA-eligible individual
The term “nonelecting TAA-eligible individual”
means a TAA-eligible individual who—
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  has a TAA-related loss of coverage; and
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  did not elect continuation coverage un-
der this part during the TAA-related election
period.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  TAA-eligible individual
The term “TAA-eligible individual” means—
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  an eligible TAA recipient (as defined in
paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of section 35(c) of Title 26), and
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  an eligible alternative TAA recipient (as
defined in paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of such section).
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  TAA-related election period
The term “TAA-related election period” means,
with respect to a TAA-related loss of coverage, the
60-day election period under this part which is a
direct consequence of such loss.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  TAA-related loss of coverage
The term “TAA-related loss of coverage” means,
with respect to an individual whose separation from
employment gives rise to being an TAA-eligible
individual, the loss of health benefits coverage as-
sociated with such separation.
