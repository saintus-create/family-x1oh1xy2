# 5240 — Payment of past due support;

Support

Payment of past due support;
termination
of
service of order of assignment; grounds; ex parte relief
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon the filing and service of a motion and a notice of
motion by the obligor, the court shall terminate the service of
an assignment order if past due support has been paid in full,
including any interest due, and if any of the following
conditions exist:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  With regard to orders for spousal support, the death or
remarriage of the spouse to whom support is owed.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  With regard to orders for child support, the death or
emancipation
of the child for whom support
is owed.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The court determines that there is good cause, as
defined in Section 5260, to terminate the assignment order.
This subdivision does not apply if there has been more than
one application for an assignment order.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The obligor meets the conditions of an alternative
arrangement specified in paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of
Section 5260, and a wage assignment has not been previously
terminated and subsequently initiated.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  There is no longer a current order for support.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The termination of the stay of an assignment order
under Section 5261 was improper, but only if that termination
was based upon the obligor’s failure to make timely support
