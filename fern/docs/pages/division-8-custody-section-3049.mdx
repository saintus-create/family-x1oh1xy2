# Custody of Children

## Section 3049

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Disabled parents; legislative intent It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this section to codify the decision of the California Supreme Court in In re Marriage of Carney (1979) 24 Cal.3d 725, with respect to custody and visitation determinations by the court involving a disabled parent.
