# § 3049 — Disabled parents; legislative intent

Disabled parents; legislative intent
It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this section to
codify the decision of the California Supreme Court in In re
Marriage of Carney (1979) 24 Cal.3d 725, with respect to
custody and visitation determinations by the court involving a
disabled parent.
