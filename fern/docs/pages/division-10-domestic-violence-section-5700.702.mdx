# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.702

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Applicability This chapter applies only to a support proceeding under the Convention. 
In such a proceeding, if a provision of this chapter is inconsistent with Chapters 1 through 6, this chapter controls.
