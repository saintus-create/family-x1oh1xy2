<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If two or more presumptions arise under Section 7611
that conflict with each other, or if one or more presumptions
under Section 7611 conflict with a claim by a person
identified as a genetic parent pursuant to Section 7555, the
presumption that on the facts is founded on the weightier
considerations of policy and logic controls. If one of the
presumed parents is also a presumed parent under Section
7540, the presumption arising under Section 7540 may only
be rebutted pursuant to Section 7541.
