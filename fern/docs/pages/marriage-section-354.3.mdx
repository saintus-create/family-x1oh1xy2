# General Provisions

## Section 354.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Recovery of Holocaust-era artwork from enumerated entities 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The following definitions govern the construction of this section: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Entity” means any museum or gallery that displays, exhibits, or sells any article of historical, interpretive, scientific, or artistic significance. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Holocaust-era artwork” means any article of artistic significance taken as a result of Nazi persecution during the period of 1929 to 1945, inclusive. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any owner, or heir or beneficiary of an owner, of Holocaust-era artwork, may bring an action to recover Holocaust-era artwork from any entity described in paragraph (1) of subdivision (a).
