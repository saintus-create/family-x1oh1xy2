# § 4561 — If a child support security deposit is ordered, the court shall

ARTICLE 2.
ORDER
FOR DEPOSIT OF MONEY
If a child support security deposit is ordered, the court shall
order that the moneys be deposited by the child support
obligor in an interest-bearing account with a state or federally
chartered commercial bank, a trust company authorized to
transact trust business in this state, or a savings and loan
association, or in shares of a federally insured credit union
doing business in this state and having a trust department,
subject to withdrawal only upon authorization of the court.
The moneys so deposited shall be used. exclusively to
guarantee the monthly payment of child support.
