# 959

Adoption

There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue concerning the intention or competence of a client executing an attested document of which the
lawyer is an attesting witness, or concerning the execution or
attestation of such a document.
(Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2,
operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
