# 12801

Of the Vehicle Code, that

of the Vehicle Code, that
contains the name, date of birth, description, and picture of
the person.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A valid passport issued by the United States or by a
foreign government.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A valid identification card issued to a member of the
United States Armed Forces that includes the date of birth
and picture of the person.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A valid consular identification document.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  An identification card issued by a federally recognized
tribal government.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  This section shall become operative on January 1,
2020.
