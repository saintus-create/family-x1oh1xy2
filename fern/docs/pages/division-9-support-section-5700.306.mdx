# § 5700.306

*If a petition or comparable pleading is received by an*

If a petition or comparable pleading is received by an
inappropriate tribunal of this state, the tribunal shall forward
the pleading and accompanying documents to an appropriate
tribunal of this state or another state and notify the petitioner
where and when the pleading was sent.
