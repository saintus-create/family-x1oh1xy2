# § 2105

*Final declaration of disclosure of current income*

Final declaration of disclosure of current income
and expenses; execution and service; contents; waiver;
perjury or noncompliance with chapter
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except by court order for good cause, before or at the
time the parties enter into an agreement for the resolution of

property or support issues other than pendente lite support,
or, if the case goes to trial, no later than 45 days before the
first assigned trial date, each party, or the attorney for the
party in this matter, shall serve on the other party a final
declaration of disclosure and a current income and expense
declaration, executed under penalty of perjury on a form
prescribed by the Judicial Council, unless the parties mutually
waive the final declaration of disclosure.
The commission of
perjury on the final declaration of disclosure by a party may
be grounds for setting aside the judgment, or any part or
parts thereof, pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with
Section 2120), in addition to any and all other remedies, civil
or criminal, that otherwise are available under law for the
commission of perjury.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The final declaration of disclosure shall include all of
the following information:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  All material facts and information regarding the char-
acterization of all assets and liabilities.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  All material facts and information regarding the valua-
tion of all assets that are contended
to be community
property or in which it is contended the community has an
interest.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  All material
facts and information
regarding
the
amounts of all obligations that are contended to be communi-
ty obligations or for which it is contended the community has
liability.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  All material facts and information regarding the earn-
ings, accumulations, and expenses of each party that have
been set forth in the income and expense declaration.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In making an order setting aside a judgment for failure
to comply with this section, the court may limit the set aside
to those portions of the judgment materially affected by the
nondisclosure.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The parties may stipulate to a mutual waiver of the
requirements of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  concerning the final declara-
tion of disclosure, by execution of a waiver under penalty of
perjury entered into in open court or by separate stipulation.
The waiver shall include all of the following representations:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Both parties have complied with Section 2104 and the
preliminary declarations of disclosure have been completed
and exchanged.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Both parties have completed and exchanged a current
income and expense declaration, that includes all material
facts and information regarding that party’s earnings, accu-
mulations, and expenses.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Both parties have fully complied with Section 2102 and
have fully augmented the preliminary declarations of disclo-
sure, including disclosure of all material facts and informa-
tion regarding the characterization of all assets and liabilities,
the valuation of all assets that are contended to be communi-
ty property or in which it is contended the community has an
interest, and the amounts of all obligations that are contend-
ed to be community obligations or for which it is contended
the community has liability.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The waiver is knowingly, intelligently, and voluntarily
entered into by each of the parties.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Each party understands that this waiver does not limit
the legal disclosure obligations of the parties, but rather is a

NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION
statement under penalty of perjury that those obligations
have been fulfilled.
Each party further understands that
noncompliance with those obligations will result in the court
setting aside the judgment.
