# § 8605.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

A child not having a presumed father under Section 7611 may not be adopted without the consent of the child’s mother, if living. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
