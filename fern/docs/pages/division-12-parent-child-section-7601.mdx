# 7601. “Natural parent” defined; “parent and child rela-

*Parent and Child Relationship*

“Natural parent” defined; “parent and child rela-
tionship” defined; finding of parent and child relation-
ship with more than two parents;
interpretation of
reference to two parents
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Natural parent” as used in this code means a nona-
doptive parent established under this part, whether biologi-
cally related to the child or not.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Parent and child relationship”, as used in. this part
means the legal relationship existing between a child and the
child’s natural or adoptive parents incident to which the law
confers or imposes rights, privileges, duties, and obligations.
The term includes the mother and child relationship and the
father and child relationship.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This part does not preclude a finding that a child has a
parent and child relationship with more than two parents.
‘<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  For purposes of state law, administrative regulations,
court rules, government policies, common law, and any other
provision or source of law governing the rights, protections,
benefits, responsibilities, obligations, and duties,of parents,
any reference to two parents shall be interpreted to apply to
every parent of a child where that child has been found to
have more than two parents under this part...
(Stats. 1992, c.
