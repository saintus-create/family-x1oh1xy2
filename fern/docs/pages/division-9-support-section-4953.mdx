# Support

## Section 4953

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Despite the clear language of subsection (b), Scheuerman v. Hauk, 116 Cal.App.4th 1140, 11 Cal.Rptr.3d 125 (2004), held that a trial court properly refused to register an Arizona child support order after an Arizona court had judicially determined that it was no longer enforceable because the Arizona statute of limitations had expired, although the California statute of limitations had not. Scheuerman reasoned that enforcement of the Arizona support order would 408 violate the full faith and credit clause of the United States Constitution.
