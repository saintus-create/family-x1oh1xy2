---
title: "6223. Custody or visitation order; application of Part 2"
description: "Custody or visitation order; application of Part 2"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-6223
---

A custody or visitation order issued in a proceeding brought pursuant to this division is subject to Part 2 (commencing with Section 3020) of Division 8 (custody of children).

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.*
