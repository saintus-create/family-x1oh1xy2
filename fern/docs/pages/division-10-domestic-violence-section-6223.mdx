# 6223

Custody or visitation order; application of Part 2

A custody or visitation order issued in a proceeding brought pursuant to this division is subject to Part 2 (commencing with Section 3020) of Division 8 (custody of children). Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.
