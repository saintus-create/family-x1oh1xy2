# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6223

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Custody or visitation order; application of Part 2 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=8.">Division 8</a> A custody or visitation order issued in a_ proceeding brought pursuant to this division is subject to Part 2 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3020.">Section 3020</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=8.">Division 8</a> (custody of children).
