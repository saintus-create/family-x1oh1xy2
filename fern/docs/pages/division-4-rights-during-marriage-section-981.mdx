# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 981

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

There is no privilege under this article if the communication was made, in whole or in part, to enable or aid anyone to commit or plan to commit a crime or a fraud. 
(Stats. 1965, c. 
299, § 2, operative Jan..1, 1967.) 
Exception: Crime or fraud
