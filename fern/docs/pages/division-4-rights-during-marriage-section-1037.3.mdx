# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1037.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Child abuse; reporting Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit any obligation to report instances of child abuse as required by <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11166.">Section 11166</a> of the Penal Code.
