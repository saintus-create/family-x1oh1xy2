# California Family Code § 2310

Dissolution of the marriage or legal separation of the parties may be based on either of the following grounds, which shall be pleaded generally:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Irreconcilable differences, which have caused the irremediable breakdown of the marriage.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Permanent legal incapacity to make decisions.
