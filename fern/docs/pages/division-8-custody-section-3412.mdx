# 3412. An order; preservation of pleadings and records

*Custody of Children*

an order; preservation of pleadings and records
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court of this state may request the appropriate court
of another state to do all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Hold an evidentiary hearing.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Order a person to produce or give evidence pursuant
to procedures of that state.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Order that an evaluation be made with respect to the
custody of a child involved in a pending proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Forward to the court of this state a certified copy of the
transcript of the record of the hearing, the evidence otherwise
presented, and any evaluation prepared in compliance with
the request.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Order a party to a child custody proceeding or any
person having physical custody of the child to appear in the
proceeding with or without the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon request of a court of another state, a court of
this state may hold a hearing or enter an order described in
subdivision (a).
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Travel and other necessary and reasonable expenses
incurred under subdivisions <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  may be assessed
against the parties according to the law of this state.
