# Custody of Children

## Section 3412

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Request of another court to hold hearing or enter an order; preservation of pleadings and records 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court of this state may request the appropriate court of another state to do all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Hold an evidentiary hearing. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Order a person to produce or give evidence pursuant to procedures of that state. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Order that an evaluation be made with respect to the custody of a child involved in a pending proceeding. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Forward to the court of this state a certified copy of the transcript of the record of the hearing, the evidence otherwise presented, and any evaluation prepared in compliance with the request. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Order a party to a child custody proceeding or any person having physical custody of the child to appear in the proceeding with or without the child. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon request of a court of another state, a court of this state may hold a hearing or enter an order described in subdivision (a). 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Travel and other necessary and reasonable expenses incurred under subdivisions (a) and (b) may be assessed against the parties according to the law of this state. 
 OF 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A court of this state shall preserve the pleadings, orders, decrees, records of hearings, evaluations, and other pertinent records with respect to a child custody proceeding until the child attains 18 years of age. 
Upon appropriate request by a court or law enforcement official of another state, the court shall forward a certified copy of those records.
