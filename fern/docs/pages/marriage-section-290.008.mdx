# General Provisions

## Section 290.008

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The court must order the probation department to forward to the all required medical information, including previously executed medical releases. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  If the youth is taking a prescribed psychotropic medication, the may continue to administer the medication for up to 60 days, provided that a physician examines the youth on arrival at the facility, and the physician recommends that the medication continue. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The court must provide to the information regarding the youth’s educational needs, including the youth’s current individualized education program if one exists. 
To facilitate this process, the court must ensure that the probation officer communicates with appropriate educational staff. 
(Formerly Rule 1494.5, adopted, eff: Jan. 1, 2003. As amended, eff. Jan. 1, 2006.
