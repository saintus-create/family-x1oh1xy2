# § 2627 — Educational loans; liabilities for death or injuries;

Educational loans; liabilities for death or injuries;
assignment
Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, and
Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, educational loans shall be
assigned pursuant to Section 2641 and liabilities subject to
paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 1000 shall be
assigned to the spouse whose act or omission provided the
basis for the liability, without offset.
