# Division of Property

## Section 2627

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Educational loans; liabilities for death or injuries; assignment Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, and Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, educational loans shall be assigned pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2641.">§ 2641</a> and liabilities subject to paragraph (2) of subdivision (b) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1000.">§ 1000</a> shall be assigned to the spouse whose act or omission provided the basis for the liability, without offset.
