## Case Annotations

<div class="case-callout">
<strong>Garcia v. Escobar (2017)</strong>

*17 Cal.App.5th*

After a juvenile court case is dismissed, domestic violence restraining orders issued by the juvenile court (sometimes called DVROs or JVROs) can be renewed by the family court, in the same way that family courts’ DVROs are renewed pursuant to the DVPA.
