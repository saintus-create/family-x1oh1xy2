# § 2602.

*Part 4 — Special Rules For Division Of Community Estate*

As an additional award or offset against existing property, the court may award, from a party’s share, the amount the court determines to have been deliberately misappropriated by the party to the exclusion of the interest of the other party in the community estate. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
