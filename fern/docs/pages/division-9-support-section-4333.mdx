# § 4333

*Retroactive application of order*

Retroactive application of order
An order for spousal support in a proceeding for dissolu-
tion of marriage or for legal separation of the parties may be
made retroactive to the date of filing the notice of motion or
order to show cause, or to any subsequent date.
(Stats. 1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
