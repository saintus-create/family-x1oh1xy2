# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1016

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Service; process to bring party into contempt; inapplicability of certain sections The foregoing provisions of this Chapter do not apply to the service of a summons or other process, or of any paper to bring a party into contempt. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
