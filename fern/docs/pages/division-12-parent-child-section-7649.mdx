# 7649. This article does not limit the rights and remedies available

*Parent and Child Relationship*

(1993)].
This article does not limit the rights and remedies available
under any other law with regard to setting aside or vacating a
judgment of parentage. (Added by Stats.2004, c. 849 (A.B.
252), § 4. Amended by Stats.2018, c. 876 (A.B.2684), $ 62,
eff. Jan. 1, 2019.)
