# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7649

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Rights and remedies available under other provisions of law This article does not limit the rights and remedies available under any other law with regard to setting aside or vacating a judgment of parentage.
