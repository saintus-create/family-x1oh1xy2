# § 7649 — Rights and remedies available under other provi-

This article does not limit the rights and remedies available under any other law with regard to setting aside or vacating a judgment of parentage. _(Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 62. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)_
