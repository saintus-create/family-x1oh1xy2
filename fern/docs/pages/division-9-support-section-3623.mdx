# 3623 — service

Support

service
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to Section 3625, an expedited support order
becomes effective 30 days after service on the obligated
parent of all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The application for an expedited child support order.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The proposed expedited child support order, which
shall include a notice of consequences of failure to file a
response.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The completed income and expense declaration for
both parents.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A worksheet setting forth the basis of the amount of
support requested.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Three blank copies of the income and expense declaration form.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Three blank copies of the response to an application
for expedited child support order and notice of hearing form.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Service on the obligated parent of the application and
other required documents as set forth in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall
be by personal service or by any method available under
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A worksheet setting forth the basis of the amount of
support requested.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
