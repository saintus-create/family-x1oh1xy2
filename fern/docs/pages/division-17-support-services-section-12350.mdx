# § 12350

*SUPPORT OF PARENTS*

SUPPORT OF PARENTS
The duty of support stated in Section 4400 is subject to Chapter 2
(commencing with Section 4410) (relief from duty to support parent
on ground of abandonment).
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1
(1993)].
Commentary
In re Marriage of Leni, 144 Cal.App:4th 1087, 50 Cal.Rptr.3d 886
(2006), held that a spouse’s use of community funds to care for his
ailing mother was not a gift for purposes of section 1100 <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  because
this section imposes upon an adult child a legal duty to support a
parent who is in need and is unable to maintain herself by work.
Moreover, the community has no right of reimbursement under
section 915, because it creates a right of reimbursement only for “a
child or spousal support obligation that does not arise out of the
marriage.”
