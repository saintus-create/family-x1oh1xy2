# § 4302.

*Part 3 — Spousal Support · Chapter 1 — Duty To Support Spouse*

A person is not liable for support of the person’s spouse when the person is living separate from the spouse by agreement unless support is stipulated in the agreement. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
