# 1590 — Gifts in contemplation of marriage; recovery

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

Gifts in contemplation of marriage; recovery
Where either party to a contemplated marriage in this
State makes a gift of money or property to the other on the
basis or assumption that the marriage will take place, in the
event that the donee refuses to enter into the marriage as
contemplated or that it is given up by mutual consent, the
donor may recover such gift or such part of its value as may,
under all of the circumstances of the case, be found by a court
or jury to be just. (Added by Stats.1939, c. 128, p. 1245, $ 3.)
1 California Transactions Forms--Estate Planning § 7:2, Types of
Gifts.
