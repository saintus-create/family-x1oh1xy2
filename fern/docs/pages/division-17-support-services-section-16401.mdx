<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Each partner is deemed to have an account that is
subject to both of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Credited with an amount equal to the money plus the
value of any other property, net of the amount of any
liabilities, the partner contributes to the partnership and the
partner’s share of the partnership profits.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Subject to Sections 16306 and 16957, charged with an
amount equal to the money plus the value of any other
property, net of the amount of any liabilities, distributed by
the partnership to the partner and the partner’s share of the
partnership losses.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Each partner is entitled to an equal share of the
partnership profits and, subject to Sections 16306 and 16957,
is chargeable with a share of the partnership losses in
proportion to the partner’s share of the profits.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A partnership shall reimburse a partner for payments
made and indemnify a partner for liabilities incurred by the
partner in the ordinary course of the business of the
partnership or for the preservation of its business or property.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A partnership shall reimburse a partner for an advance
to the partnership beyond the amount of capital the partner
agreed to contribute.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A payment or advance made by a partner that gives
rise to a partnership obligation under subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(d)</span> 
constitutes a loan to the partnership that accrues interest
from the date of the payment or advance.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Each partner has equal rights in the management and
conduct of the partnership business.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  A partner may use or possess partnership property only
on behalf of the partnership.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  A partner is not entitled to remuneration for services
performed for the partnership, except for reasonable com-
ized Management.
