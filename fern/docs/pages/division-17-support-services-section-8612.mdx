# § 8612 — Examination by court; agreement; execution; or-

Examination by court; agreement; execution; or-
der of adoption
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court shall examine all persons appearing before it
pursuant to this part. The examination of each person shall
be conducted separately but within the physical presence of
every other person unless the court, in its discretion, orders
otherwise.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The prospective adoptive parent or parents.
shall
execute and acknowledge an agreement in writing that the
child will be treated in all respects as their lawful child.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If satisfied
that the interest of the child will be
promoted by the adoption, the court may make and enter an
order of adoption of the child by the prospective adoptive
parent or parents.
