# § 7808

*Application of part*

Application of part
This part does not apply to a minor adjudged a dependent
child of the juvenile court pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of
Section 360 of the Welfare and Institutions Code on and after
January 1, 1989, during the period in which the minor is a
dependent child of the court. For those minors, the exclusive
means for the termination of parental rights are provided in
the following statutes:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Section 366.26 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Sections 8604 to 8606, inclusive, and 8700 of this code.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 7660) of Part 3 of
this division of this code.
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
Amended by Stats.1994, c. 1269
(A.B.2208), § 55.4.)
