# Support

## Section 4603

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Liability of deposit holder The deposit holder who is responsible for any money or property and for any disbursements under this chapter is not liable for any action undertaken in good faith and in conformance with this chapter.
