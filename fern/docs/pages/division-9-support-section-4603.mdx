# § 4603 — The deposit holder who is responsible for any money or

The deposit holder who is responsible for any money or
property and for any disbursements under this chapter is not
liable for any action undertaken in good faith and in
conformance with this chapter.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.
2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
The purpose of this chapter is to provide an extraordinary
remedy for cases of bad faith failure to pay child support
obligations.
(Stats.1992,
Jan. 1, 1994.)
