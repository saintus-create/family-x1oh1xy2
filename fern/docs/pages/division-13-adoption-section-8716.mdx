# Adoption

## Section 8716

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

The amount of this additional fee shall be based on the cost of drawing and storing the blood samples but at no time shall the additional fee be more than one hundred dollars ($100). 
(d)(1) The blood sample shall be stored and released in such a manner as to not identify any party to the adoption. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any results of the testing shall be stored and released in such a manner as to not identify any party to the adoption.
