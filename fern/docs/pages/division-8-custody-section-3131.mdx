# 3131 — Custody or visitation orders;

Custody of Children

Custody or visitation orders;
duties of district
attorney
If a custody or visitation order has been entered by a court
of competent jurisdiction and the child is taken or detained
by another person in violation of the order, the district
attorney shall take all actions necessary to locate and return
the child and the person who violated the order and to assist
in the enforcement of the custody or visitation order or other

order of the court by use of an appropriate civil or criminal
proceeding.
