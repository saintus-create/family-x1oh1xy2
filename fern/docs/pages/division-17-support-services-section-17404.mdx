# Support Services

## Section 17404

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

After joinder, the commissioner shall: 
(1) Refer the parents for mediation of disputed custody or visitation issues pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3170.">Section 3170</a> (2) Accept stipulated agreements concerning custody, visitation, and protective orders and enter orders pursuant to the agreements. 
(3) Refer contested issues of custody, visitation, and protective orders to a judge or to another commissioner for hearing. A child support commissioner may hear contested custody, visitation, and restraining order issues only if the court has adopted procedures to segregate the costs of hearing Title IV-D child support issues from the costs of hearing other issues pursuant to applicable federal requirements. 
(f) The local child support agency shall be served notice by the moving party of any proceeding under this section in which support is at issue. 
An order for support that is entered without the local child support agency having received proper notice shall be voidable upon the motion of the local child support agency.
