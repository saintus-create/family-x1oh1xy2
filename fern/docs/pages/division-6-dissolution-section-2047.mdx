# 2047. Protective orders; restraining orders

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

Protective orders; restraining orders
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  After notice and a hearing, the court may issue a
protective order, as defined in Section 6218, and any other
restraining order as provided in Article 2 (commencing with
Section 6340) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of .

pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  unless it meets the requirements
of Section 6305. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
