# § 2047 — Protective orders; restraining orders

(a) After notice and a hearing, the court may issue a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, and any other restraining order as provided in Article 2 (commencing with Section 6340) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of Division 10.

(b) The court may not issue a mutual protective order pursuant to subdivision (a) unless it meets the requirements of Section 6305. _(Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 246, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM3.
