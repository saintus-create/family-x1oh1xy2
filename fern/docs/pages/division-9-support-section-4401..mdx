# § 4401.

*Part 4 — Support Of Parents · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

The promise of an adult child to pay for necessaries previously furnished to a parent described in Section 4400 is binding. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
