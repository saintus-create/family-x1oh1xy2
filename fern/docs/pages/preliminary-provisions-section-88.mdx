# § 88 — The section

The section
was drawn from former Probate Code Section 662 (probate home-
stead) (repealed by 1983
Cal.Stat.
ch. 842 § 39).
The
1987
amendment <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  revised the cross-reference to the procedure for
mailing notice of the hearing and <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  deleted the provision requiring
the clerk to set the petition for hearing, this provision being
unnecessary in view of Section 1285 (now Section 1041), a general
provision that imposes this duty on the clerk.
As to the 1987
amendment,
see Communication from California Law Revision
Commission
Concerning Assembly
Bill 708, 19 Cal.L.Revision
Comm’n Reports 545, 560 (1988); Comments to Conforming Revi-
sions and Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 391, 449
(1988). .
