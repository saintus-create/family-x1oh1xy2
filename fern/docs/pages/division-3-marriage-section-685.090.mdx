# 685.090

Accrues pursuant to Sections 685.010 to 685.030, inclusive

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The addition of interest added to the judgment as it
accrues pursuant to Sections 685.010 to 685.030, inclusive.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The subtraction of the amount of any partial satisfac-
tions of the judgment.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The subtraction of the amount of any portion of the
judgment that is no longer enforceable.
