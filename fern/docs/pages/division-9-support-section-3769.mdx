# § 3769 — Discrimination prohibited; violation; penalty

Discrimination prohibited; violation; penalty
No employer shall use a health insurance coverage assign-
ment order as grounds for refusing to hire a person or for

DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
discharging or taking disciplinary action against an employee.
An employer who violates this section may be assessed a civil
penalty of a maximum of five hundred
dollars ($500).
