# § 7559.

*Part 2 — Presumption Concerning Child Of Marriage And Genetic Testing To Determine Parentage · Chapter 2 — Genetic Testing To Determine Parentage*

Payment of the cost of initial genetic testing shall be made in advance when agreed to by the parties or ordered by the court pursuant to Section 7640. (Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 22. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)
