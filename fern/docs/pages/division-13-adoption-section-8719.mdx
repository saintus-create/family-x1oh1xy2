# Adoption

## Section 8719

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Withdrawal of petition; dismissal of proceedings; notification of department If the petitioners move to withdraw the adoption petition or to dismiss the proceeding, the court clerk shall immediately notify the department at Sacramento of the action.
