# § 4611 — In a proceeding under this chapter, an obligor-parent shall

In a proceeding under this chapter, an obligor-parent shall
rebut both of the following presumptions:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonpayment of child support was willful, without
good faith.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The obligor had the ability to pay the support.
