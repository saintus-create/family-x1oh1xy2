# Support

## Section 4611

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

In a proceeding under this chapter, an obligor-parent shall rebut both of the following presumptions: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonpayment of child support was willful, without good faith. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The obligor had the ability to pay the support.
