# § 4611 — In a proceeding under this chapter, an obligor-parent shall

In a proceeding under this chapter, an obligor-parent shall rebut both of the following presumptions:

(a) The nonpayment of child support was willful, without good faith.

(b) The obligor had the ability to pay the support. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
