# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2030

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Entry of a judgment ordering, under this section, a deceased spouse’s trustee to pay attorney fees to the other spouse’s former attorneys gave the attorneys an independent right to enforce the judgment under this section, and the former attorneys were entitled to postjudgment interest after the wife barred payment, which she had no right to do under this section, and the trustee consequently failed to satisfy the judgment for seven months. In re Marriage of Green, 143 Cal.App.4th 1312, 49 Cal.Rptr.3d 908 (2006).
