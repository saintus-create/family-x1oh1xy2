# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1035.6

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Holder of the privilege As used in this article, “holder of the privilege” means: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The victim when such person has no guardian or conservator. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A guardian or conservator of the victim when the victim has a guardian or conservator. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The personal representative of the victim if the victim is dead.
