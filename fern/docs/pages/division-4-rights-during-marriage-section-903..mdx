# § 903.

*Part 3 — Liability Of Marital Property · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

A debt is “incurred” at the following time:

(a) In the case of a contract, at the time the contract is made.

(b) In the case of a tort, at the time the tort occurs.

(c) In other cases, at the time the obligation arises. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
