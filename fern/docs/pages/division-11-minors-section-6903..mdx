# § 6903.

*Part 4 — Medical Treatment · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

“Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents have legal custody, or the parent or person having legal custody, or the guardian, of a minor. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
