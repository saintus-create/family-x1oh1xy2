(C)  Except as provided in section 676(b), members of
the public must be admitted to hearings concerning allega-
tions of the offenses stated in section 676(a).
(D)  A victim of an offense alleged to have been
committed by the child who is the subject of the petition,
and up to two support persons chosen by the victim, are
entitled to attend any hearing regarding the offense.
(E)  Any persons, including the child, may move to
exclude a victim or a support person and must demonstrate
a substantial probability that overriding interests will be
prejudiced by the presence of the individual sought to be
excluded.
