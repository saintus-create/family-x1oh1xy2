# 7902 — Financial responsibility for child placed pursuant

Parent and Child Relationship

Financial responsibility for child placed pursuant
to compact
Financial responsibility for a child placed pursuant to the
Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children shall be
determined in accordance with Article 5 of the compact in
the first instance.
However, in the event of partial or
complete default of performance thereunder, the provisions
of other state laws also may be invoked.
