State and federal child support incentives 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For the 1998-99 fiscal year the department shall pay to each county a child support incentive payment. 
Every county shall receive the federal child support incentive. 
A county shall receive the state child support incentive if it elects to do both of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Comply with the reporting requirements of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17600.">§ 17600</a> while federal financial participation is available for collecting and reporting data.
