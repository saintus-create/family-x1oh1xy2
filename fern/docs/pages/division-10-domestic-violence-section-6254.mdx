# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6254

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Availability of orders; effect of vacation of household The fact that the endangered person has left the household to avoid abuse does not affect the availability of an emergency protective order.
