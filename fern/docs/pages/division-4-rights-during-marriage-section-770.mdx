# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 770

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Separate property of married person. 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Separate property of a married person includes all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  All property owned by the person before marriage. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  All property acquired by the person after marriage by gift, bequest, devise, or descent. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The rents, issues, and profits of the property described in this section. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A married person may, without the consent of the person’s spouse, convey the person’s separate property.
