# 770 — Separate property of married person.

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

Separate property of married person.
771.
Earnings and accumulations after date of separation.
772.
Earnings or accumulations after entry of judgment of
legal separation.
770.
Separate property of married person
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Separate property of a married person includes all of
the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  All property owned by the person before marriage.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  All property acquired by the person after marriage by
gift, bequest, devise, or descent.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The rents, issues, and profits of the property described
in this section.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A married person may, without the consent of the
person’s spouse, convey the person’s separate property.
