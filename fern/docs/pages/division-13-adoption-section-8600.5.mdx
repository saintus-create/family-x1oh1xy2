title: "8600.5"
description: "California Family Code 8600.5"

---

Tribal customary adoption as defined in Section 366.24 of the Welfare and Institutions Code and as applied to Indian Children who are dependents of the court, does not apply to this part. (Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 9. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
