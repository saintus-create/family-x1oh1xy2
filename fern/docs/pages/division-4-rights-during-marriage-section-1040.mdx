# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1040

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

. 
Subdivision (c) states the rule that, unlike an inter vivos deed, a revocable deed does not require acceptance. Acceptance of a donative transfer is presumed. Disclaimer procedures are available to a beneficiary. See Sections 267, 279 (disclaimer). 
A revocable deed has no effect, and confers no rights on the beneficiary, until the transferor’s death. See <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5650.">Section 5650</a> (effect during transferor’s life). [44 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 573. (2015) 
[2015-16 AR Appx. 5]] 
2018 Amendment <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5626.">Section 5626</a> is amended to expressly provide that recordation of the “Common Questions” portion of the statutory revocable transfer on death deed form is not required.. 
This rule applies retroactively, to revocable transfer on death deeds that were recorded before the effective date of the new law, ifthe transferor is still alive when the new law takes effect. 
[45 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (2017)].
