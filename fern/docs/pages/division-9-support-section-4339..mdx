# § 4339.

*Part 3 — Spousal Support · Chapter 3 — Spousal Support Upon Dissolution Or Legal Separation*

The court may order the supporting party to give reasonable security for payment of spousal support. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
