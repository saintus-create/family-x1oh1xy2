# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2250

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding based on void or voidable marriage is
commenced by filing a petition entitled “In re the marriage of
and ________”” which shall state that it is a
petition for a judgment of nullity of the marriage.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A copy of the petition together with a copy of a
summons in form and content approved by the Judicial
Council shall be served upon the other party to the marriage
in the same manner as service of papers in civil actions
generally. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan.
1, 1994.)
