# 397.5 — transfer from county

Premarital Agreements

transfer from county
Change of place of trial; grounds
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  When there is reason to believe that an impartial trial
cannot be had therein.
In any proceeding for dissolution or nullity of marriage or
legal separation of the parties under the Family Code, where
it appears that both petitioner and respondent have moved
from the county rendering the order, the court may, when the
ends of justice and the convenience of the parties would be
promoted by the change, order that the proceedings be
transferred to the county of residence of either party.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  When the convenience of witnesses and the ends of
justice would be promoted by the change.
Stats. 1980, c. 234, p. 477, § 2; Stats.1994, c. 1269 (A.B.2208),
