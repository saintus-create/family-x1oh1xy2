# § 4300.

*Part 3 — Spousal Support · Chapter 1 — Duty To Support Spouse*

Subject to this division, a person shall support the person’s spouse. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
