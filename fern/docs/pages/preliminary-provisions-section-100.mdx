# § 100 — See also Sections 11 (reference to married person

See also Sections 11 (reference to married person
includes formerly married person), 12 (meaning of “shall,” “may,”

“shall not,” and “may not”).
For comparable provisions,
see
Sections 6, 900, 2500, 3000, 3500, 3760, 4802, 5200, 6900, 8500.
[23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
