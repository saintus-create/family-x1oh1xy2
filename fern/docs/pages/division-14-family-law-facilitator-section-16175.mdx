# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16175

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Additional contents A compact entered into pursuant to the authority conferred by this chapter may contain provisions in addition to those required pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16174.">§ 16174</a>, as follows: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Provisions establishing procedures and entitlement to medical and other necessary social services for the child in accordance with applicable laws, even though the child and the adoptive parents are in a state other than the one responsible for or providing the services or the funds to defray part or all of the costs thereof. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any other provision as may be appropriate or incidental to the proper administration of the compact.
