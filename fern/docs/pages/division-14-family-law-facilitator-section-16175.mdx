Additional contents
A compact entered into pursuant to the authority con-
ferred by this chapter may contain provisions in addition to
those required pursuant to Section 16174, as follows:
(a)  Provisions establishing procedures and entitlement to
medical and other necessary social services for the child in
accordance with applicable laws, even though the child and
the adoptive parents are in a state other than the one
responsible for or providing the services or the funds to
defray part or all of the costs thereof.
(b)  Any other provision as may be appropriate or inciden-
tal to the proper administration of the compact.
