# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16175

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Additional contents A compact entered into pursuant to the authority conferred by this chapter may contain provisions in addition to those required pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16174.">Section 16174</a>, as follows: 
(a) Provisions establishing procedures and entitlement to medical and other necessary social services for the child in accordance with applicable laws, even though the child and the adoptive parents are in a state other than the one responsible for or providing the services or the funds to defray part or all of the costs thereof. 
(b) Any other provision as may be appropriate or incidental to the proper administration of the compact.
