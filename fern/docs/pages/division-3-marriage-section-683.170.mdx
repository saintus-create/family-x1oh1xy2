# § 683.170 — Subdivision (b) is designed to prevent enforcement after the 10-

Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  is designed to prevent enforcement after the 10-
year period in a case where the judgment creditor has failed to serve
notice of renewal on the judgment debtor.
