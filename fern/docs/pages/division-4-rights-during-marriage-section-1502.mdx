# § 1502 — Recording of agreements

Recording of agreements
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A premarital agreement or other marital property
agreement that is executed and acknowledged or proved in
the manner that a grant of real property is required to be
executed and acknowledged or proved may be recorded in
the office of the recorder of each county in which real
property affected by the agreement is situated.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Recording or nonrecording of a premarital agreement
or other marital property agreement has the same effect as

MARITAL AGREEMENTS
recording or nonrecording of a grant of real property.
