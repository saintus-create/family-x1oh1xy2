# § 6271 — Duties of officer who requested order

Duties of officer who requested order
A law enforcement officer who requests an emergency
protective order shall do all of the following:

PREVENTION
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Serve the order on the restrained person,
if the
restrained person can reasonably be located.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Give a copy of the order to the protected person or, if
the protected person is a minor child, to a parent or guardian
of the endangered child who is not a restrained person, if the
parent or guardian can reasonably be located, or to a person
having temporary custody of the endangered child.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  File a copy of the order with the court as soon as
practicable after issuance.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Have the order entered into the computer database
system for protective and restraining orders maintained by
the Department of Justice.
