# General Provisions

## Section 355

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Reversal of judgment; limitation on new action If an action is commenced within the time prescribed therefor, and a judgment therein for the plaintiff be reversed on appeal other than on the merits, a new action may be commenced within one year after the reversal. 
(Enacted in 1872. 
Amended by Stats.1992, c. 178 (S.B.1496),
