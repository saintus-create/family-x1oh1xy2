Application of law of state
Except as otherwise provided in this part, a responding
tribunal of this state shall:
(1)  apply the procedural and substantive law generally
applicable to similar proceedings originating in this state and
may exercise all powers and provide all remedies available in
those proceedings; and
(2)  determine the duty of support and the amount payable
in accordance with the law and support guidelines of this
state.
