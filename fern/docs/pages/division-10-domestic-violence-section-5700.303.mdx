# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.303

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Application of law of state Except as otherwise provided in this part, a responding tribunal of this state shall: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  apply the procedural and substantive law generally applicable to similar proceedings originating in this state and may exercise all powers and provide all remedies available in those proceedings; and 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  determine the duty of support and the amount payable in accordance with the law and support guidelines of this state.
