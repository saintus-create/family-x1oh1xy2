# 6710 — Right of disaffirmance

Minors

Right of disaffirmance
Treatises and Practice Aids
Except as otherwise provided by statute, a contract of a
minor may be disaffirmed by the minor before majority or
California Practice Guide Civil Procedure before Trial - Claims and
within a reasonable time afterwards or, in case of the minor’s
death within that period, by the minor’s heirs or personal
Defenses Ch. 9(1)-B; Elements of Claim.
