# Adoption

## Section 8718

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Appearance before court; adoptive parents and child The prospective adoptive parents and the child proposed to be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections 8612 and 8613.
