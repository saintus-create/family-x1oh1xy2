# Adoption

## Section 8624

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Advertising Any advertising by an adoption facilitator shall: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Identify the name of the party placing the advertisement and shall state that the party is an adoption facilitator. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Be subject to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17500.">17500</a> of the Business and Professions Code. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Provide, in any written advertisement, the disclosure required by subdivision (a) in print that is the same size and typeface as the name required pursuant to subdivision (a) or any telephone number specified in the advertisement, whichever is the larger print size. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Provide the disclosure required by subdivision (a) in the same color as the most prominent print in the advertisement where the advertisement contains more than one color. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Present the disclosure required by subdivision (a) in a readily understandable manner and at the same speed and volume, if applicable, as the rest of the advertisement if the advertisement is a television advertisement.
