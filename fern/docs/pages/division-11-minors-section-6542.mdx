# Minors

## Section 6542

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Commencement; retroactive allowances A family allowance commences on the date of the court’s order or such other time as may be provided in the court’s 
§ 6543 order, whether before or after the date of the order, as the court in its discretion determines, but the allowance may not be made retroactive to a date earlier than the date of the decedent’s death.
