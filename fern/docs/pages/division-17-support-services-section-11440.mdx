# 11440 — If it appears that a debt of the decedent has been paid or is

Support Services

If it appears that a debt of the decedent has been paid or is
payable in whole or in part by the surviving spouse, or that a
debt of the surviving spouse has been paid or is payable in
whole or in part from property in the decedent’s estate, the
personal representative, the surviving spouse, or a beneficiary
may, at any time before an order for final distribution is
made, petition for an order to allocate the debt.
Comments
1990 Enactment
Background on Section 11441 of Repealed Code
Section 11441 was added by 1987 Cal.Stat. ch. 923 § 93. The
section combined subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of former Probate Code Section
980 (repealed by 1987 Cal.Stat. ch. 923 § 48.6) and portions of
former Probate Code Sections 704.2 and 704.4 (provisions repealed
by 1987 Cal.Stat. ch. 923 § 37). [20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001
(Stats. 1990,
(1990)].
