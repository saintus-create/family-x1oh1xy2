# § 3915 — Custodian’s expenses, compensation and bond

Custodian’s expenses, compensation and bond
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A custodian is entitled to reimbursement from custodial property for reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of the custodian’s duties.
1129

PROBATE
,
Duties and Compensation of Custodian.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except for one who is a transferor under Section 3904,
a custodian has a noncumulative election during each calendar year to charge reasonable compensation for services
performed during that year.
