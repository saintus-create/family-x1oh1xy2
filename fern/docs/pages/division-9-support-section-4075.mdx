# § 4075 — Maintenance; application of article

maintenance; application of article

Section 4072 continues former Civil Code Section 4722(c) without
substantive change. The language of the former section has been
revised for clarity. See also Section 10 (singular includes plural).
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
and
separate
This article shall not be construed to affect the treatment
of spousal support and separate maintenance payments
pursuant to Section 71 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954
(26 U.S.C. Sec. 71). (Added by Stats. 1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
