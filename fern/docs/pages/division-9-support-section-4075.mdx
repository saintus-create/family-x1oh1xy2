# § 4075 — Tax treatment of spousal support and separate

Tax treatment of spousal support and separate
maintenance; application of article
This article shall not be construed to affect the treatment
of spousal support and separate maintenance payments
pursuant to Section 71 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954
(26 U.S.C. Sec. 71). (Added by Stats. 1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
