# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6323

<div class="case-callout">
<strong>Case Annotation: Gonzalez v. Munoz (2007)</strong>

**Citation:** 156 Cal.App.4

The trial court abused its discretion by failing to consider custody and visitation orders the petitioner requested in her DVPA petition. The opinion confirmed that trial courts can make such orders, and when doing so the court must consider whether failure to enter orders would jeopardize the safety of petitioner and children for whom orders are sought.
