Exception: Proceeding to determine sanity of crim-
inal defendant
There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding
under Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 1367) of Title 10
of Part 2 of the Penal Code initiated at the request of the
defendant
in a criminal
action
to determine
his sanity.
