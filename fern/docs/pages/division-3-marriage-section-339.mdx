# 339

Warrant of arrest against parent or guardian

Warrant of arrest against parent or guardian
In case such citation cannot be served, or the person served
fails to obey it, or in any case in which it appears to the court
WELFARE AND INSTITUTIONS CODE
that the citation will probably be ineffective, a warrant of
arrest may issue on the order of the court either against the
parent, or guardian, or the person having the custody of the
minor,
or with whom
the minor
is living.
