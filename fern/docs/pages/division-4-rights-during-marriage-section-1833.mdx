# § 1833 — Petition; contents

Petition; contents
The petition shall:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Allege that a controversy exists between the spouses or
parents and request the aid of the court
to effect
a
reconciliation or an amicable settlement of the controversy.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  State the name and age of each minor child whose
welfare may be affected by the controversy.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  State the name and address of the petitioner or the
names and addresses of the petitioners.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If the petition is presented by one spouse or parent
only, the name of the other spouse or parent as a respondent,
and state the address of that spouse or parent.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Name as a respondent any other person who has any
relation to the controversy, and state the address of the
person if known to the petitioner.

FAMILY CONCILIATION COURT LAW
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  If the petition arises out of an instance , so state generally and without specific allegations as
to the incident.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  State any other information the court by rule requires.
