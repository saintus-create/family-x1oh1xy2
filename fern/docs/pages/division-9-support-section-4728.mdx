# § 4728 — Enforcement of penalties; priorities

Enforcement of penalties; priorities
Penalties due pursuant to this chapter may be enforced by
the issuance of a writ of execution in the same manner as a
writ of execution may be issued for unpaid installments of
child support, as described in Chapter 7 (commencing with
Section 5100), except that payment of penalties under this
chapter may not take priority over payment of arrearages or
current support.
