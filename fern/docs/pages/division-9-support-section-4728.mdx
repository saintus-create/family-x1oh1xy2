# Support

## Section 4728

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Enforcement of penalties; priorities Penalties due pursuant to this chapter may be enforced by the issuance of a writ of execution in the same manner as a writ of execution may be issued for unpaid installments of child support, as described in Chapter 7 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5100.">Section 5100</a>), except that payment of penalties under this chapter may not take priority over payment of arrearages or current support.
