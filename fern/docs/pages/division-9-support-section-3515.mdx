---
title: "3515. Separate property; quasi-community property"
description: "Separate property; quasi-community property"
slug: division-9-support-section-3515
---

“Separate property” does not include quasi-community property.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
