# 10012
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a proceeding in which mediation is required pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 10005, where there has been a history between the parties or where a protective order as defined in Section 6218 is in effect, at the request of the party alleging domestic violence in a written declaration under penalty of perjury or protected by the order, the family law facilitator shall meet with the parties separately and at separate times.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any intake form that the office of the family law facilitator requires the parties to complete before the commencement of mediation shall state that, if a party alleging domestic violence in a written declaration under penalty of perjury or a party protected by a protective order so requests, the mediator will meet with the parties separately and at separate times.
