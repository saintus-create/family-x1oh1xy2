# Adoption

## Section 8637

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Attorneys; facilitating adoptions; application of law Notwithstanding the provisions of this chapter, an attorney who provides services specified in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8623.">8623</a> related to facilitating an adoption shall be subject only to those provisions of law regulating the practice of law.
