# 1221. Illness for excuse for not bringing in prisoner;

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Illness for excuse for not bringing in prisoner;
unnecessary restraint
Whenever, by the provisions of this title, an officer is
required to keep a person arrested on a warrant of attach-
ment in custody, and to bring him before a court or judge, the
inability, from illness or otherwise, of the person to attend, is
a sufficient excuse for not bringing him up; and the officer
must not confine a person arrested upon the warrant in a
prison, or otherwise restrain him of personal liberty, except so
far as may be necessary to secure his personal attendance.
(Enacted in 1872.
Amended by Stats.1933, c. 745, p. 1907,
$ 19; Stats.1951,
c. 1737, p. 4143, § 170, operative Jan. 1,
1952.)
