# § 3668.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 6 — Modification,Termination, Or Set Aside Of Support Orders*

The Judicial Council shall adopt forms which shall be used in the procedure provided by this article. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) ARTICLE 3. Simplified Procedure for Modification of Support Order [3680 - 3680.5] ( Article 3 added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 957, Sec. 5. )
