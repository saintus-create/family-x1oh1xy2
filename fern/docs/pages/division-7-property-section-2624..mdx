# § 2624.

*Part 6 — Debts And Liabilities*

Debts incurred by either spouse after entry of a judgment of dissolution of marriage but before termination of the parties’ marital status or after entry of a judgment of legal separation of the parties shall be confirmed without offset to the spouse who incurred the debt. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
