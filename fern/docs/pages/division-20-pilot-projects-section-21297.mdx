# 21297

Final compensation

Final compensation
For a nonmember, “final compensation” means the highest
average annual compensation earnable by the member during
the three consecutive years, or one year where applicable,
prior to the date of dissolution of marriage or legal separa-
tion. The nonmember may designate an earlier period to be
used where the time period of the nonmember’s marriage to
the member and membership correspond.
