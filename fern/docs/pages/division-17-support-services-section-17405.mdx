# § 17405 — Local child support agency; interview of custodial

Local child support agency; interview of custodial
parent
In carrying out duties under this article, the local child
support agency shall interview the custodial parent within 10
business days of opening a child support case. This interview
shall solicit financial and all other information about the
noncustodial parent. This information shall be acted upon
immediately. The local child support agency shall reinterview the custodial parent as needed. (Added by Stats.1999, c.
