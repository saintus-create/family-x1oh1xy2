# Support Services

## Section 17405

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Local child support agency; interview of custodial parent In carrying out duties under this article, the local child support agency shall interview the custodial parent within 10 business days of opening a child support case. 
This interview shall solicit financial and all other information about the noncustodial parent. 
This information shall be acted upon immediately. 
The local child support agency shall reinterview the custodial parent as needed.
