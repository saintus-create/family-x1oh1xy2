# § 4301.

*Part 3 — Spousal Support · Chapter 1 — Duty To Support Spouse*

Subject to Section 914, a person shall support the person’s spouse while they are living together out of the separate property of the person when there is no community property or quasi-community property. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
