# California Family Code § 2128

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Nothing in this chapter prohibits a party from seeking relief under Section 2556.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in this chapter changes existing law with respect to contract remedies where the contract has not been merged or incorporated into a judgment.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this chapter is intended to restrict a family law court from acting as a court of equity.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Nothing in this chapter is intended to limit existing law with respect to the modification or enforcement of support orders.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Nothing in this chapter affects the rights of a bona fide lessee, purchaser, or encumbrancer for value of real property.
