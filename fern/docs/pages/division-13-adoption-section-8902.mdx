# Adoption

## Section 8902

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Agency services For intercountry adoptions that will be finalized in this state, the licensed adoption agency shall provide all of the following services: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Assessment of the suitability of the applicant’s home. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Placement of the foreign-born child in an approved home. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Postplacement supervision. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Submission to the court of a report on the intercountry adoptive placement with a recommendation regarding the granting of the petition. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Services to applicants seeking to adopt related children living in foreign countries. 
The Legislature recognizes that these children have an impelling need for adoptive placement with their relatives.
