6800. Failure to leave person to take by testate or intestate
Sections 6600 to 6613, inclusive, do not apply if the
decedent died before July 1, 1987. If the decedent died
before July 1, 1987, the case continues to be governed by the
law applicable to the case prior to July 1, 1987. (Stats.1990,
c. 79 (A.B.759), 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
