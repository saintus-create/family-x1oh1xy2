# 20022

Tax return; review by other party; examination

The tax return submitted pursuant to Section 20020 may be reviewed by the other party. A party may be examined by the other party as to the contents of the tax return. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.
