---
title: "20022. Tax return; review by other party; examination"
description: "Tax return; review by other party; examination"
slug: division-20-pilot-projects-section-20022
---

The tax return submitted pursuant to Section 20020 may be reviewed by the other party. A party may be examined by the other party as to the contents of the tax return.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.*
