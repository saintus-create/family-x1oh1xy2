# § 20022 — Review by other party;

review by other party;
Tax return;
examination
The tax return submitted pursuant to Section 20020 may be
reviewed by the other party. A party may be examined by the
other party as to the contents of the tax return. (Added by
c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 210.)
