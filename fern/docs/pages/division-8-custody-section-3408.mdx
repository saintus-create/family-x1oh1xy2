# 3408 — JURISDICTION

Custody of Children

JURISDICTION
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The immunity granted by subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  does not
extend to civil litigation based on acts unrelated to the
participation in a proceeding under this part committed by an
individual while present in this state. (Added by Stats.1999, c.
867 (S.B.668), $ 3.)
Notice to persons outside California
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notice required for the exercise of jurisdiction when a
person is outside this state may be given in a manner
prescribed by the law of this state for service of process or by
the law of the state in which the service is made. Notice must
be given in a manner reasonably calculated to give actual
notice but may be by publication if other means are not
effective.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Proof of service may be made in the manner prescribed
by the law of this state or by the law of the state in which the
service is made.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notice is not required for the exercise of jurisdiction
with respect to a person who submits to the jurisdiction of the
court. (Added by Stats.1999, c. 867 (S.B.668), § 3.)
