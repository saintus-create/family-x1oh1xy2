# § 7902.

*Part 5 — Interstate Compact On Placement Of Children*

Financial responsibility for a child placed pursuant to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children shall be determined in accordance with Article 5 of the compact in the first instance. However, in the event of partial or complete default of performance thereunder, the provisions of other state laws also may be invoked. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
