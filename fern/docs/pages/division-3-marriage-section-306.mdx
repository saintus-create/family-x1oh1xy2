Procedural requirements;
effect of noncompliance
Except as provided in Section 307, a marriage shall be
licensed, solemnized, and authenticated, and the authenticat-
ed marriage license shall be returned to the county recorder
of the county where the marriage license was issued, as
provided in this part.
Noncompliance with this part by a
nonparty to the marriage does not invalidate the marriage.
