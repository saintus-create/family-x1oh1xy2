# § 4602

*If requested by an obligor-parent, the deposit holder shall*

If requested by an obligor-parent, the deposit holder shall
prepare a statement setting forth disbursements and receipts
made under this chapter.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)

1.
