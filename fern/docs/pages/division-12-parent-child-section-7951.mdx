---
title: "7951. This part does not apply in determining the foster care"
description: "This part does not apply in determining the foster care"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-7951
---

This part does not apply in determining the foster care setting in which the child may be placed for a period not intended to exceed 30 days. Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 884, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.
