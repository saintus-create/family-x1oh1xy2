# § 7951 — This part does not apply in determining the foster care

This part does not apply in determining the foster care setting in which the child may be placed for a period not intended to exceed 30 days. _(Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 884, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)_
