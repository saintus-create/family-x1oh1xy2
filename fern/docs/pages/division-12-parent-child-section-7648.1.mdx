# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7648.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

If the court denies a motion pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7648.">Section 7648</a>, the court shall state on the record the basis for the denial of that motion and any supporting facts.
