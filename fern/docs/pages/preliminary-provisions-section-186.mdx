# § 186

*have priority over child support*

have priority over child support
claims, unless the child support obligee establishes a legitimately
acquired interest in defendant’s frozen assets. People v. Mozes, 192
Cal.App.4th 1124, 121 Cal.Rptr.3d 808 (2011), review denied.
