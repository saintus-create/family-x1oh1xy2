# 1125 — End of mediation;

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

End of mediation;
satisfaction of conditions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For purposes of confidentiality under this chapter, a
mediation ends when any one of the following conditions is
satisfied:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The parties execute a written settlement agreement
that fully resolves the dispute.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An oral agreement that fully resolves the dispute is
reached in accordance with Section 1118.

Oral agreements; conditions to admissibility
An oral agreement made in the course of, or pursuant to, a
mediation is not made inadmissible, or protected from
disclosure, by the provisions of this chapter if any of the
following conditions are satisfied:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The agreement is in accordance with Section 1118.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The agreement is in accordance with subdivisions (a),
(b), and <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of Section 1118, and all parties to the agreement
expressly agree, in writing or orally in accordance with
Section 1118, to disclosure of the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The agreement is in accordance with subdivisions (a),
(b), and <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of Section 1118, and the agreement is used to
show fraud, duress, or illegality that is relevant to an issue in
dispute. (Added by Stats. 1997, c. 772 (A.B.939), § 3.)

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A party provides the mediator and the other mediation
participants with a writing stating that the mediation is
terminated, or words to that effect, which shall be consistent
with Section 1121. In a mediation involving more than two
parties, the mediation may continue as to the remaining
parties or be terminated in accordance with this section.
