# § 850 — Transmutation by agreement or transfer

Transmutation by agreement or transfer.
851.
Transmutation subject to fraudulent transfer laws.
852.
Requirements.

CHARACTERIZATION OF MARITAL PROPERTY
Section
853:, Characterization of property in will;
admissibility in
proceedings commenced before death of testator;
waiver of right to joint and survivor annuity or
survivor’s benefits;

nonprobate transfers of community property.
