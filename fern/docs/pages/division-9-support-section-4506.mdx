# § 4506

*Certification of abstract of judgment;*

Certification of abstract of judgment;
contents;
form; notice of support judgment
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An abstract of a judgment ordering a party to pay
spousal, child, or family support to the other party shall be
certified by the clerk of the court where the judgment was
entered and shall contain all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The title of the court where the judgment is entered
and the cause and number of the proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The date of entry of the judgment and of any renewal
of the judgment.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Where the judgment and any renewals are entered in
the records of the court.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The name and last known address of the party ordered
to pay support.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The name and address of the party to whom support
payments are ordered to be paid.

SUPPORT
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Only the last four digits of the social security number,
birth date, and driver’s license number of the party who is
ordered to pay support.
If any of those numbers are not
known to the party to whom support payments are to be paid,
that fact shall be indicated on the abstract of the court
judgment.
This paragraph shall not apply to documents
created prior to January 1, 2010.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Whether a stay of enforcement has been ordered by
the court and, if so, the date the stay ends.
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  The date of issuance of the abstract.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  Any other information deemed reasonable and appro-
priate by the Judicial Council.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The Judicial Council may develop a form for an
abstract of a judgment ordering a party to pay child, family,
or spousal support to another party which contains the
information required by subdivision (a).
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, when a
support obligation is being enforced pursuant to Title IV-D
of the Social Security Act, the agency enforcing the obligation
may record a notice of support judgment.
The notice of
support judgment shall contain the same information as the
form adopted by the Judicial Council pursuant to subdivision
