# 17211 — county demonstration projects to provide employment

Support Services

county demonstration projects to provide employment
and training services to nonsupporting noncustodial
parents
The department shall administer the Child Support Assurance Demonstration Project established by Article 5 (commencing with Section 18241) of Chapter 3.3 of Part 6 of the
Welfare and Institutions Code, and the county demonstration
projects to provide employment and training services to
nonsupporting noncustodial parents authorized by Section
18205.5 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
