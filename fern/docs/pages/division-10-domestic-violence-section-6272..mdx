# § 6272.

*Part 3 — Emergency Protective Orders · Chapter 3 — Duties Of Law Enforcement Officer*

(a) A law enforcement officer shall use every reasonable means to enforce an emergency protective order.

(b) A law enforcement officer who acts in good faith to enforce an emergency protective order is not civilly or criminally liable.
