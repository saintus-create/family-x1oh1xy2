# § 8915.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 4 — Intercountry Adoptions*

When any report or findings are submitted to the court by a licensed adoption agency, a copy of the report or findings, whether favorable or unfavorable, shall be given to the petitioner’s attorney in the proceeding, if the petitioner has an attorney of record, or to the petitioner. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
