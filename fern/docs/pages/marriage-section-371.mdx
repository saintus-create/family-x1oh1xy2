# General Provisions

## Section 371

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Spouses sued together; defense by spouse If spouses are sued together, each may defend for his or her own right, but if one spouse neglects to defend, the other spouse may defend for that spouse’s right also. 
(Enacted in 1872. 
Amended by Stats.1975, c. 1241, p. 3187, $ 3; Stats. 
2016, c. 50 (S.B.1005), § 15, eff: Jan. 1, 2017.)
