# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1035.2

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Sexual assault counselor As used in this article, “sexual assault counselor” means any of the following: 
(a) A person who is engaged in any office, hospital, institution, or center commonly known as a rape crisis center, whose primary purpose is the rendering of advice or assistance to victims of sexual assault and who has received a certificate evidencing completion of a training program in the counseling of sexual assault victims issued by a counseling center that meets the criteria for the award of a grant established pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13837.">Section 13837</a> of the Penal Code and who meets one of the following requirements: 
(1) Is a psychotherapist as defined in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1010.">Section 1010</a>; has a master’s degree in counseling or a related field; or has one year of counseling experience, at least six months of which is in rape crisis counseling. 
(2) Has 40 hours of training as described below and is supervised by an individual who qualifies as a counselor under paragraph (1). The training, supervised by a person qualified under paragraph (1), shall include, but not be limited to, the following areas: 
(A) Law. 
(B) Medicine. 
(C) Societal attitudes. 
(D) Crisis intervention and counseling techniques. 
(E) Role playing. 
(F) Referral services. 
(G) Sexuality. 
(b) A person who is engaged in a program on the campus of a public or private institution of higher education, whose primary purpose is the rendering of advice or assistance to victims of sexual assault and who has received a certificate evidencing completion of a training program in the counseling of sexual assault victims issued by a counseling center that meets the criteria for the award of a grant established pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13837.">Section 13837</a> of the Penal Code and who meets one of the following requirements: 
(1) Is a psychotherapist as defined in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1010.">Section 1010</a>; has a master’s degree in counseling or a related field; or has one 970 year of counseling experience, at least six months of which is in rape crisis counseling.

(A) Law. 
(B) Medicine. 
(C) Societal attitudes. 
(D) Crisis intervention and counseling techniques. 
(E) Role playing.
