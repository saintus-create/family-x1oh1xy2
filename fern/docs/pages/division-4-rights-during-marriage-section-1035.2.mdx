# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1035.2

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Sexual assault counselor As used in this article, “sexual assault counselor” means any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A person who is engaged in any office, hospital, institution, or center commonly known as a rape crisis center, whose primary purpose is the rendering of advice or assistance to victims of sexual assault and who has received a certificate evidencing completion of a training program in the counseling of sexual assault victims issued by a counseling center that meets the criteria for the award of a grant established pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13837.">§ 13837</a> of the Penal Code and who meets one of the following requirements: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Is a psychotherapist as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1010.">§ 1010</a>; has a master’s degree in counseling or a related field; or has one year of counseling experience, at least six months of which is in rape crisis counseling. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Has 40 hours of training as described below and is supervised by an individual who qualifies as a counselor under paragraph (1). The training, supervised by a person qualified under paragraph (1), shall include, but not be limited to, the following areas: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Law. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Medicine. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Societal attitudes. 
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  Crisis intervention and counseling techniques. 
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  Role playing. 
<span class="subsec">(F)</span>  Referral services. 
<span class="subsec">(G)</span>  Sexuality. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A person who is engaged in a program on the campus of a public or private institution of higher education, whose primary purpose is the rendering of advice or assistance to victims of sexual assault and who has received a certificate evidencing completion of a training program in the counseling of sexual assault victims issued by a counseling center that meets the criteria for the award of a grant established pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13837.">§ 13837</a> of the Penal Code and who meets one of the following requirements: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Is a psychotherapist as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1010.">§ 1010</a>; has a master’s degree in counseling or a related field; or has one 970 year of counseling experience, at least six months of which is in rape crisis counseling.
