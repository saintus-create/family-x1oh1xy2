# § 3771

*Information provided to the local child support*

Information provided to the local child support
agency
Upon request of the local child support agency the
employer shall provide the following information to the local
child support agency within 30 days:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The social security number of the absent parent.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The home address of the absent parent.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Whether the absent parent has a health insurance
policy and, if so, the policy names and numbers, and the
names of the persons covered.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Whether the health insurance policy provides coverage
for dependent children of the absent parent who do not
reside in the absent parent’s home.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  If there is a subsequent lapse in health insurance
coverage, the employer shall notify the local child support
agency, giving the date the coverage ended, the reason for the
lapse in coverage and, if the lapse is temporary, the date upon
which coverage is expected to resume.
