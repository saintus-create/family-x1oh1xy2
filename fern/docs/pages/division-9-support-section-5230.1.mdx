# § 5230.1 — Earning assignment or income withholding order

Earning assignment or income withholding order
of another state; enforceability; applicable law
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An earnings assignment or income withholding order
for support issued by a court or administrative agency of
another state is binding upon an employer of the obligor to
the same extent as an earnings assignment order made by a
court of this state.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  When an employer receives an earnings assignment
order or an income withholding order for support from a
court or administrative agency in another state, all of the
provisions of this chapter shall apply.
