# § 362.04

*10th Torts § 51 (2018), In General.*

10th Torts § 51 (2018), In General.
Babysitting of foster child
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For purposes of this section:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Caregiver” means any licensed certified foster parent,
approved relative caregiver, or approved nonrelative extended family member, or approved resource family.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Reasonable and prudent parent” or “reasonable and
prudent parent standard” has the meaning set forth in
subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 362.05.
