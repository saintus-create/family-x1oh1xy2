# 3132

District attorney to act on behalf of court

In performing the functions described in Sections 3130 and 3131, the district attorney shall act on behalf of the court and shall not represent any party to the custody proceedings. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
