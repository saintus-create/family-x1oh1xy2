# Custody of Children

## Section 3132

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

District attorney to act on behalf of court In performing the functions described in Sections 3130 and 3131, the district attorney shall act oh behalf of the court and shall not represent any party to the custody proceedings.
