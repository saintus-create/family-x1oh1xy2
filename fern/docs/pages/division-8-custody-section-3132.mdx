# § 3132

*District attorney to act on behalf of court*

District attorney to act on behalf of court
In performing the functions described in Sections 3130 and
3131, the district attorney shall act oh behalf of the court and
shall not represent any party to the custody proceedings.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
