# § 8904 — For an intercountry adoption that will be finalized in a

For an intercountry adoption that will be finalized in a
foreign country, the licensed adoption agency shall provide all
of the following services:
California
Judicial
Council
ADOPT-215,

Order.
Verification
of Compliance With Hague Adoption Convention Attachment.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Assessment of the suitability of the applicant’s home.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Certification to the United States Citizenship and
Immigration * * * Services that this state’s intercountry
adoption requirements have been met.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Readoption services as required by the United States
Citizenship and Immigration * * * Services.

,
Powers and Duties of Licensed or Accredited Adoption Agencies.
