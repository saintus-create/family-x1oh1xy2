# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7907.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Bringing or sending of Indian child into another state The Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children shall not apply to any placement, sending, or bringing of an Indian child into another state pursuant to a transfer of jurisdiction to a tribal court under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1911.">§ 1911</a> of the Indian Child Welfare Act (25 U.S.C. Sec. 1901 et seq.).
