# § 7907.3 — Bringing or sending of Indian child into another

Bringing or sending of Indian child into another
state
The Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children shall
not apply to any placement, sending, or bringing of an Indian
child into another state pursuant to a transfer of jurisdiction
to a tribal court under Section 1911 of the Indian Child
Welfare Act (25 U.S.C. Sec. 1901 et seq.).
