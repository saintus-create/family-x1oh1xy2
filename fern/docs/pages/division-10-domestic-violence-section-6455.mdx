# § 6455 — Immunity

Immunity
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  There shall be no civil liability on the part of, and no
cause of action for false arrest or false imprisonment against,

a law enforcement officer who makes an arrest pursuant to a
Canadian domestic violence protection order that is regular
upon its face, if the law enforcement officer, in making the
arrest, acts in good faith and has reasonable cause to believe
that the person against whom the order is issued has notice of
the order and has committed an act in violation of the order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in this section shall be deemed to exonerate a
law enforcement officer from liability for the unreasonable
use of force in the enforcement of the order.
The immuni-
ties afforded by this section shall not affect the availability of
any other immunity that may apply, including, but not limited
to, Sections 820.2 and 820.4 of the Government Code.
