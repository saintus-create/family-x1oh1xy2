# 853. In proceedings commenced before death of testator;

*Premarital Agreements*

in proceedings commenced before death of testator;
waiver of right to joint and survivor annuity or survivor’s benefits; written joinders or consents to nonprobate
transfers of community property
review denied, which found a transmutation when a “transmutation
agreement” contained explicit and unambiguous language of present
transmutation, even though the agreement was incident to an estate
planning trust. Accord, In re Marriage of Lund, 174 Cal.App.4th 40, 94
Cal. Rptr.3d 84 (2009).
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A statement in a will of the character of property is not
admissible as evidence of a transmutation of the property in a
proceeding commenced before the death of the person who
made the will.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A waiver of a right to a joint and survivor annuity or
survivor’s benefits under the federal Retirement Equity Act
of 1984 (Public Law 98-397) | is not a transmutation of the
community property rights of the person executing the
waiver.
