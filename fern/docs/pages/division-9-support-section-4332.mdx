# 4332 — (1993). See Commentary to section 3600.

Support

(1993). See Commentary to section 3600.
In a proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal
separation of the parties, the court shall make specific factual
findings with respect to the standard of living during the
marriage, and, at the request of either party, the court shall
make appropriate factual determinations with respect to
Other circumstances.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
