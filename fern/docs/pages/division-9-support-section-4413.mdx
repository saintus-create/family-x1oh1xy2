# Support

## Section 4413

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Jurisdiction; notice to district attorney or county counsel If the parent is a resident of this state, the court does not have jurisdiction to make an order under this chapter until 30 days after the county counsel, or the district attorney in a county not having a county counsel, of the county in which the parent resides has been served with notice of the pendency of the proceeding.
