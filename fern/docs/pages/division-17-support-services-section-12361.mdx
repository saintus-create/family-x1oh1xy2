Pursuant to the affirmative power of Congress to enact
this part under section 5 of the Fourteenth Amendment to

the Constitution, it is the purpose of this part to protect the
civil rights of victims of gender motivated violence and to
promote public safety, health, and activities affecting interstate commerce by establishing a Federal civil rights cause of

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Concurrent jurisdiction
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Cause of action
The Federal and State courts shall have concurrent
jurisdiction over actions brought pursuant to this part.
A person (including a person who acts under color of any
statute, ordinance, regulation, custom, or usage of any State)
who commits a crime of violence motivated by gender and
thus deprives another of the right declared in subsection <span class="subsec">(b)</span> 
shall be liable to the party injured, in an action for the
recovery of compensatory and punitive damages, injunctive
and declaratory relief, and such other relief as a court may
deem appropriate.
