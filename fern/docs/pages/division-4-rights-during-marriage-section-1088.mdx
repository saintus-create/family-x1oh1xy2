# 1088

Of

of
the Unemployment Insurance Code.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  When engaged in a trade or business, paying wages or
other forms of compensation for services rendered by a child
support obligor that are not reported to the Employment
Development Department as required, including, but not
limited to, payment in cash or via barter or trade.
