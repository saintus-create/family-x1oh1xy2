# § 961 — Exception:

Exception:
Validity of writing affecting property
interest
There is no privilege under this article as to a communica-
tion relevant to an issue concerning the validity of a deed of
conveyance, will, or other writing, executed by a client, now
deceased, purporting to affect an interest in property.
(Stats.
1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
