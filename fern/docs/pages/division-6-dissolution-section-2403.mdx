# § 2403 — Entry of judgment of dissolution; notice

Entry of judgment of dissolution; notice
When six months have expired from the date of the filing
of the joint petition for summary dissolution, the court shall,
unless a revocation has been filed pursuant to Section 2402,
enter the judgment dissolving the marriage.
The judgment
restores to the parties the status of single persons, and either
party may marry after the entry of the judgment.
The clerk
shall send a notice of entry of judgment to each of the parties
at the party’s
last known
address.
