# § 8806 — Acceptance of consent of birth parent; ascertain-

Acceptance of consent of birth parent; ascertain-
ment of whether child is proper subject for adoption;
determination of suitability of home
’
The department or delegated county adoption agency shall
accept the consent of the birth parents to the adoption of the

ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS
child by the petitioners and, before filing its report with the
court, shall ascertain whether the child is a proper subject for
adoption and whether the proposed home is suitable for the
child.
