# California Family Code § 2330

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties is commenced by filing a petition or joint petition entitled “In re the marriage of ____ and ____,” which shall state whether it is a petition for dissolution of the marriage or for legal separation of the parties.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In a proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties, the petition shall set forth, among other matters, as nearly as can be ascertained, the following facts:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The date of marriage.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The date of separation.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The number of years from marriage to separation.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The number of children of the marriage, if any, and if none a statement of that fact.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The age and birth date of each minor child of the marriage.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In a joint petition proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties, the joint petition shall set forth, among other matters, as nearly as can be ascertained, the following facts:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The date of the marriage.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  If there are children of the marriage, all of the following information:

<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  The number of children.

<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  The age of each child.

(iii) The date of birth of each child.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  If there are no children of the marriage, a statement of that fact.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A list of issues upon which the parties intend to resolve by agreement, which may include, but is not limited to, the following:

<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  The legal grounds for dissolution or legal separation.

<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  The date of separation.

(iii) Child custody.

<span class="subsec">(iv)</span>  Child support.

<span class="subsec">(v)</span>  Spousal support or domestic partner support.

<span class="subsec">(vi)</span>  Separate property.

(vii) Community and quasi-community property.

(viii) Restoration of former name.

<span class="subsec">(ix)</span>  Attorney’s fees and costs.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The list of issues required by subparagraph <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  shall represent all of the matters at issue in the dissolution. If the parties have an issue that they do not intend to resolve by agreement, they shall file a petition pursuant to subdivision (b).
