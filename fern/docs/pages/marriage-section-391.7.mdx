# General Provisions

## Section 391.7

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

See also In re Marriage of Christy, 26 Cal.App.4th 256 (1994), rehearing granted 7/11/94 (imposing Code of Civil Procedure <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=907.">Section 907</a> sanctions on the sister of the children’s mother because the sister brought frivolous support and custody actions against the father on behalf of herself and the children’s mother); and Simonian v. 
Patterson, 27 Cal.App.4th 773, 32 Cal.Rptr.2d 722 (1994) (attorneyplaintiff and plaintiff's attorney sanctioned $50,000 for frivolous appeal from dismissal of plaintiffs action against father of plaintiffs former fiancee). 
Application for post-judgment attorney’s fees under this section must be filed within the 40-day time limit set out by Cal. Rules of Court 870.2(c) and 26(d). In re Marriage of Freeman, 132 Cal.App.4th 1, 33 Cal.Rptr.3d 237 (2005), review denied.
