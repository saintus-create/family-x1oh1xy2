# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16521.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

(b) A commissioner or referee who is assigned to conduct hearings held pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">Section 300</a> shall meet the minimum standards for education and training established pursuant to subdivision (a), by July 31, 1998. 
(c) The Judicial Council shall submit an annual report to the Legislature on compliance by judges, commissioners, and referees with the education and training standards described in subdivisions (a) and (b).
