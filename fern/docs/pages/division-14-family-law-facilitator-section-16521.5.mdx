# 16521.5

Hearings held pursuant to Section 300 shall meet the mini-

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A commissioner or referee who is assigned to conduct
hearings held pursuant to Section 300 shall meet the mini-
mum standards for education and training established pursu-
ant to subdivision (a), by July 31, 1998.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The Judicial Council shall submit an annual report to
the Legislature on compliance by judges, commissioners, and
referees with the education and training standards described
in subdivisions <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and (b).
