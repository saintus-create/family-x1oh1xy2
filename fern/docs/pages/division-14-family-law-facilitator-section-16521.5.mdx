# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16521.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A commissioner or referee who is assigned to conduct hearings held pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">§ 300</a> shall meet the minimum standards for education and training established pursuant to subdivision (a), by July 31, 1998. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The Judicial Council shall submit an annual report to the Legislature on compliance by judges, commissioners, and referees with the education and training standards described in subdivisions (a) and (b).
