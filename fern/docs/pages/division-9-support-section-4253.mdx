# Support

## Section 4253

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Default orders Notwithstanding any other provision of law, when hearing child support matters, a commissioner or referee may enter default orders if the defendant does not respond to notice or other process within the time prescribed to respond to that notice.
