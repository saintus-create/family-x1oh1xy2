care or group care; provision of updates to Legislature
It is the intent of the Legislature that no child or youth in
foster care reside in group care for longer than one year.
The State Department of Social Services shall provide
updates to the Legislature, commencing no later than January 1, 2014, regarding the outcomes of assessments of
children and youth who have been in group homes for longer
than one year and the corresponding outcomes of transitions,
or plans to transition, them into family settings. (Added by
c. 21 (A.B.74), 41, eff. June 27, 2013.)
