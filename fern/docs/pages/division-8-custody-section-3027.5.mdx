# § 3027.5 — Sexual abuse

Sexual abuse
of child;
report
or treatment;
limitations on custody or visitation
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A parent shall not be placed on supervised visitation,
or be denied custody of or visitation with the parent’s
child, and custody or visitation rights shall. not be
limited, solely because the parent
did any of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Lawfully reported suspected sexual abuse of the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Otherwise acted lawfully, based on a reasonable belief,
to determine if the child was the victim of sexual abuse
