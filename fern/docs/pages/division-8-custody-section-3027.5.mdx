# Custody of Children

## Section 3027.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Sexual abuse of child; report or treatment; limitations on custody or visitation 
(a) A parent shall not be placed on supervised visitation, or be denied custody of or visitation with the parent’s child, and custody or visitation rights shall. not be limited, solely because the parent did any of the following: 
(1) Lawfully reported suspected sexual abuse of the child. 
(2) Otherwise acted lawfully, based on a reasonable belief, to determine if the child was the victim of sexual abuse ok
