# § 7665 — Termination of unknown biological father’s. paren-

Termination of unknown biological father’s. paren-
‘tal rights; entry of order
If, after the inquiry, the court is unable to identify the
biological father or any possible biological father and no
person has appeared claiming to be the biological father and
claiming custodial rights, the court shall enter an order
terminating the unknown biological father’s parental rights
with reference to the child.
