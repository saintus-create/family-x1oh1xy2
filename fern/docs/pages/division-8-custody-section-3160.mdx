---
title: "3160. Mediators; availability; duties of court"
description: "Mediators; availability; duties of court"
slug: division-8-custody-section-3160
---

Each superior court shall make a mediator available. The court is not required to institute a family conciliation court in order to provide mediation services. Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.87. Effective January 1, 1994.
