# § 7562.

*Part 2 — Presumption Concerning Child Of Marriage And Genetic Testing To Determine Parentage · Chapter 2 — Genetic Testing To Determine Parentage*

If a person seeking genetic testing demonstrates good cause, the court may order genetic testing of a deceased person. (Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 24. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.) FAM Family Code - FAM
