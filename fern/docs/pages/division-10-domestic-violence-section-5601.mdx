# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5601

Registration by local child support agency of support order made in another county; procedures; motion to vacate; notice of registration 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When the local child support agency is responsible for the enforcement of a support order pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17400.">§ 17400</a>, the local child support agency may register a support order made in another county by utilizing the procedures set forth in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5602.">§ 5602</a> or by filing all of the following in the superior court of the agency’s county: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  An endorsed file copy of the most recent support order or a copy thereof. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A statement of arrearages, including an accounting of amounts ordered and paid each month, together with any added costs, fees, and interest. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A statement prepared by the local child support agency showing the post office address of the local child support agency, the last known place of residence or post office address of the obligor; the most recent address of the obligor set forth in the licensing records of the Department of Motor Vehicles, if known; and a list of other states and counties in California that are known to the local child support agency in which the original order of support and any modifications are registered. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The filing of the documents described in subdivision 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  constitutes registration under this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Promptly upon registration, the local child support agency , in compliance with the requirements of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1013.">§ 1013</a> of the Code of Civil Procedure, or in any other manner as provided by law, shall serve the obligor with copies of the documents described in subdivision (a). 
' 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If a motion to vacate registration is filed under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5603.">§ 5603</a>, a party may introduce into evidence copies of pleadings, documents, or orders that have been filed in the original court or other courts where the support order has been registered or modified. 
Certified copies of the documents shall not be required unless a party objects to the authenticity or accuracy of the document, in which case it shall be the responsibility of the party who is asserting the authenticity of the document to obtain a certified copy of the questioned document. 
(ec) Upon registration, the clerk of the court shall forward a notice of registration to the courts in other counties and states in which the original order for support and any OF modifications were issued or registered. 
Further proceedings regarding the obligor’s support obligations shall not be filed in other counties.
