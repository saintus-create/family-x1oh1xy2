# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2300

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Effect of dissolution The effect of a judgment of dissolution of marriage when it becomes final is to restore the parties to the state of unmarried persons.
