# 5700.706
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, a party who is an individual or a support enforcement agency seeking recognition of a Convention support order shall register the order in this state as provided in Chapter 6.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding Sections 5700.311 and 5700.602(a), a request for registration of a Convention support order must be accompanied by:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  a complete text of the support order or an abstract or extract of the support order drawn up by the issuing foreign tribunal, which may be in the form recommended by the Hague Conference on Private International Law;

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  a record stating that the support order is enforceable in the issuing country;

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  if the respondent did not appear and was not represented in the proceedings in the issuing country, a record attesting, as appropriate, either that the respondent had proper notice of the proceedings and an opportunity to be heard or that the respondent had proper notice of the support order and an opportunity to be heard in a challenge or appeal on fact or law before a tribunal;

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  a record showing the amount of arrears, if any, and the date the amount was calculated;

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  a record showing a requirement for automatic adjustment of the amount of support, if any, and the information necessary to make the appropriate calculations; and

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  if necessary, a record showing the extent to which the applicant received free legal assistance in the issuing country.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A request for registration of a Convention support order may seek recognition and partial enforcement of the order.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A tribunal of this state may vacate the registration of a Convention support order without the filing of a contest under Section 5700.707 only if, acting on its own motion, the tribunal finds that recognition and enforcement of the order would be manifestly incompatible with public policy.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The tribunal shall promptly notify the parties of the registration or the order vacating the registration of a Convention support order.
