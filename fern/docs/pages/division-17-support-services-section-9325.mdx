# 9325

No investigation or report to the court by any public officer

No investigation or report to the court by any public officer or agency is required, but the court may require the county probation officer or the department to investigate the circumstances of the proposed adoption and report thereon, with recommendations, to the court before the hearing. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
