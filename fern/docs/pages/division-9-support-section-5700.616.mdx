# § 5700.616

*Procedure to register child-support order of*

Procedure to register child-support order of
foreign country for modification
A party or support enforcement agency seeking to modify,
or to modify and enforce, a foreign child-support order not
under the Convention may register that order in this state
under Sections 5700.601 through 5700.608 if the order has
not been registered. A petition for modification may be filed
at the same time as a request for registration, or at another
time.
The petition must specify the grounds for modifica-
tion.
