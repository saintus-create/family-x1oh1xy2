# 5700.616
*Part 6  Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 6  Registration, Enforcement, And Modification Of Support Order*

A party or support enforcement agency seeking to modify, or to modify and enforce, a foreign child-support order not under the Convention may register that order in this state under Sections 5700.601 through 5700.608 if the order has not been registered. A petition for modification may be filed at the same time as a request for registration, or at another time. The petition must specify the grounds for modification. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.) FAM Family Code - FAM
