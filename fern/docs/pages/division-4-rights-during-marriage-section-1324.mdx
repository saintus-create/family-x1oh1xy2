# 1324

Produce evidence and if, but for those Penal Code sections or

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  After complying with an order to answer a question or
produce evidence and if, but for those Penal Code sections or
this rule, the witness would have been privileged to withhold
the answer given or the evidence produced, no testimony or
other information compelled under the order or information
directly or indirectly derived from the testimony or other
information may be used against the witness in any criminal
case, including any juvenile court proceeding under
