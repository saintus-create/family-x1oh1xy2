# § 2109 — Summary dissolution of marriage; required disclo-

The provisions of this chapter requiring a final declaration of disclosure do not apply to a summary dissolution of marriage, but a preliminary declaration of disclosure is required. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 11. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)_
