# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2109

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Summary dissolution of marriage; required disclosures The provisions.of this chapter. requiring a final declaration of disclosure do not apply to.a summary dissolution of marriage, but a preliminary declaration of disclosure is required.
