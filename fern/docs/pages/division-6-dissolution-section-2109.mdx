# § 2109 — Summary dissolution of marriage; required disclo-

Summary dissolution of marriage; required disclo-
sures
The provisions.of this chapter. requiring a final declaration
of disclosure do not apply to.a summary dissolution of
marriage, but a preliminary declaration
of disclosure
is
required.
