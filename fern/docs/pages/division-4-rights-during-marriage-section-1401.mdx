Application of definitions
Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the
definitions in this chapter govern the construction of this
division. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), 14, operative July 1,
1991.)
Guardianship - Conservatorship Law
The portion of this division consisting of (commencing with Section 1400), (commencing with Section
1500), (commencing with Section 1800), and
(commencing with Section 2100) may be cited as the Guardianship-Conservatorship Law. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759),
14, operative July 1, 1991.)
