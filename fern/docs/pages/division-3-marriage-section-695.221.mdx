# Marriage

## Section 695.221

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Crediting of money judgment for support Satisfaction of a money judgment for support shall be credited as follows: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The money shall first be credited against the current month’s support. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any remaining money shall next be credited against the principal amount of the judgment remaining unsatisfied. 
If the judgment is payable in installments, the remaining money shall be credited against the matured installments in the order in which they matured. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Any remaining money shall be credited against the accrued interest that remains unsatisfied. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  In cases enforced pursuant to Part D (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=651.">§ 651</a>) of Subchapter 4 of Chapter 7 of Title 42 of 905
