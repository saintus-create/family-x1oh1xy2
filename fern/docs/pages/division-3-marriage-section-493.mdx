<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Debt” means liability on a claim. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Debtor” means a person that is liable on a claim. 
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Electronic” means relating to technology having electrical, digital, magnetic, wireless, optical, electromagnetic, or similar capabilities. 
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  “Lien” means a charge against or an interest in property to secure payment of a debt or performance of an obligation, and includes a security interest created by agreement, a judicial lien obtained by legal or equitable process or proceedings, a common-law lien, or a statutory lien. 
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  “Organization” means a person other than an individual. 
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  “Person” means an individual, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, association, government or governmental subdivision, instrumentality or agency, business trust, estate, trust, business or nonprofit entity, or other legal entity. 
<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  “Property” means anything that may be the subject of ownership. 
<span class="subsec">(k)</span>  “Record” means information that is inscribed on a tangible medium or that is stored in an electronic or other medium and is retrievable in perceivable form. 
<span class="subsec">(J)</span>  “Sign” means, with present intent to authenticate or adopt a record, to either (1) execute or adopt a tangible symbol, or (2) attach to or logically associate with the record an electronic symbol, sound, or process. 
<span class="subsec">(m)</span>  “Transfer” means every mode, direct or indirect, absolute or conditional, voluntary or involuntary, of disposing of or parting with an asset or an interest in an asset, and includes payment of money, release, lease, license, and creation of a lien or other encumbrance. 
<span class="subsec">(n)</span>  “Valid lien” means a lien that is effective against the holder of a judicial lien subsequently obtained by legal or equitable process or proceedings.
