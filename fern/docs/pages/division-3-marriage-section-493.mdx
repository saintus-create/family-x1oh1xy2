# § 493 — Of the Code of

of the Code of
Civil Procedure, of a debtor.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Debt” means liability on a claim.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Debtor” means a person that is liable on a claim.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Electronic” means relating to technology having elec-
trical, digital, magnetic, wireless, optical, electromagnetic, or
similar capabilities.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  “Lien” means
a charge against or an interest
in
property to secure payment of a debt or performance of an
obligation, and includes a security interest created by agree-
ment, a judicial lien obtained by legal or equitable process or
proceedings, a common-law lien, or a statutory lien.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  “Organization” means a person other than an individ-
ual.
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  “Person” means an individual, partnership, corpora-
tion, limited liability company, association, government or
governmental subdivision, instrumentality or agency, business
trust, estate, trust, business or nonprofit entity, or other legal
entity.
<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  “Property” means anything that may be the subject of
ownership.
<span class="subsec">(k)</span>  “Record” means information that is inscribed on a
tangible medium or that is stored in an electronic or other
medium and is retrievable in perceivable form.
<span class="subsec">(J)</span>  “Sign” means, with present intent to authenticate or
adopt a record, to either <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  execute or adopt a tangible
symbol, or <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  attach to or logically associate with the record
an electronic symbol, sound, or process.
<span class="subsec">(m)</span>  “Transfer” means every mode, direct or indirect,
absolute or conditional, voluntary or involuntary, of disposing
of or parting with an asset or an interest in an asset, and
includes payment of money,
release, lease, license, and
creation of a lien or other encumbrance.
<span class="subsec">(n)</span>  “Valid lien” means a lien that is effective against the
holder of a judicial lien subsequently obtained by legal or
equitable process or proceedings.
