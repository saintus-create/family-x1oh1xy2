# § 10012

*parties*

parties
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a proceeding in which mediation is required pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 10005,
where there has been a history between
the parties or where a protective order as defined in Section
6218 is in effect, at the request of the party alleging domestic
violence in a written declaration under penalty of perjury or
protected by the order, the family law facilitator shall meet
with the parties separately and at separate times.
