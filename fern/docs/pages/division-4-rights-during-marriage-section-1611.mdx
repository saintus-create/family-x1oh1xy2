# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1611

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Form and execution of agreement; consideration A premarital agreement shali be in writing and signed by both parties. 
It is enforceable without consideration. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 
162 
(A.B.2650), 
§ 10, operative Jan. 
1, 1994.)
