# Adoption

## Section 8605

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Children without presumed father; consent of mother required A child not having a presumed father under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7611.">Section 7611</a> may not be adopted without the consent of the child’s mother, if living.

**Source:** California Family Code Annotated (Grace Ganz Blumberg, 2020 Desktop Edition). [View on California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8605.).

</div>
