# § 4852 — Pardon as provided in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section

01) of Title 6 of Part 3 of the Penal Code or a
pardon as provided in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section
4800) or Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 4850) of Title
6 of Part 3 of the Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Following a conviction for murder or attempted mur-
der, the defendant has been paroled based in whole or in part
upon evidence presented to the Board of Prison Terms that
the defendant committed the crime because he or she was the
victim of intimate partner battering.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  The defendant was convicted of murder or attempted
murder in the second degree in a trial at which substantial
evidence was presented that the person committed the crime
because he or she was a victim of intimate partner battering.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  The defendant was unlawfully imprisoned
or re-
strained but has been released from prison after successfully
prosecuting a writ of habeas corpus pursuant to Chapter 1
(commencing with Section 1473) of Title 12 of Part 2 of the
Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the sentence or judgment is stayed, the time for the
commencement of the action shall be tolled until the stay is
lifted.
For purposes of this section, a judgment is not stayed
if the judgment is appealed or the defendant is placed on
probation.
(d)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  shall apply to any action com-
menced before, on, or after the effective date of this section,
including any action otherwise barred by a limitation of time
in effect prior to the effective date of this section, thereby
reviving those causes of action that had lapsed or expired
under the law in effect prior to the effective date of this
section.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  does not apply to either of the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Any claim that has been litigated to finality on the
merits in any court of competent jurisdiction prior to January
1, 2003.
For purposes of this section, termination of a prior
action on the basis of the statute of limitations does not
constitute a claim that has been litigated to finality on the
merits.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Any written, compromised settlement agreement that
has been entered into between a plaintiff and a defendant if
the plaintiff was represented by an attorney who was admit-
ted to practice law in this state at the time of the settlement,
and the plaintiff signed the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Any restitution paid by the defendant to the victim
shall be credited against any judgment, award, or settlement
obtained pursuant to this section.
Any judgment, award, or
settlement obtained pursuant to an action under this section
shall be subject to the provisions of Section 13963 of the
Government Code.
