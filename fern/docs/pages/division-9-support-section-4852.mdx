# Support

## Section 4852

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

01) of Title 6 of Part 3 of the Penal Code or a pardon as provided in Chapter 1 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4800.">§ 4800</a>) or Chapter 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4850.">§ 4850</a>) of Title 6 of Part 3 of the Penal Code. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Following a conviction for murder or attempted murder, the defendant has been paroled based in whole or in part upon evidence presented to the Board of Prison Terms that the defendant committed the crime because he or she was the victim of intimate partner battering. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  The defendant was convicted of murder or attempted murder in the second degree in a trial at which substantial evidence was presented that the person committed the crime because he or she was a victim of intimate partner battering. 
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  The defendant was unlawfully imprisoned or restrained but has been released from prison after successfully prosecuting a writ of habeas corpus pursuant to Chapter 1 
(commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1473.">§ 1473</a>) of Title 12 of Part 2 of the Penal Code. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the sentence or judgment is stayed, the time for the commencement of the action shall be tolled until the stay is lifted. 
For purposes of this section, a judgment is not stayed if the judgment is appealed or the defendant is placed on probation. 
(d)(1) Subdivision (b) shall apply to any action commenced before, on, or after the effective date of this section, including any action otherwise barred by a limitation of time in effect prior to the effective date of this section, thereby reviving those causes of action that had lapsed or expired under the law in effect prior to the effective date of this section. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Paragraph (1) does not apply to either of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Any claim that has been litigated to finality on the merits in any court of competent jurisdiction prior to January 1, 2003. 
For purposes of this section, termination of a prior action on the basis of the statute of limitations does not constitute a claim that has been litigated to finality on the merits. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Any written, compromised settlement agreement that has been entered into between a plaintiff and a defendant if the plaintiff was represented by an attorney who was admitted to practice law in this state at the time of the settlement, and the plaintiff signed the agreement. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Any restitution paid by the defendant to the victim shall be credited against any judgment, award, or settlement obtained pursuant to this section. 
Any judgment, award, or settlement obtained pursuant to an action under this section shall be subject to the provisions of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=13963.">§ 13963</a> of the Government Code.
