# § 9324.

*Part 3 — Adoption Of Adults And Married Minors · Chapter 2 — Procedure For Adult Adoption*

Both the prospective adoptive parent and the proposed adoptee shall appear at the hearing in person, unless the court allows otherwise pursuant to Section 8613.5. (Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 159, Sec. 10. (AB 2495) Effective January 1, 2023.)
