# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.506

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Contest by obligor 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An obligor may contest the validity or enforcement of an income-withholding order issued in another state and received directly by an employer in this state by registering the order in a tribunal of this state and filing a contest to that order as provided in Chapter 6, or otherwise contesting the order in the same manner as if the order had been issued by a tribunal of this state. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The obligor shall give notice of the contest to: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  a support enforcement agency providing services to the obligee; 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  each employer that has directly received an incomewithholding order relating to the obligor; and 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  the person designated to receive payments in the income-withholding order or, if no person is designated, to the obligee.
