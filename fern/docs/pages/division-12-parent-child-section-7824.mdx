# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7824

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Parents suffering from disability due to alcohol, or controlled substances, or moral depravity; right of action 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Disability” as used in this section means any physical or mental incapacity which renders the parent or parents unable to care for and control the child adequately. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A proceeding under this part may be brought where all of the following requirements are satisfied: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child is one whose parent or parents (A) suffer a disability because of the habitual use of alcohol, or any of the controlled substances specified in Schedules I to V, inclusive, of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.">Division 10</a> (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11000.">§ 11000</a>) of the Health and Safety Code, except when these controlled substances are used as part of a medically prescribed plan, or 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  are morally depraved. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child has been a dependent child of the juvenile court, and the parent or parents have been deprived of the child’s custody continuously for one year immediately before the filing of a petition pursuant to this part. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Physical custody by the parent or parents for insubstantial periods of time does not interrupt the running of the oneyear period.
