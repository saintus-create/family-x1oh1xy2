# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6453

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Enforcement of Canadian domestic violence protection order by tribunal 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state may issue an order enforcing or refusing to enforce a Canadian domestic violence protection order on application of any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A protected party or other person authorized by law of this state other than this part to seek enforcement of a domestic protection order. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A respondent. 
__ 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Im a proceeding under subdivision (a), the tribunal of this state shall follow the procedures of. this. state for enforcement of a domestic protection order.
