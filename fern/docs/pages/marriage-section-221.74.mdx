[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. 
Reports 1 (1993)]. 
‘ 
Commentary Ehrenclou v. MacDonald, 117 Cal.App.4th 364, 12 Cal.Rptr.3d 411 
(2004), review denied, held that two adults adopted by a trust beneficiary under Colorado adult adoption law were not the deceased trust beneficiary’s “children then living” under a trust governed by California law, because the Colorado adult adoption statute gave the adoptees heir-at-law status, but did not create a parent-child relationship between the trust beneficiary and the adoptees.
