# § 1120 — Evidence otherwise admissible

Evidence otherwise admissible
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Evidence otherwise admissible or subject to discovery
outside of a mediation or a mediation consultation shall not
be or become inadmissible or protected from disclosure solely
by reason of its introduction or use in a mediation or a
mediation consultation.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This chapter does not limit any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The admissibility of an agreement to mediate a dis-
pute.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The effect of an agreement not to take a default or an
agreement to extend the time within which to act or refrain
from acting in a pending civil action.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Disclosure of the mere fact that a mediator has served,
is serving, will serve, or was contacted about serving as a
mediator in a dispute.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The admissibility of declarations of disclosure required
by Sections 2104 and 2105 of the Family Code, even if
prepared for the purpose of, in the course of, or pursuant to,

EXTRINSIC POLICIES
a mediation or a mediation consultation.
