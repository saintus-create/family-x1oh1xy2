# 7905 — Agreements with party states; approval of finan-

Parent and Child Relationship

Agreements with party states; approval of finan-
cial obligations
The officers and agencies of this state and its subdivisions
having authority to place children areehereby empowered to
enter into agreements with appropriate officers or agencies of
or in other party states pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Article 5
of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children.
Any such agreement which contains a financial commitment
or imposes a financial obligation on this state or subdivision
or agency thereof is not binding unless it has the approval in
writing of the Controller in the case of the state and of the
chief local fiscal officer in the case of a subdivision of the
state.
