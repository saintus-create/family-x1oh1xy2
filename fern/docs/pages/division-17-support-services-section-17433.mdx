# Support Services

## Section 17433

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Relief from default judgment; mistaken identity; remedies In an action in which a judgment or order for support was entered after the entry of the default of the defendant under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17430.">§ 17430</a>, the court shall relieve the defendant from that judgment or order if the defendant establishes that the defendant was mistakenly identified in the order or in any subsequent documents or proceedings as the person having an obligation to provide support. 
The defendant shall also be entitled to the remedies specified in subdivisions (d) and 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17530.">§ 17530</a> with respect to any actions taken to enforce that judgment or order. 
This section is only intended to apply where an order has been entered against a person who is not the support obligor named in the judgment or order.
