# 4050

_— Legislative intent; compliance with federal law

In adopting the statewide uniform guideline provided in this article, it is the intention of the Legislature to ensure that this state remains in compliance with federal regulations for child support guidelines. Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.
