# § 7908

*No provision of law restricting out-of-state placement of*

No provision of law restricting out-of-state placement of
children for adoption shall apply to placements made pursuant to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
