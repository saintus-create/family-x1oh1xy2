# § 7908

A court having jurisdiction to place children adjudged wards of the court may place a ward in an out-of-state facility, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 7910, pursuant to Section 727.1 of the Welfare and Institutions Code and Article 6 of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children and shall retain jurisdiction as provided in Article 5 of the compact. _(Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 86, Sec. 3. (AB 153) Effective July 16, 2021.)_
