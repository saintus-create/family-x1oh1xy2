# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1912

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Invoking subsection (9), In re Daniel M., 110 Cal.App.4th 703, 1 Cal.Rptr.3d 897 (2003), holds that an alleged father who has not acknowledged or established his paternity lacks standing to challenge a violation of the notice provisions of the Indian Child Welfare Act. To the same effect, see In re E.G., 170 Cal. App.4th 1530, 88 Cal.Rptr.3d 871 (2009). 
A claim of Native American heritage by a presumed father who is neither a biological nor adoptive father does not require notice to his tribe. In re C.A., 24 Cal.App.5th 511, 234 Cal.Rptr.3d 319 (2018). 
In re E.R., 18 Cal.App.5th 891, 227 Cal.Rptr.3d 264 (2017).
