# § 5235

*Duties of employer; withholding and forwarding of*

Duties of employer; withholding and forwarding of
support; liability
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The employer shall continue to withhold and forward
support as required by the assignment order until served with

notice terminating the assignment order.
If an employer
withholds support as required by the assignment order, the
obligor shall not be held in contempt or subject to criminal
prosecution for nonpayment of the support that was withheld
by the employer but not received by the obligee.

employer withheld the support but failed to forward the
payments to the obligee, the employer shall be liable for the
