# 3439.13

Uniformity of application and construction

Uniformity of application and construction
This chapter shall be applied and construed to effectuate
its general purpose to make uniform the law with respect to
the subject of this chapter among states enacting it. (Former-
ly $ 3439.11, added by Stats.1986, c. 383,
