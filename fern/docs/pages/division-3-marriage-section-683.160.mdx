# § 683.160 — Notice; service; filing proof of service

Notice; service; filing proof of service
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The judgment creditor shall serve a notice of renewal
of the judgment on the judgment debtor.
Service shall be
made personally or by first-class mail and proof of service
shall be filed with the court clerk.
The notice shall be in a
form prescribed by the Judicial Council and shall inform the
judgment debtor that the judgment debtor has 30 days within
which to make a motion to vacate or modify the renewal.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Until proof of service is filed pursuant to subdivision
(a), no writ may be issued, nor may any enforcement
proceedings be commenced to enforce the judgment, except
to the extent that the judgment would be enforceable had it
not been renewed.
