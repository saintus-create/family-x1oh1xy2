# General Provisions

## Section 202

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

The court may, on its own motion or on motion of a party, at any time during the pendency of a proceeding under this code, order a separate trial of any issue or issues, including, but not limited to, the issue of the date of separation. (Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 462, Sec. 1. (AB 2700) Effective January 1, 2020.)
