---
title: "902. “Debt” means an obligation incurred by a married person"
description: "“Debt” means an obligation incurred by a married person"
slug: division-13-adoption-section-902
---

“Debt” means an obligation incurred by a married person
before or during marriage, whether based on contract, tort, or
otherwise.


[Source](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=902.)
