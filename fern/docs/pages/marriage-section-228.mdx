without substantive change. References to a “decree” of adoption have been omitted as surplus.

The reference to the “superior” court has been omitted as surplus. 
See <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=200.">Section 200</a> (jurisdiction in superior court). 
In subdivision (c), the former reference to “clerk of the superior court of the county wherein the action is brought” has been shortened to “court clerk” to eliminate surplus language and the “it shall be the duty” language has been replaced by “shall.” 
See <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=12.">Section 12</a> (“shall is mandatory).
