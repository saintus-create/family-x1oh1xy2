# § 4400.

*Part 4 — Support Of Parents · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

Except as otherwise provided by law, an adult child shall, to the extent of the adult child’s ability, support a parent who is in need and unable to self-maintain by work. (Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 54. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)
