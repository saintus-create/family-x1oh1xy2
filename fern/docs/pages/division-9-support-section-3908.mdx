# Support

## Section 3908

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Acknowledgment of delivery A written acknowledgment of delivery by a custodian constitutes a sufficient receipt and discharge for custodial property transferred to the custodian pursuant to this part.
