# 1158

Subdivision (b)(1) of Section 3912 continued

Subdivision (b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of Section 3912 continued
subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of former Civil Code Section 1159 (repealed by 1984
Cal.Stat. ch. 243 1)—a special immunity from liability for the
custodian for losses to custodial property where the custodian is not
compensated.
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of former Civil Code Section 1158 permitted a
custodian to retain any security received, without the obligation to
diversify investment.
Subdivision (b)<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of Section 3912 extended
that rule to any property received.
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 3912 was a new provision.
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  generally continued subdivision <span class="subsec">(g)</span>  of former Civil
Code Section 1158, but added the provision requiring that custodial
property consisting of an undivided interest be held as a tenant in
common.
Subdivision (€) continued subdivision <span class="subsec">(h)</span>  of former Civil Code
Section 1158, but added the requirement that income tax information
be maintained and made available for preparation of the minor’s tax
returns.
.
