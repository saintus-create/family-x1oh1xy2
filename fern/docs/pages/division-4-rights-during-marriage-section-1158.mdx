# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1158

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Subdivision (b)(1) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3912.">Section 3912</a> continued subdivision (e) of former Civil Code <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1159.">Section 1159</a> (repealed by 1984 Cal.Stat. ch. 243 § 1)—a special immunity from liability for the custodian for losses to custodial property where the custodian is not compensated. 
Subdivision (e) of former Civil Code Section 1158 permitted a custodian to retain any security received, without the obligation to diversify investment. 
Subdivision (b)(2) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3912.">Section 3912</a> extended that rule to any property received. 
Subdivision (c) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3912.">Section 3912</a> was a new provision. 
Subdivision (d) generally continued subdivision (g) of former Civil Code Section 1158, but added the provision requiring that custodial property consisting of an undivided interest be held as a tenant in common. 
Subdivision (€) continued subdivision (h) of former Civil Code Section 1158, but added the requirement that income tax information be maintained and made available for preparation of the minor’s tax returns.
