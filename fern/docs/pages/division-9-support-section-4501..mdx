# § 4501.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

A family support order is enforceable in the same manner and to the same extent as a child support order. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
