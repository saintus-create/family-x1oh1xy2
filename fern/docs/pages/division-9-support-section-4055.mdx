# Support

## Section 4055

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

A reference to maintenance and education of the child has been omitted as surplus. 
See <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=150.">Section 150</a> 
(when used in reference to a child, “support” includes maintenance and education). 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
