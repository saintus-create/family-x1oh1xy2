# § 6256

*An emergency protective order expires at the earlier of the*

An emergency protective order expires at the earlier of the
following times:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The close of judicial business on the fifth court day
following the day of its issuance.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The seventh calendar day following the day of its
issuance. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 154.
Amended by Stats.1993, c. 1229 (A.B.224), § 2.)
