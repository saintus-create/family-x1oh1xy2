# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6256

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Expiration of orders An emergency protective order expires at the earlier of the following times: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The close of judicial business on the fifth court day following the day of its issuance. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The seventh calendar day following the day of its issuance.
