# § 3406 — Binding effect of custody determinations

Binding effect of custody determinations
A child custody determination made by a court of this state
that had jurisdiction under this part binds all persons who
have been served in accordance with the laws of this state or
notified
in accordance with Section 3408 or who have
submitted to the jurisdiction of the court, and who have been
given an opportunity to be heard.
As to those persons, the
determination is conclusive as to all decided issues of law and
fact except to the extent the determination
is modified.
