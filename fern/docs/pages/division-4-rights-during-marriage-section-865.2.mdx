# 865.2

Unprofessional conduct of mental health provider;

Unprofessional conduct of mental health provider;
disciplinary action
Any sexual orientation change efforts attempted on a
patient under 18 years of age by a mental health provider
shall be considered unprofessional conduct and shall subject
a mental health provider to discipline by the licensing entity
for that mental health provider.
