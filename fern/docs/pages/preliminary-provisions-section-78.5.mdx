# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 78.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

The section continued the requirement of former Probate Code <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=644.">§ 644</a> (repealed by 1986 Cal.Stat. ch. 783 § 9) that an inventory and appraisement be filed. 
The former provision was revised to conform to the then existing provisions relating to inventory and appraisement. 
See former Probate Code Sections 600-611 (repealed by 1988 Cal.Stat. 
ch. 1199 § 51). The requirement that the inventory and appraisement be filed before the hearing of the petition was substituted for the requirement of former <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=644.">§ 644</a> that the inventory and appraisement be filed within such time as the court may allow. 
The 1988 amendment corrected section references and conformed terminology to revisions made in the Probate Code in 1988. 
As to the 1988 amendment, see Comments to Conforming Revisions and Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 1031, 1090 (1988). For background on the provisions of this chapter, see the Comment to this chapter under the chapter heading. 
[20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
