# 78.5

The section continued

The section continued
the requirement of former Probate Code Section 644 (repealed by
1986 Cal.Stat. ch. 783 9) that an inventory and appraisement be
filed.
The former provision was revised to conform to the then
existing provisions relating to inventory and appraisement.
See
former Probate Code Sections 600-611 (repealed by 1988 Cal.Stat.
ch. 1199 51). The requirement that the inventory and appraise-
ment be filed before the hearing of the petition was substituted for
the requirement of former Section 644 that the inventory and
appraisement be filed within such time as the court may allow.
The
1988 amendment corrected section references and conformed termi-
nology to revisions made in the Probate Code in 1988.
As to the
1988 amendment, see Comments to Conforming Revisions and
Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 1031, 1090 (1988). For
background on the provisions of this chapter, see the Comment to
this chapter under the chapter heading.
[20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Re-
ports 1001 (1990)].
