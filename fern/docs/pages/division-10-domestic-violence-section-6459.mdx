# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6459

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Transition This part applies to a Canadian domestic violence protection order issued before, on, or after January 1, 2018, and toa continuing action for enforcement of a Canadian domestic violence protection order commenced before, on, or after January 1, 2018. 
A request for enforcement of a Canadian domestic violence protection order made on or after January 1, 2018, for a violation of the order occurring before, on, or after January 1, 2018, is governed by this part.
