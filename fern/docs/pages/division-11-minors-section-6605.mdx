# Minors

## Section 6605

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Procedure for filing petition; time for filing 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If proceedings for the administration of the estate of the decedent are pending, a petition under this chapter shall be filed in those proceedings without the payment of an additional fee. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If proceedings for the administration of the estate of the decedent have not yet been commenced, a petition under this chapter may be filed concurrently with a petition for the probate of the decedent’s will or for administration of the estate of the decedent, or, if no petition for probate or for administration is being filed, a petition under this chapter may be filed independently. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A petition may be filed under this chapter at any time prior to the entry of the order for final distribution of the estate.
