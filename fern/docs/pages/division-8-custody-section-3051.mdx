# 3051

Lack legal capacity for a transaction requiring joinder or consent

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Where one or both spouses have a conservator of the estate or
lack legal capacity for a transaction requiring joinder or consent
under Section 1100(b)-<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  or 1102.
See, e.g., Prob. Code §3012
(legal capacity), 3071
(substitute for joinder or consent).
[23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
