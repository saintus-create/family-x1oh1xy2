Enforcement.
Enforceability
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to Section 142, a waiver is enforceable under
this section unless the surviving spouse proves either of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A fair and reasonable disclosure of the property or
financial obligations of the decedent was not provided to the
surviving spouse prior to the signing of the waiver unless the
surviving spouse waived such a fair and reasonable disclosure
after advice by independent legal counsel.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The surviving spouse was not represented by independent legal counsel at the time of signing of the waiver.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 721 of the Family Code does
not apply if the waiver is enforceable under this section.
(Cal.Cont.Ed.Bar
1983);
1992 Amendment
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 143 is amended to substitute a reference
to the Family Code provision that replaced former Civil Code
Section 5103, [22 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1 (1992)].
Background on Section 143 of Repealed Code
Section 143 was a new provision added by 1983 Cal.Stat. ch. 842
22 and amended by 1984 Cal.Stat. ch. 892 10. The 1984
amendment made substantive and clarifying revisions. For background on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part
under the part heading. [20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
