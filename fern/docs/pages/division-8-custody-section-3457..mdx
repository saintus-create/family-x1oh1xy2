# § 3457.

*Part 3 — Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction And Enforcement Act · Chapter 3 — Enforcement*

The court may assess all direct expenses and costs incurred by a district attorney under Section 3455 or 3456 pursuant to the provisions of Section 3134. (Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.) FAM Family Code - FAM
