# 3003. Joint legal custody

*Custody of Children*

Joint legal custody
“Joint legal custody” means that both parents shall share
the right and the responsibility to make the decisions relating
to the health, education, and welfare of a child.
(Stats. 1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
