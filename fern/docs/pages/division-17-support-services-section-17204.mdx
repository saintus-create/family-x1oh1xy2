---
title: "17204. Director and administrative units"
description: "Director and administrative units"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-17204
---

The department consists of the director and such division or other administrative units as the director may find necessary.

> *Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 478, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.*
