# 297
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Domestic partners are two adults who have chosen to share one another’s lives in an intimate and committed relationship of mutual caring.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A domestic partnership shall be established in California when both persons file a Declaration of Domestic Partnership with the Secretary of State pursuant to this division, and, at the time of filing, all of the following requirements are met:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Neither person is married to someone else or is a member of another domestic partnership with someone else that has not been terminated, dissolved, or adjudged a nullity.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The two persons are not related by blood in a way that would prevent them from being married to each other in this state.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Both persons are at least 18 years of age, except as provided in Section 297.1.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Both persons are capable of consenting to the domestic partnership.
