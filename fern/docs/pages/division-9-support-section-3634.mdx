# § 3634

*Forms; preparation by Judicial Council*

Forms; preparation by Judicial Council
The Judicial Council shall prepare all forms necessary to
give effect to this chapter. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
10 ,
Application and Order or Response.
10 ,
Hearing and Order After Hearing.
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
