# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2045

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

See also <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2030.">§ 2030</a> (temporary restraining order in summons). A community property dwelling may not be transferred or encumbered without joinder or consent of both spouses. 
See Sections 1100(c) (disposition or encumbrance of personal property family dwelling), 1102 
(lease, transfer, or encumbrance of real property). 
For background on former Civ. Code § 5102, see Tentative Recommendation Proposing the Enforcement of Judgments Law, 15 Cal. L, Revision Comm’n Reports 2001, 2630 (1980). 
[23 Cal.L.Rey. 
Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
