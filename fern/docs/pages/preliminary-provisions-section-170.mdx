# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 170

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Definitions; eligible membership in more than one tribe. 
Indian child of tribe not recognized to have tribal status under federal law; tribal participation at hearings.
