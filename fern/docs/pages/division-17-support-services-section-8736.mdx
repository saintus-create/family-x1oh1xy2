# § 8736 — Chapter provisions as grounds for removal of child

The requirements of this chapter shall not be used as basis for removing a child who has been placed with a relative caregiver or foster parent prior to January 1, 1999, unless the noncompliance with the standards described therein present a danger to the health, safety, or emotional well-being of the child. _(Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 983, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
