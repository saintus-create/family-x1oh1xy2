# 8736 — Chapter provisions as grounds for removal of child

Support Services

Chapter provisions as grounds for removal of child
from placement
The requirements of this chapter shall not be used as basis
for removing a child who has been placed with a relative
caregiver or foster parent prior to January 1, 1999, unless the
noncompliance with the standards described therein present
a danger to the health, safety, or emotional well-being of the
child. (Added by Stats.1998, c. 983 (A.B.2286),
