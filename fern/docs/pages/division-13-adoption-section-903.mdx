# 903 — Time debt is incurred

Adoption

Time debt is incurred
A debt is “incurred” at the following time:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In the case of a contract, at the time the contract is
made.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In the case of a tort, at the time the tort occurs.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In other cases,
at the time the obligation arises.
