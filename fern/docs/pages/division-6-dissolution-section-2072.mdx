# § 2072 — Hearings; appearances

Hearings; appearances
The employee benefit plan is not required to, but may,
appear at any hearing in the proceeding.
For purposes of the
Code of Civil Procedure, the plan shall be considered a party
appearing at the trial with respect to any hearing at which the
interest of the parties in the plan is an issue before the court.
