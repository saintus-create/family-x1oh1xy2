# 6902

“Medical care” defined

“Medical care” means X-ray examination, anesthetic, medical or surgical diagnosis or treatment, and hospital care under the general or special supervision and upon the advice of or to be rendered by a physician and surgeon licensed under the Medical Practice Act. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
