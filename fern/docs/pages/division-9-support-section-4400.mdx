---
title: "4400. Duty of adult children to support parents"
description: "Duty of adult children to support parents"
slug: division-9-support-section-4400
---

Except as otherwise provided by law, an adult child shall, to the extent of the adult child’s ability, support a parent who is in need and unable to self-maintain by work. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 54. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.*
