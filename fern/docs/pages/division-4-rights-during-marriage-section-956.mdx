# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 956

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Exception: Crime or fraud; applicability to legal services for lawful cannabis-—related activities 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  There is no privilege under this article if the services of the lawyer were sought or obtained to enable or aid anyone to commit or plan to commit a crime or a fraud. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This exception to the privilege granted by this article shall not apply to legal services rendered in compliance with state and local laws on medicinal cannabis or adult-use cannabis, and confidential communications provided for the purpose of rendering those services are confidential communications between client and lawyer, as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=952.">§ 952</a>, provided the lawyer also advises the client on conflicts with respect to federal law.
