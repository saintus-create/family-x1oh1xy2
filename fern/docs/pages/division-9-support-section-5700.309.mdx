# § 5700.309

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 3 — Civil Provisions Of General Application*

An individual may employ private counsel to represent the individual in proceedings authorized by this part. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
