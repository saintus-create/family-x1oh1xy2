# § 452.16 — Thus it was

Thus it was
immaterial that the report might have been a privileged communica-
tion for purposes of the litigation privilege under subsection <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of
this section.
Interpreting subsection (b)(1), L.G. v. M.B., 25 Cal.App.5th 211,
235 Cal.Rptr.3d 494 (2018), review denied, held that the divorce
proviso extends to any pleading or affidavit filed in a dissolution
proceeding (when a divorcing wife requested, in a related civil
harassment proceeding, a domestic violence restraining order against
the parties’ nanny, in the nanny’s tort action against the wife for
defamation, the wife’s pleadings and testimony in the dissolution
action were entitled only to the limited privilege of this subsection).
