# Marriage

## Section 452.16

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Thus it was immaterial that the report might have been a privileged communication for purposes of the litigation privilege under subsection (b) of this section. 
Interpreting subsection (b)(1), L.G. v. M.B., 25 Cal.App.5th 211, 235 Cal.Rptr.3d 494 (2018), review denied, held that the divorce proviso extends to any pleading or affidavit filed in a dissolution proceeding (when a divorcing wife requested, in a related civil harassment proceeding, a domestic violence restraining order against the parties’ nanny, in the nanny’s tort action against the wife for defamation, the wife’s pleadings and testimony in the dissolution action were entitled only to the limited privilege of this subsection).
