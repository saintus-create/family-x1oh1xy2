It should be noted that division of property pursuant to this section
is subject to the same limitations applicable to division of the
community estate.
Therefore, an express agreement of the parties
precluding partition or other division of the property and providing a
mechanism for dispute resolution or otherwise governing their rights
in the property prevails over this section.

division of community estate “[e]xcept upon the written agreement of
