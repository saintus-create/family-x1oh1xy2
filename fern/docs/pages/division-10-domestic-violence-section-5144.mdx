# § 5144 — Proof of death

Proof of death
“Proof of death” includes any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An original or attested or certified copy of a death
certificate.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A record or report that is prima facie evidence of
death under Section 103550 of the Health and Safety Code,
Sections 1530 to 1532, inclusive, of the Evidence Code, or
another statute of this state.
