# 754. Pendency of proceedings

*Premarital Agreements*

pendency of proceedings
If notice of the pendency of a proceeding for dissolution of
the marriage, for nullity of the marriage, or for legal
separation of the parties is recorded in any county in which
either spouse resides on real property that is the separate
property of the other, the real property shall not for a period
of three months thereafter be transferred, encumbered, or
otherwise disposed of voluntarily or involuntarily without the
joinder of both spouses, unless the court otherwise orders.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.2014, c. 82 (S.B.1306), § 16, eff. Jan. 1,
2015.)
