# 683.200. Stay of enforcement

*Premarital Agreements*

Stay of enforcement
A judgment may be renewed notwithstanding any stay of
enforcement of the judgment, but the renewal of the judgment does not affect the stay of enforcement.
1982, c. 1364, p. 5076, § 2, operative July 1, 1983.)
