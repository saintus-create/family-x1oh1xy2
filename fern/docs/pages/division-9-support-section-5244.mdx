# § 5244

*Local child support agency; enforcement or collec-*

Local child support agency; enforcement or collec-
tion duties
A reference to the local child support agency in this
chapter applies only when the local child support agency is
otherwise ordered
or required
to act pursuant
to law.
Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to mandate addition-
al enforcement or collection duties upon the local child
support agency beyond those otherwise imposed by law.
