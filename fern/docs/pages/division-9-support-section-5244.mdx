# § 5244 — Local child support agency; enforcement or collec-

A reference to the local child support agency in this chapter applies only when the local child support agency is otherwise ordered or required to act pursuant to law. Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to mandate additional enforcement or collection duties upon the local child support agency beyond those otherwise imposed by law. _(Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 61. Effective September 28, 2000.)_
