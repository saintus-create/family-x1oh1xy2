# 10015 — The Judicial Council shall create any necessary forms to

Support Services

The Judicial Council shall create any necessary forms to
advise the parties of the types of services provided, that there
is no attorney-client relationship, that the family law facilitator is not responsible for the outcome of any case, that the
family law facilitator does not represent any party and will not
appear in court on the party’s behalf, and that the other party
may also be receiving information and services from the
family law facilitator. (Added by Stats.1999, c. 652 (S.B.240),
$715.00)
Stats.1999, c. 652 (S.B.240), § 14.)
