# 1020

By the patient, of a duty arising out of the psychotherapist-

There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue of breach, by the psychotherapist or
by the patient, of a duty arising out of the psychotherapist-

The words “or withdraw” are added to Section 1017 to make it
clear that the psychotherapist-patient privilege applies in a case
where the defendant in a criminal proceeding enters a plea based on
insanity, submits to an examination by a court-appointed psychotherapist, and later withdraws the plea based on insanity prior to the trial
