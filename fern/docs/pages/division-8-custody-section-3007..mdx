# § 3007.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

“Sole physical custody” means that a child shall reside with and be under the supervision of one parent, subject to the power of the court to order visitation. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
