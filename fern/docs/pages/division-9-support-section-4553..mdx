# § 4553.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 2 — Deposit Of Money To Secure Future Child Support Payments*

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to permit any action or omission by the state or any of its political subdivisions that would place the state in noncompliance with any requirement of federal law, including, but not limited to, the state reimbursement requirements of Part D of Title IV of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 651 et seq.) and any regulations promulgated thereunder. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
