# 1812.34

Costs and attorney fees

Costs and attorney fees
Any person denied credit or offered credit, in violation of
Section 1812.30 who brings an action pursuant to Section
1812.31 or 1812.32 of this code may petition the court for
award of costs and reasonable attorney’s fees which the court
shall award if the action is successful. (Added by Stats.1975,
Wrongful! Denial of Credit.
