# § 9323

*interested persons*

interested persons
appearances and objections of
The court may require notice of the time and place of the
hearing to be served on any other interested person and any
interested person may appear and object to the proposed
adoption. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10, operative Jan.
1, 1994.)
