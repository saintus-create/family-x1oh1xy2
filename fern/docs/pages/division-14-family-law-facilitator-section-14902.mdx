# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 14902

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Definitions As used in this chapter: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Accredited agency The term “accredited agency” means an agency accredited under subchapter II to provide adoption services in the United States in cases subject to the Convention. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Accrediting entity The term “accrediting entity” means an entity designated under section 14922(a) of this title to accredit agencies and approve persons under subchapter II. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Adoption service The term “adoption service” means— 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  identifying a child for adoption and arranging an adoption; 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  securing necessary consent to termination of parental rights and to adoption; 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  performing a background study on a child or a home study on a prospective adoptive parent, and reporting on such a study; 
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  making determinations of the best interests of a child and the appropriateness of adoptive placement for the child; 
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  post-placement monitoring of a case until final adoption; and 
<span class="subsec">(F)</span>  where made necessary by disruption before final adoption, assuming custody and providing child care or any other social service pending an alternative placement. 
The term “providing”, with respect to an adoption service, includes facilitating the provision of the service. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Agency The term “agency” means any person other than an individual. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Approved person The term “approved person” means a person approved under subchapter II to provide adoption services in the United States in cases subject to the Convention.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Attorney General Except as used in section 14944 of this title, the term 
“Attorney General” means the Attorney General, acting through the Commissioner of Immigration and Naturalization. 
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Central authority The term “central authority” means the entity designated as such by any Convention country under Article 6(1) of the Convention. 
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  Central authority function The term “central authority function” means any duty required to be carried out by a central authority under the Convention. 
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  Convention The term “Convention” means the Convention on Protection of Children and Co-operation in Respect of Intercountry Adoption, done at The Hague on May 29, ke 
<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  Convention adoption The term “Convention adoption” means an adoption of a child resident in a foreign country party to the Convention by a United States citizen, or an adoption of a child resident in the United States by an individual residing in another Convention country. 
<span class="subsec">(11)</span>  Convention record The term “Convention record” means any item, collection, or grouping of information contained in an electronic or physical document, an electronic collection of data, a photograph, an audio or video tape, or any other information storage medium of any type whatever that contains information about a specific past, current, or prospective Convention adoption 
(regardless of whether the adoption was made final) that has been preserved in accordance with section 14941(a) of this title by the Secretary of State or the Attorney General. 
<span class="subsec">(12)</span>  Convention country The term “Convention country” means a country party to the Convention. 
<span class="subsec">(13)</span>  Other Convention country The term “other Convention country” means a Convention country other than the United States. 
<span class="subsec">(14)</span>  Person The term “person” shall have the meaning provided in section 1 of Title 1 and shall not include any agency of government or tribal government entity. 
<span class="subsec">(15)</span>  Person with an ownership or control interest The term “person with an ownership or control interest” has the meaning given such term in section 1320a-3(a)(3) of this title. 

 
<span class="subsec">(16)</span>  Secretary The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of State. 
<span class="subsec">(17)</span>  State The term “State” means the 50 States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, Guam, and the Virgin Islands. 
(Pub.L. 106-279, § 3, Oct. 6, 2000, 114 Stat. 826.)
