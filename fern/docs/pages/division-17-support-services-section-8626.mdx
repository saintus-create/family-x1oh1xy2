# 8626

Support Services

MINORS
Treatises and Practice Aids
10 ,
In General.
Description of services; disclosure of status
An adoption facilitator shall disclose in the first oral
communication in which there is a description of services,
that the facilitator is not a licensed adoption agency. (Added
by Stats.1996, c. 1135 (S.B.2035), § 1.)
