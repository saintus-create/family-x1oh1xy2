# General Provisions

## Section 273.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Willful infliction of corporal injury; violation; punishment 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Any person who willfully inflicts corporal injury resulting in a traumatic condition upon a victim described in subdivision (b) is guilty of a felony, and upon conviction thereof shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for two, three, or four years, or in a county jail for not more than one year, or by a fine of up to six thousand dollars 
($6,000), or by both that fine and imprisonment. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Subdivision (a) shall apply if the victim is or was one or more of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The offender’s spouse or former spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The offender’s cohabitant or former cohabitant.
