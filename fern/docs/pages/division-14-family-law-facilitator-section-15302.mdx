# 15302

Trust for support

Trust for support
Except as provided in Sections 15304 to 15307, inclusive, if
the trust instrument provides that the trustee shall pay
income or principal or both for the education or support of a
beneficiary, the beneficiary’s interest in income or principal
or both under the trust, to the extent the income or principal
or both is necessary for the education or support of the
beneficiary, may not be transferred and is not subject to the
enforcement of a money judgment until paid to the beneficia-
ty. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
