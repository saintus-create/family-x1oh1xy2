# § 8821 — Report or findings of department or agency; sub-

Report or findings of department or agency; sub-
mission of copy to attorney for petitioner
When any report or findings are submitted to the court by
the department or a delegated county adoption agency, a
copy of the report or findings, whether favorable or unfavora-
ble, shall be given to the petitioner’s attorney in the

ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS
proceeding, if the petitioner has an attorney of record, or to
the petitioner.
