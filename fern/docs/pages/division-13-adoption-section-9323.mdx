# Adoption

## Section 9323

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Notice of hearing; appearances and objections of interested persons The court may require notice of the time and place of the hearing to be served on any other interested person and any interested person may appear and object to the proposed adoption.
