# § 6950 — Consent of court; conditions

Consent of court; conditions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon application by a minor, the court may summarily
grant consent for enlistment by the minor in the armed forces
of the United States if the court determines
all. of. the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The minor is 16 years of age or older and resides in this
state.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The consent of a parent or guardian is necessary to
permit the enlistment, and the minor has no parent or
guardian available to give the consent.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  No fee may be charged for proceedings under this
section.
