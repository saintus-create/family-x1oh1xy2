Background on Section 147 of Repealed Code
(a)  Subject to subdivisions (c)  and (d), a waiver, agreement, or property settlement made after December 31, 1984,
is invalid insofar as it affects the rights listed in subdivision
(a)  of Section 141 unless it satisfies the requirements of this
chapter.
Section 147 was a new provision added by 1983 Cal.Stat. ch. 842
