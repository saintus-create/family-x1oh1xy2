# § 147 — Validity or effect of premarital property agreement;

validity or effect of premarital property agreement;
right to dispose of community or quasi-community
property
Background on Section 147 of Repealed Code
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to subdivisions <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  and (d), a waiver, agreement, or property settlement made after December 31, 1984,
is invalid insofar as it affects the rights listed in subdivision
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 141 unless it satisfies the requirements of this
chapter.
Section 147 was a new provision added by 1983 Cal.Stat. ch. 842
