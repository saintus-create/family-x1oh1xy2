# 1002 — Intention of deceased patient concerning writing

Family Law Facilitator Act

Intention of deceased patient concerning writing
affecting property interest
There is no privilege under this article as to a communica-
tion relevant to an issue concerning the intention of a patient,
now deceased, with respect to a deed of conveyance, will, or
other writing, executed by the patient, purporting to affect an
interest in property.
