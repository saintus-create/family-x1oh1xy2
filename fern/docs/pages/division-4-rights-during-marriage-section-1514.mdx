# § 1514

*Appointment of guardian*

Appointment of guardian
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon hearing of the petition, if it appears necessary or
convenient, the court may appoint a guardian of the person
or estate of the proposed ward or both.
(b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In appointing a guardian of the person, the court is
governed by Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 3020) and
Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 3040) of Part 2 of
of the Family Code, relating to custody of a minor.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Except as provided in Section 2105, a minor’s parent
may not be appointed as a guardian of the person of the
minor.

interest in the welfare of the proposed ward.
