# § 3162 — Uniform standards of practice; contents; adoption

Uniform standards of practice; contents; adoption
by Judicial Council
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Mediation of cases involving custody and visitation
concerning children shall be governed by uniform standards
of practice adopted by the Judicial Council.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The standards of practice shall include, but not be
limited to, all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Provision for the best interest of the child and the
safeguarding of the rights of the child to frequent and
continuing contact with both parents, consistent with Sections
3011 and 3020.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Facilitation of the transition of the family by detailing
factors to be considered in decisions concerning the child’s
future.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The conducting of negotiations in such a way as to
equalize power relationships between the parties.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In adopting the standards of practice, the Judicial
Council shall consider standards developed by recognized
associations of mediators and attorneys and other relevant
standards governing mediation of proceedings for the dissolu-
tion of marriage.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The Judicial Council shall offer training with respect to
the standards to mediators.
