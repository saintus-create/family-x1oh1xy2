# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1812.35

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Commencement of action; limitations
Any action commenced pursuant to Section 1812.31 shall
be commenced within two years from the date on which the
person is denied credit or is offered credit in violation of
Section 1812.30. (Added by Stats.1975, c. 332, p. 781, 6.)
