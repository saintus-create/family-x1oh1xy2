# § 8719.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 2 — Agency Adoptions*

If the petitioners move to withdraw the adoption petition or to dismiss the proceeding, the court clerk shall immediately notify the department at Sacramento of the action. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
