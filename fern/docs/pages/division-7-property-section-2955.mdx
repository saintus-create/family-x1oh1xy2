# 2955 — proceedings

Division of Property

proceedings
of public
guardian
to undertake
other
Section 3303 continues Section 3303 of the repealed Probate Code
without change. Although this part does not limit the California
Uniform Transfers to Minors Act, some provisions of this part
supplement that act. See Sections 3412(b), 3413(b), 3602(c)(2),
3611(e). For background on: this: section, see: Recommendation
Relating to Uniform Transfers to Minors Act, 17 Cal.L.Revision
Comm’n Reports 601 (1984). For background on the provisions of
this part, see the Comment to this part under the part heading. [20
Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit or restrict a public
guardian from undertaking any other proceeding authorized
by law.
(Added by Stats.2000, c. 813 (S.B.1742), § 1.)
