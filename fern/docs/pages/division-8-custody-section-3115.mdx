# 3115

Cross-examination of court-appointed investiga-

A statement, whether written or oral, or conduct shall not be held to constitute a waiver by a party of the right to cross-examine the court-appointed investigator, unless the statement is made, or the conduct occurs, after the report has been received by a party or the party’s attorney. _(Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 35. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)_
