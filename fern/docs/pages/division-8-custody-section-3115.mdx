# Custody of Children

## Section 3115

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Cross-examination of court-appointed investigator; waiver of right A statement, whether written or oral, or conduct shall not be held to constitute a waiver by a party of the right to crossexamine the court-appointed investigator, unless the statement is made, or the conduct occurs, after the report has been received by a party or the party’s attorney.
