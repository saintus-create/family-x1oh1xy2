# § 3115 — Cross-examination of court-appointed investiga-

Cross-examination of court-appointed investiga-
tor; waiver of right
A statement, whether written or oral, or conduct shall not
be held to constitute a waiver by a party of the right to cross-
examine the court-appointed investigator, unless the state-
ment is made, or the conduct occurs, after the report has
been received by a party or
the party’s attorney.
