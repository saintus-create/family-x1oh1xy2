# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1933

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Funds for on and off reservation programs 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Appropriated funds for similar programs of Department of Health and Human Services; appropriation in advance for payments In the establishment, operation, and funding of Indian child and family service programs, both on and off reservation, the Secretary may enter into agreements with the Secretary of Health and Human Services, and the latter Secretary is hereby authorized for such purposes to use funds appropriated for similar programs of the Department of Health and Human Services: Provided, That authority to make payments pursuant to such agreements shall be effective only to the extent and in such amounts as may be provided in advance by appropriation Acts. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Appropriation authorization under section 13 of this title Funds for the purposes of this chapter may be appropriated pursuant to the provisions of section 13 of this title. 
(Pub.L. 95-608, Title II, 203, Nov. 8, 1978, 92 Stat. 3076; Pub.L. 96-88, Title V, $ 509(b), Oct. 17, 1979, 93 Stat. 695.)
