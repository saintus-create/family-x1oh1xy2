# § 8818

*Statement to birth parents;*

Statement to birth parents;
contents;
disclosure
form; birth parent information
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department shall adopt a statement to be present-
ed to the birth parents at the time the consent to adoption is
signed and to prospective adoptive parents at the time of the
home study.
The statement shall, in a clear and concise
manner and in words calculated to ensure the confidence of
the birth parents in the integrity of the adoption process,
communicate to the birth parent of a child who is the subject
of an adoption petition all of the following facts:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  It is in the child’s best interest that the birth parents
keep the department informed of any health problems that
the parent develops that could affect the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  It is extremely important that the birth parent keep an
address current with the department in order to permit a
response to inquiries concerning medical or social history.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Section 9203 of the Family Code authorizes a person
who has been adopted and who attains the age of 21 years to
request the department to disclose the name and address of
the adoptee’s birth parents.
Consequently, it is of the utmost
importance that the birth parent indicate whether to allow
this disclosure by checking the appropriate box provided on
the form.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The birth parent may change the decision whether to
permit disclosure of the birth parent’s name and address, at
any time, by sending a notarized letter to that effect, by
certified mail, return receipt requested, to the department.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The consent will be filed in the office of the clerk of
the court in which the adoption takes place. The file is not
open to inspection by any persons other than the parties to
the adoption proceeding, their attorneys, and the department,
except upon order of a judge of the superior court.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The department shall adopt a form to be signed by the
birth parents at the time the consent to adoption is signed,
which shall provide as follows:
“Section 9203 of the Family Code authorizes a person who
has been adopted and who attains the age of 21 years to make
a request to the State Department of Social Services, or the
licensed adoption agency that joined in the adoption petition,
for the name and address of the adoptee’s birth parents.

Indicate by checking one of the boxes below whether or not
you wish your name and address to be disclosed:
YES
NO
UNCERTAIN AT THIS TIME; WILL NOTIFY
AGENCY AT LATER DATE.”
