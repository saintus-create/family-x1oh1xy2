# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1001.83

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Pretrial diversion program for primary caregivers; contents of program; requirements to take part in program; reports of progress; reinstatement of criminal proceedings if unsatisfactory progress or conviction of other crime; dismissal of criminal charges and sealing of arrest record upon successful completion of diversion.
