# § 8542.

*Part 1 — Definitions*

“Prospective adoptive parent” means a person who has filed or intends to file a petition under Part 2 (commencing with Section 8600) to adopt a child who has been or who is to be placed in the person’s physical care or a petition under Part 3 (commencing with Section 9300) to adopt an adult. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
