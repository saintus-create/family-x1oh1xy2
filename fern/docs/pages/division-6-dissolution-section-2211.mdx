# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2211

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Limitation of actions A proceeding to obtain a judgment of nullity of marriage, for causes set forth in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, must be commenced within the periods and by the parties, as follows: 
(a) For causes mentioned in subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, by any of the following: 
(1) The party to the marriage who was married under the age of legal consent, within four years after arriving at the age of consent. 
(2) A parent, guardian, conservator, or other person having charge of the minor, at any time before the married minor has arrived at the age of legal consent. 
(b) For causes mentioned in subdivision (b). of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, by either of the following: 
(1) Either party during the life of the other. 
(2) The former spouse. 
(c) For causes mentioned in subdivision (c) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, by the party injured, or by a relative or conservator of the party of unsound mind, at any time before the death of either party. 
(d) For causes mentioned in subdivision (d) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, by the party whose consent was obtained by fraud, within four years after the discovery of the facts constituting the fraud. 
(e) For causes mentioned in subdivision (e) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, by the party whose consent was obtained by force; within four years after the marriage. 
(f) For causes mentioned in subdivision (f) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2210.">Section 2210</a>, by the injured party, within four years after the marriage.
