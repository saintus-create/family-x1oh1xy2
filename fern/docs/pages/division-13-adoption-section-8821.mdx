# Adoption

## Section 8821

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Report or findings of department or agency; submission of copy to attorney for petitioner When any report or findings are submitted to the court by the department or a delegated county adoption agency, a copy of the report or findings, whether favorable or unfavorable, shall be given to the petitioner’s attorney in the OF proceeding, if the petitioner has an attorney of record, or to the petitioner.
