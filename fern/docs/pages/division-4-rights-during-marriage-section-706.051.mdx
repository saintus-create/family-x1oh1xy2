# 706.051

Debtor or family; exemption

debtor or family; exemption

Section 706.052 continues former Section 723.052 except that
subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  is added to reflect that the court’s authority under
subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  is limited by the maximum amounts that may be
withheld under federal law. See 15 U.S.C. 1673(b) <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  (Supp.
1979).
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 706.052 prescribes the exemption
applicable to a wage garnishment for the collection of delinquent
child or spousal support payments except in cases where the court has
made an equitable division pursuant to subdivision (b). The
judgment debtor’s earnings that are subject to the 50 percent
exemption under subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  are “disposable earnings” as defined
of judgment
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For the purposes of this section, “family of the
judgment debtor” includes the spouse or former spouse of
the judgment debtor.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (c), the portion of
the judgment debtor’s earnings that the judgment debtor
proves is necessary for the support of the judgment debtor or
the judgment debtor’s family supported in whole or in part by
the judgment debtor is exempt from levy under this chapter.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The exemption provided in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  is not
available if any of the following exceptions applies:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The debt was incurred pursuant to an order or award
for the payment of attorney’s fees under Section 2030, 3121,
or 3557 of the Family Code.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The debt was incurred for personal services rendered
by an employee or former employee of the judgment debtor.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The order is a withholding order for support under
Section 706.030.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The order is one governed by Article 4 (commencing
with Section 706.070) (state tax order). (Added by Stats. 1982,
c. 1364, p. 5175, 2, operative July 1, 1983. Amended by

Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  also makes clear that, in applying the 50 percent
exemption, the amount withheld from the earnings of the judgment
debtor pursuant to any wage assignment for support (defined in
subdivision <span class="subsec">(g)</span>  of Section 706.011) is included in computing the 50
percent of the judgment debtor’s earnings that may be withheld. For
example, if 30 percent of the judgment debtor’s earnings are withheld
pursuant to a wage assignment for support, an additional 20 percent
may be withheld pursuant to the earnings withholding order for the
collection of delinquent amounts payable for child or spousal
support.
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  makes the 50 percent standard provided by
subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  subject to the power of the court to make an order
that more or less of the judgment debtor’s earnings be withheld
where the earnings withholding order is issued to collect delinquent
child or spousal support payments. Subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  makes clear that
the court may not order the withholding of an amount in excess of
