# § 7891 — In chambers hearing;

In chambers hearing;
notice of right to attend;
waiver
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided’ in this section, if the
child who is the subject of the petition is 10 years of age or
older, the child shall be heard by the court in chambers on at
least the following matters:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The feelings and thoughts of the child concerning the
custody proceeding about to take place.

parent or parents.
