# California Family Code § 7891

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this section, if the child who is the subject of the petition is 10 years of age or older, the child shall be heard by the court in chambers on at least the following matters:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The feelings and thoughts of the child concerning the custody proceeding about to take place.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The feelings and thoughts of the child about the child’s parent or parents.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The child’s preference as to custody, according to Section 3042.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court shall inform the child of the child’s right to attend the hearing. However, counsel for the child may waive the hearing in chambers by the court.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not apply if the child is confined because of illness or other incapacity to an institution or residence and is therefore unable to attend.
