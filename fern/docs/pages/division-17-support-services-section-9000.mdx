# 9000 — ianship petition; order of adoption

Support Services

ianship petition; order of adoption
contents;
guard-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A stepparent desiring to adopt a child of the stepparent’s spouse may for that purpose file a petition in the county
in which the petitioner resides.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A domestic partner, as defined in Section 297, desiring
to adopt a child of * * * the other domestic partner may, for
that purpose, file a petition in the county in which the
petitioner resides.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The caption of the adoption petition shall contain the
names of the petitioners, but not the child’s name.
The
petition shall state the child’s sex and date of birth and the
name the child had before adoption.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If the child is the subject of a guardianship petition, the
adoption petition shall so state and shall include the caption
and docket number or have attached a copy of the letters of
the guardianship or temporary guardianship. The petitioners
shall notify the court of any petition for guardianship or
temporary guardianship filed after the adoption petition.
The guardianship proceeding shall be consolidated with the
adoption proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The order of adoption shall contain the child’s adopted
name, but not the name the child had before adoption.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  If the petitioner has entered into a. postadoption
contact agreement with the birth parent as set forth in
Section 8616.5, the agreement, signed. by the participating
parties, shall be attached to and filed with the petition for
adoption.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  For the purposes of this chapter, stepparent adoption
includes adoption by a domestic partner, as defined in
Adoption
of Compliance With Hague Adoption Convention Attachment.
Treatises and Practice Aids
10 ,
Powers and Duties of Licensed or Accredited Adoption Agencies.
