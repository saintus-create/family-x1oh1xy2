# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7663

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Inquiry to identify all alleged fathers and presumed parents; report 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In an effort to identify all alleged fathers and presumed parents, the court shall cause inquiry to be made of the mother and any other appropriate person by one of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The State Department of Social Services. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A licensed county adoption agency. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The licensed adoption agency to which the child is to be relinquished. 

§ 7663 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  In the case of a stepparent adoption, the licensed clinical social worker, licensed marriage and family therapist, or licensed professional clinical counselor who is performing the investigation pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=9001.">§ 9001</a>, if applicable. 
In the case of a stepparent adoption in which a licensed clinical social worker, licensed marriage and family therapist, or licensed professional clinical counselor is not performing the investigation pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=9001.">§ 9001</a>, the board of supervisors may assign those inquiries to a licensed county adoption agency, the county department designated by the board of supervisors to administer the public social services program, or the county probation department. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The inquiry shall include all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Whether the mother was married at the time of conception of the child or at any time thereafter. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Whether the mother was cohabiting with a man at the time of conception or birth of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Whether the mother has received support payments or promises of support with respect to the child or in connection with the pregnancy. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Whether any person has formally or informally acknowledged or declared possible parentage of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The names and whereabouts, if known, of every person presumed or alleged to be the parent of the child, and the efforts made to give notice of the proposed adoption to each person identified. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The agency that completes the inquiry shall file a written report of the findings with the court.
