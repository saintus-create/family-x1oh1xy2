# § 5700.702 — Applicability

Applicability
This chapter applies only to a support proceeding under
the Convention. In such a proceeding, if a provision of this
chapter is inconsistent with Chapters 1 through 6, this chapter
controls. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff. Jan.
1, 2016.)
