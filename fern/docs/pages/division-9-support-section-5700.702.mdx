# § 5700.702

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 7 — Support Proceeding Under Convention*

This chapter applies only to a support proceeding under the Convention. In such a proceeding, if a provision of this chapter is inconsistent with Chapters 1 through 6, this chapter controls. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
