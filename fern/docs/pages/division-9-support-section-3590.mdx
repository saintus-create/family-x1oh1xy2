# § 3590 — Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)]

[23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
Commentary
Relying on this section as well as subsections <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of section
3651, In re Marriage of Alter,
171 Cal.App.4th 718, 89 Cal.Rptr.3d 849
(2009), holds that a trial court always has power to modify an existing
child support order upward or downward, regardless of the parties’
contrary marital settlement agreement. Alter establishes that child
support may be modified downward despite the parties’ agreement to
the contrary.
