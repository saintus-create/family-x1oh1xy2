Redeposit of previously refunded accumulated contributions; death of nonmember 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonmember who is awarded a separate account may redeposit accumulated contributions previously refunded to the member in accordance with the determination of the court required by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=21290.">§ 21290</a>, 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The nonmember may redeposit only those accumulated contributions that were previously refunded to the member and that the court has determined to be the community property interest of the nonmember in the accumulated contributions.
