Redeposit
of previously refunded accumulated
contributions; death of nonmember
(a)  The nonmember who is awarded a separate account
may redeposit accumulated contributions previously refunded
to the member in accordance with the determination of the
court required by Section 21290,
(b)  The nonmember may redeposit only those accumulat-
ed contributions that were previously refunded to the mem-
ber and that the court has determined to be the community
property interest of the nonmember in the accumulated
contributions.
(c)  If the nonmember elects to redeposit, he or she shall
repay the accumulated contributions pursuant to Section
20750 or
