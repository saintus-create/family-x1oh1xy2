Effect of referee or commissioner’s decision
(a)  In the case of a consensual general reference pursuant
to Section 638, the decision of the referee or commissioner
upon the whole issue must stand as the decision of the court,
and upon filing of the statement of decision with the clerk of
the court, judgment may be entered thereon in the same
manner
as if the action had been tried by the court.
(b)  In the case of all other references, the decision of the
referee or commissioner is only advisory.
The court may
adopt the referee’s recommendations, in whole or in part,
after independently considering the referee’s findings and any
objections and responses thereto filed with the court.
