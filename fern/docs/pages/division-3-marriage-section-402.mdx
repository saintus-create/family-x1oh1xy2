# § 402 — Officials of nonprofit religious institutions

Officials of nonprofit religious institutions
In addition to the persons permitted to solemnize mar-
riages under Section 400, a county may license officials of a
nonprofit religious institution, whose articles of incorporation
are registered with the Secretary of State, to solemnize the
marriages of persons who are affiliated with or are members
of the religious institution.
The licensee shall possess the
degree of doctor of philosophy and must perform religious
services or rites for the institution on a regular basis.
The
marriages shall be performed without fee to the parties.
