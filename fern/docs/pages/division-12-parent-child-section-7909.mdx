# § 7909 — “Executive head” defined; appointment of compact

“Executive head” as used in Article 7 of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children means the Governor. The Governor shall appoint a compact administrator in accordance with the terms of Article 7 of the compact. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
