# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7909

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

“Executive head” defined; appointment of compact administrator 
“Executive head” as used in Article 7 of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children means the Governor. 
The Governor shall appoint a compact administrator in accordance with the terms of Article 7 of the compact.
