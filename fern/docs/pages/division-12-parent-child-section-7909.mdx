# 7909 — administrator

Parent and Child Relationship

administrator
“Executive head” as used in Article 7 of the Interstate
Compact on the Placement of Children means the Governor.
The Governor shall appoint a compact administrator in
accordance with the terms of Article 7 of the compact.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
