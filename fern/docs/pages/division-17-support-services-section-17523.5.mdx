# § 17523.5

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, in connection with the duty of the department and the local child support agency to promptly and effectively collect and enforce child support obligations under Title IV-D, the transmission, filing, and recording of a lien record by departmental and local child support agency staff that arises pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 4506 of this code or Section 697.320 of the Code of Civil Procedure against the real property of a support obligor in the form of a digital or a digitized electronic record shall be permitted and governed only by this section.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A facsimile signature that complies with the requirements of paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 27201 of the Government Code shall be accepted on any document relating to a lien that is filed or recorded pursuant to this section.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The department and the local child support agency may use the California Child Support Enforcement System to transmit, file, and record a lien record under this section.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in this section shall be construed to require a county recorder to establish an electronic recording delivery system or to enter into a contract with an entity to implement this section.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this section, the following terms have the following meanings:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Digital electronic record” means a record containing information that is created, generated, sent, communicated, received, or stored by electronic means, but not created in original paper form.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Digitized electronic record” means a scanned image of the original paper document.
