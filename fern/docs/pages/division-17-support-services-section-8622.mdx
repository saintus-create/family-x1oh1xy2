# § 8622

*Service limitations;*

Service limitations;
notice
Treatises and Practice Aids
A licensed private adoption agency whose services are
limited to a particular target population shall inform all birth
parents and prospective adoptive parents of its service
limitations before commencing any services, signing any
documents or agreements, or accepting any fees.
