# 8622

Service limitations; notice

A licensed private adoption agency whose services are limited to a particular target population shall inform all birth parents and prospective adoptive parents of its service limitations before commencing any services, signing any documents or agreements, or accepting any fees. This section shall become operative on January 1, 1995. _(Repealed (Jan. 1, 1994) and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 758, Sec. 6.2. Effective January 1, 1994. Section operative January 1, 1995, by its own provisions.)_
