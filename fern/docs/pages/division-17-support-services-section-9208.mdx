# § 9208

*Children*

Children
of Indian ancestry;
States Secretary of the Interior
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The clerk of the superior court entering a final order of
adoption concerning an Indian child shall provide the United
notice
to United

ADOPTION
States Secretary of the Interior or a designee with a
copy of the order within 30 days of the date of the order,
together with any information necessary to show the follow-
ing:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The name and tribal affiliation of the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The names and addresses of the biological parents.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The names and addresses of the adoptive parents.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The identity of any agency having files or information
relating to that adoptive placement.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the court records contain an affidavit of the biologi-
cal parent or parents that their identity remain confidential,
the court shall include that affidavit with the other informa-
tion.
