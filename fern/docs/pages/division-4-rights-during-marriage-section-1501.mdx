# § 1501 — Agreements by minors

Agreements by minors
A minor may make a valid premarital agreement or other
marital property agreement if the minor is emancipated, is
otherwise capable of contracting marriage pursuant to Sec-
tion 302 or 303, or has entered or is entering a marriage that
is valid in the jurisdiction where the marriage is solemnized.
