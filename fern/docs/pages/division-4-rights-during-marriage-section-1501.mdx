# 1501

Agreements by minors

A minor may make a valid premarital agreement or other marital property agreement if the minor is emancipated, is otherwise capable of contracting marriage pursuant to Section 302 or 303, or has entered or is entering a marriage that is valid in the jurisdiction where the marriage is solemnized. _(Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 660, Sec. 7. (SB 273) Effective January 1, 2019.)_
