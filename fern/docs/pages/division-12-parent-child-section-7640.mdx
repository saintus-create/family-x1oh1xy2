# § 7640 — Fees and costs

Fees and costs
The court may order reasonable fees of counsel, experts,
and the child’s guardian ad litem, and other costs of the
action and pretrial proceedings, including genetic testing, to
be paid by the parties, excluding any governmental entity, in
proportions and at times determined by the court.
The court
may apply the standards set forth in Sections 2032 and 7605
in making this determination.
