# 657 — Distribution of collected support

Premarital Agreements

Distribution of collected support
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In general
Subject to subsections <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  and (e), the amounts collected
on behalf of a family as support by a State pursuant to a
plan approved under this part shall be distributed as follows:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Families receiving assistance
In the case of a family receiving assistance from the
State, the State shall—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  pay to the Federal Government the Federal
share of the amount collected, subject to paragraph
(3)(A);
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  retain, or pay to the family, the State share
of the amount
collected,
subject to paragraph
(3)(B); and
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  pay to the family any remaining amount.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Families that formerly received assistance
In the case of a family that formerly received assis-
tance from the State:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Current support
To the extent that the amount collected does not
exceed the current support amount, the State shall
pay the amount to the family.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Arrearages
Except as otherwise provided in an election
made under section 654(34) of this title, to the
extent that the amount collected exceeds the cur-
rent support amount, the State—
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  shall first pay to the family the excess
amount, to the extent necessary to satisfy sup-
port arrearages not assigned pursuant to sec-
tion 608(a)<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of this title;
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  if the amount
collected
exceeds
the
amount required to be paid to the family
under clause (i), shall—
<span class="subsec">(I)</span>  pay to the Federal Government the
Federal share of the excess amount de-

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Limitations

The total of the amounts paid by the State to the

The total of the amounts retained by the State

with respect to a family shall not exceed the State
share of the amount assigned with respect to the
family pursuant to section 608(a)<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of this title.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Families that never received assistance
In the case of any other family, the State shall
distribute to the family the portion of the amount so
collected that remains after withholding any fee pursu-
ant to section 654(6)(B)<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  of this title.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Families under certain agreements
Notwithstanding paragraphs <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  through (3), in the
case of an amount collected for a family in accordance
with a cooperative agreement under section 654(33) of
this title, the State shall distribute the amount collected
pursuant to the terms of the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  State option to pass through additional support
with Federal financial participation
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Families that formerly received assistance
Notwithstanding paragraph (2), a State shall not
be required to pay to the Federal Government the
Federal share of an amount collected on behalf of
a family that formerly received assistance from the
State to the extent that the State pays the amount
to the family.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Continuation of assignments
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  State option to discontinue pre-1997 support. as-
signments
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  In general
Any rights to support obligations assigned to a
State as a condition of receiving assistance from
the State under part A and in effect on September
30, 1997 (or such earlier date on or after August
22, 1996, as the State may choose), may remain
assigned after such date.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Distribution of amounts after assignment dis-
continuation
If a State chooses to discontinue the assignment
of a support obligation described in subparagraph
(A), the State may treat amounts collected pursu-
ant to the assignment as if the amounts had never
been assigned and may distribute the amounts to
the family in accordance with subsection (a)(4).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  State option to discontinue post-1997 assignments
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  In general
Any rights to support obligations accruing before
the date on which a family first receives assistance
under part A that are assigned to a State under
that part and in effect before the implementation
date of this section may remain assigned after such
date.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Distribution of amounts after assignment dis-
continuation
If a State chooses to discontinue the assignment
of a support obligation described in subparagraph
(A), the State may treat amounts collected pursu-
ant to the assignment as if the amounts had never
been assigned and may distribute the amounts to
the family in accordance with subsection (a)(4).
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Definitions
As used in subsection (a):
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Assistance
The term “assistance from the State” means—

PUBLIC HEALTH AND WELFARE
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  assistance under the State program funded
under part A or under the State plan approved
under part A (as in effect on the day before August

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  foster care maintenance payments under the
State plan approved under part E of this subchap-
ter.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Federal share
The term “Federal share” means that portion of the
amount collected resulting from the application of the
Federal medical assistance percentage in effect for the
fiscal year in which the amount is distributed.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Federal medical assistance percentage
The term “Federal medical assistance percentage”
means—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  75 percent, in the case of Puerto Rico, the
Virgin Islands, Guam, and American Samoa;
or
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  the Federal medical assistance percentage
(as defined in section 1396d(b) of this title, as such
section was in effect on September 30, 1995) in the
case of any other State.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  State share
The term “State share” means 100 percent minus the
Federal share.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Current support amount
The term “current support amount” means, with
respect to amounts collected as support on behalf of a
family, the amount designated as the monthly support
obligation of the noncustodial parent in the order re-
quiring the support or calculated by the State based on
the order.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Gap payments not subject to distribution under this
section
At State option, this section shall not apply to any amount
collected on behalf of a family as support by the State (and
paid to the family in addition to the amount of assistance
otherwise payable to the family) pursuant to a plan ap-
proved under this part if such amount would have been paid
to the family by the State under section 602(a)<span class="subsec">(28)</span>  of this
title, as in effect and applied on the day before August 22,
1996.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Amounts collected for child for whom foster care main-
tenance payments are made
Notwithstanding the preceding provisions of this section,
amounts collected by a State as child support for months in
any period on behalf of a child for whom a public agency is
making foster care maintenance payments under part E of
this subchapter—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  shall be retained by the State to the extent neces-
sary to reimburse it for the foster care maintenance
_ payments made with respect to the child during such
period (with appropriate reimbursement of the Federal
Government to the extent of its participation in the
financing);

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  shall be paid to the public agency responsible for
supervising the placement of the child to the extent that
the amounts collected exceed the foster care mainte-

such period but not the amounts required by a court or
administrative order to be paid as support on behalf of
the child during such period; and the responsible agen-

will serve

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  shall be retained by the State, if any portion of

respect to the child (and with respect to which past

and any balance shall be paid to the State agency responsi-
ble for supervising the placement of the child, for use by
such agency in accordance with paragraph (2).

Incentive payments to States.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In general
In addition to any other payment under this part, the
Secretary shall, subject to subsection (f), make an incentive
payment to each State for each fiscal year in an amount
determined under subsection (b).
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Amount of incentive payment
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general
The incentive payment for a State for a fiscal year is
equal to the incentive payment pool for the fiscal year,
multiplied by the State incentive payment share for the
fiscal year.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Treatment of interstate collections
In computing incentive payments under this section, sup-
port which is collected by a State at the request of another
State shall be treated as having been collected in full by both
States, and any amounts expended by a State in carrying out
a special project assisted under section 655(e) of this title
shall be excluded.
( d) Administrative provisions
The amounts of the incentive payments to be made to the
States under this section for a fiscal year shall be estimated
by the Secretary at/or before the beginning of the fiscal year
on the basis of the best information available.
The Secre-
tary shall make the payments for the fiscal year, on a
quarterly basis (with each quarterly payment being made no
later than the beginning of the quarter involved), in the
amounts so estimated, reduced or increased to the extent of
any Overpayments or underpayments which the Secretary
determines were made under this section to the States
involved for prior periods and with respect to which adjust-
ment has not already been made under this subsection.
Upon the making of any estimate by the Secretary under the
preceding sentence, any appropriations available for pay-
ments under this section are deemed obligated.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Regulations
The Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as may be
necessary governing the calculation of incentive payments
under this section, including directions for excluding from
the calculations certain closed cases and cases over which
the States do not have jurisdiction.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Reinvestment
A State to which a payment is made under this section
shall expend the full amount of the payment to supplement,
and not supplant, other funds used by the State—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  to carry out the State plan approved under this
part; or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  for any activity (including cost-effective contracts
with local agencies) approved by the Secretary, whether
or not the expenditures for the activity are eligible for
reimbursement under this part, which may contribute to
improving the effectiveness or efficiency of the State
program operated under this part.
