# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.211

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify spousal-support order 
(a) A tribunal of this state issuing a spousal-support order consistent with the law of this state has continuing, exclusive 401 jurisdiction to modify the spousal-support order throughout the existence of the support obligation. 
(b) A tribunal of this state may not modify a spousalsupport order issued by a tribunal of another state or a foreign country having continuing, exclusive jurisdiction over that order under the law of that state or foreign country. 
(c) A tribunal of this state that has continuing, exclusive jurisdiction over a spousal-support order may serve as: 
(1) an initiating tribunal to request a tribunal of another state to enforce the spousal-support order issued in this state; or 
(2) a responding tribunal to enforce or modify its own spousal-support order.
