# § 356.

*Part 2 — Marriage License*

A marriage license issued pursuant to this part expires 90 days after its issuance. The calendar date of expiration shall be clearly noted on the face of the license. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
