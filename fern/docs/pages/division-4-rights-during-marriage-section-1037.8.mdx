# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1037.8

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Notice; limitations on confidential communications A domestic violence counselor shall inform a domestic violence victim of any applicable limitations on confidentiality of communications between the victim and the domestic violence counselor. 
This information may be given orally.
