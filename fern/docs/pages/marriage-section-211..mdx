# § 211.

*Part 2 — General Procedural Provisions*

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Judicial Council may provide by rule for the practice and procedure in proceedings under this code. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
