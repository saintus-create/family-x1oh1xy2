# § 49 — Personal relations, acts forbidden by

Personal relations, acts forbidden by
The rights of personal relations forbid:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The abduction or enticement of a child from a parent,
or from a guardian entitled to its custody;
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The seduction of a person under the age of legal
consent;
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Any injury to a servant which affects his ability to serve
his master, other than seduction, abduction
or criminal
conversation.
(Enacted in 1872. Amended by Stats.1905, c.
70, p. 68, § 1; Stats.1939, c. 128, p. 1245, § 1; Stats.1939, c.
1103, p. 3037,
