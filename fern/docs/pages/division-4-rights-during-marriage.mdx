## Rights and Obligations During Marriage

  <h3>Remedies for breach of fiduciary duty</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 1101</Badge></div>
        <p>Remedies for the breach of the fiduciary duty by one spouse, as set forth in Sections 721 and 1100, when the breach falls within the ambit of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=3294.">Section 3294 of the Civil Code</a>, shall include, but not be limited to, an award to the other spouse of 100 percent of any asset undisclosed or transferred in breach of the fiduciary duty.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Amended Stats. 2014, Ch. 76, Sec. 1</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2015</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Management and control of community property</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 1103</Badge></div>
        <p>Where one or both of the spouses has a conservator of the estate or lacks legal capacity to manage and control community property, the procedure for management and control of the community property is that prescribed in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=PROB&division=4.&title=&part=6.&chapter=&article=">Part 6 (commencing with Section 3000) of Division 4 of the Probate Code</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1994</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
