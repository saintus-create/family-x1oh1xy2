# § 7603.

*Part 3 — Uniform Parentage Act · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

Section 3140 is applicable to proceedings pursuant to this part. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
