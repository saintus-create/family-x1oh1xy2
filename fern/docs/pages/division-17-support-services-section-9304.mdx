---
title: "9304. Adoptee may take adoptive parent’s family name"
description: "Adoptee may take adoptive parent’s family name"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-9304
---

A person adopted pursuant to this part may take the family name of the adoptive parent.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
