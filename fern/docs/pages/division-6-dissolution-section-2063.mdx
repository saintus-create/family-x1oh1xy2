# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2063

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Employee pension benefit plan; notice of appearance; responsive pleadings 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The employee benefit plan shall file and serve a copy of a notice of appearance upon the party requesting joinder 

§ 2063 within 30 days of the date of the service upon the plan of a copy of the joinder request and summons. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The employee benefit plan may, but need not, file an appropriate responsive. pleading with its notice of appearance. 
If the plan does not file a responsive pleading, all statements of fact and requests for relief contained in any pleading served on the plan are deemed to be controverted by the plan’s notice of appearance.
