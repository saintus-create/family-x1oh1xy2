# § 2053

The insured or policyholder who is a party to the proceeding shall furnish to the other party the name, title, and address of the insurer or the insurer’s agent for service of process. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
