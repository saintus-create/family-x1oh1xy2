# 1812 — OBLIGATIONS

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

OBLIGATIONS
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Collect” and “collected” have the meaning provided
in subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of Section 1798.140.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Expenses incurred by the Attorney General in the
investigation and prosecution of the action as the court
deems appropriate.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Consumer” has the meaning provided in subdivision
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  of Section 1798.140.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any penalties, fees, and expenses recovered in an
action prosecuted under this section shall be deposited in the
Consumer Privacy Fund, created within the General Fund
pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 1798.160, with the
intent that they be used to fully offset costs incurred by the
state courts and the Attorney General in connection with this
title. (Added by Stats.2019, c. 753 (A.B.1202), § 2, eff: Jan. 1,
2020.)
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Data broker” means a business that knowingly collects and sells to third parties the personal information of a
consumer with whom the business does not have a direct
relationship. “Data broker” does not include any of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A consumer reporting agency to the.extent that it is
covered by the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C.
Sec. 1681 et seq.).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A financial institution to the extent that it is covered by
the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (Public Law 106-102)! and
implementing regulations.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  An entity to the extent that it is covered by the
Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Act (Article
6.6 (commencing with Section 1791) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of
of the Insurance Code).
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Personal information” has the meaning provided in
subdivision <span class="subsec">(0)</span>  of Section 1798.140.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Sale” or “sold” have the meaning provided in subdivision <span class="subsec">(t)</span>  of Section 1798.140.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  “Third party” has the meaning provided in subdivision
<span class="subsec">(w)</span>  of Section 1798.140.
(Added by Stats.2019, c. 753
(A.B.1202), § 2, eff: Jan. 1, 2020.)
