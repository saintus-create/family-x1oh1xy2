# § 5206 — “Earnings” defined

“Earnings” defined
“Earnings,”
to the extent that they are subject to an
earnings assignment order for support under Chapter 4
(commencing with Section 703.010) of of Title 9 of

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Wages, salary, bonus, money, and benefits described in
Sections 704.110, 704.113, and 704.115 of the Code of Civil
Procedure.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Payments due for services of independent contractors,
interest, dividends, rents, royalties, residuals, patent rights, or
mineral or other natural resource rights.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Payments or credits due or becoming due as a result of
written or oral contracts for services or sales whether
denominated as wages, salary, commission, bonus, or otherwise.
