# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 710

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Conditions in restraint of marriage Conditions imposing restraints upon marriage, except upon the marriage of a minor, are void; but this does not affect limitations where the intent was not to forbid marriage, but only to give the use until marriage. 
(Enacted in 1872. 
Amended by Code Am.1873-74, c. 612, p. 218,
