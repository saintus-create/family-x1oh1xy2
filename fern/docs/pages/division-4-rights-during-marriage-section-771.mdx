# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 771

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Earnings and accumulations after date of separation 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The earnings and accumulations of a spouse and the minor children living with, or in the custody of, the spouse, after the date of separation of the spouses, are the separate property of the spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision 
(a), the earnings and accumulations of an unemancipated minor child related to a contract of a type described in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6750.">§ 6750</a> shall remain the sole legal property of the minor child.
