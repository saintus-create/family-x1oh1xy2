# § 17390

(a) The Legislature finds and declares that there is no single statewide database containing statistical data regarding child support orders.

(b) The California Child Support Enforcement System or its replacement may be utilized to provide a single statewide registry of all child support orders in California, including orders for cases under Title IV-D of the Social Security Act and all cases with child support orders. _(Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 474, Sec. 13. (AB 2882) Effective January 1, 2017.)_
