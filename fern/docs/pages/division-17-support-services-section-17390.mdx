# California Family Code § 17390

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Legislature finds and declares that there is no single statewide database containing statistical data regarding child support orders.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The California Child Support Enforcement System or its replacement may be utilized to provide a single statewide registry of all child support orders in California, including orders for cases under Title IV-D of the Social Security Act and all cases with child support orders.
