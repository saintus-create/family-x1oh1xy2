# 1802 — Application of part

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

Application of part
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  This part applies only in counties in which the superior
court determines that the social conditions in the county and
the number of domestic relations cases in the courts render
the procedures provided in this part necessary to the full and
proper consideration of those cases and the effectuation of
the purposes of this part.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The determination under subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall be made
annually in the month of January by:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The judge of the superior court in counties having only
one superior court judge.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A majority of the judges of the superior court in
counties having more than one superior court judge.
(Stats.
1992,
c.
162 (A.B.2650),
§ 10, operative Jan.
1, 1994.)
