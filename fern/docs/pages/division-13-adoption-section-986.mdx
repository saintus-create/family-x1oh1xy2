# 986

Juvenile court proceedings

1003.
1004.
1005.
1006.
1007.
Juvenile court proceedings
There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding
under the Juvenile Court Law, Chapter 2 (commencing with
Section 200) of Part 1 of of the Welfare and
Institutions Code.
