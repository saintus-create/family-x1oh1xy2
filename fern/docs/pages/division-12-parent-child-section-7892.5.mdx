# § 7892.5 — Or control of parent

or control of parent
the Child.
The court shall not declare an Indian child free from the
custody or control of a parent, unless both of the following
apply:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court finds, supported by clear and convincing
,
Conduct of Hearing.
evidence, that active efforts were made in accordance with
Section 361.7 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
,
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court finds, supported by evidence beyond a
reasonable doubt, including testimony of one or more
“qualified expert witnesses” as described in Section 224.5 of
Testimony.
