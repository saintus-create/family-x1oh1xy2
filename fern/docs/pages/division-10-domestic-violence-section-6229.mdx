# § 6229 — Minor under 12 years of age appearing without

Minor under 12 years of age appearing without
counsel
A minor, under 12 years of age, accompanied by a duly
appointed and acting guardian ad litem, shall be permitted to
appear in court without counsel for the limited purpose of
requesting or opposing a request for a temporary restraining
order or injunction, or both, under this division as provided in
Section 374 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
