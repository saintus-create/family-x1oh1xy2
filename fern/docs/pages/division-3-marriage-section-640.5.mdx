referring discovery disputes to outside referees

(2018), Workers’ Compensation Judge.
It is the intent of the Legislature that the practice and cost
of referring discovery disputes to outside referees be thoroughly reviewed. Therefore, in addition to the requirements
of subdivision (e)  of Section 639, the Judicial Council shall
collect information from the trial courts on the use of
referees in discovery matters pursuant to either Sections 638
and 639. The collected data shall include information on the
number of referees, the cost to the parties, and the time spent
by the discovery referee. The Judicial Council shall report
thereon to the Legislature by July 1, 2003.
2001, c. 362 (S.B.475), 2.)
