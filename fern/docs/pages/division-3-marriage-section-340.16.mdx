# 340.16 — Action for damages suffered as result of sexual

Premarital Agreements

Action for damages suffered as result of sexual
assault that occurred on or after plaintiff's 18th birth-
day
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In any civil action for recovery of damages suffered as
a result of sexual assault, where the assault occurred on or
after the plaintiff's 18th birthday, the time for commence-
ment of the action shall be the later of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Within 10 years from the date of the last act, attempted
act, or assault with the intent to commit an act, of sexual
assault against the plaintiff.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Within three years from the date the plaintiff discovers
or reasonably should have discovered that an injury or illness
resulted from an act, attempted act, or assault with the intent
to commit an act, of sexual assault
against the plaintiff.
