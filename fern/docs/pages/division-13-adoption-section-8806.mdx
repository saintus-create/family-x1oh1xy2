# Adoption

## Section 8806

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Acceptance of consent of birth parent; ascertainment of whether child is proper subject for adoption; determination of suitability of home 
’ 
The department or delegated county adoption agency shall accept the consent of the birth parents to the adoption of the OF child by the petitioners and, before filing its report with the court, shall ascertain whether the child is a proper subject for adoption and whether the proposed home is suitable for the child.
