# § 141 — Rights which may be waived

Rights which may be waived
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The right of a surviving spouse to any of the following
may be waived in whole or in part by a waiver under this
chapter:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Property that would pass from the decedent by intes-
tate succession.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Property that would pass from the decedent by testa-
mentary disposition in a will executed before the waiver.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A probate homestead.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The right to have exempt property set aside.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Family allowance.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The right to have an estate set aside under Chapter 6
(commencing with Section 6600) of Part 3 of .
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The right to elect to take community
or quasi-
community property against the decedent’s will.
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  The right to take the statutory share of an omitted
spouse.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  The right to be appointed as the personal representa-
tive of the decedent’s estate.
<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  An interest in property that is the subject of a
nonprobate transfer on death under (commencing with
Section 5000) of .
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in this chapter affects or limits the waiver or
manner of waiver of rights other than those referred to in
subdivision (a), including, but not limited to, the right to
property that would pass from the decedent to the surviving
spouse by nonprobate
transfer upon the death of the
decedent, such as the survivorship interest under a joint
tenancy, a Totten trust account, or a pay-on-death account.
