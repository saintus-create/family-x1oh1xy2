# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 141

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Rights which may be waived 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The right of a surviving spouse to any of the following may be waived in whole or in part by a waiver under this chapter: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Property that would pass from the decedent by intestate succession. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Property that would pass from the decedent by testamentary disposition in a will executed before the waiver. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A probate homestead. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The right to have exempt property set aside. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Family allowance. 
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The right to have an estate set aside under Chapter 6 
(commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6600.">§ 6600</a>) of Part 3 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=6.">Division 6</a>. 
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The right to elect to take community or quasicommunity property against the decedent’s will. 
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  The right to take the statutory share of an omitted spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  The right to be appointed as the personal representative of the decedent’s estate. 
<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  An interest in property that is the subject of a nonprobate transfer on death under Part 1 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5000.">§ 5000</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=5.">Division 5</a>. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in this chapter affects or limits the waiver or manner of waiver of rights other than those referred to in subdivision (a), including, but not limited to, the right to property that would pass from the decedent to the surviving spouse by nonprobate transfer upon the death of the decedent, such as the survivorship interest under a joint tenancy, a Totten trust account, or a pay-on-death account.
