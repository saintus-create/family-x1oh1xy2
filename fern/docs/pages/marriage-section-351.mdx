# General Provisions

## Section 351

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Contents of license The marriage license shall show all of the following: 

§ 351 
(a) The identity of the parties to the marriage. 
(b) The parties’ full given names at birth or by court order, and mailing addresses. 
(c) The parties’ dates of birth.
