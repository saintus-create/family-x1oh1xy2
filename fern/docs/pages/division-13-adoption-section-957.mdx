# § 957 — Testimony—Al-

Testimony—Al-
claim.
There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue between parties all of whom claim
through a deceased client, regardless of whether the claims
are by testate or intestate succession, nonprobate transfer, or
inter vivos transaction.
