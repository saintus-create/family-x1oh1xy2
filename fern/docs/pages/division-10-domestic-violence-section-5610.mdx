# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5610

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

“Private child support collector” defined. 
Court orders for child support and child support agreements; inclusion of provision for separate money judgment to pay fee of private child support collector; enforcement; assignment.
