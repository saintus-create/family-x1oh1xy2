---
title: "5216. ‘“Obligor’” defined"
description: "‘“Obligor’” defined"
slug: division-9-support-section-5216
---

“Obligor” means a person owing a duty of support.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
