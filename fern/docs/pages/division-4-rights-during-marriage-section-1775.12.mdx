The first sentence makes it an irregularity to refer to a
mediation in a subsequent civil trial; the second sentence extends that
rule to other noncriminal proceedings, such as an administrative
adjudication.
An appropriate situation for invoking this section is
where a party urges the trier of fact to draw an adverse inference
from an adversary’s refusal to disclose mediation communications.
See Section 1115 (“mediation” defined).
[1997-98 Annual Re-
port, 27 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports App. 5 (1997)].
