---
title: "3907. Any person nominated as future custodian may decline to serve"
description: "Any person nominated as future custodian may decline to serve"
slug: division-9-support-section-3907
---

Any person nominated as future custodian may decline to serve
before the transfer occurs and may resign at any time after the
transfer.
