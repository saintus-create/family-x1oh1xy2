# 3200 — lines

Custody of Children

lines
guide-
The Judicial Council shall develop standards for supervised
visitation providers in accordance with the guidelines set
forth in this section. For the purposes of the development of
these standards, the term “provider” shall include any
individual who functions as a visitation monitor, as well as
supervised visitation centers.
Provisions shall be made
within the standards to allow for the diversity of supervised
visitation providers.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When developing standards, the Judicial Council shall
consider all of the following issues:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The. provider’s qualifications, experience, and edu-—
cation.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Safety and security procedures, including ratios of
children per supervisor.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Any conflict of interest.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Maintenance and disclosure of records, including confidentiality policies.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Procedures for screening, delineation of terms and
conditions, and termination of supervised visitation services.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Procedures for emergency or extenuating situations.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Orientation to and guidelines for cases in which there
are allegations , child abuse, substance
abuse, or special circumstances.
