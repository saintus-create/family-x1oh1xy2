---
title: "7602. Application regardless of marital status of parents"
description: "Application regardless of marital status of parents"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-7602
---

The parent and child relationship extends equally to every child and to every parent, regardless of the marital status of the parents.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
