# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7602

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Application regardless of marital status of parents The parent and child relationship extends equally to every child and to every parent, regardless of the marital status of the parents.
