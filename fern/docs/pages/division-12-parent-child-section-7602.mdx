# § 7602

*provision or source of law governing the rights, protections,*

provision or source of law governing the rights, protections,
benefits, responsibilities, obligations, and duties,of parents,
any reference to two parents shall be interpreted to apply to
every parent of a child where that child has been found to
have more than two parents under this part... (Stats. 1992, c.
Application regardless of marital status of parents
The parent and child relationship extends equallyto every
child and to every parent, regardless of the marital status of
the parents.
