Conveyance or contract; rescission; rebuttable pre-
sumption
(a)  A conveyance or other contract of a person of unsound
mind, but not entirely without understanding, made before
the incapacity of the person has been judicially determined, is
subject to rescission, as provided in Chapter 2 (commencing
with Section 1688) of Title 5 of Part 2 of .
(b)  A rebuttable presumption affecting the burden of
proof that a person is of unsound mind shall exist. for
purposes of this section if the person is substantially unable to
manage his or her own financial resources or resist fraud or
undue influence.
Substantial inability may not be proved
solely by isolated incidents of negligence or improvidence.
