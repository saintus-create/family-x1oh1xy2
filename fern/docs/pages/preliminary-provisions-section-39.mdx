# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 39

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Conveyance or contract; rescission; rebuttable presumption 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A conveyance or other contract of a person of unsound mind, but not entirely without understanding, made before the incapacity of the person has been judicially determined, is subject to rescission, as provided in Chapter 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1688.">§ 1688</a>) of Title 5 of Part 2 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=3.">Division 3</a>. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A rebuttable presumption affecting the burden of proof that a person is of unsound mind shall exist. for purposes of this section if the person is substantially unable to manage his or her own financial resources or resist fraud or undue influence. 
Substantial inability may not be proved solely by isolated incidents of negligence or improvidence.
