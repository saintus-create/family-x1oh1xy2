# 6401 — 10th Husband

Prevention of Domestic Violence

10th Husband
and Wife

Definitions
In this part:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Foreign protection order” means a protection order
issued by a tribunal of another state.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Issuing state” means the state whose tribunal issues a
protection order.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  “Mutual foreign protection orden? means a foreign
protection order that includes provisions in favor of both the
protected individual seeking enforcement, of the order and
the respondent.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  “Protected individual” means an individual protected
by a protection order.
