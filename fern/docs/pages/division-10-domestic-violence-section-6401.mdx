# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6401

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

10th Husband
and Wife
§ 409
Definitions
In this part:
(1) “Foreign protection order” means a protection order
issued by a tribunal of another state.
(2) “Issuing state” means the state whose tribunal issues a
protection order.
(3) “Mutual foreign protection orden? means a foreign
protection order that includes provisions in favor of both the
protected individual seeking enforcement, of the order and
the respondent.
(4) “Protected individual” means an individual protected
by a protection order.
