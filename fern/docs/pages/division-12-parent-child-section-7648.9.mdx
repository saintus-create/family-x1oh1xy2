# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7648.9

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Assisted reproduction and surrogacy agreements This article does not establish a basis for setting aside or vacating a judgment establishing paternity with regard to a child conceived by assisted reproduction pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7613.">§ 7613</a> or a child conceived pursuant to a surrogacy agreement.
