# § 7648.9 — Assisted reproduction and surrogacy agreements

Assisted reproduction and surrogacy agreements
This article does not establish a basis for setting aside or
vacating a judgment establishing paternity with regard to a
child conceived by assisted reproduction pursuant to Section
7613 or a child conceived pursuant to a surrogacy agreement.
