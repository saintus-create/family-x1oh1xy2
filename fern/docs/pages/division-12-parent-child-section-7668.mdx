# § 7668 — Continuance of proceedings; purpose

Continuance of proceedings; purpose
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may continue the proceedings for not more
than 30 days as necessary to appoint counsel and to enable

PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP
counsel to prepare for the case adequately or for other good
cause.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In order to obtain an order for a continuance of the
hearing, written notice shall be filed within two court days of
the date set for the hearing, together with affidavits or
declarations detailing specific facts showing that a continu-
ance is necessary, unless the court for good cause entertains
an oral motion for continuance.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Continuances shall be granted only upon a showing of
good cause.
Neither a stipulation between counsel nor the
convenience of the parties is in and of itself a good cause.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A continuance shall be granted only for that period of
time shown to be necessary by the evidence considered at the
hearing on the motion.
Ifa continuance is granted, the facts
proven which require the continuance shall be entered upon
the minutes of the court.
