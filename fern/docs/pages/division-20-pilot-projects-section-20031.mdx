# 20031

The pilot project applies to all hearings, for temporary or

The pilot project applies to all hearings, for temporary or permanent child or spousal support, modifications thereof, health insurance, custody, or visitation in a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage, legal separation of the parties, exclusive custody, or pursuant to the Uniform Parentage Act (Part 3 (commencing with Section 7600) of Division 12). Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.
