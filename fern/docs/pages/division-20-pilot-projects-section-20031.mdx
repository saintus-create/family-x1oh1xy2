# 20031. The pilot project applies to all hearings, for temporary or

*Pilot Projects*

The pilot project applies to all hearings, for temporary or
permanent child or spousal support, modifications thereof,
health insurance, custody, or visitation in a proceeding for
dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage, legal separation
of the parties, exclusive custody, or pursuant to the Uniform
Parentage Act ( (commencing with Section 7600) of
).
1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
