# § 6325.5 — Ex parte order regarding insurance coverage

Ex parte order regarding insurance coverage
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may issue an ex parte order restraining any
party from cashing, borrowing against, canceling, transferring,
disposing of, or changing the beneficiaries of any insurance or
other coverage held for the benefit of the parties, or their
child or children, if any, for whom support may be ordered, or
both.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall become operative on July 1, 2014.
