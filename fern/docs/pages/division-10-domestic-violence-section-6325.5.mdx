# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6325.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Ex parte order regarding insurance coverage 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may issue an ex parte order restraining any party from cashing, borrowing against, canceling, transferring, disposing of, or changing the beneficiaries of any insurance or other coverage held for the benefit of the parties, or their child or children, if any, for whom support may be ordered, or both. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall become operative on July 1, 2014.
