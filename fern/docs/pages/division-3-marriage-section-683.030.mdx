# § 683.030

*able in installments*

able in installments
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The judgment may not be enforced.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  All enforcement procedures pursuant to the judgment
or to a writ or order issued pursuant to the judgment shall
cease.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Any lien created by an enforcement procedure pursuant to the judgment is extinguished. (Added by Stats. 1982, c.
1364, p. 5073, § 2, operative July 1, 1983.)
