able in installments
(a)  The judgment may not be enforced.
(b)  All enforcement procedures pursuant to the judgment
or to a writ or order issued pursuant to the judgment shall
cease.
(c)  Any lien created by an enforcement procedure pursuant to the judgment is extinguished. (Added by Stats. 1982, c.
1364, p. 5073, 2, operative July 1, 1983.)
