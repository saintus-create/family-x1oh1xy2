# § 2543

*(d) Except as provided in subdivision (e), this section does*

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (e), this section does
not excuse compliance with the requirements for notice to a
person who has requested special notice pursuant to Chapter
10 (commencing with Section 2700) of .
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The court, for good cause, may dispense with the
notice otherwise required to be given to a person as provided
in this section.
