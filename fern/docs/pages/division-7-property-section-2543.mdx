# Division of Property

## Section 2543

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

(d) Except as provided in subdivision (e), this section does not excuse compliance with the requirements for notice to a person who has requested special notice pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2700.">Section 2700</a>) of Part 4. 
(e) The court, for good cause, may dispense with the notice otherwise required to be given to a person as provided in this section.
