# 1424

Interested person

Interested person
“Interested person” includes, but is not limited to:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Any interested state, local, or federal entity or agency.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any interested public officer or employee of this state
or of a local public entity of this state or of the federal
government.
