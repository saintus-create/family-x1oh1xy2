# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1020

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Exception: Breach of duty arising out of psychotherapist-patient relationship There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue of breach, by the psychotherapist or by the patient, of a duty arising out of the psychotherapistpatient relationship.
