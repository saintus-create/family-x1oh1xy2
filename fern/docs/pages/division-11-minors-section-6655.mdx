# § 6655

(a) This part does not affect a right or remedy available under any other law of the state.

(b) This part does not have any effect on a minor who produces their own content. _(Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 611, Sec. 1. (SB 764) Effective January 1, 2025.)_
