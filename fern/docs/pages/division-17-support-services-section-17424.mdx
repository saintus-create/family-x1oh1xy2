# Support Services

## Section 17424

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Medical insurance form; submission 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A parent who has been served with a medical insurance form shall complete and return the form to the local child support agency’s office within 20 calendar days of the date the form was served. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The local child support agency shall send the completed medical insurance form to the department in the manner prescribed by the department.
