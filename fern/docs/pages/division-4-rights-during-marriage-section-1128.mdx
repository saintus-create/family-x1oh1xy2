# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1128

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Subsequent trials; references to mediation Any reference to a mediation during any subsequent trial is an irregularity in the proceedings of the trial for the purposes of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=657.">Section 657</a> of the Code of Civil Procedure. 
Any reference to a mediation during any other subsequent noncriminal proceeding is grounds for vacating or modifying the decision in that proceeding, in whole or in part, and granting a new or further hearing on all or part of the issues, if the reference materially affected the substantial rights of the party requesting relief.
