Subsequent trials; references to mediation
Any reference to a mediation during any subsequent trial is
an irregularity in the proceedings of the trial for the purposes

EXTRINSIC POLICIES
of Section 657 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
Any
reference
to a mediation
during any other subsequent
noncriminal proceeding is grounds for vacating or modifying
the decision in that proceeding, in whole or in part, and
granting a new or further hearing on all or part of the issues,
if the reference materially affected the substantial rights of
the party requesting relief.
