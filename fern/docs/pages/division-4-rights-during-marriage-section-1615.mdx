# 1615. Unenforceable

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Unenforceable
voluntariness
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A premarital agreement is not enforceable if the party
against whom enforcement is sought proves either of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  That party did not execute the agreement voluntarily.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The agreement was unconscionable when it was exe-
cuted and, before execution of the agreement, all of the
following applied to that party:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  That party was not provided a fair, reasonable, and
full disclosure of the property or financial obligations of the
other party.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  That party did not voluntarily and expressly waive, in
writing, any right to disclosure of the property or financial
obligations of the other party beyond the disclosure provided.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  That party did not have, or reasonably could not have
had, an adequate knowledge of the property or financial
obligations of the other party.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An issue of unconscionability of a premarital agree-
ment shall be decided by the court as a matter of law.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For the purposes of subdivision (a), it shall be deemed
that a premarital agreement was not executed voluntarily
unless the court finds in writing or on the record all of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The party against whom enforcement is sought was
represented by independent legal counsel at the time of
Signing the agreement
or, after being advised
to seek
independent legal counsel, expressly waived, in a separate
writing, representation by independent legal counsel.
The
advisement to seek independent legal counsel shall be made

signed.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  One of the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  For an agreement executed between January 1,
2002, and January 1, 2020, the party against whom enforce-
ment is sought had not less than seven calendar days between
the time that party was
first presented with the final
agreement and advised to seek independent legal counsel and
the time the agreement was signed.
This requirement does
not apply to nonsubstantive amendments that do not change
the terms of the agreement.
B) For an agreement executed on or after January
1,
2020, the party against whom enforcement is sought had not
less than seven calendar days between the time that party was
first_presented with the final agreement and the time the
agreement _was signed, regardless of whether the party is
represented by legal counsel._This requirement does not
apply to nonsubstantive amendments that do not change the
terms of the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The party against whom enforcement is sought, if
unrepresented by legal counsel, was fully informed of the
terms and basic effect of the agreement as well as the rights
and obligations the party was giving up by signing the
agreement, and was proficient in the language in which the
explanation of the party’s rights was conducted and in which
the agreement was written.
The explanation of the rights
and obligations relinquished shall be memorialized in writing
and delivered to the party prior fo signing the agreement.
The unrepresented party shall, on or before the signing of the
premarital agreement, execute a document declaring that
the party received the information required by this
paragraph and indicating who provided that information.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The agreement and the writings executed pursuant to
paragraphs <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  were not executed under duress,
fraud, or undue influence, and the parties did not lack
capacity to enter into the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Any other factors the court deems relevant.
