# General Provisions

## Section 273.4

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Female genital mutilation; additional punishment 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the act constituting a felony violation of subdivision 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 273a was female genital mutilation, as defined in subdivision (b), the defendant shall be punished by an additional term of imprisonment in the state prison for one year, in addition and consecutive to the punishment prescribed by Section 273a. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Female genital mutilation” means the excision or infibulation of the labia majora, labia minora, clitoris, or vulva, performed for nonmedical purposes. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section shall preclude prosecution under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=203.">§ 203</a>, 205, or 206 or any other provision of law.
