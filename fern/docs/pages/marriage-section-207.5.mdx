# 207.5

Misrepresentation or false identification to secure

Misrepresentation or false identification to secure
admission to facility; misdemeanor
Every person who misrepresents or falsely identifies him-
self or herself either verbally or by presenting any fraudulent
written instrument
to any probation
officer,
or to any
superintendent, director, counselor, or employee of a juvenile
hall, ranch, or camp for the purpose of securing admission to
the premises or grounds of any juvenile hall, ranch, or camp,
or to gain access to any minor detained therein, and who
would not otherwise qualify for admission or access thereto,
is guilty of a misdemeanor.
