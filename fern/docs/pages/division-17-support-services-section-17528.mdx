# § 17528 — Ing overdue support obligations

ing overdue support obligations

withhold-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  As authorized by subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 704.110 of
the Code of Civil Procedure, the following actions shall be
taken in order to enforce support obligations that are not
being met:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Within 18 months of implementation of the California
Child Support Enforcement System (CSE), or its replacement as prescribed by former Section 10815 of the Welfare
,
In General.
