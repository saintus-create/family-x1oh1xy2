# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7805

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Inspection of petitions, reports, and court records and briefs 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A petition filed in a proceeding under this part, or a report of the probation officer or county department designated by the board of supervisors to administer the public social services program filed in a proceeding under this part, may be inspected only by the following persons: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Court personnel. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child who is the subject of the proceeding. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The parents or guardian of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The attorneys for the parties. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Any other person designated by the judge. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In a proceeding before the court of appeal or Supreme Court to review a judgment or order entered in a proceeding under this part, the court record and briefs filed by the parties may be inspected only by the following persons: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Court personnel. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A party to the proceeding. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The attorneys for the parties. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Any other person designated by the presiding judge of the court before which the matter is pending. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, if it is believed that the welfare of the child will be promoted thereby, the court and the probation officer may furnish information, pertaining to a petition under this part, to any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The State Department of Social Services. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Acounty welfare department. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A public welfare agency. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A private welfare agency licensed by the State Department of Social Services.
