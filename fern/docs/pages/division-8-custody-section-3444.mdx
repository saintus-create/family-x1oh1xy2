# Custody of Children

## Section 3444

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Temporary order enforcing out-of-state visitation order 
(a) Acourt of this state which does not have jurisdiction to modify a child custody determination may issue a temporary order enforcing either: 
.. 
(1) A visitation schedule made by a court of another state. 
(2) The visitation provisions of a child custody determination of another state that does not provide for a specific visitation schedule. 
(b) If a court of this state makes an order under paragraph 
(2) of subdivision (a), it shall specify in the order a period that it considers adequate to allow the petitioner to obtain an order from a court having jurisdiction under the criteria specified in Chapter 2 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3421.">Section 3421</a>). The order remains in effect until an order is obtained from the 

§ 3444 other court or the period expires.
