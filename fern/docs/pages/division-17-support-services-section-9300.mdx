---
title: "9300. Adoption of adults or married minors; authority"
description: "Adoption of adults or married minors; authority"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-9300
---

(a) An adult may be adopted by another adult, including a stepparent, as provided in this part.

(b) A married minor may be adopted in the same manner as an adult under this part.

> *Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 266, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.*
