# § 9300 — Adoption of adults or married minors; authority

(a) An adult may be adopted by another adult, including a stepparent, as provided in this part.

(b) A married minor may be adopted in the same manner as an adult under this part. _(Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 266, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
