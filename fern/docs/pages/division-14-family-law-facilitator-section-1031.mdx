# § 1031 — Practice

Practice
Guide:
Administrative
Law
Ch. 9-A,
Penitent
As used in this article, “penitent” means a person who has
made a penitential communication to a member of the clergy.
(Stats.1965, c. 299, $ 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967.
(1965)].
Amended by
c. 806 (A.B.3027), § 20.)
