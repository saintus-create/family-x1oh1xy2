# § 356.5 — Of county

Whenever the court, before or during the hearing on the
petition, is of the opinion that the minor is mentally ill or if
the court is in doubt concerning the mental health of any such
person, the court may order that such person be held
temporarily in the psychopathic ward of the county hospital
or hospital whose services have been approved and/or
contracted for by the department of health of the county, for
observation and recommendation concerning the future care,
supervision, and treatment of such person.
