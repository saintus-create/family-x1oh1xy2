# § 3443

*(a) A court of this state shall recognize and enforce a child*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court of this state shall recognize and enforce a child
custody determination of a court of another state if the latter
court exercised jurisdiction in substantial conformity with this
part or the determination was made under factual circumstances meeting the jurisdictional standards of this part and
the determination has not been modified in accordance with
this part.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A court of this state may utilize any remedy available
under other laws of this state to enforce a child custody
determination made by a court of another state.
The
remedies provided in this chapter are cumulative and do not
affect the availability of other remedies to enforce a child
custody determination.
