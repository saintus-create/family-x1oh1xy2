---
title: "8514. “Days” defined"
description: "“Days” defined"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-8514
---

“Days” means calendar days, unless otherwise specified.

> *Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 585, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.*
