# § 8514

*(2) Assesses the birth parents, prospective adoptive parents, or child.*

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Assesses the birth parents, prospective adoptive parents, or child.
“Days” defined
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Places children for adoption.
“Days” means calendar days, unless otherwise specified.
(Added by Stats.1994, c. 585 (A.B.3336), § 2.)
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Supervises adoptive placements.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Private full-service adoption agencies shall be organized and operated on a nonprofit basis. As a condition of
licensure to provide intercountry adoption services, a private
full-service adoption agency shall be accredited by the
Council on Accreditation, or supervised by an accredited
primary provider, or acting as an exempted provider, in
compliance with Subpart F (commencing with Section 96.29)
of Part 96 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations.
