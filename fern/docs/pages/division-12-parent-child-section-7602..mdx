# § 7602.

*Part 3 — Uniform Parentage Act · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

The parent and child relationship extends equally to every child and to every parent, regardless of the marital status of the parents. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
