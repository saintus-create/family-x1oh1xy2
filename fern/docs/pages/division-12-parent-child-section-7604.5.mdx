# § 7604.5 — Evidence

evidence
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, bills for
pregnancy, childbirth, and genetic testing shall be admissible
as evidence without third-party foundation testimony and
shall constitute prima facie evidence of costs incurred for
those services. (Added by Stats.1997, c. 599 (A.B.573), § 44.)
