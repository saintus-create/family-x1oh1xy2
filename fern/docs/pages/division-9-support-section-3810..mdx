# § 3810.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 8 — Deferred Sale Of Home Order*

This chapter is applicable regardless of whether the deferred sale of home order is made before or after January 1, 1989. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
