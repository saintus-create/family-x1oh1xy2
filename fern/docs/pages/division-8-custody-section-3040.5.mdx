# § 3040.5

*Part 2 — Right To Custody Of Minor Child · Chapter 2 — Matters To Be Considered In Granting Custody*

When making a determination of the best interests of a child pursuant to Section 3011, the court shall consider any relevant, admissible evidence that a parent has caused human trafficking of the child or other parent. (Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 452, Sec. 1. (AB 1375) Effective January 1, 2026.)
