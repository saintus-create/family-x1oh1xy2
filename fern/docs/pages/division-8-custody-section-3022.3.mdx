# Custody of Children

## Section 3022.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Statement of decision Upon the trial of a question of fact in a proceeding to determine the custody of a minor child, the court shall, upon the request of either party, issue a statement of the decision explaining the factual and legal basis for its decision pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=632.">Section 632</a> of the Code of Civil Procedure.
