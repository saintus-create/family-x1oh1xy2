# § 3022.3 — Statement of decision

Statement of decision
Upon the trial of a question of fact in a proceeding to
determine the custody of a minor child, the court shall, upon
the request of either party, issue a statement of the decision
explaining the factual and legal basis for its decision pursuant

TO CUSTODY
to Section 632 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
c. 496 (A.B.402), § 3.)
(Added by

CHILD
,
Trial.
,
In General.
