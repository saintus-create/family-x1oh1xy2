# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2342

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Joint petitions for summary dissolutions; revocation; final judgment Where a joint petition under Chapter 5 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2400.">Section 2400</a>) is thereafter revoked and either party commences a proceeding pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2330.">Section 2330</a> within 90 days from the date of the filing of the revocation, the date the judgment becomes a final judgment under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2339.">Section 2339</a> shall be calculated by deducting the period of time which has elapsed from the date of filing the joint petition to the date of filing the revocation.
