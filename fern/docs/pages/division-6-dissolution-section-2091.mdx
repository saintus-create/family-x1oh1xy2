# § 2091

*A divorce obtained in another jurisdiction shall be of no*

A divorce obtained in another jurisdiction shall be of no
force or effect in this state if both parties to the marriage
were domiciled in this state at the time the proceeding for the
divorce was commenced.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
Application of title; full faith and credit
