# § 7666

*If*

If
an alleged biological father fails to appear or, if appearing,
fails to claim parental rights, those parental rights with
reference to the child shall be terminated.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the biological father or a man claiming to be
the biological father claims parental rights, the court shall
determine biological parentage.
The court shall then
determine if it is in the best interest of the child that the
biological father retain
parental rights, or that an
adoption of the child be allowed to proceed.
The court, in
making that determination, may consider all relevant evi-
dence, including the efforts made by the biological father to
obtain custody, the age and prior placement of the child, and
the effects of a change of placement on the child.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the court finds that it is in the best interest of the
child that the biological father should be allowed to retain
parental rights, the court shall order that the
biological father’s consent is necessary for an adoption.
If
the court finds that the man claiming parental rights is not
the biological father, or that if the man is the biological
father it is in the child’s best interest that an adoption be
allowed to proceed, the court shall order that the consent of
that man is not required for an adoption.
This finding
terminates all parental rights and responsibilities with respect
to the child.
