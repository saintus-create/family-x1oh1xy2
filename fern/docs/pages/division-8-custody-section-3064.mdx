# 3064 — Restrictions on ex parte orders granting or modify-

Custody of Children

Restrictions on ex parte orders granting or modify-
ing custody order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court shall refrain from making an order granting
or modifying a custody order on an ex parte basis unless there
has been a showing of immediate harm to the child or
immediate risk that the child will be removed from the State
of California.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Immediate harm to the child” includes, but is not
limited to, the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Having a parent who has committed acts , where the court determines that the acts of domes-
tic violence are of recent origin or are a part of a demonstrat-
ed and continuing pattern of acts .
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Sexual abuse of the child, where the court determines
that the acts of sexual abuse are of recent origin or are a part
of a demonstrated and continuing pattern of acts of sexual
abuse.
