# § 7560.

*Part 2 — Presumption Concerning Child Of Marriage And Genetic Testing To Determine Parentage · Chapter 2 — Genetic Testing To Determine Parentage*

The court or local child support agency shall order additional genetic testing upon the request of a person who contests the results of the initial testing under Section 7555. (Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 23. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)
