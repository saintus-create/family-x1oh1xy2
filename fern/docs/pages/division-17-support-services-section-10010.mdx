# § 10010 — Standards for office

The Judicial Council shall adopt minimum standards for the office of the family law facilitator and any forms or rules of court that are necessary to implement this division. _(Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 957, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1997.)_
