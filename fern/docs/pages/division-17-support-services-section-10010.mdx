# § 10010

*Standards for office*

Standards for office
The Judicial Council shall adopt minimum standards for
the office of the family law facilitator and any forms or rules
of court that are necessary to implement this division.
(Added by Stats.1996, c. 957 (A.B.1058), § 9.)
