# 20017. Family Law Evaluator; licensure as attorney

*Pilot Projects*

[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
Family Law Evaluator; licensure as attorney
The Family Law Evaluator shall be an attorney, licensed to
practice in this state. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
announced oral order, unless one of the parties is represented
