---
title: "20017. Family Law Evaluator; licensure as attorney"
description: "Family Law Evaluator; licensure as attorney"
slug: division-20-pilot-projects-section-20017
---

The Family Law Evaluator shall be an attorney, licensed to practice in this state.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.*
