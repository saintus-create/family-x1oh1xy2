# § 6920 — Capacity of minor to consent

Subject to the limitations provided in this chapter, notwithstanding any other provision of law, a minor may consent to the matters provided in this chapter, and the consent of the minor’s parent or guardian is not necessary. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
