# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 912

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Quasi-community property; treatment For the purposes of this part, quasi-community property is liable to the same extent, and shall be treated the same in all other respects, as community property.
