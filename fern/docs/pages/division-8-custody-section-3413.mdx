# Custody of Children

## Section 3413

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Order of court if no guardianship exists If the minor has no guardian of the estate and there is money belonging to the minor, the court may order that a guardian of the estate be appointed and that the money be paid to the guardian or the court may order any one or more of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  That the money be deposited in an insured account in a financial institution in this state, or in a single-premium deferred annuity, subject to withdrawal only upon authorization of the court. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  That all or any part of the money be transferred to a custodian for the benefit of the minor under the California Uniform Transfers to Minors Act, Part 9 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3900.">§ 3900</a>). 
 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the money belonging to the minor does not exceed twenty thousand dollars ($20,000), that the money be held on any other condition that the court in its discretion determines to be in the best interests of the minor. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If the money belonging to the minor does not exceed five thousand dollars ($5,000), that all or any part of the money be paid to a parent of the minor, without bond, upon the terms and under the conditions specified in Article 1 
(commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3400.">§ 3400</a>). 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  That the remaining balance of any money paid or to be paid be deposited with the county treasurer, if all of the following conditions are met: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The county treasurer has been authorized by the county board of supervisors to handle the deposits. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The county treasurer shall receive and safely keep all money deposited with the county treasurer pursuant to this subdivision, shall pay the money out only upon the order of the court, and shall credit each estate with the interest earned by the funds deposited less the county treasurer’s actual cost authorized to be recovered under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=27013.">§ 27013</a> of the Government Code. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The county treasurer and sureties on the official bond of the county treasurer are responsible for the safekeeping and payment of the money. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The county treasurer shall ensure that the money deposited is to earn interest or dividends, or both, at the highest rate which the county can reasonably obtain as a prudent investor. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Funds so deposited with the county treasurer shall only be invested or deposited in compliance with the provisions governing the investment or deposit of state funds set forth in Chapter 5 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16640.">§ 16640</a>) of Part 2 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of Title 2 of the Government Code, the investment or deposit of county funds set forth in Chapter 4 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=53600.">§ 53600</a>) of Part 1 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a> of Title 5 of the Government Code, or as authorized under Chapter 6 
(commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2400.">§ 2400</a>) of Part 4. (Stats.1990, c. 79 
(A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.
