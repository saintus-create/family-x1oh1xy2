<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition challenging a voluntary declaration of parentage pursuant to this section shall be supported by a declaration under oath alleging specific facts to support standing under this section. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the court holds a hearing to determine standing, the hearing shall be held on an expedited basis. 
If the person challenging the voluntary declaration of parentage is an alleged genetic parent, genetic testing shall be ordered on an expedited basis.
