# § 2555.

*Part 2 — General Provisions*

The disposition of the community estate, as provided in this division, is subject to revision on appeal in all particulars, including those which are stated to be in the discretion of the court. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
