# Support

## Section 3909

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(p)</span>  “Transferor” means a person who makes a transfer under this part. 
<span class="subsec">(q)</span>  “Trust company” means a financial institution, corporation, or other legal entity, authorized to exercise general trust powers.
