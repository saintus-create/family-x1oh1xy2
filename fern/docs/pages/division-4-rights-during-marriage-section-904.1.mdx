# § 904.1 — Pursuant to this section, and any party who files an opposition

(j)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Any party who files a special motion to strike
pursuant to this section, and any party who files an opposition
to a special motion to strike, shall, promptly upon so filing,
transmit to the Judicial Council, by e-mail or facsimile, a copy
of the endorsed,
filed caption page of the motion
or
opposition, a copy of any related notice of appeal or petition
for a writ, and a conformed copy of any order issued pursuant
to this section, including any order granting or denying a
special motion to strike, discovery, or fees.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The Judicial Council shall maintain a public record of
information transmitted pursuant to this subdivision for at
least three years, and may store the information on microfilm
or other appropriate electronic media.
