# Minors

## Section 6711

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Obligations entered into under express statutory authority A minor cannot disaffirm an obligation, otherwise valid, entered into by, the minor under the express, authority or direction of a statute.
