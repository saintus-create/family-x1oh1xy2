# § 6711

*authority*

authority
Planning § 1:29, Options
Involving Gifts to Minors.
Right of Disaffirmance.
A minor cannot disaffirm an obligation, otherwise valid,
entered into by, the minor under the express, authority or
direction of a statute. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
Treatises and Practice Aids
California Practice Guide Civil Procedure before Trial - Claims and
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
Defenses Ch. 9(I)-B, Elements of Claim.

1 , Contract
for Necessaries.
