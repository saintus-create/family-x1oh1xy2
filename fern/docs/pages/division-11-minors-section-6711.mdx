# § 6711 — Obligations entered into under express statutory

A minor cannot disaffirm an obligation, otherwise valid, entered into by the minor under the express authority or direction of a statute. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
