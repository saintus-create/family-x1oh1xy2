---
title: "6711. Obligations entered into under express statutory"
description: "Obligations entered into under express statutory"
slug: division-11-minors-section-6711
---

A minor cannot disaffirm an obligation, otherwise valid, entered into by the minor under the express authority or direction of a statute.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
