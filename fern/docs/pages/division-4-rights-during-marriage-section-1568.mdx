# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1568

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Consent obtained by cause defeating reality or freedom TO BY , . 
Consent is deemed to have been obtained through one of the causes mentioned in the last section only when it would not have been given had such cause not existed. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
