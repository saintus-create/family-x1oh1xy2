Procedure for obtaining assignment
The obligee seeking issuance of an assignment order to
enforce a support order described in Section 5250 may use
the procedure set forth in this article by filing an application
under Section 5252, or by notice of motion or order to show
cause, or pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 5230.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
