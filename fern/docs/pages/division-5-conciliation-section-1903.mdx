# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1903

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Thus a trial court, a court-connected investigator, and a petitioner have an affirmative duty to inquire whether a child is or may be an Indian child in proceedings for foster care placement, guardianship, conservatorship, custody placement with a non-parent pursuant to Family Code section 3041, a declaration freeing a child from parental custody and control, termination of parental rights, or adoption. In re Noreen G., 181 Cal.App.4th 1359, 105 Cal.Rptr.3d 521 
(2010), review denied. 
The Indian Child Welfare Act does not apply when an Indian child was removed from his mother and placed with his presumed father because, under subsection (c), a proceeding for placement with a parent is not an “Indian child custody proceeding.” In re K\L., 27 Cal.App.5th 332, 237 Cal.Rptr.3d 915, review denied (2018).
