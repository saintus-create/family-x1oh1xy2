# § 4332.

*Part 3 — Spousal Support · Chapter 3 — Spousal Support Upon Dissolution Or Legal Separation*

In a proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal separation of the parties, the court shall make specific factual findings with respect to the standard of living during the marriage, and, at the request of either party, the court shall make appropriate factual determinations with respect to other circumstances. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
