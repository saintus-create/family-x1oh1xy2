# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1775.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

To accommodate a wide range of mediation styles, the definition is broad, without specific limitations on format. 
For example, it would include a mediation conducted as a number of sessions, only some of which involve the mediator. 
The definition focuses on the nature of a proceeding, not its label. 
A proceeding may be a “mediation”. for purposes of this chapter, even though it is denominated differently. 
Under subdivision (b), a mediator must be neutral. 
The neutrality requirement is drawn from Code of Civil Procedure
