To accommodate a wide range of media-
tion styles, the definition is broad, without specific limitations on
format.
For example, it would include a mediation conducted as a
number of sessions, only some of which involve the mediator.
The
definition focuses on the nature of a proceeding, not its label.
A
proceeding may be a “mediation”. for purposes of this chapter, even
though it is denominated differently.
Under subdivision (b), a mediator must be neutral.
The neutrality
requirement is drawn from Code of Civil Procedure
