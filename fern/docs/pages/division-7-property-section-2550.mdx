# 2550

Division of Property

Except upon the written agreement of the parties, or on
oral stipulation of the parties in open court, or as otherwise
provided in this division, in a proceeding for dissolution of
marriage or for legal separation of the parties, the court shall,
either in its judgment of dissolution of the marriage, in its
judgment of legal separation of the parties, or at a later time
if it expressly reserves jurisdiction to make such a property
division, divide the community estate of the parties equally.

Manner of division of community estate
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
