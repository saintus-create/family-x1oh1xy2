# 304.7

For judges

for judges
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Judicial Council shall develop and implement
standards for the education and training of all judges who
conduct hearings pursuant to Section 300. The training shall
include, but not be limited to, all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A component relating to Section 300 proceedings for
newly appointed or elected judges and an annual training
session in Section 300 proceedings.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Cultural competency and sensitivity relating to, and
best practices for, providing adequate care to lesbian, gay,
bisexual, and transgender youth.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The information described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of Section
This section shall not be construed to divest the domestic
relations division of a superior court from hearing any issues
regarding the custody of a child when that child is no longer a
dependent of the juvenile court. (Added by Stats.1987, c.
1485, 8. Amended by Stats.1989, c. 137, 1; Stats.1992, c.
163 (A.B.2641), 135, operative Jan. 1, 1994;
c.
16501.4.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The information described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(i)</span>  of Section
16521.5.
