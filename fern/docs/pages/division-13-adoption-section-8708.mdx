# Adoption

## Section 8708

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Race, color, or national origin of adoptive parent or child; nonresident status of adoptive parent 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The adoption agency to which a child has been freed for adoption by either relinquishment or termination of parental rights shall not do any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Deny to any person the opportunity to become an adoptive parent on the basis of the race, color, or national origin of the person or the child involved. 

§ 8708 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Delay or deny the placement of a child for adoption on the basis of the race, color, or national origin of the adoptive parent or the child involved. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Delay or deny the placement of a child for adoption solely because the prospective, approved adoptive family resides outside the jurisdiction of the department, county adoption agency, or licensed adoption agency. 
For purposes of this paragraph, an approved adoptive family means a family approved pursuant to the California adoptive applicant assessment standards or approved as a resource family pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1517.">§ 1517</a> of the Health and Safety Code or
