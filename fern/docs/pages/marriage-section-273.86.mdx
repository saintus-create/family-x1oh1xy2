# 273.86. Characterization of defendant as spousal abuser

*Marriage*

Characterization of defendant as spousal abuser
The characterization of a defendant as a “spousal abuser”
as defined by this chapter shall not be communicated to the
trier of fact. (Added by Stats.1985, c.
1122,
