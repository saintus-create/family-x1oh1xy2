# § 6511 — Petition; notice

Petition; notice
A petition for an order under Section 6510 may be filed by
any interested person. Notice of the hearing on the petition
shall be given as provided in Section 1220. (Stats.1990, c. 79

,
Temporary Possession of Dwelling and Exempt Property.
(A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
CHAPTER 2. SETTING ASIDE EXEMPT PROPERTY
OTHER THAN FAMILY DWELLING
