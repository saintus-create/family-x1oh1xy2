# Support

## Section 4725

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Motion for judgment; enforcement If the child support owed, or any arrearages, interest, or penalty, remains unpaid more than 30 days after serving the notice of delinquency, the support obligee may file a motion to obtain a judgment for the amount owed, which shall be enforceable in any manner provided by law for the enforcement of judgments.
