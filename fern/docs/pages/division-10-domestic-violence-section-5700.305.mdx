# California Family Code § 5700.305

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When a responding tribunal of this state receives a petition or comparable pleading from an initiating tribunal or directly pursuant to Section 5700.301(b), it shall cause the petition or pleading to be filed and notify the petitioner where and when it was filed.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A responding tribunal of this state, to the extent not prohibited by other law, may do one or more of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  establish or enforce a support order, modify a child-support order, determine the controlling child-support order, or determine parentage of a child;

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  order an obligor to comply with a support order, specifying the amount and the manner of compliance;

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  order income withholding;

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  determine the amount of any arrearages, and specify a method of payment;

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  enforce orders by civil or criminal contempt, or both;

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  set aside property for satisfaction of the support order;

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  place liens and order execution on the obligor’s property;

<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  order an obligor to keep the tribunal informed of the obligor’s current residential address, electronic-mail address, telephone number, employer, address of employment, and telephone number at the place of employment;

<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  issue a bench warrant for an obligor who has failed after proper notice to appear at a hearing ordered by the tribunal and enter the bench warrant in any local and state computer systems for criminal warrants;

<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  order the obligor to seek appropriate employment by specified methods;

<span class="subsec">(11)</span>  award reasonable attorney’s fees and other fees and costs; and

<span class="subsec">(12)</span>  grant any other available remedy.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A responding tribunal of this state shall include in a support order issued under this part, or in the documents accompanying the order, the calculations on which the support order is based.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A responding tribunal of this state may not condition the payment of a support order issued under this part upon compliance by a party with provisions for visitation.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  If a responding tribunal of this state issues an order under this part, the tribunal shall send a copy of the order to the petitioner and the respondent and to the initiating tribunal, if any.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  If requested to enforce a support order, arrears, or judgment or modify a support order stated in a foreign currency, a responding tribunal of this state shall convert the amount stated in the foreign currency to the equivalent amount in dollars under the applicable official or market exchange rate as publicly reported.
