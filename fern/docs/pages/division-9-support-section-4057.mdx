# Support

## Section 4057

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

If the court makes that finding, the court shall divide child support obligations among the parents in a manner that is just and appropriate based on income and amount of time spent with the child by each parent, applying the principles set forth in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4053.">§ 4053</a> and this article. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section shall be construed to require reprogramming of the California Child Support Enforcement System, a change to the statewide uniform guideline for determining child support set forth in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4055.">§ 4055</a>, or a revision by the Department of Child Support Services of its regulations, policies, procedures, forms, or training materials.
