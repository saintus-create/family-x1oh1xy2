# § 20036 — Pilot project proceedings;

Upon motion by either party or on the court’s own motion, any proceeding that would otherwise fall within this pilot project may by judicial order be exempted from its requirements. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
