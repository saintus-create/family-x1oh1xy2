# § 6900 — Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the

Construction of Witkin, California Summary 10th Torts § 460 (2018), In General.
Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the
definitions in this chapter govern the construction of this part.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
