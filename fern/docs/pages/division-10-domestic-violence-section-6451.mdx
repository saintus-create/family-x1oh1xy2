# § 6451 — In this part:

In this part:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Canadian domestic violence protection order” means
a judgment or part of a judgment or order issued in English
in a civil proceeding by a court of Canada under law of the
issuing jurisdiction that relates to domestic violence and
prohibits a respondent from doing any of the following:

PREVENTION
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Being in physical proximity to a protected individual or
following a protected individual.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Directly or indirectly contacting or communicating
with a protected individual or other individual described in
the order.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Being within a certain distance of a specified place or
location associated with a protected individual.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Molesting, annoying, harassing, or engaging in threat-
ening conduct directed at a protected individual.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Domestic protection order” means an injunction or
other order issued by a tribunal that relates to domestic or
family violence laws to prevent an individual from engaging in
violent or threatening acts against, harassment of, direct or
indirect contact or communication with, or being in physical
proximity to, another individual.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Issuing court” means the court that issues a Canadian
domestic violence protection order.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Law enforcement officer” means an individual au-
thorized by law of this state to enforce a domestic protection
order.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Person” means
an individual,
estate, business
or
nonprofit entity, public corporation, government or govern-
mental subdivision, agency, or instrumentality, or other legal
entity.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Protected individual” means an individual protected
by a Canadian domestic violence protection order.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  “Record” means information that is inscribed on a
tangible medium or that is stored in an electronic or other
medium and is retrievable in perceivable form.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  “Respondent” means an individual against whom a
Canadian domestic violence protection order is issued.
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  “State” means a state of the United States, the District
of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the United States Virgin Islands,
or any territory or insular possession subject to the jurisdic-
tion of the United States.
The term includes a federally
recognized Indian tribe.
<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  “Tribunal” means
a court, agency, or other entity
authorized by law to establish, enforce, or modify a domestic
protection order.
