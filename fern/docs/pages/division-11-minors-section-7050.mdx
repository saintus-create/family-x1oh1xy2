# § 7050 — An emancipated minor shall be considered as being an adult for the following purposes:

An emancipated minor shall be considered as being an adult for the following purposes:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The minor’s right to support by the minor’s parents.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The right of the minor’s parents to the minor’s earnings and to control the minor.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The application of Sections 300 and 601 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Ending all vicarious or imputed liability of the minor’s parents or guardian for the minor’s torts. Nothing in this section affects any liability of a parent, guardian, spouse, or employer imposed by the Vehicle Code, or any vicarious liability that arises from an agency relationship.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The minor’s capacity to do any of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Consent to medical, dental, or psychiatric care, without parental consent, knowledge, or liability.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Enter into a binding contract or give a delegation of power.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Buy, sell, lease, encumber, exchange, or transfer an interest in real or personal property, including, but not limited to, shares of stock in a domestic or foreign corporation or a membership in a nonprofit corporation.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Sue or be sued in the minor’s own name.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Compromise, settle, arbitrate, or otherwise adjust a claim, action, or proceeding by or against the minor.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Make or revoke a will.

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Make a gift, outright or in trust.

<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  Convey or release contingent or expectant interests in property, including marital property rights and any right of survivorship incident to joint tenancy, and consent to a transfer, encumbrance, or gift of marital property.

<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  Exercise or release the minor’s powers as donee of a power of appointment unless the creating instrument otherwise provides.

<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  Create for the minor’s own benefit or for the benefit of others a revocable or irrevocable trust.

<span class="subsec">(11)</span>  Revoke a revocable trust.

<span class="subsec">(12)</span>  Elect to take under or against a will.

<span class="subsec">(13)</span>  Renounce or disclaim any interest acquired by testate or intestate succession or by inter vivos transfer, including exercise of the right to surrender the right to revoke a revocable trust.

<span class="subsec">(14)</span>  Make an election referred to in Section 13502 of, or an election and agreement referred to in Section 13503 of, the Probate Code.

<span class="subsec">(15)</span>  Establish the minor’s own residence.

<span class="subsec">(16)</span>  Apply for a work permit pursuant to Section 49110 of the Education Code without the request of the minor’s parents.

<span class="subsec">(17)</span>  Enroll in a school or college.
