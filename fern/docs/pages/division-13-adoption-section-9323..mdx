# § 9323.

*Part 3 — Adoption Of Adults And Married Minors · Chapter 2 — Procedure For Adult Adoption*

The court may require notice of the time and place of the hearing to be served on any other interested person and any interested person may appear and object to the proposed adoption. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
