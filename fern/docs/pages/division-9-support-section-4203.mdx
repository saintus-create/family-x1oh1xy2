# Support

## Section 4203

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Charges and expenses 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in Section, 4202, expenses of the county officer designated by the court, and expenses of the local child support agency incurred in the enforcement of an order of the type described in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4200.">§ 4200</a> or 4201, are a charge upon the county where the proceedings are pending. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Fees for service of process in the enforcement of an order of the type described in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4200.">§ 4200</a> or 4201 are a charge upon the county where the process is served. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.2000, c. 808 (A.B.1358), § 39, eff. Sept. 28, 2000.)
