Section 3 may further limit the
application of this chapter to an instrument that became irrevocable
prior to the operative date of the chapter.
See Section 3(d)-(f), (h).
An instrument that is not governed by this chapter would be
governed by the law that applied to the instrument prior to the
operative date of this chapter.
See Section 3(g). [37 Cal.L.Rev.
Comm. Reports 359 (2007)].
