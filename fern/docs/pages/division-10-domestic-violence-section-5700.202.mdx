Duration of personal jurisdiction
Personal jurisdiction acquired by a tribunal of this state ina
proceeding under this part or other law of this state relating
to a support order continues as long as a tribunal of this state
has continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify its order or
continuing jurisdiction to enforce its order as provided by
Sections 5700.205, 5700.206, and 5700.211.
