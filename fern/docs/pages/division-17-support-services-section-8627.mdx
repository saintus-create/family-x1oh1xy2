# § 8627

*(c) A statement that the information provided by any party*

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A statement that the information provided by any party
is not confidential but that this waiver of confidentiality shall
only apply to the parties to the facilitation contract and any
disclosures required by Chapter 7 (commencing with Section
9200) of Part 2 of .
If the facilitator is acting on behalf of more than one party,
all of the parties on whose behalf the facilitator is acting shall
have signed a written agreement authorizing the facilitator to
act on behalf of all of the parties. (Added by Stats.1996, c.
1135 (S.B.2035), § 1.)
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A statement that the adoption facilitator cannot provide any services for which the facilitator is not properly
licensed, such as legal or therapeutic counseling.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A list of all the services that the adoption facilitator is
required to provide under the contract.
