---
title: "3930. Duty to support grandchild"
description: "Duty to support grandchild"
slug: division-9-support-section-3930
---

A parent does not have the duty to support a child of the parent’s child.

> *Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 132. Effective January 1, 1994.*
