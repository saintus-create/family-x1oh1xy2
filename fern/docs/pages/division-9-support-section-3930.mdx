# 3930. Duty to support grandchild

*Support*

Duty to support grandchild
A parent does not have the duty to support a child of the
parent’s child. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
