# General Provisions

## Section 320

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Entry; sufficiency; one and five year limitation of actions No entry upon real estate is deemed sufficient or valid as a claim, unless an action be commenced thereupon within one year after making such entry, and within five years from the time when the right to make it descended or accrued. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
