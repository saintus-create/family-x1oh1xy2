# General Provisions

## Section 278.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Deprivation of custody of child or right to visitation; punishment 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Every person who takes, entices away, keeps, withholds, or conceals a child and maliciously deprives a lawful custodian of a right to custody, or a person of a right to visitation, shall be punished by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding one year, a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or both that fine and imprisonment, or by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1170.">§ 1170</a> for 16 months, or two or three years, a fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars ($10,000), or both that fine and imprisonment. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing contained in this section limits the court’s contempt power. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A custody order obtained after the taking, enticing away, keeping, withholding, or concealing of a child does not constitute a defense to a crime charged under this section.
