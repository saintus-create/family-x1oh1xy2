# 278.5

Violation of Section 278 or 278.5, or both, the court shall

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  At the sentencing hearing following a conviction for a
violation of Section 278 or 278.5, or both, the court shall
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Every person who takes, entices away, keeps, withholds, or conceals a child and maliciously deprives a lawful
custodian of a , or a person of a right to
visitation, shall be punished by imprisonment in a county jail
not exceeding one year, a fine not exceeding one thousand
dollars ($1,000), or both that fine and imprisonment, or by
imprisonment pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(h)</span>  of Section 1170 for
consider any relevant factors and circumstances in aggravation, including, but not limited to, all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child was exposed to a substantial risk of physical
injury or illness.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The defendant inflicted or threatened to inflict physical
harm on a parent or lawful custodian of the child or on the
child at the time of or during the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The defendant harmed or abandoned the child during
the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The child was taken, enticed away, kept, withheld, or
concealed outside the United States.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The child has not been returned to the lawful custodian.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The defendant previously abducted or threatened to
abduct the child.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The defendant substantially altered the appearance or
the name of the child.
(8). The defendant denied the child appropriate education
during the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  The length of the abduction.
<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  The age of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  At the sentencing hearing following a conviction for a
16 months, or two or three years, a fine not exceeding ten
thousand dollars ($10,000), or both that fine and imprisonment.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing contained in this section limits the court’s
contempt power.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A custody order obtained after the taking, enticing
away, keeping, withholding, or concealing of a child does not
constitute a defense to a crime charged under this section.
