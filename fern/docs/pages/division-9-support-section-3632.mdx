# 3632

Effect

effect
CHAPTER 6. MODIFICATION, TERMINATION,
OR SET ASIDE OF SUPPORT ORDERS
retroactive
An order after hearing shall become effective not more
than 30 days after the filing of the response to the application
for the expedited support order and may be given retroactive
effect to the date of the filing of the application. (Stats. 1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650), 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
