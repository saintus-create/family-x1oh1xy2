# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2333

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Irreconcilable differences; order for dissolution Subject to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2334.">§ 2334</a>, if from the evidence at the hearing the court finds that there are irreconcilable differences which 155 
§ 2335 have caused the irremediable breakdown of the marriage, the court shall order the dissolution of the marriage or a legal separation of the parties.
