# Section 753

## Rights and Obligations During Marriage

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

### Exclusion of spouse from other's dwelling
Notwithstanding § 752 and except as provided in Article 2 (commencing with § 2045), Article 3 (commencing with § 2047), or Article 4 (commencing with § 2049) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of Division 6, neither spouse may be excluded from the other's dwelling.

(Stats. 1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
