# California Family Code § 7540

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in Section 7541, the child of spouses who cohabited at the time of conception and birth is conclusively presumed to be a child of the marriage.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The conclusive marital presumption in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  does not apply if the court determines that the husband of the woman who gave birth was impotent or sterile at the time of conception and that the child was not conceived through assisted reproduction.
