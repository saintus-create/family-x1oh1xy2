# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7540

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Conclusive presumption as child of marriage; exceptions. 
Resolution of question of parentage upon finding of court based upon genetic testing that spouse who is presumed parent is not genetic parent of child; action to challenge parentage.
