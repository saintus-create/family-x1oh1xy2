# § 3023

*of*

of
child
In General.
abuse.
OF MINOR
Section 3024 continues former Civil Code Section 4600.5(m)
without substantive change. Although former Civil Code Section
4600.5 related to joint custody, subdivision <span class="subsec">(m)</span>  of that section was
not by its terms limited to a joint custody order. Accordingly,
Section 3024 applies to any custody order, not only a joint custody
order. See also Section 3131 (action byedistrict attorney where child
taken or detained in violation of custody order). [23 Cal.L.Rev.
Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
Sole contested issue or order for separate trial on
issue; preference for trial date
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If custody of a minor child is the sole contested issue,
the case shall be given preference over other civil cases,
except matters to which special precedence may be given by
law, for assigning a trial date and shall be given an early
hearing.
