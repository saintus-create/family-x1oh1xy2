# § 3023 — Sole contested issue or order for separate trial on

Sole contested issue or order for separate trial on
issue; preference for trial date
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If custody of a minor child is the sole contested issue,
the case shall be given preference over other civil cases,
except matters to which special precedence may be given by
law, for assigning a trial date and shall be given an early
hearing.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If there is more than one contested issue and one of
the issues is the custody of a minor child, the court, as to the
issue of custody, shall order a separate trial.
The separate
trial shall be given preference over other civil cases, except
matters to which special precedence may be given by law, for
assigning a trial date.
