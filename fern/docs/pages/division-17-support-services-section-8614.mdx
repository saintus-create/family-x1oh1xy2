# 8614 — Certificate of adoption

Support Services

Certificate of adoption
Upon the request of the adoptive parents or the adopted
child, a clerk of the superior court may issue a certificate of
adoption that states the date and place of adoption, the
birthday of the child, the names of the adoptive parents, and
the name the child has taken. Unless the child has been
adopted by a stepparent or by a relative, as defined in
subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 8616.5, the certificate shall not state

deposition, as it deems necessary. The deposition shall be
