# § 3063

*Treatises and Practice Aids*

Treatises and Practice Aids
In conjunction with any ex parte order seeking or modifying an order of custody, the court shall enter an order
restraining the person receiving custody from removing the
child from the state pending notice and a hearing on the
order seeking or modifying custody.
(Stats.1992, c. 162
(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
