# § 7644 — Voluntary declaration of paternity;

Voluntary declaration of paternity;
child custody
and support actions; force and effect
Section operative until Jan. 1, 2020. . See,
also, § 7644 operative Jan. 1, 2020.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, an action for child
custody and support and for other relief as provided in
Section 7637 may be filed based upon a voluntary declaration
of paternity as provided in Chapter 3, (commencing with
Section 7570) of .
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in Section 7576, the voluntary
declaration of paternity shall be given the same force and
effect as a judgment of parentage entered by a court of
competent jurisdiction.
The court shall make appropriate
orders as specified in Section 7637 based upon the voluntary
declaration of paternity unless evidence is presented that the
voluntary declaration of paternity has-been rescinded by the
parties or set aside as provided in Section 7575 of the Family
Code.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The Judicial Council shall develop the forms and
procedures necessary to implement this section.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  This section shall remain in effect only until January 1,
2020, and as of that date is repealed.
