# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7644

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Voluntary declaration of paternity; child custody and support actions; force and effect Section operative until Jan. 1, 2020. . See, also, § 7644 operative Jan. 1, 2020. 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, an action for child custody and support and for other relief as provided in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7637.">§ 7637</a> may be filed based upon a voluntary declaration of paternity as provided in Chapter 3, (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7570.">§ 7570</a>) of Part 2. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7576.">§ 7576</a>, the voluntary declaration of paternity shall be given the same force and effect as a judgment of parentage entered by a court of competent jurisdiction. 
The court shall make appropriate orders as specified in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7637.">§ 7637</a> based upon the voluntary declaration of paternity unless evidence is presented that the voluntary declaration of paternity has-been rescinded by the parties or set aside as provided in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7575.">§ 7575</a> of the Family Code. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The Judicial Council shall develop the forms and procedures necessary to implement this section. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  This section shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2020, and as of that date is repealed.
