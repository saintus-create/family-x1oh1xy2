# 5700.209. Cal. App.4th 305, 9 Cal. Rpir.3d 902 (2004)

*Support*

Cal. App.4th 305, 9 Cal. Rpir.3d 902 (2004).
Applying this section together with sections 5700.205 and 5700.207,
In re Marriage of Connolly, 20. Cal.App.5th 395, 228 Cal.Rptr.3d 890
(2018), review denied, held that a Utah judgment determining the
amount of spousal support and child support arrearage due under a
California support order was the not the controlling order for
purposes of the Uniform Interstate Family Support Act. Under
section 5700.207 the California Court had continuing exclusive
jurisdiction over spousal and child support because the Utah order
did not modify the California order. It merely calculated spousal and
child support arrearage. Subsection <span class="subsec">(h)</span>  of section 5700.207 required
the California court to enforce the Utah judgment for arrears.
Credit for payments
A tribunal of this state shall credit amounts collected for a
particular period pursuant to any child-support order against
the amounts owed for the same period under any other childsupport order for support of the same child issued by a
tribunal of this state, another state, or a foreign country.
2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff. Jan. 1, 2016.)
However, the trial court erred when it added California interest to
the Utah judgment because the Full Faith and Credit clause (U.S.
Const., article ITV, section 1) required that California enforce the
Utah judgment according to its terms and Utah law.
