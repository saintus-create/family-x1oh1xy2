# § 3060.

*Part 2 — Right To Custody Of Minor Child · Chapter 3 — Temporary Custody Order During Pendency Of Proceeding*

A petition for a temporary custody order, containing the statement required by Section 3429, may be included with the initial filing of the petition or action or may be filed at any time after the initial filing. (Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 497, Sec. 110. (AB 991) Effective January 1, 2020.)
