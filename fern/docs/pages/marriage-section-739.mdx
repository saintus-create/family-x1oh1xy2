# 739. The State Department of Social Services, the Medical Board

*Marriage*

5, the Judicial Council shall consult with
the State Department of Social Services, the Medical Board
of California, the County Welfare Directors Association of
California, the Chief Probation Officers of California, and
groups representing foster children, dependency counsel, and
children’s advocates to help ensure that the child and the
child’s attorney are provided with sufficient information to
understand the request for authorization to obtain the child’s
medical information and the reasons for the request.

the person completing the form affirm that the child or
child’s attorney has been asked about the authorization.
