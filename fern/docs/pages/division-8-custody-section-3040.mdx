# 3040. Order of preference;

*Custody of Children*

<div class="case-callout">
<strong>Ellis v. Lyons (2016)</strong>

*2 Cal.App.5th*

This is the first published case to clarify that an out-of-state court’s finding of DV triggers the rebuttable presumption against granting custody to a parent who committed abuse Family Violence Appellate Project against the other parent in the past five years under Family Code section 3044. The case also holds that a trial court cannot rely on a preference that both parents have “frequent and continuing contact” with their children to rebut this presumption.
