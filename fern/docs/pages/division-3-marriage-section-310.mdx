# § 310

*Methods of dissolution*

Methods of dissolution
Marriage is dissolved only by one of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The death of one of the parties.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A judgment of dissolution of marriage.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A judgment of nullity of marriage.
