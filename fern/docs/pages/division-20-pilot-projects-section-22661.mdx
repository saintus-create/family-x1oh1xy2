Accumulated retirement contributions; nonmember spouse’s right to refund; lump-sum payment; mode of deposit and notice 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonmember spouse who is awarded a separate account under this part shall have the right to a refund of the accumulated retirement contributions in the account under the Defined Benefit Program, and a return of the Defined Benefit Supplement account balance, of the nonmember spouse under this part. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The nonmember spouse shall file an application on a form provided by the system to obtain a refund or lump-sum payment. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (i), the refund of accumulated retirement contributions in the account under the Defined Benefit Program and the return of the accumulated Defined Benefit Supplement account balance under this part are effective when the system deposits in the United States mail an initial warrant drawn in favor of the nonmember spouse and addressed to the latest address for the nonmember spouse on file with the system. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (i), if the nonmember spouse has elected on a form provided by the system to transfer all or a specified portion of the accumulated retirement contributions or accumulated Defined Benefit Supplement account balance that are eligible for direct trustee-to-trustee transfer to the trustee of a qualified plan under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=402.">§ 402</a> of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C.
