<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonmember spouse who is awarded a separate
account under this part shall have the right to a refund of the
accumulated retirement contributions in the account under
the Defined Benefit Program, and a return of the Defined
Benefit Supplement account balance, of the nonmember
spouse under this part.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The nonmember spouse shall file an application on a
form provided by the system to obtain a refund or lump-sum
payment.

accumulated retirement contributions in the account under

part are effective when the system deposits in the United
