# § 2621.

*Part 6 — Debts And Liabilities*

Debts incurred by either spouse before the date of marriage shall be confirmed without offset to the spouse who incurred the debt. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
