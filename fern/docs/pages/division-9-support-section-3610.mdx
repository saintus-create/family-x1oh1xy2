# § 3610 — Section 3605 is new. Section 3605 permits reimbursement from

Section 3605 is new. Section 3605 permits reimbursement from
special needs trusts established under Section 3604, but only on
Section 3605 does not affect other trusts,
including special needs trusts to receive damages or settlement
proceeds established pursuant to court order before the operative
date of this section.
A court order under subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  directing the trustee to pay the
claim or denying the claim is appealable. Section 17207.
Except for statutory liens ordered paid under subdivision <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of
Section 3604, all reimbursement rights of public agencies are
deferred while the special needs trust is in existence. On the death
of the special needs trust beneficiary or on termination of the trust,
trust property may become subject to reimbursement claims under
federal or state law. See, e.g., 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(b)(1)<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  (Medicaid); Welf. & Inst.Code §§ 7276, 7513-7513.2 (state hospital costs),
