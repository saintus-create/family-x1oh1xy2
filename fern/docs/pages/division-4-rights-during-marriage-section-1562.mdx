<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Any failure by an out-of-state group home facility to

for a private interview or make files available for review shall
be grounds to deny or discontinue the certification.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Certifications made pursuant to this subdivision shall
be reviewed annually.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A county shall be required to obtain an assessment and
placement recommendation by a county multidisciplinary
team prior to placement of a child in an out-of-state group
home facility.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Any failure by an out-of-state group home to obtain or
maintain its certification, as required by subdivision (c), shall
preclude the use of any public funds, whether county, state,
or federal, in the payment for the placement of any child in
that out-of-state group home, pursuant to the Interstate
Compact on the Placement of Children.
(f)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A multidisciplinary team shall consist of participat-
ing members from county social services, county mental
health, county probation, county superintendents of schools,
and other members, as determined by the county.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Participants shall have knowledge or experience in the
prevention, identification, and treatment of child abuse and
neglect cases, and shall be qualified to recommend a broad
range of services related to child abuse or neglect.
(g)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The department may deny, suspend, or discontinue
the certification of the out-of-state group home
if the
department makes a finding that the group home is not
operating in compliance with the requirements of subdivision
(c).

<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  The certification requirements of this section shall not

pursuant to the federal Individuals with Disabilities Edu-

effective date of this section shall be eligible for public funds
pending the department’s certification under this section.
