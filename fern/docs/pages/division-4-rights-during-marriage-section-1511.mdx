# 1511. Hearing shall be given as provided in subdivisions (b), (c), (d),

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in subdivisions <span class="subsec">(f)</span>  and (g), at least
15 days before the hearing on the petition for the appointment of a guardian, notice of the time and place of the
hearing shall be given as provided in subdivisions (b), (c), (d),
and <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of this section. The notice shall be accompanied by a
copy of the petition. The court shall not shorten the time for
giving the notice of hearing under this section.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notice shall be served in the manner
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Has not been given notice as required by this section
because the person cannot with reasonable diligence be given
the notice or because the giving of notice to that person
would be contrary to the interest of justice.
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  If notice is required by Section 1460.2 to be given to an
Indian custodian or tribe, notice shall be mailed as required.
(Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.
Amended by Stats.1992, c. 1064 (S.B.1445), § 2; Stats.1996, c.
563 (S.B.392), § 6; Stats.2006, c. 838 (S.B.678), § 23; Stats.
2012, c. 440 (A.B.1488), § 41, eff. Sept. 22, 2012; Stats.2017, c.
319 (A.B.976), § 30, eff: Jan. 1, 2018.)
provided in
Section 415.10 or 415.30 of the Code of Civil Procedure, or in
any manner authorized by the court, on all of the following
persons:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The proposed ward if 12 years of age or older.
