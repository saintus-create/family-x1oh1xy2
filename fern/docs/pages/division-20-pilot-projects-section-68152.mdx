# 68152

When an Agreement and Judgment of Parentage (form DV-

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notice of Entry of Judgment
When an Agreement and Judgment of Parentage (form DV-
180) is filed, the court must serve a Notice of Entry of
Judgment (form FL-190) on the parties.
(Adopted, eff: Jan. 1,
2012. As amended, eff. Jan. 1, 2017.)
