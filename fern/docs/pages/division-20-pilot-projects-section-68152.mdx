# Pilot Projects

## Section 68152

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notice of Entry of Judgment When an Agreement and Judgment of Parentage (form DV180) is filed, the court must serve a Notice of Entry of Judgment (form FL-190) on the parties. 
(Adopted, eff: Jan. 1, 2012. As amended, eff. Jan. 1, 2017.)
