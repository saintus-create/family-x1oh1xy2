# 11487

Aid repaid to the state by means of child support

Aid repaid to the state by means of child support
collections; state, federal, and county shares
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Whenever any aid under this chapter is repaid to the
state by means of child support collections, the state shall be
entitled to the amount received or recovered, except to the
extent that county and federal funds were expended.
If funds
advanced by the federal government were paid, the federal
government
shall be entitled to a share of the amount
received or recovered, proportionate to the amount of federal
funds paid.
Except as provided in subdivision (b), if funds
were paid by a county, the county shall be entitled to a share
of the amount received or recovered, proportionate to the
amount of county funds paid.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For the 2011-12 and 2012-13 fiscal years, the county
share of funds received or recovered pursuant to subdivision
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall instead be suspended and these funds shall be
retained by the state.
