---
title: "6255. Issuance of orders without prejudice"
description: "Issuance of orders without prejudice"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-6255
---

An emergency protective order shall be issued without prejudice to any person.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.*
