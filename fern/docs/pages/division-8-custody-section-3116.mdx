# 3116

Investigator’s duty to assist court; scope of duty

Nothing in this chapter limits the duty of a court-appointed investigator to assist the appointing court in the transaction of the business of the court. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.81. Effective January 1, 1994.
