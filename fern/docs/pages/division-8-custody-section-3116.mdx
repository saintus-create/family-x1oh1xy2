---
title: "3116. Investigator’s duty to assist court; scope of duty"
description: "Investigator’s duty to assist court; scope of duty"
slug: division-8-custody-section-3116
---

Nothing in this chapter limits the duty of a court-appointed investigator to assist the appointing court in the transaction of the business of the court.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.81. Effective January 1, 1994.*
