# Custody of Children

## Section 3116

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Investigator’s duty to assist court; scope of duty Nothing in this chapter limits the duty of a court-appointed investigator to assist the appointing court in the. transaction of the business of the court.
