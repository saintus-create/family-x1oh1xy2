# § 6253 — Contents of orders

Contents of orders
An emergency protective order shall include all of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Astatement of the grounds asserted for the order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The date and time the order expires.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The address of the superior court for the district or
county in which the endangered person or child in danger of
being abducted resides.

EMERGENCY: PROTECTIVE ORDERS
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The following statements, which shall be printed in
English and Spanish:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “To the Protected Person:
This order will last only
until the date and time noted above.
If you wish to seek
continuing protection, you will have to apply for an order
from the court, at the address noted above.
You may seek
the advice of an attorney as to any matter connected with
your application for any future court orders.
The attorney
should be consulted promptly so that the attorney may assist
you in making your application.”
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “To the Restrained Person: This order will last until
the date and time noted above.
The protected party may,
however, obtain a more permanent restraining order from the
court.
You may seek the advice of an attorney as to any
matter connected with the application.
The attorney should
be consulted promptly so that the attorney may assist you in
responding to the application.”

statement, which shall be printed in English and Spanish:
