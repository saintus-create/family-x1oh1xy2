# 13552

Use of data identifying claimants owing past-due

Use of data identifying claimants owing past-due
child support; compliance with privacy protection laws
Section operative Jan. 1, 2020.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Data obtained pursuant to this article shall only be
used for the purpose of identifying claimants who are also
obligors who owe past-due child support. If the Department
of Child Support Services does not identify an obligor in the
data obtained pursuant to this article with a child support
obligor, the Department of Child Support Services shall not
maintain that data and shall immediately destroy that data.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An insurer that provides, attempts to provide, or in any
way accesses data pursuant to this article shall comply with all
applicable state and federal laws for the protection of the
privacy and the security of that data, including, but not
limited to, the Insurance Information and Privacy Protection
Act (Article 6.6 (commencing with Section 791) of Chapter 1
of Part 2 of ), the Information Practices Act of 1977
(Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 1798) of Title 1.8 of
Part 4 of of the Civil Code), and the federal Health
Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (Public
Law 104-191).!
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The Department of Child Support Services shall consider any information received from an insurer as confidential. That information shall be used or disclosed only for the
purpose of collecting past-due child support.
