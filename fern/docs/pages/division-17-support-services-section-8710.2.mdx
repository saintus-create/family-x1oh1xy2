# 8710.2. Exchange system; information access

*Support Services*

Exchange system; information access
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department shall ensure that information regarding families and children registered with the statewide
exchange system described in Section 8710.2 is accessible by
licensed adoption agency personnel throughout the: state.

Exchange system; establishment
,
Exchange System.

paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 8708, the department shall establish a statewide exchange system that interjurisdictionally matches waiting children and approved adoptive
