Commentary
Applying section 8339(j)<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  and subsection (h)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of this section,
Downing v. Office of Personnel Management, 619 F.3d 1374 (D.C. Cir.
2010), held that a former wife was not entitled to a former spouse
survivor annuity under the Civil Service Retirement System when
her former husband failed to re-elect former spouse survivor bene-
fits within two years of their divorce and the divorce decree did not
mention survivor benefits, even though the benefit her husband
received after divorce continued to be reduced to reflect his pre-
divorce election of a survivor benefit for his wife. This result would
not occur with an ERISA-regulated private sector pension. See
Carmona
v. Carmona, 544 F.3d 988 (9th Cir. 2008), discussed in
Commentary to 29 USC §§ 1055 and 1056.
