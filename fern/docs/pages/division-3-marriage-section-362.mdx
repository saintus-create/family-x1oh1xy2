# 362 — Exemption of existing causes of action

Premarital Agreements

Exemption of existing causes of action
This Title does not extend to actions already commenced,
nor to cases where the time prescribed in any existing statute
for acquiring a right or barring a remedy has fully run, but the
laws now in force are applicable to such actions and cases,
and are repealed subject’ to the provisions of this section.
(Enacted in 1872.)
