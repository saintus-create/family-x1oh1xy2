# 3465 — Proceedings begun before effective date of this act

Custody of Children

Proceedings begun before effective date of this act
A motion or other request for relief made in a child
custody proceeding or to enforce a child custody determination that was commenced before the effective date of this
part is governed by the law in effect at the time the motion or
other request was made.
(Added by Stats.1999, c. 867
(S.B.668), $ 3.)
Expenses incurred under § 3455 or 3456
The court may assess all direct expenses and costs incurred
by a district attorney under Section 3455 or 3456 pursuant to
the provisions of Section 3134. (Added by Stats.1999, c. 867
(S.B.668), § 3.)
