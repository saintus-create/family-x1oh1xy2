# Custody of Children

## Section 3465

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Proceedings begun before effective date of this act A motion or other request for relief made in a child custody proceeding or to enforce a child custody determination that was commenced before the effective date of this part is governed by the law in effect at the time the motion or other request was made.
