# § 3465 — Proceedings begun before effective date of this act

Proceedings begun before effective date of this act
A motion or other request for relief made in a child
custody proceeding or to enforce a child custody determina-
tion that was commenced before the effective date of this
part is governed by the law in effect at the time the motion or
other request was made.
