# § 3465 — Proceedings begun before effective date of this act

A motion or other request for relief made in a child custody proceeding or to enforce a child custody determination that was commenced before the effective date of this part is governed by the law in effect at the time the motion or other request was made. _(Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 3.
