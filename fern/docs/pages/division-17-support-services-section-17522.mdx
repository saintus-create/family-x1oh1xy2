# Support Services

## Section 17522

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Delinquent support obligors; collection or lien enforcement by levy 
(a) Notwithstanding any other law, if a support obligor is delinquent in the payment of support for at least 30 days and the local child support agency is enforcing the support obligation pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17400.">Section 17400</a>, the local child support agency may collect the delinquency or enforce a lien by levy served on all persons having in their possession, or who will have in their possession or under their control, credits or personal property belonging to the delinquent support obligor, or who owe any debt to the obligor at the time they receive the notice of levy. 
(b) A levy may be issued by a local child support agency for a support obligation that accrued under a court order or judgment if the obligor had notice of the accrued support arrearage as provided in this section, and did not make a timely request for review. 
(c) The notice requirement shall be satisfied by the local child support agency sending a statement of support arrearages to the obligor at the obligor’s last known address by firstclass mail, postage prepaid. 
The notice shall advise the obligor of the amount of the support arrearage. 
The notice shall advise the obligor that the obligor may have the arrearage determination reviewed by administrative proce714 dures and state how the review may be obtained.
