# § 1620.

*Part 5 — Marital Agreements · Chapter 3 — Agreements Between Spouses*

Except as otherwise provided by law, spouses cannot, by a contract with each other, alter their legal relations, except as to property. (Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 21. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.) FAM Family Code - FAM
