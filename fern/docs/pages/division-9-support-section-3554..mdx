# § 3554.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 2 — General Provisions*

An appeal may be taken from an order or judgment under this division as in other civil actions. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
