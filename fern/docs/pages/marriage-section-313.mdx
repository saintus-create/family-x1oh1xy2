# General Provisions

## Section 313

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Claims against local public entities The general procedure for the presentation of claims as a prerequisite to commencement of actions for money or OF damages against the State of California, counties, cities, cities and counties, districts, local authorities, and other political subdivisions of the State, and against the officers, employees, and servants thereof, is prescribed by <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=3.6.">Division 3.6</a> (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=810.">810</a>) of Title 1 of the Government Code.
