# § 695.060 — Activity

activity
to engage
in business,
profession
or
Except as provided in Section 708.630, a license issued by a
public entity to engage in any business, profession, or activity
is not subject to enforcement of a money judgment. (Added
by Stats.1982, c. 1364, p. 5104, § 2, operative July I, 1983.)
