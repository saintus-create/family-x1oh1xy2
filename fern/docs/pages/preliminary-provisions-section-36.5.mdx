# § 36.5 — Motion for preference; affidavit

Motion for preference; affidavit
An affidavit submitted in support of a motion for preference under subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 36 may be signed by the
attorney for the party seeking preference based upon information and belief as to the medical diagnosis and prognosis
of any party. The affidavit is not admissible for any purpose

COURTS

OF JUSTICE
other than a motion for preference under subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of
Section 36. (Added by Stats.1990, c. 1232 (A.B.3820), § 1.)
(A.B.3532), § 10, operative July 1, 1991;
(A.B.2641), § 12, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
