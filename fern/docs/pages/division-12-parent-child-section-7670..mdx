# § 7670.

*Part 3 — Uniform Parentage Act · Chapter 5 — Termination Of Parental Rights In Adoption Proceedings*

There shall be no filing fee charged for a petition filed pursuant to Section 7662. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
