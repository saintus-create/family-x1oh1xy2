# California Family Code § 299

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A registered domestic partnership may be terminated without filing a proceeding for dissolution of domestic partnership by the filing of a Notice of Termination of Domestic Partnership with the Secretary of State pursuant to this section, provided that all of the following conditions exist at the time of the filing:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The Notice of Termination of Domestic Partnership is signed by both registered domestic partners.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  There are no children of the relationship of the parties born before or after registration of the domestic partnership or adopted by the parties after registration of the domestic partnership, and neither of the registered domestic partners, to their knowledge, is pregnant.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The registered domestic partnership is not more than five years in duration.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Neither party has any interest in real property wherever situated, with the exception of the lease of a residence occupied by either party which satisfies the following requirements:

<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The lease does not include an option to purchase.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The lease terminates within one year from the date of filing of the Notice of Termination of Domestic Partnership.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  There are no unpaid obligations in excess of the amount described in paragraph <span class="subsec">(6)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 2400, as adjusted by subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 2400, incurred by either or both of the parties after registration of the domestic partnership, excluding the amount of any unpaid obligation with respect to an automobile.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The total fair market value of community property assets, excluding all encumbrances and automobiles, including any deferred compensation or retirement plan, is less than the amount described in paragraph <span class="subsec">(7)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 2400, as adjusted by subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 2400, and neither party has separate property assets, excluding all encumbrances and automobiles, in excess of that amount.

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The parties have executed an agreement setting forth the division of assets and the assumption of liabilities of the community property, and have executed any documents, title certificates, bills of sale, or other evidence of transfer necessary to effectuate the agreement.

<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  The parties waive any rights to support by the other domestic partner.

<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  The parties have read and understand a brochure prepared by the Secretary of State describing the requirements, nature, and effect of terminating a domestic partnership.

<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  Both parties desire that the domestic partnership be terminated.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The registered domestic partnership shall be terminated effective six months after the date of filing of the Notice of Termination of Domestic Partnership with the Secretary of State pursuant to this section, provided that neither party has, before that date, filed with the Secretary of State a notice of revocation of the termination of domestic partnership, in the form and content as shall be prescribed by the Secretary of State, and sent to the other party a copy of the notice of revocation by first-class mail, postage prepaid, at the other party’s last known address. The effect of termination of a domestic partnership pursuant to this section shall be the same as, and shall be treated for all purposes as, the entry of a judgment of dissolution of a domestic partnership.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The termination of a domestic partnership pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  does not prejudice nor bar the rights of either of the parties to institute an action in the superior court to set aside the termination for fraud, duress, mistake, or any other ground recognized at law or in equity. A court may set aside the termination of domestic partnership and declare the termination of the domestic partnership null and void upon proof that the parties did not meet the requirements of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  at the time of the filing of the Notice of Termination of Domestic Partnership with the Secretary of State.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The superior courts shall have jurisdiction over all proceedings relating to the dissolution of domestic partnerships, nullity of domestic partnerships, and legal separation of partners in a domestic partnership. The dissolution of a domestic partnership, nullity of a domestic partnership, and legal separation of partners in a domestic partnership shall follow the same procedures, and the partners shall possess the same rights, protections, and benefits, and be subject to the same responsibilities, obligations, and duties, as apply to the dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage, and legal separation of spouses in a marriage, respectively, except as provided in subdivision (a), and except that, in accordance with the consent acknowledged by domestic partners in the Declaration of Domestic Partnership form, proceedings for dissolution, nullity, or legal separation of a domestic partnership registered in this state may be filed in the superior courts of this state even if neither domestic partner is a resident of, or maintains a domicile in, the state at the time the proceedings are filed.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Parties to a registered domestic partnership who are also married to one another may petition the court to dissolve both their domestic partnership and their marriage in a single proceeding, in a form that shall be prescribed by the Judicial Council.
