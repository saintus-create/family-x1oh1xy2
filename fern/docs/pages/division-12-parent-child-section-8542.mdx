# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8542

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

“Prospective adoptive parent” means a person who’ has filed or intends to file a petition under Part 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8600.">§ 8600</a>) to adopt a child who has been or who is to be placed in the person’s physical care or a petition under Part 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=9300.">§ 9300</a>) to adopt an adult.
