# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16162

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Appointment The director, in consultation with a committee of interested individuals, shall appoint an ombudsperson qualified by training and experience to perform the duties of the office for a term of four years. 
The director may reappoint the ombudsperson for consecutive terms. 
The director shall select the committee members, the majority of whom shall be representatives of children’s advocacy organizations and current or former foster youth.
