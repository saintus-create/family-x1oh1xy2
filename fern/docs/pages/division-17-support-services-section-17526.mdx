# Support Services

## Section 17526

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of this section, 
“notice. 
of support delinquency” means a notice issued to a support obligor that includes a specific statement of the amount of delinquent support due and payable. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not require a state or local entity to calculate the amount of a support delinquency, except as otherwise required by law.
