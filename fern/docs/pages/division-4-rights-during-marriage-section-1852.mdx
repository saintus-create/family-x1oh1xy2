# 1852. Made to the state from private sources to be used for the

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

made to the state from private sources to be used for the
purposes of this part shall be deposited into the Family Law
Trust Fund.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Moneys deposited in the Family Law Trust Fund shall
be placed in an interest bearing account.
Any interest
earned shall accrue to the fund and shall be disbursed
pursuant to subdivision (d).
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Money deposited in the Family Law Trust Fund shall
be disbursed for purposes specified in this part and for other
family law related activities.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Moneys deposited in the Family Law Trust Fund shall
be administered by the Judicial Council.
The Judicial
Council may, with appropriate guidelines, delegate the administration of the fund to the Administrative Office of the
Courts.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Any moneys in the Family Law Trust Fund that are
unencumbered at the end of the fiscal year are automatically
appropriated to the Family Law Trust Fund of the following
year.

Wife
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  In order to defray the costs of collection of these funds,

pursuant to this section, the local registrar, county clerk, or
(2018), Family Conciliation Courts.
