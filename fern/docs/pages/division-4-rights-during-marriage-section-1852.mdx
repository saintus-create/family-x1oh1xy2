# 1852
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  There is in the State Treasury the Family Law Trust Fund.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Moneys collected by the state pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 103625 of the Health and Safety Code, Section 70674 of the Government Code, and grants, gifts, or devises made to the state from private sources to be used for the purposes of this part shall be deposited into the Family Law Trust Fund.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Moneys deposited in the Family Law Trust Fund shall be placed in an interest bearing account. Any interest earned shall accrue to the fund and shall be disbursed pursuant to subdivision (d).

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Money deposited in the Family Law Trust Fund shall be disbursed for purposes specified in this part and for other family law related activities.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Moneys deposited in the Family Law Trust Fund shall be administered by the Judicial Council. The Judicial Council may, with appropriate guidelines, delegate the administration of the fund to the Administrative Office of the Courts.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Any moneys in the Family Law Trust Fund that are unencumbered at the end of the fiscal year are automatically appropriated to the Family Law Trust Fund of the following year.

<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  In order to defray the costs of collection of these funds, pursuant to this section, the local registrar, county clerk, or county recorder may retain a percentage of the funds collected, not to exceed 10 percent of the fee payable to the state pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 103625 of the Health and Safety Code.
