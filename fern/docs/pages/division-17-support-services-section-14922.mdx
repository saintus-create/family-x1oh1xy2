<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Fees
The amount of fees assessed by accrediting entities for the
costs of accreditation shall be subject to approval by the
Secretary. Such fees may not exceed the costs of accreditation. In reviewing the level of such fees, the Secretary shall
consider the relative size of, the geographic location of, and
the number of Convention adoption cases managed by the
agencies or persons subject to accreditation or approval by
the accrediting entity.
