# § 2625.

*Part 6 — Debts And Liabilities*

Notwithstanding Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, all separate debts, including those debts incurred by a spouse during marriage and before the date of separation that were not incurred for the benefit of the community, shall be confirmed without offset to the spouse who incurred the debt. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
