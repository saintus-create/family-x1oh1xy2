# General Provisions

## Section 340.15

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Action for damages suffered as result of domestic violence 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In any civil action for recovery of damages suffered as a result of domestic violence, the time for commencement of the action shall be the later of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Within three years from the date of the last act of domestic violence by the defendant against the plaintiff. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Within three years from the date the plaintiff discovers or reasonably should have discovered that an injury or illness resulted from an act of domestic violence by the defendant against the plaintiff. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  As used in this section, “domestic violence” has the same meaning as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6211.">6211</a> of the Family Code.
