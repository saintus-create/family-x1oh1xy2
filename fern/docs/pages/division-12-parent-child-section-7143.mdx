# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7143

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Notification to Department of Motor Vehicles upon voiding or rescission of declaration of emancipation; invalidation of identification cards If a declaration of emancipation is voided or rescinded, notice shall be sent immediately to the Department of Motor Vehicles which shall remove the information relating to emancipation in its law enforcement computer network. Any identification card issued stating emancipation shall be invalidated.
