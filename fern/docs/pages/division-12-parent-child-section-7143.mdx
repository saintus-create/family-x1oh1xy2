# 7143

Notification to Department of Motor Vehicles upon

If a declaration of emancipation is voided or rescinded, notice shall be sent immediately to the Department of Motor Vehicles which shall remove the information relating to emancipation in its law enforcement computer network. Any identification card issued stating emancipation shall be invalidated. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
