# § 960 — Writing affecting property interest

writing affecting property interest
There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue concerning the intention of a client,
now deceased, with respect to a deed of conveyance, will, or
other writing, executed by the client, purporting to affect an
interest in property. (Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan. 1,
