# Support Services

## Section 18100.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Paragraph (2) of subdivision (b) provides that a nonprobate transfer to a former spouse does not fail on the transferor’s death if there is clear and convincing evidence that the transferor intended to preserve the nonprobate transfer. For example, if after divorcing, the transferor modified the beneficiary terms of a trust without changing the designation of the former spouse as primary beneficiary, this might be sufficiently clear and convincing evidence of the transferor’s intent to preserve the nonprobate transfer to the former spouse so as to prevent the operation of subdivision (a).
