# 18100.5

Paragraph (2) of subdivision (b) provides that a nonprobate

Paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  provides that a nonprobate
transfer to a former spouse does not fail on the transferor’s death if
there is clear and convincing evidence that the transferor intended to
preserve the nonprobate transfer. For example, if after divorcing, the
transferor modified the beneficiary terms of a trust without changing
the designation of the former spouse as primary beneficiary, this
might be sufficiently clear and convincing evidence of the transferor’s
intent to preserve the nonprobate transfer to the former spouse so as
to prevent the operation of subdivision (a).
