<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may make an award of attorney’s fees and
costs under Section 2030 or 2031 where the making of the
award, and the amount of the award, are just and reasonable
under the relative circumstances of the respective parties.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In determining what is just and reasonable under the
relative circumstances, the court shall take into consideration
the need for the award to enable each party, to the extent
