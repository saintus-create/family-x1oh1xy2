---
title: "5220. “Timely payment” means receipt of support payments by"
description: "“Timely payment” means receipt of support payments by"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-5220
---

“Timely payment” means receipt of support payments by
the obligee or assigned obligee within five days of the due
date.
