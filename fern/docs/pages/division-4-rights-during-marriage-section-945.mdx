# 945

Of the Government Code

of the Government Code.
To safeguard the minor or
incompetent from an inadvertent reliance on the tolling provision of
Section 352, notice of rejection of his claim in the form provided in
Government Code Section 913 is required to be given by the public
entity.
If notice is not given the claimant has two years from the
accrual of his cause of action in which to sue.
See Government Code
