To safeguard the minor or incompetent from an inadvertent reliance on the tolling provision of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=352.">§ 352</a>, notice of rejection of his claim in the form provided in Government Code <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=913.">§ 913</a> is required to be given by the public entity.
