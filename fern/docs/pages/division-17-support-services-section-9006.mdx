# § 9006 — Notification of probation officer, court investigator or

notification of probation officer, court investigator or
welfare department; consent refused by birth parent
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the petitioner moves to withdraw the adoption
petition or to dismiss the proceeding, the court clerk shall
immediately notify the probation officer, qualified court
investigator, or county welfare department of the action,
unless a home investigation was not required pursuant to
Section 9000.5.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The probation officer, qualified court investigator, or
county welfare department shall, before the hearing of the
motion or petition for withdrawal, file a full report with the
court and shall appear at the hearing to represent the
interests of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If a birth parent has refused to give the required
consent, the adoption petition shall be dismissed.
(Stats.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  At the hearing, the parties may appear in person or
with counsel. The hearing shall be held in chambers, but the
court reporter shall report the proceedings and, on court
order, the fee therefor shall be paid from the county treasury.
