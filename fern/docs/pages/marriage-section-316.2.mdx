# General Provisions

## Section 316.2

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Presumed or alleged fathers; inquiry by court; notice; termination of rights; jurisdiction 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  At the detention hearing, or as soon thereafter as practicable, the court shall inquire of the mother and any other appropriate person as to the identity and address of all presumed or alleged fathers. 
The presence at the hearing of a man claiming to be the father shall not relieve the court of its duty of inquiry. The inquiry shall include at least all of the following, as the court deems appropriate: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Whether a judgment of paternity already exists. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Whether the mother was married or believed she was married at the time of conception of the child or at any time thereafter. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Whether the mother was cohabiting with a man at the time of conception or birth of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Whether the mother has received support payments or promises of support with respect to the child or in connection with her pregnancy. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Whether any man has formally or informally acknowledged or declared his possible paternity of the child, including by signing a voluntary declaration of paternity. 
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Whether paternity tests have been administered and the results, if any. 
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Whether any man otherwise qualifies as a presumed father pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7611.">7611</a>, or any other provision, of the Family Code. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If, after the court inquiry, one or more men are identified as an alleged father, each alleged father shall be provided notice at his last and usual place of abode by certified mail return receipt requested alleging that he is or could be the father of the child. 
The notice shall state that the child is the subject of proceedings under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">300</a> and that the proceedings could result in the termination of parental rights and adoption of the child. 
Judicial Council form Paternity—-Waiver of Rights (JV—505) shall be included with the notice. 
Nothing in this section shall preclude a court from terminating a father’s parental rights even if an action has been filed under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7630.">7630</a> or 7631 of the Family Code. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The court may determine that the failure of an alleged father to return the certified mail receipt is not good cause to continue a hearing pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=355.">355</a>, 358, 360, 366.21, or 366.22. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If a man appears in the dependency action and files an action under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7630.">7630</a> or 7631 of the Family Code, the court shall determine if he is the father.
