# 9322 — Setting matter for hearing

Support Services

Setting matter for hearing
When the petition for approval of the adoption agreement
is filed, the court clerk shall set the matter for hearing.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
