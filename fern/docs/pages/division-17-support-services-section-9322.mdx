---
title: "9322. Setting matter for hearing"
description: "Setting matter for hearing"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-9322
---

When the petition for approval of the adoption agreement is filed, the court clerk shall set the matter for hearing.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
