# California Family Code § 7720

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  After notice and a hearing, the court may issue a protective order as defined in Section 6218 and any other restraining order as provided in Article 2 (commencing with Section 6340) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of .

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may not issue a mutual protective order pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  unless it meets the requirements of Section 6305.
