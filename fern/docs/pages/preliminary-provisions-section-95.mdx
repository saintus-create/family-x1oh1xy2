---
title: "95. “Income and expense declaration” means the form for an"
description: "“Income and expense declaration” means the form for an"
slug: preliminary-provisions-section-95
---

“Income and expense declaration” means the form for an income and expense declaration in family law matters adopted by the Judicial Council.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
