# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6361

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

If an order is included in a judgment pursuant to this article, the judgment shall state on its face both of the following: Statements on face of order included in judgment 
(a) Which provisions of the judgment are the orders. 
(b) The date of expiration of the orders, which shall be not more than five years from the date the judgment is issued, unless extended by the court after notice and a hearing.
