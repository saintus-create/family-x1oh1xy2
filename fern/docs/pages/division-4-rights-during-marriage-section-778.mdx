# 778 — (e) If parental rights are terminated and the court orders a

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  If parental rights are terminated and the court orders a
dependent child or ward to be placed for adoption, the
county adoption agency or the State Department of Social
Services shall take all of the following steps to facilitate
ongoing sibling contact, except in those cases provided in
subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  where the court determines by clear and
convincing evidence that sibling interaction is contrary to the
safety or well-being of the child:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Include in training provided to prospective adoptive
parents information about the importance of sibling relation-
ships to the adopted child and counseling on methods for
maintaining sibling relationships.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Provide prospective adoptive parents with information
about siblings of the child, except the address where the
siblings of the children reside. However, this address may be
disclosed by court order for good cause shown.
