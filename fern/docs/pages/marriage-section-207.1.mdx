# 207.1

Testing

implementation assistance; compliance exemptions; extensions; ‘special purpose juvenile halls; evaluation and
testing
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court, judge, referee, peace officer, or employee of a
detention facility shall not knowingly detain any minor in a
jail or lockup, except as provided in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  or (d).
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any minor who is alleged to have committed an
offense described in subdivision (b), paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision (d), or subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of Section 707 whose case is
transferred to a court of criminal jurisdiction pursuant to
Section 707.1 after a finding is made that * * * the minor is
not a fit and proper subject to be dealt with under the
juvenile court law, or any minor who has been charged
directly in or transferred to a court of criminal jurisdiction
pursuant to Section 707.01, maybe detained in a jail or other
secure facility for the confinement of adults if all. of the
following conditions are met:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The juvenile court or the court of criminal jurisdiction
makes a finding that the minor’s further detention in the
juvenile hall would endanger the safety of the public or would
be detrimental to the other minors in the juvenile hall.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Contact between the minor and adults in the facility is
restricted in accordance with Section 208.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The minor is adequately supervised.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A minor who is either found not to be a fit and proper
subject to be dealt with under the juvenile court law or who
will be transferred to a court of criminal jurisdiction pursuant
to Section 707.01, at the time of transfer to a court of criminal
jurisdiction or at the conclusion of the fitness hearing, as the
case may be, shall be entitled to be released on-bail or on
* * * the minor’s own recognizance upon the same circumstances, terms, and conditions as an adult who is alleged to
have committed the same offense.
(d)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A minor 14 years of age or older who is taken into
temporary custody by a peace officer on the basis-of being a
person described by Section 602, and who, in the reasonable
belief of the peace officer, presents a serious security risk of
harm to self or others, may be securely detained ina law
enforcement facility that contains a lockup for adults, if all of
the following conditions are met:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The minor is held in temporary custody for the
purpose of investigating the case, facilitating release of the
minor to a parent or guardian, or arranging transfer of the
minor to an appropriate juvenile facility.
