Proceeding between spouses
There is no privilege under this article in:
(a)  A proceeding brought by or on behalf of one spouse
against the other spouse.
(b)  A proceeding between a surviving spouse and a person
who claims through the deceased spouse, regardless of
whether such claim is by testate or intestate succession or by
inter vivos transaction.
