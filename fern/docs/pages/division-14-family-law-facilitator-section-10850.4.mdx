<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit the
authority of the court to seal records or restrict the dissemi-
nation of an autopsy report or evidence associated with the
examination of a victim under case law, other statutory law,
or the rules of court.
<span class="subsec">(m)</span>  The provisions of this section are severable.
If any
provision of this section or its application is held invalid, that
invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that
can be given effect without the invalid provision or applica-
tion.
