---
title: "3177. Confidentiality of proceedings"
description: "Confidentiality of proceedings"
slug: division-8-custody-section-3177
---

Mediation proceedings pursuant to this chapter shall be held in private and shall be confidential. All communications, verbal or written, from the parties to the mediator made in the proceeding are official information within the meaning of Section 1040 of the Evidence Code. Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.87. Effective January 1, 1994.
