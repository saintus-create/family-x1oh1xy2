# § 4004 — Disclosure of party receiving or intending to re-

In a proceeding where there is at issue the support of a child, the court shall require the parties to reveal whether a party is currently receiving, or intends to apply for, public assistance under the Family Economic Security Act of 1982 (Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11200) of Part 3 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code) for the maintenance of the child. _(Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 134. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
