# Support

## Section 4004

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support"><a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=9.">Division 9</a>: Support</a></div>

Disclosure of party receiving or intending to receive public assistance for maintenance of child In a proceeding where there is at issue the support of a child, the court shall require the parties to. reveal whether a party is currently receiving, or intends to apply for, public assistance under the Family Economic Security Act of 1982 
(Chapter 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11200.">§ 11200</a>) of Part 3 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=9.">Division 9</a> of the Welfare and Institutions Code) for the maintenance of the child.
