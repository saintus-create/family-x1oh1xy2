# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7648.8

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Termination of adoption; effect on obligations This article does not establish a basis for termination of any adoption, and does not affect any obligation of an adoptive parent to an adoptive child.
