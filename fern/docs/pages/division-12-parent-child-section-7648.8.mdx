# § 7648.8

*Termination of adoption; effect on obligations*

Termination of adoption; effect on obligations
ARTICLE 2.. DETERMINATION OF MOTHER
AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP
This article does not establish a basis for termination of any
adoption, and does not affect any obligation of an adoptive
parent to an adoptive child. (Added by Stats.2004, c. 849
(A.B.252), § 4.)

7650.
