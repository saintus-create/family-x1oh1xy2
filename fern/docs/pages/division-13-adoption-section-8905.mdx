# Adoption

## Section 8905

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Domestic and foreign adoption agencies; work with state agencies; written agreement required Licensed adoption agencies may work only with domestic and foreign adoption agencies with whom they have written agreements that specify the responsibilities of each. 
The agreements may not violate any statute or regulation of the United States or of this state.
