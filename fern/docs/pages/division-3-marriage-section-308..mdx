# § 308.

*Part 1 — Validity Of Marriage*

A marriage contracted outside this state that would be valid by laws of the jurisdiction in which the marriage was contracted is valid in California. (Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 474, Sec. 4. (AB 2882) Effective January 1, 2017.)
