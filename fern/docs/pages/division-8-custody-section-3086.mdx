# § 3086 — Orders of joint physical custody or joint legal

Orders of joint physical custody or joint legal
custody; designation of primary caretaker and primary
home of child
In making an order of joint physical custody or joint legal
custody, the court may specify one parent as the primary
caretaker of the child and one home as the primary home of
the child, for the purposes of determining eligibility for public
assistance.
