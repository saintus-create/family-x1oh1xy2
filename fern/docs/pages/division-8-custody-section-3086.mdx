# Custody of Children

## Section 3086

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Orders of joint physical custody or joint legal custody; designation of primary caretaker and primary home of child In making an order of joint physical custody or joint legal custody, the court may specify one parent as the primary caretaker of the child and one home as the primary home of the child, for the purposes of determining eligibility for public assistance.
