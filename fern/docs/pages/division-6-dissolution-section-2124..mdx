# § 2124.

*Part 1 — General Provisions · Chapter 10 — Relief From Judgment*

The negligence of an attorney shall not be imputed to a client to bar an order setting aside a judgment, unless the court finds that the client knew, or should have known, of the attorney’s negligence and unreasonably failed to self-protect. (Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 21. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)
