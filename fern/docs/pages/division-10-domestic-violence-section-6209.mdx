# 6209. "Protective order" defined

*Prevention of Domestic Violence*

<div class="case-callout">
<strong>Kane v. Irvine (1996)</strong>

*47 Cal.App.4th*

Two roommates were subleasing different rooms in the same house, had not met each other previously, and otherwise had no personal relationship with each other. Thus, the appellate court held they were not “cohabitants” within the meaning of the DVPA. For this conclusion, the appellate court looked to, among other things, the purpose of the DVPA.

<span class="case-statutes">Statutes: Family Code sections 6209, 6211, and 6220</span>
</div>

“Cohabitant” defined
“Cohabitant” means a person who regularly resides in the
household. “Former cohabitant” means a person who for-
merly regularly resided in the household.
