# § 6209 — "Protective order" defined

“Cohabitant” means a person who regularly resides in the household. “Former cohabitant” means a person who formerly regularly resided in the household. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
