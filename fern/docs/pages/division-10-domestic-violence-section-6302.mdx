# § 6302 — A notice of hearing under

A notice of hearing under
this part shall notify the
respondent that, if * * * the respondent does not attend the
hearing, the court may make orders against * * * the
respondent that could last up to five years.. (Added by
c. 572 (A.B.1596), § 14, operative Jan. 1, 2012.
Amended by Stats.2019, c. 115 (A.B.1817), § 61, eff: Jan. 1,
2020.)
