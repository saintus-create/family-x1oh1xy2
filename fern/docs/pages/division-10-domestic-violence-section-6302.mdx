M. v. Silvino M. (2015) 239 Cal.App.4th 11 Trial courts may issue mutual DVROs only if both parties have filed their own DVRO petitions, and only if the court makes detailed findings of fact as required by Family Code section 6305. One party’s conviction for a DV crime, by itself, does not excuse the trial court from making the required detailed findings of fact about both parties being a primary or dominant aggressor who was not acting in self-defense. Thus, the appellate court here reversed the DVRO against one party and sent the case back to the trial court. Statutes used or affected: Family Code sections 6302, 6305, and 6345
