# 5124. “Agent” means a person who has a present right, subject to

*Support*

“Agent” means a person who has a present right, subject to
request, to payment from an account as an attorney in fact
under a power of attorney. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14,
operative July 1, 1991.)
2 California Transactions Forms--Estate Planning § 10:34, Multiple
Party Accounts.
