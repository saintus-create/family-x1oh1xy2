# Support Services

## Section 17516

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Social service benefits use for support obligation In no event shall public social service benefits, as defined in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=10051.">Section 10051</a> of the Welfare and Institutions Code, or benefits paid pursuant to Title of the Social Security Act ' be employed to satisfy a support obligation.
