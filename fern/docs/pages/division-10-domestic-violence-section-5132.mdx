# 5132

Multiple-party account

Multiple-party account
A “multiple-party account” is any of the following types of
account:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A joint account.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A P.O.D. account.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A Totten trust account.
14, operative July 1, 1991.)
