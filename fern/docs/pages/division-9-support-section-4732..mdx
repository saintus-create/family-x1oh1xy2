# § 4732.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 5 — Civil Penalty For Child Support Delinquency*

The Judicial Council shall adopt forms or notices for the use of the procedures provided by this chapter. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
