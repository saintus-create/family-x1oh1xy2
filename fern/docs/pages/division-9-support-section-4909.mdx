Interpreting subsection (a)  of the prior section,
Stone v. Davis, 148 Cal.App.4th 596, 55 Cal.Rptr.3d 833 (2007), held
that a trial court improperly transferred continuing exclusive jurisdic-
tion of a child support order to another state when one party
continued to reside in California and the other parties had not filed
consent in writing to jurisdiction in another state.
Applying this section together with sections 5700.207 and 5700.211,
In re Marriage of Connolly, 20 Cal.App.5th 395, 228 Cal.Rptr.3d 890
(2018), review denied, held that a Utah judgment determining the
amount of spousal support and child support arrearage due under a
California support order was the not the controlling order for
purposes of the Uniform Interstate Family Support Act. Under
