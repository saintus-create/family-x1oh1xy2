# General Provisions

## Section 352

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Grounds for denial of license No marriage license shall be granted if either of the applicants lacks the capacity to enter into a valid marriage or is, at the time of making the application for the license, under 48 the influence of an intoxicating liquor or narcotic drug.
