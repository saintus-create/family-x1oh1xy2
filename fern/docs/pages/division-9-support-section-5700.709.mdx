# § 5700.709

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 7 — Support Proceeding Under Convention*

If a tribunal of this state does not recognize and enforce a Convention support order in its entirety, it shall enforce any severable part of the order. An application or direct request may seek recognition and partial enforcement of a Convention support order. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
