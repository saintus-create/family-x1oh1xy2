# § 5700.709

*enforcement*

enforcement
Partial enforcement
If a tribunal of this state does not recognize and enforce a
Convention support order in its entirety, it shall enforce any
severable part of the order. An application or direct request
may seek recognition and partial.enforcement of a Convention support order. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646),
§ 5, eff. Jan. 1, 2016.)
