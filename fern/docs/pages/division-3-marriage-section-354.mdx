# 354 — Identification; examination of applicants; addition-

Premarital Agreements

Identification; examination of applicants; addition-
al documentary proof; armed forces members serving
overseas in war or conflict; compliance by attorney-in-
fact
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Each applicant for a marriage license shall be required
to present authentic photo identification acceptable to the
county clerk as to name and date of birth. A credible witness
affidavit or affidavits may be used in lieu of authentic photo
identification.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For the purpose of ascertaining the facts mentioned or
required in this part, if the clerk deems it necessary, the clerk
may examine the applicants for a marriage license on oath at
the time of the application.
The clerk shall reduce the
examination
to writing and the applicants shall sign it.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If necessary, the clerk may request additional docu-
mentary proof as
to the accuracy
of the facts stated.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Applicants for a marriage license shall not be required
to state, for any purpose, their race or color.
<span class="subsec">(ce)</span>  If a marriage
is to be entered
into pursuant to
subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 420, the attorney in fact shall
comply with the requirements of this section on behalf of the
applicant who is overseas, if necessary.
