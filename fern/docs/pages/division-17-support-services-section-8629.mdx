# § 8629 — Contracts; revocation without penalty

Contracts; revocation without penalty
,
Contracts With Facilitator.
For a period of 72 hours after signing a contract or after
the payment of any fee, the birthparents or the prospective
adoptive parents may revoke the contract and request the
return of any fees paid, without penalty, except for any
reasonable fees actually earned by the facilitator and which
are supported by written records or documentation. (Added
