# Support

## Section 4070

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Financial hardship; income deductions If a parent is experiencing extreme financial hardship due to justifiable expenses resulting from the circumstances enumerated in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4071.">§ 4071</a>, on the request of a party, the court may allow the income deductions under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4059.">§ 4059</a> that may be necessary to accommodate those circumstances.
