# 4070

Financial hardship; income deductions

If a parent is experiencing extreme financial hardship due to justifiable expenses resulting from the circumstances enumerated in Section 4071, on the request of a party, the court may allow the income deductions under Section 4059 that may be necessary to accommodate those circumstances. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.
