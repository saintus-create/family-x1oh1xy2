# General Provisions

## Section 224.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Full faith and credit to tribal proceedings and records In an Indian child custody proceeding, the court shall give full faith and credit to the public acts, records; judicial proceedings, and judgments of any Indian tribe applicable to the proceeding to the same extent that such entities give full faith and credit to the public acts, records, judicial proceedings, and judgments of any other entity.
