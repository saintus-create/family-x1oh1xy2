In an Indian child custody proceeding, the court shall give
full faith and credit to the public acts, records; judicial
proceedings, and judgments of any Indian tribe applicable to
the proceeding to the same extent that such entities give full
faith and credit to the public acts, records, judicial proceedings, and judgments of any other entity. (Added by Stats.
