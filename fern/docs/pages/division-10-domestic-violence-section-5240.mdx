# § 5240

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon the filing and service of a motion and a notice of motion by the obligor, the court shall terminate the service of an assignment order if past due support has been paid in full, including any interest due, and if any of the following conditions exist:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  With regard to orders for spousal support, the death or remarriage of the spouse to whom support is owed.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  With regard to orders for child support, the death or emancipation of the child for whom support is owed.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The court determines that there is good cause, as defined in Section 5260, to terminate the assignment order. This subdivision does not apply if there has been more than one application for an assignment order.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The obligor meets the conditions of an alternative arrangement specified in paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 5260, and a wage assignment has not been previously terminated and subsequently initiated.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  There is no longer a current order for support.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The termination of the stay of an assignment order under Section 5261 was improper, but only if that termination was based upon the obligor’s failure to make timely support payments as described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 5261.

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The employer or agency designated to provide services under Title IV-D of the Social Security Act or the State Disbursement Unit is unable to deliver payment for a period of six months due to the failure of the obligee to notify that employer or agency or the State Disbursement Unit of a change in the obligee’s address.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In lieu of filing and serving a motion and a notice of motion pursuant to subdivision (a), an obligor may request ex parte relief, except ex parte relief shall not be available in the circumstances described in paragraphs <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(4)</span>  of subdivision (a).
