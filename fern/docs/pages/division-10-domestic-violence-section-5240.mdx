# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5240

Payment of past due support; termination of service of order of assignment; grounds; ex parte relief 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon the filing and service of a motion and a notice of motion by the obligor, the court shall terminate the service of an assignment order if past due support has been paid in full, including any interest due, and if any of the following conditions exist: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  With regard to orders for spousal support, the death or remarriage of the spouse to whom support is owed. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  With regard to orders for child support, the death or emancipation of the child for whom support is owed. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The court determines that there is good cause, as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5260.">§ 5260</a>, to terminate the assignment order. 
This subdivision does not apply if there has been more than one application for an assignment order. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The obligor meets the conditions of an alternative arrangement specified in paragraph (2) of subdivision (b) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5260.">§ 5260</a>, and a wage assignment has not been previously terminated and subsequently initiated.
