# Adoption

## Section 8731

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Adoption sought by foster parent; time for initiation; assessment or home study If the prospective adoptive parent of a child is a foster parent, the assessment or home study described in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8730.">Section 8730</a> shall not be initiated until the child to be adopted has resided in the home of the foster parent for at least six months.
