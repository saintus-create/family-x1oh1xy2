# Pilot Projects

## Section 20035

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Temporary child support orders; compliance with uniform guidelines; basis in economic evidence Orders for temporary support issued pursuant to this chapter shall comply with the statewide uniform guideline set forth in Article 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4050.">§ 4050</a>) of Chapter 2 of Part 2 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=9.">Division 9</a> and shall be based on the economic evidence supplied by the parties or otherwise available to the court. 
‘(Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
