# § 20035 — Temporary child support orders; compliance with

Temporary child support orders; compliance with
uniform guidelines; basis in economic evidence
Orders for temporary support issued pursuant to this
chapter shall comply with the statewide uniform guideline set
forth in Article 2 (commencing with Section 4050) of Chapter
2 of Part 2 of and shall be based on the economic
evidence supplied by the parties or otherwise available to the
court.
‘(Added by Stats.1993,
c. 219 (A.B.1500),
