The person

The person
notified may attend all hearings and may submit for filing an
original and eight copies of written information he or she
deems relevant to the court.
The court clerk shall provide
the current parties and attorneys of record with a copy of the
written information immediately upon receipt and complete,
file, and distribute a proof of service.
