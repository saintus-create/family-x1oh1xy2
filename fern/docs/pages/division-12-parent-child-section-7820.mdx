# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7820

declaration of
Indian children.
Application of chapter
A proceeding may be brought under this part for the
purpose of having a child under the age of 18 years declared
free from the custody and control of either or both parents if
the child comes within any of the descriptions set out in this
chapter. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10, operative Jan. 1,
1994.)
