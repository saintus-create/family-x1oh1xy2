# 985

Made admissible by the exception provided in this section: Cf

made admissible by the exception provided in this section: Cf.
People v. Pierce, 61 Cal.2d 879, 40 Cal.Rptr. 845, 395 P.2d 893 (1964)
(husband and wife who conspire only between themselves against
others cannot claim immunity from prosecution for conspiracy on the
basis of their marital status).
[7 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1
(1965)].
Criminal proceedings
There is no privilege under this article: in a criminal
proceeding in which one spouse is charged with:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A crime committed at any time against the person or
property of the other spouse or of a child of either.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A crime committed at any time against the person or
property of a third person committed in the course of
committing a crime against the person or property of the
other spouse.
