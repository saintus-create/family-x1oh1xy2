# § 7720.

*Part 3 — Uniform Parentage Act · Chapter 6 — Protective And Restraining Orders*

(a) After notice and a hearing, the court may issue a protective order as defined in Section 6218 and any other restraining order as provided in Article 2 (commencing with Section 6340) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of Division 10.

(b) The court may not issue a mutual protective order pursuant to subdivision

(a) unless it meets the requirements of Section 6305. (Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 246, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.) ARTICLE 4. Orders Included in Judgment [7730- 7730.] ( Article 4 repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 179.6. )
