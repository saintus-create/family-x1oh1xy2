Electronic filing and service; conditions
(a)  Unless otherwise provided by law, a document in a
juvenile court matter may be filed and served electronically,
as prescribed by Section 1010.6 of the Code of Civil
Procedure, under the following conditions:
(1)  Electronic service is authorized only if the county and
the court permit electronic service.
(2)(A)  On or before December 31, 2018, electronic service
on a party or other person is permitted only if the party or
other person has consented to accept electronic service in
that specific action. A party or other person may subsequently withdraw its consent to electronic service.
(B)  On or after January 1, 2019, electronic service on a
party or other person is permitted only if the party or other
person has expressly consented, as provided in Section 1010.6
of the Code of Civil Procedure. A party or other person may
subsequently withdraw its consent to electronic service by
completing the appropriate Judicial Council form.
(3)  Consent, or the withdrawal of consent, to receive
electronic service may be completed by a party or other
person entitled to service, or that person’s attorney.
