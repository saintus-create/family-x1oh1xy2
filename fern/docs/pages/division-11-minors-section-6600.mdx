# § 6600

A minor is civilly liable for a wrong done by the minor, but is not liable in exemplary damages unless at the time of the act the minor was capable of knowing that the act was wrongful. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
