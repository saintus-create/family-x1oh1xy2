1 , Parent’s
Agreement to Arbitrate.
Approval of contract for attorney’s fees for minor; fees
in absence of contract.
Minors;
civil liability
_ A minor is civilly liable for a wrong done by the minor, but
is not liable in exemplary damages unless at the time of the
act the minor was capable of knowing that the act was
wrongful. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan.
I, 1994.)
