# 7002 — Emancipated minor; description

Minors

Emancipated minor; description
A person under the age of 18 years is an emancipated
minor if any of the following conditions is satisfied:
5 , In General.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The person has entered into a valid marriage, or has
established a valid domestic partnership, regardless of whether the marriage or the domestic partnership has been
dissolved.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The person is on active duty with the Armed Forces of
the United States.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The person has received a declaration of emancipation
pursuant
to Section
7122.
