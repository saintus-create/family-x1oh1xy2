# § 8600.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

An unmarried minor may be adopted by an adult as provided in this part. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
