# 706.050

Maximum amount of disposable earnings of an

Maximum amount of disposable earnings of an
individual judgment debtor subject to levy.

706.050.
Maximum amount of disposable earnings of an
individual judgment debtor subject to levy
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the
maximum amount of disposable earnings of an individual
judgment debtor for any workweek that is subject to levy
under an earnings withholding order shall not exceed the
lesser of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Twenty-five percent of the individual’s disposable
earnings for that week.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Fifty percent of the amount by which the individual’s
disposable earnings for that week exceed 40 times the state
minimum hourly wage in effect at the time the earnings are
payable.
If a judgment debtor works in a location where the
local minimum hourly wage is greater than the state mini-
mum hourly wage, the local minimum hourly wage in effect at
the time the earnings are payable shall be used for the
calculation made pursuant to this paragraph.

OF CIVIL ACTIONS
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For any pay period other than weekly, the following
multipliers shall be used to determine the maximum amount
of disposable earnings subject to levy under an earnings
withholding order that is proportional in effect
to the
calculation described in paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision (a),
except as specified in paragraph (1):
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  For a daily pay period, the amounts shall be identical
to the amounts described in subdivision (a).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  For a biweekly pay period, multiply the applicable
hourly minimum wage by 80 work hours.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  For a semimonthly pay period, multiply the applicable
hourly minimum wage by 86 % work hours.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  For a monthly pay period, multiply the applicable
hourly minimum wage by 173 4 work hours.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section shall become operative on July 1, 2016.
