# § 2000.

*Part 1 — General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Application Of Part*

This part applies to a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, or for legal separation of the parties. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
