# California Family Code § 531

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An application for approval to authorize confidential marriages pursuant to this part shall be submitted to the county clerk in the county in which the notary public who is applying for the approval resides. The county clerk shall exercise reasonable discretion as to whether to approve applications.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The application shall include all of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The full name of the applicant.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The date of birth of the applicant.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The applicant’s current residential address and telephone number.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The address and telephone number of the place where the applicant will issue confidential marriage licenses.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The full name of the applicant’s employer if the applicant is employed by another person.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Whether or not the applicant has engaged in any of the acts specified in Section 8214.1 of the Government Code.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The application shall be accompanied by the fee provided for in Section 536.
