# § 1819 — Destruction of records, papers or documents in

Destruction of records, papers or documents in
office of counselor; exception; microfilming
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (b), upon order of
the judge of the family conciliation court, the supervising
counselor of conciliation may destroy any record, paper, or
document filed or kept in the office of the supervising
counselor of conciliation which is more than two years old.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Records described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of child custody
or visitation mediation may be destroyed when the minor or
minors involved are 18 years of age.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In the judge’s discretion, the judge of the family
conciliation court may order the microfilming of any record,
paper, or document described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  or (b).
