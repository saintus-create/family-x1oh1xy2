# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16163

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Personnel; priority The department shall hire the necessary personnel to perform the functions of the office. 
Priority shall be given to former foster youth in hiring decisions.
