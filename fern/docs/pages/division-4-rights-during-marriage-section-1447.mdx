# 1447

Definitions

II—SURVIVOR
BENEFIT PLAN
Definitions.
Application of Plan.
Election to discontinue participation: one-year opportunity after second anniversary of commencement of payment of retired pay.
Payment of annuity: beneficiaries.

In this subchapter:
1So in original.
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Plan.—The term “Plan” means the Survivor Ben-
efit Plan established by this subchapter.
