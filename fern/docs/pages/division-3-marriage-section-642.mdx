# Marriage

## Section 642

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Objections to reference or to referee Objections, if any, to a reference or to the referee or referees appointed by the court shall be made in writing, and must be heard and disposed of by the court, not by the referee.
