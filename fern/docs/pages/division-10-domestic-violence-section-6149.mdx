# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6149

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Written fee contract as confidential communication A written fee contract shall be deemed to be a confidential communication within the meaning of subdivision (e) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6068.">§ 6068</a> and of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=952.">§ 952</a> of the Evidence Code.
