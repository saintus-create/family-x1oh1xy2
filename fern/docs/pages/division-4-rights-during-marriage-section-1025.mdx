# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1025

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Exception: Proceeding to establish competence There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding brought by or on behalf of the patient to establish his competence.
