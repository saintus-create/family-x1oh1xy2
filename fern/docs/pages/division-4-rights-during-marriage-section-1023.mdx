# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1023

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Exception: Proceeding to determine sanity of criminal defendant There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding under Chapter 6 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1367.">Section 1367</a>) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Penal Code initiated at the request of the defendant in a criminal action to determine his sanity.
