# § 4613 — The court shall not issue an order pursuant to this article

The court shall not issue an order pursuant to this article
unless the court determines that one or more of the following
conditions exist:
Issuance of orders; conditions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The obligor-parent is not receiving salary or wages
subject to an assignment pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing
with Section 5200) and there is reason to believe that the
obligor-parent has earned income from some source of
employment.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An assignment of a portion of salary or wages pursuant
to Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 5200) would not be
sufficient to meet the amount of the support obligation, for
reasons other than a change of circumstances which would
qualify for a reduction in the amount of child support
ordered.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The job history of the obligor-parent shows that an
assignment of a portion of salary or wages pursuant to
Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 5200), would be diffi-
cult to enforce or would not be a practical means for securing
the payment of the support obligation, due to circumstances
including, but not limited to, multiple concurrent or consecu-
tive employers.
