# Support

## Section 4613

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

The court shall not issue an order pursuant to this article unless the court determines that one or more of the following conditions exist: Issuance of orders; conditions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The obligor-parent is not receiving salary or wages subject to an assignment pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5200.">§ 5200</a>) and there is reason to believe that the obligor-parent has earned income from some source of employment. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An assignment of a portion of salary or wages pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5200.">§ 5200</a>) would not be sufficient to meet the amount of the support obligation, for reasons other than a change of circumstances which would qualify for a reduction in the amount of child support ordered. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The job history of the obligor-parent shows that an assignment of a portion of salary or wages pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5200.">§ 5200</a>), would be difficult to enforce or would not be a practical means for securing the payment of the support obligation, due to circumstances including, but not limited to, multiple concurrent or consecutive employers.
