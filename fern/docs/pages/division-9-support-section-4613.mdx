Issuance of orders; conditions
The court shall not issue an order pursuant to this article
unless the court determines that one or more of the following
conditions exist:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The obligor-parent is not receiving salary or wages
subject to an assignment pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing
with Section 5200) and there is reason to believe that the
obligor-parent has earned income from some source of
employment.

qualify for a reduction in the amount of child support
ordered.
