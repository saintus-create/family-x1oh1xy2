# § 5696 — Fraud, undue influence, duress, mistake, or other

Fraud, undue influence, duress, mistake, or other
invalidating causes; petition by conservator or guardian
of transferor
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Nothing in this chapter limits the application of
principles of fraud, undue influence, duress, mistake, or other
invalidating cause to a transfer of property by a revocable
transfer on death deed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 5692, the
conservator or guardian of a transferor may, before the
transferor’s death, petition the court for invalidation of a
revocable transfer on death deed executed by the transferor.
