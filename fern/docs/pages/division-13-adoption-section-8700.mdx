# Adoption

## Section 8700

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Where the birth parent has relinquished the child for adoption to a licensed or authorized child-placing agency in another jurisdiction pursuant to the law of that jurisdiction.
