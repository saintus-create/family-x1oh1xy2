# § 1832.

*Part 1 — Family Conciliation Court Law · Chapter 3 — Proceedings For Conciliation*

The petition shall be captioned substantially as follows: In the Superior Court of the State of California in and for the County of ____ Upon the petition of ⎫ ⎪ Petition for (Petitioner) ⎪ Conciliation And concerning ⎬ (Under the Family and ⎪ Conciliation ⎪ Court Law) _____________ , Respondents ⎭ To the Family Conciliation Court: (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
