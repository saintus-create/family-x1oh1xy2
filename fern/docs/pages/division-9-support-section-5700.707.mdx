# 5700.707. Contest of registered Convention support order

*Support*

Contest of registered Convention support order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, Sections
5700.605 through 5700.608 apply to a contest of a registered
Convention support order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A party contesting a registered Convention support
order shall file a contest not later than 30 days after notice of
the registration, but if the contesting party does not reside in
the United States, the contest must be filed not later than 60

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the nonregistering party fails to contest the registered Convention support order by the time specified in
subsection (b), the order is enforceable.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A contest of a registered Convention support order
may be based only on grounds set forth in Section 5700.708.
The contesting party bears the burden of proof.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  In a contest of a registered Convention support order,

tribunal based its jurisdiction; and
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  may not review the merits of the order.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A tribunal of this state deciding a contest of a
registered Convention support order shall promptly notify the
parties of its decision.
