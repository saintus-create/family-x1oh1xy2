# § 1024 — _ Law Revision Commission Comments

_ Law Revision Commission Comments
[7 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1965)].
Exception:
1970 Addition
Section 1027 provides an exception to the psychotherapist-patient
privilege that is analogous to the exception provided by Section 1024
(patient dangerous to himself or others). The exception provided by
Patient dangerous to himself or others
There is no privilege under this article if the psychotherapist has reasonable cause to believe that the patient is in such
mental or emotional condition as to be dangerous to himself
or to the person or property of another and that disclosure of
the communication is necessary to prevent the threatened
danger.
