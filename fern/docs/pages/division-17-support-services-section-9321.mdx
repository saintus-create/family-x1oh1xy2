# § 9321 — Petition for approval of adoption agreement;

Petition for approval of adoption agreement;
fil-
ing; contents of petition
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The prospective adoptive parent and the proposed
adoptee may file in the county in which either person resides
a petition for approval of the adoption agreement.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition for approval of the adoption agreement
shall state all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The length and nature of the relationship between the
prospective adoptive parent and the proposed adoptee.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The degree of kinship, if any.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The reason the adoption is sought.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A statement as to why the adoption would be in the
best interest of the prospective adoptive parent, the proposed
adoptee, and the public.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The names and addresses of any living birth parents or
adult children of the proposed adoptee.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Whether the prospective adoptive parent or the pro-
spective adoptive parent’s spouse has previously adopted any
other adult and, if so, the name of the adult, together with the
date and place of the adoption.
