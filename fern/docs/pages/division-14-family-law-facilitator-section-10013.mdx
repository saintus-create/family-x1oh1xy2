Facilitator; attorney-client relationship; notice
The family law facilitator shall not represent any party. No
attorney-client relationship is created between a party and
the family law facilitator as a result of any information or
services provided to the party by the family law facilitator.
The family law facilitator shall give conspicuous notice that
no attorney-client relationship exists between the facilitator,
its staff, and the family law litigant. The notice shall include
the advice that the absence of an attorney-client relationship
means that communications between the party and the family
law facilitator are not privileged and that the family law
facilitator may provide services to the other party.
