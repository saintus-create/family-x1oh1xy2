# § 530.

*Part 4 — Confidential Marriage · Chapter 2 — Approval Of Notaries To Authorize Confidential Marriages*

(a) No notary public shall issue a confidential marriage license pursuant to this part unless the notary public is approved by the county clerk to issue confidential marriage licenses pursuant to this chapter.

(b) A violation of subdivision

(a) is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine not to exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000) or six months in jail.
