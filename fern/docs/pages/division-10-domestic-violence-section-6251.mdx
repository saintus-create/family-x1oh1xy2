# 6251. And Wife

*Prevention of Domestic Violence*

10th Husband
and Wife

Findings of court
An emergency protective order may be issued only if the
judicial officer finds both of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  That reasonable grounds have been asserted to believe
that an immediate and present danger
exists, that a child is in immediate and present danger of
abuse or abduction, or that an elder or dependent adult is in
immediate and present danger of abuse as defined in Section
15610.07 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  That an emergency protective order is necessary to
prevent the occurrence
