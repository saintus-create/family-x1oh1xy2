# 6929.1
*Part 4  Medical Treatment · Chapter 3  Consent By Minor*

Notwithstanding any other law, a minor 16 years of age or older may consent to opioid use disorder treatment that uses buprenorphine at a physician’s office, clinic, or health facility, by a licensed physician and surgeon or other health care provider acting within the scope of their practice, whether or not the minor also has the consent of their parent or guardian. (Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 456, Sec. 2. (AB 816) Effective January 1, 2024.)
