# § 4729 — Enforcement of child support obligations by local

The local child support agency or any other agency providing support enforcement services pursuant to Title IV-D of the federal Social Security Act may not enforce child support obligations utilizing the penalties provided for by this chapter. _(Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 50. Effective September 28, 2000.)_
