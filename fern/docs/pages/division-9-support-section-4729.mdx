# Support

## Section 4729

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Enforcement of child support obligations by local child support agency or other agency The local child support agency or any other agency providing support enforcement services pursuant to Title [V— 
D of the federal Social Security Act may not enforce child support obligations utilizing the penalties provided for by this chapter.
