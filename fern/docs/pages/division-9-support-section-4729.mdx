# 4729 — child support agency or other agency

Support

child support agency or other agency
The local child support agency or any other agency
providing support enforcement services pursuant to Title [V—
D of the federal Social Security Act may not enforce child
support obligations utilizing the penalties provided for by this
chapter. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. I,
1994. Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 148;
Stats.1994, c. 959 (A.B.3072), § 3, eff. Sept. 28, 1994; Stats.
2000, c. 808 (A.B.1358), § 50, eff: Sept. 28, 2000.)
California Practice Guide: Rutter Family Law Forms Form 6:19,
Request for Order to Determine Arrearages and to Show Cause
Why Family Code §4720 et seq. Penalties Should Not be
Imposed.
Civil Penalty for Child Support Delinquency.

10 ,
Avoiding or Reducing Penalty.
