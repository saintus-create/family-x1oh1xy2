## Domestic Partner Registration

  <h3>Definition — registered domestic partner</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 297.5</Badge></div>
        <p>"Registered domestic partner" means a person who has filed with the Secretary of State a Declaration of Domestic Partnership and who has not filed a notice of termination, except that nothing in this section applies to modify eligibility for long-term care plans pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&division=5.&title=2.&part=3.&chapter=15.&article=">Chapter 15 (commencing with Section 21660) of Part 3 of Division 5 of Title 2 of the Government Code</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Added Stats. 1999, Ch. 588, Sec. 2</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2000</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Registration</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 298</Badge></div>
        <p>A domestic partnership may be registered by filing a Declaration of Domestic Partnership with the Secretary of State, accompanied by the required fee. The State Department of Public Health shall make available a brochure for training workshops on domestic violence, conducted pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=13823.15.">Section 13823.15 of the Penal Code</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1999, Ch. 588, Sec. 3</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2000</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Confidentiality and identification</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 298.5</Badge></div>
        <p>A Certificate of Registered Domestic Partnership, and a copy of the brochure made available to county clerks and the Secretary of State by the State Department of Public Health pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=HSC&sectionNum=358.">Section 358 of the Health and Safety Code</a>, shall be distributed to individuals receiving a confidential marriage license pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=503.">Section 503</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Added Stats. 2001, Ch. 577, Sec. 2</Badge>

  <h3>Name change</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 298.6</Badge></div>
        <p>A certified copy of a Declaration of Domestic Partnership containing the new name shall constitute proof that the use of the new name is lawful for purposes of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=12800.7.">Section 12800.7 of the Vehicle Code</a>. This section shall be applied in a manner consistent with the requirements of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=1653.5.">Sections 1653.5</a> and <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=12801.">12801 of the Vehicle Code</a>, or to petition the superior court for a change of name pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&division=3.&title=&part=3.&chapter=&article=">Title 8 (commencing with Section 1275) of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Added Stats. 2003, Ch. 234, Sec. 1</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2004</Badge>
        </div>

    </div>
  
  <h3>Termination</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 299</Badge></div>
        <p>There are no unpaid obligations in excess of the amount described in subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2400.">Section 2400</a>, as adjusted by subdivision (b) of Section 2400. The total fair market value of community property assets is less than the amount described in paragraph (7) of subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2400.">Section 2400</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
