# Support

## Section 3613

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Orders or judgments with respect to adults who have capacity to consent Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a court may not make an order or give a judgment pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3600.">§ 3600</a>, 3601, 3602, 3610, or 3611 with respect to an adult who has the capacity within the meaning of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=812.">§ 812</a> to consent to the order and who has no conservator of the estate with authority to make that decision, without the express consent of that person.
