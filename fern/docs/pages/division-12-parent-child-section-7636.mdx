# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7636

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Effect of judgment or order; exceptions The judgment or order of the court determining the existence or nonexistence of the parent and child relationship is determinative for all purposes except for actions brought pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=270.">Section 270</a> of the Penal Code.
