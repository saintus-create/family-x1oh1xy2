# § 902 — “Debt” means an obligation incurred by a married person

“Debt” means an obligation incurred by a married person before or during marriage, whether based on contract, tort, or otherwise. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
