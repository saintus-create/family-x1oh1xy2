# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.612

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Recognition of order modified in another state If a child-support order issued by a tribunal of this state is modified by a tribunal of another state which assumed jurisdiction pursuant to the Uniform Interstate Family Support Act, a tribunal of this state: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  may enforce its order that was modified only as to arrears and interest accruing before the modification; 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  may provide appropriate relief for violations of its order which occurred before the effective date of the modification; and 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  shall recognize the modifying order of the other state, upon registration, for the purpose of enforcement.
