# California Family Code § 5700.207

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If a proceeding is brought under this part and only one tribunal has issued a child-support order, the order of that tribunal controls and must be recognized.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If a proceeding is brought under this part, and two or more child-support orders have been issued by tribunals of this state, another state, or a foreign country with regard to the same obligor and same child, a tribunal of this state having personal jurisdiction over both the obligor and individual obligee shall apply the following rules and by order shall determine which order controls and must be recognized:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If only one of the tribunals would have continuing, exclusive jurisdiction under this part, the order of that tribunal controls.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If more than one of the tribunals would have continuing, exclusive jurisdiction under this part:

<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  an order issued by a tribunal in the current home state of the child controls; or

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  if an order has not been issued in the current home state of the child, the order most recently issued controls.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  If none of the tribunals would have continuing, exclusive jurisdiction under this part, the tribunal of this state shall issue a child-support order, which controls.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If two or more child-support orders have been issued for the same obligor and same child, upon request of a party who is an individual or that is a support enforcement agency, a tribunal of this state having personal jurisdiction over both the obligor and the obligee who is an individual shall determine which order controls under subsection (b). The request may be filed with a registration for enforcement or registration for modification pursuant to Chapter 6, or may be filed as a separate proceeding.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A request to determine which is the controlling order must be accompanied by a copy of every child-support order in effect and the applicable record of payments. The requesting party shall give notice of the request to each party whose rights may be affected by the determination.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The tribunal that issued the controlling order under subsection (a), (b), or <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  has continuing jurisdiction to the extent provided in Section 5700.205 or 5700.206.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A tribunal of this state that determines by order which is the controlling order under subsection (b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  or (c), or that issues a new controlling order under subsection(b)(3), shall state in that order:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the basis upon which the tribunal made its determination;

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the amount of prospective support, if any; and

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  the total amount of consolidated arrears and accrued interest, if any, under all of the orders after all payments made are credited as provided by Section 5700.209.

<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Within 30 days after issuance of an order determining which is the controlling order, the party obtaining the order shall file a certified copy of it in each tribunal that issued or registered an earlier order of child support. A party or support enforcement agency obtaining the order that fails to file a certified copy is subject to appropriate sanctions by a tribunal in which the issue of failure to file arises. The failure to file does not affect the validity or enforceability of the controlling order.

<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  An order that has been determined to be the controlling order, or a judgment for consolidated arrears of support and interest, if any, made pursuant to this section must be recognized in proceedings under this part.
