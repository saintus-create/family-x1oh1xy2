---
title: "508. Application to obtain certified copy of confidential"
description: "Application to obtain certified copy of confidential"
slug: division-3-marriage-section-508
---

Upon issuance of a confidential marriage license, parties shall be provided with an application to obtain a certified copy of the confidential marriage certificate from the county clerk.

> *Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 816, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative January 1, 2008, by Sec. 56 of Ch. 816.*
