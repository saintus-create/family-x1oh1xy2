# Marriage

## Section 508

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Application to obtain certified copy of confidential marriage license Upon issuance of a confidential marriage license, parties shall be provided with an application to obtain a certified copy of the confidential marriage certificate from the county clerk.
