# § 508

*marriage license*

marriage license
Upon issuance of a confidential marriage license, parties
shall be provided with an application to obtain a certified
copy of the confidential marriage certificate from the county
clerk. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The county clerk may charge a fee to cover the actual
costs of issuing a duplicate marriage license.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Ifa marriage license is lost, damaged, or destroyed
before a marriage ceremony takes place, the applicants shall
purchase a new marriage license and the old license shall be
voided. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
Amended by Stats.2001, c. 39 (A.B.1323), § 7; Stats.
c. 816 (A.B.1102), § 28, operative Jan. 1, 2008.)
1994. Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219. (A.B.1500),. § 99;
Stats.2001, c. 39 (A.B.1323), § 9; Stats.2006, c. 816 (A.B.
1102), § 30, operative Jan. 1, 2008.)
