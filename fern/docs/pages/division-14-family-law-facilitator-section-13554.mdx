# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 13554

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

“Central reporting organization” defined Section operative Jan. 1, 2020. 
For the purposes of this article, “central reporting organization” means a third-party service that automates the claims identifying process or provides interactive lookups.
