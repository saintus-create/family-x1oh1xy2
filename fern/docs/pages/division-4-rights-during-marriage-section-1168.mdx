# 1168

Regulations

Regulations
The Secretary may prescribe regulations to carry out the
provisions of this part.
(Pub.L. 93-406, Title I, 608, as
added Pub.L. 99-272, Title X, 10002(a), Apr. 7, 1986, 100
Stat. 231.)
