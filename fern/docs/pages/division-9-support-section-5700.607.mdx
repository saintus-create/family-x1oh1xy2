# 5700.607 — (a)(1) “personal jurisdiction,’ ” Wéillmer

Support

(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “personal jurisdiction,’ ” Wéillmer
v.Willmer, 144 Cal.App.4th 951, 51 Cal.Rptr.3d 10 (2006), held that a
German child or spousal support judgment is enforceable in California even though Germany based its child and spousal support
jurisdiction on grounds that California does not have, so long as
those grounds did not violate fundamental American principles of
due process.
