# § 5122

*Account*

Account
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Account” means
a contract of deposit of funds
between a depositor and a financial institution, and includes a
1148

NONPROBATE TRANSFERS
checking account, savings account, certificate of deposit,
share account, and other like arrangement.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Account” does not include:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  An account established for deposit of funds of a
partnership, joint venture, or other association for business
purposes.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An account controlled by one or more persons as the
duly authorized agent or trustee for a corporation, unincor-
porated association,
or charitable
or civic organization.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A regular fiduciary or trust account where the relation-
ship is established other than by deposit agreement.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  An account established for the deposit of funds of the
estate of a ward, conservatee, or decedent.
