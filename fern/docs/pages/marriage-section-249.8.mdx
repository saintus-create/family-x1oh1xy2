# § 249.8 — Hearing and order

hearing and order
Joint tenants; rights by survivorship
survivorship
Notwithstanding Section 249.6, any interested person may
file a petition in the manner prescribed in Section 248 or
17200 requesting a distribution of property of the decedent or
death benefits payable by reason of decedent’s death that are
subject to the delayed distribution provisions of Section 249.6.
incidents.
(Stats.1990,
c. 79 (A.B.759),
§ 14,
operative July 1, 1991.)
