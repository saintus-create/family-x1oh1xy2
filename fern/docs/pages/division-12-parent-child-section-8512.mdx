title: "8512. Marriage that is the subject of the proceeding, with respect to"

slug: division-12-parent-child-section-8512

“Birth parent” means the biological parent or, in the case of a person previously adopted, the adoptive parent.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
