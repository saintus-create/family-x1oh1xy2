# § 8512

*“Birth parent” defined*

“Birth parent” defined
“Birth parent” means the biological parent or, in the case
of a person previously adopted, the adoptive parent. (Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
