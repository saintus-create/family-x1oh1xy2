# § 8512 — Marriage that is the subject of the proceeding, with respect to

“Birth parent” means the biological parent or, in the case of a person previously adopted, the adoptive parent. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
