# 6241 — (b) “Law enforcement officer” means one of the following

Prevention of Domestic Violence

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Law enforcement officer” means one of the following
officers who requests or enforces an emergency protective
order under this part:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A police officer.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A sheriff's officer.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A peace officer of the Department of the California
Highway Patrol.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A peace officer of the University of California Police
Department.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  A peace officer of the California State University and
College Police Departments.
