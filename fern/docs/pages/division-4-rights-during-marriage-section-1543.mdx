*Suitability of proposed guardian for guardianship;*

Suitability of proposed guardian for guardianship;
report; confidentiality
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the petition as filed or as amended states that an
adoption petition has been filed, a report with respect to the
suitability of the proposed guardian for guardianship shall be
filed with the court by the agency investigating the adoption.

supervisors to provide public social services shall file a report
with the court with respect to the proposed guardian of the
