(a)  Every law enforcement agency in this state shall
develop, adopt, and implement written policies and standards
for officers’ responses to domestic violence calls by January 1,
1986. These policies shall reflect that domestic violence is
alleged criminal conduct. Further, they shall reflect existing
policy that a request for assistance in a situation involving
domestic violence is the same as any other request for
assistance where violence has occurred.
