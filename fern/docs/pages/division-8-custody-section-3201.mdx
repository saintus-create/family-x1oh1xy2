# § 3201

Any supervised visitation maintained or imposed by the court shall be administered in accordance with Standard 5.20 of the California Standards of Judicial Administration recommended by the Judicial Council. _(Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 213, Sec. 2. (AB 1579) Effective January 1, 2022.)_
