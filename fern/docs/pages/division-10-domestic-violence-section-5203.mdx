# 5203

Words used to create accounts; effect of different

Words used to create accounts; effect of different
wording
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Words in substantially the following form in a signature
card, passbook, contract, or instrument evidencing an ac-
count, or words to the same effect, executed before, on, or
after July 1, 1990, create the following accounts:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Joint account: “This account or certificate is owned by
the named
parties.
Upon the death of any of them,
ownership passes to the survivor(s).”
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  P.O.D. account with single party:
“This account or
certificate is owned by the named party. Upon the death of
that party, ownership passes to the named pay-on-death
payee(s).”
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  P.O.D. account with multiple parties: “This account or
certificate is owned by the named parties. Upon the death of
any of them, ownership passes to the survivor(s).
Upon the
death of all of them, ownership passes to the named pay-on-
death payee(s).”
PROBATE CODE
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Joint account of spouses with right of survivorship:
“This account or certificate is owned by the named parties,
who are spouses, and is presumed to be their community
property.
Upon the death of either of them, ownership
passes to the survivor.”
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Community property account of spouses:
“This ac-
count or certificate is the community property of the named
parties who are spouses.
The ownership during lifetime and
after the death of a spouse
is determined by the law
applicable to community property generally and may be
affected by a will.”
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Tenancy in common account: “This account or certifi-
cate is owned by the named parties as tenants in common.
Upon the death of any party, the ownership interest of that
party passes to the named pay-on-death payee(s) of that party
or, if none, to the estate of that party.”
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Use of the form language provided in this section is
not necessary to create an account that is governed by this
part. Ifthe contract of deposit creates substantially the same
relationship between the parties as an account created using
the form language provided in this section, this part applies to
the same extent as if the form language had been used.
