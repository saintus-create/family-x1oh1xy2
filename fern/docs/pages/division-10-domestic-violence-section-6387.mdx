# § 6387 — Husband

Husband
and
Wife

Copies of order to be provided to petitioner
The court shall order the clerk of the court to provide to a
petitioner, without cost, up to three certified, stamped, and
endorsed copies of any order issued under this part, and of an
extension, modification, or termination of the order.
(Added
by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 154. Amended by Stats.
2001, c. 176 (S.B.210), § 5; Stats.2010, c. 572 (A.B.1596),
§ 22, operative Jan. 1, 2012.)
