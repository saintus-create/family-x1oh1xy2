# § 3917 — Liability to third persons

Liability to third persons
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A claim based on <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  a contract entered into by a
custodian acting in a custodial capacity, <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  an obligation
arising from the ownership or control of custodial property,
or <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  a tort committed during the custodianship, may be
asserted against the custodial property by proceeding against
the custodian in the custodial capacity, whether or not the
custodian or the minor is personally liable therefor.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A custodian is not personally liable for either of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  On a contract properly entered into in the custodial
capacity unless the custodian fails to reveal that capacity and
to identify the custodianship in the contract.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  For an obligation arising from control of custodial
property or for a tort committed during the custodianship
unless the custodian is personally at fault.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A minor is not personally liable for an obligation
arising from ownership of custodial property or for a tort
committed during the custodianship unless the minor is
personally at fault.
(Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14,
operative July 1, 1991.)
