# General Provisions

## Section 361.49

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Date of foster care entry Regardless of his or her age, a child shall be deemed to have entered foster care on the earlier of the date of the jurisdictional hearing held pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=356.">§ 356</a> or the date that is 60 days after the date on which the child was initially removed from the physical custody of his or her parent or guardian.

<div class="source-note">

**Source:** California Family Code Annotated (Grace Ganz Blumberg, 2020 Desktop Edition). [View on California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=361.49.).
