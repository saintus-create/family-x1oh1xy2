# § 6450.

*Part 6 — Uniform Recognition And Enforcement Of Canadian Domestic Violence Protection Orders Act*

This part may be cited as the Uniform Recognition and Enforcement of Canadian Domestic Violence Protection Orders Act. (Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 98, Sec. 1. (SB 204) Effective January 1, 2018.)
