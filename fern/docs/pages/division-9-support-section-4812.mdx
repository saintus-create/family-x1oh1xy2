See also Section 4013 (child support obligation discharged in
bankruptcy).
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
Commentary
In re Marriage of Lynn, 101 Cal.App.4th 120, 123 Cal.Rptr.2d 611
(2002), holds that this section applies only to a bankruptcy discharge
of a spouse’s obligation under a property settlement agreement. If the
2v5

obligation was instead established by court adjudication, this section
is inapplicable and any modification of spousal support must be made
according to 4320, in which case the discharge in bankruptcy of a
spouse’s obligation is merely one of many factors.
