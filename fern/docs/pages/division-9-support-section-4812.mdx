# Support

## Section 4812

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

See also <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4013.">§ 4013</a> (child support obligation discharged in bankruptcy). 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)]. 
Commentary In re Marriage of Lynn, 101 Cal.App.4th 120, 123 Cal.Rptr.2d 611 
(2002), holds that this section applies only to a bankruptcy discharge of a spouse’s obligation under a property settlement agreement. If the 2v5 
§ 3600 obligation was instead established by court adjudication, this section is inapplicable and any modification of spousal support must be made according to § 4320, in which case the discharge in bankruptcy of a spouse’s obligation is merely one of many factors.
