# § 5700.401

*Issuance of support orders;*

Issuance of support orders;
support orders
<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  A voluntary acknowledgment of paternity, certified as a
true copy, is admissible to establish parentage of the child.
(Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
