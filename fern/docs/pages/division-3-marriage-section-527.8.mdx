# 527.8 — (C) A protective order pursuant to Division 10 (commenc-

Premarital Agreements

<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A protective order pursuant to (commenc-
ing with Section 6200) of the Family Code.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A protective order pursuant to Sections 7710 and 7720
of the Family Code.
The court may, either upon motion or in its own discretion,
and after considering reasonable objections by the minor to
the appointment of specific individuals, appoint a guardian ad
litem to assist the minor in obtaining or opposing the order,
provided that the appointment of the guardian ad litem does
not delay the issuance or denial of the order being sought.
In
making the determination concerning the appointment of a
particular guardian ad litem, the court shall consider whether
the minor and the guardian have divergent interests.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  For purposes of this subdivision only, upon the issu-
ance of an order pursuant to paragraph (1), if the minor
initially appeared
in court seeking an order without
a
guardian or guardian ad litem, and if the minor is residing
with a parent or guardian, the court shall send a copy of the
order to at least one parent or guardian designated by the
minor, unless, in the discretion of the court, notification of a
parent or guardian would be contrary to the best interest of
the minor.
The court is not required to send the order to
more than one parent or guardian.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The Judicial Council shall adopt forms by July 1, 1999,
to facilitate the appointment of a guardian ad litem pursuant
to this subdivision.
(c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision (a), a minor may ap-
pear in court without a guardian ad litem in the following
proceedings if the minor is a parent of the child who is the
subject of the proceedings:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Family court proceedings pursuant to (com-
mencing with Section 7600) of of the Family
Code.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Dependency proceedings pursuant to Chapter 2 (com-
mencing with Section 200) of Part 1 of of the
Welfare and Institutions Code.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Guardianship proceedings for a minor child pursuant
to (commencing with Section 1500) of of the
Probate Code.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  Any other proceedings concerning child custody, visi-
tation, or support.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the court finds that the minor parent is unable to
understand the nature of the proceedings or to assist counsel
in preparing the case, the court shall, upon its own motion or
upon a motion by the minor parent or the minor parent’s
counsel, appoint a guardian ad litem.
(Enacted in 1872.
