# § 146 — Agreement; requirements

Agreement; requirements
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  As used in this section, “agreement” means a written
agreement signed by each spouse or prospective spouse
altering, amending, or revoking a waiver under this chapter.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in subdivisions <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of
Section 147, unless the waiver specifically otherwise provides,
a waiver under this chapter may not be altered, amended, or
revoked except by a subsequent written agreement signed by
each spouse or prospective spouse.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Subject to subdivision (d), the agreement is enforce-
able only if it satisfies the requirements of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  and
is enforceable under either subdivision (€) or subdivision (f).
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Enforcement of the agreement against a party to the
agreement is subject to the same defenses as enforcement of
any other contract, except that:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Lack of consideration is not a defense to enforcement
of the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A minor intending to marry may enter into the
agreement as if married, but the agreement becomes effective
only upon the marriage.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Subject to subdivision (d), an agreement is enforceable
under this subdivision unless the party to the agreement
against whom enforcement is sought proves either of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A fair and reasonable disclosure of the property or
financial obligations of the other spouse was not provided to
the spouse against whom enforcement is sought prior to the
signing of the agreement unless the spouse against whom
enforcement is sought waived such a fair and reasonable
disclosure after advice by independent legal counsel.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The spouse against whom enforcement is sought was
not represented by independent legal counsel at the time of
signing of the agreement.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Subject to subdivisions <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  and (g), an agreement is
enforceable under this subdivision if the court determines
PROBATE CODE
that the agreement at the time of signing made a fair and
reasonable disposition of the rights of the spouses.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  If, after considering all relevant facts and circum-
stances, the court finds that enforcement of the agreement
pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(f)</span>  would be unconscionable under
the circumstances existing at the time enforcement is sought,
the court may refuse to enforce the agreement, enforce the
remainder of the agreement without the unconscionable
provisions, or limit the application of the unconscionable
provisions to avoid an unconscionable result.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 721 of the Family Code does
not apply if the agreement is enforceable under this section.
