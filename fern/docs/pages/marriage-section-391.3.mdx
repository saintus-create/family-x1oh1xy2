# General Provisions

## Section 391.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

The motion for an order requiring the plaintiff to furnish security shall be based upon the ground, and supported by a showing, that the plaintiff is a vexatious litigant and that there is not a reasonable probability that he or she will prevail in the litigation against the moving defendant.
