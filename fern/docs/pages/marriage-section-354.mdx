# General Provisions

## Section 354

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Identification; examination of applicants; additional documentary proof; armed forces members serving overseas in war or conflict; compliance by attorney-infact 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Each applicant for a marriage license shall be required to present authentic photo identification acceptable to the county clerk as to name and date of birth. A credible witness affidavit or affidavits may be used in lieu of authentic photo identification. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For the purpose of ascertaining the facts mentioned or required in this part, if the clerk deems it necessary, the clerk may examine the applicants for a marriage license on oath at the time of the application. 
The clerk shall reduce the examination to writing and the applicants shall sign it. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If necessary, the clerk may request additional documentary proof as to the accuracy of the facts stated. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Applicants for a marriage license shall not be required to state, for any purpose, their race or color. 
(ce) If a marriage is to be entered into pursuant to subdivision (b) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=420.">§ 420</a>, the attorney in fact shall comply with the requirements of this section on behalf of the applicant who is overseas, if necessary.
