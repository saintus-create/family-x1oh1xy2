# 311

Privilege against self-incrimination and right to confrontation by, and cross-examination

privilege against self-incrimination and right to confrontation by, and cross-examination
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the probation officer determines that the minor shall
be retained in custody, he or she shall immediately file a
petition pursuant to Section 332 with the clerk of the juvenile
court who shall set the matter for hearing on the detention
hearing calendar.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In the hearing, the child, parents, or guardians have a
privilege against self-incrimination and have a right to
confrontation by, and cross-examination of, any person
examined by the court as provided in Section 319. (Added by
c. 1068, p. 4760, 7. Amended by Stats.1982, c.
