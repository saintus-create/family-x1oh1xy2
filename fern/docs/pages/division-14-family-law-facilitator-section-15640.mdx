# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 15640

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Criminal activity and abuse instances requiring reports; referring agencies; abuse by licensed health practitioners; abuse at long-term care facilities; consent of victim for reporting; neglect or abandonment.
