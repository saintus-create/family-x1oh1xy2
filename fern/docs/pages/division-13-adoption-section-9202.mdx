# Adoption

## Section 9202

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Medical report; request of adopted person or adoptive parents; access 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, the department or licensed adoption agency that made a medical report required by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8706.">§ 8706</a>, 8817, or 8909 shall provide a copy of the medical report, in the manner the department prescribes by regulation, to any of the following persons upon the person’s request: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A person who has been adopted pursuant to this part and who has attained the age of 18 years or who presents a certified copy of the person’s marriage certificate. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The adoptive parent of a person under the age of 18 years who has been adopted pursuant to this part. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A person who is denied access to a medical report pursuant to regulations adopted pursuant to this section may petition the court for review of the reasonableness of the department’s or licensed adoption agency’s decision: 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The names and addresses of any persons contained in the report shall be removed unless the person requesting the report has previously received the information. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.2000, c. 910 (A.B.2921),
