# § 3803

*Contents of order*

Contents of order
A deferred sale of home order shall state the duration of
the order and may include the legal description and assessor’s
parcel number of the real property which is subject to the
order.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
1994.)
