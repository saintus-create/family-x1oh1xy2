# 536 — Application fees; renewal fees; deposit of fees

Premarital Agreements

Application fees; renewal fees; deposit of fees
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The fee for an application for approval to authorize
confidential marriages pursuant to this chapter is three
hundred dollars ($300).
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The fee for renewal of an approval is three hundred
dollars ($300).

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Fees received pursuant to this chapter shall be deposit-
ed in a trust fund established by the county clerk.
The
money in the trust fund shall be used exclusively for the
administration of the programs described in this chapter.
