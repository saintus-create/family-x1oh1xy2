# Marriage

## Section 682

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Ownership by several persons; types The ownership of property by several persons is either: 
(a) Of joint interest. 
(b) Of partnership interests. 
(c) Of interests in common. 
(d) Of community interest of spouses. 
(Enacted in 1872. 
Amended by Stats.2016, c. 50 (S.B.1005), $7, eff. Jan. 1, 2017.)
