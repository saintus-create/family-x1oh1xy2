# § 682

*Ownership by several persons; types*

Ownership by several persons; types
The ownership of property by several persons is either:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Of joint interest.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Of partnership interests.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Of interests in common.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Of community interest of spouses. (Enacted in 1872.
