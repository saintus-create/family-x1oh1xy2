# § 7550 — This chapter shall govern both of the following:

This chapter shall govern both of the following:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The genetic testing of a person who either voluntarily submits to genetic testing or is ordered to submit to genetic testing pursuant to an order of a court or local child support agency.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The use of genetic testing in a proceeding to determine parentage.
