# General Provisions

## Section 333

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Unverified petition; dismissal without prejudice Any petition filed in juvenile court to commence proceedings pursuant to this chapter that is not verified may be dismissed without prejudice by such court.
