# 5046 — of marriage to maintain former spouse as beneficiary or

Support

of marriage to maintain former spouse as beneficiary or
preserve joint tenancy
2015 Addition
Section 5048 continues former Section 5604 without change. [36
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 103 (2006)].
Nothing in this chapter is intended to limit the court’s
authority to order a party to a dissolution or annulment of
marriage to maintain the former spouse as a beneficiary on
any nonprobate transfer described in this chapter, or to
preserve a joint tenancy in favor of the former spouse.
Treatises and Practice Aids
14 ,
In General.
(Formerly § 5603, added by Stats.2001, c. 417 (A.B.873), § 9,
operative Jan. 1, 2002. Renumbered $ 5046 and amended by
Stats.2015, c. 293 (A.B.139), § 15, eff. Jan. 1, 2016.)

Operative Effect
MULTIPLE-PARTY
For operative effect and application of Chapter
3, see Probate Code §$ 5048.
