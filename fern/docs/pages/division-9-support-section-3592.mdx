# § 3592 — Discharge in bankruptcy; power of court to make

Discharge in bankruptcy; power of court to make
new orders
If an obligation under an agreement for settlement of
property to a spouse or for support of a spouse is discharged
in bankruptcy, the court may make all proper orders for the
support of the spouse, as the court determines are just,
having regard for the circumstances of the parties and the
amount of the obligations under the agreement that are
discharged.
