# 2025 — Appeals of bifurcated issues; certification by supe-

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

Appeals of bifurcated issues; certification by supe-
rior court
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, if the court has
ordered an issue or issues bifurcated for separate trial or
hearing in advance of the disposition of the entire case, a
court of appeal may order an issue or issues transferred to it
for hearing and decision when the court that heard the issue
or issues certifies that the appeal is appropriate.
Certifica-
tion by the court shall be in accordance with rules promulgat-
ed by the Judicial Council.
