# § 3465.

*Part 3 — Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction And Enforcement Act · Chapter 4 — Miscellaneous Provisions*

A motion or other request for relief made in a child custody proceeding or to enforce a child custody determination that was commenced before the effective date of this part is governed by the law in effect at the time the motion or other request was made. (Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.) FAM Family Code - FAM
