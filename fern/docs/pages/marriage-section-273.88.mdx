# General Provisions

## Section 273.88

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Administrative costs; limit Administrative costs incurred by the Department of Justice pursuant to the Spousal Abuser Prosecution Program shall not exceed 5 percent of the total funds allocated for the program.
