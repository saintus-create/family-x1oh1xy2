# § 5216.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 8 — Earnings Assignment Order*

“Obligor” means a person owing a duty of support. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
