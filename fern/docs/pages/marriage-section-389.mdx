# General Provisions

## Section 389

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Petition for sealing records; notice; hearing; grounds for and effect of order; inspection and destruction of records. 
Information, documents, and services to be provided to a dependent child at the first review hearing after turning 16 years old and the last review hearing before turning 18 years old; termination of jurisdiction over nonminor; hearing; duties of county welfare department; continued jurisdiction.
