# Custody of Children

## Section 3184

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Appointment of counsel to represent minor child; recommendations Except as provided in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3188.">§ 3188</a>, nothing in this chapter prohibits the mediator from recommending to the court that counsel be appointed, pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3150.">§ 3150</a>), to represent the minor child. 
In making this recommendation, the mediator shall inform the court of the reasons why it would be in the best interest of the minor child to have counsel appointed.
