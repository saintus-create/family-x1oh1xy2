# § 3184 — Appointment of counsel to represent minor child;

Appointment of counsel to represent minor child;
recommendations
Except as provided in Section 3188, nothing in this chapter
prohibits the mediator from recommending to the court that
counsel be appointed, pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing
with Section 3150), to represent the minor child.
In making
this recommendation, the mediator shall inform the court of
the reasons why it would be in the best interest of the minor
child to have counsel appointed.
