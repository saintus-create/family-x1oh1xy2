# California Family Code § 7120

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A minor may petition the superior court of the county in which the minor resides or is temporarily domiciled for a declaration of emancipation.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition shall set forth with specificity all of the following facts:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The minor is at least 14 years of age.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The minor willingly lives separate and apart from the minor’s parents or guardian with the consent or acquiescence of the minor’s parents or guardian.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The minor is managing their own financial affairs. As evidence of this, the minor shall complete and attach a declaration of income and expenses as provided in Judicial Council form FL-150.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The source of the minor’s income is not derived from any activity declared to be a crime by the laws of this state or the laws of the United States.
