# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6322

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Ex parte order enjoining specified behavior The court may issue an ex parte order enjoining a party from specified behavior that the court determines is necessary to effectuate orders under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6320.">§ 6320</a> or 6321.
