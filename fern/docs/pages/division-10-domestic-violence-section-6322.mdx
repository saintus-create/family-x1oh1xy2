---
title: "6322. Protective orders for children"
description: "Protective orders for children"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-6322
---

The court may issue an ex parte order enjoining a party from specified behavior that the court determines is necessary to effectuate orders under Section 6320 or 6321.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.*
