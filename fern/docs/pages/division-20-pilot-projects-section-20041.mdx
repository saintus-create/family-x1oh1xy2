# 20041

The court shall centralize, augment, and coordinate all

The court shall centralize, augment, and coordinate all presently existing programs under the court’s supervision that relate to children, including, but not limited to, mental health special masters, appointment of attorneys for children, supervised visitation, and other supporting personnel. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.
