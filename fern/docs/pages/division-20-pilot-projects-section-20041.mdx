# § 20041 — The court shall centralize, augment, and coordinate all

The court shall centralize, augment, and coordinate all
presently existing programs under the court’s supervision that
relate to children, including, but not limited to, mental health
special masters, appointment of attorneys for children, super-
vised visitation, and other supporting personnel.
