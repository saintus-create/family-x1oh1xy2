# Pilot Projects

## Section 20041

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

The court shall centralize, augment, and coordinate all presently existing programs under the court’s supervision that relate to children, including, but not limited to, mental health special masters, appointment of attorneys for children, supervised visitation, and other supporting personnel.

<div class="source-note">

**Source:** California Family Code Annotated (Grace Ganz Blumberg, 2020 Desktop Edition). [View on California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=20041.).
