# 2340

Statement of effective date of judgment

A judgment of dissolution of marriage shall specify the date on which the judgment becomes finally effective for the purpose of terminating the marriage relationship of the parties. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
