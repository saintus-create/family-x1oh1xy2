# 7864

Continuance of proceedings; purpose

The court may continue the proceeding for not to exceed 30 days as necessary to appoint counsel and to enable counsel to become acquainted with the case. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
