# 4616 — Sale of assets; hearing; notice

Support

Sale of assets; hearing; notice
ARTICLE 3.
Upon deposit of an asset which is not readily convertible
into money, the court may, after a hearing, order the sale of
that asset and the deposit of the proceeds with the deposit
holder. Not less than 20 days written notice of the hearing
shall be served on the obligor-parent.
