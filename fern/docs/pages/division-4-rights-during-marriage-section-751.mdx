# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 751

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Community property; interests of parties The respective interests of each spouse in community property during continuance of the marriage relation are present, existing, and equal interests.
