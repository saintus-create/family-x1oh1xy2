# 7960. For purposes of this part, the following terms have the

*Parent and Child Relationship*

RELATIONSHIP

For purposes of this part, the following terms have the
following meanings:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Assisted reproduction agreement” has the same
meaning as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 7606.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Fund management agreement” means the agreement
between the intended parents and the surrogacy or donor
facilitator relating to the fee or other valuable consideration
for services rendered or that will be rendered by the
surrogacy or donor facilitator.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Intended parent” means an individual, married or
unmarried, who manifests the intent to be legally bound as
the parent of a child resulting from assisted reproduction.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Nonattorney surrogacy or donor facilitator” means a
surrogacy or donor practitioner who is not an attorney in
good standing licensed to practice law in this state.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Surrogacy or donor facilitator” means a person or
organization that engages in either of the following activities:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Advertising for the purpose of soliciting parties to an
assisted reproduction agreement or for the donation of
oocytes for use by a person other than the provider of the
oocytes, or acting as an intermediary between the parties to
an assisted reproduction agreement or oocyte donation.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Charging a fee or other valuable consideration for
services rendered relating to an assisted reproduction agreement or oocyte donation.
direct the client to deposit all client funds into either of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  An independent, bonded escrow depository maintained by a licensed, independent, bonded escrow company.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A trust account maintained by an attorney.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of this section, a nonattorney surrogacy
or donor facilitator may not have a financial interest in any
escrow company holding client funds. A nonattorney surrogacy or donor facilitator and any of its directors or employees
shall not be an agent of any escrow company holding client
funds.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Client funds may only be disbursed by the attorney or
escrow agent as set forth in the assisted reproduction
agreement and fund management agreement.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  This section shall not apply to funds that are both of
the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Not provided for in the fund management agreement.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Paid directly to a medical doctor for medical services
or a psychologist for psychological services.

child for another through medically assisted reproduction and
pursuant to a written agreement, as set forth in Sections 7606
and 7962.
