# 423. Granting permission to marry

*Premarital Agreements*

granting permission to marry
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The person solemnizing the marriage shall return the
marriage license, endorsed as required in Section 422, to the
county recorder of the county in which the license was issued
within 10 days after the ceremony.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of Section 102356 of the Health and
Safety Code, the person solemnizing the marriage shall
include with the marriage license described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span> 
a copy of the court order granting permission to marry
860 (2018), review denied (parties’ decision not to file solemnized
marriage license did not invalidate marriage).
