# Marriage

## Section 423

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Return of license; inclusion of copy of court order granting permission to marry 
(a) The person solemnizing the marriage shall return the marriage license, endorsed as required in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=422.">Section 422</a>, to the county recorder of the county in which the license was issued within 10 days after the ceremony. 
(b) For purposes of Section 102356 of the Health and Safety Code, the person solemnizing the marriage shall include with the marriage license described in subdivision (a) 
a copy of the court order granting permission to marry 53 
§ 425 described in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=304.">Section 304</a>, if one or both of the parties to the marriage were minors at the time of solemnization of the marriage.
