# General Provisions

## Section 274

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Attempted murder of a spouse; attorney’s fees and costs; notice and hearing; source of funds 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, if the injured spouse is entitled to a remedy authorized pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4324.">§ 4324</a>, the injured spouse shall be entitled to an award of reasonable attorney’s fees and costs as a sanction puneuant to this section. 

 OF & 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An award of attorney’s fees and costs as a sanction pursuant to this section shall be imposed only after notice to the party against whom the sanction is proposed to be imposed and opportunity for that party to be heard. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An award of attorney’s fees and costs as a sanction pursuant to this section is payable only from the property or income of the party against whom the sanction is imposed, except that the award may be against the sanctioned party’s share of the community property. 
In order to obtain an award under this section, the party requesting an award of attorney’s fees and costs is not required to demonstrate any financial need for the award.
