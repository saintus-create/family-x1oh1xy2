# Pilot Projects

## Section 21611

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Spouse not to receive share; circumstances The spouse shall not receive a share of the estate under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=21610.">§ 21610</a> if any of the following is established: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The decedent’s failure to provide for the spouse in the decedent’s testamentary instruments was intentional and that intention appears from the testamentary instruments. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The decedent provided for the spouse by transfer outside of the estate passing by the decedent’s testamentary instruments and the intention that the transfer be in lieu of a provision in said instruments is shown by statements of the decedent or from the amount of the transfer or by other evidence. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The spouse made a valid agreement waiving the right to share in the decedent’s estate. 
d)(1) 
If both of the following apply: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The spouse was a care custodian, as that term is defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=21362.">§ 21362</a>, of the decedent who was a dependent adult, as that term is defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=21366.">§ 21366</a>, and the marriage commenced while the care custodian provided services to the decedent, or within 90 days after those services were last provided to the decedent. 
§ 21611 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The decedent died less than six months after the marriage commenced. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Notwithstanding paragraph (1), a spouse described by this subdivision shall be entitled to receive a share of the estate pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=21610.">§ 21610</a> if the spouse proves by clear and convincing evidence that the marriage between the spouse and the decedent was not the product of fraud or undue influence.
