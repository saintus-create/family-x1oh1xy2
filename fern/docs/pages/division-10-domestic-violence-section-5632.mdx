# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5632

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Execution and recording of revocation 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An instrument revoking a revocable transfer on death deed shall be executed and recorded before the transferor’s death in the same manner as execution and recordation of a revocable transfer on death deed. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Joinder, consent, or agreement of, or notice to, the beneficiary is not required for revocation of a revocable transfer on death deed.
