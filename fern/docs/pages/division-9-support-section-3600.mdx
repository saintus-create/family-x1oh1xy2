# Support

## Section 3600

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Support orders. 
(a) either spouse to pay any amount that is necessary for the support of the other spouse, consistent with the requirements of subdivisions (i) and (m) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4320.">Section 4320</a> and <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4325.">Section 4325</a>, or (b) either or both parents to pay any amount necessary for the support of the child, as the case may be.
