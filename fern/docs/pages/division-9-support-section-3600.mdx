# 3600. During the pendency of any proceeding for dissolution of

*Support*

During the pendency of any proceeding for dissolution of
marriage or for legal separation of the parties or under
(commencing with Section 3000) ( ) or in any proceeding where there is at issue the
support of a minor child or a child for whom support is
authorized under Section 3901 or 3910, the court may order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  either spouse to pay any amount that is necessary for the
support of the other spouse, consistent with the requirements
of subdivisions <span class="subsec">(i)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(m)</span>  of Section 4320 and Section 4325,
or <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  either or both parents to pay any amount necessary for
the support of the child, as the case may be. (Stats.1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by
c. 293 (S.B.1221), § 1; Stats.2002, c. 759 (A.B.
3033), § 1; Stats.2014, c. 82 (S.B.1306), § 35, eff. Jan. 1,
2015.)
