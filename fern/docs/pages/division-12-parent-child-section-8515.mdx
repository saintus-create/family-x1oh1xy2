# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8515

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

“Delegated county adoption agency” defined 
“Delegated county adoption agency” means a county adoption agency that has agreed to provide the services described in Chapter 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8800.">§ 8800</a>) of Part 2. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.2012, c. 35 (S.B.1013), $ 5, eff. June 27, 2012)
