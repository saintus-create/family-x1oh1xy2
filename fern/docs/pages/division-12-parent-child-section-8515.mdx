# 8515. “Delegated county adoption agency” defined

*Parent and Child Relationship*

“Delegated county adoption agency” defined
“Delegated county adoption agency” means
a county
adoption agency that has agreed to provide the services
described in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 8800) of
. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
1994. Amended by Stats.2012, c. 35 (S.B.1013), $ 5, eff. June
27, 2012)
