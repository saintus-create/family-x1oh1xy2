# § 5700.211

*Continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify*

Continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify
spousal-support order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state issuing a spousal-support order
consistent with the law of this state has continuing, exclusive

jurisdiction to modify the spousal-support order throughout
the existence of the support obligation.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A tribunal of this state may not modify a spousal-
support order issued by a tribunal of another state or a
foreign country having continuing, exclusive jurisdiction over
that order under the law of that state or foreign country.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A tribunal of this state that has continuing, exclusive
jurisdiction over
a spousal-support order may serve
as:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  an initiating tribunal to request a tribunal of another
state to enforce the spousal-support order issued in this state;
or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  a responding tribunal to enforce or modify its own
spousal-support order.
