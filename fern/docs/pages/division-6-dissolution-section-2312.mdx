# 2312

Permanent legal incapacity to make decisions

A marriage may be dissolved on the grounds of permanent legal incapacity to make decisions only upon proof, including competent medical or psychiatric testimony, that the spouse was at the time the petition was filed, and remains, permanently lacking the legal capacity to make decisions. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 144, Sec. 10. (AB 1847) Effective January 1, 2015.)_
