# § 2322.

*Part 3 — Dissolution Of Marriage And Legal Separation · Chapter 3 — Residence Requirements*

For the purpose of a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, each spouse may have a separate domicile or residence depending upon proof of the fact and not upon legal presumptions. (Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 27. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.) FAM Family Code - FAM
