# § 5700.704

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a support proceeding under this chapter, the Department of Child Support Services shall:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  transmit and receive applications; and

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  initiate or facilitate the institution of a proceeding regarding an application in a tribunal of this state.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The following support proceedings are available to an obligee under the Convention:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  recognition or recognition and enforcement of a foreign support order;

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  enforcement of a support order issued or recognized in this state;

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  establishment of a support order if there is no existing order, including, if necessary, determination of parentage of a child;

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  establishment of a support order if recognition of a foreign support order is refused under Section 5700.708(b)(2), (4), or (9);

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  modification of a support order of a tribunal of this state; and

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  modification of a support order of a tribunal of another state or a foreign country.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The following support proceedings are available under the Convention to an obligor against which there is an existing support order:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  recognition of an order suspending or limiting enforcement of an existing support order of a tribunal of this state;

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  modification of a support order of a tribunal of this state; and

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  modification of a support order of a tribunal of another state or a foreign country.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A tribunal of this state may not require security, bond, or deposit, however described, to guarantee the payment of costs and expenses in proceedings under the Convention.
