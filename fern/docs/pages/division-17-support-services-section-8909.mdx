# 8909 — Medical report on child and biological parents;

Support Services

Medical report on child and biological parents;
contents; blood sample
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An agency may not place a child for adoption unless a
written report on the child’s medical background and, if
available, the medical background of the child’s biological
parents so far as ascertainable, has been submitted to the
prospective adoptive parents and they have acknowledged in
writing the receipt of the report.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The report on the child’s background shall contain all
known diagnostic information,
including current medical
reports on the child, psychological evaluations, and scholastic
information, as well as all known information regarding the
child’s developmental history and family life.
(c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The biological parents may provide a blood sample
at a clinic or hospital approved by the State Department of
Health Services.
The biological parents’ failure to provide a
blood sample shall not affect the adoption of the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The blood sample shall be stored at a laboratory under
contract with the State Department of Health Services for a
period of 30 years following the adoption of the child.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The purpose of the stored sample of blood is to
provide a blood sample from which DNA testing can be done
at a later date after entry of the order of adoption at the
request of the adoptive parents or the adopted child.
