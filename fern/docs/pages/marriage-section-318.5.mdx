# General Provisions

## Section 318.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Representation of petitioner by county counsel or district attorney In a juvenile court hearing, where the parent or guardian is represented by counsel, the county counsel or district attorney shall, at the request of the juvenile court judge, appear and participate in the hearing to represent the petitioner. 
(Formerly § 351, added by Stats.1976, c. 1068,
