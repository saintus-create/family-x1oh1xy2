# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2264

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Restitution 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In general.—Notwithstanding section 3663 or 3663A, and in addition to any other civil or criminal penalty authorized by law, the court shall order restitution for any offense under this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Scope and nature of order.— 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Directions.—The order of restitution under this section, shall direct the defendant to pay the victim 
(through the appropriate court mechanism) the full amount of the victim’s losses as determined by the court pursuant to paragraph (2). 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Enforcement.—An order of restitution under this section shall be issued and enforced in accordance with section 3664 in the same manner as an order under section 3663A. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Definition.—For purposes of this subsection, the term “full amount of the victim’s losses” includes any costs incurred by the victim for— 
18 § 2265 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  medical services relating to physical, psychiatric, or psychological care; 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  physical and occupational therapy or rehabilitation; 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  necessary transportation, temporary housing, and child care expenses; 
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  lost income; 
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  attorneys’ fees, plus any costs incurred in obtaining a civil protection order; 
<span class="subsec">(F)</span>  veterinary services relating to physical care for the victim’s pet, service animal, emotional support animal, or horse; and 
<span class="subsec">(G)</span>  any other losses suffered by the victim as a proximate result of the offense. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Order mandatory.—(A) The issuance of a restitution order under this section is mandatory. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A court may not decline to issue an order under this section because of— 
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  the economic circumstances of the defendant; or 
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  the fact that a victim has, or is entitled to, receive compensation for his or her injuries from the proceeds of insurance or any other source. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Victim defined.—For purposes of this section, the term “victim” means the individual harmed as a result of a commission of a crime under this chapter, including, in the case of a victim who is under 18 years of age, incompetent, incapacitated, or deceased, the legal guardian of the victim or representative of the victim’s estate, another family member, or any other person appointed as suitable by the court, but in no event shall the defendant be named as such representative or guardian.
