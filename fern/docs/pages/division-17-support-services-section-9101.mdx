# § 9101 — Decree or order of adoption set aside; proceedings;

Decree or order of adoption set aside; proceedings;
care and custody of child
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If an order of adoption is set aside as provided in
Section 9100, the court making the order shall direct the
district attorney, the county counsel, or the county welfare
department to take appropriate action under the Welfare and
Institutions Code.
The court may also make any order
relative to the care, custody, or confinement of the child
pending the proceeding the court sees fit.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The county in which the proceeding for adoption was
had is liable for the child’s support until the child is able to
be self-supporting.
