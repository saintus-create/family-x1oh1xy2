# Adoption

## Section 9305

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Legal relationship of parent and child After adoption, the adoptee and the adoptive parent or parents shall sustain towards each other the legal relationship of parent and child and have all the rights and are subject to all the duties of that relationship.
