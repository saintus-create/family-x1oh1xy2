# § 7910 — Of state law

of state law
Approval of an interstate placement of a child for adoption
shall not be granted by the compact administrator if the
placement is in violation of either Section 8801 of this code or
Section 273 of the Penal Code.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.
2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
