654a.
Automated data processing
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In general
In order for a State to meet the requirements of this
section, the State agency administering the State program
under this part shall have in operation a single statewide
automated data processing and information retrieval system
which has the capability to perform the tasks specified in this
42 654a
section with the frequency and in the manner required by or
under this part.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Program management
: The automated system required by this section shall per-
form such functions as the Secretary may specify relating to
management of the State program under this part, includ-
ing—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  controlling and accounting for use of Federal,
State, and local funds in carrying out the program; and
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  maintaining the data necessary to meet Federal
reporting requirements under this part on a timely
basis.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Calculation of performance indicators
In order to enable the Secretary to determine the incen-
tive payments and penalty adjustments required by sections
652(g) and 658a of this title, the State agency shall—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  use the automated system—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  to maintain the requisite data on State per-
formance with respect to paternity establishment
and child support enforcement in the State; and
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  to calculate the paternity establishment per-
centage for the State for each fiscal year;
and
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  have in place systems controls to ensure
the
completeness and reliability of, and ready access to, the
data described in paragraph (1)(A), and the accuracy of
the calculations described in paragraph (1)(B).
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Information integrity and security
The State, agency shall have in effect safeguards on the
integrity, accuracy, and completeness of, access to, and use
of data in the automated system required by this section,
which shall include the following (in addition to such other
safeguards as the Secretary may specify in regulations):
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Policies restricting access
Written policies concerning access to data by State
agency personnel, and sharing of data with other per-
sons, which—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  permit access to and use of data only to the
extent necessary to carry out the State program
under this part; and
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  specify the data which may be used for
particular program purposes, and the personnel
permitted access to such data.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Systems controls
Systems controls (such as passwords or blocking of
fields) to ensure strict adherence to the policies de-
scribed in paragraph (1).
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Monitoring of access
Routine monitoring of access
to and use of the
automated system, through methods such as audit trails
and
feedback’
mechanisms,
to guard
against
and
promptly identify unauthorized access or use.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Training and information
Procedures to ensure that all personnel (including
State and local agency staff and contractors) who may
have access to or be required to use confidential pro-
gram data are informed of applicable requirements and
penalties (including those in section 6103 of the Inter-
nal Revenue Code of 1986), and are adequately trained
in security procedures.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Penalties
Administrative penalties (up to and including dismiss-
al from employment) for unauthorized access to, or
disclosure or use of, confidential data.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  State case registry
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Contents
The automated system required by this section shall
include a registry (which shall be known as the “State
case registry”) that contains records with respect to—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  each case in which services are being provid-
ed by the State agency under the State plan ap-
proved under this part; and
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  each support order established or modified
in the State on or after October 1, 1998.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Linking of local registries
The State case registry may be established by linking
local case registries of support orders through an auto-
mated information network, subject to this section.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Use of standardized data elements
Such records shall use standardized data elements for
both parents (such as names, social security numbers
and other uniform
identification
numbers,
dates of
birth, and case identification numbers), and contain
such other information (such as on case status) as the
Secretary may require.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Payment records
Each case record in the State case registry with
respect to which services are being provided under the
State plan approved under this part and with respect to
which a support order has been established shall include
a record of—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  the amount of monthly (or other periodic)
support owed under the order, and other amounts
(including arrearages, interest or late payment pen-
alties, and fees) due or overdue under the order;
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  any amount described in subparagraph <span class="subsec">(A)</span> 
that has been collected;
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  the distribution of such collected amounts;
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  the birth date and, beginning not later than
October 1, 1999, the social security number, of any
child for whom the order requires the provision of
support; and
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  the amount of any lien imposed with respect
to the order pursuant to section 666(a)<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  of this
title.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Updating and monitoring
The State agency operating the automated system
required by this section shall promptly establish and
update, maintain, and regularly monitor, case records in
the State case registry with respect to which services are
being provided under the State plan approved under
this part, on the basis of—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  information on administrative
actions and
administrative and judicial proceedings and orders
relating to paternity and support;
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  information obtained from comparison with
Federal,
State, or local sources
of information;
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  information on support collections and dis-
tributions; and
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  any other relevant information.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Information comparisons and other disclosures of infor-
mation
The State shall use the automated system required by this
section to extract information from (at such times, and in
such standardized format or formats, as may be required by
the Secretary), to share and compare information with, and
to receive information from, other data bases and informa-
tion comparison services, in order to obtain (or provide)
information necessary to enable the State agency (or the
Secretary or other State or Federal agencies) to carry out
this part, subject to section 6103 of the Internal Revenue
Code of 1986.
Such information comparison activities shall
include the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Federal Case Registry of Child Support Orders
Furnishing to the Federal Case Registry of Child
Support Orders established under section 653(h) of this
title (and update as necessary, with information includ-
ing notice of expiration of orders) the minimum amount
of information on child support cases recorded in the
State case registry that is necessary to operate the
registry (as specified by the Secretary in regulations).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Federal Parent Locator Service
Exchanging information with the Federal Parent Lo-
cator Service for the purposes specified in section 653
of this title.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Temporary family assistance and medicaid agencies
Exchanging information with State agencies (of the
State and of other States) administering programs fund-
ed under part A, programs operated under a State plan
approved under subchapter XIX, and other programs
designated by the Secretary, as necessary to perform
State agency responsibilities under this part and under
such programs.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Intrastate and interstate information comparisons
Exchanging information with other agencies of the
State, agencies of other States, and interstate informa-
tion networks, as necessary and appropriate to carry out
(or assist other States to carry out) the purposes of this
part.

Collection and disbursement of support payments
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  State disbursement unit
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general
In order for a State to meet the requirements of this
section, the State agency must establish and operate a
unit (which shall be known as the “State disbursement
_ unit” ) for the collection and disbursement of payments
under support orders—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  in all cases being enforced by the State
pursuant to section 654(4) of this title; and
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  in all cases not being enforced by the State
under
this part in which the support order is
initially issued in the State on or after January 1,
1994, and in which the income of the noncustodial
parent is subject to withholding pursuant to section
666(a)(8)<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  of this title.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Operation
The State disbursement unit shall be operated—
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  directly by the State agency (or 2 or more
State agencies under a regional cooperative agree-
ment), or (to the extent appropriate) by a contrac-
tor responsible directly, to the State agency;
and
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  except
in cases
described
in paragraph
(1)(B), in coordination with the automated system
established by the State pursuant to section 654a of
this title.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Linking of local disbursement units
The State disbursement unit may be established by
linking local disbursement units through an automated
information network, subject to this section, if the Sec-
retary agrees that the system will not cost more nor take
more time to establish or operate than a centralized
system.
In addition, employers shall be given 1 location
to which income withholding is sent.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Required procedures
The State disbursement unit shall use automated proce-
dures, electronic processes, and computer-driven technology
to the maximum extent feasible, efficient, and economical,
for the collection and disbursement of support payments,
including procedures—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  for receipt of payments from parents, employers,
and other States, and for disbursements to custodial
parents and other obligees, the State agency, and the
agencies of other States;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  for accurate identification of payments;
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  to ensure prompt disbursement of the custodial
parent’s share of any payment; and
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  to furnish to any parent, upon request, timely
information on the current status of support payments
under an order requiring payments to be made by or to
the parent, except that in cases described in subsection
(a)(1)(B), the State disbursement unit shall not be
required to convert and maintain in automated form
records
of

kept

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Timing of disbursements
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general
Except as provided in paragraph (2), the State dis-
bursement unit shall distribute
all amounts payable
under section 657(a) of this title within 2 business days
after receipt from the employer or other source of
periodic income, if sufficient information identifying the
payee is provided.
The date of collection for amounts
collected and distributed under this part is the date of
receipt by the State disbursement unit, except that if
current support
is withheld by an employer in the
month when due and is received by the State disburse-
ment unit in a month other than the month when due,
the date of withholding may be deemed to be the date
of collection.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Permissive retention of arrearages
The State disbursement unit may delay the distribu-
tion of collections toward arrearages until the resolution
of any timely appeal with respect to such arrearages.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Business day” defined
As used in this section, the term “business day” means a
day on which State offices are open for regular business.
