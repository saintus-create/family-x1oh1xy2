# § 7806.

*Part 4 — Freedom From Parental Custody And Control · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

There shall be no filing fee charged for a proceeding brought under this part. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
