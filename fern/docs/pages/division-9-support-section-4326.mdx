<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (d), in a proceeding
in which a spousal support order exists or in which the court
has retained jurisdiction over a spousal support order, if a
companion child support order is in effect, the termination of
child support pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 3901
constitutes a change of circumstances that may be the basis
for a request by either party for modification of spousal
support.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A motion to modify spousal support based on the
change of circumstances described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall be
filed by either party no later than six months from the date
the child support order terminates.
