# § 4072

*Deduction for hardship expenses;*

Deduction for hardship expenses;
statement by
court of reasons for supporting deductions; statement
of amount and underlying facts; duration
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If a deduction for hardship expenses is allowed, the
court shall do both of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  State the reasons supporting the deduction in writing
or on the record.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Document
the amount of the deduction and the
underlying facts and circumstances.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Whenever possible, the court shall specify the duration
of the deduction.
