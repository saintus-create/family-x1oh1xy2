## General Provisions

  <h3>Jurisdiction</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 200</Badge></div>
        <p>California courts have jurisdiction over family law proceedings as provided by statute and the Constitution. This section anchors the court's authority to hear the cases governed by this Code.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Applicable rules of practice and procedure</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 210</Badge></div>
        <p>Except to the extent that any other statute or rules adopted by the Judicial Council provide applicable rules, the rules of practice and procedure applicable to civil actions generally apply to proceedings under this code. This includes <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&division=2.&title=&part=2.&chapter=&article=">Title 3a (commencing with Section 391) of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1994</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Mediator or evaluator responsibilities</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 216</Badge></div>
        <p>A mediator or evaluator may have responsibilities as a mandated reporter pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=11165.9.">Section 11165.9 of the Penal Code</a> and responsibilities to warn under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=43.92.">Section 43.92 of the Civil Code</a>. Subdivisions (a) and (b) shall not apply in situations involving allegations of domestic violence as set forth in Sections <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3113.">3113</a>, <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3181.">3181</a>, and <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3192.">3192</a>, or pursuant to <a href="https://www.courts.ca.gov/rules/index.htm">Rule 5.215 of the California Rules of Court</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Amended Stats. 2020, Ch. 386, Sec. 4</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2021</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Date for trial of action</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 218</Badge></div>
        <p>The date initially set for trial of the action specified in subdivision (a) of Section 2024.020 of the <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=2024.020.">Code of Civil Procedure</a> shall mean the date the postjudgment proceeding is set for hearing.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1994</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Temporary restraining order in summons</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 233</Badge></div>
        <p>A willful and knowing violation of the order included in the summons by removing a child from the state without the written consent of the other party or an order of the court is punishable as provided in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=278.5.">Section 278.5 of the Penal Code</a>. A willful and knowing violation of any of the other orders included in the summons is punishable as provided in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=273.6.">Section 273.6 of the Penal Code</a>.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Amended Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 7</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 2020</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Attorney's fees and costs</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 273</Badge></div>
        <p>The court shall not award attorney's fees against any governmental agency involved in a family law matter except when sanctions are appropriate pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=128.5.">Section 128.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure</a> or Section 271 of this code.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1994</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 291</Badge></div>

  <h3>Order to Show Cause and Affidavit for Contempt</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 292</Badge></div>
        <p><a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=1211.5.">Section 1211.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure</a> shall apply to the Order to Show Cause and Affidavit for Contempt (Family Law) and the Affidavit of Facts Constituting Contempt.</p>

  <h3>Tribal marriages and divorces</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 295</Badge></div>
        <p>Recognizes the validity of tribal marriages and divorces for purposes of this Code, consistent with federal law and tribal sovereignty. For the purpose of application of the laws of succession set forth in the <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?tocCode=PROB">Probate Code</a> to a decedent, and for the purpose of determining the validity of a marriage under the laws of this state, an alliance entered into before 1958 shall be governed by the laws in effect at that time.</p>
        <div className="section-meta">
          <Badge intent="info" minimal>Enacted Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10</Badge>
          <Badge intent="note" minimal>Eff. Jan 1, 1994</Badge>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
