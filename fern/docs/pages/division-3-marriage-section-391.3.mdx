title: "391.3. The"
description: "The"
slug: division-3-marriage-section-391.3
---

The
motion for an order requiring the plaintiff to furnish security
shall be based upon the ground, and supported by a showing,
that the plaintiff is a vexatious litigant and that there is not a
reasonable probability that he or she will prevail in the
litigation against the moving defendant.
