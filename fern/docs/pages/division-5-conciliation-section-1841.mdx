# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1841

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Dissolution, legal separation or judgment of nullity; minor child involved; transfer If a petition for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, or for legal separation of the parties is filed, the OF & case may be transferred at any time during the pendency of the proceeding to the family conciliation court for proceedings for reconciliation of the spouses or amicable settlement of issues in controversy in accordance with this part if both of the following appear to the court: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  There is a minor child of the spouses, or of either of them, whose welfare may be adversely affected by the dissolution of the marriage or the disruption of the household or a controversy involving child custody. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  There is some reasonable possibility of a reconciliation being effected.
