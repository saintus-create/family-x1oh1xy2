# § 1454

*Court investigator; appointment; qualifications*

Court investigator; appointment; qualifications
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court shall appoint a court investigator when one
is required for the purposes of a proceeding under this
division.
The person appointed as the court investigator
shall be an officer or special appointee of the court with no
personal or other beneficial interest in the proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The person appointed as the court investigator shall
have the following qualifications:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The training or experience, or both, necessary <span class="subsec">(i)</span>  to
make the investigations required under this division, <span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  to
communicate with, assess, and deal with persons who are or
may be the subject of proceedings under this division, and
(iii) to perform the other duties required of a court investiga-
tor.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A demonstrated sufficient knowledge of law so as to be
able to inform conservatees and proposed conservatees of the
nature and effect of a conservatorship proceeding and of
their rights, to answer their questions, and to inform conser-
vators concerning their powers and duties.
