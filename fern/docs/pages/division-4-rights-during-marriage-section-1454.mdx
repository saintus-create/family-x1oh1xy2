# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1454

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Court investigator; appointment; qualifications 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court shall appoint a court investigator when one is required for the purposes of a proceeding under this division. 
The person appointed as the court investigator shall be an officer or special appointee of the court with no personal or other beneficial interest in the proceeding. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The person appointed as the court investigator shall have the following qualifications: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The training or experience, or both, necessary (i) to make the investigations required under this division, (ii) to communicate with, assess, and deal with persons who are or may be the subject of proceedings under this division, and 
<span class="subsec">(iii)</span>  to perform the other duties required of a court investigator. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A demonstrated sufficient knowledge of law so as to be able to inform conservatees and proposed conservatees of the nature and effect of a conservatorship proceeding and of their rights, to answer their questions, and to inform conservators concerning their powers and duties.
