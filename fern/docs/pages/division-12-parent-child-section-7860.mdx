# § 7860

*At the beginning of the proceeding on a petition filed*

At the beginning of the proceeding on a petition filed
pursuant to this part, counsel shall be appointed as provided
in this article. The public defender or private counsel may be
appointed as counsel pursuant to this article. The same
counsel shall not be appointed to represent both the child
