# § 2212 — Effect of judgment of nullity; conclusiveness

(a) The effect of a judgment of nullity of marriage is to restore the parties to the status of unmarried persons.

(b) A judgment of nullity of marriage is conclusive only as to the parties to the proceeding and those claiming under them. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
