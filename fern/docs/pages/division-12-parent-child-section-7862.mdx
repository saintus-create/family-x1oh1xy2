# § 7862 — Appointment of counsel for parent

Appointment of counsel for parent
If a parent appears without counsel and is unable to afford
counsel, the court shall appoint counsel for the parent, unless
that representation is knowingly and intelligently waived.
(Stats.1992, c.

162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
