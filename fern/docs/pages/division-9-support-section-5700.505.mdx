# § 5700.505 — Penalties for noncompliance

(2018), Proceedings Under UIFSA.
Penalties for noncompliance
An employer that willfully fails to comply with an incomewithholding order issued in another state and received for
enforcement is subject to the same penalties that may be
imposed for noncompliance with an order issued by a tribunal
of this state. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff.
Jan. 1, 2016.)
