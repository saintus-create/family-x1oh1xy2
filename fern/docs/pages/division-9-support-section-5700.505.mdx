# § 5700.505

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 5 — Enforcement Of Support Order Without Registration*

An employer that willfully fails to comply with an income-withholding order issued in another state and received for enforcement is subject to the same penalties that may be imposed for noncompliance with an order issued by a tribunal of this state. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
