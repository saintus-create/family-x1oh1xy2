# 3110

Or evaluator as described in Section 3110 or Section 730 of

As used in this chapter, “court-appointed investigator” means a probation officer, domestic relations investigator, or court-appointed evaluator directed by the court to conduct an investigation pursuant to this chapter. Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.81. Effective January 1, 1994.
