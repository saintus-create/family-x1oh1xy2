# § 3110 — Or evaluator as described in Section 3110 or Section 730 of

5, or a court-appointed investigator
or evaluator as described in Section 3110 or Section 730 of
the Evidence Code.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An evaluator shall participate in a program of continu-
ing instruction in domestic violence, including child abuse, as
may be arranged and provided to that evaluator.
This
training may utilize domestic violence training programs
conducted by nonprofit community organizations with an
expertise in domestic violence issues.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Areas of basic instruction shall include, but are not
limited to, the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The effects on children.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The nature and extent .
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The social and family dynamics .
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Techniques for identifying and assisting families affect-
ed by domestic violence.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Interviewing, documentation of, and appropriate rec-
ommendations for, families affected by domestic violence.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The legal rights of, and remedies available to, victims.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Availability of community and legal domestic violence
resources.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  An evaluator shall also complete 16 hours of advanced
training within a 12—month period.
Four hours of that
advanced training shall include community resource network-
ing intended to acquaint the evaluator with domestic violence
resources in the geographical communities where the family
being evaluated may reside.
Twelve hours of instruction, as
approved by the Administrative Office of the Courts, shall
include all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The appropriate structuring of the child custody evalu-
ation process,
including, but not limited
to, all of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Maximizing safety for clients, evaluators, and court
personnel.
_
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Maintaining objectivity.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Providing and gathering balanced information from
the parties and controlling for bias.
