# 4412. Time for hearing; service of citation and petition

*Support*

Time for hearing; service of citation and petition
On the filing of a petition under this chapter, the clerk shall
set the matter for hearing by the court and shall issue a
citation, stating the time and place of the hearing, directed to
the parent and. to the parent’s conservator, if any, or, if the
parent is deceased, the personal representative of the par-
ent’s estate.
At least five days before the date of the hearing,
the citation and a copy of the petition shall be personally
served on each person to whom it is directed, in the same
manner as provided by law for the service of summons.
