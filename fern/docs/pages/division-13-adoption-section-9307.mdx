# Adoption

## Section 9307

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Open and public hearing A hearing with regard to adoption under Chapter 2 
(commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=9320.">9320</a>) or termination of a parent and child relationship under Chapter 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=9340.">9340</a>) may, in the discretion of the court, be open and public.
