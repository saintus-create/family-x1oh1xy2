# 4405

Jurisdiction to modify or terminate order of sup-

The court retains jurisdiction to modify or terminate an order for support where justice requires. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
