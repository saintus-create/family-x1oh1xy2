---
title: "4405. Jurisdiction to modify or terminate order of sup-"
description: "Jurisdiction to modify or terminate order of sup-"
slug: division-9-support-section-4405
---

The court retains jurisdiction to modify or terminate an order for support where justice requires.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
