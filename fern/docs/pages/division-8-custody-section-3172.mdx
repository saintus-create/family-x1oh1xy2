# § 3172

*ceedings*

ceedings
operative Jan. 1, 2020.)
Mediation shall not be denied to the parties on the basis
that paternity is at issue in a proceeding before the court.
(Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 116.87.)
