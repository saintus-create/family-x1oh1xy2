# Custody of Children

## Section 3172

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Paternity disputes; availability of mediation proceedings Mediation shall not be denied to the parties on the basis that paternity is at issue in a proceeding before the court.
