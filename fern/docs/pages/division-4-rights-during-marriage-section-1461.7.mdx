# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1461.7

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Time and place of hearing on petition, report, or account; copies Unless the court for good cause dispenses with such notice, notice of the time and place of the hearing on a petition, report, or account, together with a copy of the petition, 1070 — report, or account, shall be given to the same persons who are required to be given notice under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2581.">Section 2581</a> for the period and in the manner provided in this chapter if both of the following conditions exist: 
(a) A conservator of the estate has been appointed under Article 5 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1845.">Section 1845</a>) of Chapter 1 of Part 3 for a person who is missing and whose whereabouts is unknown. 
(b) The petition, report, or account is filed in the conservatorship proceeding under any one or more of the following provisions: 
(1) <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1861.">Section 1861</a> or 2423. 
(2) Article 7 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2540.">Section 2540</a>) of Chapter 6 of Part 4. 
(3) <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2570.">Section 2570</a>, 2571, 2580,.2592, or 2620, 
(4) Chapter 8 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2640.">Section 2640</a>) of Part 4. 
(5) Chapter 9.5 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2670.">Section 2670</a>) of Part 4. 
(6) Chapter 3 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3100.">Section 3100</a>) of Part 6.
