# § 3702 — Section 3700 continues Section 3700 of the repealed Probate Code

Section 3700 continues Section 3700 of the repealed Probate Code
without substantive change. The reference to “divorce” is omitted as
unnecessary in view of Section 36 (“dissolution of marriage” includes
divorce). For background on this section, see Recommendation
Relating to Missing Persons, 16 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports
105, 124-25 (1982). [20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
A petition that personal property of an absentee be set
aside as provided in this chapter may be filed by any of the
following persons:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A person in whose favor the personal property of the
absentee may be set aside.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A person to whom the absentee has issued a general
power of attorney while serving in the armed forces of the
United States or while an employee of any agency or
department of the United States, provided the power of
