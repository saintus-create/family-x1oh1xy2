# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2108

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Liquidation of community or quasi-community assets to avoid market or investment risks; authority of court At any time during the proceeding, the court has the authority, on application of a party and for good cause, to order the liquidation of community or quasi-community assets so as to avoid unreasonable market or investment risks, given the relative nature, scope, and extent of the community estate. 
However, in no event shall the court grant the application unless, as provided in this chapter, the appropri139 
§ 2110 ate declaration of disclosure has been served by the moving party.
