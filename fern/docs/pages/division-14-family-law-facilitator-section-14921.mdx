# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 14921

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Accreditation or approval required in order to provide adoption services in cases subject to the Convention 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In general Except as otherwise provided in this subchapter, no person may offer or provide adoption services in connection with a Convention adoption in the United States unless that person— 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  is accredited or approved in accordance with this subchapter; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  is providing such services through or under the supervision and responsibility of an accredited agency or approved person. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Exceptions Subsection (a) shall not apply to the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Background studies and home studies The performance of a background study on a child or a home study on a prospective adoptive parent, or any 1746 report on any such study by a social work professional or organization who is not providing any other adoption service in the case, if the background or home study is approved by an accredited agency. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Child welfare services The provision of a child welfare service by a person who is not providing any other adoption service in the case. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Legal services The provision of legal services by a person who is not providing any adoption service in the case. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Prospective adoptive parents acting on own behalf The conduct of a prospective adoptive parent on his or her own behalf in the case, to the extent not prohibited by the law of the State in which the prospective adoptive parent resides. 
(Pub.L. 106-279, Title IT, § 201, Oct. 6, 2000, 114 Stat. 830.)
