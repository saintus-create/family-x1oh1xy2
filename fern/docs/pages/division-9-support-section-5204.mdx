# § 5204 — “Due date of support payments” is the date specifically

“Due date of support payments” is the date specifically stated in the order of support or, if no date is stated in the support order, the last day of the month in which the support payment is to be paid. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
