# 547.7

The former reference to “a proceeding concerning

The former reference to “a proceeding concerning
the custody of, or visitation with, a minor” has been omitted.
This
section applies to a determination of custody or visitation in a
proceeding for dissolution,
nullity, or legal separation, and in
proceedings pursuant to the Domestic Violence Prevention Act and
the Uniform Parentage Act.
See Section 3021 (application of part).
See also Section 3011 (factors in determining best interest of child).
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Report 1 (1993)].
