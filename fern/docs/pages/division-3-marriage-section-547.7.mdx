# Marriage

## Section 547.7

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

The former reference to “a proceeding concerning the custody of, or visitation with, a minor” has been omitted. 
This section applies to a determination of custody or visitation in a proceeding for dissolution, nullity, or legal separation, and in proceedings pursuant to the Domestic Violence Prevention Act and the Uniform Parentage Act. 
See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3021.">§ 3021</a> (application of part). 
See also <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3011.">§ 3011</a> (factors in determining best interest of child). 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Report 1 (1993)].
