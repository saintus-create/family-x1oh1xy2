# Pilot Projects

## Section 21310

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Definitions As used in this part: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Contest” means a pleading filed with the court by a beneficiary that would result in a penalty under a no contest clause, if the no contest clause is enforced. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Direct contest” means a contest that alleges the invalidity of a protected instrument or one or more of its terms, based on one or more of the following grounds: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Forgery. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Lack of due execution. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Lack of capacity. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Menace, duress, fraud, or undue influence, 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Revocation of a will pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6120.">§ 6120</a>, revocation of a trust pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=15401.">§ 15401</a>, or revocation of an instrument other than a will or trust pursuant to the procedure for revocation that is provided by statute or by the instrument. 
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Disqualification of a beneficiary under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6112.">§ 6112</a>, 21350, or 21380. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “No contest clause” means a provision in an otherwise valid instrument that, if enforced, would penalize a beneficiary for filing a pleading in any court. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Pleading” means a petition, complaint, cross-complaint, objection, answer, response, or claim. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Protected instrument” means all of the following instruments: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The instrument that contains the no contest clause. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An instrument that is in existence on the date that the instrument containing the no contest clause is executed and is expressly identified in the no contest clause, either individually or as part of an identifiable class of instruments, as being governed by the no contest clause.
