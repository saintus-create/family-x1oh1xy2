# § 21310

*Definitions*

Definitions
As used in this part:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Contest” means a pleading filed with the court by a
beneficiary that would result in a penalty under a no contest
clause, if the no contest clause is enforced.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Direct contest” means
a contest that alleges the
invalidity of a protected instrument or one or more of its
terms, based on one or more of the following grounds:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Forgery.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Lack of due execution.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Lack of capacity.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Menace, duress, fraud, or undue influence,
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Revocation of a will pursuant to Section 6120, revoca-
tion of a trust pursuant to Section 15401, or revocation of an
instrument
other than a will or trust pursuant
to the
procedure for revocation that is provided by statute or by the
instrument.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Disqualification of a beneficiary under Section 6112,
21350, or 21380.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “No contest clause” means a provision in an otherwise
valid instrument that, if enforced, would penalize a beneficia-
ry for filing a pleading in any court.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Pleading” means a petition, complaint, cross-com-
plaint, objection, answer, response, or claim.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Protected instrument” means
all of the following
instruments:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The instrument that contains the no contest clause.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  An instrument that is in existence on the date that the
instrument containing the no contest clause is executed and is
expressly identified in the no contest clause, either individual-
ly or as part of an identifiable class of instruments, as being
governed by the no contest clause.
