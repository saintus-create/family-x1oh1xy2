<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For the purpose of ascertaining the facts mentioned or

may examine the applicants for a marriage license on oath at
the time of the application. The clerk shall reduce the
examination to writing and the applicants shall sign it.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If necessary, the clerk may request additional documentary proof as to the accuracy of the facts stated.

to state, for any purpose, their race or color.
