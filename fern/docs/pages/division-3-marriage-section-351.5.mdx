# § 351.5 — Certificate of registry and marriage license;

Certificate of registry and marriage license;
ad-
dress information
Notwithstanding subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 351 or 359 of
this code, or Section 103175 of the Health and Safety Code, if
either of the applicants for, or any witness to, a certificate of
registry of marriage and a marriage license requests, the
certificate of registry and the marriage license shall show the
business address or United States Postal Service post office
box for that applicant or witness instead of the residential
address of that person.
