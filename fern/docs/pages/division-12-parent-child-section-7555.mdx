# § 7555 — As a genetic

as a genetic
parent of the child and the identification is not successfully
challenged.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Admits parentage in a pleading, when making an
appearance,
or during a hearing, the court accepts the
admission, and the court determines that the person is
a genetic parent of the child.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Declines to submit to genetic testing ordered by the
court or a local child support agency, in which case, the court
may find that the person is a parent of the child even if the person denies a genetic relationship with the child.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Is in default after service of process and the court
determines the person to be a genetic parent of the child.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Is neither identified nor excluded as a genetic parent
by genetic testing and, based on other evidence, the court
determines the person to be a genetic parent of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If more than one person other than the woman who
gave birth asserts a claim under this division to be the child’s
parent, parentage shall be determined under provisions of
this division.
