# § 6387.

*Part 4 — Protective Orders And Other Domestic Violence Prevention Orders · Chapter 3 — Registration And Enforcement Of Orders*

The court shall order the clerk of the court to provide to a petitioner, without cost, up to three certified, stamped, and endorsed copies of any order issued under this part, and of an extension, modification, or termination of the order. (Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 572, Sec. 22. (AB 1596) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 28 of Ch. 572.)
