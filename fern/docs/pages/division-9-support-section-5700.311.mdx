# § 5700.311 — Pleadings and accompanying documents

Pleadings and accompanying documents
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a proceeding under this part, a petitioner seeking to
establish a support order, to determine parentage of a child,
or to register and modify a support order of a tribunal of
another state or a foreign country must file a petition.
Unless otherwise ordered under Section 5700.312, the petition or accompanying documents must provide, so far as
