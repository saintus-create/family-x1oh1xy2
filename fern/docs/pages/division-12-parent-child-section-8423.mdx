Trust company named as executor; sale to, merger
with, etc., another trust company
If the executor named in the will is a trust company that
has sold its business and assets to, has consolidated or merged
with, or is in any manner provided by law succeeded by,
another trust company, the court may, and to the extent
required by the Banking Law ( (commencing with
Section 99) of the Financial Code) shall, appoint the succes-
sor trust company as executor.
