(a) As used in this section, the following definitions apply: (1) "Abuse" means any of the following: (A) Physical injury or assault. (B) Sexual assault. (C) Coercive control, which is a pattern of behavior that in purpose or effect unreasonably interferes with a person's free will and personal liberty. (D) Stalking. (E) Domestic violence, as defined in Section 6211. (b) Abuse is not limited to the actual infliction of physical injury or assault. (Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 30, Sec. 1. (SB 1141) Effective January 1, 2021.)
