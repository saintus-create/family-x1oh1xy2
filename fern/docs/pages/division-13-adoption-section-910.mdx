# 910 — Section 902 continues former Civil Code Section 5120.030 without

Adoption

Section 902 continues former Civil Code Section 5120.030 without
change.
For background on former Civ. Code § 5120.030, see
Recommendation Relating to Liability of Marital Property for Debts, 17
Cal. L. Revision Comm’n Reports 1 (1984). [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.
Reports 1 (1993)].
Treatises and Practice Aids
11 Witkin, California Summary 10th Community
(2018), Nature and Scope of Revision.
Community estate; liability for debts
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise expressly provided by statute, the
community estate is liable for a debt incurred by either
spouse before or during marriage, regardless of which spouse
has the management and control of the property and
regardless of whether one or both spouses are parties to the
debt or to a judgment for the debt.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “During marriage” for purposes of this section does
not include the period after the date of separation, as defined
in Section 70, and before a judgment of dissolution of
marriage or legal separation of the parties. (Stats.1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by
Stats.2016, c. 114 (S.B.1255), § 3, eff. Jan. 1, 2017.)
