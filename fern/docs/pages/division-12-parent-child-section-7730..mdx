# § 7730.

*Part 3 — Uniform Parentage Act · Chapter 6 — Protective And Restraining Orders*

A judgment entered in a proceeding under this part may include a protective order as defined in Section 6218 and any other restraining order as provided in Article 3 (commencing with Section 6360) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of Division 10. (Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 179.6. Effective January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
