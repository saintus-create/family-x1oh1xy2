# 5206
“Earnings,” to the extent that they are subject to an earnings assignment order for support under Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 703.010) of of Title 9 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, include:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Wages, salary, bonus, money, and benefits described in Sections 704.110, 704.113, and 704.115 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Payments due for services of independent contractors, interest, dividends, rents, royalties, residuals, patent rights, or mineral or other natural resource rights.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Payments or credits due or becoming due as a result of written or oral contracts for services or sales whether denominated as wages, salary, commission, bonus, or otherwise.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Payments due for workers’ compensation temporary disability benefits.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Payments due as a result of disability from benefits described in Section 704.130 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Any other payments or credits due or becoming due, regardless of source.
