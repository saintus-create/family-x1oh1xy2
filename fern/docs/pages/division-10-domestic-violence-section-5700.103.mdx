# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.103

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

State tribunal and state enforcement agency 
(a) The superior court is the tribunal of this state. 
(b) The Department of Child Support Services is the support enforcement agency of this state.
