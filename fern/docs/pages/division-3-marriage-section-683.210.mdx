# Marriage

## Section 683.210

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Stay of enforcement A judgment may be renewed notwithstanding any stay of enforcement of the judgment, but the renewal of the judgment does not affect the stay of enforcement.
