# § 2300.

*Part 3 — Dissolution Of Marriage And Legal Separation · Chapter 1 — Effect Of Dissolution*

The effect of a judgment of dissolution of marriage when it becomes final is to restore the parties to the state of unmarried persons. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
