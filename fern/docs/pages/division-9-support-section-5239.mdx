# 5239. Arrearages of support payments; computation

*Support*

Arrearages of support payments; computation
Arrearages of support payments shall be computed on the
basis of the payments owed and unpaid on the date that the
obligor has been given notice of the assignment order as
required by Section 5234. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
$ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
