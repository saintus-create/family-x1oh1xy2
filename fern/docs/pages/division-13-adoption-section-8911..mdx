# § 8911.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 4 — Intercountry Adoptions*

As a condition of placement, the prospective adoptive parents shall file a petition to adopt the child under Section 8912 within 30 days of placement. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
