# § 911

*(a) The earnings of a married person during marriage are*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The earnings of a married person during marriage are
not liable for a debt incurred by the person’s spouse before
marriage. After the earnings of the married person are paid,
they remain not liable so long as they are held in a deposit
account in which the person’s spouse has no right of
withdrawal and are uncommingled with other property in the
community estate, except property insignificant in amount.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  As used in this section:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Deposit account” has the meaning prescribed in
paragraph <span class="subsec">(29)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 9102 of the
Commercial Code.

without substantive change. The term “community estate” has been
substituted for “community property.” This is not a substantive
change. See Section 63 (“community estate” defined) & Comment.

5120.110(c) is not continued in this section because it was not
