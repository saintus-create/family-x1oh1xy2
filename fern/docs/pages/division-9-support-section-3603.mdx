# Support

## Section 3603

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Time for modification or termination of orders; exceptions An order made pursuant to this chapter may be modified or terminated at any time except as to an amount that accrued before the date of the filing of the notice of motion or order to show cause to modify or terminate. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
