# § 3603 — Time for modification or termination of orders;

Time for modification or termination of orders;
exceptions
An order made pursuant to this chapter may be modified
or terminated at any time except as to an amount that
accrued before the date of the filing of the notice of motion
or order to show cause to modify or terminate.
(Stats. 1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
