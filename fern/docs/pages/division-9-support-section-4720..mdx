# § 4720.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 5 — Civil Penalty For Child Support Delinquency*

“Support” for the purposes of this chapter means support as defined in Section 150. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
