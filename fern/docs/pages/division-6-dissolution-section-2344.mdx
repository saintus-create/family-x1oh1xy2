# § 2344 — Death of party after entry of judgment

Death of party after entry of judgment
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The death of either party after entry of the judgment
does not prevent the judgment from becoming a final
judgment under Sections 2339 to 2343, inclusive.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  does not validate a marriage by either
party before the judgment becomes final, nor does it consti-
tute a defense in a criminal prosecution against either party.
