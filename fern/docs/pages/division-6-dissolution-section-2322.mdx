# 2322. Separate domicile or residence

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

Separate domicile or residence
For the purpose of a proceeding for dissolution of mar-
riage, each spouse may have a separate domicile or residence
depending upon proof of the fact and not upon legal
presumptions.
