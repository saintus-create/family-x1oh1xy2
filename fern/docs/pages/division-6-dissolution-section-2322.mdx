# § 2322 — Separate domicile or residence

For the purpose of a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, each spouse may have a separate domicile or residence depending upon proof of the fact and not upon legal presumptions. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 27. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
