---
title: "2322. Separate domicile or residence"
description: "Separate domicile or residence"
slug: division-6-dissolution-section-2322
---

For the purpose of a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, each spouse may have a separate domicile or residence depending upon proof of the fact and not upon legal presumptions. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 27. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.*
