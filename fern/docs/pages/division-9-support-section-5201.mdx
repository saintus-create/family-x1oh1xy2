# § 5201

“Arrearage” or “arrearages” is the amount necessary to satisfy a support judgment or order pursuant to Section 695.210 of the Code of Civil Procedure. _(Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 599, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1998.)_
