# 5201. “Arrearage” or “arrearages” is the amount necessary to

*Support*

“Arrearage” or “arrearages” is the amount necessary to
satisfy a support judgment or order pursuant to Section
695.210 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

1997, c. 599 (A.B.573), § 21.)

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Payments due as a result of disability from benefits

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Any other payments or credits due or becoming due,
regardless of source.
