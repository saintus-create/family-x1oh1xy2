# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5230.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Earning assignment or income withholding order of another state; enforceability; applicable law 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An earnings assignment or income withholding order for support issued by a court or administrative agency of another state is binding upon an employer of the obligor to the same extent as an earnings assignment order made by a court of this state. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  When an employer receives an earnings assignment order or an income withholding order for support from a court or administrative agency in another state, all of the provisions of this chapter shall apply.
