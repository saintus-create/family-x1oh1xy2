# § 11478.1 — And child abduction records; authorized disclosure of

and child abduction records; authorized disclosure of
information; definitions; violations
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  It is the intent of the Legislature to protect individual
rights of privacy, and to facilitate and enhance the effectiveness of the child and spousal support enforcement program,
by ensuring the confidentiality of support enforcement and
child abduction records, and to thereby encourage the full
and frank disclosure of information relevant to all of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The establishment or maintenance of parent and child
relationships and support obligations.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The enforcement of the child support liability of absent
clerical, medical, mental health, or other professional from
whom the applicant or recipient has sought assistance in
dealing with abuse, physical evidence of abuse, or any other
evidence that supports the claim of good cause.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Statements under penalty of perjury from individuals,
including the applicant or recipient, with knowledge of the
circumstances that provide the basis for the good cause claim.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Birth certificates or medical, mental health, rape crisis,
domestic violence program, or law enforcement records that
indicate that the child was conceived as the result of incest or
rape.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Court documents or other records that indicate that
legal proceedings for adoption are pending before a court of
competent jurisdiction.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  A written statement from a public or licensed private
adoption agency that the applicant or recipient is being
assisted by the agency to resolve the issue of whether to keep
the child or relinquish the child for adoption.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A sworn statement by a victim shall be sufficient to
establish abuse unless the agency documents in writing an
independent, reasonable basis to find the recipient not
credible.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Applicants or recipients who inquire about or claim
good cause, or otherwise indicate that they or their children
are at risk of abuse, shall be given referrals by the county
welfare department to appropriate community, legal, medical, and support services. Followup by the applicant or
recipient on those referrals shall not affect eligibility for
assistance under this chapter or the determination of cooperation. (Added by Stats.1997, c. 270 (A.B.1542), § 151,. eff:
