## Parent and Child Relationship

  <h3>Presumption — child of wife cohabiting with husband</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 7540</Badge></div>
        <p>A child born to a wife cohabiting with her husband is conclusively presumed to be a child of the marriage. This presumption applies unless a court determines otherwise under § 7541.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Definitions — Uniform Parentage Act</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 7610</Badge></div>
        <p>Defines key terms: "adjudicated child" means a child declared to be a child of a parent by judgment. Provides the framework for establishing parent and child relationships.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Voluntary declaration of parentage</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 7660</Badge></div>
        <p>A voluntary declaration of parentage is a form prescribed by the Judicial Council that, when signed, has the same force and effect as a judgment of parentage.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
