---
title: "3085. Grant of joint legal custody without joint physical"
description: "Grant of joint legal custody without joint physical"
slug: division-8-custody-section-3085
---

In making an order for custody with respect to both parents, the court may grant joint legal custody without granting joint physical custody.

> *Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.72. Effective January 1, 1994.*
