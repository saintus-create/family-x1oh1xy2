# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 14943

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Authorization of appropriations; collection of fees 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Authorization of appropriations 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to agencies of the Federal Government implementing the Convention and the provisions of this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Availability of funds Amounts appropriated pursuant to paragraph (1) are authorized to remain available until expended. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Assessment of fees 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The Secretary may charge a fee for new or enhanced services that will be undertaken by the Department of State to meet the requirements of this chapter with respect to intercountry adoptions under the Convention and comparable services with respect to other intercountry adoptions. 
Such fee shall be prescribed by regulation and shall not exceed the cost of such services. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Fees collected under paragraph (1) shall be retained and deposited as an offsetting collection to any Department of State appropriation to recover the costs of providing such services. 
Such fees shall remain available for obligation until expended. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Repealed.
