# § 14943 — Authorization

Authorization
of appropriations;
collection
of
fees
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Authorization of appropriations
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  In general
There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as
may be necessary to agencies of the Federal Govern-
ment implementing the Convention and the provisions
of this chapter.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Availability of funds
Amounts appropriated pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  are
authorized to remain available until expended.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Assessment of fees
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The Secretary may charge a fee for new or
enhanced services that will be undertaken by the De-
partment of State to meet the requirements of this
chapter with respect to intercountry adoptions under
the Convention and comparable services with respect to
other intercountry adoptions.
Such fee shall be pre-
scribed by regulation and shall not exceed the cost of
such services.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Fees collected under paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  shall be re-
tained and deposited as an offsetting collection to any
Department of State appropriation to recover the costs
of providing such services.
Such fees shall remain
available for obligation until expended.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Repealed.
Pub.L. 112-276, § 3(a), Jan. 14, 2013, 126
Stat. 2467
(Pub.L. 106-279, Title IV, § 403, Oct. 6, 2000, 114 Stat. 841;
Pub.L. 107-228, Div. A, Title II, § 211(a), Sept. 30, 2002, 116
Stat. 1365; Pub.L. 112-276, § 3(a), Jan. 14, 2013, 126 Stat.
2467.)
