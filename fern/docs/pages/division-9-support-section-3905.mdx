Transfer authorized by will or trust 
(a) A personal representative or trustee may make an irrevocable transfer pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3909.">Section 3909</a> to a custodian for the benefit of a minor as authorized in the governing will or trust. 
(b) If the testator or settlor has nominated a custodian under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3903.">Section 3903</a> to receive the custodial property, the transfer shall be made to that person.
