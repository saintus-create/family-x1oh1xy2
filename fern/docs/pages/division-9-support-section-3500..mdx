# § 3500.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions in this chapter govern the construction of this division. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
