# § 6240 — As used in this part:

As used in this part:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Judicial officer” means a judge, commissioner, or referee designated under Section 6241.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Law enforcement officer” means one of the following officers who requests or enforces an emergency protective order under this part:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A police officer.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A sheriff’s officer.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A peace officer of the Department of the California Highway Patrol.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A peace officer of the University of California Police Department.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  A peace officer of the California State University and College Police Departments.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  A peace officer of the Department of Parks and Recreation, as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(f)</span>  of Section 830.2 of the Penal Code.

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  A peace officer of the Department of General Services of the City of Los Angeles, as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 830.31 of the Penal Code.

<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  A housing authority patrol officer, as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of Section 830.31 of the Penal Code.

<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  A peace officer for a district attorney, as defined in Section 830.1 or 830.35 of the Penal Code.

<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  A parole officer, probation officer, or deputy probation officer, as defined in Section 830.5 of the Penal Code.

<span class="subsec">(11)</span>  A peace officer of a California Community College police department, as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 830.32.

<span class="subsec">(12)</span>  A peace officer employed by a police department of a school district, as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 830.32.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Abduct” means take, entice away, keep, withhold, or conceal.
