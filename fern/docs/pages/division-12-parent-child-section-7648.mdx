# 7648 — Denial of motion to set aside or vacate judgment

Parent and Child Relationship

Denial of motion to set aside or vacate judgment
establishing parentage; factors
The court may deny the motion to set aside or vacate a
judgment establishing parentage if it determines that denial

UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT
of the motion is in the best interest of the child, after
consideration of the following factors:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The age of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The length of time since the entry of the judgment
establishing parentage.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The nature, duration, and quality of any relationship
between the previously established father and the child,
including the duration and frequency of any time periods
during which the child and the previously established father
resided in the same household or enjoyed a parent and child
relationship.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The request of the previously established father that
the parent and child relationship continue.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Notice by the biological father of the child that the biological father does not oppose preservation of the
relationship between the previously established father and
the child.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  The benefit or detriment to the child in establishing the
genetic father as the parent of the child.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Whether the conduct of the previously established
father has impaired the ability to ascertain the identity of, or
get support from, the biological father.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Additional factors deemed by the court to be relevant
to its determination of the best interest of the child.
