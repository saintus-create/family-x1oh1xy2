# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 984

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Proceeding between spouses There is no privilege under this article in: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding brought by or on behalf of one spouse against the other spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A proceeding between a surviving spouse and a person who claims through the deceased spouse, regardless of whether such claim is by testate or intestate succession or by inter vivos transaction.
