# § 4413.

*Part 4 — Support Of Parents · Chapter 2 — Relief From Duty To Support Parent Who Abandoned Child*

If the parent is a resident of this state, the court does not have jurisdiction to make an order under this chapter until 30 days after the county counsel, or the district attorney in a county not having a county counsel, of the county in which the parent resides has been served with notice of the pendency of the proceeding. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
