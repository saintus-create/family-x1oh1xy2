# 236.1. Not limited to, those crimes described in Sections 240, 242,

*Marriage*

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Sexual assault, spousal abuse, or both, include, but are
not limited to, those crimes described in Sections 240, 242,
243.4, 261, 261.5, 262, 264.1, 266, 266a, 266b, 266c, 266f,
273.5, 273.6, 285, 288, and 289.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section shall apply to confidential
communications between an attorney and his or her client.
