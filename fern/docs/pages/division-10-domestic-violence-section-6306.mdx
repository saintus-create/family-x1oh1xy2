<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Prior to a hearing on the issuance or denial of an order
under this part, the court shall ensure that a search is or has
been conducted to determine if the subject of the proposed
order has a prior criminal conviction for a violent felony
specified in Section 667.5 of the Penal Code or a serious
felony specified in Section 1192.7 of the Penal Code; has a
misdemeanor conviction involving domestic violence, weapons, or other violence; has an outstanding warrant; is
currently on parole or probation; has a registered firearm; or
has a prior restraming order or a violation of a prior
restraining order. The search shall be conducted of all
records and databases readily available and reasonably accessible to the court, including, but not limited to, the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The California Sex and Arson Registry (CSAR).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The Supervised Release File.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  State summary criminal history information maintained by the Department of Justice pursuant to Section
11105 of the Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The Federal Bureau of Investigation’s nationwide
database.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Locally maintained criminal history records or databases.
However, a record or database need not be searched if the
information available in that record or database can be
obtained as a result of a search conducted in another record
or database.
