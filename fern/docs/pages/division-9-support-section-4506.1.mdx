# Support

## Section 4506.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

The notice of support judgment shall have the same force and effect as an abstract of judgment certified by the clerk of the court where the judgment was entered. 
The local child support agency or other Title [V-D agency shall not be subject to any civil liability as a consequence of causing a notice of support judgment to be recorded. 
(d) As used in this section, “judgment” includes an order for child, family, or spousal support.
