# § 4506.1 — The notice of support judgment shall

The notice of support judgment shall
have the same force and effect as an abstract of judgment
certified by the clerk of the court where the judgment was
entered.
The local child support agency or other Title [V-D
agency
shall
not be subject to any civil
liability
as
a
consequence of causing a notice of support judgment to be
recorded.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  As used in this section, “judgment” includes an order
for child, family, or spousal support.
