# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2313

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Support of spouse lacking legal capacity to make decisions No dissolution of marriage granted on the ground of permanent legal incapacity to make decisions relieves a 

§ 2313 spouse from any obligation imposed by law as a result of the marriage for the support of the spouse who lacks legal capacity to make decisions, and the court may make an order for support, or require a bond therefor, as the circumstances require.
