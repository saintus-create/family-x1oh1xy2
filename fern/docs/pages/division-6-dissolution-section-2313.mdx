# 2313 — Support of spouse lacking legal capacity to make

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

Support of spouse lacking legal capacity to make
decisions
No dissolution of marriage granted on the ground of
permanent legal incapacity to make decisions relieves a

spouse from any obligation imposed by law as a result of the
marriage for the support of the spouse who lacks legal
capacity to make decisions, and the court may make an order
for support, or require a bond therefor, as the circumstances
require.
