# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6250.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Valid orders An emergency protective order is valid only if it is issued by a judicial officer after making the findings required by <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6251.">Section 6251</a> and pursuant to a specific request by_a law enforcement officer.
