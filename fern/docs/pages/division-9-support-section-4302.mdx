# § 4302

*A person is not liable for support of the person’s spouse*

A person is not liable for support of the person’s spouse
when the person is living separate from the spouse by
agreement unless support is stipulated in the agreement.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
