# § 16167

*(c) Information obtained by the office from a complaint,*

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Information obtained by the office from a complaint,
regardless of whether it is investigated by the office, referred
to another entity for investigation, or determined not to be
the proper subject of an investigation, shall remain confidential under relevant state and federal confidentiality laws.
Disclosure of information shall occur only as necessary to
carry out the mission of the office and as permitted by law.
Toll-free number

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Social workers shall provide foster children with the
toll-free number for the office and verbal or written information regarding the existence and purpose of the office.

1998.)
