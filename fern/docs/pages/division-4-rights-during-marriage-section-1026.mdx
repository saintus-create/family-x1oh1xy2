# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1026

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Exception: Required report There is no privilege under this article as to information that the psychotherapist or the patient is required to report to a public employee or as to information required to be 968 recorded in a public office, if such report or record is open to public inspection.
