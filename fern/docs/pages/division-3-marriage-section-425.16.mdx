# 425.16

Anti-SLAPP motion

Anti-SLAPP motion
motion to strike;
remedies.
limitations
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Legislature finds and declares that there has been
a disturbing increase in lawsuits brought primarily to chill the
valid exercise of the constitutional rights of freedom of
speech and petition for the redress of grievances.
The
Legislature finds and declares that it is in the public interest
to encourage continued participation in matters of public
significance, and that this participation should not be chilled
through abuse of the judicial process. To this end, this
section shall be construed broadly.
