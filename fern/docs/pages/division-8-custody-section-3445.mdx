# § 3445 — Registration of out-of-state custody determination;

Registration of out-of-state custody determination;
method; duties of court; notice; contesting validity of
registration; confirmation of registered order
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A child custody determination issued by a court of
another state may be registered in this state, with or without a
simultaneous request for enforcement, by sending all of the
following to the appropriate court in this state:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A letter or other document requesting registration.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Two copies, including one
certified
copy, of the
determination sought to be registered, and a statement under
penalty of perjury that to the best of the knowledge and belief
of the person seeking registration the order has not been
modified.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in Section 3429, the
name and address of the person seeking registration and any
parent or person acting as a parent who has been awarded
custody or visitation in the child custody determination
sought to be registered.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  On receipt of the documents required by subdivision
(a), the registering court shall do both of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Cause the determination
to be filed as a foreign
judgment, together with one copy of any accompanying
documents and information, regardless of their form.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Serve notice upon the persons named pursuant to
paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and provide them with an
opportunity to contest the registration in accordance with this
section.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The notice required by paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span> 
shall state all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  That a registered determination is enforceable as of
the date of the registration
in the same manner
as a
determination issued by a court of this state.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  That a hearing to contest the validity of the registered
determination must be requested within 20 days after service
of the notice.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  That failure to contest the registration will result in
confirmation of the child custody determination and preclude
further contest of that determination with respect to any
matter that could have been asserted.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A person seeking to contest the validity of a registered
order must request a hearing within 20 days after service of
the notice.
At that hearing, the court shall confirm the
registered order unless the person contesting registration
establishes any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  That the issuing court did not have jurisdiction under
Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 3421).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  That the child custody determination sought to be
registered has been vacated, stayed, or modified by a court
having jurisdiction to do so under Chapter 2 (commencing
with Section 3421).

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  That the person contesting registration was entitled to
notice, but notice was not given in accordance with the
standards of Section 3408, in the proceedings before the
court that issued the order for which registration is sought.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  If a timely request for a hearing to contest the validity
of the registration is not made, the registration is confirmed
as a matter of law and the person requesting registration and
all persons served shall be notified of the confirmation.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Confirmation of a registered order, whether by opera-
tion of law or after notice and hearing, precludes further
contest of the order with respect to any matter that could
have been asserted at the time of registration.
