---
title: "5020. a provision for a nonprobate transfer of community"
description: "A provision for a nonprobate transfer of community"
slug: division-9-support-section-5020
---

A provision for a nonprobate transfer of community
property on death executed by a married person without the
written consent of the person’s spouse (1)  is not effective as
to the nonconsenting spouse’s interest in the property and (2) 
does not affect the nonconsenting spouse’s disposition on
death of the nonconsenting spouse’s interest in the community property by will, intestate succession, or nonprobate
transfer.
1992, c. 51 (A.B.1719), 6.)
