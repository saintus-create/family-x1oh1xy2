# General Provisions

## Section 344

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Balance upon mutual, open and current account; accrual of cause In an action brought to recover a balance due upon a mutual, open, and current account, where there have been reciprocal demands between the parties, the cause of action is deemed to have accrued from the time of the last item proved in the account on either side. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
