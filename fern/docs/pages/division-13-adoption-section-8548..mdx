# § 8548.

*Part 1 — Definitions*

“Stepparent adoption” means an adoption of a child by a stepparent where one birth parent retains custody and control of the child. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
