# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7551.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Genetic test facilitation for enforcement purposes All hospitals, local child support agencies, welfare offices, and family courts shall facilitate genetic tests for purposes of enforcement of this chapter. 
This may include having a health care professional available for purposes of extracting samples to be used for genetic testing.
