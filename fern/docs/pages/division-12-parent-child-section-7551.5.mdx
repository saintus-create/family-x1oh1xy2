# § 7551.5 — Genetic test facilitation for enforcement purposes

Genetic test facilitation for enforcement purposes
All hospitals, local child support agencies, welfare offices,
and family courts shall facilitate genetic tests for purposes of
enforcement of this chapter.
This may include having a
health care professional available for purposes of extracting
samples to be used for genetic testing.
