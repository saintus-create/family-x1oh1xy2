# § 234 — Ex parte orders; admissibility as evidence

The automatic granting of the ex parte temporary restraining order under this part is not a court determination or competent evidence in any proceeding of any prior history of the conduct so proscribed occurring between the parties. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
