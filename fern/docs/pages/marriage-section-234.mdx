# General Provisions

## Section 234

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Ex parte orders; admissibility as evidence The automatic granting of the ex parte temporary restraining order under this part is not a court determination or competent evidence in any proceeding of any prior history of the conduct so proscribed occurring between the parties.
