# § 234 — Ex parte orders; admissibility as evidence

Ex parte orders; admissibility as evidence
The automatic granting of the ex parte temporary restrain-
ing order under this part is not a court determination or
competent evidence in any proceeding of any prior history of
the conduct so proscribed occurring between the parties.
