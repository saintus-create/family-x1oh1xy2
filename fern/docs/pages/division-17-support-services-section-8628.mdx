# § 8628 — Prospective adoptive parents

prospective adoptive parents
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Notice that for a period of 72 hours after signing the
contract any party may revoke the contract, and if a fee has
been paid by the prospective adoptive parents, they may,
within that 72-hour period, request the return of the fees
paid, except for any reasonable fee actually earned by the
facilitator that is supported by written record or documentation. (Added by Stats.1996, c. 1135 (S.B.2035), § 1.)
report to
An adoption facilitator shall report in writing to the
prospective adoptive parents all information that is provided
to the facilitator by the birthparents concerning a particular
child. (Added by Stats.1996, c. 1135 (S.B.2035), § 1.)
