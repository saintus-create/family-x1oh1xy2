<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  To “intimidate” means to place a person in reasonable

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  “Nonviolent” means conduct that would not constitute
a crime of violence.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  “Physical obstruction” means rendering ingress to or
egress from a facility impassable to another person, or
rendering passage to or from a facility unreasonably difficult
or hazardous to another person.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A person aggrieved by a violation of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span> 
may bring a civil action to enjoin the violation, for compensa-
tory and punitive damages, for injunctive relief, and for the
cost of suit and reasonable attorney’s and expert witness’ fees.
With respect to compensatory damages, the plaintiff may
elect, at any time prior to the rendering of a final judgment,
to recover, in lieu of actual damages, an award of statutory
damages in the amount of five thousand dollars ($5,000) per
violation
of paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision
(a), and) one
thousand dollars ($1,000) per violation of paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of
subdivision (a).
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The Attorney General, a district attorney, or a city
attorney may bring a civil action to enjoin a violation of
subdivision (a), for compensatory damages to persons or
entities aggrieved by the violation, and for the imposition of a
civil penalty against each respondent.
The civil penalty for a
violation of paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall not exceed

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  This section shall not be construed to impair the right
to engage in any constitutionally protected activity, including,
but not limited to, speech, protest, or assembly.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  The adoption of the act that added this section is an
exercise of the police power of the state for purposes of
protecting the health, safety, and welfare of the people of
California, and this section shall be liberally construed to
effectuate that purpose.
