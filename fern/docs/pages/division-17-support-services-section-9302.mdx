# 9302. Consent of spouse of proposed adoptee; consent of

*Support Services*

Consent of spouse of proposed adoptee; consent of
others not required
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A married person who is not lawfully separated from
the person’s spouse may not be adopted without the consent
of the spouse, provided that the spouse is capable of giving
that consent.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The consent of the parents of the proposed adoptee, of
the department, or of any other person is not required.
