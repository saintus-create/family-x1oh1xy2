# § 3654

*Statement of decision*

Statement of decision
At the. request of either party, an order modifying,
terminating, or setting aside a support order shall include a
statement of decision. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.1999, c. 653
(A.B.380), § 5.)
