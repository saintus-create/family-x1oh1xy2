# § 6409 — Application of Part

Application of Part
This part applies to protection orders issued before Janu-
ary 1, 2002, and to continuing actions for enforcement of
foreign protection orders commenced before January 1, 2002.
A request for enforcement of a foreign protection order
made on or after January 1, 2002, for violations of a foreign
protection order occurring before January 1, 2002, is gov-
erned by this part.
