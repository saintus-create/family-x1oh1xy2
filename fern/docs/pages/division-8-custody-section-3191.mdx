# 3191. ,

*Custody of Children*

,
Ex Parte Communications.
,
Order to Participate in Counseling.
Goals of outpatient counseling
The counseling pursuant to this chapter shall be specifically
designed to facilitate communication between the parties
regarding their minor child’s best interest, to reduce conflict
regarding custody or visitation, and to improve the quality of
parenting skills of each parent.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.
2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
