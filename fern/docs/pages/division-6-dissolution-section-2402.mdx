# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2402

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Revocation of joint petition; termination of proceedings; notice; filing; copy to other party 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  At any time before the filing of application for judgment pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2403.">§ 2403</a>, either party to the marriage may revoke the joint petition and thereby terminate the summary dissolution proceeding filed pursuant to this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The revocation shall be effected by filing with the clerk of the court where the proceeding was commenced a notice of revocation in such form and content as shall be prescribed by the Judicial Council. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The revoking party shall send a copy of the notice of revocation to the other party by first-class mail, postage prepaid, at the other party’s last known address. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
