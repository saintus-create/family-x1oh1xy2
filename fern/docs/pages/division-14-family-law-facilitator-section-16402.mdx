# 16402

Business

business
reasonable charge, covering the costs of labor and material,
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  On demand, any other information concerning the
partnership’s business and affairs, except to the extent the
demand or the information demanded is unreasonable or
otherwise improper under the circumstances.
1996, c. 1003 (A.B.583), 2. Amended by Stats.2004, c.
254 (S.B.1306), 45.)
