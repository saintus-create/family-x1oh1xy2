# 2123. Restrictions

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

Restrictions
on grounds
for relief;
judgments
inequitable
Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, or any
other law, a judgment may not be set aside simply because the
court finds that it was inequitable when made, nor simply
because subsequent circumstances caused the division of
assets or liabilities to become inequitable, or the support to
become inadequate.
