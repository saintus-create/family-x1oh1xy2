# 953. Holder of the privilege

*Adoption*

Holder of the privilege
As used in this article, “holder of the privilege” means:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The client, if the client has no guardian or conservator.
(b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A guardian or conservator of the client, if the client
has a guardian or conservator, except as provided in para-
graph (2).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the guardian
or conservator
has an actual
or
apparent conflict of interest with the client, then the guardian
or conservator does not hold the privilege.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The personal representative of the client if the client is
dead, including a personal representative appointed pursuant
to Section 12252 of the Probate Code.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A successor,
assign, trustee
in dissolution,
or any
similar representative of a firm, association, organization,
partnership, business trust, corporation, or public entity that
is no longer in existence. . (Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2, operative
Jan. 1, 1967.
Amended by Stats.2009, c. 8 (A.B.1163), § 1;
c. 475 (A.B.1290), § 1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019.)
