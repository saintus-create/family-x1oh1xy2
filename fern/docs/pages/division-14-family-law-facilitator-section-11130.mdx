<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  As used in this section, “act in furtherance of a
person’s right of petition or free speech under the United
States or California Constitution in connection with a public
issue” includes: <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  any written or oral statement or writing
made before a legislative, executive, or judicial proceeding, or
any other official proceeding authorized by law, <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  any
written or oral statement or writing made in connection with
an issue under consideration
or review by a legislative,
executive, or judicial body, or any other official proceeding
authorized by law, <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  any written or oral statement or writing
made in a place open to the public or a public forum in
connection with an issue of public interest, or <span class="subsec">(4)</span>  any other
conduct in furtherance of the exercise of the constitutional
right of petition or the constitutional right of free speech in
connection with a public issue or an issue of public interest.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  The special motion may be filed within 60 days of the

later time upon terms it deems proper.

conditions of the court require a later hearing.
