# 2061 — Service of documents by party requesting joinder

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

Service of documents by party requesting joinder
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The party requesting joinder shall serve all of the
following upon the employee benefit plan:
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 2060 continues the first sentence of
former Civil Code Section 4363.1(a) without substantive change.
The reference to a proceeding under “this part,” meaning the former
Family Law Act (former (commencing with former Section
4000) of of the Civil Code), has been omitted as surplus.
See Section 2000 (application to dissolution, nullity, or legal separation proceedings).
Subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  continues the last part of former Civil Code
Section 4351 without substantive change. For an exception to this
general rule, see Section 5103 (support order enforceable against
employee pension benefit plan regardless of whether joined as party).
See also Sections 80 (“employee pension benefit plan” defined), 755
(discharge of employee benefit plan from adverse claims). [23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
