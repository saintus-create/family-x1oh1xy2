# § 3161 — Purpose of mediation proceedings

Purpose of mediation proceedings
The purposes of a mediation proceeding are as follows:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  To reduce acrimony that may exist between the parties.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  To.develop an agreement assuring the child close and
continuing contact with both parents that is in the best
interest of the child, consistent with Sections 3011 and 3020.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  To effect a settlement of the issue of visitation rights of
all parties that is in the best interest of the child.
