# Custody of Children

## Section 3161

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Purpose of mediation proceedings The purposes of a mediation proceeding are as follows: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  To reduce acrimony that may exist between the parties. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  To.develop an agreement assuring the child close and continuing contact with both parents that is in the best interest of the child, consistent with Sections 3011 and 3020. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  To effect a settlement of the issue of visitation rights of all parties that is in the best interest of the child.
