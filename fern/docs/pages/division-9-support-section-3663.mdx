# § 3663 — Discovery requests; restrictions

In the absence of a pending motion for modification or termination of a support order, a request for discovery pursuant to this article may be undertaken not more frequently than once every 12 months. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
