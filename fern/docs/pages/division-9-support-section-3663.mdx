# Support

## Section 3663

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Discovery requests; restrictions In the absence of a pending motion for modification or termination of a support order, a request for discovery pursuant to this article may be undertaken not more frequently than once every 12 months.
