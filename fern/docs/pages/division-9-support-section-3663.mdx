# § 3663

*tax*

tax
Discovery requests; restrictions
In the absence of a pending motion for modification or
termination of a support order, a request for discovery
pursuant to this article may be undertaken not more frequently than once every 12 months.

(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
