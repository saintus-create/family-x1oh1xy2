# General Provisions

## Section 241.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

The suspension shall be in effect while the child is a ward of the court. 
If the jurisdiction established pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=601.">§ 601</a> or 602 is 
§ 368 terminated without the need for continued dependency proceedings concerning the child, the juvenile court shall terminate the child’s dual status. 
If the termination of the <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=601.">§ 601</a> or 602 jurisdiction is likely and reunification of the child with his or her parent or guardian would be detrimental to the child, the county probation department and child welfare services department shall jointly assess and produce a recommendation regarding whether the court’s dependency jurisdiction shall be resumed.
