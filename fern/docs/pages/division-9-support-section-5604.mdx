# § 5604

*Effect of previous determination of paternity made*

Effect of previous determination of paternity made
by another state
A previous determination of paternity made by another
state, whether established through voluntary acknowledgment
procedures in effect in that state or through an administrative
or judicial process shall be given full faith and credit by the
courts in this state, and shall have the same effect as a
paternity determination made in this state and may be
enforced and satisfied in a like manner.
