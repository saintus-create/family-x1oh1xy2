Notification to Department of Motor Vehicles upon
voiding or rescission of declaration of emancipation;
invalidation of identification cards
If a declaration of emancipation is voided or rescinded,
notice shall be sent immediately to the Department of Motor
Vehicles which shall remove the information relating to
emancipation in its law enforcement computer network. Any
identification card issued stating emancipation shall be invali-
dated.
