# General Provisions

## Section 281

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

proof
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Every person having a spouse living, who marries or
enters into a registered domestic partnership with any other
person, except in the cases specified in Section 282, is guilty
of bigamy.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon a trial for bigamy, it is not necessary to prove
either of the marriages or registered domestic partnerships by
Oct. 1, 2011; Stats.2016, c. 50 (S.B.1005), § 72, eff: Jan. 1,
2017.)
