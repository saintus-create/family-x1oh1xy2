# Support

## Section 4566

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Notice of application; response Upon the filing of an application under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4565.">§ 4565</a> with the court and the service of the application upon the child support obligee and any other party to the proceedings, the court shall provide notice and opportunity for any party opposing the application to file responsive financial and other information setting forth the factual and legal bases for the party’s opposition.
