# § 4566 — Upon the filing of an application under Section 4565 with

Upon the filing of an application under Section 4565 with
the court and the service of the application upon the child
support obligee and any other party to the proceedings, the
court shall provide notice and opportunity for any party
opposing the application to file responsive financial and other
information setting forth the factual and legal bases for the
party’s opposition.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10,
Section 4563 continues former Civil Code Section 4710(c) without
substantive change. [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
