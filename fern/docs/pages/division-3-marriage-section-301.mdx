# § 301

*marriage*

marriage
capability to consent
to and consummate
Two unmarried persons 18 years of age or older, who are
not otherwise disqualified, are capable of consenting to and
consummating marriage.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),

VALIDITY
$ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended
(S.B.1306), § 3, eff. Jan. 1, 2015.)
by Stats.2014, c. 82
