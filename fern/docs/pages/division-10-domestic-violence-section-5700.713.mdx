# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.713

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Record in original language; English translation A record filed with a tribunal of this state under this chapter must be in the original language and, if not in English, must be accompanied. by an English translation.
