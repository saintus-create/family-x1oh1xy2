# § 7550.5

*The following definitions shall apply to this chapter:*

The following definitions shall apply to this chapter:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Combined
relationship index” means the product of
all tested relationship indices.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Ethnic or racial group” means, for the purpose of
genetic testing, a recognized group that a person identifies as
ancestry,
or part of the ancestry,
or that is
identified by other information.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Genetic testing” means any genetic testing that complies with Section 7552.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Hypothesized genetic relationship” means an asserted
genetic relationship between a person and a child.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  “Probability of parentage” means, for the ethnic or
racial group to which a person alleged to be a parent belongs,
the probability that a hypothesized genetic relationship is
supported, compared to the probability that a genetic relationship is supported between the child and a random person
of the ethnic or racial group used in the hypothesized genetic
relationship, expressed as a percentage incorporating the
combined relationship index and a prior probability.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  “Relationship index” means a likelihood ratio that
compares the probability of a genetic marker given a
hypothesized genetic relationship and the probability of the
genetic marker given a genetic relationship between the child
and a random person of the ethnic or racial group used in the
hypothesized genetic relationship. (Added by Stats.2018, c.
876 (A.B.2684), § 10, eff: Jan. 1, 2019.)
