# Minors

## Section 6800

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Failure to leave person to take by testate or intestate succession; escheat. 
Property distributable from trusts; benefit plans; reversion to trust or fund from which distributable.
