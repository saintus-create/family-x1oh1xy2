# Pilot Projects

## Section 22653

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Status of nonmember spouse; limitation of rights and benefits 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonmember spouse who is awarded a separate account under this part pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=22652.">§ 22652</a> is not a member of the Defined Benefit Program based on that award. 
The nonmember spouse is entitled only to rights and benefits based on that award explicitly established by. this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall not be construed to limit any right arising from the account of a nonmember spouse under this part that exists because the nonmember spouse is or was 944 employed to perform creditable service subject to coverage by the Defined Benefit Program.
