# 22653. Status of nonmember spouse; limitation of rights

*Pilot Projects*

Status of nonmember spouse; limitation of rights
and benefits
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The nonmember spouse who is awarded a separate
account under this part pursuant to Section 22652 is not a
member of the Defined Benefit Program based on that
award.
The nonmember spouse is entitled only to rights and
benefits based on that award explicitly established by. this
chapter.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall not be construed to limit any right
arising from the account of a nonmember spouse under this
part that exists because the nonmember spouse is or was

EDUCATION CODE
employed to perform creditable service subject to coverage by
the Defined Benefit Program.
