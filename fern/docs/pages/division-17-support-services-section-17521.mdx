# Support Services

## Section 17521

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Order to show cause or notice of motion for judicial review of district attorney’s decision; appropriate court The order to show cause or notice of motion described in subdivision (j) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17520.">Section 17520</a> shall be filed and heard in the superior court.
