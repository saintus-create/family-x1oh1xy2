# § 17521 — Order to show cause or notice of motion for

The order to show cause or notice of motion described in subdivision (j) of Section 17520 shall be filed and heard in the superior court. _(Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 115. Effective January 1, 2003.)_
