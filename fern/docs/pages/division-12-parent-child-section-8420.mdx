---
title: "8420. The person named as executor in the decedent’s will has"
description: "The person named as executor in the decedent’s will has"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-8420
---

The person named as executor in the decedent’s will has
the right to appointment as personal representative. (Stats.
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
court.
