# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8420

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

The person named as executor in the decedent’s will has
the right to appointment as personal representative. (Stats.
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
court.
