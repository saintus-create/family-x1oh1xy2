# 996. Petition alleging the child was sexually abused by a parent. Karen P. v

*Adoption*

EVIDENCE
petition alleging the child was sexually abused by a parent. Karen P. v.
Superior Court, 200 Cal.App.4th 908, 133 Cal.Rptr.3d 67 (2011).
Patient-litigant exception
There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue concerning the condition of the
patient if such issue has been tendered by:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The patient;
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any party claiming through or under the patient;
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Any party claiming as a beneficiary of the patient
through a contract to which the patient is or was a party; or
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The plaintiff in an action brought under Section 376 or
377 of the Code of Civil Procedure for damages for the injury
or death of the patient. (Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan.
1, 1967.)
