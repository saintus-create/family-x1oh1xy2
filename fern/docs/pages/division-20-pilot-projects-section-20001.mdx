# § 20001

*Santa Clara and San Mateo Counties*

Santa Clara and San Mateo Counties
FAMILY LAW PILOT PROJECTS
Chapter
Section
1. General Provisions..................0...:...005 20000
2. San Mateo County Pilot Project................. 20010
3. Santa Clara County Pilot Project................ 20030
CHAPTER
1.
The Superior Courts of the Counties of Santa Clara and
San Mateo may conduct pilot projects pursuant to this part.
Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 20010) shall govern the
San Mateo County Pilot Project, and Chapter 3 (commencing
with Section 20030) shall govern the Santa Clara County Pilot
Project. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 210.)
