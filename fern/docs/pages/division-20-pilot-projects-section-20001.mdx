# Pilot Projects

## Section 20001

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Authorization of pilot projects; superior courts of Santa Clara and San Mateo Counties The Superior Courts of the Counties of Santa Clara and San Mateo may conduct pilot projects pursuant to this part. 
Chapter 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=20010.">§ 20010</a>) shall govern the San Mateo County Pilot Project, and Chapter 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=20030.">§ 20030</a>) shall govern the Santa Clara County Pilot Project.
