# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6381

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Enforceability of orders; receipt of copy by law enforcement agency; California Restraining and Protective Order System 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6380.">§ 6380</a> and subject to subdivision (b), an order issued under this part is enforceable in any place in this state. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An order issued under this part is not enforceable by a law enforcement agency of a political subdivision unless that law enforcement agency has received a copy of the order, or the officer enforcing the order has been shown a copy of the order or has obtained information, through the California Restraining and Protective Order System maintained by the Department of Justice, of the contents of the order, as described in subdivision (b). 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The data contained in the California Restraining and Protective Order System shall be deemed to be original, self-authenticating, documentary evidence of the court orders.
