# California Family Code § 7573.5

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A voluntary declaration of parentage is void if, at the time of signing, any of the following are true:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A person other than the woman who gave birth to the child or a person seeking to establish parentage through a voluntary declaration of parentage is a presumed parent under Section 7540 or subdivision (a), (b), or <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 7611.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A court has entered a judgment of parentage of the child.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Another person has signed a valid voluntary declaration of parentage.

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The child has a parent under Section 7613 or 7962 other than the signatories.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The person seeking to establish parentage is a donor pursuant to Section 7613.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The person seeking to establish parentage asserts that the person is a parent under Section 7613 and the child was not conceived through assisted reproduction.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In an action in which a party is seeking a determination that a voluntary declaration of parentage is void under this section, notice shall be provided pursuant to Section 7635.
