<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A final earnings withholding order for costs and

earnings withholding orders.

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A “withholding order for. support” is an earnings
withholding order issued on a writ of execution to collect
delinquent amounts payable under a judgment for the
support of a child, or spouse or former spouse, of the
judgment debtor. A withholding order for support shall be
denoted as such on its face.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Satisfaction of the amount stated as owing in a final
earnings withholding order for costs and interest is equivalent
to satisfaction of the money judgment. For this purpose,
interest ceases to accrue on the date of issuance of the final
earnings withholding order and no additional costs may be
added after that date, except for the statutory fee for service
of the order and any other statutory fees for performing
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The local child support agency may issue a withholding
order for support on a notice of levy pursuant to Section
17522 of the Family Code to collect a support obligation.
