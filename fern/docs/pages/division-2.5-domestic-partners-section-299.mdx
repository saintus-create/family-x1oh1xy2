# Domestic Partner Registration

## Section 299

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-2.5-domestic-partners">Division 2.5: Domestic Partner Registration</a></div>

Termination of registered domestic partnership; filing of Notice of Termination of Domestic Partnership; conditions; effective date; setting aside termination; jurisdiction; dissolution of marriage.
