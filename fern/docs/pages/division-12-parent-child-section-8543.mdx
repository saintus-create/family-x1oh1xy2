# 8543 — “Qualified court investigator” means a superior court

Parent and Child Relationship

“Qualified court investigator” means a superior court
investigator with the same minimum qualifications as a
probation officer or county welfare worker designated to
conduct stepparent adoption investigations in stepparent
adoption proceedings and proceedings to declare a minor
free from parental custody and control. (Added by Stats.
1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 185.)
