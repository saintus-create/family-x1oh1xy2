# § 8543 — “Qualified court investigator” means

“Qualified court investigator” means
a superior court
investigator with the same minimum qualifications
as a
probation officer or county welfare worker designated to
conduct stepparent adoption investigations
in stepparent
adoption proceedings and proceedings to declare a minor
free from parental custody and control.
