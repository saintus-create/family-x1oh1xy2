# General Provisions

Mediators or evaluators appointed by or connected to the court; limitations upon communication with said persons; exceptions P 
(a) In the absence of a stipulation by the parties to the contrary, there shall be no ex parte communication between the attorneys for any party to an action and any courtappointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator, or between a court-appointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator and the court, in any proceedings under this code, except with regard to the scheduling of appointments.
