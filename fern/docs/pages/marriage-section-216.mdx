# California Family Code § 216

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In the absence of a stipulation by the parties to the contrary, there shall be no ex parte communication between the attorneys for any party to an action and any court-appointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator, or between a court-appointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator and the court, in any proceedings under this code, except with regard to the scheduling of appointments.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  There shall be no ex parte communications between counsel appointed by the court pursuant to Section 3150 and a court-appointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator, except when it is expressly authorized by the court or undertaken pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(5)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 3151.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Subdivisions <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  shall not apply in the following situations:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  To allow a mediator or evaluator to address a case involving allegations as set forth in Sections 3113, 3181, and 3192.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  To allow a mediator or evaluator to address a case involving allegations as set forth in Rule 5.215 of the California Rules of Court.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  If the mediator or evaluator determines that ex parte communication is needed to inform the court of the mediator’s or evaluator’s belief that a restraining order is necessary to prevent an imminent risk to the physical safety of the child or the party.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  This section shall not be construed to limit the responsibilities a mediator or evaluator may have as a mandated reporter pursuant to Section 11165.9 of the Penal Code or the responsibilities a mediator or evaluator have to warn under Tarasoff v. Regents of the University of California (1976) 17 Cal.3d 425, Hedlund v. Superior Court (1983) 34 Cal.3d 695, and Section 43.92 of the Civil Code.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The Judicial Council shall, by July 1, 2006, adopt a rule of court to implement this section.
