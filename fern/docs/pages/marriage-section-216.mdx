# § 216

*to the court; limitations upon communication with said*

to the court; limitations upon communication with said
persons; exceptions
P
However, if there has been no
pleading filed in the action for a period of six months after
the entry of the bifurcated judgment, service shall be upon

(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), $ 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219
(A.B.1500), § 84; Stats.1999, c. 980 (A.B.1671), § 2; Stats.
2010, c. 352 (A.B.939), § 2; Stats.2016, c. 67 (A.B.1735), § 1,
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In the absence of a stipulation by the parties to the
contrary, there shall be no ex parte communication between
the attorneys for any party to an action and any courtappointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator, or
between a court-appointed or court-connected evaluator or
mediator and the court, in any proceedings under this code,
except with regard to the scheduling of appointments.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  There shall be no ex parte communications between
counsel appointed by the court pursuant to Section 3150 and
a court-appointed or court-connected evaluator or mediator,
except when it is expressly authorized by the court or
undertaken pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(5)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of
Section 3151.
