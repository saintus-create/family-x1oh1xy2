# 7560

Additional genetic testing upon request of person

The court or local child support agency shall order additional genetic testing upon the request of a person who contests the results of the initial testing under Section 7555. _(Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 23. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)_
