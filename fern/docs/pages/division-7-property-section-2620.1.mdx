# 2620.1 — The Judicial Council shall, by January 1, 2009, develop

Division of Property

The Judicial Council shall, by January 1, 2009, develop
guidelines to assist investigators and examiners in reviewing
accountings and detecting fraud. (Added by Stats.2007, c. 553
(A.B.1727), § 21.)
CHAPTER 8. COMPENSATION OF GUARDIAN,
CONSERVATOR, AND ATTORNEY
Section
2647. Attorney fees.
