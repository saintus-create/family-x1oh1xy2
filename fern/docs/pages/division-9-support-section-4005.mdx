# Support

## Section 4005

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Circumstances forming basis for child support order; findings At the request of either party, the court shall make appropriate findings with respect to the circumstances on which the order for support of a child is based.
