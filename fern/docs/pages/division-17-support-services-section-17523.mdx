# § 17523 — Perfection; priority; enforcement

perfection; priority; enforcement

(2018), In General.
,
Levy by Agency.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, if a
support obligor is delinquent in the payment of support and
the local child support agency is enforcing the support
obligation pursuant to Section 17400 or 17402, a lien for child
support shall arise against the personal property of the
support obligor in either of the following circumstances:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  By operation of law for all amounts of overdue
support, regardless’ of whether the amounts have been
adjudicated or otherwise determined.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  When either a court having continuing jurisdiction or
the local child support agency determines a specific amount
of arrearages is owed by the support obligor.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The lien for child support shall be perfected by filing a
notice of child support lien with the. Secretary of State
pursuant to Section 697.510 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
Once filed, the child support lien shall have the same priority,
force, and effect as a judgment lien on personal property
pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 697.510) of
Chapter 2 of of Article 9 of the Code of Civil
Procedure.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this section, the following definitions
shall apply:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Notice of child support lien” means a document filed
with the Secretary of State that substantially complies with
the requirements of Section 697.530 of the Code of Civil
Procedure.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Support obligor is delinquent in payment of support”
means that the support obligor has failed to make payment
equal to one month’s support obligation.
