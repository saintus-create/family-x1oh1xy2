# California Family Code § 7500

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The mother of an unemancipated minor child, and the father, if presumed to be the father under Section 7611, are equally entitled to the services and earnings of the child.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If one parent is dead, is unable or refuses to take custody, or has abandoned the child, the other parent is entitled to the services and earnings of the child.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section shall not apply to any services or earnings of an unemancipated minor child related to a contract of a type described in Section 6750.
