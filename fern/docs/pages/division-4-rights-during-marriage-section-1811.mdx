# 1811 — Assignment of judges; number of sessions

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

Assignment of judges; number of sessions
The presiding judge of the superior court shall annually, in
the month of January, designate at least one judge to hear all
cases under this part. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
