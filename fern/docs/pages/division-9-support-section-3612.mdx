# Support

## Section 3612

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Continuing jurisdiction until minor reaches majority; continuing jurisdiction over trust of person with a disability who reaches majority 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law and except to the extent the court orders otherwise, the court making the order under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3611.">§ 3611</a> shall have continuing jurisdiction of the money and other property paid, delivered, deposited, or invested under this article until the minor reaches 18 years of age. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision (a), the trust of an individual who meets the definition of a person with a disability under paragraph (3) of subdivision (b) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3603.">§ 3603</a> and who reaches 18 years of age, shall continue and be under continuing court jurisdiction until terminated by the court.
