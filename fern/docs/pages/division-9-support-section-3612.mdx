# § 3612 — Disability who reaches majority

Continuing jurisdiction until minor reaches majority; continuing jurisdiction over trust of person with a
disability who reaches majority
3 California Transactions Forms--Estate Planning § 17:15, Required
Findings [Prob C § 3604(B)].

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law and except
to the extent the court orders otherwise, the court making the
order under Section 3611 shall have continuing jurisdiction of
the money and other property paid, delivered, deposited, or
invested under this article until the minor reaches 18 years of
age.
15 Witkin,
c. 79 (A.B.759),
Summary
10th
Wills
and
Probate

CHAPTER 5. PROPERTY OF ABSENT
FEDERAL PERSONNEL
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision (a), the trust of an individual who meets the definition of a person with a disability
under paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 3603 and
who reaches 18 years of age, shall continue and be under
continuing court jurisdiction until terminated by the court.
