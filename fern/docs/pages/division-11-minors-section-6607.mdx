# 6607

Petition for probate or administration

petition for probate or administration

Who may file
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A petition may be filed under this chapter by any of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The person named in the will of the decedent as
executor.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The surviving spouse of the decedent.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The guardian of a minor child of the decedent.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A child of the decedent who was a minor at the time
the decedent died.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The personal representative if a personal representative has been appointed for the decedent’s estate.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The guardian of a minor child of the decedent may file
the petition without authorization or approval of the court in
which the guardianship proceeding is pending. (Stats.1990, c.
