# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6320.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Order denying petition for ex parte order; reasons; right to noticed hearing; right to waive hearing 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An order denying a petition for an ex parte order pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6320.">§ 6320</a> shall include the reasons for denying the petition. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An order denying a jurisdictionally adequate petition for an ex parte order, pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6320.">§ 6320</a>, shall provide the petitioner the right to a noticed hearing on the earliest date that the business of the court will permit, but not later than 21 days or, if good cause appears to the court, 25 days from the date of the order. The petitioner shall serve on the respondent, at least five days before the hearing, copies of all supporting papers filed with the court, including the application and affidavits. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision (b), upon the denial of the ex parte order pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6320.">§ 6320</a>, the petitioner shall have the option of waiving the right to a noticed hearing. 
However, this section does not preclude a petitioner who waives the right to a noticed hearing from refiling a new petition, without prejudice, at a later time.
