# § 663

*Ceremonial marriage*

Ceremonial marriage
A ceremonial marriage is presumed to be valid.
(Stats.
1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
Herrera v. Pons, 2018 WL 2229369 (U.S. Dist. Ct. §.D. Cal. 2018), a
federal bankruptcy appeal, takes Valli one step further. In Valli, the
wife was listed as the owner, without
EVIDENCE;
quirements had to be satisfied, and that a formally adequate
transmutation (a wife’s quit-claim deed) was ineffective because the
husband could not overcome the presumption of undue influence
that arose when he gained an advantage and his wife was thereby
disadvantaged.
AND INFERENCES
Section
662.
