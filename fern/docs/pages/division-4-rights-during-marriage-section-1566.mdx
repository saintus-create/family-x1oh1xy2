# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1566

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Consent not free; rescission , . 
A consent which is not free is nevertheless not absolutely void, but may be rescinded by the parties, in the manner prescribed by the Chapter on Rescission. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
