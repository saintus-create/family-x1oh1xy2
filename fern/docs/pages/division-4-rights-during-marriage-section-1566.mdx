# 1566. Consent not free; rescission

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Consent not free; rescission
CONSENT, WHEN VOIDABLE.
A consent which is not free is
nevertheless not absolutely void, but may be rescinded by the
parties, in the manner prescribed by the Chapter on Rescis-
sion.
(Enacted in 1872.)
