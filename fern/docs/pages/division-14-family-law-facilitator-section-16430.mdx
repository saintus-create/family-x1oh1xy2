---
title: "16430. The person giving the consent"
description: "The person giving the consent"
slug: division-14-family-law-facilitator-section-16430
---

The person giving the consent
to but not signing or verifying the application shall not be
subject to the civil liability specified in Sections 17707 and
17708 merely by reason of having given consent.
