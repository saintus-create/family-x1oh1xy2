# § 6327.

*Part 4 — Protective Orders And Other Domestic Violence Prevention Orders · Chapter 2 — Issuance Of Orders*

Part 4 (commencing with Section 240) of Division 2 applies to the issuance of any ex parte order under this article, other than an order under Section 6322.5. (Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 511, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.) ARTICLE 2. Orders Issuable After Notice and Hearing [6340 - 6347] ( Article 2 added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. )
