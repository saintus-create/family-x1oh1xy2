# 1587 — A proposal is revoked by any of the following:

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

A proposal is revoked by any of the following:
ACCEPTANCE BY PERFORMANCE OF CONDITIONS. Performance
of the conditions of a proposal, or the acceptance of the
consideration offered with a proposal, is an acceptance of the
proposal. (Enacted in 1872.)
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  By the communication of notice of revocation by the
proposer to the other party, in the manner prescribed by
Sections 1581 and 1583, before his or her acceptance has
been communicated to the former.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  By the lapse of the time prescribed in the proposal for
its acceptance or, if no time is prescribed, the lapse of a
reasonable time without communication of the acceptance.
Treatises and Practice Aids
1 , Conduct
as Acceptance.
1 , Notification to Offeror.
