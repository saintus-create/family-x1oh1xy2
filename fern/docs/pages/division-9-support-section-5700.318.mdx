# § 5700.318

*Assistance with discovery*

Assistance with discovery
A tribunal of this state may:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  request a tribunal
obtaining discovery; and
outside
this state
to assist in
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  upon request, compel a person over which it has
jurisdiction to respond to a discovery order issued by a
tribunal outside this state.
(Added by Stats.2015, c. 493
(S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
