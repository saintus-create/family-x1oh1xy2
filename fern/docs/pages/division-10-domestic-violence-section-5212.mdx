# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5212

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

“IV-D Case” defined 
“IV-D Case” means any case being established, modified, or enforced by the local child support agency pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=654.">§ 654</a> of Title 42 of the United States Code (<a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=454.">§ 454</a> of the Social Security Act).
