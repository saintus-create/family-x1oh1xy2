# 366.24. Tribal customary adoptions

*Premarital Agreements*

Tribal customary adoptions
(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  For purposes of this section, “tribal customary
adoption” means adoption by and through the tribal custom,
traditions, or law of an Indian child’s tribe.
Termination of
parental rights is not required to effect the tribal customary
adoption.

includes a nonminor dependent as described in subdivision
<span class="subsec">(v)</span>  of Section 11400, unless the nonminor dependent has
elected not to be considered an Indian child pursuant to
subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 224.1.
