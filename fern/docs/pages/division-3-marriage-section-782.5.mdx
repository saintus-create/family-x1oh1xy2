# § 782.5 — Attempted murder or soliciting the murder of a

Attempted murder or soliciting the murder of a
spouse; remedies; community property interests
In addition to any other remedy authorized by law, when a
spouse is convicted of attempting to murder the other spouse,
as punishable pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 664 of
the Penal Code, or of soliciting the murder of the other
spouse, as punishable pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section
653f of the Penal Code, the injured spouse shall be entitled to
an award to the injured spouse
of, 100 percent
of, the
community property interest in the retirement and pension
benefits of the injured spouse.
