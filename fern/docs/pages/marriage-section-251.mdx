# § 251

*A joint tenant who feloniously and intentionally kills*

A joint tenant who feloniously and intentionally kills
another joint tenant thereby effects a severance of the
interest of the decedent so that the share of the decedent
passes as the decedent’s property and the killer has no rights
by survivorship. This section applies to joint tenancies in real
and personal property, joint and multiple-party accounts in
financial institutions, and any other form of coownership with
Posthumous Conception and Birth of Donor’s Child.
