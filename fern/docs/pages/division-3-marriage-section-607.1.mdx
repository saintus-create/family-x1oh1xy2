# 607.1. Retention of jurisdiction; criteria

*Premarital Agreements*

CODE
Retention of jurisdiction; criteria
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  This section shall become operative on the 90th day
after the enactment of the act adding this section.!
(b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Notwithstanding Section 607, the court shall retain
jurisdiction as described in paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  over any person
who meets both of the following criteria:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The person has been discharged from the physical
custody of a facility of the Department of Corrections and
Rehabilitation, Division of Juvenile Facilities.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The person is subject to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section
1766 or subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 1766.01.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The court shall retain jurisdiction over a person who is
described in paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  until one of the following applies:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The person attains the age of 25 years.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The court terminates jurisdiction pursuant to Section
778 or 779, or any other applicable law.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Jurisdiction is terminated by operation of any other
applicable law. (Added by Stats.2010, c. 729 (A.B.1628),
§ 10, eff. Oct. 19, 2010, operative Jan. 17, 2011.)
' Stats.2010, c. 729 (A.B.1628), eff. Oct. 19, 2010.
