# Custody of Children

## Section 3449

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Service of petition and order Except as otherwise provided in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3451.">Section 3451</a>, the petition and order shall be served, by any method authorized by the law of this state, upon the respondent and any person who has physical custody of the child.
