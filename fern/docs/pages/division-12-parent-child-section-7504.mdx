# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7504

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Relinquishment of parental rights; abandonment The parent, whether solvent or insolvent, may relinquish to the child the right of controlling the child and receiving the child’s earnings. Abandonment by the parent is presumptive 

§ 7504 evidence of that relinquishment.
