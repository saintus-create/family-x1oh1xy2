# § 7504 — Relinquishment of parental rights; abandonment

Relinquishment of parental rights; abandonment
The parent, whether solvent or insolvent, may relinquish to
the child the right of controlling the child and receiving the
child’s earnings. Abandonment by the parent is presumptive

evidence of that relinquishment.
