# § 5013

*Waivers or other instruments or agreements affect-*

Waivers or other instruments or agreements affect-
ing rights in community property
Nothing in this chapter limits the effect of a surviving
spouse’s waiver of rights in community property under
Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 140) of Part 3 of
or other instrument or agreement that affects a
married person’s interest in community property.
