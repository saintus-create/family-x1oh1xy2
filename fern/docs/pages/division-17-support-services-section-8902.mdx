# 8902. Agency services

*Support Services*

Agency services
For intercountry adoptions that will be finalized in this
state, the licensed adoption agency shall provide all of the
following services:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Assessment of the suitability of the applicant’s home.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Placement of the foreign-born child in an approved
home.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Postplacement supervision.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Submission to the court of a report on the intercountry
adoptive placement with a recommendation regarding the
granting of the petition.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Services to applicants seeking to adopt related children
living in foreign countries. The Legislature recognizes that
these children have an impelling need for adoptive placement
with their relatives. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
