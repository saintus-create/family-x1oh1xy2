# § 3902.

*Part 2 — Child Support · Chapter 1 — Duty Of Parent To Support Child*

The court may direct that an allowance be made to the parent of a child for whom support may be ordered out of the child’s property for the child’s past or future support, on conditions that are proper, if the direction is for the child’s benefit. (Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1994.) ARTICLE 2. Support of Adult Child [3910- 3910.] ( Article 2 enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. )
