# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 704.115

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=82.">§ 82</a> (“trusts” 
defined to exclude trusts for the primary purpose of paying pensions). 
Subdivision (b) is drawn from <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=156.">§ 156</a>(2) of the Restatement 
(Second) of Trusts (1957). 
The limitation on the amount that may be reached by transferees and creditors to the proportionate amount of the settlor’s contribution is drawn from Wisconsin law. 
See Wis.Stat.Ann.
