# § 2404.

*Part 3 — Dissolution Of Marriage And Legal Separation · Chapter 5 — Summary Dissolution*

Entry of the judgment pursuant to Section 2403 constitutes:

(a) A final adjudication of the rights and obligations of the parties with respect to the status of the marriage and property rights.

(b) A waiver of their respective rights to spousal support, rights to appeal, and rights to move for a new trial. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
