# § 4611.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 3 — Deposit Of Assets To Secure Future Child Support Payments*

In a proceeding under this chapter, an obligor-parent shall rebut both of the following presumptions:

(a) The nonpayment of child support was willful, without good faith.

(b) The obligor had the ability to pay the support. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
