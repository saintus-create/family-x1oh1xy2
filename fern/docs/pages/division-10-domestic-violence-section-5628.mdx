# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5628

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Effect of multiple recordings for same property 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If a revocable transfer on death deed is recorded for the same property for which another revocable transfer on death deed is recorded, the later executed deed is the operative instrument and its recordation revokes the earlier executed deed. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Revocation of a revocable transfer on death deed does not revive an instrument earlier revoked by recordation of that deed.
