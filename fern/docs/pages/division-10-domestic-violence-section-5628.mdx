Effect of multiple recordings for same property
(a)  If a revocable transfer on death deed is recorded for
the same property for which another revocable transfer on
death deed is recorded, the later executed deed
is the
operative instrument and its recordation revokes the earlier
executed deed.
(b)  Revocation of a revocable transfer on death deed does
not revive an instrument earlier revoked by recordation of
that deed.
