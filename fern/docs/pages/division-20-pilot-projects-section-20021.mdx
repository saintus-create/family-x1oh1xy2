# 20021

Contested proceedings;

A party who fails to submit documents to the court as required by Section 20020 may, in the court’s discretion, not be granted the relief requested, or the court may impose evidentiary sanctions. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.
