# Pilot Projects

## Section 20021

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Contested proceedings; failure to provide documents; sanctions A party who fails to submit documents to the court as required by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=20020.">§ 20020</a> may, in the court’s discretion, not be granted the relief requested, or the court may impose evidentiary sanctions.
