# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7580

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Voluntary declaration of parentage; minor parents; validity; rescission; presumption; admissibility 
(a) Notwithstanding subdivision (c) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7573.">Section 7573</a>, a voluntary declaration of parentage that is signed by a minor parent does not establish parentage until 60 days after 508 both signatories. have reached 18 years of age or are emancipated, whichever first occurs. 
(b) A person who signs a voluntary declaration of parentage asa minor may rescind the voluntary declaration of parentage at any time up to 60 days after the signatory reaches 18 years of age or becomes emancipated, whichever first occurs. 
(c) A voluntary declaration of parentage signed by a minor creates a rebuttable presumption for or against parentage until the date that it establishes parentage as specified in subdivision (a). 
(d) A voluntary declaration of parentage signed by a minor shall be admissible as evidence in a civil action to establish parentage of the minor named in the voluntary declaration.
