<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Time limit on custody—certification of child detained in custody (§ 604) 
 OF A child must be released from custody within 48 hours, excluding noncourt days, after certification to juvenile court under rules 4.116 and 5.516(d) unless a petition has been filed. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Time limit for detention hearing—warrant or nonward charged with nonviolent misdemeanor (§ 632) 
A detention hearing must be set and commenced as soon as possible, but no later than 48 hours, excluding noncourt days, after the child has been taken into custody, if: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child has been taken into custody on a warrant or by the authority of the probation officer; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child is not on probation or parole and is alleged to have committed a misdemeanor not involving violence, the threat of violence, or the possession or use of a weapon. 
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Time limit for detention hearing—felony, violent misdemeanor, or ward (§ 632) 
A detention hearing must be set and commenced as soon as possible, but no later than the expiration of the next court day after the petition or notice of probation violation has been filed, if: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child is alleged to have committed a felony; 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child is alleged to have committed a misdemeanor involving violence, the threat of violence, or the possession or use of a weapon; or 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The child is a ward currently on probation or parole. 
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Time limit for hearing—arrival at detention facility 
(§ 632) 
A detention hearing must be set and commenced as soon as possible, but no later than 48 hours, excluding noncourt days, after the child arrives at a detention facility within the county if: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child was taken into custody in another county and transported in custody to the requesting county; 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child was ordered transported in custody when transferred by the juvenile court of another county under rule 5.610; or 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The child is a ward temporarily placed in a secure facility pending a change of placement.
