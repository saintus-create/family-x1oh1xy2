# § 4908 — Decided under that section, section 200 and section 2010(c), in

Decided under that section, section 200 and section 2010(c), in
a proceeding to dissolve a marriage a California Superior Court has
jurisdiction to award child support even though a child’s home state
is Japan, when no support action has ever been initiated in the child’s
home state. In re Marriage of Richardson, 179 Cal.App.4th 1240, 102
Cal. Rptr.3d 391 (2009).
So long as the issue of support has not been raised in a divorce
action pending in another jurisdiction, a California court is not
divested of jurisdiction to order support. Under former Family code
section 4908, In re Marriage of Newman, 80 Cal.App.4th 846, 95
Cal.Rptr.3d 691 (2000), interpreted a subsection <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “petition or
comparable pleading” to refer only to a support proceeding or a
proceeding that raises a support issue, rather than any proceeding in
which support could be sought.
