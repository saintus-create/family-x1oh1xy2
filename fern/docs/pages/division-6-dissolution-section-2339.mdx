# 2339. Finality of judgment; waiting period

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

Finality of judgment; waiting period
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  and to Sections 2340 to 2344,
inclusive, no judgment of dissolution is final for the purpose
of terminating the marriage relationship of the parties until
six months have expired from the date of service of a copy of
summons and petition or the date of appearance of the
respondent, whichever occurs first.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may extend the six-month period described
in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  for good cause shown.
