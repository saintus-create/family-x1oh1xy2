Advisory committee
The Judicial Council shall establish an advisory committee
of persons representing a broad spectrum of interest in and
knowledge about family law.
The committee shall recom-
mend criteria for determining grant recipients pursuant to
subdivision (c)  of Section 1850, which shall include proposal
evaluation guidelines and procedures for submission of the
results to the Legislature, the Governor, and family law
courts.
In accordance with established criteria, the commit-
tee shall receive grant proposals and shall recommend the
priority of submitted proposals.
