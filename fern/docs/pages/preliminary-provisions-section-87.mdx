# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 87

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

The section was drawn from former Probate Code <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=662.">§ 662</a> (probate homestead) (repealed by 1983 Cal.Stat. 
ch. 842 § 39). 
The 1987 amendment (1) revised the cross-reference to the procedure for mailing notice of the hearing and (2) deleted the provision requiring the clerk to set the petition for hearing, this provision being unnecessary in view of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1285.">§ 1285</a> which is a general provision that imposes this duty on the clerk. 
As to the 1987 amendment, see Communication from California Law Revision Commission Concerning Assembly Bill 708, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 545, 560 
(1988); Comments to Conforming Revisions and Repeals, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 391, 449 (1988). 
For background 1192 on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part under the part heading. 
.
