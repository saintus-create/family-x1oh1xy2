# 87 — The section

Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

The section
was drawn from former Probate Code Section 662 (probate home-
stead) (repealed by 1983
Cal.Stat.
ch. 842 § 39).
The 1987
amendment <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  revised the cross-reference to the procedure for
mailing notice of the hearing and <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  deleted the provision requiring
the clerk to set the petition for hearing, this provision being
unnecessary in view of Section 1285 which is a general provision that
imposes this duty on the clerk.
As to the 1987 amendment, see
Communication from California Law Revision Commission Concern-
ing Assembly Bill 708, 19 Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 545, 560
(1988);
Comments
to Conforming Revisions and Repeals,

Cal.L.Revision Comm’n Reports 391, 449 (1988).
For background
1192

WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION
on the provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part under the
part heading.
.
