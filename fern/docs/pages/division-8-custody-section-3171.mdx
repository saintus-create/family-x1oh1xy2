# § 3171 — Stepparent or grandparent visitation; setting mat-

Stepparent or grandparent visitation; setting mat-
ter for mediation; waiver of parental right to object or
require a hearing
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If a stepparent
or grandparent has petitioned, or
otherwise applied, for a visitation order pursuant to Chapter
5 (commencing with Section 3100), the court shall set the
matter for mediation.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A natural or adoptive parent who is not a party to the
proceeding is not required to participate in the mediation
proceeding, but failure to participate is a waiver of that
parent’s right to object to a settlement reached by the other
parties during mediation or to require a hearing on the
matter.
