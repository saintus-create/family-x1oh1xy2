# Adoption

## Section 9208

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Children of Indian ancestry; States Secretary of the Interior 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The clerk of the superior court entering a final order of adoption concerning an Indian child shall provide the United notice to United 656 States Secretary of the Interior or a designee with a copy of the order within 30 days of the date of the order, together with any information necessary to show the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The name and tribal affiliation of the child. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The names and addresses of the biological parents. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The names and addresses of the adoptive parents. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The identity of any agency having files or information relating to that adoptive placement. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the court records contain an affidavit of the biological parent or parents that their identity remain confidential, the court shall include that affidavit with the other information.
