# Marriage

## Section 645.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Payment of referee’s fees; court order 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When a referee is appointed pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=638.">§ 638</a>, the referee’s fees shall be paid as agreed by the parties. 
If the parties do not agree on the payment of fees and request the matter to be resolved by the court, the court may order the parties to pay the referee’s fees as set forth in subdivision 
(b). 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  When a referee is appointed pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=639.">§ 639</a>, at any time after a determination of ability to pay is made as specified in paragraph (6) of subdivision (d) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=639.">§ 639</a>, the court may order the parties to pay the fees of referees who are not employees or officers of the court at the time of appointment, as fixed pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1023.">§ 1023</a>, in any manner determined by the court to be fair and reasonable, including an apportionment of the fees among the parties. 
For purposes of this section, the term “parties” does not include parties’ counsel.
