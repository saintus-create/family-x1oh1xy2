# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 43.6

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Immunity from liability; actions against parents on childbirth claims; defenses and damages in third party actions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  No cause of action arises against a parent of a child based upon the claim that the child should not have been conceived or, if conceived, should not have been allowed to have been born alive. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The failure or refusal of a parent to prevent the live birth of his or her child shall not be a defense in any action against a third party, nor shall the failure or refusal be considered in awarding damages in any such action. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  As used in this section “conceived” means the fertilization of.a human ovum by a human sperm.
