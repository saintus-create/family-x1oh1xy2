# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5040

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Nonprobate transfer to former spouse executed before or during marriage or registered domestic partnership; failure of transfer due to dissolution or annulment of marriage or termination of registered domestic partnership; situations that do not cause a nonprobate transfer to fail; rights of subsequent purchaser. 
Operative Effect For operative effect and application of Chapter 3, see Probate Code
