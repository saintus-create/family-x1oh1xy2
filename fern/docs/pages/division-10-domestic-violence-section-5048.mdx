# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5048

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Operative date and application 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  This chapter, formerly Part 4 (commencing with Section 5600), is operative on January 1, 2002. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision 
(c), this chapter applies to an instrument making a nonprobate transfer or creating a joint tenancy whether executed before, on, or after the operative date of this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Sections 5040 and 5042 do not apply, and the applicable law in effect before the operative date of this chapter applies, to an instrument making a nonprobate transfer or creating a joint tenancy in either of the following circumstances: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The person making the nonprobate transfer or creating the joint tenancy dies before the operative date of this chapter. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The dissolution of marriage or other event that terminates the status of the nonprobate transfer beneficiary or joint tenant as a surviving spouse occurs before the operative date of this chapter. 
(Formerly § 5604, added by Stats.2001, c. 417 (A.B.873), $ 9, operative Jan. 1, 2002. 
Renumbered 
§ 5048 and amended by Stats.2015, c. 293 (A.B.139), § 16, eff. 
Jan. 1, 2016.)
