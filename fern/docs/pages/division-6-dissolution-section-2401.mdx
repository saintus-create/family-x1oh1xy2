# 2401. California

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

California
Summary
Joint petition; filing; form; contents
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding for summary dissolution of the marriage
shall be commenced by filing a joint petition in the form
prescribed by the Judicial Council.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition shall be signed under
spouses, and shall include all of the following:
oath
by both
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A statement that as of the date of the filing of the joint
petition all of the conditions set forth in Section 2400 have
been met.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The mailing address of each spouse.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A statement whether a spouse elects to have his or her
former name restored, and, if so, the name to be restored.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. I, 1994.
Amended by Stats.2014, c. 82 (S.B.1306), § 29, eff. Jan. 1,
2015.)
