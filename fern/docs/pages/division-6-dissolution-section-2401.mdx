# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2401

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

(a) A proceeding for summary dissolution of the marriage shall be commenced by filing a joint petition in the form prescribed by the Judicial Council. 
(b) The petition shall be signed under oath by both spouses, and shall include all of the following: Joint petition; filing; form; contents 
(1) A statement that as of the date of the filing of the joint petition all of the conditions set forth in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2400.">Section 2400</a> have been met. 
(2) The mailing address of each spouse. 
(3) A statement whether a spouse elects to have his or her former name restored, and, if so, the name to be restored.
