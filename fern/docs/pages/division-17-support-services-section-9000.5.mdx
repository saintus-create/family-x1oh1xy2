# 9000.5 — Procedures

Support Services

Procedures
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The procedures provided in this section apply to
a stepparent adoption where the child was born during the
marriage or domestic partnership, including a registered
domestic partnership or civil union from another jurisdiction,
and either of the following circumstances applies:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  One of the spouses or partners gave birth to the child.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child was born through a gestational surrogacy
process brought about by one or both of the spouses or
partners, and the parentage of only one spouse or partner was
established pursuant to the Uniform Parentage Act (
(commencing with Section 7600) of ) or another
parentage proceeding related to the surrogacy.

under this section unless otherwise ordered by the court for
good cause:
