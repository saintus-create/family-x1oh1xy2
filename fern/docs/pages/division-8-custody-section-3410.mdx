# Custody of Children

## Section 3410

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Communication between courts 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court of this state may communicate with a court in another state concerning a proceeding arising under this part. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may allow the parties to participate in the communication. 
If the parties are not able to participate in the communication, they must be given the opportunity to present facts and legal arguments before a decision on jurisdiction is made. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Communication. between courts on schedules, calendars, court records, and similar matters may occur without informing the parties. 
A record need not be made of the communication. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in subdivision (c), a record must be made of a communication under this section. 
The parties must be informed promptly of the communication and granted access to the record. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  For the purposes of this section, “record” means information that is inscribed on a tangible medium or that is stored in an electronic or other medium and is retrievable in perceivable form.
