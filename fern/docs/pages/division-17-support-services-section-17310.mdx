# Support Services

## Section 17310

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Regulations and general policies 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The director shall formulate, adopt, amend, or repeal regulations and. general policies affecting the purposes, responsibilities, and jurisdiction of the department that are consistent with law and necessary for the administration of 

§ 17310 the state plan for securing child support and enforcing spousal support orders and determining paternity. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, all regulations, including, but not limited to, regulations of the State Department of Social Services and the State Department of Health Services, relating to child support enforcement shall remain in effect and shall be fully enforceable by the department. The department may readopt, amend, or repeal the regulations in accordance with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17312.">§ 17312</a> as necessary and appropriate.
