# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5272

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Error or excess in amount of current support or arrearage; modification of order A finding of error in the amount of the current support or arrearage or that the amount exceeds federal or state limits is not grounds to vacate the assignment order. The court shall modify the order to reflect the correct or allowable amount of support or arrearages. 
The fact that the obligor may have subsequently paid the arrearages does not relieve the court of its duty to enter the assignment order.
