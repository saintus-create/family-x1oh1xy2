# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7649.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Distribution from estate of decedent made in good faith reliance on judgement establishing paternity; finality; appeal; liability of estate, trust, personal representative or trustee.
