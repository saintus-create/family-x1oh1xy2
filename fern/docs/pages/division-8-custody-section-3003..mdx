# § 3003.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

“Joint legal custody” means that both parents shall share the right and the responsibility to make the decisions relating to the health, education, and welfare of a child. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
