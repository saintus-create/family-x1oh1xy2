# § 7852.

*Part 4 — Freedom From Parental Custody And Control · Chapter 3 — Procedure*

“Qualified court investigator,” as used in this article, has the meaning provided by Section 8543. (Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 182. Effective January 1, 1994.) ARTICLE 4. Appointment of Counsel [7860 - 7864] ( Article 4 enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. )
