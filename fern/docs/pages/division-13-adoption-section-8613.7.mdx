# § 8613.7

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

On and after January 1, 2014, the court shall provide to any petitioner for adoption pursuant to this part a notice informing the petitioner that they may be eligible for reduced-cost coverage through the California Health Benefit Exchange established under Title 22 (commencing with Section 100500) of the Government Code or no-cost coverage through Medi-Cal. The notice shall include information on obtaining coverage pursuant to those programs, and shall be developed by the California Health Benefit Exchange. (Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 114. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)
