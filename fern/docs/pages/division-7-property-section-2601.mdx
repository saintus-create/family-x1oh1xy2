# Division of Property

## Section 2601

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Conditional award of an asset of the community estate to one party Where economic circumstances warrant, the court may award an asset of the community estate to one party on such conditions as the court deems proper to effect a substantially equal division of the community estate.
