# § 930

Except as otherwise provided by statute, this part governs the liability of separate property and property in the community estate and the personal liability of a married person for a debt enforced on or after January 1, 1985, regardless of whether the debt was incurred before, on, or after that date. _(Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 100.6. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
