# § 5700.708 — The contesting party bears the burden of proof

The contesting party bears the burden of proof.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  In a contest of a registered Convention support order,
a tribunal of this state:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  is bound by the findings of fact on which the foreign
tribunal based its jurisdiction; and
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  may not review the merits of the order.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A tribunal
of this state deciding
a contest of a
registered Convention support order shall promptly notify the
parties of its decision.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  A challenge or appeal, if any, does not stay the
enforcement of a Convention support order unless there are
exceptional circumstances.
