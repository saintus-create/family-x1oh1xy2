# § 271.5

*surrendering custody of baby*

surrendering custody of baby
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  No parent or other individual having lawful custody of
a minor child 72 hours old or younger may be prosecuted for
a violation of Section 270, 270.5, 271, or 271la if he or she
voluntarily surrenders physical custody of the child to personnel on duty at a safe-surrender site.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  For purposes of this section, “‘safe-surrender site” has
the same meaning as defined in paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of subdivision
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 1255.7 of the Health and Safety Code.
(c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  For purposes of this section, “lawful custody” has
the same meaning as defined in subdivision <span class="subsec">(j)</span>  of Section
1255.7 of the Health and Safety Code.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  For purposes of this section, “personnel” has the same
meaning as defined in paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of
Section 1255.7 of the Health and Safety Code. (Added by
