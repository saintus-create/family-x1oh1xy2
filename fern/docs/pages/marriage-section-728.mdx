# 728 — (B) The juvenile court that had jurisdiction over the minor

Marriage

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The juvenile court that had jurisdiction over the minor
or nonminor dependent when his or her adoption was
finalized.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A nonminor described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  may submit a
petition to assume dependency jurisdiction to the juvenile
court in the county where he or she resides.
A petition
submitted pursuant to this paragraph shall, within five days of
submission, be forwarded to the court that had jurisdiction
over the child at the time of the guardianship or adoption.
The clerk of the court that had jurisdiction over the child at
the time of the guardianship or adoption shall file the petition
within one judicial day of receipt.
(c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The juvenile court in which the petition was filed
shall order a hearing to be held within 15 judicial days of the
date the petition was filed if there is a prima facie showing
that the nonminor satisfies
all of the following criteria:
