ry
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The declarant was related to the other by blood or
marriage;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The declarant was otherwise so intimately associated
with the other’s family as to be likely to have had accurate
information concerning the matter declared and made the
statement <span class="subsec">(i)</span>  upon information received from the other or
from a person related by blood or marriage to the other or
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  upon repute in the other’s family.
Marriage, baptismal and similar certificates.
Statement concerning declarant’s own family histo-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to subdivision (b), evidence of a statement by a
declarant who is unavailable as a witness concerning his own
birth, marriage, divorce, a parent and child relationship,
relationship by blood or marriage, race, ancestry, or other
similar fact of his family history is not made inadmissible by
the hearsay rule, even though the declarant had no means of
acquiring personal knowledge of the matter declared.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Evidence of a statement is inadmissible under this
section if the statement was made under circumstances such
