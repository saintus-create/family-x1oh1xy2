# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5692

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Time for commencing action; accrual of limitations period 
(a) A contest proceeding pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5690.">Section 5690</a> shall not be commenced before the transferor’s death. 
(b) For the purposes of the applicable limitations period, a contest proceeding accrues on the date of the transferor’s death.
