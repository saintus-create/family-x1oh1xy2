# California Family Code § 240

This part applies where a temporary restraining order, including a protective order as defined in Section 6218, is issued under any of the following provisions:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Article 2 (commencing with Section 2045) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of (dissolution of marriage, nullity of marriage, or legal separation of the parties).

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Article 3 (commencing with Section 4620) of Chapter 3 of Part 5 of (deposit of assets to secure future child support payments).

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Article 1 (commencing with Section 6320) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of (Domestic Violence Prevention Act), other than an order under Section 6322.5.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Article 2 (commencing with Section 7710) of Chapter 6 of Part 3 of (Uniform Parentage Act).
