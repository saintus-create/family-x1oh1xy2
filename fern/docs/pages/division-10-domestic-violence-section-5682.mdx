# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5682

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Rights and protections of beneficiary same as distributee from estate; conditions If both of the following conditions are satisfied, a person dealing with a beneficiary of a revocable transfer on death deed of real property shall have the same rights and protections as the person would have if the beneficiary had been named as a distributee of the property in an order for distribution of the transferor’s estate that had become final: 
(a) The person acted in good faith and for a valuable consideration. 
(b) An affidavit of death was recorded for the property under Chapter 2 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=210.">Section 210</a>) of Part 4 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a>. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 293 (A.B.139), § 17, eff. 
Jan. 1, 2016.) 
Repeal For repeal of Part 4, see Probate Code $ 5600.
