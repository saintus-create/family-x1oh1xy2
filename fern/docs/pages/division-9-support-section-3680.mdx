# California Family Code § 3680

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Legislature finds and declares the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  There is currently no simple method available to parents to quickly modify their support orders when circumstances warrant a change in the amount of support.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The lack of a simple method for parents to use to modify support orders has led to orders in which the amount of support ordered is inappropriate based on the parents’ financial circumstances.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Parents should not have to incur significant costs or experience significant delays in obtaining an appropriate support order.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Therefore, it is the intent of the Legislature that the Judicial Council adopt rules of court and forms for a simplified method to modify support orders. This simplified method should be designed to be used by parents who are not represented by counsel.
