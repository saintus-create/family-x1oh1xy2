# 6210

"Disturbing the peace" defined

“Dating relationship” means frequent, intimate associations primarily characterized by the expectation of affection or sexual involvement independent of financial considerations. Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 110, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.
