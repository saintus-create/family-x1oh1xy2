---
title: "6. Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the"
description: "Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the"
slug: preliminary-provisions-section-6
---

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the general provisions and rules of construction in this part govern the construction of this code.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
