# § 1766.01 — Described in paragraph (1) until one of the following applies:

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The court shall retain jurisdiction over a person who is
described in paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  until one of the following applies:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The person attains the age of 25 years.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The court terminates jurisdiction pursuant to Section
778 or 779, or any other applicable law.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Jurisdiction is terminated by operation of any other
applicable law.
