# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1766.01

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The court shall retain jurisdiction over a person who is described in paragraph (1) until one of the following applies: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The person attains the age of 25 years. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The court terminates jurisdiction pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=778.">§ 778</a> or 779, or any other applicable law. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Jurisdiction is terminated by operation of any other applicable law.
