Residence of adoptive parents; effect on eligibil-
ity
Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 11105, the
residence of the adoptive parents at the time of or subsequent
to adoptive placement shall not terminate the eligibility of a
child who is otherwise eligible for adoptive assistance payments. (Added by Stats.1982, c. 977, p. 3521, § 28.5, eff. Sept.
13, 1982, operative Oct. 1, 1982.)
