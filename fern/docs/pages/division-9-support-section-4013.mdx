# § 4013 — Duty for child support discharged in bankruptcy;

If obligations for support of a child are discharged in bankruptcy, the court may make all proper orders for the support of the child that the court determines are just. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
