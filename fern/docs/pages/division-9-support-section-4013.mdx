# Support

## Section 4013

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Duty for child support discharged in bankruptcy; order for child support If obligations for support of a child are discharged in bankruptcy, the court may make all proper orders for the support of the child that the court determines are just.
