# § 6613 — Attorney’s fees

Attorney’s fees
The attorney’s fees for services performed in connection
with the filing of a petition and the obtaining of a court order
under this chapter shall be determined by private agreement
between the attorney and the client and are not subject to
approval by the court.
If there is no agreement between the
attorney and the client concerning the attorney’s fees for
services performed in connection with the filing of a petition
and obtaining of a court order under this chapter and there is
a dispute concerning the reasonableness of the attorney’s fees
for those services, a petition may be filed with the court in the
same proceeding requesting that the court determine the
reasonableness of the attorney’s fees for those services.
If
there is an agreement between the attorney and the client
concerning the attorney’s fees for services performed in
connection with the filing of a petition and obtaining a court
order under this chapter and there is a dispute concerning the
meaning of the agreement, a petition may be filed with the
court in the same proceeding requesting that the court
determine the dispute.
