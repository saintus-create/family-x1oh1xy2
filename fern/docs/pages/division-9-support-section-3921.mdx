# Support

## Section 3921

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Subject to the power of the court to transfer actions and proceedings as provided in the Code of Civil Procedure, a petition filed under this part shall be heard and proceedings thereon held in the superior court in the proper county, which shall be determined as follows: Proceedings on petition; place 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the minor resides in this state, in either of the following counties: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Where the minor resides. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Where the custodian resides. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the minor does not reside within this state, in any of the following counties: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Where the transferor resides. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Where the custodian resides. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Where the estate of a deceased or legally incapacitated custodian is being administered. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Where a parent of the minor resides. 
(c). If neither the minor, nor the transferor, nor any parent resides within this state, and no estate of a deceased or legally incapacitated custodian is being administered within this state, in any county.
