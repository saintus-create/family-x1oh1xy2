# § 5700.703

*Part 6 — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 7 — Support Proceeding Under Convention*

The Department of Child Support Services is recognized as the agency designated by the United States central authority to perform specific functions under the Convention. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
