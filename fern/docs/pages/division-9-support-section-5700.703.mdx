# § 5700.703

*Relationship of Department of Child Support*

Relationship of Department of Child Support
Services to United States central authority
The Department of Child Support Services is recognized as
the agency designated by the United States central authority
to perform specific functions under the Convention. (Added
by Stats.2015,
c. 493
(S.B.646),
§ 5, eff. Jan.
1, 2016.)
