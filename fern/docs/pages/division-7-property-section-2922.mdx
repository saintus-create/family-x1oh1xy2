# Division of Property

## Section 2922

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Letters; bond and oath If the public guardian is appointed as guardian or conservator: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Letters shall be issued in the same manner and by the same proceedings as letters are issued to other persons. 
Letters may be issued to “the public guardian” of the county without naming the public guardian. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The official bond and oath of the public guardian are in lieu of the guardian or conservator’s bond.and oath on the grant of letters.
