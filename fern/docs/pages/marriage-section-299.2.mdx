# 299.2. As a valid domestic partnership

*Marriage*

as a valid domestic partnership
A legal union of two persons * * *, other than a marriage,
that was validly formed in another jurisdiction, and that is
substantially equivalent to a domestic partnership as defined
in this part, shall be recognized as a valid domestic partnership in this state regardless of whether it bears the name
domestic partnership. (Added by Stats.2003, c. 421 (A.B.205),
$ 9, operative Jan. 1, 2005. Amended by Stats.2019, c. 135
(S.B.30), $ 7, eff: Jan. 1, 2020.)
