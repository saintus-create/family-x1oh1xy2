# Division of Property

## Section 2625

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Separate debts incurred before date of separation; confirmation Notwithstanding Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, all separate debts, including those debts incurred by a spouse during marriage and before the date of separation that were not incurred for the benefit of the community, shall be confirmed without offset to the spouse who incurred the debt. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), 
§ 10, operative Jan. 
1, 1994.)
