# General Provisions

## Section 283

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Bigamy; punishment Bigamy is punishable by a fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars ($10,000) or by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding one year or in the state prison. 
(Enacted in 1872. 
Amended by Stats.1905, c. 272, p. 245, § 1; Stats.1949, c. 1252, p. 2205, § 1; Stats.1976, c. 1139, p. 5110, § 172, operative July 1, 1977; Stats.1983, c. 1092, § 264, eff: Sept. 27, 1983, operative Jan. 1, 1984.)
