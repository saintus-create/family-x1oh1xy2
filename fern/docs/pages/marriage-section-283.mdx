# 283. Bigamy is punishable by a fine not exceeding ten thousand

*Marriage*

Bigamy is punishable by a fine not exceeding ten thousand
dollars ($10,000) or by imprisonment in a county jail not
exceeding one year or in the state prison. (Enacted in 1872.
