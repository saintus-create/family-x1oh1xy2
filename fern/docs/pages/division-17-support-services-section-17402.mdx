# Support Services

## Section 17402

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

It also includes past due support or arrearage when it exists. “Support,” when used with reference to a 11 
§ 155 minor child or a child described in <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3901.">Section 3901</a>, includes maintenance and education.
