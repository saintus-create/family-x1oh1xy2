# 5044. Property who relies on affidavit or declaration

*Support*

property who relies on affidavit or declaration
of real
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Nothing in this chapter affects the rights of a purchaser
or encumbrancer of real property for value who in good faith
relies on an affidavit or a declaration under penalty of perjury
under the laws of this state that states all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The name of the decedent.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The date and place of the decedent’s death.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A description of the real property transferred to the
affiant or declarant by an instrument making a nonprobate
transfer or by operation of joint tenancy survivorship.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Either of the following, as appropriate:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The affiant or declarant is the surviving spouse of the
decedent.
not exclusive. See, e.g., Civ. Code 683.2.
Where a joint tenancy involves three or more joint tenants,
severance by operation of this section converts the decedent’s
interest into a tenancy in common, but does not sever the joint
tenancy as between the other joint tenants. For example, husband,
wife, and a third person create a joint tenancy during husband and
wife’s marriage to each other. On husband’s death, wife is not
husband’s surviving spouse and the joint tenancy is severed by
1146
NONPROBATE
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The affiant or declarant is not the surviving spouse of
the decedent, but the rights of the affiant or declarant to the
described property are not affected by Section 5040 or 5042.

,
In General.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A person relying on an affidavit or declaration made
pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  has no duty to inquire into the
truth of the matters stated in the affidavit or declaration.
