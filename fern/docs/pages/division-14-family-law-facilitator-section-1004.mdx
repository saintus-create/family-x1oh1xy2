# 1004

Personal Injury (The Rutter Group, California, Practice Guide) Ch

[7 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports

Personal Injury (The Rutter Group, California, Practice Guide) Ch.
6-A, Scope of Discovery and Limitations.
Commitment or similar proceeding
There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding to
commit the patient or otherwise place him or his property, or
both, under the control of another because of his alleged
mental or physical condition.
(Stats.1965, c. 299, $ 2,
operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
