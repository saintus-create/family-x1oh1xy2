# § 3604.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 4 — Spousal And Child Support During Pendency Of Proceeding*

An order made pursuant to this chapter does not prejudice the rights of the parties or the child with respect to any subsequent order which may be made. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
