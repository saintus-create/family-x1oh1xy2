# 3422. Continuing jurisdiction

*Custody of Children*

Continuing jurisdiction
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in Section 3424, a court
of this state that has made a child custody determination
consistent with Section 3421 or 3423 has exclusive, continuing
jurisdiction over the determination until either of the following occurs:

UNIFORM
CHILD
CUSTODY
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A court of this state or a court of the other state
determines that the child, the child’s parents, and any person
acting as a parent do not presently reside in the other state.
