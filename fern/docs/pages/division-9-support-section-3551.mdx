# 3551

Inapplicability of privilege;

Laws attaching a privilege against the disclosure of communications between spouses are inapplicable under this division. Spouses are competent witnesses to testify to any relevant matter, including marriage and parentage. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 32. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)_
