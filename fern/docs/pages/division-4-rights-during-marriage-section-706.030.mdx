# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 706.030

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Withholding order for support; operation with earnings withholding order 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A “withholding order for. support” is an earnings withholding order issued on a writ of execution to collect delinquent amounts payable under a judgment for the support of a child, or spouse or former spouse, of the judgment debtor. 
A withholding order for support shall be denoted as such on its face. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The local child support agency may issue a withholding order for support on a notice of levy pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17522.">§ 17522</a> of the Family Code to collect a support obligation.
