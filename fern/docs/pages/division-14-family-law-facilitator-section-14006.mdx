# § 14006 — For Medi-Cal)

1-14006.6 (eligibility
for Medi-Cal).
Section 915 provides that a child or spousal support obligation that
does not arise out of the marriage is to be treated as a debt incurred
before marriage.
Hence, such an obligation is governed by the
provisions of Sections 910 and 911.
