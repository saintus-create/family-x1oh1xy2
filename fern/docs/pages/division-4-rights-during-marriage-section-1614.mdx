# 1614 — 1, 2002.

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

1, 2002.
Property § 25
Amendment or revocation of agreements
After marriage, a premarital agreement may be amended
or revoked only by a written agreement signed by the parties.
The amended agreement or the revocation is enforceable
without consideration. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
For further discussion of retroactive application of amendments to
the Family Code, see the Commentary to Family Code sections 4 and
1615.
