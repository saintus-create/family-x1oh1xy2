# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1614

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Amendment or revocation of agreements After marriage, a premarital agreement may be amended or revoked only by a written agreement signed by the parties. 
The amended agreement or the revocation is enforceable without consideration.
