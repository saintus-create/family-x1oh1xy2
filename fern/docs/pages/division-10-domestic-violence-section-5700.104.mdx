# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.104

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Cumulative remedies 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Remedies provided by this part are cumulative and do not affect the availability of remedies under other law or the recognition of a foreign support order on the basis of comity. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This part does not: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  provide the exclusive method of establishing or enforcing a support order under the law of this state; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  grant a tribunal of this state jurisdiction to render judgment or issue an order relating to child custody or visitation in a proceeding under this part.
