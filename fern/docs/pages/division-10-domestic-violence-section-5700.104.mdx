# 5700.104

Cumulative remedies

Cumulative remedies
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Remedies provided by this part are cumulative and do
not affect the availability of remedies under other law or the
recognition of a foreign support order on the basis of comity.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This part does not:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  provide the exclusive method of establishing or enforc-
ing a support order under the law of this state; or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  grant a tribunal of this state jurisdiction to render
judgment or issue an order relating to child custody or
visitation
in a proceeding under this part.
