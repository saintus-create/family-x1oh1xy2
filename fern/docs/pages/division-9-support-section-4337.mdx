# § 4337 — See also Sections 3557 (attorney’s fees for

Except as otherwise agreed by the parties in writing, the obligation of a party under an order for the support of the other party terminates upon the death of either party or the remarriage of the other party. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
