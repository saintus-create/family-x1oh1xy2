# 3080

Presumption of joint custody

There is a presumption, affecting the burden of proof, that joint custody is in the best interest of a minor child, subject to Section 3011, where the parents have agreed to joint custody or so agree in open court at a hearing for the purpose of determining the custody of the minor child. Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.70. Effective January 1, 1994.
