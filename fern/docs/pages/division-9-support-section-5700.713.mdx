# 5700.713
*Part 6  Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 7  Support Proceeding Under Convention*

A record filed with a tribunal of this state under this chapter must be in the original language and, if not in English, must be accompanied by an English translation. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.) FAM Family Code - FAM
