# Minors

## Section 6543

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Termination; limitation; continuation 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A family allowance shall terminate no later than the entry of the order for final distribution of the estate or, if the estate is insolvent, no later than one year after the granting of letters. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Subject to subdivision (a), a family allowance shall continue until modified or terminated by the court or until such time as the court may provide in its order. 
(Stats. 1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
