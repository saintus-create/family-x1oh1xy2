# § 17202 — Title [IV-D state plan administration;

Title [IV-D state plan administration;
state plan
functions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department is hereby designated the single organi-
zational unit whose duty it shall be to administer the Title
IV-D state plan for securing child and spousal support,
medical support, and determining paternity.
State plan
functions shall be performed by other agencies as required by
law, by delegation of the department, or by cooperative
agreements.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The department shall appoint the local child support
agency, as defined in Section 17304, or any other entity
receiving federal tax information in performance of its child

SUPPORT SERVICES
support duties as its designee for purposes of paragraph <span class="subsec">(26)</span> 
of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 11105 of the Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this section, “federal tax information”
is as defined in Section 1044 of the Government Code.
