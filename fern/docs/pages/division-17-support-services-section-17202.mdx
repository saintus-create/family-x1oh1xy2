# Support Services

## Section 17202

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Title [IV-D state plan administration; state plan functions 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department is hereby designated the single organizational unit whose duty it shall be to administer the Title IV-D state plan for securing child and spousal support, medical support, and determining paternity. 
State plan functions shall be performed by other agencies as required by law, by delegation of the department, or by cooperative agreements. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The department shall appoint the local child support agency, as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17304.">§ 17304</a>, or any other entity receiving federal tax information in performance of its child 668 support duties as its designee for purposes of paragraph (26) 
of subdivision (b) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11105.">§ 11105</a> of the Penal Code. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this section, “federal tax information” 
is as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1044.">§ 1044</a> of the Government Code.
