# Adoption

## Section 8906

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Financial responsibility for child; transfer or sharing between agency and prospective adoptive parents Nothing in this chapter may be construed to prohibit the licensed adoption agency from entering into an agreement with the prospective adoptive parents to share or transfer financial responsibility for the child.
