# Adoption

## Section 8910

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Removal of child from county; concealment of child; permission for removal; objections; hearing; notice; application of section 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In no event may a child who has been placed for adoption be removed from the county in which the child was placed, by any person who has not petitioned to adopt the child, without first obtaining the written consent of the licensed adoption agency responsible for the child. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  During the pendency of an adoption proceeding: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child proposed to be adopted may not be concealed within the county in which the adoption proceeding is pending. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child may not be removed from the county in which the adoption proceeding is pending unless the petitioners or other interested persons first obtain permission for the removal from the court, after giving advance written notice of intent to obtain the court’s permission to the licensed adoption agency responsible for the child. 
Upon proof of giving notice, permission may be granted by the court if, within a period of 15 days after the date of giving notice, no objections are filed with the court by the licensed adoption agency responsible for the child. 
If the licensed adoption agency files objections within the 15-day period, upon the request of the petitioners the court shall immediately set the matter for hearing and give to the objector, the petitioners, and the party or parties requesting permission for the removal reasonable notice of the hearing by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the address of each as shown in the records of the adoption proceeding. Upon a finding that the objections are without good cause, the court may grant the requested permission for remoyal of the child, subject to any limitations that appear to be in the child’s best interest. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not apply in any of the following situations: OF 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Where the child is absent for a:period of not more than 30 days from the county in which the adoption proceeding is pending, unless a notice of recommendation of denial of petition has been personally served on the petitioners or the court has issued an order prohibiting the removal of the child from the county pending consideration of any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The suitability of the petitioners. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The care provided the child. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  The availability of the legally required agency consents to the adoption. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Where the child has been returned to and remains in the custody and control of the child’s birth parent or parents. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Where written consent for the removal of the child is obtained from the licensed adoption agency responsible for the child. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A violation of this section is a violation of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=280.">280</a> of the Penal Code. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Neither this section nor <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=280.">280</a> of the Penal Code may be construed to render lawful any act that is unlawful under any other applicable law.
