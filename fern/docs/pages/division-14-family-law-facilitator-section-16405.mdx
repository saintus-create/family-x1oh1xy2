# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16405

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Breach of partnership agreement; violation of duty; action by partnership against partner 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A partnership may maintain an action against a partner for a breach of the partnership agreement, or for the violation of a duty to the partnership, causing harm to the partnership. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A partner may maintain an action against the partnership or another partner for legal or equitable relief, with or without an accounting as to partnership business, to do any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Enforce the partner’s rights under the partnership agreement. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Enforce the partner’s rights under this chapter, including all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The partner’s rights under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16401.">§ 16401</a>, 16403, or 16404. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The partner’s right on dissociation to have the partner’s interest in the partnership purchased pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16701.">§ 16701</a> or 16701.5, or to enforce any other right under Article 6 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16601.">§ 16601</a>) or 7 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16701.">§ 16701</a>). 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  The partner’s right to compel a dissolution and winding up of the partnership business under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16801.">§ 16801</a> or enforce any other right under Article 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16801.">§ 16801</a>). 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Enforce the rights and otherwise protect the interests of the partner, including rights and interests arising independently of the partnership relationship. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The accrual of, and any time limitation on, a right of action for a remedy under this section is governed by other law. A right to an accounting upon a dissolution and winding up does not revive a claim barred by law.
