# California Family Code § 6360

A judgment entered in a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, for legal separation of the parties, in a proceeding brought pursuant to this division, or in an action brought pursuant to the Uniform Parentage Act ( (commencing with Section 7600) of ) may include a protective order as defined in Section 6218.
