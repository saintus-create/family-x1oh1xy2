# Adoption

## Section 8627

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Representation of multiple parties; written agreements If the facilitator is acting on behalf of more than one party, all of the parties on whose behalf the facilitator is acting shall have signed a written agreement authorizing the facilitator to act on behalf of all of the parties.
