# § 2456 — Or upon petition of any person interested in the guardianship

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Upon ex parte petition of the guardian or conservator
or upon petition of any person interested in the guardianship
or conservatorship estate, the court making the order or
giving the judgment referred to in Section 3600 may for good
cause shown order one or more of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  That all or part of the remaining balance of money not
become a part of the guardianship or conservatorship estate
and instead be deposited in an insured account in a financial
institution in this state, or in a single-premium deferred
annuity, subject to withdrawal only upon authorization of the
court.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If there is a guardianship of the estate of the minor,
that all or part of the remaining balance of money and other
property not become a part of the guardianship estate and
instead be transferred to a custodian for the benefit of the
minor under the California Uniform Transfers to Minors Act,
(commencing with Section 3900).
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  That all or part of the remaining balance of money and
other property not become a part of the guardianship estate
