# Division of Property

## Section 2923

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Continuing education requirements On or before January 1, 2008, the public guardian shall comply with the continuing education requirements that are established by the California State Association of Public Administrators, Public Guardians, and Public Conservators.
