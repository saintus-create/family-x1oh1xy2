# § 3906 — Other transfer by fiduciary

CODE
Other transfer by fiduciary
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to subdivision (c), a personal representative or
trustee may make an irrevocable transfer to another adult or
trust company as custodian for the benefit of a minor
pursuant to Section 3909, in the absence of a will or under a
will or trust that does not contain an authorization to do so.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Subject to subdivision (c), a conservator may make an
irrevocable transfer to another adult or trust company as
custodian for the benefit of the minor pursuant to Section
Background on Section 3906 of Repealed Code
Section 3906 was a new provision added by 1984 Cal.Stat. ch. 243
