result in death or substantial bodily harm
Ch.
3,
act likely to
There is no privilege under this article if the lawyer
reasonably believes that disclosure of any confidential communication relating to representation of a client is necessary
to prevent a criminal act that the lawyer reasonably believes is
likely to result in the death of, or substantial bodily harm to,
Ch. 29-F,
Exemptions from Right of Inspection and Copying.
