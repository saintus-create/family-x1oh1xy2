# Support

## Section 4701.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

This provision is included for drafting convenience. 
Throughout this chapter the new term has been substituted, without substantive change, for the former specific references to those persons now referred to as the “deposit holder.” 
See, e.g., Sections 4602-4604, 4610, 4616, 4630, 4640-4641. 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. 
Reports 1 (1993)].
