# § 4701.1 — This

This
provision
is included
for drafting convenience.
Throughout this chapter the new term has been substituted, without
substantive change, for the former specific references to those
persons now referred to as the “deposit holder.”
See, e.g., Sections
4602-4604, 4610, 4616, 4630, 4640-4641.
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.
Reports 1 (1993)].
