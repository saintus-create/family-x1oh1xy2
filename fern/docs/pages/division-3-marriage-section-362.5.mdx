# 362.5

File; access to file

file; access to file
opening of separate court
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The clerk of the superior court shall open a separate
court file for nonminor dependents under the dependency,
delinquency, or transition jurisdiction of the court.
In re T.H., 190 Cal.App.4th 1119, 119 Cal.Rptr.3d 1 (2010), held
that a juvenile court terminating dependency jurisdiction erred when
its exit order granting one parent physical custody and the other
parent supervised visitation “to be determined by the parents” when
the parents did not get along. 7-H. reasoned that the order granted
the custodial parent an effective veto over the other parent’s
. <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Access to the nonminor dependent court file shall be
limited to all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Court personnel.
