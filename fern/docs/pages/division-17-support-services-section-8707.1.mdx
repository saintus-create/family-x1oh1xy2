# § 8707.1 — Nic, racial, and cultural diversity

nic, racial, and cultural diversity
eth-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The agency responsible for recruitment of potential
adoptive parents shall make diligent efforts to recruit individuals who reflect the ethnic, racial, and cultural diversity of
children for whom adoptive homes are needed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall not be construed to affect the
application of the federal Indian Child Welfare Act. (Added
by Stats.2014,
c. 772 (S.B.1460),
§ 1, eff: Jan. 1, 2015.)

,
Ethnic, Cultural, and Jurisdictional Considerations.
