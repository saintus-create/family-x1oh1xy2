# 8707.1

Recruitment of potential adoptive parents;

Recruitment of potential adoptive parents;
eth-
nic, racial, and cultural diversity
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The agency responsible for recruitment of potential
adoptive parents shall make diligent efforts to recruit individ-
uals who reflect the ethnic, racial, and cultural diversity of
children for whom adoptive homes are needed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section shall not be construed
to affect the
application of the federal Indian Child Welfare Act.
