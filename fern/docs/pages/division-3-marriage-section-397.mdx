# 397

Change of place of trial; grounds

Change of place of trial; grounds
The court may, on motion, change the place of trial in the
following cases:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  When the court designated in the complaint is not the
proper court.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  When there is reason to believe that an impartial trial
cannot be had therein.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  When the convenience of witnesses and the ends of
justice would be promoted by the change.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  When from any cause there is no judge of the court
qualified to act.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  When a proceeding for dissolution of marriage has
been filed in the county in which the petitioner has been a
resident for three months next preceding the commencement
of the proceeding, and the respondent at the time of the
commencement of the proceeding is a resident of another
county in this state, to the county of the respondent’s
residence when the ends of justice would be promoted by the
change.
If a motion to change the place of trial is made
pursuant to this paragraph, the court may, prior to the
determination of such motion, consider and determine mo-
tions for allowance of temporary spousal support, support of
children, temporary restraining orders, attorneys’ fees, and
costs, and make all necessary and proper orders in connection
therewith.
(Enacted in 1872. Amended by Stats.1907, c. 369,
p. 701, 5; Stats.1933, c. 744, p. 1843, 9; Stats.1955, c. 832,
p. 1448, 2; Stats.1969, c. 1608, p. 3346, 13, operative Jan.
1, 1970; Stats.1992, c. 163 (A.B.2641), 19, operative Jan. 1,
1994.)
