# 20012. Family Law Evaluator; duties

*Pilot Projects*

Family Law Evaluator; duties
The court shall appoint a Family Law Evaluator, who shall
be available to assist parties. By local.rule the superior court
may designate the duties of the Family Law Evaluator, which
may include, but are not limited to, the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Requiring litigants in actions which involve temporary
child support, temporary spousal support, and temporary
maintenance of health insurance in which at least one litigant
is unrepresented, to meet with the Family Law Evaluator
prior to the support hearing.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Preparing support schedules based on standardized
formulae
accessed through existing up-to-date computer
technology.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Drafting stipulations to include all issues agreed to by
the parties.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Prior to, or at, any hearing pursuant to this chapter,
reviewing the paperwork by the court, advising the judge
whether or not the matter is ready to proceed, and making a
recommendation to the court regarding child support, spous-
al support, and health insurance.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Assisting the clerk in maintaining records.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Preparing a formal order consistent with the court’s
announced oral order, unless one of the parties is represented
by an attorney.

<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Assisting the court with research and any other respon-
sibilities which will enable the court to be responsive to the
litigants’ needs.
