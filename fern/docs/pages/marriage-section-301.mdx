# General Provisions

## Section 301

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Adults; capability to consent to and consummate marriage Two unmarried persons 18 years of age or older, who are not otherwise disqualified, are capable of consenting to and consummating marriage.
