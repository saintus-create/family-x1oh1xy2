# General Provisions

## Section 310

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Methods of dissolution Marriage is dissolved only by one of the following: 
(a) The death of one of the parties. 
(b) A judgment of dissolution of marriage. 
(c) A judgment of nullity of marriage.
