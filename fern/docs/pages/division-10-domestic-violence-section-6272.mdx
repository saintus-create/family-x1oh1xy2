# § 6272

*ing orders*

ing orders
liability of officers enforc-
A law enforcement officer who requests an emergency
protective order shall reduce the order to writing and sign it.
(Added by Stats. 1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 154.)
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A law enforcement officer shall use every reasonable
means to enforce an emergency protective order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A law enforcement officer who acts in good faith to
enforce an emergency protective order is not civilly or
criminally liable. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
