title: "6272. Enforcement of orders; liability of officers enforc-"
description: "Enforcement of orders; liability of officers enforc-"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-6272
---

(a) A law enforcement officer shall use every reasonable means to enforce an emergency protective order.

(b) A law enforcement officer who acts in good faith to enforce an emergency protective order is not civilly or criminally liable.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.*
