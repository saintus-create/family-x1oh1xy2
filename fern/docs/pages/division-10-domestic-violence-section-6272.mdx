# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6272

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Enforcement of orders; liability of officers enforcing orders 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A law enforcement officer shall use every reasonable means to enforce an emergency protective order. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A law enforcement officer who acts in good faith to enforce an emergency protective order is not civilly or criminally liable.
