# 6272

Enforcement of orders; liability of officers enforc-

(a) A law enforcement officer shall use every reasonable means to enforce an emergency protective order.

(b) A law enforcement officer who acts in good faith to enforce an emergency protective order is not civilly or criminally liable. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.
