# § 6272 — Enforcement of orders; liability of officers enforc-

Enforcement of orders; liability of officers enforc-
ing orders
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A law enforcement officer shall use every reasonable
means to enforce an emergency protective order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A law enforcement officer who acts in good faith to
enforce an emergency protective order is not civilly or
criminally liable.
