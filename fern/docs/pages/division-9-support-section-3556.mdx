# Support

## Section 3556

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Child support; failure or refusal to implement custody or visitation rights The existence or enforcement of a duty of support owed by a noncustodial parent for the support of a minor child is not affected by a failure or refusal by the custodial parent to implement any rights as to custody or visitation granted by a court to the noncustodial parent.
