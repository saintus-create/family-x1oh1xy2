# § 3556

*custody or visitation rights*

custody or visitation rights
The existence or enforcement of a duty of support owed by
a noncustodial parent for the support of a minor child is not
affected by a failure or refusal by the custodial parent to
implement any rights as to custody or visitation granted by a
court to the noncustodial parent. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.
An appeal may be taken from an order or judgment under
this division as in other civil actions.
(Stats.1992, c. 162
(A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
