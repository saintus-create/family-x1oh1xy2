# 8425

Court appointment

Court appointment
of fewer than
all persons
named in will as executors;
authority of appointees
If the court does not appoint all the persons named in the
will as executors, those appointed have the same authority to
act in every respect as all would have if appointed.
(Stats.
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
