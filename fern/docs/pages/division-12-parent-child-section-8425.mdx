# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8425

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Court appointment of fewer than all persons named in will as executors; authority of appointees If the court does not appoint all the persons named in the will as executors, those appointed have the same authority to act in every respect as all would have if appointed. 
(Stats. 
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
