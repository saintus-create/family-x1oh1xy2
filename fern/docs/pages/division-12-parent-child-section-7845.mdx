# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7845

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Filing of petition The petition shall be filed in any of the following: 
(a) The county in which a minor described in Chapter 2 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7820.">Section 7820</a>) resides or is found. 
(b) The county in which any of the acts which are set forth in Chapter 2 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7820.">Section 7820</a>) are alleged to have occurred. 
(c) The county in which a petition for the adoption of the child has been filed or the adoption agency to which the child has been relinquished or proposed to be relinquished has an office.
