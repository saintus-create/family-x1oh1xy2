# § 7845 — Filing of petition

Filing of petition
The petition shall be filed in any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The county in which a minor described in Chapter 2
(commencing with Section 7820) resides or is found.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The county in which any of the acts which are set forth
in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 7820) are alleged to
have occurred.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The county in which a petition for the adoption of the
child has been filed or the adoption agency to which the child
has been relinquished or proposed to be relinquished has an
office.
