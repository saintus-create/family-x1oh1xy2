# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 752

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Separate property; interest of parties Except as otherwise provided by statute, neither spouse has any interest in the separate property of the other. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.2014, c. 82 (S.B.1306), § 15, eff: Jan. 1, 2015.)
