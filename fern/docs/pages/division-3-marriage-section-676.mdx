# § 676 — For special definitions of “state” applicable to

1(12).
For special definitions of “state” applicable to
specific acts, see Sections 3402(j) (Uniform Child Custody Jurisdic-
tion Act), 4802(j) (Uniform Reciprocal Enforcement of Support
Act). [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
