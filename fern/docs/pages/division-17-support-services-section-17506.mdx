# 17506. Court of the last known address of the obligor and the name

*Support Services*

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon receiving the requested information, notify the
court of the last known address of the obligor and the name
and address of the obligor’s last known employer.
(Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amend-
ed by Stats.2000, c. 808 (A.B.1358), § 67, eff: Sept. 28, 2000.)
