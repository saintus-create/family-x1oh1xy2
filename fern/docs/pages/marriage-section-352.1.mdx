# General Provisions

## Section 352.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

(a) If a person entitled to bring an action, mentioned in Chapter 3 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=335.">Section 335</a>), is, at the time the cause of action accrued, imprisoned on a criminal charge, or in execution under the sentence of a criminal court for a term less than for life, the time of that disability is not a part of the time limited for the commencement of the action, not to exceed two years. 
Disability of imprisonment 
(b) This section does not apply to an action against a public entity or public employee upon a cause of action for which a claim is required to be presented in accordance with Chapter 1 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=900.">Section 900</a>) or Chapter 2 
(Commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=910.">Section 910</a>) of Part 3, or Chapter 3 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=950.">Section 950</a>) of Part 4, of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=3.6.">Division 3.6</a> of Title 1 of the Government Code. 
This subdivision shall not apply to any claim presented to a public entity prior to January 1, 1971. 
(c) This section does not apply to an action, other than an action to recover damages or that portion of an action that is for the recovery of damages, relating to the conditions of confinement, including an action brought by that person pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1983.">Section 1983</a> of Title 42 of the United States Code.
