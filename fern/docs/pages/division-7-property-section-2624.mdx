# § 2624 — Marital debts incurred after entry of judgment of

Marital debts incurred after entry of judgment of
dissolution or after entry of judgment of legal separa-
tion; confirmation
Debts incurred by either spouse after entry of a judgment
of dissolution of marriage but before termination of the
parties’ marital status or after entry of a judgment of legal
separation of the parties shall be confirmed without offset to

the spouse who incurred
the debt.
