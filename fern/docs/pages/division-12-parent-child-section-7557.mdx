# § 7557 — Right of parties to produce other expert evidence;

Nothing in this part prevents a party to an action or proceeding from producing other expert evidence on the matter covered by this part; but, where other expert witnesses are called by a party to the action or proceeding, their fees shall be paid by the party calling them and only ordinary witness fees shall be taxed as costs in the action or proceeding. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
