# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7557

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Right of parties to produce other expert evidence; payment of fees Nothing in this part prevents a party to an action or proceeding from producing other expert evidence on the matter covered by this part; but, where other expert witnesses are called by a party to the action or proceeding, their fees shall be paid by the party calling them and only ordinary witness fees shali be taxed as costs in the action or proceedPS (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 94.)
