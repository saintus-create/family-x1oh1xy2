# 7557. Right of parties to produce other expert evidence;

*Parent and Child Relationship*

Right of parties to produce other expert evidence;
payment of fees
Nothing in this part prevents a party to an action or
proceeding from producing other expert evidence on the
matter covered by this part; but, where other expert wit-
nesses are called by a party to the action or proceeding, their
fees shall be paid by the party calling them and only ordinary
witness fees shali be taxed as costs in the action or proceed-
PS (Stats.1992,
c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
94.)
