# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16001.9

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Rights of minors and nonminors in foster care 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  All children placed in foster care, either voluntarily or after being adjudged a ward or dependent of the juvenile court pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">§ 300</a>, 601, or 602, shall have the rights specified in this section. 
These rights also apply to nonminor dependents in foster care, except when they conflict with nonminor dependents’ retention of all their legal decisionmaking authority as an adult. 
The rights are as follows: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  To live in a safe, healthy, and comfortable home where they are treated with respect. 
If the child is an Indian child, to live in a home that upholds the prevailing social and cultural standards of the child’s Indian community, including, but not limited to, family, social, and political ties.
