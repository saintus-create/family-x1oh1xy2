# 3912. Duties of custodians;

*Support*

Duties of custodians;
standard of care; records
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A custodian shall do all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Take control of custodial property.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Register or record title to custodial property if appropriate.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Collect, hold, manage,
invest, and reinvest custodial
property.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In dealing with custodial property, a custodian shall
observe the standard of care that would be observed by a
prudent person dealing with property of another and is not
limited by any other statute restricting investments by fiduciaries except that:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If a custodian is not compensated for his or her
services, the custodian is not liable for losses to custodial
property unless they result from the custodian’s bad faith,
intentional wrongdoing, or gross negligence, or from the
custodian’s failure to maintain the standard of prudence in
investing the custodial property provided in this section:
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A custodian, in the custodian’s discretion and without
liability to the minor or the minor’s estate, may retain any
custodial property received from a transferor.

insurance or endowment policies on <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the life of the minor
only if the minor or the minor’s estate is the sole beneficiary
