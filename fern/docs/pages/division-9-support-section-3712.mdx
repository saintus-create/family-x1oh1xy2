The time within which an absentee may commence an
action against any person who executes an affidavit and
receives property pursuant to this article commences to run
on the earlier of the following dates:
Limitation of actions
(a)  Ninety days after the absentee returns to the United
States after the termination of the condition that caused the
classification of an absentee.
(b)  Two years after the termination of the condition that
caused the classification of an absentee.
