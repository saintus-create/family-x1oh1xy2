# Support Services

## Section 17530

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Support enforcement action; allegation of error due to mistaken identity; administrative and judicial remedies; penalty 
(a) Notwithstanding any other law, this section applies to any actions taken to enforce a judgment or order for support entered as a result of action filed by the local child support agency pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17400.">Section 17400</a>, 17402, or 17404, where it is alleged that the enforcement actions have been taken in error against a person who is not the support obligor named in the judgment or order. 
(b) A person claiming that a support enforcement action has been taken against that person, or the person’s. wages or assets, in error, shall file a claim of mistaken identity with the local child support agency. 
The claim shall include verifiable information or documentation to establish that the person against whom the enforcement actions have been taken is not the person named in the support order or judgment.
