# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1473.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Circumstances under which writ of habeas corpus may be prosecuted relating to evidence of intimate partner battering and its effects; limitation to Section violent felonies; expert testimony; grounds for denial; legislative intent.
