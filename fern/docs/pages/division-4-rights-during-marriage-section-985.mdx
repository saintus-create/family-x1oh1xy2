# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 985

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Criminal proceedings There is no privilege under this article: in a criminal proceeding in which one spouse is charged with: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A crime committed at any time against the person or property of the other spouse or of a child of either. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A crime committed at any time against the person or property of a third person committed in the course of committing a crime against the person or property of the other spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Bigamy. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A crime defined by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=270.">§ 270</a> or 270a of the Penal Code.
