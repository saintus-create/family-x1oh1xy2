# 6382 — Availability of information concerning orders; law

Prevention of Domestic Violence

Availability of information concerning orders; law
enforcement officers
Each appropriate law enforcement agency shall make
available to any law enforcement officer responding to the
scene of reported domestic violence, through an existing
system for verification, information as to the existence, terms,
and current status of an order issued under this part. (Added
by Stats. 1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
