# 6382

Availability of information concerning orders; law

Each appropriate law enforcement agency shall make available to any law enforcement officer responding to the scene of reported domestic violence, through an existing system for verification, information as to the existence, terms, and current status of an order issued under this part. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.
