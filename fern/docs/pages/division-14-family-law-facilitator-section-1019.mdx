# § 1019

*tient*

tient
Comments

Parties claiming through deceased pa-
There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue between parties all of whom claim
through a deceased patient, regardless of whether the claims
are by testate or intestate succession or by inter vivos
transaction.
(Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
