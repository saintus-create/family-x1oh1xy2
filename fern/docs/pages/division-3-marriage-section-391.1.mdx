Motion for order requiring security; grounds
In any litigation pending in any court of this state, at any
time until final judgment is entered, a defendant may move
the court, upon notice and hearing, for an order requiring the
plaintiff to furnish security or for an order dismissing the
litigation pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 391.3. The
motion for an order requiring the plaintiff to furnish security
shall be based upon the ground, and supported by a showing,
that the plaintiff is a vexatious litigant and that there is not a
reasonable probability that he or she will prevail in the
litigation against the moving defendant.
