# 5700.604

Choice of law

Choice of law
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in subsection (d), the law
of the issuing state or foreign country governs:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the nature, extent, amount, and duration of current
payments under a registered support order;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the computation and payment of arrearages and accru-
al of interest on the arrearages under the support order; and
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  the existence and satisfaction of other obligations
under the support order.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In a proceeding for arrears under a registered support
order, the statute of limitation of this state, or of the issuing
state or foreign country, whichever is longer, applies.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A responding tribunal of this state shall apply the
procedures and remedies of this state to enforce current
support and collect arrears and interest due on a support
order of another state or a foreign country registered in this
state.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  After a tribunal of this state or another state deter-
mines which is the controlling order and issues an order
consolidating arrears, if any, a tribunal of this state shall
prospectively apply the law of the state or foreign country
issuing the controlling order, including its law on interest on
arrears, On current and future support, and on consolidated
arrears.
