# § 242 — Deadline for hearing on the petition

Deadline for hearing on the petition
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Within 21 days, or, if good cause appears to the court,
25 days from the date that a temporary restraining order is

EX PARTE TEMPORARY RESTRAINING ORDERS
granted or denied, a hearing shall be held on the petition.
If
no request for a temporary restraining order is made, the
hearing shall be held within 21 days, or, if good cause appears
to the court, 25 days from the date that the petition is filed.
