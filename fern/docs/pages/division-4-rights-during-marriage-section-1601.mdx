# 1601

Effective date of chapter

This chapter is effective on and after January 1, 1986, and applies to any premarital agreement executed on or after that date. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
