# California Family Code § 400

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Although marriage is a personal relation arising out of a civil, and not a religious, contract, a marriage may be solemnized by a priest, minister, rabbi, or authorized person of any religious denomination who is 18 years of age or older. A person authorized by this subdivision shall not be required to solemnize a marriage that is contrary to the tenets of the person’s faith. Refusal to solemnize a marriage under this subdivision, either by an individual or by a religious denomination, shall not affect the tax-exempt status of any entity.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Consistent with Section 94.5 of the Penal Code and provided that any compensation received is reasonable, including payment of actual expenses, a marriage may also be solemnized by any of the following persons:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A judge or retired judge, commissioner of civil marriages or retired commissioner of civil marriages, commissioner or retired commissioner, or assistant commissioner of a court of record in this state.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A judge or magistrate who has resigned from office.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Any of the following judges or magistrates of the United States:

<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A justice or retired justice of the United States Supreme Court.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A judge or retired judge of a court of appeals, a district court, or a court created by an act of the United States Congress the judges of which are entitled to hold office during good behavior.

<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A judge or retired judge of a bankruptcy court or a tax court.

<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A United States magistrate or retired magistrate.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Except as provided in subdivision (d), a marriage may also be solemnized by any of the following persons who are 18 years of age or older:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A Member of the Legislature or constitutional officer of this state or a Member of Congress of the United States who represents a district within this state, or a former Member of the Legislature or constitutional officer of this state or a former Member of Congress of the United States who represented a district within this state.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person that holds or formerly held an elected office of a city, county, or city and county.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A city clerk of a charter city or serving in accordance with subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 36501 of the Government Code, while that person holds office.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A person listed in subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  shall not accept compensation for solemnizing a marriage while holding office.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A person listed in subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  shall not solemnize a marriage pursuant to this section if they have been removed from office due to committing an offense or have been convicted of an offense that involves moral turpitude, dishonesty, or fraud.
