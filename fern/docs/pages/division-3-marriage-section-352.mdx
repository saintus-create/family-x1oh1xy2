# § 352

*Grounds for denial of license*

Grounds for denial of license
No marriage license shall be granted if either of the
applicants lacks the capacity to enter into a valid marriage or
is, at the time of making the application for the license, under

MARRIAGE
the influence of an intoxicating liquor or narcotic drug.
