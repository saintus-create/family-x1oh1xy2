# § 391.6 — Stay of proceedings

Stay of proceedings
Except as provided in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 391.3,
when a motion pursuant to Section 391.1 is filed prior to trial
the litigation is stayed, and the moving defendant need not
plead, until 10 days after the motion shall have been denied,
or if granted, until 10 days after the required security has
been furnished and the moving defendant given written
notice thereof. When a motion pursuant to Section 391.1 is
made at any time thereafter, the litigation shall be stayed for
such period after the denial of the motion or the furnishing of
the required security as the court shall determine. (Added by
