# Custody of Children

## Section 3089

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Conciliation court; consultation by court or parties In counties having a conciliation court, the court or the parties may, at any time, pursuant to local rules of court, consult with the conciliation court for the purpose of assisting the parties to formulate a plan for implementation of the custody order or to resolve a controversy which has arisen in the implementation of a plan for custody.
