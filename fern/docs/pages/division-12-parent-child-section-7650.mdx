# § 7650

(a) Any interested person may bring an action to determine the existence or nonexistence of a mother and child relationship. Insofar as practicable, the provisions of this division applicable to the father and child relationship apply.

(b) A woman is presumed to be the natural mother of a child if the child is in utero after the death of the decedent and the conditions set forth in Section 249.5 of the Probate Code are satisfied. _(Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 63. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)_<div class="source-note">
