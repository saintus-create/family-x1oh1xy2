Blank forms; assistance in preparing and present-
ing petition; references; coextensive jurisdiction
(a)  The clerk of the court shall provide, at the expense of
the court, blank forms for petitions for filing pursuant to this
part.
(b)  The probation officers of the county and the attachés
and employees of the family conciliation court shall assist a
person in the preparation and presentation of a petition
under this part if the person requests assistance.
(c)  All public officers in each county shall refer to the
family conciliation court all petitions and complaints made to
them in respect to controversies within the jurisdiction of the
family conciliation court.
(d)  The jurisdiction of the family conciliation court in
respect to controversies arising out of an instance is not exclusive but is coextensive with any other
remedies either civil or criminal in nature that may be
available.
