# § 3022.5 — Motion by parent for reconsideration of child

Motion by parent for reconsideration of child
custody order after conviction of spouse for false accusa-
tion of child abuse against parent
A motion by a parent for reconsideration of an existing
child custody order shall be granted if the motion is based on
the fact that the other parent was convicted of a crime in
connection with falsely accusing the moving parent of child
abuse.
