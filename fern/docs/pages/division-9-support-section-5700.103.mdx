# 5700.103
*Part 6  Uniform Interstate Family Support Act · Chapter 1  General Provisions*

(a) The superior court is the tribunal of this state.

(b) The Department of Child Support Services is the support enforcement agency of this state. (Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
