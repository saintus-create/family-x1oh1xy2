# § 5700.103

*State tribunal and state enforcement agency*

State tribunal and state enforcement agency
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The superior court is the tribunal of this state.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The Department of Child Support Services
is the
support enforcement agency of this state.
