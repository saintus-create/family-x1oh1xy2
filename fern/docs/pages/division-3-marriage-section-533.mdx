Section 580 is amended to accommodate unification of the

The last sentence of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  continues former Section 86(c)

Subdivision (b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  makes explicit that although the jurisdiction of a
unified superior court includes matters in which the amount in
controversy exceeds the maximum for a limited civil case as provided
in Section 85, the court cannot grant substantive relief exceeding that
maximum in a limited civil case. Formerly, each county had one or
more municipal courts and a superior court, and the jurisdictional
limit of the municipal courts constrained the relief awardable in
matters tried in those courts. See Stokus v. Marsh, 217 Cal.App.3d
647, 653, 266 Cal.Rptr. 90 (1990) (‘we view the jurisdictional limit of
