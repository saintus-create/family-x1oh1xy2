# California Family Code § 533

An approval to issue confidential marriage licenses pursuant to this chapter is valid for one year. The approval may be renewed for additional one-year periods provided the following conditions are met:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The applicant has not violated any of the provisions provided for in Section 531.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The applicant has successfully completed the course prescribed in Section 532.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The applicant has paid the renewal fee provided for in Section 536.
