# § 7135 — Effect upon contractual and property obligations

Voiding or rescission of the declaration of emancipation does not alter any contractual obligation or right or any property right or interest that arose during the period that the declaration was in effect. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM3.
