# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7135

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Effect upon contractual and property obligations Voiding or rescission of the declaration of emancipation does not alter any contractual obligation or right or any property right or interest that arose during the period that the 481 
§ 7141 declaration was in effect.
