Sexual battery by adult in position of authority
over minor; consent as defense
(a)  Notwithstanding Section 3515, consent shall not be a
defense in any civil action under Section 1708.5 if the person
who commits the sexual battery is an adult who is in a
position of authority over the minor.
(b)  For purposes of this section, an adult is in a “position
of authority” if he or she, by reason of that position, is able to
exercise undue influence over a minor. A “position .
authority” includes, but is not limited to, a natural parent,
stepparent, foster parent, relative, partner of any such parent
or relative, caretaker, youth leader, recreational. director,
