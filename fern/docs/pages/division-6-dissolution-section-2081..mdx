# § 2081.

*Part 1 — General Provisions · Chapter 7 — Restoration Of Spouse’S Former Name*

The restoration of a former name or birth name requested under Section 2080 shall not be denied on the basis that the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different name, or for any other reason except fraud. (Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 780, Sec. 3. (SB 1150) Effective January 1, 2025.)
