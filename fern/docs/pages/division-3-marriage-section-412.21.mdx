The word
“judgment” has been substituted
for “decree.”
This is not
a
substantive change. See Section 100 (“judgment” includes decree, as
appropriate).
Subdivision (b)  continues without substantive change the fifth
paragraph of subdivision (a)  and the second paragraph of subdivision
(b)  of former Code of Civil Procedure
