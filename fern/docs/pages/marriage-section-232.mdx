# General Provisions

## Section 232

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Statement in summons as to enforceability of order The summons shall state on its face that the order is enforceable in any place in this state by any law enforcement agency that has received mailed notice of the order or has otherwise received a copy of the order and any officer who has been shown a copy of the order.
