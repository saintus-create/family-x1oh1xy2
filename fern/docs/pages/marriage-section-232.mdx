# § 232 — Statement in summons as to enforceability of order

Statement in summons as to enforceability of order
The summons shall state on its face that the order is
enforceable in any place in this state by any law enforcement
agency that has received mailed notice of the order or has
otherwise received a copy of the order and any officer who
has been shown a copy of the order.
