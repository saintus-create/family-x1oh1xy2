# Support

## Section 4617

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Deposit of real property; certification of order as abstract of judgment; recordation 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the asset ordered to be deposited is real property, the order shall be certified as an abstract of judgment in accordance with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=674.">§ 674</a> of the Code of Civil Procedure. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A deposit of real property is made effective by recordation of the certified abstract with the county recorder. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The deposited real property and the rights, benefits, and liabilities attached to that property shall continue in the possession of the legal owner.
