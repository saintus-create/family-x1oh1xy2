# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.709

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Partial enforcement If a tribunal of this state does not recognize and enforce a Convention support order in its entirety, it shall enforce any severable part of the order. An application or direct request may seek recognition and partial.enforcement of a Convention support order.
