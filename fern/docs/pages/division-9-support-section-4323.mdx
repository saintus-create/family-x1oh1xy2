# § 4323 — Cohabitation with nonmarital partner; rebuttable

Cohabitation with nonmarital partner; rebuttable
presumption of decreased need for support; modifica-
tion or termination of support
(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Except as otherwise agreed to by the parties in
writing, there is a rebuttable presumption, affecting the
burden of proof, of decreased need for spousal support if the
supported party is cohabiting with a nonmarital partner.
Upon a determination that circumstances have changed, the
court may modify or terminate
the spousal support
as
provided for in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 3650) of
.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Holding oneself out to be the spouse of the person
with whom one is cohabiting is not necessary to constitute
cohabitation as the term is used in this subdivision.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The income
of a supporting spouse’s subsequent
spouse or nonmarital partner shall not be considered when
determining or modifying spousal support.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section precludes later modification or
termination
of spousal support on proof of change of
circumstances.
