# § 1616 — Void marriage, effect on agreement

If a marriage is determined to be void, an agreement that would otherwise have been a premarital agreement is enforceable only to the extent necessary to avoid an inequitable result. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
