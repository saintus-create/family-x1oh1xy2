# § 1616

*Void marriage, effect on agreement*

Void marriage, effect on agreement
If a marriage is determined to be void, an agreement that
would otherwise have been a premarital agreement is enforceable only to the extent necessary to avoid an inequitable
result. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. I,
1994.)
