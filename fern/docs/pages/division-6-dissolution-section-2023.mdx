# § 2023 — Payment of obligation directly to creditor

Payment of obligation directly to creditor
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  On a determination that payment of an obligation of a
party would benefit either party or a child for whom support
may be ordered, the court may order one of the parties to pay
the obligation, or a portion thereof, directly to the creditor.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The creditor has no right to enforce the order made
under this section, nor are the creditor’s rights affected by the
determination made under this section.
