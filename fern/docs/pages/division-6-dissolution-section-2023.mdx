# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2023

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Payment of obligation directly to creditor 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  On a determination that payment of an obligation of a party would benefit either party or a child for whom support may be ordered, the court may order one of the parties to pay the obligation, or a portion thereof, directly to the creditor. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The creditor has no right to enforce the order made under this section, nor are the creditor’s rights affected by the determination made under this section.
