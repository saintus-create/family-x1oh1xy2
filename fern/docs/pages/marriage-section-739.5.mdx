# § 739.5 — Subparagraph (A) shall address all of the following:

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The rules of court and forms developed pursuant to
subparagraph <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  shall address all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  The child and their caregiver and court-appoint-
ed special advocate, if any, have an opportunity to provide
input on the medications being prescribed.
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  Information regarding the child’s overall mental health
assessment and treatment plan is provided to the court.
(iii) Information regarding the rationale for the proposed
medication, provided in the context of past and current
treatment efforts, is provided to the court.
This information
shall include, but not be limited to, information on other
pharmacological and nonpharmacological treatments
that
have been utilized and the child’s response to those treat-
ments, a discussion of symptoms not alleviated or ameliorat-
ed by other current
or past treatment
efforts, and an
explanation of how the psychotropic medication being pre-
scribed is expected to improve the child’s symptoms.
<span class="subsec">(iv)</span>  Guidance is provided to the court on how to evaluate
the request for authorization, including how to proceed if
information, otherwise required to be included in a request
for authorization under this section, is not included in a
request for authorization submitted to the court.
