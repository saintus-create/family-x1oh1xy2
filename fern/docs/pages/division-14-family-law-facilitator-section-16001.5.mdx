# § 16001.5 — Promotion of self-esteem

Promotion of self-esteem
distribution of information
in foster
children;
The State Department of Social Services shall annually
distribute information declaring the importance of promoting
self-esteem with respect to foster children to all of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Each county independent living program administrator.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Each licensed foster family agency, group home, and
small family home.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Each county welfare department.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Each county director of child protective services.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Each county director of social services.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Each county foster home services director.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  The Director of the Community Care Licensing Division of the State Department of Social Services.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  The Director of State Adoptions Branch of the State
Department of Social Services. (Added by Stats.1997, c. 542
(S.B.916), § 3.)
