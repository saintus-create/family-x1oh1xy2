# § 5700.502

*Employer’s compliance with income-withholding order of another state*

Employer’s compliance with income-withholding order of another state
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Upon receipt of an income-withholding order, the
obligor’s employer shall immediately provide a copy of the
order to the obligor.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The employer shall treat an income-withholding order
issued in another state which appears regular on its face as if
it had been issued by a tribunal of this state.
