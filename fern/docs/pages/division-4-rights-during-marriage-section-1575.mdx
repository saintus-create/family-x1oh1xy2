# § 1575 — Undue influence

Undue influence
UNDUE INFLUENCE, WHAT.
Undue influence consists:
1.
In the use, by one in whom a confidence is reposed by
another, or who holds a real or apparent authority over him,
of such confidence or authority for the purpose of obtaining
an unfair advantage over him;

2.
In taking an unfair advantage of another’s weakness of
mind; or,
3.
In taking a grossly oppressive and unfair advantage of
another’s necessities or distress.
(Enacted in 1872.)
