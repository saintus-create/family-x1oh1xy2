# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1575

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Undue influence , . 
Undue influence consists: 1. 
In the use, by one in whom a confidence is reposed by another, or who holds a real or apparent authority over him, of such confidence or authority for the purpose of obtaining an unfair advantage over him; 771 
§ 1577 2. 
In taking an unfair advantage of another’s weakness of mind; or, 3. 
In taking a grossly oppressive and unfair advantage of another’s necessities or distress. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
