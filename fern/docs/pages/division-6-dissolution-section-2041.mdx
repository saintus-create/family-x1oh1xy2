title: "2041. Application of temporary restraining order provi-"
description: "Application of temporary restraining order provi-"
slug: division-6-dissolution-section-2041

Nothing in Section 2040 adversely affects the rights, title, and interest of a purchaser for value, encumbrancer for value, or lessee for value who is without actual knowledge of the restraining order.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 106.7. Effective January 1, 1994.*
