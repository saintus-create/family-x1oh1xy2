# § 2041

*See generally Schnabel v. Superior Court, 21 Cal.App.4th 548, 26*

See generally Schnabel v. Superior Court, 21 Cal.App.4th 548, 26
Cal. Rptr.2d 169 (1993) (Schnabel III).
Nothing in Section 2040 adversely affects the rights, title,
and interest of a purchaser for value, encumbrancer for value,
or lessee for value who is without actual knowledge of the
restraining order. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
$ 106.7.)
