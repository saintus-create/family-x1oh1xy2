# 1541. Petition for guardianship;

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Petition for guardianship;
1200-1221,
1460-1469.
See also Sections 1260-1265
(proof of giving notice). For background on the provisions of this
part, see the Comment to this part under the part heading. [20
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1001 (1990)].
additional contents
In addition to the other required contents of the petition
for appointment of a guardian, the petition shall include both
of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A statement by the proposed guardian that, upon
request by an agency referred to in Section 1543 for
information relating to the investigation referred to in that
section, the proposed guardian will promptly submit the
information required.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A disclosure of any petition for adoption by the
proposed guardian of the minor who is the subject of the
guardianship petition regardless of when or where filed.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A statement whether or not the home of the proposed
guardian is a licensed foster family home, a certified family
home of a licensed foster family agency, or a resource family
home approved by a county or a licensed foster family agency.

,
In General.
,
Additional Requirements for Nonrelative Guardianship.
