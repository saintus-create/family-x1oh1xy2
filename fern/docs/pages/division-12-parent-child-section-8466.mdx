# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8466

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Creditor; court denial of appointment If a person whose only priority is that of a creditor claims appointment as administrator, the court in its discretion may deny the appointment and appoint another person. 
(Stats. 
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
