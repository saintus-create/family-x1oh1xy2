# § 8466

*Creditor; court denial of appointment*

Creditor; court denial of appointment
If a person whose only priority is that of a creditor claims
appointment as administrator, the court in its discretion may
deny the appointment and appoint another person.
(Stats.
1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
