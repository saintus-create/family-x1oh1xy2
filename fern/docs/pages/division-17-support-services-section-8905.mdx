# 8905

Domestic and foreign adoption agencies;

Licensed adoption agencies may work only with domestic and foreign adoption agencies with whom they have written agreements that specify the responsibilities of each. The agreements may not violate any statute or regulation of the United States or of this state. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
