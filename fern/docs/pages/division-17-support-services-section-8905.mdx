# 8905. With state agencies; written agreement required

*Support Services*

with state agencies; written agreement required
work
Licensed adoption agencies may work only with domestic
and foreign adoption agencies with whom they have written
agreements that specify the responsibilities of each. The
agreements may not violate any statute or regulation of the
United States or of this state. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),

Section 8903 continues former Civil Code Section 226.21 without
substantive change. See also Sections 8527 (“intercountry adoption”
defined), 8530 (“licensed adoption agency” defined), 8542 (“‘prospective adoptive parent” defined). [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1
(1993)].
$ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
