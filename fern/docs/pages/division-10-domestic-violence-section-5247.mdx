Civil liability of local child support agency or
employer
Neither the local child support agency nor an employer
shall be subject to any civil liability for any amount withheld
and paid to the obligee, the local child support agency, or the
State Disbursement Unit pursuant to an earnings assignment
order or notice of assignment.
