# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5247

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Civil liability of local child support agency or employer Neither the local child support agency nor an employer shall be subject to any civil liability for any amount withheld and paid to the obligee, the local child support agency, or the State Disbursement Unit pursuant to an earnings assignment order or notice of assignment.
