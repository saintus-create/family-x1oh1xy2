# § 356

*, operative*

§ 12, operative
Section 357 restates without substantive change the second paragraph of former Civil Code Section 4204. In subdivision (c), the
phrase “person solemnizing their marriage” has been substituted for
“person marrying them” to conform with other sections in this
division. See, e.g., Section 420. [23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1
