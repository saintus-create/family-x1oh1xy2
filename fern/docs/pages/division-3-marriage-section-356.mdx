Expiration of license
A marriage license issued pursuant to this part expires 90
days after its issuance.
The calendar date of expiration shall
be clearly noted on the face of the license.
(Stats. 1992, c. 162
(A.B.2650), 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
