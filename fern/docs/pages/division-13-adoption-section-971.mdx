# 971. Except as otherwise provided by statute, a married person

*Adoption*

Except as otherwise provided by statute, a married person
whose spouse is a party to a proceeding has a privilege not to
be called as a witness by an adverse party to that proceeding
without the prior express consent of the spouse having the
privilege under this section unless the party calling the spouse
does so in good faith without knowledge of the marital
relationship.
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A crime against the person or property of the other
spouse or of a child, parent, relative, or cohabitant of either,
whether committed before or during marriage.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A crime against the person or property of a third
person committed in the course of committing a crime
against the person or property of the other spouse, whether
committed before or during marriage.
(Stats.1965, c. 299, § 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
