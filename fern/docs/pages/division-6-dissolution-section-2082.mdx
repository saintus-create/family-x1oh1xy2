# § 2082

*Common law rights*

Common law rights
included in the petition.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
Nothing in this code shall be construed to abrogate the
common law right of any person to change one’s name.
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
(S.B.1033), $ 5.)
