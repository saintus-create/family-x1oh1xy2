---
title: "2082. Common law rights"
description: "Common law rights"
slug: division-6-dissolution-section-2082
---

This code does not abrogate the common law right of any person to change one’s name. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 780, Sec. 4. (SB 1150) Effective January 1, 2025.*
