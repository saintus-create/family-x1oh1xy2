# § 6241.

*Part 3 — Emergency Protective Orders · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

The presiding judge of the superior court in each county shall designate at least one judge, commissioner, or referee to be reasonably available to issue orally, by telephone or otherwise, emergency protective orders at all times whether or not the court is in session. (Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
