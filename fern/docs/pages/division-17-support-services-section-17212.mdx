# Support Services

## Section 17212

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Privacy rights; confidentiality of records 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  It is the intent of the Legislature to protect individual rights of privacy, and to facilitate and enhance the effectiveness of the child and spousal support enforcement program, by ensuring the confidentiality of support enforcement and child abduction records, and to thereby encourage the full and frank disclosure of information relevant to all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The establishment or maintenance of parent and child relationships and support obligations. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The enforcement of the child support liability of absent parents.
