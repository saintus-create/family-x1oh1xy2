---
title: "8618. Adopted child may take adoptive parent’s family"
description: "Adopted child may take adoptive parent’s family"
slug: division-17-support-services-section-8618
---

A child adopted pursuant to this part may take the family name of the adoptive parent.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
