# § 351.6 — Mailing address

Mailing address
Notwithstanding Section 307, 351, 351.5, 359, or 422 of this
code, or Section 103175 or 103180 of the Health and Safety
Code, a mailing address used by an applicant, witness, or
person solemnizing or performing the marriage ceremony
shall be a residential address, a business address, or a United
States Postal Service post office box.
