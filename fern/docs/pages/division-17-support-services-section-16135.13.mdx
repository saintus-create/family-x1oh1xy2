# 16135.13. Recruited adoptive parents; special training

*Support Services*

Recruited adoptive parents; special training
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A participating county shall provide special training to
recruited adoptive parents to care for eligible children.
The
training curriculum shall include, but is not limited to, all of
the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Orientation.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Effect of alcohol and controlled substances on the
fetus and children.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Normal and abnormal infant and early childhood
development.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Special medical needs and disabilities.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Recovery from addiction to alcohol and controlled
substances.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Self-care for the caregiver.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  HIV/AIDS in children.
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  Issues in parenting and providing lifelong permanency
and substance abuse prevention to, children with prenatal
alcohol and other controlled substances exposure.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  Issues specific to caring for a child who tests HIV
positive.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Participating counties may provide the same special
training to relative caretakers in the process of adopting
program-eligible children.
