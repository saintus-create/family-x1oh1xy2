# § 679

*Absolute ownership defined*

Absolute ownership defined
WHEN ABSOLUTE. The ownership of property is absolute
when a single person has the absolute dominion over it, and
may use it or dispose of it according to his pleasure, subject
only to general laws. (Enacted in 1872.)
