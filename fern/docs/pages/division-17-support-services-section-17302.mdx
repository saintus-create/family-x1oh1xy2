# § 17302 — Duties

Duties
The director shall do all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Be responsible for the management of the department.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Administer all federal and state laws and regulations
pertaining to the administration of child support enforcement
obligations.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Perform all duties as may be prescribed by law, and any
other administrative and executive duties imposed by law.
