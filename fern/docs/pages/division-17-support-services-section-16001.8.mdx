# 16001.8 — (a) The State Department of Social Services shall convene

Support Services

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The State Department of Social Services shall convene
a working group regarding the rights of all minors and
nonminors in foster care, as specified in Section 16001.9, in
order to educate foster youth, foster care providers, and
others. Responsibilities of the working group shall include
all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  By January 1, 2018, make recommendations to the
Legislature for revising the rights based on a review of state
law.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  By July 1, 2018, develop standardized information
regarding the revised rights in an age-appropriate manner
and reflective of any relevant licensing requirements with
respect to the foster care providers’ responsibilities to adequately supervise children in care.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  By July 1, 2018, develop recommendations regarding
methods for disseminating the standardized information
specified in paragraph (2), including whether to require the
signature of a foster child verifying that he or she has
received and understands his or her rights.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  By July 1, 2018, develop recommendations for measuring and improving, if necessary, the degree to which foster
youth are adequately informed of their rights.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The working group shall be composed of all of the
following:
Z
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The Office of the State Foster Care Ombudsperson.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The bureau at the Department of Justice whose
mission is to protect the rights of children.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The County Welfare Directors Association of California.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The Chief Probation Officers of California.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The County Behavioral Health Directors Association
of California.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Current and former foster youth.
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Foster parents and caregivers.
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  Foster children advocacy groups.
<span class="subsec">(9)</span>  Foster care provider associations.
<span class="subsec">(10)</span>  Any other interested parties. (Added by Stats.2016, c.
851 (A.B.1067), § 1, eff. Jan. 1, 2017.)
