# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6227

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Remedies in this chapter additional to other remedies The remedies provided in this division are in addition to any other civil or criminal remedies that may be available to the petitioner.
