title: "6227. Remedies in this chapter additional to other reme-"
description: "Remedies in this chapter additional to other reme-"

The remedies provided in this division are in addition to any other civil or criminal remedies that may be available to the petitioner.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.*
