# 6227 — Remedies in this chapter additional to other reme-

Prevention of Domestic Violence

Remedies in this chapter additional to other reme-
dies
The remedies provided in this division are in addition to
any other civil or criminal remedies that may be available to
the petitioner.
(Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
