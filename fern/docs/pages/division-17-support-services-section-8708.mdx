*or child; nonresident status of adoptive parent*

or child; nonresident status of adoptive parent
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The adoption agency to which a child has been freed
for adoption by either relinquishment or termination of
parental rights shall not do any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Deny to any person the opportunity to become an
adoptive parent on the basis of the race, color, or national
origin of the person or the child involved.

assessment standards or approved as a resource family
