Powers of guardian or conservator
The guardian or conservator may do any of the following:
(a)  Contract for the guardianship or conservatorship, per-
form outstanding contracts, and, thereby, bind the estate.
(b)  Purchase tangible personal property.
(c)  Subject to the provisions of Chapter 8 (commencing
with Section 2640), employ an attorney
to advise and
represent the guardian or conservator in all matters, includ-
ing the conservatorship proceeding and all other actions or
proceedings.
~
(d)  Employ and pay the expense of accountants, invest-
ment advisers, agents, depositaries, and employees.
(e)  Operate for a period of 45 days after the issuance of
the letters of guardianship or conservatorship, at the risk of
the estate, a business, farm, or enterprise constituting an asset
of the estate.
