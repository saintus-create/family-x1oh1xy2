# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2451.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Powers of guardian or conservator The guardian or conservator may do any of the following: 
(a) Contract for the guardianship or conservatorship, perform outstanding contracts, and, thereby, bind the estate. 
(b) Purchase tangible personal property. 
(c) Subject to the provisions of Chapter 8 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2640.">Section 2640</a>), employ an attorney to advise and represent the guardian or conservator in all matters, including the conservatorship proceeding and all other actions or proceedings. 
~ 
(d) Employ and pay the expense of accountants, investment advisers, agents, depositaries, and employees. 
(e) Operate for a period of 45 days after the issuance of the letters of guardianship or conservatorship, at the risk of the estate, a business, farm, or enterprise constituting an asset of the estate.
