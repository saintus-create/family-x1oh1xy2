# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 170.4

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Powers of disqualified judges 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A disqualified judge, notwithstanding his or her disqualification may do any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Take any action or issue any order necessary to maintain the jurisdiction of the court pending the assignment of a judge not disqualified. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Request any other judge agreed upon by the parties to sit and act in his or her place. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Hear and determine purely default matters. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Issue an order for possession prior to judgment in eminent domain proceedings. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Set proceedings for trial or hearing. 
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  Conduct settlement conferences.
