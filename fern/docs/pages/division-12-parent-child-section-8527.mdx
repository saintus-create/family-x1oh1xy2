# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 8527

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

“Intercountry adoption” means the adoption of a foreignborn child for whom federal law makes a special immigration visa available. 
Intercountry adoption includes completion of the adoption in the child’s native country or completion of the adoption in this state.
