# § 8527

“Intercountry adoption” means the adoption of a foreignborn child for whom federal law makes a special immigration
visa available. Intercountry adoption includes completion of
the adoption in the child’s native country or completion of
the adoption in this state.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),
