---
title: "2127. Actions or motions; statement of decision"
description: "Actions or motions; statement of decision"
slug: division-6-dissolution-section-2127
---

As to actions or motions filed under this chapter, if a timely request is made, the court shall render a statement of decision where the court has resolved controverted factual evidence. _(Amended (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219) by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 17. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)_
