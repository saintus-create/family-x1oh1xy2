# § 2127

*statement of decision*

statement of decision
Actions or motions;
Property § 254
Treatises and Practice Aids
As to actions or motions filed under this chapter, if a timely
request is made, the court shall render a statement of
decision where the court has resolved controverted factual
evidence. (Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 108.
Amended by Stats.1993, c. 1101 (A.B.1469), § 17, eff. Oct. 11,
