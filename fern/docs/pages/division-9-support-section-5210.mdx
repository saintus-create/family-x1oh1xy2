# 5210. Covered parent and noncovered parent having custody of the

*Support*

<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  For purposes of this section, the insurer shall notify the
covered parent and noncovered parent having custody of the
child or any other person having custody of the child in
writing at any time that health insurance for the child is
terminated.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  The requirements of subdivision <span class="subsec">(g)</span>  shall not apply
unless the court, employer, or person having custody of the
child provides the insurer with one of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A qualified medical child support order that meets the
requirements of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 1169 of Title 29 of
the United States Code.
