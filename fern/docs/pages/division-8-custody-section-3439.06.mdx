# Custody of Children

## Section 3439.06

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

When transfer is made or obligation is incurred For the purposes of this chapter: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A transfer is made: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  With respect to an asset that is real property other than a fixture, but including the interest of a seller or purchaser under a contract for the sale of the asset, when the transfer is so far perfected that a good faith purchaser of the asset from the debtor against which applicable law permits the transfer to be perfected cannot acquire an interest in the asset that is superior to the interest of the transferee; and 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  With respect to an asset that is not real property or that is a fixture, when the transfer is so far perfected that a creditor on a simple contract cannot acquire a judicial lien otherwise than under this chapter that is superior to the interest of the transferee. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If applicable law permits the transfer to be perfected as provided in subdivision 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and the transfer is not so perfected before the commencement of an action for relief under this chapter, the transfer is deemed made immediately before the commencement of the action. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If applicable law does not permit the transfer to be perfected as provided in subdivision (a), the transfer is made when it becomes effective between the debtor and the transferee. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A transfer is not made until the debtor has acquired rights in the asset transferred. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  An obligation is incurred: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If oral, when it becomes effective between the parties; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If evidenced by a record, when the record signed by the obligor is delivered to or for the benefit of the obligee.
