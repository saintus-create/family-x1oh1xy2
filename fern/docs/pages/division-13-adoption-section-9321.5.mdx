# Adoption

## Section 9321.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Petitions for adult adoption; filing; residents; nonresidents; venue 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=9321.">§ 9321</a>, a person who is a resident of this state may file a petition for adult adoption with the court in any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The county in which the prospective adoptive parent resides. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The county in which the proposed adoptee was born or resides at the time the petition was filed. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The county in which an office of the public or private agency that placed the proposed adoptee for foster care or adoption as a minor or dependent child is located. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A petitioner who is not a resident of this state may file a petition for adult adoption with the court in a county specified in paragraph (3) of subdivision (a).
