# § 9004 — Consent form; notice; contents

Consent form; notice; contents
In a stepparent adoption, the form prescribed by the
department for the consent of the birth parent shall contain
substantially the following notice:
“Notice to the parent who gives the child for adoption: If
you and your child lived together at any time as parent and
child, the adoption of your child through a stepparent
adoption does not affect the child’s right to inherit your
property or the property of other blood relatives.”
(Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amend-
ed by Stats.2001, c. 893 (A.B.25),
