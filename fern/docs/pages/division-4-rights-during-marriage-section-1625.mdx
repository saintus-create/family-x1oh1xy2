# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1625

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Written contracts; effect on negotiations or stipulations The execution of a contract in writing, whether the law requires it to be written or not, supersedes all the negotiations or stipulations concerning its matter which preceded or accompanied the execution of the instrument. 
(Enacted in 1872. Amended by Stats.1905, c. 451, p. 611,
