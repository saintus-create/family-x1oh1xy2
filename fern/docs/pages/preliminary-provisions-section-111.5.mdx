# 111.5

It provides:

It provides:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For the purpose of division of property upon dissolution of
marriage or legal separation of the parties:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Property acquired by the parties during marriage on or after
January 1, 1984, and before January 1, 1987, in joint tenancy form
is presumed to be community property.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Property acquired by the parties during marriage on or after
January 1, 1987, in joint form, including property held in tenancy in
common, joint tenancy, tenancy by the entirety, or as community
property is presumed to be community property.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The presumptions under subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  are presumptions
affecting the burden of proof and may be rebutted by either of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A clear statement
in the deed or other documentary
evidence of title by which the property is acquired that the
property is separate property and not community property.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Proof that the parties have made a written agreement that
the property is separate property.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section affects the character of property
acquired by married persons that is not described in subdivision (a).
The Law Revision Commission’s Comments regarding this former
Section 2580, which are provided for informational purposes only,
read as follows:
Subdivision (a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of Section 2580 restates the first sentence of
former Civil Code
