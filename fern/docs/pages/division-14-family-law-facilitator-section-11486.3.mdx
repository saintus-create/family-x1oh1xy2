# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 11486.3

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Examination of CalWORKs sanction policy; scope of examination; development of recommendations; report 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department, in consultation with system stakeholders, including county welfare departments, shall examine the CalWORKs sanction policy, its implementation, and effect on work participation, including but not limited to all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The characteristics of the persons being sanctioned. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The reason participants are being sanctioned. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The length of time in sanctioned status. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Positive and negative sanction outcomes. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  County variances in sanction policies, rates, and outcomes. 
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  The relationship between sanction rates and work participation. 
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  The impact of sanctions on families and their ability to become self-sufficient. 
<span class="subsec">(8)</span>  Adequacy of procedures to resolve noncompliance prior to the implementation of sanctions. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The department shall develop recommendations to improve the effectiveness of sanctions in achieving participant compliance, assisting families in becoming self-sufficient, and other desired program outcomes. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The department shall report its findings and recommendations to the appropriate fiscal and policy committees of the Legislature by April 1, 2005:
