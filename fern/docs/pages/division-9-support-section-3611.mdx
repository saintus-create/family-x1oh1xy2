# § 3611

LAW
Order of court
In any case described in Section 3610, the court making the
order or giving the judgment referred to in Section 3600 shall,
upon application of counsel for the minor or person with a
disability, order any one or more of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  That a guardian of the estate or conservator of the
estate be appointed and that the remaining balance of the
money and other property be paid or delivered to the person
so appointed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  That the remaining balance of any money paid or to be
paid be deposited in an insured account in a financial
institution in this state, or in a single-premium deferred
annuity, subject to withdrawal only upon the authorization of
the court, and that the remaining balance of any other
property delivered or to be delivered be held on conditions
the court determines to be in the best interest of the minor or
person with a disability.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  After a hearing by the court, that the remaining
balance of any money and other property be paid to a special
needs trust established under Section 3604 for the benefit of
the minor or person with a disability. Notice of the time and
place of the hearing and a copy of the petition shall be mailed
the court, and shall credit each estate with the interest earned
by the funds deposited less the county treasurer’s actual cost
authorized to be recovered under Section 27013 of the
Government Code.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The county treasurer and sureties on the official bond
of the county treasurer are responsible for the safekeeping
and payment of the money.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The county treasurer shall ensure that the money
deposited is to earn interest or dividends, or both, at the
highest rate which the county can reasonably obtain as a
prudent investor.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Funds so deposited with the county treasurer shall only
be invested or deposited in compliance with the provisions
governing the investment or deposit of state funds set forth in
Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 16640) of Part 2 of
of Title 2 of the Government Code, the investment
or deposit of county funds set forth in Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 53600) of Part 1 of of Title 5 of
the Government Code, or as authorized under Chapter 6
(commencing with Section 2400) of .
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  That the remaining balance of the money and other
property be paid or delivered to the person with a disability.
to the State Director of Health Care Services, the Director of
State Hospitals, and the Director of Developmental Services
at the office of each director in Sacramento at least 15 days
before the hearing.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  If the remaining balance of the money to be paid or
delivered does not exceed twenty thousand dollars ($20,000),
that all or any part of the money be held on any other
conditions the court in its discretion determines to be in the
best interest of the minor or person with a disability.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  If the remaining balance of the money and other
property to be paid or delivered does not exceed five
thousand dollars ($5,000) in value and is to be paid or
delivered for the benefit of a minor, that all or any part of the
money and the other property be paid or delivered to a

For application of this chapter, see Probate Code § 3600.
