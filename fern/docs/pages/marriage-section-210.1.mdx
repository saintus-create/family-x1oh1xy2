# General Provisions

## Section 210.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Guidelines for operation and maintenance of nonsecure placement facilities The Board of Corrections shall develop guidelines for the operation and maintenance of nonsecure placement facilities for persons alleged or found to be persons coming within the terms of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=601.">Section 601</a> or 602. (Added by Stats.1978, c. 1157, p. 
3553, § 1, eff: Sept. 26, 1978. Amended by Stats.1996, c. 12 
(A.B.1397), § 6, eff. Feb. 14, 1996.)
