# 8610. Accounting report; filing by petitioners; contents;

*Support Services*

Accounting report; filing by petitioners; contents;
application
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The petitioners in a proceeding for adoption of a child
shall file with the court a full accounting report of all
disbursements of anything of value made or agreed to be
made by them or on their behalf in connection with the birth
of the child, the placement of the child with the petitioners,
any medical or hospital care received by the child’s birth
mother or by the child in connection with the child’s birth,
any other expenses of either birth parent, or the adoption.
The accounting report shall be made under penalty of perjury
and shall be submitted to the court on or before the date set
for the hearing on the adoption petition, unless the court
grants an extension of time.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The accounting report shall be itemized in detail and
shall show the services relating to the adoption or to the
placement of the child for adoption that were received by the
petitioners, by either birth parent, by the child, or by any
other person for whom payment was made by or on behalf of
the petitioners.
The report shall also include the dates of

each payment, the names and addresses of each attorney,
physician and surgeon, hospital, licensed adoption agency, or
other person or organization who received any funds of the
petitioners in connection with the adoption or the placement
of the child with them, or participated in any way in the
handling of those funds, either directly or indirectly.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not apply to an adoption by a
stepparent where one birth parent or adoptive parent retains
custody and control of the child.
