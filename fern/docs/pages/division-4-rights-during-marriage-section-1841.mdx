# 1841 — If a petition for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of

Rights and Obligations During Marriage

If a petition for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of
marriage, or for legal separation of the parties is filed, the

COORDINATION
OF FAMILY
MEDIATION
case may be transferred at any time during the pendency of
the proceeding to the family conciliation court for proceedings for reconciliation of the spouses or amicable settlement
of issues in controversy in accordance with this part if both of
the following appear to the court:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  There is a minor child of the spouses, or of either of
them, whose welfare may be adversely affected by the
dissolution of the marriage or the disruption of the household
or a controversy involving child custody.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  There is some reasonable possibility of a reconciliation
being effected.
