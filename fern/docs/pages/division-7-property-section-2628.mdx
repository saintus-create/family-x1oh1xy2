# Division of Property

## Section 2628

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Joint California income tax liabilities; revision by court in marriage dissolution proceeding Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, and Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, joint California income tax liabilities may be revised by a court in a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, provided the requirements of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=19006.">§ 19006</a> of the Revenue and Taxation Code are satisfied.
