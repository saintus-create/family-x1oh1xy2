# § 2628 — Joint California income tax liabilities; revision by

Joint California income tax liabilities; revision by
court in marriage dissolution proceeding
Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, and
Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, joint California income tax
liabilities may be revised by a court in a proceeding for
dissolution of marriage, provided the requirements of Section
19006 of the Revenue and Taxation Code are satisfied.
