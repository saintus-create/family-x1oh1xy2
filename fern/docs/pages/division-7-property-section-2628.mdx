# 2628

Joint California income tax liabilities; revision by

Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, and Sections 2620 to 2624, inclusive, joint California income tax liabilities may be revised by a court in a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, provided the requirements of Section 19006 of the Revenue and Taxation Code are satisfied. Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 374, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.<div class="source-note">
