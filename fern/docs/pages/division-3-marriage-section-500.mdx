---
title: "500. Unmarried persons who have been living together as"
description: "Unmarried persons who have been living together as"
slug: division-3-marriage-section-500
---

When two unmarried people, not minors, have been living together as spouses, they may be married pursuant to this chapter by a person authorized to solemnize a marriage under Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 400) of Part 3. 

> *Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 474, Sec. 6. (AB 2882) Effective January 1, 2017.*
