# § 500 — Unmarried persons who have been living together as

When two unmarried people, not minors, have been living together as spouses, they may be married pursuant to this chapter by a person authorized to solemnize a marriage under Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 400) of Part 3. _(Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 474, Sec. 6. (AB 2882) Effective January 1, 2017.)_
