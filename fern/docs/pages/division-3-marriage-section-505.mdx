# § 505 — Form of license; contents

Form of license; contents
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The form of the confidential marriage license shall be
prescribed by the State Registrar of Vital Statistics.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The form shall be designed to require that the parties
to be married declare or affirm that they meet all of the
requirements of this chapter.
