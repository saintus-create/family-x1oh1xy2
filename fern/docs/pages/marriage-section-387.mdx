Order for removal from custody and placement in foster home or institution; hearing upon supplemental petition; dependency jurisdiction for dual status children 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An order changing or modifying a previous order by removing a child from the physical custody of a parent, guardian, relative, or friend and directing placement in a foster home, or commitment to a private or county institution, shall be made only after noticed hearing upon a supplemental petition.
