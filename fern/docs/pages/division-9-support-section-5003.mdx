# 5003. Described in Section 5000 may transfer the property in

*Support*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A holder of property under an instrument of a type
described in Section 5000 may transfer the property in
compliance with a provision for a nonprobate transfer on
death that satisfies the terms of the instrument, whether or
not the transfer is consistent with the beneficial ownership of
the property as between the person who executed the
provision for transfer of the property and other persons
having an interest in the property or their successors, and
whether or not the transfer is consistent with the rights of the
person named as beneficiary.
