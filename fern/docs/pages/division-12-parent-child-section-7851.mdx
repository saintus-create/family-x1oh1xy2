# 7851. Gator, licensed clinical social worker, licensed marriage and

*Parent and Child Relationship*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The juvenile probation officer, qualified court investi-
gator, licensed clinical social worker, licensed marriage and
family therapist, licensed professional clinical counselor, or
the county department shall render to the court a written
report of the investigation with a recommendation of the
proper disposition to be made in the proceeding in the best
interest of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The report shall include all of the following:
Recommendations to court; contents of report
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A statement that the person making the report ex-
plained to the child the nature of the proceeding to end
parental custody and control.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A statement
of the child’s feelings and thoughts
concerning the pending proceeding.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A statement of the child’s attitude towards the child’s
parent or parents and particularly whether or not the child
would prefer living with
the parent.
or parents.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A statement that the child was informed of the child’s
right to attend the hearing on the petition and the child’s
feelings concerning attending the hearing.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the age, or the physical, emotional, or other condi-
tion of the child precludes the child’s meaningful response to
the explanations, inquiries, and information required by
subdivision (b), a description of the condition shall satisfy the
requirement of that subdivision.

PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The court shall receive the report in evidence and shall
read and consider
its contents
in rendering the court’s
judgment.
