# § 8709 — Consideration of religious background; best inter-

Consideration of religious background; best inter-
est of child
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The department, county adoption agency, or licensed
adoption agency to which a child has been freed for adoption
by either relinquishment or termination of parental rights
may consider the child’s religious background in determining
an appropriate placement.
(b), This section shall not be. construed
to affect the
application of the federal Indian Child Welfare Act (25
U.S.C. Sec. 1901 and following).
