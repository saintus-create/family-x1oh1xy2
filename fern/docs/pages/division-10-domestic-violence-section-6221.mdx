X.K. v. M.C. (2025) 112 Cal.App.5th 1287 In this case, M.C., a U.S. citizen, subjected ex-wife and mother of their child, X.K., an emigrant, to years of physical, sexual, and emotional abuse, including coercive control—both in the U.S. and abroad in China. X.K. sought a DVRO against M.C. based on this abuse. The trial court denied X.K.’s request after a hearing, noting its belief that the case was more about custody and visitation issues rather than a DVRO, and its question of whether it had the power to consider abuse that occurred in another country. The appellate court reversed and remanded for a new hearing.
