<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Full faith and credit to public acts, records, and judicial
proceedings of Indian tribes
The United States, every State, every territory or possession of the United States, and every Indian tribe shall give
full faith and credit to the public acts, records, and judicial
proceedings of any Indian tribe applicable to Indian child
custody proceedings to the same extent that such entities
give full faith and credit to the public acts, records, and
judicial proceedings of any other entity. (Pub.L. 95-608,
Title I, 101, Nov. 8, 1978, 92 Stat. 3071.)
