# 353.1

If a person entitled to bring an action or other proceeding,

If a person entitled to bring an action or other proceeding,
which action or other proceeding has not been filed or
otherwise instituted, is represented by an attorney over whose
practice a court of this state has assumed jurisdiction
pursuant to Section 6180 or Section 6190 of the Business and
Professions Code, and the application for the court to assume
jurisdiction is filed prior to the expiration of the applicable
statute of limitation or claim statute, the person shall have six
months from the date of entry of the order assuming
jurisdiction within which to file or otherwise institute the
