# § 781 — A married person in satisfaction of a judgment for damages

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Money or other property received or to be received by
a married person in satisfaction of a judgment for damages
for personal injuries, or pursuant to an agreement for the
settlement or compromise of a claim for those damages, is
the separate property of the injured person if the cause of
action for the damages arose as follows:
Separate property
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  After the entry of a judgment of dissolution of a
marriage or legal separation of the parties.
: Spada the injured spouse is living separate
rom the other spouse.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision (a), if the spouse of the
injured person has paid expenses by reason of the personal
injuries from separate property or from the community
property, the spouse is entitled to reimbursement of the
separate property or the community property for those
expenses from the separate property received by the injured
person under subdivision (a).
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Notwithstanding subdivision (a), if one spouse has a
cause of action against the other spouse that arose during the
marriage of the parties, money or property paid or to be paid
by or on behalf of a party to the party’s spouse of that
marriage in satisfaction of a judgment for damages for
personal injuries to that spouse, or pursuant to an agreement
for the settlement or compromise of a claim for the damages,
is the separate property of the injured spouse.
