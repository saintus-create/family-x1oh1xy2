# § 3455 — Locating missing child or party; district attorney

Locating missing child or party; district attorney
authorized to proceed pursuant to § 3130 et seq.
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Ina
case arising under this part or involving the Hague
Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child
Abduction,
a district attorney
is authorized
to proceed
pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 3130) of
.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A district attorney acting under this section acts on
behalf of the court and may not represent any party.
