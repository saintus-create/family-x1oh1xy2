# California Family Code § 2021

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to subdivision (b), the court may order that a person who claims an interest in the proceeding be joined as a party to the proceeding in accordance with rules adopted by the Judicial Council pursuant to Section 211.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An employee benefit plan may be joined as a party only in accordance with Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 2060).
