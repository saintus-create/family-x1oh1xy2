# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 43.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Wrongs not actionable No cause of action arises for: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Alienation of affection. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Criminal conversation. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Seduction of a person over the age of legal consent. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Breach of promise of marriage.
