# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 43.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Wrongs not actionable No cause of action arises for: 
(a) Alienation of affection. 
(b) Criminal conversation. 
(c) Seduction of a person over the age of legal consent. 
(d) Breach of promise of marriage.
