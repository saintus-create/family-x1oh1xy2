4980.397. 
Registrants or applicants for licensure; required examinations; time for California law and ethics examination; eligibility for clinical examination 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A registrant or an applicant for licensure as a marriage and family therapist shall pass the following two examinations as prescribed by the board: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A California law and ethics examination. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Aclinical examination. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Upon registration with the board, an associate marriage and family therapist’ shall, within the first year of registration, take an examination on California law and ethics. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A registrant or an applicant for licensure may take the clinical examination only upon meeting all of the following requirements: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Completion of all required supervised work experience. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Completion of all education requirements, 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Passage of the California law and ethics examination.
