re-

(a)  A registrant or an applicant for licensure as a marriage
and family therapist shall pass the following two examinations
as prescribed by the board:
(1)  A California law and ethics examination.
(2)  Aclinical examination.
(b)  Upon registration with the board, an associate mar-
riage and family therapist’ shall, within the first year of
registration, take an examination on California law and
ethics.
(c)  A registrant or an applicant for licensure may take the
clinical examination only upon meeting all of the following
requirements:
(1)  Completion of all required supervised work experi-
ence.
(2)  Completion of all education requirements,
(3)  Passage of the California law and ethics examination.
