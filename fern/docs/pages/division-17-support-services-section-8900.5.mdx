# 8900.5. As used in this chapter:

*Support Services*

As used in this chapter:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Accredited agency” means an agency that has been
accredited by an accrediting entity, in accordance with the
primary
standards in Subpart F (commencing with Section 96.29) of
Part 96 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations, to
provide adoption services in the United States in cases
subject to the convention.
Accredited agency does not
include a temporarily accredited agency.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Adoption service” means any of the following services:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Identifying
adoption.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The primary provider shall provide to the department a
copy of the written agreement with each supervised provider
containing all provisions required pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span> 
of Section 96.45 of Title 22 of the Code of Federal
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Amended by Stats.2007, c. 583 (S.B.703), § 5.)

a child for adoption
and arranging
an
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Securing the necessary consent to termination of
parental rights and to adoption.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Performing a background study on a child or a home
study on any prospective adoptive parent, and reporting on
the study.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Making nonjudicial determinations of the best interests
of a child and the appropriateness of an adoptive placement
for the child.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  Monitoring a case after a child has been placed with
any prospective adoptive parent until final adoption.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  If necessary because of a disruption before final
adoption, assuming custody and providing or facilitating child
care or any other social service pending an alternative
placement.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A private full-service adoption agency or a noncustodial adoption agency, when acting as the primary provider and
using a supervised provider, shall ensure that each supervised
provider operates under a written agreement with the primary provider pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 96.45 of
Title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations.
