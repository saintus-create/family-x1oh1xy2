Payment
“Payment” of sums on deposit includes all of the following:
(a)  A withdrawal, including payment on check or other
directive of a party.
(b)  A pledge of sums of deposit.
(c)  Asetoff, reduction, or other disposition of all or part of
an account pursuant to a pledge.
