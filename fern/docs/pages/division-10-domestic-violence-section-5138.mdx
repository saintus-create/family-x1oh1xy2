# 5138

Payment

Payment
“Payment” of sums on deposit includes all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A withdrawal, including payment on check or other
directive of a party.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A pledge of sums of deposit.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Asetoff, reduction, or other disposition of all or part of
an account pursuant to a pledge.
