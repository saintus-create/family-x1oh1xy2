# 8462. Surviving spouse or domestic partner, relative, or

*Parent and Child Relationship*

Surviving spouse or domestic partner, relative, or
relative of predeceased spouse or domestic partner;
conditions of priority
Background on Section 8463 of Repealed Code
The surviving spouse or domestic partner of the decedent,
a relative of the decedent, or a relative of a predeceased
spouse or domestic partner of the decedent, has priority
under Section 8461 only if one of the following conditions is
satisfied:
Section 8463 was added by 1988 Cal.Stat. ch. 1199 § 81.5. The
section superseded subdivision (a)<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  and the second paragraph of
subdivision (a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of former Probate Code Section 422 (repealed by
1988 Cal.Stat. ch. 1199 § 45). For background on the provisions of
this part, see the Comment to this part under the part heading. [20
Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The surviving spouse, domestic partner, or relative is
entitled to succeed to all or part of the estate.
