# § 5128

Section 5122 was added by 1989 Cal.Stat. ch. 397 § 25. Subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  restated subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of former Probate Code Section 5101
(repealed by 1989 Cal.Stat. ch. 397 § 24) without change. Paragraphs (1)-<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  restated the second sentence of
subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of former Section 5101 without substantive change.
Paragraph <span class="subsec">(4)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  was new and superseded the third
sentence of subdivision <span class="subsec">(g)</span>  of former Section 5101.
The new
paragraph made clear that the rules applicable to an account
established for funds of a guardianship, conservatorship, or decedent’s estate are not affected by this part. For background on the
provisions of this part, see the Comment to this part under the part
heading. [20 Cal.L.Rev.Comm.Reports 1001 (1990)].
Financial institution
“Financial institution” includes:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A financial institution as defined in Section 40.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An industrial loan company as defined in Section
18003 of the Financial Code.
§ 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
