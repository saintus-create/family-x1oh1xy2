# 2347. Judgment of dissolution

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

judgment of dissolution
A judgment of legal separation of the parties does not bar
a subsequent judgment of dissolution of the marriage granted
pursuant to a petition for dissolution filed by either party.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
