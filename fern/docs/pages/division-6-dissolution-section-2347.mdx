# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2347

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Judgment of legal separation; effect on subsequent judgment of dissolution A judgment of legal separation of the parties does not bar a subsequent judgment of dissolution of the marriage granted pursuant to a petition for dissolution filed by either party.
