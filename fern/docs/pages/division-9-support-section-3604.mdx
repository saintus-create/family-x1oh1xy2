---
title: "3604. Rights of the parties or children with respect to"
description: "Rights of the parties or children with respect to"
slug: division-9-support-section-3604
---

An order made pursuant to this chapter does not prejudice the rights of the parties or the child with respect to any subsequent order which may be made.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
