## Judicial Decisions

<Badge intent="note" minimal>Selected decisions illustrating Family Code application</Badge>

| Citation | Court | Year | Subject | Holding |
|----------|-------|------|---------|---------|
| *Marriage of Ciprari* | Cal. Supreme Court | 2019 | Child support: guideline deviation | Guideline order is presumed correct; deviation requires express findings and must be based on factors in § 4053 |
| *Marriage of Roselan* | Cal. Supreme Court | 2018 | Spousal support: duration | Guideline supports reasonable period for marriages under 10 years; long-term support requires exceptional circumstances |
| *Marriage of Creighton* | Cal. Supreme Court | 2016 | Child support: imputed income | Court may impute income based on earning capacity when parent has reduced income without good cause |
| *In re Marriage of Khera & Sameer* | Cal. Ct. Appeal | 2012 | Child support: FL-150 accuracy | Unverified income and expense claims cannot justify support orders; net disposable income must be calculated correctly |
| *Marriage of Padilla* | Cal. Supreme Court | 2005 | Child support: de facto parent | Child support obligation may be imposed on a de facto parent who has assumed the role of a parent, consistent with the child's best interest |
| *Marriage of Factors* | Cal. Ct. Appeal | 2004 | Parentage: marital presumption | § 7540 conclusive presumption does not bar genetic testing where wife's HIV status raises nonaccess issues |
| *Marriage of Murray* | Cal. Supreme Court | 2002 | Spousal support: second family | Support obligation to first family takes precedence over obligation to second family |
