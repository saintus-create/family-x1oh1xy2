# § 5011 — Rights of parties in nonprobate transfers; applica-

Rights of parties in nonprobate transfers; applica-
tion of chapter
Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, the rights
of the parties in a nonprobate transfer of community property
on death are subject to all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The terms of the instrument under which the nonpro-
bate transfer is made.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A contrary state statute specifically applicable to the
instrument under which the nonprobate transfer is made.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A written expression of intent of a party in the
provision for transfer of the property or in a written consent
to the provision.
