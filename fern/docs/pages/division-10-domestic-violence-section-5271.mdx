Motion and notice of motion to quash;
filing;
hearing; service on obligee
(a)  The motion and notice of motion
to quash the
assignment order shall be filed with the court issuing the
order within 10 days after delivery of the copy of the
assignment order to the obligor by the employer.
(b)  The clerk of the court shall set the motion to quash for
hearing within not less than 15 days, nor more than 20 days,
after receipt of the notice of motion.
(c)  The obligor shall serve personally or by first-class mail,
postage prepaid, a copy of the motion and notice of motion
on the obligee named in the assignment order no less than 10
days before the date of the hearing.
