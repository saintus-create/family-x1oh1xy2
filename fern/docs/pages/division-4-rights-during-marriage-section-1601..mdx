# § 1601.

*Part 5 — Marital Agreements · Chapter 2 — Uniform Premarital Agreement Act*

This chapter is effective on and after January 1, 1986, and applies to any premarital agreement executed on or after that date. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) ARTICLE 2. Premarital Agreements [1610 - 1617] ( Article 2 enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. )
