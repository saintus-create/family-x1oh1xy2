# 20019. Child custody and visitation contested;

*Pilot Projects*

Child custody and visitation contested;
pursuant to § 3170

documents to be provided
In a contested proceeding for temporary child or spousal
support under this chapter, both the moving party and the
responding party shall provide all of the following documents
to the Family Law Evaluator, and to the court at the time of
the hearing:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Copies of the last two federal and state income tax
returns filed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Paycheck stubs for all paychecks received in the four
months immediately prior to the hearing. (Added by Stats.
1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 210.)
