# § 695.050

A money judgment against a public entity is not enforceable under this division if the money judgment is subject to
Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 965) of, or Article 1
(commencing with Section 970) of Chapter 2 of, Part 5 of
.6 of Title 1 of the Government Code. (Added by
