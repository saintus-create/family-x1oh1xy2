# Marriage

## Section 695.050

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Money judgment against public entity A money judgment against a public entity is not enforceable under this division if the money judgment is subject to Chapter 1 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=965.">Section 965</a>) of, or Article 1 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=970.">Section 970</a>) of Chapter 2 of, Part 5 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=3.6.">Division 3.6</a> of Title 1 of the Government Code.
