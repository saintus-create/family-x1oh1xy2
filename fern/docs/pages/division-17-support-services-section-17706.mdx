# § 17706 — Counties with 10 best performance standards;

Counties with 10 best performance standards;
additional incentives
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  It is the intent of the Legislature to encourage counties
to elevate the visibility and significance of the child support
enforcement program in the county.
To advance this goal,
effective July 1, 2000, the counties with the 10 best perform-
ance standards pursuant to clause <span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  of subparagraph <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  of
paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 17704 shall
receive an additional 5 percent of the state’s share of those
counties’ collections that are used to reduce or repay aid that
is paid pursuant to Article 6 (commencing with Section
11450) of Chapter 2 of Part 3 of of the Welfare
and Institutions Code.
The counties shall use the increased
recoupment for child support-related activities that may not
be eligible for federal child support funding under Part D of
Title IV of the Social Security Act, including, but not limited
to, providing services to parents to help them better support
their children financially, medically, and emotionally.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The operation of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  shall be suspended for
the 2002-03, 2003-04, 2004-05, 2005-06, 2006-07, 2007-08,
2008-09, 2009-10,
2010-11, 2011-12,
2012-13, 2013-14,
2014-15, 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2018-19, 2019-
20, and 2020-21 fiscal years.
