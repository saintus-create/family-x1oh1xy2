# § 7135.

*Part 6 — Emancipation Of Minors Law · Chapter 3 — Court Declaration Of Emancipation*

Voiding or rescission of the declaration of emancipation does not alter any contractual obligation or right or any property right or interest that arose during the period that the declaration was in effect. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) ARTICLE 4. Identification Cards and Information [7140 - 7143] ( Article 4 enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. )
