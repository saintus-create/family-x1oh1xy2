See also Sections 3029 (order for support required when custodial
parent receiving public assistance), 3555 (forwarding of support
payments paid through county officer), 4550-4573 (deposit of money
to secure future child support payments), 4600-4641 (deposit of
assets to secure future child support payments).
For a comparable
procedure for spousal support payments, see Section 4350 et seg.
[23
Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
