# 409.3
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Service of petition
.
Service of the petition for relief and all supporting papers
must be made in the manner provided by law for service of
summons in civil actions,
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  No memorandum required
Unless ordered by the court, no memorandum is required
in support of or opposition to a petition for relief.
(Adopted,
eff. Jan. 1, 2012.)
