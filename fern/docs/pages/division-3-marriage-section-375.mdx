# 375. Disability of party; effect on action

*Premarital Agreements*

Disability of party; effect on action
An action or proceeding does not abate by the disability of
a party. The court, on motion, shall allow the action or
proceeding to be continued by or against the party’s representative.
1992, c. 178 (S.B.1496), § 17.)
