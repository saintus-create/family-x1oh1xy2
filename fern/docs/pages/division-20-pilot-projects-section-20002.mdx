---
title: "20002. Duration of projects"
description: "Duration of projects"
slug: division-20-pilot-projects-section-20002
---

The duration of the pilot projects shall be two years.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.*
