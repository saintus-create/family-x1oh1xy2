# § 6610

*defense, cross-complaint,*

defense, cross-complaint,
available
Effect of order
Upon becoming final, an order under Section 6609 shall be
conclusive on all persons, whether or not they are then in
being. (Stats.1990, c. 79 (A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1,
1991.)
