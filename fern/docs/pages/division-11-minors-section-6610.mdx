# Minors

## Section 6610

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Effect of order Upon becoming final, an order under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6609.">§ 6609</a> shall be conclusive on all persons, whether or not they are then in being.
