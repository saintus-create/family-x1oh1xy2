# 6122.1. Domestic partnership of testator; revocation by

*Prevention of Domestic Violence*

Domestic partnership of testator; revocation by
termination
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Unless the will expressly provides otherwise, if after
executing a will the testator’s domestic partnership is termi-
nated, the termination revokes all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Any disposition or appointment of property made by
the will to the former domestic partner.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any provision of the will conferring a general or
special power of appointment
on the former domestic
partner.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Any provision of the will nominating the former
domestic partner as executor, trustee, conservator, or guard-
ian.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If any disposition or other provision of a will is revoked
solely by this section, it is revived by the testator establishing
another domestic partnership with the former domestic
partner.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In case of revocation by termination of a domestic
partnership:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Property prevented from passing to a former domestic
partner because of the revocation passes as if the former
domestic partner failed to survive the testator.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Other provisions of the will conferring some power or
office on the former domestic partner shall be interpreted as
if the former domestic partner failed to survive the testator.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  This section shall apply only to wills executed on or
after January 1, 2002.
