# § 5243

An assignment order for support has priority as against any attachment, execution, or other assignment as specified in Section 706.031 of the Code of Civil Procedure. _(Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 876, Sec. 26. Effective October 6, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 876.)_
