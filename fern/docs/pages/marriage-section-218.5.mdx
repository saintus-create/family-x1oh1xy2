# 218.5. Domestic violence training

*Marriage*

Domestic violence training
ipation by counsel; requirements

All counsel performing duties under this chapter, including, but not limited to, county counsel, court appointed
counsel, or volunteer counsel, shall participate in mandatory
training on domestic violence where available through existing programs at no additional cost to the county.
The
training shall meet the requirements of Section 16206.
1996, c. 1139 (A.B.2647), § 4.)
