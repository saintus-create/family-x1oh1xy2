# § 683.190 — Liens

Liens
other than judgment
liens;
effect
of
renewal
If a lien (other than a judgment lien on an interest in real
property or an execution
lien) has been created by an
enforcement procedure pursuant to a judgment and the
judgment is renewed pursuant to this article, the duration of
the lien is extended, subject to any other limitations on its
duration under this title, until 10 years from the date of the
filing of the application for renewal of the judgment if, before
the expiration of the lien, a certified copy of the application
for renewal is served on or filed with the same person and in
the same manner as the notice or order that created the lien.
