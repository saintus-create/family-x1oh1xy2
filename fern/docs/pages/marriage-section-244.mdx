# General Provisions

## Section 244

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Precedence of hearing and trial 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  On the day of the hearing, the hearing on the petition shall take precedence over all other matters on the calendar that day, except older matters of the same character, and matters to which special precedence may be given by law. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The hearing on the petition shall be set for trial at the earliest possible date and shall take precedence over all other matters, except older matters of the same character, and matters to which special precedence may be given by law.
