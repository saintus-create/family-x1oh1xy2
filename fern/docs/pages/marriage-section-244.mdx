# 244. Precedence of hearing and trial

*Marriage*

Precedence of hearing and trial
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  On the day of the hearing, the hearing on the petition
shall take precedence over all other matters on the calendar
that day, except older matters of the same character, and
matters to which special precedence may be given by law.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The hearing on the petition shall be set for trial at the
earliest possible date and shall take precedence over all other
