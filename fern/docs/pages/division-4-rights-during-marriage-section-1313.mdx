# § 1313

*Reputation in family concerning family history*

Reputation in family concerning family history
Evidence of reputation among members of a family is not
made inadmissible by the hearsay rule if the reputation
concerns the birth, marriage, divorce, death, parent and child
relationship, race, ancestry, relationship by blood or mar-
riage, or other similar fact of the family history of a member
of the family by blood or marriage.
