# 721

Contracts with each other and third parties; fidu-

Contracts with each other and third parties; fidu-
ciary relationship
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Subject to subdivision (b), either spouse may enter into
any transaction with the other, or with any other person,
respecting property, which either might if unmarried.

GENERAL PROVISIONS
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as provided in Sections 143, 144, 146, 16040,
16047, and 21385 of the Probate Code, in transactions
between themselves, spouses are subject to the general rules
governing fiduciary relationships that control the actions of
persons occupying confidential relations with each other.
This confidential relationship imposes a duty of the highest
good faith and fair dealing on each spouse, and neither shall
take any unfair advantage of the other.
This. confidential
relationship is a fiduciary relationship subject to the same
rights and duties of nonmarital business partners, as provided
in Sections 16403, 16404, and 16503 of the Corporations
Code, including, but not limited to, the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Providing each spouse access at all times to any books
kept regarding a transaction for the purposes of inspection
and copying.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Rendering upon request, true and full information of
all things affecting any transaction that concerns the commu-
nity property.
Nothing in this section is intended to impose a
duty for either spouse to keep detailed books and records of
community property transactions.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Accounting to the spouse, and holding as a trustee, any
benefit or profit derived from any transaction by one spouse
without the consent of the other spouse that concerns the
community property.
