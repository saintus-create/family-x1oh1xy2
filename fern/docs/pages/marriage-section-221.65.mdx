# General Provisions

## Section 221.65

See also <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8542.">Section 8542</a> (“prospective adoptive parent” defined). 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)]. 
2002 Amendment Subdivision (d) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8613.">Section 8613</a> is amended to reflect elimination of the county clerk’s role as ex officio clerk of the superior court. See former Gov’t Code § 26800 (county clerk acting as clerk of superior court). The powers, duties, and responsibilities formerly exercised by the county clerk as ex officio clerk of the court are delegated to the court administrative or executive officer, and the county clerk is relieved of those powers, duties, and responsibilities. See Gov't Code 
§§ 69840 (powers, duties, and responsibilities of clerk of court and 

§ 8613 deputy clerk of court), 71620 (trial court personnel). 
[32 Cal.L.Rev. 
Comm. Reports 165 (2002)].
