# § 6306.6

*Part 4 — Protective Orders And Other Domestic Violence Prevention Orders · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

(a) Information about access to self-help services regarding domestic violence restraining orders shall be prominently visible on the superior court’s internet website.

(b) The Judicial Council shall develop or amend rules as necessary to implement this section. (Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 681, Sec. 2. (AB 887) Effective January 1, 2022.)
