# 4053. Principles to be followed by court

principles to be followed by court
uniform
guideline;
In implementing the statewide uniform guideline, the
courts shall adhere to the following principles:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A parent’s first and principal obligation is to support

* * * the parent’s minor children according to the parent’s
circumstances and station in life.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Both parents are mutually responsible for the support
of their children.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The guideline takes into account each parent’s actual
income and level of responsibility for the children.
