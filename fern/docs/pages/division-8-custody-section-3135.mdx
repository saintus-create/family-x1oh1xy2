---
title: "3135. Effect of on authority of district attorney or"
description: "Effect of on authority of district attorney or"
slug: division-8-custody-section-3135
---

Part 3 (commencing with Section 3400) does not limit the authority of a district attorney or arresting agency to act pursuant to this chapter, Section 279.6 of the Penal Code, or any other applicable law.

> *Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.*
