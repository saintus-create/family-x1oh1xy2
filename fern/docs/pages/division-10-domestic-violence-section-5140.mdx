# § 5140 — P.O.D. account

P.O.D. account
“P.O.D. account” means any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An account payable on request to one person during
the person’s lifetime and on the person’s death to one or
more P.O.D. payees.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An account payable on request to one or more persons
during their lifetimes and on the death of all of them to one
or more P.O.D. payees.
