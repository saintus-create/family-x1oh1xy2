P.O.D. account
“P.O.D. account” means any of the following:
(a)  An account payable on request to one person during
the person’s lifetime and on the person’s death to one or
more P.O.D. payees.
(b)  An account payable on request to one or more persons
during their lifetimes and on the death of all of them to one
or more P.O.D. payees.
