# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 102

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Transfer of quasi-community property; restoration of decedent’s estate; requirements 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The decedent’s surviving spouse may require the transferee of property in which the surviving spouse had an expectancy under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=101.">§ 101</a> at the time of the transfer to restore to the decedent’s estate one-half of the property if the transferee retains the property or, if not, one-half of its proceeds or, if none, one-half of its value at the time of transfer, if all of the following requirements are satisfied: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The decedent died domiciled in this state. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The decedent made a transfer of the property to a person other than the surviving spouse without receiving in exchange a consideration of substantial value and without the written consent or joinder of the surviving spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The transfer is any of the following types: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A transfer under which the decedent retained at the time of death the possession or enjoyment of, or the right to income from, the property. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A transfer to the extent that the decedent retained at the time of death a power, either alone or in conjunction with any other person, to revoke or to consume, invade, or dispose of the principal for the decedent’s own benefit. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A transfer whereby property is held at the time of the decedent’s death by the decedent and another with right of survivorship. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Nothing in this section requires a transferee to restore to the decedent’s estate any life insurance, accident insurance, joint annuity, or pension payable to a person other than the surviving spouse. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  All property restored to the decedent’s estate under this section belongs to the surviving spouse pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=101.">§ 101</a> as though the transfer had not been made.
