title: "7802. Proceeding for declaration of freedom from paren-"
description: "Proceeding for declaration of freedom from paren-"
slug: division-12-parent-child-section-7802
---

A proceeding may be brought under this part for the purpose of having a minor child declared free from the custody and control of either or both parents.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
