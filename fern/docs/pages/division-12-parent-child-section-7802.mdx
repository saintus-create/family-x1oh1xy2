# § 7802 — Proceeding for declaration of freedom from paren-

A proceeding may be brought under this part for the purpose of having a minor child declared free from the custody and control of either or both parents. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
