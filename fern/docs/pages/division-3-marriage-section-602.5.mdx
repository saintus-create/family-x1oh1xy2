11th Juvenile

Minor’s criminal history; report
The juvenile court shall report the complete criminal
history of any minor found to be a person adjudged to be a
ward of the court under Section 602 because of the commission of any felony offense to the Department of Justice. The
Department of Justice shall retain this information and make
it available in the same manner as information gathered
pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 13100) of
Title 3 of Part 4 of the Penal Code. (Added by Initiative
