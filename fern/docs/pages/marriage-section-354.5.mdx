# General Provisions

## Section 354.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Holocaust victims; insurance policy claims purchased in Europe; legal action to recover on claims 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The following definitions govern the construction of this section: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Holocaust victim” means any person who was persecuted during the period of 1929 to 1945, inclusive, by Nazi Germany, its allies, or sympathizers.
