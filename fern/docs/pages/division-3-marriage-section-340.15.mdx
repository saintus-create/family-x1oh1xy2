Action for damages suffered as result
(a)  In any civil action for recovery of damages suffered as
a result , the time for commencement of
the action shall be the later of the following:
(1)  Within three years from the date of the last act by the defendant against the plaintiff.
(2)  Within three years from the date the plaintiff discovers
or reasonably should have discovered that an injury or illness
resulted from an act by the defendant
against the plaintiff.
(b)  As used in this section, “domestic violence” has the
same meaning as defined in Section 6211 of the Family Code.
1995, c. 602 (S.B.924), 1. Amended by
c. 123 (S.B.1939), 1.)
