# 340.15. Action for damages suffered as result

*Premarital Agreements*

Action for damages suffered as result
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In any civil action for recovery of damages suffered as
a result , the time for commencement of
the action shall be the later of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Within three years from the date of the last act by the defendant against the plaintiff.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Within three years from the date the plaintiff discovers
or reasonably should have discovered that an injury or illness
resulted from an act by the defendant
against the plaintiff.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  As used in this section, “domestic violence” has the
same meaning as defined in Section 6211 of the Family Code.
1995, c. 602 (S.B.924), § 1. Amended by
c. 123 (S.B.1939), § 1.)
