# Support

## Section 4301

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Support of spouse from separate property Subject to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=914.">§ 914</a>, a person shall support the person’s spouse while they are living together out of the separate property of the person when there is no community property Or quasi-community property.
