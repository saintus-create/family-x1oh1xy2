# § 6925.

*Part 4 — Medical Treatment · Chapter 3 — Consent By Minor*

(a) A minor may consent to medical care related to the prevention or treatment of pregnancy.

(b) This section does not authorize a minor to be sterilized without the consent of the minor’s parent or guardian. (Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 136, Sec. 12. (AB 260) Effective September 26, 2025.)
