For purposes of this chapter:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  “Mediation” means
a process
in which
a neutral
person or persons facilitate communication between the
disputants to assist them in reaching a mutually acceptable
agreement.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Mediator” means a neutral person who conducts a
mediation. “Mediator” includes any person designated by a
mediator either to assist in the mediation or to communicate
with the participants in preparation for a mediation.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  “Mediation consultation” means a communication be-
tween a person and a mediator for the purpose of initiating,
considering, or reconvening a mediation or retaining the
mediator.
