# Support

## Section 3558

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Child or family support proceedings; court-ordered job training, placement, or vocation rehabilitation; documentation In a proceeding involving child or family support, a court may require either parent to attend job training, job placement and vocational rehabilitation, and work programs, as designated by the court, at regular intervals and times and for durations specified by the court, and provide documentation of participation in the programs, in a format that is acceptable to the court, in order to enable the court to make a finding that good faith attempts at job training and placement have been undertaken by the parent.
