# 3558 — Child or family support proceedings;

Support

Child or family support proceedings;
court-or-
dered job training, placement, or vocation rehabilita-
tion; documentation
In a proceeding involving child or family support, a court
may require either parent to attend job training, job place-
ment and vocational rehabilitation, and work programs, as
designated by the court, at regular intervals and times and for
durations specified by the court, and provide documentation
of participation in the programs, in a format that is accept-
able to the court, in order to enable the court to make a
finding that good faith attempts at job training and placement
have been undertaken by the parent.
