# 3902

Property of child; allowance to parent

The court may direct that an allowance be made to the parent of a child for whom support may be ordered out of the child’s property for the child’s past or future support, on conditions that are proper, if the direction is for the child’s benefit. Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
