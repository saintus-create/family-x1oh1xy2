# § 3902

*Property of child; allowance to parent*

Property of child; allowance to parent
The court may direct that an allowance be made to the
parent of a child for whom support may be ordered out of the
child’s property for the child’s past or future support, on
conditions that are proper, if the direction is for the child’s

CHILD SUPPORT
benefit.
