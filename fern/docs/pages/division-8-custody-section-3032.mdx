# Custody of Children

## Section 3032

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Pilot program to provide interpreter in child custody or protective order cases; lack of English proficiency and financial ability of party; report to Legislature 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Judicial Council shall establish a state-funded oneyear pilot project beginning July 1, 1999, in at least two counties, including Los Angeles County, pursuant to which, in any child custody proceeding, including mediation proceedings pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3170.">§ 3170</a>, any action or proceeding under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.">Division 10</a> (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6200.">§ 6200</a>), any action or proceeding under the Uniform Parentage Act (Part 3 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7600.">§ 7600</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=12.">Division 12</a>), and any proceeding for dissolution or nullity of marriage or legal separation of the parties in which a protective order as been granted or is being sought pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6221.">§ 6221</a>, the court shall, notwithstanding <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=68092.">§ 68092</a> of the Government Code, appoint an interpreter to interpret the proceedings at court expense, if both of the following conditions are met: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  One or both of the parties is unable to participate fully in the proceeding due to a lack of proficiency in the English language.
