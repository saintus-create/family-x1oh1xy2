# Minors

## Section 6606

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Who may file 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A petition may be filed under this chapter by any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The person named in the will of the decedent as executor. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The surviving spouse of the decedent. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The guardian of a minor child of the decedent. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A child of the decedent who was a minor at the time the decedent died. 
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  The personal representative if a personal representative has been appointed for the decedent’s estate. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The guardian of a minor child of the decedent may file the petition without authorization or approval of the court in which the guardianship proceeding is pending.
