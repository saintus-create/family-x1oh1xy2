# § 2018 — Be construed to prohibit the disclosure of any relevant work

010) of Title 4 of Part 4 of the Code of Civil Procedure
be construed to prohibit the disclosure of any relevant work
product of the attorney in connection with: <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  an arbitration
hearing or mediation pursuant to this article; <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  a trial after
arbitration; or <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  judicial confirmation, correction, or vaca-
tion of an
arbitration
award.
In no
event
shall such
disclosure be deemed a waiver of the confidential character
of such matters for any other purpose.
