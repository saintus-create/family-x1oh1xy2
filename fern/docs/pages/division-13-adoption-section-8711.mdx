# Adoption

## Section 8711

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Application of §§ 8708 to 8710.4 Sections 8708 to 8710.4, inclusive, apply only in determining the placement of a child who has been relinquished for adoption or has been declared free from the custody and control of the birth parents.
