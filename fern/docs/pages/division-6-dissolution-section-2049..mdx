# § 2049.

*Part 1 — General Provisions · Chapter 4 — Protective And Restraining Orders*

A judgment may include a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, and any other restraining order as provided in Article 3 (commencing with Section 6360) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of Division 10. (Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 106.7. Effective January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
