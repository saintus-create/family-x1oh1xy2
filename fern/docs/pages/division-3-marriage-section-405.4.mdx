# Marriage

## Section 405.4

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Under subsection (c) of this section, section 200, and section 4908, in a proceeding to dissolve a marriage a California Superior Court has jurisdiction to award child support even though a child’s home state is Japan, when no support action has ever been initiated in the child’s home state. In re Marriage of Richardson, 179 Cal.App.4th 1240, 102 Cal.Rptr.3d 391 (2009). 
When a wife was not the debtor spouse, Jn re Marriage of Guasch, 201 Cal.App.4th 942, 134 Cal.Rptr.3d 358 (2011), affirmed a judgment granting a wife’s motion to quash a judgment creditor’s writ of execution and enjoining further enforcement against community property, without requiring the wife to post an undertaking under Code of Civil Procedure
