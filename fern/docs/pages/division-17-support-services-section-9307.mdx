# § 9307 — Open and public hearing

A hearing with regard to adoption under Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 9320) or termination of a parent and child relationship under Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 9340) may, in the discretion of the court, be open and public. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_<div class="source-note">
