# General Provisions

## Section 263

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

The former reference to a proceeding “brought under this part,” meaning the former Family Law Act (former Part 5 (commencing with former <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4000.">§ 4000</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of the Civil Code), has been omitted as unnecessary. 
See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3021.">§ 3021</a> (application of part). The reference to “court-appointed investigator” has been substituted for the former list of officers. 
This is not a substantive change. 
See <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3110.">§ 3110</a> 
(“court-appointed investigator” defined). 
See also <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3081.">§ 3081</a> (investigation concerning whether joint custody appropriate). 
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)]. 
Commentary The 2015 amendments to this section (SB 594) are intended to overrule In re Marriage of Winternitz, 235 Cal.App.4th 644, 185 Cal.Rptr.3d 458 (2015), review denied (affirming a trial court order 230 OF denying a mother’s request to strike a defective child custody evaluation report).
