# § 8906

*Financial responsibility for child; transfer or shar-*

Financial responsibility for child; transfer or shar-
ing between agency and prospective adoptive parents
Nothing in this chapter may be construed to prohibit the
licensed adoption agency from entering into an agreement
with the prospective adoptive parents to share or transfer
financial responsibility for the child.
