<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  There is currently no simple method available to
parents to quickly modify their support orders when circumstances warrant a change in the amount of support.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The lack of a simple method for parents to use to
modify support orders has led to orders in which the amount
of support ordered is inappropriate based on the parents’
financial circumstances.
