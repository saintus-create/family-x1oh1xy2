# § 297

*Domestic partners and partnership;*

Domestic partners and partnership;
establishment
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Domestic partners are two adults who have chosen to
share
one
another’s
lives in an
intimate
and
committed
relationship of mutual caring.
.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A domestic partnership shall be established in California when both persons file a Declaration of Domestic
Partnership with the Secretary of State pursuant to this
division,
and,
at the
time
of filing, all of the following
requirements are met:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Neither person is married to someone else or is a
member of another domestic partnership with someone else
that has not been terminated, dissolved, or adjudged a nullity.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The two persons are not related by blood in a way that
would prevent them from being married to each other in this
