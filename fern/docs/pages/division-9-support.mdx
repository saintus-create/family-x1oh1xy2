## Support

  <h3>Duty of support</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 3500</Badge></div>
        <p>Husband and wife owe each other the duty of support. Child support and spousal support are governed by the guidelines and priority rules of this Division.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Guidelines — child support</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 4050</Badge></div>
        <p>The statewide uniform guideline formula for child support is based on the incomes of the parents and the amount of time each parent has primary physical responsibility for the children.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Factors for deviation</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 4053</Badge></div>
        <p>The court may deviate from the guideline when it finds that a party is suffering an extreme economic hardship or other circumstances make the formula unjust.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Presumption — spousal support</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 4320</Badge></div>
        <p>The court shall consider all relevant circumstances in determining spousal support, including the marital standard of living, duration of marriage, the supporting spouse's ability to pay, and the supported spouse's needs.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
