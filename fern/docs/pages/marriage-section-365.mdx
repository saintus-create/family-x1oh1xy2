# General Provisions

## Section 365

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Failure to comply with chapter; effect Failure to comply with this chapter shall not invalidate any proceedings of any court of this state, nor shall it affect the jurisdiction of the court to render a judgment therein. 
However, failure to comply with such provisions by any attorney at law shall be grounds for professional discipline and the State Bar of California shall investigate and take appropriate action in any such cases brought to its attention.
