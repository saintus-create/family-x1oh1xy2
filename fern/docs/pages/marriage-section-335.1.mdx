# General Provisions

## Section 335.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Two years; actions for assault, battery, or injury to, or for death of, individual caused by wrongful act or neglect Within two years: An action for assault, battery, or injury to, or for the death of, an individual caused by the wrongful act or neglect of another.
