# § 7604 — Finding of parent-child relationship

finding of parent-child relationship

,
Visitation Privileges.
A court may order pendente lite relief consisting of a
custody or visitation order pursuant to (commencing
with Section 3020) of , if the court finds both of the
following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A parent and child relationship exists pursuant to
Section 7540 or 7541.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The custody or visitation order would be in the best
interest of the child. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.1993, c. 219
(A.B.1500), § 175.5; Stats.2018, c. 876 (A.B.2684), § 46, eff.
Jan. 1, 2019.)
