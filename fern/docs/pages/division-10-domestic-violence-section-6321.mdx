# 6321. Exclusive possession of residence

*Prevention of Domestic Violence*

Ex parte order excluding party from dwelling
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court may issue an ex parte order excluding a party
from the family dwelling, the dwelling of the other party, the
common dwelling of both parties, or the dwelling of the
person who has care, custody, and control of a child to be
protected from domestic violence for the period of time and
on the conditions the court determines, regardless of which
party holds legal or equitable title or is the lessee of the
dwelling.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may issue an order under subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span> 
only on a showing of all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Facts sufficient for the court to ascertain that the party
who will stay in the dwelling has a right under color of law to
possession of the premises.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  That the party to be excluded has assaulted or threatens to assault the other party or any other person under the
care, custody, and control of the other party, or any minor
child of the parties or of the other party.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  That physical or emotional harm would otherwise
result to the other party, to any person under the care,
custody, and control of the other party, or to any minor child
of the parties or of the other party. (Added by Stats.1993, c.
219 (A.B.1500), § 154.)
