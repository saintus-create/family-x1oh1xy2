# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 37

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Preference; felony; time 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A civil action shall be entitled to preference, if the action is one in which the plaintiff is seeking damages which were alleged to have been caused by the defendant during the commission of a felony offense for which the defendant has been criminally convicted. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court shall endeavor to try the action within 120 days of the grant of preference.
