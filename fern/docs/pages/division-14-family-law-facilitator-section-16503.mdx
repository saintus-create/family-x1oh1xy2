Transfer of transferable interest; effects

Transfer of transferable interest; effects.
Transfer of transferable interest; effects
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A transfer, in whole or in part, of a partner’s transferable interest in the partnership is permissible. However, a
transfer does not do either of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  By itself cause the partner’s dissociation or a dissolution and winding up of the partnership business.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  As against the other partners or the partnership, entitle
the transferee, during the continuance of the partnership, to
participate in the management or conduct of the partnership
business, to require access to information concerning partnership transactions, or to inspect or copy the partnership books
or records.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A transferee of a partner’s transferable interest in the
partnership has a right to all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  To receive, in accordance with the transfer, distributions to which the transferor would otherwise be entitled.
