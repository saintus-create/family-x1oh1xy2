# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16503

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Transfer of transferable interest; effects 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A transfer, in whole or in part, of a partner’s transferable interest in the partnership is permissible. 
However, a transfer does not do either of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  By itself cause the partner’s dissociation or a dissolution and winding up of the partnership business. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  As against the other partners or the partnership, entitle the transferee, during the continuance of the partnership, to participate in the management or conduct of the partnership business, to require access to information concerning partnership transactions, or to inspect or copy the partnership books or records. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A transferee of a partner’s transferable interest in the partnership has a right to all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  To receive, in accordance with the transfer, distributions to which the transferor would otherwise be entitled. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  To receive upon the dissolution and winding up of the partnership business, in accordance with the transfer, the net amount otherwise distributable to the transferor. 

§ 16503 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  To seek under paragraph (6) of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=16801.">§ 16801</a> a judicial determination that it is equitable to wind up the partnership business. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In a dissolution and winding up, a transferee is entitled to an account of partnership transactions only from the date of the latest account agreed to by all of the partners. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Upon transfer, the transferor retains the rights and duties of a partner other than the interest in distributions transferred. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A partnership need not give effect to a transferee’s rights under this section until it has notice of the transfer. 
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A transfer of a partner’s transferable interest in the partnership in violation of a restriction on transfer contained in the partnership agreement is ineffective as to a person having notice of the restriction at the time of transfer.
