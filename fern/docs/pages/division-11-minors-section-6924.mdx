# Minors

## Section 6924

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Mental health treatment or counseling services; involvement of parents or guardians; liability of parents or guardians 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  As used in this section: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Mental health treatment or counseling services” 
means the provision of mental health treatment or counseling on an outpatient basis by any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A governmental agency. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A person or agency having a contract with a governmental agency to provide the services. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  An agency that receives funding from community united funds. 
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A runaway house or crisis resolution center. 
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  A professional person, as defined in paragraph (2). 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Professional person” means any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A person designated as a mental health professional in Sections 622 to 626, inclusive, of Article 8 of Subchapter 3 of Chapter 1 of Title 9 of the California Code of Regulations. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A marriage and family therapist as defined in Chapter 13 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4980.">§ 4980</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a> of the Business and Professions Code. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A licensed educational psychologist as. defined in Article 5 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=4986.">§ 4986</a>) of Chapter 13 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a> of the Business and Professions Code.
