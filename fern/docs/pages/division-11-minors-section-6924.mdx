# 6924 — Mental health treatment or counseling services;

Minors

Mental health treatment or counseling services;
involvement of parents or guardians; liability of parents
or guardians
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  As used in this section:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Mental
health
treatment
or counseling
services”
means the provision of mental health treatment or counseling
on an outpatient basis by any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A governmental agency.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A person or agency having a contract with a govern-
mental agency to provide the services.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  An agency that receives funding from community
united funds.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A runaway house or crisis resolution center.
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  A professional person, as defined in paragraph (2).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Professional person” means any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  A person designated as a mental health professional in
Sections 622 to 626, inclusive, of Article 8 of Subchapter 3 of
Chapter 1 of Title 9 of the California Code of Regulations.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  A marriage and family therapist as defined in Chapter
13 (commencing with Section 4980) of of the
Business and Professions Code.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A licensed educational psychologist
as. defined in
Article 5 (commencing with Section 4986) of Chapter 13 of
of the Business and Professions Code.
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A credentialed school psychologist as described: in
Section 49424 of the Education Code.
