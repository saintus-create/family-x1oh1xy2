# § 282

*Section 281 does not extend to any of the following:*

Section 281 does not extend to any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  To any person by reason of any former marriage or
former registered domestic partnership whose spouse by such
marriage or registered domestic partnership has been absent
for five successive years without being known to such person
within that time to be living.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  To any person by reason of any former marriage, or
any former registered domestic partnership, which has been
pronounced void, annulled, or dissolved by the judgment of a
competent court.
(Enacted in 1872.
Incest

void, who intermarry with each other, or who being 14 years
of age or older, commit fornication or adultery with each
other, are punishable by imprisonment in the state prison.
