# 683.050

Nothing in this chapter limits any right the judgment

Nothing in this chapter limits any right the judgment
creditor may have to bring an action on a judgment, but any
such action shall be commenced within the period prescribed
by Section 337.5. (Added by Stats.1982, c. 1364, p. 5073, $ 2,
operative July 1, 1983.)
