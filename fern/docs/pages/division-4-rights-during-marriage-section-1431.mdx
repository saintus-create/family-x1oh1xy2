# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1431

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Proceedings to establish a limited conservatorship 
“Proceedings to establish a limited conservatorship” include proceedings to modify or revoke the powers or duties of a limited conservator.
