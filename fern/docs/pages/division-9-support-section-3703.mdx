# § 3703

*Contents of petition*

Contents of petition
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The petition shall contain all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A statement
that the petition is filed under this
chapter.
Liit

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  In its caption, the last known military rank or grade
and the social security account number of the absentee.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A specific description and estimate of the value of all
of the absentee’s property, wherever situated (including all
sums due the absentee from the United States).
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  A designation of the property to be set aside, and the
facts establishing that setting aside the property is necessary
and in the best interest of the absentee.
<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  If the property is to be set aside for the benefit of the
spouse of the absentee, an allegation that the spouse is an
eligible spouse.
<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  So far as known. to the petitioner, the names and
addresses of all persons comprising the family of the absen-
tee, and an allegation whether a guardian of the estate or a
conservator of the estate of any member of the family of the
absentee has been appointed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  There shall be attached to the petition a certificate of
missing status.
The certificate of missing status shall be
received as evidence of that fact and the court shall not
determine the status of the absentee inconsistent with the
status shown in the certificate.
