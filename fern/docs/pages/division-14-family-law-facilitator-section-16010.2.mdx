# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16010.2

Plan for ongoing oversight and coordination of health care services; gender affirming treatment; development of best practices; foster youth seeking access to gender affirming health care 
(a) The department, in consultation with pediatricians, other health care experts, including public health nurses, and experts in and recipients of child welfare services, including parents, shall develop a plan for the ongoing oversight and coordination of health care services for a child in a foster care placement. 
The plan shall ensure a coordinated strategy to identify and respond to the health care needs of foster children, including mental health and dental needs, consistent with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=205.">Section 205</a> of the federal Fostering Connections to Success and Increasing Adoptions Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-351).
