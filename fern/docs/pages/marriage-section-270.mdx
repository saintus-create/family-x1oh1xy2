# § 270

If a court orders a party to pay attorney’s fees or costs under this code, the court shall first determine that the party has or is reasonably likely to have the ability to pay. _(Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 87. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
