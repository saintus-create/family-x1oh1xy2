# Adoption

## Section 8717

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Report or findings submitted to court; copy to attorney for petitioner When any report or findings are submitted to the court by the department, county adoption agency, or licensed adoption agency, a copy of the report or findings, whether favorable or unfavorable, shall be given to the petitioner’s attorney in the proceeding, if the petitioner has an attorney of record, or to the petitioner.
