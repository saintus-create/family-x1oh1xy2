# Pilot Projects

## Section 20011

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-20-pilot-projects">Division 20: Pilot Projects</a></div>

Motions for temporary orders; time for hearing Motions for temporary orders under this chapter shall be heard as soon as practicable, consistent with the rules governing other civil actions.
