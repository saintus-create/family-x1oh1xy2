# § 20011

*The clerk shall stamp all moving papers in which a party is*

The clerk shall stamp all moving papers in which a party is
not represented by counsel with a notice of a requirement to
see the Family Law Evaluator. The unrepresented party
shall serve the stamped pleadings on the other party. (Added
by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 210.)
