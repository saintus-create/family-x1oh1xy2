---
title: "20011. Motions for temporary orders; time for hearing"
description: "Motions for temporary orders; time for hearing"
slug: division-20-pilot-projects-section-20011
---

Motions for temporary orders under this chapter shall be heard as soon as practicable, consistent with the rules governing other civil actions.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.*
