# § 145 — State

State
“State” means a state of the United States, the District of
Columbia, or a commonwealth, territory, or insular posses-
sion subject to the jurisdiction of the United States.
(Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amend-
ed by Stats.1999, c. 661 (A.B.825),
