# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 145

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

State 
“State” means a state of the United States, the District of Columbia, or a commonwealth, territory, or insular possession subject to the jurisdiction of the United States. 
(Stats. 
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats.1999, c. 661 (A.B.825),
