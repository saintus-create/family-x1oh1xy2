# California Family Code § 17702.5

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Child Support Collections Recovery Fund is hereby created in the State Treasury, and shall be administered by the department for the purposes specified in subdivision (c).

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this section, the fund shall consist of both of the following:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  All public moneys transferred by public agencies to the department for deposit into the fund, as permitted under Section 304.30 of Title 45 of the Code of Federal Regulations or any other applicable federal statutes.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any interest that accrues on amounts in the fund.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Upon appropriation by the Legislature, all moneys in the fund shall be used to make payments or advances to local child support agencies of the federal share of administrative payments for costs incurred pursuant to this article.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Upon repeal of this section, the Legislature intends that any moneys remaining in the fund shall be returned to the federal agency that provides federal financial participation to the department.
