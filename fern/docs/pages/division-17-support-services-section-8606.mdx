OF UNMARRIED
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  After the entry of a final decree of adoption of an
Indian child, the Indian child’s parent may withdraw consent
to the adoption upon the grounds that consent was obtained
through fraud or duress and may petition the court to vacate
the decree. Upon a finding that * * * consent was obtained
through fraud or duress, the court shall vacate the decree and
return the child to the parent, provided that no adoption that
has been effective for at least two years may be invalidated
unless otherwise permitted under state law. (Added by
c. 838 (S.B.678), § 8 Amended by Stats.2019, c.
115 (A.B.1817), § 113, eff. Jan. 1, 2020.)
