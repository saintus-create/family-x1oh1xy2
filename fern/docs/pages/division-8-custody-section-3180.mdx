# § 3180 — Duties of mediators

Duties of mediators
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In mediation proceedings pursuant to this chapter, the
mediator has the duty to assess the needs and interests of the
child involved in the controversy, and is entitled to interview
the child when the mediator considers the interview appropri-
ate or necessary.
