# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5600

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Registration of order for support or earnings withholding obtained in another county. 
Registration by local child support agency of support order made in another county; procedures; motion to vacate; notice of registration. 
Registration of order by obligee; procedure; notice. 
Motion to vacate registration; procedure; hearing. 
Effect of previous determination of paternity made by another state. 
(a) A local child support agency or obligee may register an order for support or earnings withholding, or both, obtained in another county of the state. 
(b) An obligee may register a support order in the court of another county of this state in the manner, with the effect, 390 and for the purposes provided in this part. The orders may be registered in any county in which the obligor, the obligee, or the child who is the subject of the order resides, or in any county in which the obligor has income, assets, or any other property.
