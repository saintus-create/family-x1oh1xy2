# § 177 — Governing law in Indian child custody proceedings

Governing law in Indian child custody proceedings
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In an Indian child custody proceeding, the court shall
apply Sections 224.2 to 224.6, inclusive, and Sections 305.5,
361.31, and 361.7 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, and
the following rules from the California Rules of Court, as
they read on January 1, 2007:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Paragraph <span class="subsec">(7)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Rule 5.530.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Subdivision <span class="subsec">(i)</span>  of Rule 5.534.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In the provisions cited in subdivision (a), references to
social workers, probation officers, county welfare depart-
ment, or probation department shall be construed as meaning

the party seeking a foster care placement, guardianship, or
adoption under this code.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section shall only apply to proceedings involving
an Indian child.
