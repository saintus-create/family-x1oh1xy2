# Division of Property

## Section 2602

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Additional award or offset against existing property; award of amount determined to have been misappropriated As an additional award or offset against existing property, the court may award, from a party’s share, the amount the court determines to have been deliberately misappropriated by the party to the exclusion of the interest of the other party in the community estate.
