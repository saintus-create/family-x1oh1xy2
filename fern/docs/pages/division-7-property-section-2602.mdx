# 2602. Additional award or offset against existing proper-

*Division of Property*

Additional award or offset against existing proper-
ty; award of amount determined to have been misappro-
priated
As an additional award or offset against existing property,
the court may award, from a party’s share, the amount the
court determines to have been deliberately misappropriated
by the party to the exclusion of the interest of the other party
in the community estate.
