# § 681 — Several ownership defined

2000, c. 645 (A.B.2913), § 1, operative July 1,
2001. Amended by Stats.2016, c. 50 (S.B.1005), § 8, eff. Jan.
1, 2017.)
Several ownership defined
SEVERAL OWNERSHIP, WHAT. The ownership of property by
a single person is designated as a sole or several ownership.
(Enacted in 1872.)
1A California Real Estate Forms (Miller & Starr) § 1:133 (2d ed.),
Grant Deed.

Interspousal Grant Deed.
