# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1851

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

Advisory committee The Judicial Council shall establish an advisory committee of persons representing a broad spectrum of interest in and knowledge about family law. 
The committee shall recommend criteria for determining grant recipients pursuant to subdivision (c) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1850.">Section 1850</a>, which shall include proposal evaluation guidelines and procedures for submission of the results to the Legislature, the Governor, and family law courts. 
In accordance with established criteria, the committee shall receive grant proposals and shall recommend the priority of submitted proposals.
