# § 5281

*employment*

employment
obligee of change
of
An assignment order required or authorized by this chapter
shall include a requirement that the obligor notify the obligee
of any change of employment and of the name and address of
the obligor’s new employer within 10 days of obtaining new
employment. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
