# § 4724.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 5 — Civil Penalty For Child Support Delinquency*

The notice of delinquency may be served personally or by certified mail or in any manner provided for service of summons. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
