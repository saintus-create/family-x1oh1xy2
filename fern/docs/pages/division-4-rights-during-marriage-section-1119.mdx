# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1119

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Written or oral communications during mediation process; admissibility Except as otherwise provided in this chapter: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  No evidence of anything said or any admission made for the. purpose of, in the course of, or pursuant to, a mediation or a mediation consultation is admissible or subject to discovery, and disclosure of the evidence shall not be compelled, in any arbitration, administrative adjudication, civil. action, or other noncriminal proceeding in which, pursuant to law, testimony can be compelled to be given. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  No writing, as defined in <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=250.">§ 250</a>, that is prepared for the purpose of, in the course of, or pursuant to, a mediation or a mediation consultation, is admissible or subject to discovery, and disclosure of the writing shall not be compelled, in any arbitration, administrative adjudication, civil action, or other noncriminal proceeding in which, pursuant to law, testimony can be compelled to be given. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  All communications, negotiations, or settlement discussions by and between participants in the course of a 

§ 1119 mediation or a mediation consultation shall remain confidential. (Added by Stats.1997, c. 772 (A.B.939),
