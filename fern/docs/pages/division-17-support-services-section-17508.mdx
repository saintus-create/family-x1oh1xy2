# 17508. Employment Development Department; access to

*Support Services*

Employment Development Department; access to
information
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Employment Development Department
shall,
when requested by the Department of Child Support Services
local child support agency,
the federal
Parent Locator
Service, or the California Parent Locator Service, provide
access
to information
collected pursuant
to
(commencing with Section 100) of the Unemployment Insur-
ance Code to the requesting department or agency for
purposes of administering the child support enforcement
program, and for purposes
of verifying employment of
applicants and recipients of aid under
this chapter
or
CalFresh under Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 18900)
of Part 6 of of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
(b)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  To the extent possible, the Employment Develop-
ment Department shall share information collected under
Sections 1088.5 and 1088.8 of the Unemployment Insurance
Code immediately upon receipt.
This sharing of information
may include electronic means.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  This subdivision shall not authorize the Employment
Development Department to share confidential information
with any individuals not otherwise permitted by law to receive
the information or preclude batch runs or comparisons of
data.
