# Adoption

## Section 8808

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Interview of petitioners and persons from whom consent is required; filing of petition 
(a) The department or delegated county adoption agency shall interview the petitioners within 45 working days, excluding legal holidays, after the department or delegated county adoption agency receives 50 percent of the investigation fee together with a stamped file copy of the adoption petition. 
(b) The department or delegated county adoption agency shall interview all persons from whom consent is required and whose addresses are known.
