# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 58

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Child for whom support may be ordered 
“Child for whom support may be ordered” means a minor child and a child for whom support is authorized under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3587.">§ 3587</a>, 3901, or 3910.
