# 68070

Prohibiting a fee by the superior court for a particular service

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  In the absence of a statute or rule authorizing or
prohibiting a fee by the superior court for a particular service
or product, the court may charge a reasonable fee not to
exceed the costs of providing the service or product, if the
Judicial Council approves the fee, as provided by Govern-
ment Code
