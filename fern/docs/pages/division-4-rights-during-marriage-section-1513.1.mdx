Assessments
(a)  Each court or county shall assess (1)  the parent,
parents, or other person charged with the support and
maintenance of the ward or proposed ward, and (2)  the
guardian, proposed guardian, or the estate of the ward or
proposed ward, for court or county expenses incurred for any
investigation or review conducted by the court investigator,
probation officer, or domestic relations investigator. Subject
to Section 68631 of the Government Code, the court may
