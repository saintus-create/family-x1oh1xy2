# Marriage

## Section 645

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

Exception and review; decision as special verdict The decision of the referee appointed pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=638.">§ 638</a> or commissioner may be excepted to and reviewed in like manner as if made by the court. 
When the reference is to report the facts, the decision reported has the effect of a special verdict:
