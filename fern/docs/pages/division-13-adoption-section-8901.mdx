# Adoption

## Section 8901

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Administration of program
The department shall adopt regulations to administer the
intercountry adoption program.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.
2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
