# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5626

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Recording; delivery to beneficiary not required; acceptance by beneficiary not required; recordation of 
“Common Questions” language not required 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A revocable transfer on death deed is not effective unless the deed is recorded on or before 60 days after the date it was executed. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The transferor is not required to deliver a revocable transfer on death deed to the beneficiary during the transferor’s life. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The beneficiary is not required to accept a revocable transfer on death deed from the transferor during the transferor’s life.
