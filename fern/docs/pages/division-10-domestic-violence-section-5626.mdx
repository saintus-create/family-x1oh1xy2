# § 5626 — Recording;

Recording;
delivery to beneficiary not required;
acceptance by beneficiary not required; recordation of
“Common Questions” language not required
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A revocable transfer on death deed is not effective
unless the deed is recorded on or before 60 days after the
date it was executed.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The transferor is not required to deliver a revocable
transfer on death deed to the beneficiary during the transfer-
or’s life.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The beneficiary is not required to accept a revocable
transfer on death deed from the transferor during the
transferor’s life.
