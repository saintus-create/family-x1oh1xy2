# Domestic Partner Registration

## Section 299.6

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-2.5-domestic-partners">Division 2.5: Domestic Partner Registration</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  This section shall be applied in a manner consistent with the requirements of Sections 1653.5 and 12801 of the Vehicle Code. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  The adoption of a new name, or the choice not to adopt a new name, by means of a Declaration of Domestic Partnership pursuant to paragraph (1) shall not abrogate the right of either party to adopt a different name through usage at a future date, or to petition the superior court for a change of name pursuant to Title 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1275.">§ 1275</a>) 
of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not abrogate the common law right of a person to change the person’s name, or the right of a person to petition the superior court for a change of name pursuant to Title 8 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1275.">§ 1275</a>) of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
