# California Family Code § 2010

In a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, or for legal separation of the parties, the court has jurisdiction to inquire into and render any judgment and make orders that are appropriate concerning the following:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The status of the marriage, including any marriage under subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  of Section 308.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The children of the marriage.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The support of children for whom support may be ordered, including children born after the filing of the initial petition or the final decree of dissolution.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The support of either party.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The settlement of the property rights of the parties.

<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  The award of attorney’s fees and costs.
