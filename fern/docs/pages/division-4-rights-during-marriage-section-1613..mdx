# § 1613.

*Part 5 — Marital Agreements · Chapter 2 — Uniform Premarital Agreement Act*

A premarital agreement becomes effective upon marriage. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
