# 2113. This chapter applies to any proceeding commenced on or

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

This chapter applies to any proceeding commenced on or
after January 1, 1993. (Formerly § 2109, added by Stats.1993,
c. 219, (A.B.1500), § 107. Renumbered § 2113 and amended

1101
SEPARATION

Section 2110 continues former Civil Code Section 4800.10(i)
without substantive change.
[23 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1
(1993)].
