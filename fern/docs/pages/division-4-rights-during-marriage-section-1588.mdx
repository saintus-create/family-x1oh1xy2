# § 1588

*Ratification of voidable contract*

Ratification of voidable contract
RATIFICATION OF CONTRACT, VOID FOR WANT OF CONSENT. A
contract which is voidable solely for want of due consent, may
be ratified by a subsequent consent.
(Enacted in 1872.)
