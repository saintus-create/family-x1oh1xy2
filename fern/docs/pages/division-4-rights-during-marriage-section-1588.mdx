# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1588

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Ratification of voidable contract OF , OF . A contract which is voidable solely for want of due consent, may be ratified by a subsequent consent. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
