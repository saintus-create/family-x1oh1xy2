# § 7900 — Adoption

The Interstate Compact on Placement of Children as set forth in Section 7901 is hereby adopted and entered into with all other jurisdictions joining therein. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
