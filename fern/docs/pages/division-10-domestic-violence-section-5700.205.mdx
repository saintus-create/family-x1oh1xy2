# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.205

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify child-support order i 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A tribunal of this state that has issued a child-support order consistent with the law of this state has and shall exercise continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify its childsupport order if the order is the controlling order and: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  at the time of the filing of a request for modification this state is the residence of the obligor, the individual obligee, or the child for whose benefit the support. order is issued; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  even if this state is not the residence of the obligor, the individual obligee, or the child for whose benefit the support order is issued, the parties consent in a record or in open court that the tribunal of this state may continue to exercise jurisdiction to modify its order. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A tribunal of this state that has issued a child-support order consistent with the law of this state may not exercise continuing, exclusive jurisdiction to modify the order if: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  all of the parties who are individuals file consent in a record with the tribunal of this state that a tribunal of another state that has jurisdiction over at least one of the parties who is an individual or that is located in the state of residence of the child may modify the order and assume continuing, exclusive jurisdiction; or 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  its order is not the controlling order. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If a tribunal of another state has issued a child-support order pursuant to the Uniform Interstate Family Support Act or a law substantially similar to that Act which modifies a child-support order of a tribunal of this state, tribunals of this state shall recognize the continuing, exclusive jurisdiction of the tribunal of the other state.
