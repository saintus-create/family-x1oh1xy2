# Adoption

## Section 8732

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Adoption sought by foster parent of six months or relative caregiver; medical examination and report. 
Chapter provisions as grounds for removal of child from placement.
