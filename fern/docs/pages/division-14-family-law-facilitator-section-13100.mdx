# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 13100

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

(b) If the conservator seeks authority pursuant to subdivision (b) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2540.">Section 2540</a> to sell the conservatee’s personal residence, whether or not it is real property, or if the conservator seeks authority pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2590.">Section 2590</a> to sell the conservatee’s real property, valued in excess of ten thousand dollars ($10,000), or an item of personal property valued in excess of ten thousand dollars ($10,000) that is not a security sold pursuant to subdivision (a) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2544.">Section 2544</a>, that property shall be appraised by a probate referee.
