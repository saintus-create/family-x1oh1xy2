# § 13100 — Sion (b) of Section 2540 to sell the conservatee’s personal

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If the conservator seeks authority pursuant to subdivi-
sion <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  of Section 2540 to sell the conservatee’s personal
residence, whether or not it is real property, or if the
conservator seeks authority pursuant to Section 2590 to sell
the conservatee’s real property, valued in excess of ten
thousand dollars ($10,000), or an item of personal property
valued in excess of ten thousand dollars ($10,000) that is not
a security sold pursuant to subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 2544,
that property shall be appraised by a probate referee.
