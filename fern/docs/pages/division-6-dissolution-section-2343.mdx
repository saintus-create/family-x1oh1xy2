# § 2343

*Retention of jurisdiction; purposes; effect of date*

Retention of jurisdiction; purposes; effect of date
of termination of marital status
The court may, upon notice and for good cause shown, or
on stipulation of the parties, retain jurisdiction over the date
of termination of the marital status, or may order that the
marital status be terminated at a future specified date.
On
the date of termination of the marital status, the parties are
restored to the status of unmarried persons.
