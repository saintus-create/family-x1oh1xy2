# § 997 — Exception: crime or tort

Exception: crime or tort
There is no privilege under this article if the services of the
physician were sought or obtained to enable or aid anyone to
commit or plan to commit a crime or.a tort or to escape
detection or apprehension after the commission of a crime or
a tort.
