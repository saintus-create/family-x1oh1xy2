In re Marriage of Duffy, 91 Cal.App.4th 923, 111 Cal.Rptr.2d 160
(2001), held that so long as a managing spouse acts in good faith, he
has not breached the fiduciary duty even though he makes unwise or
improvident investments. However, Duffy was decided under a prior
version of this section, which incorporated by reference former
Corporations Code §§ 15019-15022, sections that were superseded
in 1996 by Corporations Code §§ 16401-16503. This section was not
amended to incorporate the new Corporations Code sections until
2002. 2002 Cal.Stat., ch. 310 (S.B.1936).! Although the 2002 amendment expresses legislative intent merely to clarify existing law, thus
paving the way for retroactive application of the amendment, to the
extent that the amendment imposes duties that did not exist under
prior law, it may not be applied retroactively to events that occurred
before its effective date. In re Marriage of Walker, 138 Cal. App.4th
1408, 42 Cal.Rptr.3d 325 (2006) (duty to disclose without demand
applies prospectively only because pre-amendment duty was duty to
disclose “on demand”).
