# 4062 — Additional child support

Support

Additional child support
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court shall order the following as additional child
support:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Child care costs related to employment or to reasonably necessary education or training for employment skills.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The reasonable uninsured health care costs for the
children as provided in Section 4063.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may order the following as additional child
support:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Costs related to the educational or other special needs
of the children.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Travel expenses for visitation.|
219 (A.B.1500), § 138. Amended
(S.B.1807), § 1.)
