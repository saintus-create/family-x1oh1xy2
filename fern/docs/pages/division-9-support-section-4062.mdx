# California Family Code § 4062

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The court shall order the following as additional child support:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Childcare costs, if those expenses are actually incurred, related to employment or to reasonably necessary education or training for employment skills, as described in Section 4063, unless those costs are specifically included in the guideline calculation itself.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The reasonable uninsured health care costs for the children as provided in Section 4063.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may order the following as additional child support:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Costs related to the educational or other special needs of the children.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Travel expenses for visitation.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section shall be operative September 1, 2024.
