# § 3665 — Attachments to income and expense declaration;

Attachments to income and expense declaration;
tax returns; disclosure
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A copy of the prior year’s federal and state personal
income tax returns shall be attached to the income and
expense declaration of each party.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A party shall not disclose the contents or provide
copies of the other party’s tax returns to anyone except the
court, the party’s attorney, the party’s accountant, or other
financial consultant assisting with matters relating to the
proceeding, or any other person permitted by the court.
