<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For purposes of this title, family justice centers shall be

order to reduce the number of times victims must tell their
story, reduce the number of places victims must go for help,
and increase access to services and support for victims and
their children. Staff members at a family justice center may
be comprised of, but are not limited to, the following:
