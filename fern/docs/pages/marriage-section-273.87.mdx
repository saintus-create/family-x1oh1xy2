# General Provisions

## Section 273.87

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Use of federal funds; implementation of chapter The Department of Justice is encouraged to utilize Federal Victims of Crimes Act () funds’ or any other federal funds that may become available in order to implement this chapter.
