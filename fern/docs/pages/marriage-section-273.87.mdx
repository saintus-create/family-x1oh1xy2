Use of federal funds; implementation of chapter
The Department of Justice is encouraged to utilize Federal
Victims of Crimes Act (VOCA) funds’ or any other federal
funds that may become available in order to implement this
chapter. (Added by Stats.1985, c. 1122, § 1. Amended. by
Stats. 1994, c.599 (A.B.801), § 8, eff. Sept. 16, 1994.)
