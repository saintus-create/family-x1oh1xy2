# § 7669 — Consent for adoption; appeal from order and judgment

consent for adoption; appeal from order and judgment
c. 162
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An order requiring or dispensing with an alleged
father’s consent for the adoption of a child may be appealed
from in the same manner as an order of the juvenile court
declaring a person to be a ward of the juvenile court and is
conclusive and binding upon the alleged father.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  After making the order, the court has no power to set
aside, change, or modify that order.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section limits the right to appeal from
the order and judgment. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10,
operative Jan. 1, 1994.
Amended by Stats.2002, c. 260
(S.B.1512), § 2; Stats.2003, c. 251 (S.B.182), § 4; Stats.2013,
c. 510 (A.B.1403), § 23.)
