# California Family Code § 7669

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  An order requiring or dispensing with an alleged father’s consent for the adoption of a child may be appealed from in the same manner as an order of the juvenile court declaring a person to be a ward of the juvenile court and is conclusive and binding upon the alleged father.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  After making the order, the court has no power to set aside, change, or modify that order.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Nothing in this section limits the right to appeal from the order and judgment.
