# § 504.

*Part 4 — Confidential Marriage · Chapter 1 — General Provisions*

A confidential marriage license is valid only for a period of 90 days after its issuance by the county clerk. (Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 913, Sec. 16. (AB 2747) Effective January 1, 2015.)
