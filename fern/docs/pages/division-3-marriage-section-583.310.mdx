# Marriage

## Section 583.310

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

(c)(1) Whenever the court grants relief from a default, default judgment, or dismissal based on any of the provisions of this section, the court may do any of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Impose a penalty of no greater than one thousand dollars ($1,000) upon an offending attorney or party. 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Direct that an offending attorney pay an amount no greater than one thousand dollars ($1,000) to the State Bar Client Security Fund. 
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Grant other relief as is appropriate. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  However, where the court grants relief from a default or default judgment pursuant to this section based upon the affidavit of the defaulting party’s attorney attesting to the attorney’s mistake, inadvertence, surprise, or neglect, the relief shall not be made conditional upon the attorney’s payment of compensatory legal fees or costs or monetary penalties imposed by the court or upon compliance with other sanctions ordered by the court. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The court may, upon motion of the injured party, or its own motion, correct clerical mistakes in its judgment or orders as entered, so as to conform to the judgment or order directed, and may, on motion of either party after notice to the other party, set aside any void judgment or order.
