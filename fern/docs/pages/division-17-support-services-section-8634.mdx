# § 8634

*Construction of contract; remedies*

Construction of contract; remedies
Any contract entered into pursuant to this chapter is
subject to the rules and remedies relating to contracts
generally.
(Added by Stats.1996, c. 1135 (S.B.2035), § 1.)
