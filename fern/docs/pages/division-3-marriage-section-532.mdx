# 532 — Proof of completion of course of instruction

Premarital Agreements

Proof of completion of course of instruction
No approval, or renewal of the approval, shall be granted
pursuant to this chapter unless the notary public shows
evidence of successful completion of a course of instruction
concerning the issuance of confidential marriage licenses that
was conducted by the county clerk in the county of registra-
tion.
The course of instruction shall not exceed six hours in
duration.
