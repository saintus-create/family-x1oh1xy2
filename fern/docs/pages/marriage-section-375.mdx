# General Provisions

## Section 375

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Disability of party; effect on action An action or proceeding does not abate by the disability of a party. 
The court, on motion, shall allow the action or proceeding to be continued by or against the party’s representative.
