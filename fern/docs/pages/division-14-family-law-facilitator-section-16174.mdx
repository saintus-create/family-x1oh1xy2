# Family Law Facilitator Act

## Section 16174

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-14-family-law-facilitator">Division 14: Family Law Facilitator Act</a></div>

Required contents A compact entered into pursuant to the authority conferred by this chapter shall contain all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A provision making it available for joinder by all states. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A provision for withdrawal from the compact upon written notice to the parties, with a period of one year between the date of the notice and the effective date of the withdrawal. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A requirement that the protections afforded by the compact continue in force for the duration of the adoption assistance and be applicable to all children and their adoptive parents who on the effective date of the withdrawal are receiving adoption assistance from a party state other than the one in which they are resident and have their principal place of abode. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A requirement that each instance of adoption assistance to which the compact applies be covered by an adoption assistance agreement in writing between the adoptive parents and the state child welfare agency of the state which undertakes to provide the adoption assistance, and further, that any such agreement be expressly for the benefit of the adopted child and enforceable by the adoptive parents, and the state agency providing the adoption. assistance. 
 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Any other provision as may be appropriate to implement the proper administration of the compact.
