# 16174

Required contents

Required contents
A compact entered into pursuant to the authority con-
ferred by this chapter shall contain all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A provision making it available for joinder by all states.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A provision for withdrawal from the compact upon
written notice to the parties, with a period of one year
between the date of the notice and the effective date of the
withdrawal.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A requirement that the protections afforded by the
compact continue in force for the duration of the adoption
assistance and be applicable to all children and their adoptive
parents who on the effective date of the withdrawal are
receiving adoption assistance from a party state other than
the one in which they are resident and have their principal
place of abode.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  A requirement that each instance of adoption assis-
tance
to which the compact applies be covered by an
adoption assistance agreement in writing between the adop-
tive parents and the state child welfare agency of the state
which undertakes to provide the adoption assistance, and
further, that any such agreement be expressly for the benefit
of the adopted child and enforceable by the adoptive parents,
and the state agency providing the adoption. assistance.
WELFARE AND INSTITUTIONS CODE
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Any other provision as may be appropriate to imple-
ment the proper administration of the compact.
