# 2400. Conditions necessary at commencement of proceed-

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

Conditions necessary at commencement of proceed-
ings.
2401.
Joint petition; filing; form; contents.
2402.
Revocation of joint petition; termination of proceed-
ings; notice; filing; copy to other party.
2403.
Entry of judgment of dissolution; notice.
2404.
Final judgment as final adjudication of rights and
obligations.
2405.
Actions to set aside final judgment.
2406.
Brochure to describe proceedings; availability; distri-
bution; contents and form.
