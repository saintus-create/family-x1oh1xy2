# Adoption

## Section 8911

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Conditions of placement; filing of petition As a condition of placement, the prospective adoptive parents shall file a petition to adopt the child under <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=8912.">§ 8912</a> within 30 days of placement.
