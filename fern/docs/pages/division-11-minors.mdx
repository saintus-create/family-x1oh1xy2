## Minors

  <h3>Age of majority</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 6500</Badge></div>
        <p>A person is an adult and of full age when he or she attains the age of 18 years, except as otherwise provided by law.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Capacity to contract</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 6700</Badge></div>
        <p>A minor may make a contract in the same manner as an adult, with the right to disaffirm the contract upon reaching majority.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  
  <h3>Emancipation</h3>

    <div className="statute-card">
      <div className="section-body">
        <div className="section-meta"><Badge intent="info" minimal>Sec. 7120</Badge></div>
        <p>A minor may petition the court for a declaration of emancipation. The court shall grant the declaration if the minor is at least 14, managing their own financial affairs, and emancipation is in their best interest.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
