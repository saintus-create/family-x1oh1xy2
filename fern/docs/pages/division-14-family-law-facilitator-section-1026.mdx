# 1026. Exception: Required report

*Family Law Facilitator Act*

Exception: Required report
There is no privilege under this article as to information
that the psychotherapist or the patient is required to report to
a public employee or as to information required to be

EVIDENCE CODE
recorded in a public office, if such report or record is open to
public inspection.
