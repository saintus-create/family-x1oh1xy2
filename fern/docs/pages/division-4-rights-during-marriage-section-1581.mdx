# § 1581 — Consent can be communi-

COMMUNICATION OF CONSENT.
Consent can be communi-
cated with effect, only by some act or omission of the party
contracting, by which he intends to communicate it, or which
necessarily tends to such communication.
(Enacted in 1872.)
Communication; consent
