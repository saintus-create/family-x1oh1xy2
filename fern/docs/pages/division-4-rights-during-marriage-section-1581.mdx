# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1581

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

OF . 
Consent can be communicated with effect, only by some act or omission of the party contracting, by which he intends to communicate it, or which necessarily tends to such communication. 
(Enacted in 1872.) 
Communication; consent
