# § 3801 — Determination of economic feasibility of deferred

Determination of economic feasibility of deferred
sale
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If one of the parties has requested a deferred sale of
home order pursuant to this chapter, the court shall first
determine whether it is economically feasible to maintain the
payments of any note secured by a deed of trust, property
taxes, insurance for the home during the period the sale of
the home
is deferred, and the condition
of the home
comparable to that at the time of trial.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In making this determination, the court shall consider
all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The resident parent’s income.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The availability of spousal support, child support, or
both spousal and child support.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Any other sources of funds available to make those
payments.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  It is the intent of the Legislature, by requiring the
determination under this section, to do all of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Avoid the likelihood
of possible defaults on the
payments of notes and resulting foreclosures.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Avoid inadequate insurance coverage.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Prevent deterioration of the condition of the family
home.
;
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Prevent any other circumstance which would jeopard-
ize both parents’ equity in the home.
