# California Family Code § 6252

An emergency protective order may include any of the following specific orders, as appropriate:

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A protective order, as defined in Section 6218.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  An order determining the temporary care and control of any minor child of the endangered person and the person against whom the order is sought.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An order authorized in Section 213.5 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, including provisions placing the temporary care and control of the endangered child and any other minor children in the family or household with the parent or guardian of the endangered child who is not a restrained party.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  An order determining the temporary care and control of any minor child who is in danger of being abducted.

<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  An order authorized by Section 15657.03 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
