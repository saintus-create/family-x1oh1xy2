# § 4615.

*Part 5 — Enforcement Of Support Orders · Chapter 3 — Deposit Of Assets To Secure Future Child Support Payments*

In lieu of depositing cash or other assets as provided in Section 4614, the obligor-parent may, if approved by the court, provide a performance bond secured by real property or other assets of the obligor-parent and equal in value to one year of payments. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
