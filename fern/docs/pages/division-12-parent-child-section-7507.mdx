# 7507 — Abuse of parental authority; remedy

Parent and Child Relationship

Abuse of parental authority; remedy
The abuse of parental authority is the subject of judicial
cognizance in a civil action brought by the child, or by the
child’s relative within the third degree, or by the supervisors
of the county where the child resides; and when the abuse is
established, the child may be freed from the dominion of the
parent, and the duty of support and education enforced.
