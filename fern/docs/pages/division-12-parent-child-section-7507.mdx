# § 7507 — Abuse of parental authority; remedy

The abuse of parental authority is the subject of judicial cognizance in a civil action brought by the child, or by the child’s relative within the third degree, or by the supervisors of the county where the child resides; and when the abuse is established, the child may be freed from the dominion of the parent, and the duty of support and education enforced. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 170. Effective January 1, 1994.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
