# General Provisions

## Section 362.05

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Extracurricular, enrichment, and social activities for dependent children 
(a)(1) Every child adjudged a dependent child of the juvenile court shall be entitled to participate in age-appropriate extracurricular, enrichment, and social activities, including, but not limited to, access to computer technology and the Internet. 
A state or local regulation or policy shall not prevent, or create barriers to, participation in those activities. 
Each state and local entity shall ensure that private agencies that provide foster care services to dependent children have policies consistent with this section and that those agencies promote and protect the ability of dependent children to participate in age-appropriate extracurricular, enrichment, and social activities, including, but not limited to, access to computer technology and the Internet.
