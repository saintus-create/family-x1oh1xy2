# Minors

## Section 6910

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Medical treatment of minor; adult entrusted with consensual power The parent, guardian, or caregiver of a minor who is a relative of the minor and who may authorize medical care and dental care under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6550.">Section 6550</a>, may authorize in writing an adult into whose care a minor has been entrusted to consent to medical care or dental care, or both, for the minor.

<div class="source-note">

**Source:** California Family Code Annotated (Grace Ganz Blumberg, 2020 Desktop Edition). [View on California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=6910.).
