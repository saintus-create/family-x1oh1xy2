# § 7647

*Requirements for granting motion to set aside or*

Requirements for granting motion to set aside or
vacate parentage judgment; proof of service
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court may grant a motion to set aside or vacate a
judgment establishing parentage only if all of the following
conditions are met:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The motion is filed in a court of proper venue.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The motion contains, at a minimum, all of the follow-
ing information, if known:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The legal name, age, county of residence, and resi-
dence address of the child.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The names, mailing addresses, and counties of resi-
dence, or, if deceased, the date and place of death, of the
following persons:
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  The previously established parents and the alleged
father of the child.
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  The guardian of the child, if any.
(iii) Any person who has physical custody of the child.
