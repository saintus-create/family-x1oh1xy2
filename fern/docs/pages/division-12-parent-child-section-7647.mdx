# California Family Code § 7647

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A court may grant a motion to set aside or vacate a judgment establishing parentage only if all of the following conditions are met:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The motion is filed in a court of proper venue.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The motion contains, at a minimum, all of the following information, if known:

<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The legal name, age, county of residence, and residence address of the child.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The names, mailing addresses, and counties of residence, or, if deceased, the date and place of death, of the following persons:

<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  The previously established parents and the alleged father of the child.

<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  The guardian of the child, if any.

(iii) Any person who has physical custody of the child.

<span class="subsec">(iv)</span>  The guardian ad litem of the child, if any, as appointed pursuant to Section 7647.5.

<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  A declaration that the person filing the motion believes that the previously established father is not the genetic father of the child, the specific reasons for this belief, and a declaration that the person desires that the motion be granted. The moving party is not required to present evidence of genetic testing indicating that the previously established father is not the genetic father of the child in order to bring this motion pursuant to Section 7646.

<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  A declaration that the marital presumption set forth in Section 7540 does not apply and that an action is not barred under paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 7630.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The court finds that the previously established father is not a genetic parent pursuant to Section 7555.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The motion shall include a proof of service upon the following persons, excluding the person bringing the motion:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The parties to the action resulting in the judgment of parentage.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The local child support agency, if services are being provided to the child pursuant to Title IV-D or IV-E of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 651 et seq. and 42 U.S.C. Sec. 670 et seq.).

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The child’s guardian ad litem, if any.
