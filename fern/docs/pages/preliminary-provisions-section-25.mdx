# § 25 — See also

12[1].
See also
Rosenthal v. Rosenthal, 197 Cal.App.2d 289, 297-98, 17 Cal.Rptr.
186 (1961) (order to pay attorney’s fees not precluded even though
party’s expenses exceed income); Section 271 (sanction may not put
“unreasonable financial burden” on sanctioned party).
[23 Cal.
L.Rev.Comm. Reports 1 (1993)].
