# Division of Property

## Section 2581

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Division of property; presumptions For the purpose of division of property on dissolution of marriage or legal separation of the parties, property acquired 173 
§ 2581 by the parties during marriage in joint form, including property held in tenancy in common, joint tenancy, or tenancy by the entirety, or as community property, is presumed to be community property. 
This presumption is a presumption affecting the burden of proof and may be rebutted by either of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A clear statement in the deed or other documentary evidence of title by which the property is acquired that the property is separate property and not community property. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Proof that the parties have made a written agreement that the property is separate property.
