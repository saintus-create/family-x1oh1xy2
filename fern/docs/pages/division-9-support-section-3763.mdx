# § 3763 — Time for order of health insurance coverage as-

Time for order of health insurance coverage as-
signment order; modification
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The health insurance coverage assignment order may
be ordered at the time of trial or entry of a judgment ordering
health insurance coverage.
The order operates as an assign-
ment and is binding on any existing or future employer of the
obligor parent, or other person providing health insurance to
the obligor, upon whom a copy of the order has been served.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The order of assignment may be modified at any time
by the court.
