# General Provisions

## Section 363

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

“Action” defined The word “action” as used in this Title is to be construed, whenever it is necessary so to do, as including a special proceeding of a civil nature. 
(Enacted in 1872, unpublished Act of 1872.)
