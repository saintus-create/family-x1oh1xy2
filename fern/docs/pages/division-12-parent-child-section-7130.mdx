# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7130

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Grounds for voiding or rescinding declaration. 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A declaration of emancipation obtained by fraud or by the withholding of material information is voidable. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A declaration of emancipation of a minor who is indigent and has no means of support is subject to rescission.
