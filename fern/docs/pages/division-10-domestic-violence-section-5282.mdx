Duty of employer to inform obligee of obligor’s
change of employment
After the obligor has left employment with the employer,
the employer, at the time the next payment is due on the
assignment order, shall notify the obligee designated in the
assignment order by first-class mail, postage prepaid, to the
last known address of the obligee that the obligor has left
employment.
