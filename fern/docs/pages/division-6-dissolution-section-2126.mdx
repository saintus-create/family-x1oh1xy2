# § 2126 — Valuation date of assets or liabilities for which

As to assets or liabilities for which a judgment or part of a judgment is set aside, the date of valuation shall be subject to equitable considerations. The court shall equally divide the asset or liability, unless the court finds upon good cause shown that the interests of justice require an unequal division. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 108. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
