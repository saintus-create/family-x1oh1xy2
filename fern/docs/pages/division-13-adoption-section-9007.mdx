# Adoption

## Section 9007

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  For stepparent adoptions filed under this section, the following shall be filed with the petition for adoption: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A copy of the parties’ marriage certificate, registered domestic partner certificate, or civil union from another jurisdiction. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  A copy of the child’s birth certificate. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Declarations by the parent who gave birth, or who caused the birth through gestational surrogacy, and the spouse or partner who is adopting explaining the circumstances of the child’s conception in detail sufficient to identify whether there may be other persons with a claim to parentage of the child who is required to be provided notice of, or who must consent to, the adoption. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The court may order a hearing to ascertain whether there are additional persons who must be provided notice of, or who must consent to, the adoption if it appears from the face of the pleadings and the evidence that proper notice or consent have not been provided. 
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  The court shall grant the stepparent adoption under this section upon finding both of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  That the parent who gave birth , or who caused _ 
the birth through gestational surrogacy, and the spouse or partner who is adopting were married or in a domestic partnership, including a registered domestic partnership or civil union from another jurisdiction, at the time of the child’s birth. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Any other person with a claim to parentage of the child who is required to be provided notice of, or who must consent OF to, the adoption has been noticed or provided consent to the adoption.
