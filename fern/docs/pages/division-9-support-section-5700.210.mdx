A tribunal of this state exercising personal jurisdiction over
a nonresident in a proceeding under this part, under other
law of this state relating to a support order, or recognizing a
foreign support order may receive evidence from outside this
state pursuant to Section 5700.316, communicate with a
tribunal outside this state pursuant to Section 5700.317, and
obtain discovery through a tribunal outside this state pursuant to Section 5700.318. In all other respects, Chapters 3
through 6 do not apply, and the tribunal shall apply the
procedural and substantive law of this state.
(Added by
Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
