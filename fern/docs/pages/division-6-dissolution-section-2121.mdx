# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2121

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

Authority of court to provide relief 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In proceedings for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, or for legal separation of the parties, the court may, on any terms that may be just, relieve a spouse from a judgment, or any part or parts thereof, adjudicating support or division of property, after the six-month time limit of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=473.">§ 473</a> of the Code of Civil Procedure has run, based on the grounds, and within the time limits, provided in this chapter.

§ 2122 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In all proceedings under this chapter, before granting relief, the court shall find that the facts alleged as the grounds for relief materially affected the original outcome and that the moving party would materially benefit from the granting of the relief.
