# § 7908.5

*Court Law*

Court Law

“Jurisdiction” defined; legislative intent
For the purposes of an interstate adoption placement, the
term “jurisdiction” as used in Article 5 of the Interstate
Compact on the Placement of Children means “jurisdiction
over or legal responsibility for the child.” It is the intent of
the Legislature that this section make a technical clarification
to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children and
not a substantive change.
(Added by Stats.2002, c. 260
(S.B.1512), § 7.)
