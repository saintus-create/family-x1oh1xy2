# 2074. Order pursuant to Section 2073, any party may present further

*Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation*

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  At any hearing on a motion to set aside or modify an
order pursuant to Section 2073, any party may present further
evidence on any issue relating to the rights of the parties
under the employee benefit plan or the extent of the parties’
community or quasi-community property interest in the plan,
except where the parties have agreed in writing to the
contrary.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Any statement of decision issued by the court with
respect to the order which is the subject of the motion shall
take account of the evidence referred to in subdivision (a).
Motion to set aside or modify; hearing

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  If the provisions of the order affecting the employee
benefit plan are modified or set aside, the court, on motion
by either party, may set aside or modify other provisions of
the order and associated orders or judgments related to or
affected by the provisions affecting the plan.
