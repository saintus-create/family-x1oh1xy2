# California Family Code § 3101

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the court may grant reasonable visitation to a stepparent, if visitation by the stepparent is determined to be in the best interest of the minor child.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  If a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, has been directed to a stepparent to whom visitation may be granted pursuant to this section, the court shall consider whether the best interest of the child requires that any visitation by the stepparent be denied.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Visitation rights may not be ordered under this section that would conflict with a right of custody or visitation of a birth parent who is not a party to the proceeding.

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  As used in this section:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Birth parent” means “birth parent” as defined in Section 8512.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Stepparent” means a person who is a party to the marriage that is the subject of the proceeding, with respect to a minor child of the other party to the marriage.
