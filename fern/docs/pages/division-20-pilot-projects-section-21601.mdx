# § 21601 — Instruments” means the decedent’s will or revocable trust

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  For purposes of this part, “decedent’s testamentary
instruments” means the decedent’s will or revocable trust.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  “Estate” as used in this part shall include a decedent’s
probate estate and all property held in any revocable trust
that becomes irrevocable on the death of the decedent.
