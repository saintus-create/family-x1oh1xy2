# General Provisions

## Section 343

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Four years; relief not otherwise provided for An action for relief not hereinbefore provided for must be commenced within four years after the cause of action shall have accrued. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
