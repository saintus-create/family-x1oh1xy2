# 7825

Parent and Child Relationship

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Physical custody by the parent or parents for insubstantial periods of time does not interrupt the running of the oneyear period. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650),.§ 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by Stats,2018, c. 83 (A.B.2792), § 1,

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A proceeding under this part may be brought where
both of the following requirements are satisfied:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The child is one whose parent or parents are convicted
of a felony.

FREEDOM
FROM
CUSTODY
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The facts of the crime of which the parent or parents
were convicted are of such a nature so as to prove the
unfitness of the parent or parents to have the future custody
and control of the child.
In making a determination
pursuant to this section, the court may consider the parent’s
criminal record prior to the felony conviction to the extent
that the criminal record demonstrates a pattern of behavior
substantially related to the welfare of the child or the parent’s
ability to exercise custody and control regarding * * * the
child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The mother of a child may bring a proceeding under
this part against the father of the child, where the child was
conceived as a result of an act in violation of Section 261 of
the Penal Code, and where the father was convicted of that
violation.
