683.030.
Application for renewal; effect
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The judgment creditor may renew a judgment by filing
an application for renewal of the judgment with the court in
which the judgment was entered.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Except as otherwise provided in this article, the filing
of the application renews the judgment in the amount
determined under Section 683.150 and extends the period of
enforceability of the judgment as renewed for a period of 10
years from the date the application is filed.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In the case of a money judgment payable in installments, for the purposes of enforcement and of any later
renewal, the amount of the judgment as renewed shall be
treated as a lump-sum money judgment entered on the date
the application is filed. (Added by Stats.1982, c. 1364, p.
5074, § 2, operative July 1, 1983.)
Stats.1992, c. 718 (A.B.568), § 4; Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.
1500), § 66; Stats.1993, c. 876 (S.B.1068), § 8, eff. Oct. 6,
1993; Stats.2000, c. 808 (A.B.1358), § 4, eff. Sept. 28, 2000.)
