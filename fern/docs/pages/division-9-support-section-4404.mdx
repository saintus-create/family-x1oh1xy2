# Support

## Section 4404

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Determination of amount due for support; circumstances In determining the amount to be ordered for support, the court shall consider the following circumstances of each party: 
(a) Earning capacity and needs. 
(b) Obligations and assets. 
(c) Age and health. 
(d) Standard of living. 

§ 4404 
(ec) Other factors the court deems just and equitable.
