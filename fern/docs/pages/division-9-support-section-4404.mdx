# § 4404 — Determination of amount due for support; circum-

Determination of amount due for support; circum-
stances
In determining the amount to be ordered for support, the
court shall consider the following circumstances of each
party:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Earning capacity and needs.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Obligations and assets.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Age and health.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Standard of living.

where the adult child resides.
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
<span class="subsec">(ec)</span>  Other factors the court deems just and equitable.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
