Injuries to married person by third party;
extent
concurring negligence. of spouse allowable as defense
If a married person is injured by the negligent or wrongful
act or omission of a person other than the married person’s
spouse, the fact that the negligent or wrongful act or omission
of the spouse of the injured person was a concurring cause of
the injury is not a defense in an action brought by the injured
person to recover damages for the injury except in cases
where the concurring negligent or wrongful act or omission
would be a defense if the marriage did not exist.
