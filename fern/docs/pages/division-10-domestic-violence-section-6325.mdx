# § 6325 — Temporary child custody

The court may issue an ex parte order restraining a married person from specified acts in relation to community, quasi-community, and separate property as provided in Section 2045. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
