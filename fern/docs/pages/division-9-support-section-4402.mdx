---
title: "4402. Cumulative nature of duties"
description: "Cumulative nature of duties"
slug: division-9-support-section-4402
---

The duty of support under this part is cumulative and not in substitution for any other duty.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
