# General Provisions

## Section 202.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Probation officers’ duties deemed social service; governing authority The duties of the probation officer, as described in this chapter with respect to minors alleged or adjudged to be described by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=300.">§ 300</a>, whether or not delegated pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=272.">§ 272</a>, shall be deemed to be social service as defined by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=10051.">§ 10051</a>, and subject to the administration, supervision and regulations of the State Department of Social Services.
