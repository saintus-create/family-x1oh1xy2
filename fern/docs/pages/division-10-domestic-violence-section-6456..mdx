# § 6456.

*Part 6 — Uniform Recognition And Enforcement Of Canadian Domestic Violence Protection Orders Act*

An individual who seeks a remedy under this part may seek other legal or equitable remedies. (Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 98, Sec. 1. (SB 204) Effective January 1, 2018.)
