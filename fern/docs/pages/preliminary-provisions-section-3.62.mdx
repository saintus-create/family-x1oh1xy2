# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 3.62

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

1, a juvenile court did not abuse its discretion in granting the exboyfriend visitation with her son, when the child had a strong bond with him and, given that there is no presumption of parental fitness in a dependency proceeding, the court determined that it would be in the child’s best interest. 
In re J.P.. 37 Cal.App.5th 1111, 249 Cal.Rptr.3d 916 (2019).
