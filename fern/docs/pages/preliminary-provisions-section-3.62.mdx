# § 3.62

*1,*

1,
a juvenile court did not abuse its discretion in granting the ex-
boyfriend visitation with her son, when the child had a strong bond
with him and, given that there is no presumption of parental fitness in
a dependency proceeding, the court determined that it would be in
the child’s best interest.
In re J.P.. 37 Cal.App.5th
1111, 249
Cal.Rptr.3d 916 (2019).
