# Conciliation Proceedings

## Section 1934

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-5-conciliation">Division 5: Conciliation Proceedings</a></div>

“Indian” defined for certain purposes For the purposes of sections 1932 and 1933 of this title, the term “Indian” shall include persons defined in section 1603(c) of this title. 
(Pub.L. 95-608, Title IT, 204, Nov. 8, 1978, 92 Stat. 3077.)
