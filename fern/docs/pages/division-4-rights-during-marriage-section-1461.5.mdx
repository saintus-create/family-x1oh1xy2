# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1461.5

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Veterans Administration; notice of hearing on petition, report, account or inventory; time; conditions Notice of the time and place of hearing on a petition, report, or account, and a notice of the filing of an inventory, together with a copy of the petition, report, inventory, or account, shall be delivered pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1215.">Section 1215</a> to the office of the Veterans Administration having jurisdiction over the area in which the court is located at least 15 days before the hearing, or within 15 days after the inventory is filed, if both of the following conditions exist: 
(a) The guardianship or conservatorship estate consists or will consist wholly or in part of any of the following: 
(1) Money received from the Veterans Administration. 
(2) Revenue or profit from such money or from property acquired wholly or in part from such money. 
(3) Property acquired wholly or in part with such money or from such property. 
(b) The petition, report, inventory, or account is filed under any one or more of the following provisions: <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1510.">Section 1510</a>, 1601, 1820, 1861, 1874, 2422, or 2423; Article 7 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2540.">Section 2540</a>) of Chapter 6 of Part 4; <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2570.">Section 2570</a>, 2571, 2580, 2592, 2610, 2613, or 2620; Chapter 8 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2640.">Section 2640</a>) of Part 4; Chapter 9.5 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2670.">Section 2670</a>) of Part 4; <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3080.">Section 3080</a> or 3088; or Chapter 3 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3100.">Section 3100</a>) of Part 6.
