# § 1461.5 — Veterans Administration;

Veterans Administration;
notice of hearing on
petition, report, account or inventory; time; conditions
Notice of the time and place of hearing on a petition,
report, or account, and a notice of the filing of an inventory,
together with a copy of the petition, report, inventory, or
account, shall be delivered pursuant to Section 1215 to the
office of the Veterans Administration having jurisdiction over
the area in which the court is located at least 15 days before
PROBATE CODE
the hearing, or within 15 days after the inventory is filed, if
both of the following conditions exist:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The guardianship or conservatorship estate consists or
will consist wholly or in part of any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Money received from the Veterans Administration.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Revenue or profit from such money or from property
acquired wholly or in part from such money.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Property acquired wholly or in part with such money or
from such property.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition, report, inventory, or account is filed
under any one or more of the following provisions: Section
1510, 1601, 1820, 1861, 1874, 2422, or 2423; Article 7
(commencing with Section 2540) of Chapter 6 of ;
Section 2570, 2571, 2580, 2592, 2610, 2613, or 2620; Chapter
8 (commencing with Section 2640) of ; Chapter 9.5
(commencing with Section 2670) of ; Section 3080 or
3088; or Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 3100) of .
