# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1003

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Validity of writing affecting property interest There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue concerning the validity of a deed of conveyance, will, or other writing, executed by a patient, now deceased, purporting to affect an interest in property. 
(Stats. 
1965, c. 299, 2, operative Jan. 1, 1967.)
