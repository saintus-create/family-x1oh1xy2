# § 11486 — Fraudulent or misleading statements relating to

Fraudulent or misleading statements relating to
eligibility for or amount of aid; sanctions
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The needs of any individual who is a member of a
family applying for, or receiving, aid under this chapter shall
not be taken into account in making the determination under
Section 11450 with respect to his or her family beginning on
the date, or at any time thereafter, the individual is found in
state or federal court
or pursuant to an administrative
hearing decision, including any determination made on the
basis of a plea of guilty or nolo contendere,
to have
committed any of the following acts:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Making a fraudulent statement or representation with
respect to the place of residence of the individual in order to
receive assistance simultaneously from two or more states or
counties.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Submitting documents for nonexistent children, or
submitting false documents for the purpose of showing
ineligible children to be eligible for aid.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  When there has been a receipt of cash benefits that
exceeds ten thousand dollars ($10,000) as a result of inten-
tionally and willfully doing any of the following acts for the
purpose of establishing or maintaining the family’s eligibility
for aid or increasing or preventing a reduction in the amount
of aid:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Making a false or misleading statement or misrepre-
senting, concealing, or withholding facts.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Committing any act intended to mislead, misrepresent,
conceal, or withhold facts or propound a falsity.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The needs of any individual who is a member of a
family applying for, or receiving, aid under this chapter shall
not be taken into account in making the determination under
Section 11450 with respect to his or her family for the
following periods beginning on the date or any time thereaf-
ter the individual is convicted of a felony in state or federal
court, including any determination made on the basis of a
plea of guilty or nolo contendere, for committing fraud in the
receipt or attempted receipt of aid:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  For two years, if the amount of aid is less than two
thousand dollars ($2,000).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  For five years, if the amount of aid is two thousand
dollars ($2,000) or more but is less than five thousand dollars
($5,000).
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Permanently, if the amount of aid is five thousand
dollars ($5,000) or more.

(c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Except as. provided in subdivisions <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and (b), the
needs of any individual who is a member of a family applying
for, or receiving, aid under this chapter to whom paragraph
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  applies shall not be taken into account in making the
determination under Section 11450 with respect to his or her
family for the following periods:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  For a period of six months upon the first occasion of
any offense referred to in paragraph (2).
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  For a period of 12 months upon the second occasion
of any of those offenses referred
to in paragraph
(2).
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Permanently, upon the third occasion of any offense
referred to in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  and paragraph (2).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Except as provided in subdivisions (a), (b), and (d),
paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  shall apply to any individual who is found by a
federal or state court, or pursuant to a special administrative
hearing meeting the requirements of regulations adopted by
the United States Secretary of Health and Human Services,
including any determination made on the basis of a plea of
guilty or nolo contendere, to have done any of the following
acts for the purpose of establishing or maintaining the
family’s eligibility for aid or increasing, or preventing a
reduction in, the amount of that aid:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Making a false or misleading statement or misrepre-
senting, concealing, or withholding facts.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Committing any act intended to mislead, misrepresent,
conceal, or withhold facts or propound a falsity.
(d)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Except as provided in subdivisions <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  and (b), and
notwithstanding subdivision (c), the needs of any individual
who is a member of a family applying for, or receiving, aid
under this chapter to whom paragraph <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  applies shall not be
taken into account
in making the determination
under
Section 11450 with respect to his or her family for the
following periods:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  For a period of two years upon the first occasion of
any offense referred to in paragraph (2).
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  For a period of four years upon the second occasion of
any offense referred to in paragraph (2).
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Permanently, upon the third occasion of any offense
referred to in subdivision <span class="subsec">(b)</span>  and paragraph (2).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Paragraph <span class="subsec">(1)</span>  shall apply to any individual who is
found by a federal or state court, or pursuant to a special
administrative hearing meeting the requirements of regula-
tions adopted by the United States Secretary of Health and
Human Services, including any determination made on the
basis of a plea of guilty or nolo contendere, to have submitted
more than one application for the same type of aid for the
same period of time, for the purpose of receiving more than
one grant of aid in order to establish or maintain the family’s
eligibility for aid or increasing, or preventing a reduction in,
the amount of that aid.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Proceedings against any individual alleged to have
committed an offense described in subdivision <span class="subsec">(c)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  may
be held either by hearing, pursuant to Section 10950 and in
conformity with the regulations of the United States Secre-
tary of Health and Human Services, if appropriate, or by
referring the matter to the appropriate authorities for civil or
criminal action in court.
