# Adoption

## Section 8622

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Service limitations; notice A licensed private adoption agency whose services are limited to a particular target population shall inform all birth parents and prospective adoptive parents of its service limitations before commencing any services, signing any documents or agreements, or accepting any fees. 
This section shall become operative on January 1, 1995.
