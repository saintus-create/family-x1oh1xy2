# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1589

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Consent by acceptance of benefits OF BY OF . A voluntary acceptance of the benefit of a transaction is equivalent to a consent to all the obligations arising from it, 

§ 1589 so far as the facts are known, or ought to be known, to the person accepting. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
