# 1589. Revocation of proposal; time

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Revocation of proposal; time
(Enacted in 1872.)
Consent by acceptance of benefits
ASSUMPTION OF OBLIGATION BY ACCEPTANCE OF BENEFITS. A
voluntary acceptance of the benefit of a transaction is
equivalent to a consent to all the obligations arising from it,
REVOCATION OF PROPOSAL. A proposal may be revoked at
any time before its acceptance is communicated to the
proposer, but not afterwards. (Enacted in 1872.)
