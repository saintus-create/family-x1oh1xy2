Placement according to gender identity
Children and nonminor dependents in out-of-home care
shall be placed according to their gender identity, regardless
of the gender or sex listed in their court or child welfare
records. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 805 (S.B.731), $ 3, eff: Jan.
1, 2016.)
