# § 7637 — Contents of judgment or order

Contents of judgment or order
The judgment or order may contain any other provision
directed against the appropriate party to the proceeding,
concerning the duty of support, the custody and guardianship
of the child, visitation privileges with the child, the furnishing
of bond or other security for the payment of the judgment, or
any other matter in the best interest of the child.
The
judgment or order may direct the parent to pay the reason-
able expenses of the mother’s pregnancy and confinement.
