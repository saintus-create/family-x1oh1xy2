# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7637

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Contents of judgment or order The judgment or order may contain any other provision directed against the appropriate party to the proceeding, concerning the duty of support, the custody and guardianship of the child, visitation privileges with the child, the furnishing of bond or other security for the payment of the judgment, or any other matter in the best interest of the child. 
The judgment or order may direct the parent to pay the reasonable expenses of the mother’s pregnancy and confinement.
