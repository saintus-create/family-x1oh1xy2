# § 92.

*Part 2 — Definitions*

“Family support” means an agreement between the parents, or an order or judgment, that combines child support and spousal support without designating the amount to be paid for child support and the amount to be paid for spousal support. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
