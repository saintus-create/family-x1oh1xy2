# § 17407 — Support order or support-related order; appeal

Support order or support-related order; appeal
taken or opposed by Attorney General; expenses
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the Attorney General is of the opinion that a support
order or support-related order is erroneous and presents a

CHILD SUPPORT ENFORCEMENT
question of law warranting an appeal, or that an order is
sound and should be defended on appeal, in the. public
interest the Attorney General may:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Perfect or oppose an appeal to the proper appellate
court if the order was issued by a court of this state.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the order was issued in. another state, cause an
appeal to be taken or opposed in the other state.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In either case, expenses of the appeal may be paid on
order of the Attorney General from funds appropriated for
the Office of the Attorney General.
