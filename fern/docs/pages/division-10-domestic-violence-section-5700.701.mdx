# 5700.701

In this chapter:

In this chapter:

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  “Application” means a request under the Convention by an obligee or obligor, or on behalf of a child, made through a central authority for assistance from another central authority.

<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  “Central authority” means the entity designated by the United States or a foreign country described in Section 5700.102(5)<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  to perform the functions specified in the Convention.

<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  “Convention support order” means a support order of a tribunal of a foreign country described in Section 5700.102(5)(D).

<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  “Direct request” means a petition filed by an individual in a tribunal of this state in a proceeding involving an obligee, obligor, or child residing outside the United States.

<span class="subsec">(5)</span>  “Foreign central authority” means the entity designated by a foreign country described in Section 5700.102(5)<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  to perform the functions specified in the Convention.

<span class="subsec">(6)</span>  “Foreign support agreement”:

<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  means an agreement for support in a record that:

<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  is enforceable as a support order in the country of origin;

<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  has been:

<span class="subsec">(I)</span>  formally drawn up or registered as an authentic instrument by a foreign tribunal; or

<span class="subsec">(II)</span>  authenticated by, or concluded, registered, or filed with a foreign tribunal; and

(iii) may be reviewed and modified by a foreign tribunal; and

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  includes a maintenance arrangement or authentic instrument under the Convention.

<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  “United States central authority” means the Secretary of the United States Department of Health and Human Services.
