# § 6346 — Custody and visitation orders; notice and hearing

Custody and visitation orders; notice and hearing
The court may make appropriate custody and visitation
orders pursuant to the Uniform Parentage Act (
(commencing with Section 7600) of ) after notice
and a hearing under this section when the party who has
requested custody or visitation has not established a parent
and child relationship under subparagraph <span class="subsec">(B)</span>  of paragraph
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 6323, but has taken steps to
establish that relationship by filing an action under the
Uniform Parentage Act.
