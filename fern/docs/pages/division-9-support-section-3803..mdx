# § 3803.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 8 — Deferred Sale Of Home Order*

A deferred sale of home order shall state the duration of the order and may include the legal description and assessor’s parcel number of the real property which is subject to the order. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
