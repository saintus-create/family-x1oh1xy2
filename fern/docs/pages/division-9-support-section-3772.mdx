# 3772. The Judicial Council shall adopt forms for the health

*Support*

The Judicial Council shall adopt forms for the health
insurance coverage assignment required or authorized by this
article, including, but not limited to, the application, the
order, the statement of the obligor’s rights, and an employer’s
return form which shall include information on the limitations on the total amount that may be withheld from earnings
for obligations, including health insurance assignments, under
subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 706.052 of the Code of Civil
Procedure and Section 1673 of Title 15 of the United States
