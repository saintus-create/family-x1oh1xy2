# § 8719 — Withdrawal of petition; dismissal of proceedings;

Withdrawal of petition; dismissal of proceedings;
notification of department
If the petitioners move to withdraw the adoption petition
or to dismiss the proceeding, the court clerk shall immediate-
ly notify the department
at Sacramento
of the action.
