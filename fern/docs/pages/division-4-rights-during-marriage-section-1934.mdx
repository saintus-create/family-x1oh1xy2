# § 1934

*“Indian” defined for certain purposes*

“Indian” defined for certain purposes
For the purposes of sections 1932 and 1933 of this title,
the term “Indian” shall include persons defined in section
1603(c) of this title.
(Pub.L. 95-608, Title IT, § 204, Nov. 8,
1978, 92 Stat. 3077.)
