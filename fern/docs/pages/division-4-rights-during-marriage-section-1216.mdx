# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1216

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Warrant of attachment; undertaking; return The officer must return the warrant of arrest and undertaking, if any, received by him from the person arrested, by the return day specified therein. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
