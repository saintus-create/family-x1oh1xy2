# § 3804.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 8 — Deferred Sale Of Home Order*

A deferred sale of home order may be recorded in the office of the county recorder of the county in which the real property is located. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
