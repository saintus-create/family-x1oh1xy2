# § 1011

*Patient*

Patient
As used in this article, “patient” means a person who
consults a psychotherapist or submits to an examination by a
psychotherapist for the purpose of securing a diagnosis or
preventive, palliative, or curative treatment of his mental or
emotional condition or who submits to an examination of his
mental or emotional condition for the purpose of scientific
research on mental or emotional problems.
