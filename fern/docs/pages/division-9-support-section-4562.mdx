# 4562 — Evidence of deposit

Support

Evidence of deposit
The court shall also order that evidence of the deposit shall
be provided by the child support obligor in the form specified
by the court, which shall be served upon the child support
obligee and filed with the court within a reasonable time
specified by the court, not to exceed 30 days. (Stats. 1992, c.
Comments
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
