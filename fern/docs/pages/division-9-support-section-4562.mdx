# Support

## Section 4562

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Evidence of deposit The court shall also order that evidence of the deposit shall be provided by the child support obligor in the form specified by the court, which shall be served upon the child support obligee and filed with the court within a reasonable time specified by the court, not to exceed 30 days. 
(Stats. 1992, c. 
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
