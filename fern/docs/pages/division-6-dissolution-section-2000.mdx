# § 2000 — Application of part

This part applies to a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, or for legal separation of the parties. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
