# § 6388 — A willful and knowing violation of a protective order, as

A willful and knowing violation of a protective order, as
defined in Section 6218, is a crime punishable as provided by
Section 273.6 of the Penal Code. (Added by Stats. 1993, c. 219
Registration and Enforcement of Orders.
(A.B.1500), § 154.)
