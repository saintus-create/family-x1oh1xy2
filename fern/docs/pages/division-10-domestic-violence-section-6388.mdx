# § 6388 — Willful and knowing violation of order; penalty

A willful and knowing violation of a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, is a crime punishable as provided by Section 273.6 of the Penal Code. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
