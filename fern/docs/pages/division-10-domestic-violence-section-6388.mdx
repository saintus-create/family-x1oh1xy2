---
title: "6388. Willful and knowing violation of order; penalty"
description: "Willful and knowing violation of order; penalty"
slug: division-10-domestic-violence-section-6388
---

A willful and knowing violation of a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, is a crime punishable as provided by Section 273.6 of the Penal Code.

> *Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.*
