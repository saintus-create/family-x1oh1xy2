# § 903 — Time debt is incurred

A debt is “incurred” at the following time:

(a) In the case of a contract, at the time the contract is made.

(b) In the case of a tort, at the time the tort occurs.

(c) In other cases, at the time the obligation arises. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
