# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 903

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Time debt is incurred A debt is “incurred” at the following time: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In the case of a contract, at the time the contract is made. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  In the case of a tort, at the time the tort occurs. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In other cases, at the time the obligation arises.
