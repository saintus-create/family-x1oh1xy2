# Support

## Section 4074

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Application of article to all awards for support of children This article applies to an award for the support of children, including those awards designated as “family support,” that contain provisions for the support of children as well as for the support of the spouse.
