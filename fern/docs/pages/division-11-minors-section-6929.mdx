<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-11-minors">Division 11: Minors</a></div>

Diagnosis or treatment of drug and alcohol abuse; liability for cost of services; disclosure of medical information 
(a) As used in this section: 

§ 6929 
(1) “Counseling” means the provision of counseling services by a provider under a contract with the state or a county to provide alcohol or drug abuse counseling services pursuant to Part 2 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5600.">Section 5600</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=5.">Division 5</a> of the Welfare and Institutions Code or pursuant to <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.5."><a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.">Division 10</a>.5</a> 
(commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11750.">Section 11750</a>) of the Health and Safety Code. 
(2) “Drug or alcohol” includes, but is not limited to, any substance listed in any of the following: 
(A) <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=380.">Section 380</a> or 381 of the Penal Code. 
(B) <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=10.">Division 10</a> (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=11000.">Section 11000</a>) of the Health and Safety Code. 
(C) Subdivision (f) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=647.">Section 647</a> of the Penal Code.
