# § 2452 — The Judicial Council may, by rule, increase the procedures

The Judicial Council may, by rule, increase the procedures set forth in this chapter. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 311, Sec. 1. (AB 2745) Effective January 1, 2015.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
