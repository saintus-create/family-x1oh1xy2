# 2452 — The Judicial Council may, by rule, increase the procedures

Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

The Judicial Council may, by rule, increase the procedures
set forth in this chapter.
(Added by Stats.1996, c. 56
(S.B.389), § 3. Amended by Stats.2014, c. 311 (A.B.2745),
§ 1, eff. Jan. 1, 2015.)
<span class="subsec">(7)</span>  Bifurcation of issues for trial.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  This section does not provide any additional authority
to the court to appoint experts beyond that permitted under
other provisions of law.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The Judicial Council shall, by January 1, 2012, adopt a
statewide rule of court to implement this section.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  The changes made to this section by the act adding this
subdivision ' shall become operative on January 1, 2012.
