Responsibilities of the Attorney General
In addition to such other responsibilities as are specifically
conferred upon the Attorney General by this chapter, the
central authority functions specified in Article 14 of the
Convention (relating to the filing of applications by prospec-
tive adoptive parents to the central authority of their country
of residence) shall be performed by the Attorney General.
(Pub.L. 106-279, Title I, 103, Oct. 6, 2000, 114 Stat. 829.)
