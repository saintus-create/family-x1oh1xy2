# § 6205 — "Affinity" defined

“Affinity,” when applied to the marriage relation, signifies the connection existing in consequence of marriage between each of the married persons and the blood relatives of the other. _(Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
