# Custody of Children

## Section 3439.03

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Value Value is given for a transfer or an obligation if, in exchange for the transfer or obligation, property is transferred or an antecedent debt is secured or satisfied, but value does not include an unperformed promise made otherwise than in the ordinary course of the promisor’s business to furnish support
