# Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

## Section 156

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/preliminary-provisions">Division 1: Preliminary Provisions and Definitions</a></div>

Subdivision (a) codifies the case-law rule applicable under prior law. 
See, e.g., Nelson v. 
California Trust Co., 33 Cal.2d 501, 202 P.2d 1021 (1949). 
This section does not affect the protection of certain pension trusts by Code of Civil Procedure
