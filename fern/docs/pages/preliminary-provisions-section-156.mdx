Subdivision (a)  codifies the
case-law rule applicable under prior law.
See, e.g., Nelson
v.
California Trust Co., 33 Cal.2d 501, 202 P.2d 1021 (1949).
This
section does not affect the protection of certain pension trusts by
Code of Civil Procedure
