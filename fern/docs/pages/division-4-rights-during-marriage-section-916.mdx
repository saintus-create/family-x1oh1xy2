# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 916

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

See also Code Civ. Proc. § 371 (right of married person to defend suit for spouse’s right). 
The introductory and concluding clauses of subdivision (a) make clear that the community estate is liable for all debts of either spouse absent an express statutory exception. 
Thus, community property under the management and control of one spouse pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1100.">§ 1100</a>(d) (spouse operating or managing business), Financial Code <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=851.">§ 851</a> (one-spouse bank account), or Probate Code OF <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3051.">§ 3051</a> (conservatorship) remains liable for the debts of the other spouse. 
For an express statutory exception from liability of a community estate, see <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=911.">§ 911</a> (liability of earnings of nondebtor spouse).
