# § 685.020 — In re Marriage of McClellan, 130 Cal.App.4th 247, 30 Cal.Rptr.3d 5

In re Marriage of McClellan, 130 Cal.App.4th 247, 30 Cal.Rptr.3d 5
(2005), holds that the 2000 amendment to this section applies
retroactively because it clarified, rather than altered, existing law.
Accordingly, it applies in calculating the interest due on child support
arrearages that were the subject of arrearage orders entered before
the amendment took effect.
