# Marriage

## Section 685.020

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-3-marriage">Division 3: Marriage</a></div>

In re Marriage of McClellan, 130 Cal.App.4th 247, 30 Cal.Rptr.3d 5 
(2005), holds that the 2000 amendment to this section applies retroactively because it clarified, rather than altered, existing law. 
Accordingly, it applies in calculating the interest due on child support arrearages that were the subject of arrearage orders entered before the amendment took effect.
