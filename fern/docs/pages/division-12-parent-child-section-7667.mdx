# Parent and Child Relationship

## Section 7667

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-12-parent-child">Division 12: Parent and Child Relationship</a></div>

Time of hearing; parental rights ex parte orders terminating 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notwithstanding any other law, an action to terminate the parental rights of an alleged father of a child as specified in this part shall be set for hearing not more than 45 days after filing of the petition, except as provided in subdivision (c). 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The matter so set shall have precedence over all other civil matters on the date set for trial, except an action to terminate parental rights pursuant to Part 4 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7800.">§ 7800</a>). 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  The court may dispense with a hearing and issue an ex parte order terminating parental rights if any of the following applies: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The identity or whereabouts of the alleged father are unknown. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The alleged father has validly executed a waiver of the right to notice or a denial of parentage. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  The alleged father has been served with written notice of alleged parentage and the proposed adoption, and has failed to bring an action pursuant to subdivision (c) 
of <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7630.">§ 7630</a> within 30 days of service of the notice or the birth of the child, whichever is later.
