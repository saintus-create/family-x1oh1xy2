# Division of Property

## Section 2921

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-7-property">Division 7: Division of Property</a></div>

Persons under jurisdiction of Department of State Hospitals or Department of Developmental Services; consent to application An application of the public guardian for guardianship or conservatorship of the person, the estate, or the person and estate, of a person who is under the jurisdiction of the State Department of State Hospitals or the State Department of Developmental Services shall not be granted without the written consent of the department having jurisdiction of the person.
