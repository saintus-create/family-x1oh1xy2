# 2921 — Persons under jurisdiction of Department of State

Division of Property

Persons under jurisdiction of Department of State
Hospitals or Department of Developmental Services;
consent to application
An application of the public guardian for guardianship or
conservatorship of the person, the estate, or the person and
estate, of a person who is under the jurisdiction of the State
Department of State Hospitals or the State Department of
Developmental Services shall not be granted without the
written consent of the department having jurisdiction of the
person.
