# 21112 — A condition in a transfer of a present or future interest that

Pilot Projects

A condition in a transfer of a present or future interest that
refers to a person’s death “with” or “without” issue, or to a
person’s “having”
or “leaving” issue or no issue, or a
condition based on words of similar import, is construed to
refer to that person’s being dead at the time the transfer
takes effect in enjoyment and to that person either having or
not having, as the case may be, issue who are alive at the time
of enjoyment.
