# California Family Code § 3910

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Each parent of a child has an equal responsibility to maintain, to the extent of their ability, their child of whatever age who is incapacitated from earning a living and without sufficient means.

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The court may order that a support payment be paid to a special needs trust. For purposes of this section, “special needs trust” means a trust that meets the requirements described in subparagraph <span class="subsec">(A)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(C)</span>  of paragraph <span class="subsec">(4)</span>  of subsection <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  of Section 1396p of Title 42 of the United States Code and paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  or <span class="subsec">(4)</span>  of subdivision <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  of Section 50489.9 of Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations.

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  This section does not limit the duty of support under Sections 3900 and 3901.
