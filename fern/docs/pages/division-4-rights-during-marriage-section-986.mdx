# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 986

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Juvenile court proceedings There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding under the Juvenile Court Law, Chapter 2 (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=200.">§ 200</a>) of Part 1 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=2.">Division 2</a> of the Welfare and Institutions Code.
