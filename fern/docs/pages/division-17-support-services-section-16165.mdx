Complaints related to foster care; authority
In his or her efforts to resolve complaints related to foster
care, the ombudsperson may do all of the following:
(a)  Conduct whatever investigation he or she deems neces-
sary.
(b)  Attempt to resolve the complaint informally.

(c)  Submit a written plan to the relevant state or county
agency recommending a course of action to resolve the
complaint.
If the ombudsperson makes a written recommen-
dation, the state or county agency shall submit a written
response
to the ombudsperson within 30 business days.
