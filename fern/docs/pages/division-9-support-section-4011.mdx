# § 4011 — Priority of payments; creditors

Payment of child support ordered by the court shall be made by the person owing the support payment before payment of any debts owed to creditors. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
