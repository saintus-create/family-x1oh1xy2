# § 5700.319 — Temporary child-

temporary child-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If a support order entitled to recognition under this
part has not been issued, a responding tribunal of this state
with personal jurisdiction over the parties may issue a support
order if:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  the individual seeking the order resides outside this
state; or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the support enforcement agency seeking the order is
located outside this state.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The tribunal may issue a temporary child-support
order if the tribunal determines that such an order is
appropriate and the individual ordered to pay is:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  a presumed father of the child;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  petitioning to have his paternity adjudicated;
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  identified as the father of the child through genetic
testing;
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  an alleged father who has declined to submit to genetic
testing;

tribunal, and the status of a proceeding. A tribunal of this
