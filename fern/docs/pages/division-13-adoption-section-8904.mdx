# Adoption

## Section 8904

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-13-adoption">Division 13: Adoption</a></div>

Intercountry adoptions finalized in foreign country; agency services For an intercountry adoption that will be finalized in a foreign country, the licensed adoption agency shall provide all of the following services: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Assessment of the suitability of the applicant’s home. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Certification to the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services that this state’s intercountry adoption requirements have been met. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Readoption services as required by the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services. 
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Postadoption services pursuant to any written_agreement between the licensed adoption agency and any other person or entity for which the postadoption services have been paid.
