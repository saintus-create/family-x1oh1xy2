# § 6656

This part shall not apply to a contract for vlogging services between a minor and their parent or guardian only if a court approves the contract pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 6751. In determining whether to approve such a contract, the court shall consider whether the terms of the contract are at least as beneficial to the minor as the compensation the minor would otherwise receive under this part. _(Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 611, Sec. 1. (SB 764) Effective January 1, 2025.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
