# § 2253.

*Part 2 — Judicial Determination Of Void Or Voidable Marriage · Chapter 3 — Procedural Provisions*

In a proceeding under this part, custody of the children shall be determined according to Division 8 (commencing with Section 3000). (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
