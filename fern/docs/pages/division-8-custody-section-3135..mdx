# § 3135.

*Part 2 — Right To Custody Of Minor Child · Chapter 8 — Location Of Missing Party Or Child*

Part 3 (commencing with Section 3400) does not limit the authority of a district attorney or arresting agency to act pursuant to this chapter, Section 279.6 of the Penal Code, or any other applicable law. (Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.) FAM Family Code - FAM
