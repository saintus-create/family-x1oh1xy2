# 3626. ,

*Support*

,
Time set for hearing
Hearing and Order After Hearing.
The obligated parent shall cause the court clerk to, and the
court clerk shall, set a hearing on the application for the
expedited support order not less than 20 nor more than 30
days after the filing of the response to the application for the
expedited support order and income and expense declaration.
(Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.)
