# 1456.2. Continuing education requirements;

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Continuing education requirements;
by public conservator
compliance
On or before January 1, 2010, the public conservator shall
comply with the continuing education requirements that are
established by the California State Association of Public
child’s
and Public Conservators.
