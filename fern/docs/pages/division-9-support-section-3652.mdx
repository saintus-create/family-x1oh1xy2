# 3652

Attorney’s fees and court costs

Except as against a governmental agency, an order modifying, terminating, or setting aside a support order may include an award of attorney’s fees and court costs to the prevailing party. Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 653, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.
