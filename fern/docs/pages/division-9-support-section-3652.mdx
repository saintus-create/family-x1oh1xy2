# Support

## Section 3652

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Attorney’s fees and court costs Except as against a governmental agency, an order modifying, terminating, or setting aside a support order may include an award of attorney’s fees and court costs to the prevailing party.
