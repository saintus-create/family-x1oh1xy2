# 3652. Attorney’s fees and court costs

*Support*

Attorney’s fees and court costs
153 Cal.App.4th 1203, 63 Cal.Rptr.3d 757 (2007), held that an
agreement between parties to forgive a portion of support arrearages
is unenforceable when it does not resolve any bona fide dispute
between the parties.
In view of subsection (c)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  of this section and sections 3603 and
3653(a), a trial court acted in excess of its jurisdiction when it
suspended child support arrears accrued when a payor was imprisoned. Nor, under section 3692, may a court set aside a support order
because it finds that the order was inequitable when made or because
it was excessive or inadequate due to circumstances existing after the
order was made. S.C. v. G.S., 38 Cal. App.5th 591, 250 Cal.Rptr.3d 696
Except as against a governmental agency, an order modifying, terminating, or setting aside a support order may include
an award of attorney’s fees and court costs to the prevailing
party. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1,
1994. Amended by Stats.1994, c. 1269 (A.B.2208), § 31.6;
