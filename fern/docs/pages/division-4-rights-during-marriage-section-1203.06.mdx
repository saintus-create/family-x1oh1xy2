# § 1203.06 — Soon as possible after filing, the prosecuting attorney must

<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Procedures for consideration (§ 790)
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Before filing a petition alleging a felony offense, or as
soon as possible after filing, the prosecuting attorney must
review the child’s file to determine if the requirements of <span class="subsec">(a)</span> 
are met.
If the prosecuting attorney’s review reveals that the
requirements of <span class="subsec">(a)</span>  have been met, the prosecuting attorney
must file Determination of Eligibility—Deferred Entry of Judg-
ment—Juvenile (form JV—750) with the petition.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the court determines that the child is eligible and
suitable for a deferred entry of judgment,
and would derive
benefit from education, treatment, and rehabilitation efforts,
the court may grant deferred entry of judgment.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Citation (§ 792)
The court must issue Citation and Written Notification for
Deferred Entry of Judgment—Juvenile (form JV-751) to the
child’s custodial parent, guardian, or foster parent. The form
must be personally served on the custodial adult at least 24
hours before
the time set for the appearance
hearing.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Determination without a hearing; supplemental infor-
mation (§ 791)
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The court may grant a deferred entry of judgment as
stated in <span class="subsec">(2)</span>  or (3).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  If the child admits each allegation contained in the
petition
as charged and waives
the right to a speedy
disposition hearing, the court may summarily grant the
deferred entry of judgment.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  When appropriate, the court may order the probation
department to prepare a report with recommendations on the
suitability of the child for deferred entry of judgment or set a
hearing on the matter, with or without the order to the
probation department for a report.
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The probation report must address the following:
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  The child’s age, maturity, educational background,
family relationships, motivation, any treatment history,
and any other relevant factors regarding the benefit the
child would
derive from
education,
treatment,
and
rehabilitation efforts; and
<span class="subsec">(ii)</span>  The programs best suited to assist the child and
the child’s family.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The probation report must be submitted to the
court, the child, the prosecuting attorney, and the child’s
attorney at least 48 hours, excluding noncourt days, before
the hearing.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  Written notification of ineligibility (§ 790)
If it is determined that the child is ineligible for deferred
entry of judgment, the prosecuting attorney must complete
and provide to the court, the child, and the child’s attorney
Determination of Eligibility—Deferred Entry of Judgment—
Juvenile (form JV-750).
RULES OF COURT
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  Conduct of hearing (§§ 791, 794)
At the hearing, the court must consider the declaration of
the prosecuting attorney, any report and recommendations
from the probation department, and any other relevant
material provided by the child or other interested parties.
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  If the child consents to the deferred entry of judgment,
the child must enter an admission as stated in rule 5.778(c)
and (d). A no-contest plea must not be accepted.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The child must waive the right to a speedy disposition
hearing.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  After acceptance of the child’s admission, the court
must set a date for review of the child’s progress and a date
by which the probation department must submit to the court,
the child, the child’s parent or guardian, the child’s attorney,
and the prosecuting attorney a report on the child’s adher-
ence to the conditions set by the court.
Although the date
set may be any time within
the following 36 months,
consideration of dismissal of the petition may not occur until
at least 12: months have passed since the court granted the
deferred entry of judgment.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  If the court grants the deferred entry of judgment, the
court must order search-and-seizure probation conditions
and may order probation conditions regarding the following:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Education;
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Treatment;
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  Testing for alcohol and other drugs, if appropriate;
<span class="subsec">(D)</span>  Curfew and school attendance requirements;
<span class="subsec">(E)</span>  Restitution; and
<span class="subsec">(F)</span>  Any other conditions consistent with the identified
needs of the child and the factors that led to the conduct of
the child.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Compliance with conditions; progress review
Twelve months after the court granted the deferred entry
of judgment and on receipt of the progress report ordered at
the hearing on the deferred entry of judgment, the court may:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Find that the child has complied satisfactorily with the
conditions imposed, dismiss. the petition, seal the court
records in compliance with section 793(c), and vacate the
date set for review hearing; or
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Confirm the review hearing. At the hearing the court
must:
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  Find that the child has complied satisfactorily with
the conditions imposed, dismiss the petition, and seal the
court records in compliance with section 793(c); or
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  Find that the child has. not.complied satisfactorily
with the conditions imposed, lift the deferred entry of
judgment, and set a disposition hearing.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Failure to comply with conditions (§ 793)
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Before the date of the progress hearing, if the child is
found to have committed a misdemeanor offense or more
than one misdemeanor offense on a single occasion, the court
may schedule a hearing within 15 court days.
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  At the hearing, the court must follow the procedure
stated in rule 5.580 <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  to determine if the deferred
entry of judgment should be lifted, with a disposition
hearing to be conducted thereafter.

<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The disposition hearing must be conducted as stated
in rules 5.785 through 5.795.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  The child’s admission
of the charges under
a
deferred entry of judgment must not constitute a finding
that a petition has been sustained unless a judgment is
entered under section 793(b).
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Before the date of the progress hearing, on the court’s
own motion, or if the court receives a declaration from the
probation department or the prosecuting attorney alleging
that the child has not complied with the conditions imposed
or that the conditions are not benefiting the child, or if the
child is found to have committed a felony offense or two or
more misdemeanor offenses on separate occasions, the court
must schedule a hearing within 10 court days.
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  At the hearing, the court must follow the procedure
stated in rule 5.580 <span class="subsec">(d)</span>  and <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  to determine if the deferred
entry of judgment should be lifted, with a disposition
hearing to be conducted thereafter.
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The disposition hearing must be conducted as stated
in rules 5.785 through 5.795.
<span class="subsec">(C)</span>  The child’s admission
of the charges under
a
deferred entry of judgment must not constitute a finding
that a petition has been sustained unless a judgment is
entered under section 793(b).
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Ifthe child is found to have committed a felony offense
or two or more misdemeanor offenses on separate occasions,
the court must schedule a disposition hearing within 10 court
days. The disposition hearing must be conducted as stated in
rules 5.785 through 5.795.
