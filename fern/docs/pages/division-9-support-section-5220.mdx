# 5220

“Timely payment” means receipt of support payments by

“Timely payment” means receipt of support payments by the obligee or assigned obligee within five days of the due date. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.<div class="fl-section-nav">
