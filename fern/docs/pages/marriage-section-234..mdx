# § 234.

*Part 3 — Temporary Restraining Order In Summons*

The automatic granting of the ex parte temporary restraining order under this part is not a court determination or competent evidence in any proceeding of any prior history of the conduct so proscribed occurring between the parties. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
