# Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation

## Section 2100

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-6-dissolution">Division 6: Nullity, Dissolution, and Legal Separation</a></div>

assets and liabilities
The Legislature finds and declares the following:
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  It is the policy of the State of California (1) to marshal,
preserve, and protect community and quasi-community assets
and liabilities that exist at the date of separation so as to
avoid dissipation of the community estate before distribution,
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  to ensure fair and sufficient child and spousal support
awards, and (3) to achieve a division of community and quasicommunity assets and liabilities on the dissolution or nullity
of marriage or legal separation of the parties as provided
under California law.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Sound public policy further favors the reduction of the
adversarial nature of marital dissolution and the attendant
costs by fostering full disclosure and cooperative discovery.

accurate disclosure of all assets and liabilities in which one or
