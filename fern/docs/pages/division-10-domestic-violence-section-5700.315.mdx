# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5700.315

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Nonparentage as defense A party whose parentage of a child has been previously determined by or pursuant to law may not plead nonparentage as a defense to a proceeding under this part. (Added by Stats.2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
