# § 3515.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 1 — Definitions*

“Separate property” does not include quasi-community property. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
