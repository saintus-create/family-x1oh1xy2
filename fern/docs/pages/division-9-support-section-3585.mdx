# 3585 — Severability of child support provisions; orders based

Support

Severability of child support provisions; orders based
on agreements.
3586.
Child support and spousal support agreements; failure
to designate amount of payments.
3587.
Approval of stipulated agreements to pay support for
adult children.
3585.
Severability of child support provisions;
orders
based on agreements
The provisions of an agreement between the parents for
child support shall be deemed to be separate and severable
from
all other provisions of the agreement relating to
property and support of either spouse.
An order for child
support based on the agreement shall be imposed by law and
shall be made under the power of the court to order child
support.
