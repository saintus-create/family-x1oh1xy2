# Support

## Section 3585

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Severability of child support provisions; orders based on agreements. 
An order for child support based on the agreement shall be imposed by law and shall be made under the power of the court to order child support.
