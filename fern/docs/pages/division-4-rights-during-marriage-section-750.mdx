# § 750 — Methods of holding property

Spouses may hold property as joint tenants or tenants in common, or as community property, or as community property with a right of survivorship. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 13. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)_
