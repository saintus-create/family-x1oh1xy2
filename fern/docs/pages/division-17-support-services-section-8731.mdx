# § 8731

*ation; assessment or home study*

ation; assessment or home study
time for initi-
If the prospective adoptive parent of a child is a foster
parent, the assessment or home study described in Section
8730 shall not be initiated until the child to be adopted has
resided in the home of the foster parent for at least six
months.
(Added by Stats.1998,
c. 983 (A.B.2286),
