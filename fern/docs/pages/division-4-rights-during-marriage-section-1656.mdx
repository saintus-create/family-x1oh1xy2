# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1656

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage"><a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a>: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

The scope of the former provisions has been expanded to apply to the entire Family Code. 
The former provisions applied only to the uniform acts of which they were a part, ie., the Uniform Parentage Act (former Part 7 
(commencing with former <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7000.">Section 7000</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=4.">Division 4</a> of the Civil Code, now Part 3 (commencing with <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7600.">Section 7600</a>) of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=12.">Division 12</a> of the Family Code), and the Revised Uniform Reciprocal Enforcement of Support Act of 1968 (former Title 10a (commencing with former <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=1650.">Section 1650</a>) of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure, now Chapter 6 (commencing with 4800) of Part 5 of <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=9.">Division 9</a> of the Family Code). 
This section is a standard provision found in many other codes.
