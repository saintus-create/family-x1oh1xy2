# § 5700.905 — This part applies to proceedings begun on or after January

This part applies to proceedings begun on or after January
1, 2016, to establish a support order or determine parentage
of a child or to register, recognize, enforce, or modify a prior
support order, determination, or agreement, whenever issued
c. 493
(S.B.646),
§ 5, eff: Jan.
1, 2016.)
Emergency regulations
The Department of Child Support Services may adopt
emergency regulations as appropriate to implement this part.
2015, c. 493 (S.B.646), § 5, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
