# § 4571 — Service upon support obligor

The court shall cause a copy of the application, as well as its order to disburse and replenish funds, to be served upon the child support obligor, who shall be subject to contempt of court for failure to comply with the order. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
