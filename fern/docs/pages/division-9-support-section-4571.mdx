# Support

## Section 4571

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Service upon support obligor The court shall cause a copy of the application, as well as its order to disburse and replenish funds, to be served upon the child support obligor, who shall be subject to contempt of 365 
§ 4573 court for failure to comply with the order.
