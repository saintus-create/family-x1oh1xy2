# § 8718.

*Part 2 — Adoption Of Unmarried Minors · Chapter 2 — Agency Adoptions*

The prospective adoptive parents and the child proposed to be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections 8612 and 8613. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
