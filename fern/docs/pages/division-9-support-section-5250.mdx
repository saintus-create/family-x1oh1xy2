# § 5250 — Filing of the application with knowledge of the falsity of the

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  In addition to any other penalty provided by law, the
filing of the application with knowledge of the falsity of the
declaration or notice is punishable as a contempt pursuant to
Section 1209 of the Code of Civil Procedure. (Stats.1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amended by
c. 808 (A.B.1358), § 64, eff. Sept. 28, 2000.)
Application of article
For a support order first issued or modified before July 1,
1990, this article provides a procedure for obtaining an
earnings assignment order for support when the court in
ordering support or modification of support did not issue an
assignment order.
