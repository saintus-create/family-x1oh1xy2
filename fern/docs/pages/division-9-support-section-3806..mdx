# § 3806.

*Part 1 — Definitions And General Provisions · Chapter 8 — Deferred Sale Of Home Order*

The court may make an order specifying the parties’ respective responsibilities for the payment of the costs of routine maintenance and capital improvements. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
