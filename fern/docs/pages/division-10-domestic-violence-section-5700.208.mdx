Child-support order for two or more obligees
In responding to registrations or petitions for enforcement
of two or more child-support orders in effect at the same time
with regard to the same obligor and different individual
obligees, at least one of which was issued by a tribunal of
another state or a foreign country, a tribunal of this state shall
enforce those orders in the same manner as if the orders had
been issued by a tribunal of this state.
