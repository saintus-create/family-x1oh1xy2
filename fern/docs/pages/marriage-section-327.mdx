# General Provisions

## Section 327

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/marriage">Division 2: General Provisions</a></div>

Right of possession; death of person in possession; effect of descent The right of a person to the possession of real property is not impaired or affected by a descent cast in consequence of the death of a person in possession of such property. 
(Enacted in 1872.)
