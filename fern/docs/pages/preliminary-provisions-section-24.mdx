By postponing the determination of class membership until the gift
takes effect in enjoyment where the class is indefinite (e.g., to
“heirs”), Section 21114 should reduce the uncertainty of result under
prior law. See Halbach, Future Interests: Express and Implied Conditions of Survival, 49 Cal. L. Rev. 297, 317-20 (1961). Section 21114 is
consistent with Estate of Easter, 24 Cal.2d 191, 148 P.2d 601 (1944).
[31 Cal.L.Rev.Comm. Reports 201 (2001)].
