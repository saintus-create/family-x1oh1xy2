# § 6701.

*Part 3 — Contracts · Chapter 1 — Capacity To Contract*

A minor cannot do any of the following:

(a) Give a delegation of power.

(b) Make a contract relating to real property or any interest therein.

(c) Make a contract relating to any personal property not in the immediate possession or control of the minor. (Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.) FAM Family Code - FAM
