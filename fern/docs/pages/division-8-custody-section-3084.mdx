# § 3084

*In making an order of joint physical custody, the court shall*

In making an order of joint physical custody, the court shall
specify the rights of each parent to physical control of the
child in sufficient detail to enable a parent deprived of that
control to implement laws for relief of child snatching and
kidnapping. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
