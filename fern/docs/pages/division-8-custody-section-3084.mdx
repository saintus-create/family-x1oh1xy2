# Custody of Children

## Section 3084

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Rights of parents to physical control of child In making an order of joint physical custody, the court shall specify the rights of each parent to physical control of the child in sufficient detail to enable a parent deprived of that control to implement laws for relief of child snatching and kidnapping.
