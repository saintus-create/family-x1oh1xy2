# § 7506 — Support of adult child; compensation

Support of adult child; compensation
Where a child, after attaining the age of majority, continues to serve and to be supported by the parent, neither party
is entitled to compensation, in the absence of an agreement
for the compensation.
1993, c. 219 (A.B.
Rights of Parents.
1500), § 169.)
