# 7506

Support of adult child; compensation

Where a child, after attaining the age of majority, continues to serve and to be supported by the parent, neither party is entitled to compensation, in the absence of an agreement for the compensation. Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 169. Effective January 1, 1994.
