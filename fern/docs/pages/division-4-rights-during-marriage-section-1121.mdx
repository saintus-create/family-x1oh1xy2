Neither a mediator nor anyone else may submit to a court
or other adjudicative body, and a court or other adjudicative
body may not consider, any report, assessment, evaluation,
recommendation, or finding of any kind by the mediator
concerning a mediation conducted by the mediator, other
than a report that is mandated by court rule or other law and
that states only whether an agreement was reached, unless all
parties to the mediation expressly agree otherwise in writing,
