# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5044

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Rights of purchaser or encumbrancer of real property who relies on affidavit or declaration 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Nothing in this chapter affects the rights of a purchaser or encumbrancer of real property for value who in good faith relies on an affidavit or a declaration under penalty of perjury under the laws of this state that states all of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The name of the decedent. 
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  The date and place of the decedent’s death. 
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  A description of the real property transferred to the affiant or declarant by an instrument making a nonprobate transfer or by operation of joint tenancy survivorship. 
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Either of the following, as appropriate: 
<span class="subsec">(A)</span>  The affiant or declarant is the surviving spouse of the decedent. 
1146 
<span class="subsec">(B)</span>  The affiant or declarant is not the surviving spouse of the decedent, but the rights of the affiant or declarant to the described property are not affected by <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=5040.">§ 5040</a> or 5042. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  A person relying on an affidavit or declaration made pursuant to subdivision (a) has no duty to inquire into the truth of the matters stated in the affidavit or declaration. 
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  An affidavit or declaration made pursuant to subdivision (a) may be recorded. 
(Formerly § 5602, added by Stats.2001, c. 417 (A.B.873), § 9, operative Jan. 1, 2002. 
Renumbered § 5044 and amended by Stats.2015, c. 293 
(A.B.139), § 14, eff: Jan. 1, 2016.)
