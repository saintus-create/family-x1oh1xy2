<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The superior court shall appoint one or more subordinate judicial officers as child support commissioners to
perform the duties specified in Section 4251. The child
support commissioners’ first priority always shall be to hear
Title IV-D child support cases. The child support commissioners shall specialize in hearing child support cases, and
their primary responsibility shall be to hear Title [V—D child
support cases. Notwithstanding Section 71622 of the Government Code, the number of child support commissioner
positions allotted to each court shall be determined by the
Judicial Council in accordance with caseload standards
developed pursuant to paragraph <span class="subsec">(3)</span>  of subdivision (b),
subject to appropriations in the annual Budget Act.

commissioners.

personnel that are assigned to Title [V—D child support cases.
