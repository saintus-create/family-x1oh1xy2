# 331 — Failure of social worker to file petition;

Premarital Agreements

Failure of social worker to file petition;
decision
review of
When any person has applied to the social worker,
pursuant to Section 329, to commence juvenile court proceedings and the social worker fails to file a petition within
three weeks after the application, the person may, within one
month after making the application, apply to the juvenile
court to review the decision of the social worker, and the
court may either affirm the decision of the social worker or
order him or her to commence juvenile court proceedings.
(Added by Stats.1976,
c. 1068, p. 4765, § 8
Amended
by
Stats. 1998, c. 1054 (A.B.1091), § 13.)
