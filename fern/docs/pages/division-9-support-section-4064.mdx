# Support

## Section 4064

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Adjustment of award to accommodate parent’s seasonal or fluctuating income The court may adjust the child support. order.as appropriate to accommodate seasonal or fluctuating income of either parent.
