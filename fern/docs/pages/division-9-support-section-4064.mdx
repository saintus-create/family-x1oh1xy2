---
title: "4064. Adjustment of award"
description: "Adjustment of award"
slug: division-9-support-section-4064
---

The court may adjust the child support order as appropriate to accommodate seasonal or fluctuating income of either parent. Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.
