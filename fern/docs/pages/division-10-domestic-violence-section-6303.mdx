# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 6303

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

Support persons for victims of domestic violence; powers and duties; discretion of court 
(a) It is the function of a support person to provide moral and emotional support for a person who alleges to bea victim of domestic violence. 
The person who alleges to be a victim of domestic violence may select any individual to act as a support person. 
No certification, training, or other special qualification is required for an individual to act as a support person.
