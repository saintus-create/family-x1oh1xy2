Adoptions finalized in foreign country; reporting
arrival of adoptee in state
(a)  An intercountry adoption agency shall report the
arrival of an adoptee whose adoption was finalized in a
foreign country to the department within 14 calendar days of
the adoptee’s arrival in California.
(b)  If the adoption agency fails to report to the depart-
ment pursuant to subdivision (a), the department may take
appropriate disciplinary action against the adoption agency if
it is licensed in the State of California.
