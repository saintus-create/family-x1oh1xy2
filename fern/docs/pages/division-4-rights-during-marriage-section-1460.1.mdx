# Rights and Obligations During Marriage

## Section 1460.1

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-4-rights-during-marriage">Division 4: Rights and Obligations During Marriage</a></div>

Children under 12 years of age; exceptions to notice requirements Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, no notice is required to be given to any child under the age of 12 years if the court determines either of the following: 
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Notice was properly given to a parent, guardian, or other person having legal custody of the minor, with whom the minor resides. 
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The petition is brought by a parent, guardian, or other person having legal custody of the minor, with whom the minor resides.
