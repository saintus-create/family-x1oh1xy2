# 7804 — Appointment of party to act in minor’s behalf

Parent and Child Relationship

Appointment of party to act in minor’s behalf
In a proceeding under this part, the court may appoint a
suitable party to act in behalf of the child and may order such
further notice of the proceedings to be given as the court
deems proper. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative
Jan. 1, 1994.)
