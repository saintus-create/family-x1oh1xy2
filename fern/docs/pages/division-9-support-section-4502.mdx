# § 4502 — Judgments or orders for support; enforcement and

The period for enforcement and procedure for renewal of a judgment or order for child, family, or spousal support is governed by Section 291. _(Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 86, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2007.)_
