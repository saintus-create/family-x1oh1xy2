# § 3809 — Reservation of jurisdiction

Reservation of jurisdiction
In making an order pursuant to this chapter, the court shall
reserve jurisdiction to determine any issues that arise with

respect to the deferred sale of home order including, but not
limited
to, the maintenance
of the home and the tax
consequences to each party.
