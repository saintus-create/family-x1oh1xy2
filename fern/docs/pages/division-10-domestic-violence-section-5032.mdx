# 5032

Revocations; effect on transfers

Revocations; effect on transfers
On revocation of a spouse’s written consent to a nonpro-
bate transfer of community property on death, the property
passes in the same manner as if the consent had not been
given. (Added by Stats.1992, c. 51 (A.B.1719),
