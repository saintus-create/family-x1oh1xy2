# 15303. Discretion of trustee;

*Support Services*

Discretion of trustee;
transferee or creditor of
beneficiary; power to compel trustee to pay any amount;
liability of trustee to creditor
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  If the trust instrument provides that the trustee shall
pay to or for the benefit of a beneficiary so much of the
income or principal or both as the trustee in the trustee’s
discretion sees fit to pay, a transferee or creditor of the
beneficiary may not compel the trustee to pay any amount
that may be paid only in the exercise of the trustee’s
discretion.
