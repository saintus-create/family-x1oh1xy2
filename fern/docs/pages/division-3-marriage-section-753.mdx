# § 753 — Exclusion of spouse from other’s dwelling

Exclusion of spouse from other’s dwelling
Notwithstanding Section 752 and except as provided in
Article 2 (commencing with Section 2045), Article 3 (com-
mencing with Section 2047), or Article 4 (commencing with
Section 2049) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of , neither
spouse may be excluded from the other’s dwelling.
(Stats.
1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994. Amend-
ed by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500),
