# § 6408 — And Wife

10th Husband
and Wife

Severability clause
If any provision of this part or its application to any person
or circumstance is held invalid, the invalidity does not affect
other provisions or applications of this part which can be
given effect without the invalid provision or application, and
to this end the provisions of this part are severable. (Added
by Stats.2001, c. 816 (A.B.731), §° 3.)

OF DOMESTIC
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Being in physical proximity to a protected individual or
following a protected individual.

(2018), In General.
