# § 8823 — Prospective adoptive parents;

The prospective adoptive parents and the child proposed to be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections 8612 and 8613. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_<div class="fl-section-nav">
