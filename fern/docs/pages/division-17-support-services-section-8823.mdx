# § 8823 — Prospective adoptive parents;

Prospective adoptive parents;
court
appearance
before
The prospective adoptive parents and the child proposed to
be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections
8612 and 8613. (Stats.1992, c. 162 (A.B.2650), § 10, operative

,
Investigation, Report, and Recommendation.
Jan. 1, 1994.)
