# § 6903 — “Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents

“Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents have legal custody, or the parent or person having legal custody, or the guardian, of a minor. _(Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)_
