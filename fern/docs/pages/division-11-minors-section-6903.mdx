---
title: "6903. “Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents"
description: "“Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents"
slug: division-11-minors-section-6903
---

“Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents have legal custody, or the parent or person having legal custody, or the guardian, of a minor.

> *Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.*
