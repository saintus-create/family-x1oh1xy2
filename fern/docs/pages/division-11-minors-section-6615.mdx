# 6615 — References to former sections deemed references to

Minors

References to former sections deemed references to
comparable provisions of this chapter
the Statutes of 1986, shall be deemed to be a reference to the
provisions of this chapter.
(Stats.1990,
c. 79
(A.B.759), § 14, operative July 1, 1991.)
