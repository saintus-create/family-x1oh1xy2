# 4331 — (a) In a proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal

Support

<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  In a proceeding for dissolution of marriage or for legal
separation of the parties, the court may order a party to
submit to an examination by a vocational training counselor.
The examination shall include an assessment of the party’s
ability to obtain employment based upon the party’s age,
health, education, marketable skills, employment history, and
the current availability of employment opportunities. The
focus of the examination shall be on an assessment of the
party’s ability to obtain employment that would allow the
party to maintain * * * their marital standard of living.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The order may be made only on motion, for good
cause, and on notice to the party to be examined and to all
parties. The order shall specify the time, place, manner,

<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  A party who does not comply with an order under this

<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  “Vocational training counselor” for the purpose of this
section means an individual with sufficient knowledge, skill,
experience, training, or education in interviewing, administering, and interpreting tests for analysis of marketable skills,
formulating career goals, planning courses of training and
study, and assessing the job market, to qualify as an expert in
vocational training under Section 720 of the Evidence Code.
<span class="subsec">(e)</span>  A vocational training counselor shall have at least the
following qualifications:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  A master’s degree in the behavioral sciences, or other
postgraduate degree that the court finds provides sufficient
training to perform a vocational evaluation.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Qualification to administer and interpret inventories
for assessing career potential.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Demonstrated ability in interviewing clients and assessing marketable skills with an understanding of age constraints, physical and mental health, previous education and
experience, and time and geographic mobility constraints.
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  Knowledge of current employment conditions, job
market, and wages in the indicated geographic area.
(5S) Knowledge of education and training programs in the
