# 14953. Relationship to other laws

*Support Services*

Relationship to other laws
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  Preemption of inconsistent State law
The Convention and this chapter shall not be construed to
preempt any provision of the law of any State or political
subdivision thereof, or prevent a State or political subdivi-
sion thereof from enacting any provision of law with respect
to the subject matter of the Convention or this chapter,
UNITED STATES CODE
except to the extent that such provision of State law is
inconsistent with the Convention or this chapter, and then
only to the extent of the inconsistency.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  Applicability of the Indian Child Welfare Act
The Convention and this chapter shall not be construed to
affect the application of the Indian Child Welfare Act of
1978.(25. U:SiC1901iet séq).
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  Relationship to other laws
Sections 3506(c), 3507, and 3512 of Title 44 shall not
apply to information collection for purposes of sections
14914, 14922(b)(4), and 14932(d) of this title or for use as a
Convention
record
as defined
in this chapter.
(Pub.L.
106-279, Title V, § 503, Oct. 6, 2000, 114 Stat. 843.)
