<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The civil penalty prescribed by this section shall be
assessed and recovered in a civil action brought in the name
of the people of the State of California by the Attorney
General or by any district attorney, county counsel, or city
attorney in any court of competent jurisdiction. An action
brought pursuant to this section to recover such civil penalties
shall take special precedence over all civil matters on the
calendar of the court except those matters to which equal
precedence on the calendar is granted by law.
