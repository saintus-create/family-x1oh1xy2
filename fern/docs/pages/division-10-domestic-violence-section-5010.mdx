# Prevention of Domestic Violence

## Section 5010

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-10-domestic-violence">Division 10: Prevention of Domestic Violence</a></div>

As used in this chapter, “written consent” to a provision for
a nonprobate transfer of community property on death
includes a written joinder in such a provision. (Added by
Stats.1992, c. 51 (A.B.1719), § 6.)
