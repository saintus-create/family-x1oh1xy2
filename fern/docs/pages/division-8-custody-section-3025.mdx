# 3025

Parental access to records

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, access to records and information pertaining to a minor child, including, but not limited to, medical, dental, and school records, shall not be denied to a parent because that parent is not the child’s custodial parent. Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.
