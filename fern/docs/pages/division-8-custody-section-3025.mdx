# Custody of Children

## Section 3025

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-8-custody">Division 8: Custody of Children</a></div>

Parental access to records Notwithstanding any other provision of law, access to records and information pertaining to a minor child, including, but not limited to, medical, dental, and school records, 

§ 3025 shall not be denied to a parent because that parent is not the child’s custodial parent.
