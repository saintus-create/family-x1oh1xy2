# § 1620

Except as otherwise provided by law, spouses cannot, by a contract with each other, alter their legal relations, except as to property. _(Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 21. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)_ FAMFamily Code - FAM
