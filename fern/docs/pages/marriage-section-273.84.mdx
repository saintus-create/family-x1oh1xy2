*Marriage*

Each district attorney’s or city attorney’s office establishing
a spousal abuser prosecution unit and receiving state support
under this chapter shall adopt and pursue the following
policies for spousal abuser cases:
following:
(a)<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Vertical prosecutorial representation, whereby the
prosecutor who, or prosecution unit that, makes all major
court appearances on that particular case through its conclusion, including bail evaluation, preliminary hearing, signifi-
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  All reasonable prosecutorial efforts shall be made to

cant law and motion litigation, trial, and sentencing.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Vertical counselor representation, whereby a trained
domestic violence counselor maintains liaison from initial
court appearances through the case’s conclusion, including
the sentencing phase.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  The assignment of highly qualified investigators and
prosecutors to spousal abuser cases. “Highly qualified” for
the purposes’ of this chapter means any of the following:
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  Individuals with one year of experience in the investigation and prosecution of felonies.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Individuals with at least two years of experience in the
investigation and prosecution of misdemeanors.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Individuals who have attended a program providing
domestic violence training as approved by the Office of
Emergency Services or the Department of Justice.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  All reasonable prosecutorial efforts shall be made, to
persuade the court to impose the most severe authorized
sentence upon a person convicted after prosecution as a
spousal abuser. In the prosecution of an intrafamily sexual
abuse case, discretion may be exercised as to the type and
nature of sentence recommended to the court.
<span class="subsec">(c)</span>  All reasonable prosecutorial efforts shall be made to
reduce the time between arrest and disposition of charge
against an individual meeting spousal abuser criteria. (Added by Stats.1985, c. 1122, § 1. Amended by Stats.1994, c. 599
(A.B.801), § 6, eff. Sept. 16, 1994; Stats.2000, c. 135 (A.B.
2539), § 131.)
