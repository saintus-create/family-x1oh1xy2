# 7501 — restrictions; public policy

Parent and Child Relationship

restrictions; public policy
<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  A parent entitled to the custody of a child has a right to
change the residence of the child, subject to the power of the
court to restrain a removal that would prejudice the rights or
welfare of the child.
<span class="subsec">(b)</span>  It is the intent of the Legislature to affirm the decision
in In re Marriage of Burgess (1996) 13 Cal.4th 25, and to
declare that ruling to be the public policy and law of this
state.
(Added by Stats.1993, c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 164.
Amended by Stats.2003, c. 674 (S.B.156), § 1.)
