# § 264 — Of the Penal Code

of the Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(d)</span>  Rape of a spouse, as defined in Section 262 of the
Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(ce)</span>  Sodomy, as defined in Section 286 of the Penal Code,
except a violation of subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of that section.
<span class="subsec">(f)</span>  A violation of Section 288 of the Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(g)</span>  Oral copulation, as defined in Section 287 of, or former
Section 288a of, the Penal Code, except a violation of
subdivision <span class="subsec">(e)</span>  of those sections.
<span class="subsec">(h)</span>  Sexual penetration, as defined in Section 289 of the
Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(i)</span>  Annoying or molesting a child under 18, as defined in
Section 647a of the Penal Code.
<span class="subsec">(j)</span>  Any attempt to commit any of the above acts.
