<span class="subsec">(a)</span>  The Secretary of State shall create a document no later
than March 1, 2020, with annual updates no later than March

and in which one or both of the parties were minors at the

<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  The total number of those registered domestic partnerships.
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  Itemized for each of those registered domestic partnerships, the age of each party at the time the domestic
partnership was established.
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  Itemized for each of those registered domestic partnerships, the gender of each party as documented on the court
order pursuant to Section 297.1, unless the court order does
not include the gender.

not contain a registered domestic partnership or a copy of the
