Insolvency
(a)  A debtor is insolvent if, at a fair valuation, the sum of
the debtor’s debts is greater than the sum of the debtor’s
assets.
(b)  A debtor that is generally not paying the debtor’s debts
as they become due other than as a result of a bona fide
dispute is presumed to be insolvent.
The presumption
