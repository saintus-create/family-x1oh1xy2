# § 4052

*Adherence of courts to uniform guidelines*

Adherence of courts to uniform guidelines
The court shall adhere to the statewide uniform guideline
and may depart from the guideline only in the special
circumstances set forth in this article. (Added by Stats.1993,
c. 219 (A.B.1500), § 138.)
