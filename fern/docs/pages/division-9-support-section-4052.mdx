# Support

## Section 4052

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Adherence of courts to uniform guidelines The court shall adhere to the statewide uniform guideline and may depart from the guideline only in the special circumstances set forth in this article.
