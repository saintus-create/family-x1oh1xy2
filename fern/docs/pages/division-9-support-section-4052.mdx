# § 4052 — Adherence of courts to uniform guidelines

The court shall adhere to the statewide uniform guideline and may depart from the guideline only in the special circumstances set forth in this article. _(Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.)_
