# § 4006

*Health insurance coverage;*

Health insurance coverage;
consideration by court
In a proceeding for child support under this code, including, but not limited to, (commencing with Section
17000), the court shall consider the health insurance coverage, if any, of the parties to the proceeding. (Stats.1992, c.
162 (A.B.2650),
Stats.2000,
§ 10, operative Jan. 1, 1994.
c. 808
(A.B.1358),
and Supporting Declaration (Governmental).
by
Treatises and Practice Aids
§ 33, eff. Sept. 28, 2000.)
