# § 4006 — Health insurance coverage; consideration by court

Health insurance coverage; consideration by court
In a proceeding for child support under this code, includ-
ing, but not limited to, (commencing with Section
17000), the court shall consider the health insurance cover-
age, if any, of the parties to the proceeding.
