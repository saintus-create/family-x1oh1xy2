# 4006

Health insurance coverage; consideration by court

In a proceeding for child support under this code, including, but not limited to, Division 17 (commencing with Section 17000), the court shall consider the health insurance coverage, if any, of the parties to the proceeding. Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 33. Effective September 28, 2000.
