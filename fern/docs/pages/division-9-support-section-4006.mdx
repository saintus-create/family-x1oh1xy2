# Support

## Section 4006

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-9-support">Division 9: Support</a></div>

Health insurance coverage; consideration by court In a proceeding for child support under this code, including, but not limited to, <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&division=17.">Division 17</a> (commencing with <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17000.">§ 17000</a>), the court shall consider the health insurance coverage, if any, of the parties to the proceeding.
