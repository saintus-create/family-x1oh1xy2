# Support Services

## Section 17414

<div class="source-note">Back to <a href="/division-17-support-services">Division 17: Support Services</a></div>

Parentage stipulation In any action or proceeding brought by the local child support agency to establish parentage pursuant to <a class="statute-link" href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=17400.">§ 17400</a>, the court shall enter a judgment establishing parentage upon the filing of a written stipulation between the parties provided that the stipulation is accompanied by a written advisement and waiver of rights which is signed by the defendant. 
The written advisement and waiver of rights shall be developed by the Judicial Council.
