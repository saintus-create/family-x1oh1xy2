# 1932. Grants for off-reservation programs for additional

*Rights and Obligations During Marriage*

Grants for off-reservation programs for additional
services
The Secretary is also authorized to make grants to Indian
organizations to establish and operate off-reservation Indian
child and family service programs which may include, but are
not limited to—
<span class="subsec">(1)</span>  a system for regulating, maintaining, and support-
ing Indian foster and adoptive homes, including a subsi-
dy program under which Indian adoptive children may
be provided support comparable to that for which they
would be eligible as Indian foster children, taking into
account the appropriate State standards of support for
maintenance and medical needs;
<span class="subsec">(2)</span>  the operation and maintenance of facilities and
services for counseling and treatment of Indian families
and Indian foster and adoptive children;
25 § 1951
<span class="subsec">(3)</span>  family
assistance,
including
homemaker
and
home counselors, day care, afterschool care, and em-
ployment, recreational activities, and respite care; and
<span class="subsec">(4)</span>  guidance, legal representation, and advice to In-
dian families involved in child custody proceedings.
(Pub.L, 95-608, Title II, § 202, Nov. 8, 1978, 92 Stat. 3076.)
