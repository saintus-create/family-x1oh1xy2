(a) Scope of service

This standard defines the standards of practice, including
duties and obligations, for providers of supervised visitation
under Family Code sections 3200 and 3200.5.
Unless

apply to all providers of supervised visitation, whether the
provider is a friend, relative, paid independent

contractor,

employee, intern, or volunteer operating independently or
through a supervised visitation center or agency. The goal of
these standards of practice is to assure the safety and welfare
of the child, adults, and providers of supervised visitation.
Once safety is assured, the best interest of the child is the

paramount consideration at all stages and particularly in
deciding the manner in which supervision is provided. Each
court is encouraged to adopt local court rules necessary to
implement these standards of practice.

Family Code section 3200 defines the term “provider” as
including any individual or supervised visitation center that
monitors visitation. Supervised visitation is contact between
a noncustodial party and one or more children in the
presence of a neutral third person.
