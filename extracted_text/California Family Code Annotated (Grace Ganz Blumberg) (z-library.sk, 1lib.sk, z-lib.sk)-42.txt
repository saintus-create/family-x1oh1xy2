§ 278. Noncustodial persons; detainment or concealment
of child from legal custodian; punishment

Every person, not having a right to custody, who maliciously takes, entices away, keeps, withholds, or conceals any child
with the intent to detain or conceal that child from a lawful
custodian shall be punished by imprisonment in a county jail
not exceeding one year, a fine not exceeding one thousand
dollars ($1,000), or both that fine and imprisonment, or by
imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 for
two, three, or four years, a fine not exceeding ten thousand
dollars ($10,000), or both that fine and imprisonment.
