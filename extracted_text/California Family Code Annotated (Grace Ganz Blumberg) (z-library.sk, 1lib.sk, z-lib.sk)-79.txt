Commentary

Terminated

In re Marilyn A., 148 Cal.App.4th 285, 55 Cal.Rptr.3d 647 (2007),
held that a juvenile court may return a dependent child to a parent’s
home at an 18—month dependency hearing, but also continue services
and court supervision to ensure the child’s safety. Marilyn A. relies on
the broad discretion conferred on the court by subsection (a) of
Welfare and Institutions Code section 366.22 and the explicit
authorization, by Cal. Rules of Ct., Rule 5.710(e)(2), of continued
services and court supervision at six-month reviews.
